Is there any way to get the visual odometry from the viso2ros package to the robotposeekf. From what I have had a look , the robotposeekf requires data on the /vo topic. Is the data coming out of visi2ros can be published on the /vo and fed into the robotposeekf??

yes, just hook up the viso2_ros/odometry to the robot_pose_ekf/vo since they are both nav_msgs/Odometry messages.
