I want you to read this contents as a reference. This might be good answer. I wrote following sentences in minimal.launch 

  &lt arg name="base"       default="$(optenv TURTLEBOT_BASE kobuki)"/ &gt
  &lt arg name="battery"    default="$(optenv TURTLEBOT_BATTERY /sys/class/power_supply/BAT0)"/ &gt
  &lt arg name="stacks"     default="$(optenv TURTLEBOT_STACKS hexagons)"/ &gt
  &lt arg name="3d_sensor"  default="$(optenv TURTLEBOT_3D_SENSOR kinect)"/ &gt
  &lt arg name="sensor"          default="$(optenv TURTLEBOT_SENSOR hokuyo)"/ &gt 
  &lt arg name="simulation" default="$(optenv TURTLEBOT_SIMULATION false)"/ &gt
  &lt arg name="tf_prefix" default="" / &gt



&lt param name="/use_sim_time" value="$(arg simulation)"/ &gt



&lt include file="$(find turtlebot_bringup)/launch/includes/robot.launch.xml &gt
    &lt arg name="base" value="$(arg base)" / &gt
    &lt arg name="stacks" value="$(arg stacks)" / &gt
    &lt arg name="3d_sensor" value="$(arg 3d_sensor)" / &gt
    &lt arg name="sensor" value="$(arg sensor)" / &gt
  &lt /include &gt
In turtlebot_bringup/launch/includes/robot.launch.xml, I added two sentences to robot.launch.xml 
&lt launch &gt
  &lt arg name="sensor"/ &gt



&lt include file="$(find turtlebot_bringup)/launch/includes/description_kobuki.launch.xml" &gt



&lt arg name="sensor" value="$(arg sensor)" / &gt 
&lt /include &gt
In kobuki_description.launch, 
&lt launch &gt
  &lt arg name="base"/&gt
  &lt arg name="stacks"/&gt
  &lt arg name="3d_sensor"/&gt
  &lt arg name="sensor"/&gt



&lt arg name="urdf_file" default="$(find xacro)/xacro.py '$(find turtlebot_description)/robots/$(arg base)$(arg stacks)$(arg 3d_sensor)_$(arg sensor).urdf.xacro'" /&gt
  &lt param name="robot_description" command="$(arg urdf_file)" / &gt
&lt /launch&gt
In kobuki_hexagons_kinect_hokuyo.urdf.xacro, 
&lt robot name="turtlebot" xmlns:xacro="http://ros.org/wiki/xacro"&gt



&lt xacro:include filename="$(find turtlebot_description)/urdf/turtlebot2_library.urdf.xacro" /&gt



&lt kobuki/&gt
  &lt stack_hexagons parent="base_link"/&gt
  &lt sensor_kinect  parent="plate_top_link"/&gt
  &lt sensor_hokuyo parent="plate_middle_link"&gt
&lt/robot &gt
After that, I just write *.urdf.xacro.
