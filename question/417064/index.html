Ok, I think I get it now.

The Red line of base_link faces from the center of the robot forwards. The Red line of base_laser points in the forwards direction of the laser - which is at 90 deg to base_link, as my Lidar is mounted offset by 90 degrees.

Base_laser is raised above base link as the Lidar is higher than the base of the robot. Base_laser is slighly 'forwards' of base_link as the Lidar is mounted slightly forwards of center on the robot.

This helped me figure it out: http://wiki.ros.org/hector_slam/Tutorials/SettingUpForYourRobot

I re edited the ld19.launch file to reset the spin direction to counter clockwise - which whilst it feels wrong, it does work, as the manual maybe suggests why.
