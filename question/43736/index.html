Turtlebot 2D Nav Goal not working

This question has also been migrated to Robotics StackExchange as https://robotics.stackexchange.com/questions/44094/turtlebot-2d-nav-goal-not-working.

I am running the turtlebot navigation demo in fuerte and 12.04 Precise. Everything works fine up until I try and set a nav goal in rviz, then nothing happens. The console output says that a goal is being set, but the turtlebot does not move and no messages are sent on movebasesimple/goal. I am using the versions of rviz and the demos directly from the repositories with no modifications. Does anyone know why this might be happening?

Answers

It sounds like you don't have the proper goal topic configured in rviz.

In particular, you should set the 2D Nav Toal topic to /move_base_simple/goal in the Tool Properties window.

Take a look at http://ros.org/wiki/navigation/Tutorials/Using%20rviz%20with%20the%20Navigation%20Stack
