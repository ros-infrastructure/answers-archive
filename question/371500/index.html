Google cartographer Laser from wrong origin

Hello!

I'm trying to use Google Cartographer with a LiDAR and a robot with wheel odometry data. As the laser messages are being processed, the points that they "hit" are derived correctly with the "laserlink" as origin however the "rays" of free area is then derived using the "baselink" which is offcource wrong since that gives the wrong perspective.

To clearly illustrate the problem, here is a case where the laser is inside a building and the joint to the "baselink" is set far away such that it is outside of the building. All of the rays are clearly derived from the "baselink" even though the 2D points that are found by the LiDAR are all correct.

(I could not post an image but here is a link)

https://imgur.com/a/HiDsFMg

As can bee seen the robot is moving a bit but it is still clear that the "rays of unoccupied space" has its origin in the baselink when is should originate from the LiDAR. Not again that all the points are in the correct area even though they are given as distance/angle which means that they are in fact derived from the "laserlink".
