Why is the turtlebot gazebo kinect not in color?

This question has also been migrated to Robotics StackExchange as https://robotics.stackexchange.com/questions/34865/why-is-the-turtlebot-gazebo-kinect-not-in-color.

I noticed that the kinect sensor in turtlebotgazebo (in the unstable release) produces only black and white images. The gazebo.urdf.xacro file looks like it is supposed to produce color. Also, gazeborosopennikinect.cpp looks like it should be producing color. Are there plans to get the kinect gazebo working in color soon?

Answers

It actually is in color, it just turns the pixels that are beyond the maximum depth of the kinect sensor to a gray color.
