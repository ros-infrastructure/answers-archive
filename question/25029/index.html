Is ign-gazebo the future?

Yes! There won't be other major Gazebo-classic releases as explained on its project status.

Should we start trying to use it now?

You definitely can. Whether that's the preferred route will depend on your specific use case.

Is there a roadmap to feature parity?

We're working towards it. See the feature comparison and our roadmap.
