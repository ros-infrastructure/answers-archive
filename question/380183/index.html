The problem is at the frequency; my ROS node runs with a ROS rate of 10 Hz and even if I overclock the node, it won't meet the base frequency of a PWM Signal of a MOSFET which is 100kHz. Asked by abhishek47 on 2021-06-13 23:31:38 UTC

Regardless of whether anyone would be using ROS: running a distributed 100kHz control loop and achieving all latency and jitter requirements / conditions will be rather challenging with COTS hw standard PCs and OS.

We can look at the code, but I doubt that will allow us to help.

At these control frequencies, keeping the lowest-level loops (ie: those running at very high frequencies) as close as possible to the hw is customary, and you'd use a dedicated bit of embedded hw to run those loops. External systems can then provide setpoints at a (much) lower frequency. That would decouple most of the system from the 100 kHz requirement.

But for generating PWM signals, I'd personally look at using some dedicated hw, such as the PRUs many Beaglebone boards support.
