This could also be a problem with your choice of coordinate frames in rviz. If your fixed or target frame isn't valid, rviz will fail to find a transform from the the camera frame to your target frame, so it displays nothing. Try choosing a different frames.

Asked by Robert Buzink on 2012-01-11 02:50:43 UTC

Apparently I messed around with rviz settings too much. After I set fixed_frame to /base_link and target Frame to in .Global Options, everything worked as expected. You can set the options in the rviz window or in .rviz/config and .rviz/display_config.
