Asked by dagiopia on 2019-03-01 07:34:20 UTC

Gazebo uses an East, North, Up coordinate system and the angles most commonly associated with that.

The (0,0,0) vector points east. Counter-intuitively, positive pitch swings the vector DOWN (it makes sense when you draw out the axes, I swear!). Positive yaw moves the vector counter-clockwise (math, not navigation).

You check these assumptions by spawning a small, static model. This answer may still be relevant; I haven't had a chance to check: http://answers.gazebosim.org/question/5642/show-reference-frames/
