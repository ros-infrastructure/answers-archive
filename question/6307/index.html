Hi,

when running the 

echo '/usr/local/lib' | sudo tee /etc/ld.so.conf.d/gazebo.conf 
sudo ldconfig

command make sure you change '/usr/local/lib' to where your gazebo libraries are, do a $ locate libgazebo in the terminal to find out where they are.

Cheers, Andrei
