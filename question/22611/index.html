Actually, libhusky_gazebo_plugins.so simply doesn't exist. What you have to do is remove the related plugin line from your SDF, and you will notice no change in the movements of the husky bot. And to fix the way husky is running, you can try changing the mass of the model and friction prams. In my case, changing mass worked.
