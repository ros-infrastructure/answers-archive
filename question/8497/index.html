Standard Units of Measure and Coordinate Conventions in Gazebo

I just wonder what are the standard units of measure in Gazebo? Is there anything like ROS REP 103 to standardize the units?

Answers

As far as I know, there's no complete documentation like the ROS REP 103.

In general, Gazebo uses SI units, for example:

  • length: meter (m)
  • mass: kilogram (Kg)
  • time: second (s)
  • angle: radian (gazebo::math::Angle::Degree() to convert to degrees)
  • force: Newton (N)

Coordinate frames are right-handed, with Z up, X forward and Y to the left.

Euler angles follow the roll (about X axis), pitch (about Y axis), yaw (about Z axis) order about a fixed reference frame.
