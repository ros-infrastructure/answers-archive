Logitech extreme 3d pro joystick

This question has also been migrated to Robotics StackExchange as https://robotics.stackexchange.com/questions/84924/logitech-extreme-3d-pro-joystick.

This joystick isn't driving the diffdrivecontroller right. Forward is good, reverse is good, steering in reverse is good, but steering forward is backwards. When I want to go left, robot goes right! Is the best solution for this to remap the joystick output? I see there's a node that can change input values into others, for keypad or joystick. joystickremapper. I see good examples and description of button remapping but none of axis remapping. Or would this behavior be corrected with different values in the covariance matrix in the parameters for diffdrive? My robot is the RosJet (or very like it.)

Answers

This corrected itself. I was driving the unit backwards and didn't know it. When I changed from the small (test) robot to the large one, the motor connections happened to be reversed, so it went correctly. The steering in reverse with joystick is a little strange, the caster portion of robot moves the direction of the joystick but the base motion is the other way as you drive. This would be just a matter of choice, I suppose, that the creators of diff_drive decided. It isn't a problem now, since I expect to spend little time driving in reverse.
