I was struggling to get them to function in MoveIt! since closed kinematic loops are not supported in URDF or MoveIt! Today I got it to work relatively well by just not including the mimic joints that would close the loops in the control groups and turning off collisions for them (since they are mostly stationary until the movement is actually executed).
