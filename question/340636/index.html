What are *.urdf.xacro and *.gazebo.xacro files?

I am new to ros. I would like to know what are *.urdf.xacro file. similarly what is *.gazebo.xacro file. However I understand .urdf and .xacro file formats

I am using ubuntu 16.04 and ros kinetic version.

These are not special files, but they do follow a naming convention.

Typically, files with .xacro suffix are files that should be processed by xacro before being passed on to other programs.

After processing, convention dictates to remove the .xacro suffix.

So for the two examples of file extensions you mention in your question, we get this:

  • .urdf.xacro: after processing by xacro, remove .xacro and we end up with a .urdf
  • .gazebo.xacro: after processing by xacro, remove the .xacro and we end up with a .gazebo
