Hello everyone, I have some problems in emergency obstacle avoidance by adding ultrasonic sensors in the navigation. I used the lidar and a front ultrasonic sensor in my car. The lidar alone can avoid obstacles, but in order to make the blind area smaller, I added an ultrasonic. I used the rangesensorlayer tool, and the range can be displayed normally in RVIZ. However, it seems that the obstacles detected by ultrasonic wave are not considered in the cost map, so my car will encounter obstacles when moving. Please help me, thank you.
