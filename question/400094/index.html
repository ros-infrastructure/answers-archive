Asked by ixtora on 2022-05-05 10:42:18 UTC

Your error doesn't come from the sensor it self. It's simply not localised in the world. RViz doesn;t have the information to localise it properly. The most simple way to fix this is to add a static_transform. This open course explains it quite well: https://ocw.tudelft.nl/course-lectures/5-3-3-tf-tf2-ros-command-line-tools-static_transform_publisher/

I found the soultion, the problem was in this line:

range_msg.header.frame_id =  "ultrasound";
