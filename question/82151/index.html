You could call MoveIt's compute_ik service (service description) and then use the computed joint positions to generate motions commands for your robot.

PS: MoveIt's mailing list is (currently) the better place to ask MoveIt-related questions: https://groups.google.com/forum/#!forum/moveit-users As of April 2017 answers.ros.org is the recommended QA site.
