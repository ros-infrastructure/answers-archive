Asked by verena on 2019-06-24 09:37:32 UTC

I just solved my problem.

I had some broken spring contacts inside the LWA4P, f.e. the CAN_ERB_H and CAN_ERB_L. That's why my CAN modules have not been recognized. I fixed it and finally it works like a charm.
