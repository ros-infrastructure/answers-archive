Is wait_for_service required before every service call?

This question has also been migrated to Robotics StackExchange as https://robotics.stackexchange.com/questions/81197/is-wait-for-service-required-before-every-service-call.

Hi, I don't fully understand the mechanics of rospy's waitforservice function. Are we required to call wait for service every time we want to call the service, or only once when creating the ServiceProxy?

Asked by hamzamerzic on 2017-06-08 03:34:59 UTC

Comments

Answers

You don't need to call wait_for_service, but it is highly advisable, since you want to make sure that your service is available before calling it.
