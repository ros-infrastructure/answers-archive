Kinect light flashing with openni, but works with freenect

This question has also been migrated to Robotics StackExchange as https://robotics.stackexchange.com/questions/44906/kinect-light-flashing-with-openni-but-works-with-freenect.

Hello all,

I am running Ubuntu 12.04 with ROS Fuerte and the latest openicamera and opennilaunch packages that are available through Ubuntu's package manager. I have an XBox 360 Kinect. When I run roslaunch openni_launch openni.launch, I get the output below. No errors are present, but yet the green light continues to flash, the IR light never turns on, and the unit just sits there endlessly. Firing up rviz and setting fixed and target frames to /camera_depth_optical_frame does nothing.

Strangely, when I use the test programs for libfreenect, it works just fine. Any ideas?

Thanks.
