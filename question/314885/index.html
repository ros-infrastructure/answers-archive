Thank you for your inquiry about Dynamixels.

Simply, the advantage of using RS-485 is more robustness when you need to long long communication line. If you have a plan to make humanoid robot, you could use TTL rather than RS-485. And you can use Dynamixels up to 200 connected with additional power.
