Yes this is possible in ROS. One suggestion, you have put your whole question in "Title" section, please observe the practice of keeping title as small as possible and remaining question can be asked in detail.

Coming to your question now: I would advise you to study ROS communications medium at beginning (i.e Topics, Services and Actions) Then you should look at ROS Localisation, Mapping and Navigation individually then I would take on SLAM.

Along with this i would advise you to study about turtlebot (especially it's laser and camera sesnor's data handling ), doing a small simulation with turtlebot would help you.

I would suggest a good book for doing this all:- Programming_Robots_with_ROS by OREILLY media.

Best of luck for ROS endeavours
