On a side note, you seem to have a misunderstanding about understanding Quaternion. There are several ways to represent orientation in 3D space. One of the ways uses 3 numbers, commonly known as roll, pitch, and yaw. The other way, Quaternion, uses 4 parameters, x, y, z, and w. The mathematics behind Quaternion is really fascinating. Lastly, there are many other ways to represent orientation in 3D space.
