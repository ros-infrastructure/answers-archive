I've closed this as it's a problem with Gazebo, not a ROS one. More specifically this error message points to problems with the (virtual) graphics hardware and the way the drivers in your VM are trying to use them. Gazebo is a heavy user of OpenGL/3D accelerated graphics, and without support, will crash most of the time.

Please post your question on the Gazebo Answers forum.
