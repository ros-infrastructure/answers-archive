Thanks, this answer has fixed my problem. It seems the SW2URDF exporter only works on assemblies, not individual part files as the tutorials for the plug-in here suggests.If anyone else is experiencing this problem, I would recommend just skipping the part tutorial and going to the next tutorial, the one on exporting assemblies to URDF.
