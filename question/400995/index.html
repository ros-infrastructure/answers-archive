How to output in the terminal from a ros plugin ?

Hello everyone,

I am working on the package gravitycompensationcontroller_tutorial on a real tiago, and I wish to get some real-time outputs in the terminal. Thus I used ROS_INFO_STREAM() and output:'screen' in the launch file, but I don't get any of my outputs in the terminal. Looking into the log files, i found with grep the outputs in :

./deployer.pal.log/@400000005a7091d92cfb6f2c.s

./deployer.pal.log/current

./rosout.log

./rosout.log.2

./rosout.log.1

I guess the output:'screen' is not targeting the right thing, do you happen to know what I need to do to get those outputs in the terminal ? I don't know if this is a relevant info but what's in the package is a plugin of ros_control.

Answers

I have found a workaround to show my outputs using rosconsole:

rosconsole echo -l info

and once I spotted the name of the logger where my outputs originates from, I added a FILTER:

rosconsole echo -l info /rtt_deployer

I hope this can helps others :)
