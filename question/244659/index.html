I want get the robot position(something like the coordinate x,y,z) , I know that hectorgazeboplugins can do this,but I don't know how to use the API,did something more simple to get the robot position in gazebo?

Answers

Simple. Use GetPose utility. For eg:

gazebo::math::Pose pose = this->model->gazebo::physics::ModelState::GetPose();

You can convert it into your preferred coordinate system through simple transformation.
