This question has also been migrated to Robotics StackExchange as https://robotics.stackexchange.com/questions/77034/how-to-run-tf-view-frames-for-a-remapped-tf.

I remapped /tf to /tf_0, but I can't get rosrun tf view_frames to show me the tree for /tf_0. How to do it?

Asked by DavidN on 2016-10-31 01:37:12 UTC

Answers

You can use normal command-line based remapping:

rosrun tf view_frames /tf:=/tf_0

should do it.
