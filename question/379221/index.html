Answers

RVIZ2 display cylinders with a radius bigger than 1m (and maybe truncates non-integer values). To avoid this just type :

export LC_NUMERIC="en_US.UTF-8"

You can add this line to your bashrc.

ref: https://answers.ros.org/question/389967/urdf-and-rviz2-cylinder-not-showing/
