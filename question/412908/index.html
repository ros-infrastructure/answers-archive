Hi,

UWSim is more of a nice renderer that takes either your robot current pose or cmd_vel as an input.

In order to do path planning you have to get a representation of the robot and its surrounding environment in a dedicated planner. How you fill this environment with information is up to you. UWSim can publish 3D point clouds but you could just use the ground truth when testing the planner itself.

To my point of view the resulting trajectory should be visualized in RViz and not UWSim.
