rosdoc and wiki updates

This question has also been migrated to Robotics StackExchange as https://robotics.stackexchange.com/questions/32152/rosdoc-and-wiki-updates.

What needs to happen for rosdoc to automatically find and generate stuff for a PackageHeader on the wiki? Specifically, I'm making the wiki page for pr2markercontrol, which is in trunk of pr2objectmanipulation, and it still says "Newly proposed, mistyped, or obsolete package. Could not find package "pr2markercontrol" in rosdoc."

I suspect this is because the released version of pr2objectmanipulation is a branch; does rosdoc only look through specific branches of stacks?

Answers

The rosdoc indexer is controlled by this rosinstall file:

https://code.ros.org/svn/ros/stacks/rosorg/trunk/rosdoc_rosorg/repos.rosinstall

The rosinstall file can be updated by people with commit access (usually me).
