Answers

I can't comment on pocketsphinx specifically but setting the upper frequency at 6.8kHz when you're sampling at 8kHz doesn't seem correct.

I suggest you put the sample rate back to the default 16kHz or reduce the upperf to 3kHz. Those coefficients are for the FFT filter and the max frequency out of the filter should be a healthy margin below 50% of the sampling frequency.
