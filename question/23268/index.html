Follow this tutorial: http://gazebosim.org/tutorials?tut=actor#Animation

You can set the <skin> tag to your Blender collada file and the <animation> to the desired animation. Servicesim may be of use to you if you want an example of a simulation that contains moving actors: https://www.openrobotics.org/blog/2018/5/22/service-robot-simulator
