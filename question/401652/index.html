There is a package rviz_visual_tools which implements a field of view in RViz:

  • https://github.com/RoboticsYY/rviz_visual_tools

  • https://github.com/ros-planning/moveit/issues/1070#issuecomment-514142891

  • https://github.com/PickNikRobotics/rviz_visual_tools/pull/117

I hope you can get what you wanted out of it
