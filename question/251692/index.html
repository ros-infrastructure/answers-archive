Here there are some repos for underwater simulation and development.

  • COLA2: ROS and Gazebo
  • VRX: ROS and Gazebo
  • UUV Simulator: ROS and Gazebo
  • Plankton: ROS2 and Gazebo
  • Ignition native underwater vehicles: Ignition
  • UW Vehicle Simulated in Ignition: Ignition
  • Project Dave: ROS and Gazebo
  • DS SIM: ROS and Gazebo
  • Hydrographic Messages: ROS

Check this link if you are interested in helping the community developing open maritime robotics software: https://discourse.ros.org/t/new-working-group-proposal-maritime-robotics/23832
