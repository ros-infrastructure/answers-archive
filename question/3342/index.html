How to just rotate the sensor on a bot in gazebo?

I want to rotate (up and down) the Hokuyo sensor monted on a quadrotor, how ca it be done?

Asked by Mrit91 on 2013-06-26 10:38:02 UTC

Answers

If there is a joint connected to the hokuyo sensor, then you can use the joint control GUI widget to make the Hokuyo move.

Grab the dotted white handle located on the far right side of the gui, and drag to the left. The handle is centered vertically.
