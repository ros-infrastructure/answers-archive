allow two robots occupying the same pose

Hi,

Is it possible to have two robots occupying the same pose, and invisible to each other? i.e. they will not have collision detected with each other, and can cross each other as if nothing is present in motion planning.

Answers

If you don't want them to collide with other things and don't care about dynamics, you can remove their collisions and set gravity to off. Then just move them calling SetWorldPose.
