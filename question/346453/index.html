Quick comment: ROS does not "contain" PCL at all. The packages in perception_pcl only depend on the system-provided libpcl-dev (at least, that is the name of the library on Debian/Ubuntu). So to determine whether certain fuctionality is present, look at what the system-provided PCL contains.
