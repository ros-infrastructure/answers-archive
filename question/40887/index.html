I have had no rviz problems since the launch of Fuerte with my current setup ROS Fuerte installed through the recommended repositories in ROS documentation, on Ubuntu 10.4

Thanks in advance

Update: For some reason I can't add an answer for this post so here is the "solution" to my problem.

Update RVIZ to the latest version, as of a week after I originally asked the question.

I originally had in my post that I suspected the source of my problem was a broken code commit. This hypothesis was removed by a moderator. Given the solution to the problem, it is almost certain that my hypothesis was correct.
