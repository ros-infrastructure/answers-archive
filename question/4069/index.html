Try using continous joints instead of revolute s. http://wiki.ros.org/urdf/XML/joint

revolute - a hinge joint that rotates along the axis and has a limited range specified by the upper and lower limits. continuous - a continuous hinge joint that rotates around the axis and has no upper and lower limits
