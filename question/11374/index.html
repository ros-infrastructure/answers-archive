Although this does not directly answer your question, there are alternatives to nav_view.

University of Colorado at Boulder:

svn checkout http://prairiedog.googlecode.com/svn/trunk/nav_view_cu nav_view_cu-read-only

Stanford University:

svn co https://sail-ros-pkg.svn.sourceforge.net/svnroot/sail-ros-pkg/trunk/semistable/visualization/nav_view_sdl nav_view_sdl

or here for tarball.
