Change view to what the camera is viewing

This question has also been migrated to Robotics StackExchange as https://robotics.stackexchange.com/questions/28465/change-view-to-what-the-camera-is-viewing.

I have created a world with a robot, a pioneer 2DX and added a camera.

I want to see what the camera is seeing but I don't know how.

How can I change to what the camera is seeing?

Asked by VansFannel on 2018-04-17 10:34:12 UTC

Comments

Answers

  1. On the top menu, choose Window -> Topic Visualization
  2. Look for gazebo.msgs.ImageStamped and your camera's topic under it
  3. Double click it
