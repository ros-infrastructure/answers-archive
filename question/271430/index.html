Answers

'support' will depend a bit on what you are after: motoman_driver supports YRC1000 controllers starting from v1.5.0, but will not work with any of the collaborative features.

We also don't yet have a robot support package for the HC10, but those are relatively trivial to create.

The Motoman HC10 and HC20 are now available in the Motoman repo with the motoman_drive v1.9.0. However some of the collaborative robot feather have limitations under ROS. You should carefully review the associated note: HC Robot Notes for ROS
