Some of your options are:

  • Create an STL or DAE and import it, as you mentioned. Here's a tutorial.

  • Use the building editor and make the curved walls out of many small straight walls.

  • Instead of making the walls by hand with the editor, make them programmatically with embedded ruby, here's an example.

Since you mentioned you're new to Gazebo, I also recommend the beginner tutorial series to get you started.
