Answers

You are missing 

# Generate added messages and services with any dependencies listed here
 generate_messages(
   DEPENDENCIES
   actionlib_msgs
   geometry_msgs 
   std_msgs
 )

call generate_messages, right after you add the action, srv, or msg files.
