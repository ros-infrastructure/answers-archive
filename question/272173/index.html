Boeing is a sponsor of ROS-Industrial: http://rosindustrial.org/news/2015/8/19/scan-n-plan-for-robotic-blending-milestone-3, and Airbus and GKN Aerospace are listed on http://rosindustrial.org/ric/current-members/ (it would be nice instead of an image full of logos there were clickable links to the different companies, and some of them are too small to read...).

It doesn't mean they are using ROS internally necessarily, but the interest is there.
