check your urdf: my case was

<joint
    name="right_forward_steering_joint"
    type="continuous">
    <origin
      xyz="-0.71499 0.65792 0.2505"
      rpy="1.5708 0 0" />
    <parent
      link="base_link" />
    <child
      link="right_forward_steering" />
    <axis
      xyz="0 0 0" />
</joint>

the xyz axis was (0 0 0), i solved it by adding 1 in the axis of rotation so it will be 

<axis xyz="0 1 0" />
