Pointcloud/frame problems on Rviz switching from ROS Melodic to Noetic

Hello, I am working on Rviz with a drone facing a boat. On ROS Melodic, everything worked fine, for example the pointcloud in Rviz faces the drone as expected and as seen in Gazebo. But when I swith to Noetic, using the same code, the pointcloud is generated up to the drone, and not facing it anymore. Another curious behavior : in ROS Melodic, I needed to use a statictransformpublisher between baselink and depthcameralink, if I remove this transform it didn't work and the pointcloud was not generated. On ROS Noetic, if I remove the statictransform, we can still see the pointcloud up to the drone.

Any idea ?

In noetic, make sure all your frame_id are consistent: for a particular frame_id you can no longer have some with a leading slash and some without.

Asked by Mike Scheutzow on 2022-12-14 08:37:13 UTC

P.S. The ros convention is to NOT use a leading slash on a frame name.
