catkin is not creating package.

This question has also been migrated to Robotics StackExchange as https://robotics.stackexchange.com/questions/75421/catkin-is-not-creating-package.

cd ~/catkinws/src
catkincreatepkg mypack
cd ..
catkinmake

Everything is ok.

roscd mypack
mypack not found.

why?

Asked by Oper on 2016-07-25 15:38:00 UTC

Comments

Answers

roscd will not know where to locate your package until you have sourced your setup.bash. After you have compiled, in the catkin_ws directory in the terminal enter 'source devel/setup.bash'.
