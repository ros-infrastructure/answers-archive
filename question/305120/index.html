If I understand the error message correctly, you are not running ZSH, but Bash.

You should not source the setup.zsh, but the setup.bash file.

im trying to understand ROS but even the simplest of things gives me errors. might it be because im using a VRdisk from OSboxes.org and im not a root of the system?

not being root has most likely nothing to do with this. You just cannot source the ZSH version of that file, unless you are also using ZSH as your shell (which you are not).

im putting all these commands in a raw shell without having started hrwros (the course im following). should i be working in that?

yes, you should be running all ROS commands in the HRWROS shell. I believe the course instructs you to do that a number of times.
