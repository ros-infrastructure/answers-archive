    print('SHEDROBOT :',goal)
    time.sleep(0.1)

talker.unadvertise()

Answers

talker = roslibpy.Topic(client, '/chatter', 'Pose')

needs to be

talker = roslibpy.Topic(client, '/chatter', 'geometry_msgs/Pose')

see https://roslibpy.readthedocs.io/en/latest/reference/index.html#roslibpy.Topic.
