Gazebo8 (to be released in January 2017) will ship with a plugin that draws a line while your model is moving:

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=egWAMbU7f6Y

Until then, you might want to implement your own solution, where you create visuals with DynamicLines directly in the scene as the model moves.
