Husky Gazebo robot_state_publisher not broadcasting transforms

This question has also been migrated to Robotics StackExchange as https://robotics.stackexchange.com/questions/95535/husky-gazebo-robot-state-publisher-not-broadcasting-transforms.

I'm trying to record a bag from the Husky Gazebo simulation for Cartographer SLAM on Kinetic and Ubuntu 18.04 VM. The problem I'm facing is that some of the TF transforms are not being included in the bag file. Here's the steps I took: Launch the simulator: roslaunch husky_gazebo husky_playpen.launch This correctly launches the simulator. I think the error is that `robotstate_publisher` is not sending out TF transforms. When I record a bag with rosbag record -O Husky /tf /odometry/filtered /scan [..]

what about /tf_static?

Even though the average rate is 10,000 hz

those are most likely static frames, which don't get broadcast periodically. They're given a 'fake' rate of 10kHz.

Asked by luketheduke on 2020-04-21 09:44:25 UTC

As gvdhoorn pointed out, I wasn't recording /tf_static and that meant that I wasn't recording any static TF transforms, which were the ones that were missing. So the fix was just to rerecord with rosbag record -O Husky /tf /tf_static /odometry/filtered /scan
