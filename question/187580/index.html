Flip a video file

Hi, I use gscam to play a video file. My config line in the launch file looks like this

<param name="gscam_config" value="filesrc location=$(arg FILENAME) ! decodebin ! ffmpegcolorspace"/>

I have videos that needs to be flipped vertically. I am trying to add the flip to this line, but it doesn't work. I have found this in the gstreamer doc :

http://www.freedesktop.org/software/gstreamer-sdk/data/docs/2012.5/gst-plugins-good-plugins-0.10/gst-plugins-good-plugins-videoflip.html

I have tried :

<param name="gscam_config" value="filesrc location=$(arg FILENAME) ! decodebin ! ffmpegcolorspace ! videoflip method=vertical-flip"/> but no luck.

Hi, you might as well try Joyoshare Video Cutter, an all-in-one video editor to flip your video without any hassle. With it, you just drag the video to it - click Edit and choose Adjust. Under Adjust editing tab, you can flip video vertically or horizontally, rotate video clockwise or anti-clockwise, flexibly crop video, etc.
