Answers

  • Gazebo is used in Industry for R&D:

    • Agile Robotics for Industrial Automation Competition (ARIAC): https://www.nist.gov/el/intelligent-systems-division-73500/agile-robotics-industrial-automation-competition
    • Fraunhofer IPA: https://www.ipa.fraunhofer.de/content/dam/ipa/en/documents/Expertises/Roboter--und-Assistenzsysteme/Product_sheet_ROS_Industrial.pdf

  • Due to Gazebos Open World approach there is also a strong use for Outdoor applications, especially by Companys in Agriculture:

    • Clearpath: https://www.clearpathrobotics.com/warthog-unmanned-ground-vehicle-robot/
    • Naio: https://www.fira-agtech.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/07/MYR2017-SIMULATOR.pdf
    • Bosch: http://www.fieldrobot.com/ieeeras/Downloads/20150923-Sander-Presentation.pdf
    • Claas: https://www.hs-osnabrueck.de/fileadmin/HSOS/Forschung/Recherche/Laboreinrichtungen_und_Versuchsbetriebe/Labor_fuer_Mikro_und_Optoelektronik/Veroeffentlichungen/2017-VDI-A_multilevel_simulation_framework_for_highly_automated_harvest_processes_enabled_by_environmental_sensor_systems.pdf

I would not claim that list to be exhaustive. There are probably many more ...
