How to estimate velocity from robot_pose_ekf

This question has also been migrated to Robotics StackExchange as https://robotics.stackexchange.com/questions/96039/how-to-estimate-velocity-from-robot-pose-ekf.

I have a differential drive mobile robot. and I am using robotposeekf to estimate the state of the robot [x,y,θ]

How to estimate [v,ω ] the translational and rotational velocities. from robotposeekf output or adding [v,ω ] as an output from robotposeekf [x,y,θ,v,ω] .

Asked by Ahmed_Desoky on 2020-06-02 16:18:44 UTC

Comments

Answers

The output topic is http://docs.ros.org/api/geometry_msgs/html/msg/PoseWithCovarianceStamped.html which is not providing velocity, I don't think robot pose EKF allows you to do this. Robot Localization however will allow you to do this.
