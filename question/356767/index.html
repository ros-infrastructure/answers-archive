  </sensor>

Any tips on how to solve this issue? Thank you.

update: I noticed that, when I removed this line from start_gaserver: 

export GAZEBO_PLUGIN_PATH=$pack_path/plugins:$GAZEBO_PLUGIN_PATH

Then the Kinect camera can publish topics, but the drone topics are missing. On contrast, when I include this line, I got the drone topics but not the Kinect topics.
