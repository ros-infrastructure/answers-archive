In general that page holds the aggregated results of individuals testing. Hopefully as people try new cards they will write back there what works or doesn't work. It's not complete and there's no systematic updating. And unfortunately someone always has to take the risk and be the first to try the newer cards. Though recently the likelyhood of getting a card which works has gone up.

If you want to look outside for more options I'd suggest that you look at what cards people report being able to run Ogre or other Ogre based applications.
