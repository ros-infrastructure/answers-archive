Hi @aspq:

There are many ways to start. If you are new to ROS I suggest you start with ROS Noetic, get some projects under your belt and move to ROS2. There is excellent information for ROS2 out there but for a beginner it may not the easiest to start.

Second invest in good books, there are many: http://wiki.ros.org/Books with great examples that will walk you step by step how to achieve what you are looking for.

If you prefer to watch videos, I suggest this series from James Bruto : https://youtu.be/q1u_cC-5Sac

Also make sure to complete the tutorials: http://wiki.ros.org/ROS/Tutorials

And for Navigation: http://wiki.ros.org/navigation/Tutorials

And of course if you have questions, ask them in this forum.
