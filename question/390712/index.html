Also:

Is there a roadmap for ROS to support collaborative features in the future?

this is not really a question you can ask. "roadmap for ROS" does not exist, or at least not in the way I believe you are using that phrase here.

There is a "roadmap for ROS" (here), but that would be ROS 2, and it deals with the development of "core ROS 2", not with specific drivers for specific brands of HW.

There is a safety working group though, for which this may be a topic. You could reach out to them (via ROS Discourse probably) and see whether they've discussed this.
