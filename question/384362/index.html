what does <rosparam param="a_list">[1, 2, 3, 4]</rosparam> mean?

What does [1, 2, 3, 4] mean in launch file? especially ">" between "a_list" and "[1,2,3,4]"

Asked by cslxiao on 2021-08-12 18:50:03 UTC

Comments

Answers

It's just XML tags. The '>' closes the rosparam tag. Read it like:

rosparam(attribute "param":=a_list)
    [1,2,3,4]
endrosparam
