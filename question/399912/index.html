ros2 topic echo

but unlike the command above, also for services and actions. As I am sniffing the data I do not need to instrument the ROS-Nodes sending the data.

I would however prefer to have the actual node name instead of the appId. I however cannot seem to find a correct way to "link" the appId to a node name.

Can anyone point me in the right direction? Is this something that is dependent on the ROS 2 middleware being used? I am currently using Eprosima's fastdds.
