Answers

It is embarrassing but just after posting this I came across the answer: https://answers.ros.org/question/361966/rosrun-still-use-python27-instead-of-38-inside-noetic/

I was missing '3' in the script's shebang: 

#!/user/bin/env python3

fixed everything.
