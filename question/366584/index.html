There's way too much in your follow-up post for me to comment on.

As a high-level response:

  1. if you use ros_control, you don't write anything, except a hardware_interface implementation, which translates between your specific hardware and "the rest" of ros_control. After that, you get to reuse all the controllers from ros_control. Including the joint_trajectory_controller.
  2. re: your action server: well, conceptually, that's indeed what's needed for MoveIt to be able to communicate with your hw. But as I wrote in #1, there may be easier ways to achieve this.
  3. re: "did not find this in the tutorials": if you're referring to the MoveIt tutorials, then I'm not surprised. MoveIt is a motion planning framework. It does not deal with hw directly. At some point they will have to assume you have the infrastructure working so their documentation can focus on what they do, which is planning.
