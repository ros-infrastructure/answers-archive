Hi,

I was trying to connect to the turtlebot and I think I accidentally changed the ROSMASTERURI and ROS_HOSTNAME. Now, whenever I run roscore I get this:

WARNING: ROSMASTERURI port [80] does not match this roscore [11311]

Any help on this would be appreciated. Thanks.

Answers

Ok I figured it out.. Just changed my ROS_MASTER_URI to localhost. ie., export ROS_MASTER_URI=http://localhost:11311
