Cannot launch the latest drcsim

This question has also been migrated to Robotics StackExchange as https://robotics.stackexchange.com/questions/26449/cannot-launch-the-latest-drcsim.

Hi.

I tried to launch drcsim like this:

$ roslaunch atlas_utils qual_task_1.launch

then gazebo process dies generating the following message:

Warning [gazebo_main.cc:62] escalating to SIGKILL on client

Here is the specification of my PC:

  • Ubuntu 12.04, 64bit
  • ROS : fuerte
  • drcsim : 2.6.2-1~precise
  • gazebo : 1.8.1-1~precise

Moreover, I can successfully launch drcsim like this:

$ optirun roslaunch atlas_utils qual_task_1.launch

What is the cause of this problem? Is there any way to launch drcsim without optirun?
