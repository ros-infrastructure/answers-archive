Try setting min_depth in your model like so

<surface>
  <contact>
    <ode>
      <min_depth>0.001</min_depth>
    </ode>
  </contact>
  <friction>
    <ode>
       <mu>10000</mu>
       <mu2>10000</mu2>
    </ode>
  </friction>
</surface>

Other contact parameters of interest might be kp and kd.
