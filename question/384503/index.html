navigation stack cannot navigate to goal

Hi, I am trying to use nagivation stack to navigate to a goal that is about 1km away. I am simulating my robot in Gazebo. My robot is successfully able to navigate to very close goals (~10m), but when I set the goal to the 1km waypoint, the robot goes into recovery mode and fails to go to the goal. Instead it rotates in place. I have increased my global costmap to encompass my waypoint. Is there anything else I need to edit to get my robot to navigate to this farther waypoint?

Comments

Answers

rewrote 

pathStep = 0.5;

to

pathStep = 1.0;

in navfn/src/navfn.cpp.

This is a solution to a similar problem I had before. At that time, the issue was that I could not set a goal 50 meters away, so in your case, you may need to increase your set to about 20.0 instead of 1.0.

ref : https://answers.ros.org/question/371846/global-plan-breaks-in-the-middle/

You may also want to consider splitting the trip into multiple trips instead of going to the goal 1km away at a time.
