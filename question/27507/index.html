Interesting, after updating Gazebo to 11.9.0 (and the Create3 sim) I am no longer seeing gzserver crashing.

Discovered a tip: If Gazebo 'save world as' dialog box does not display, - press cntl-alt(option)-dn_arrow, then cntl-alt(option)-up_arrow to display the dialog, and to redisplay the dialog after clicking any button/folder or typing chars.
