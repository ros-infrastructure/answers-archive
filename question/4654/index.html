Rviz displays information it receives from tf. When you apply a force in Gazebo, the model moves and new pose info is passed along to rviz.

It's tough to answer your question without more information. What model are you using (the urdf would be most useful), how are you sending the joint commands, what version of Gazebo and ros are you using?
