Set joint rotation range?

This question has also been migrated to Robotics StackExchange as https://robotics.stackexchange.com/questions/27226/set-joint-rotation-range.

Hi everyone, I was wondering if there is a way to specify minimum and maximum angles of rotation for a joint? I want part of my robot to be partially articulated but the specific function shouldn't rotate the full 360 degrees.

Answers

On your model's SDF file, you can specify the joint's upper and lower limits in radians.
