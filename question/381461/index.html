I am not sure what you mean by "autonomous driving" but Autoware.Auto certainly supports the LGSVL simulator https://autowarefoundation.gitlab.io/autoware.auto/AutowareAuto/lgsvl.html and currently autonomous parking https://autowarefoundation.gitlab.io/autoware.auto/AutowareAuto/avpdemo.html#avpdemo-simulation

We are in the process of adding more general autonomous-driving capabilities to the stack, and those, too, will be testable with the same simulator
