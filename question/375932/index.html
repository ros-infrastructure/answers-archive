Hi! I generally use PCL_ROS to communicate with other nodes about pointclouds. Once I have a pointcloud, I work with the PCL library directly. So to learn about filters, I would start with https://pointclouds.org/documentation/group__filters.html and try to understand the examples, to learn about communicating Pointclouds with other nodes, I would try to follow the explanation here http://wiki.ros.org/pcl_ros.
