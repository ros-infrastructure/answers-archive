Hi

I'm trying to generate metrics for packages I maintain to see how many times they've been installed from ros buildfarm deployment system through apt-get/other package managers. Is there a way to find this info from repositories.ros.org or any other links? Anyone I can contact to get these metrics?

Answers

It's a bit indirect and your package(s) may not be listed, but from ROS Metrics Report extension on ROS Discourse you can get to awstats.osuosl.org/list/packages.ros.org which is the Awstats page for packages.ros.org.

From there to this page for December, 2018 and then finally to the full list for the Downloads (Top 10) section.

ctrl+f on your package name should give you some statistics.
