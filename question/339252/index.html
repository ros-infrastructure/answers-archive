If its for the hardware, doesn't matter what so ever.

If its in simulation, it probably doesn't matter as long as you don't expect good contact dynamics (ei, you run into stuff or try to accelerate really fast). If you see a turtlebot flying away, you may want to adjust. Otherwise, I wouldn't worry about it.
