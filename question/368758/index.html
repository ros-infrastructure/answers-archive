Answers

I do not understand this completely, but as noted in this answer, call is likely spawning a subprocess that will in turn spawn another subprocess to execute your script. Now if the subscriber gets a new msg before the processing of the other script is complete, it is likely to mess things up!

I'd suggest you ditch the additional script and combine it into one node.
