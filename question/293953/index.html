I'm part of an initiative called ROS-Industrial. We have some experience with working with OEMs that are looking to provide official support for their products in ROS. What works best we've noticed is if the OEM is directly involved. Either by doing the development ..

Asked by gvdhoorn on 2018-06-12 14:30:20 UTC

.. themselves, or by cooperating closely with a company that does it for them. Writing ROS components (so called nodes) for (industrial) cameras is not that hard (though needs to be done correctly), and in the end the OEM knows their product best. Setting up customer support is also much easier ..

Asked by gvdhoorn on 2018-06-12 14:31:47 UTC

.. if you have in-house devs that have worked with/on the product you're supporting. That also tends to reduce overhead related to support related queries (less parties involved) and strengthens relationships with customers.
