As Groovy is the latest release, i would go for that one under Ubuntu 11.10 The reasons are because many tools have been upgraded or updated.

Please note that Groovy is the latest release, Fuerte is the previous one (and my second choice as it is a relatively stable and well-documented version) and electric is the oldest one you mentioned.

For further info consult this link: ROS Installation - Ubuntu 11.10
