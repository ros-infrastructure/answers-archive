I have a RGB camera (ifm3d). And I figured out that the cartographer expects 4 inputs: Range data, Odometry pose, IMU data and fixed frame pose. You can look it up here: https://google-cartographer.readthedocs.io/en/latest/

And to feed the sensor data to the cartographer, you have to use remap.
