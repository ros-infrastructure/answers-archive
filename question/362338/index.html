Video stream freezing when adding subscriber at remote PC

Problem Description

I am currently trying to send a video stream (as ROS2 topic) from a host PC to a remote PC through 4G. To do this:

  • An mp4 file is published as a ROS2 topic using the image_publisher package. The video was successfully being published at a rate of 30 Hz, and visualized in rqt_image_view
  • When a subscriber to the video-stream was initiated at the remote PC, the frequency of the topic was found to be ~1Hz
  • Moreover, the frequency of the topic at the host PC also reduced to ~1Hz when a subscriber was initiated at the remote PC.

Platform
