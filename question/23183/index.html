Microsoft has been leading the port of ROS1 and Gazebo 9 to Windows, documented here: https://aka.ms/ros .

Once Gazebo 9 works on Windows, gazebo_ros_pkgs's ROS 2 branches should be mostly ready for it too. I don't know of anyone who has tried it though.
