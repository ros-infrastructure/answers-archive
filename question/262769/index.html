Hey,

I can only answer question 1 and only about the ZED Cam: The ZED Cam gives you as output principally:

  • two stereo images
  • a depth image
  • the camera pose

(according to the ZED ROS wrapper doc)

So I see no reason why it should not be possible to use the mentioned depthimage-to-laserscan pkg.
