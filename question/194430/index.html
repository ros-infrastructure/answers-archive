How do I Install OpenCV2

This question has also been migrated to Robotics StackExchange as https://robotics.stackexchange.com/questions/63160/how-do-i-install-opencv2.

I'm trying to get imagetransport to work, but when I tried both roscreate-pkg and catkincreatekpg, I always get the error along the lines of "the dependency opencv2 is missing". I used locate for opencv2 and found opencv2 under /usr/include. How do I get it working with ROS Indigo? Do I still need visionopencv and all the other libopencv* packages like described in this page (http://wiki.ros.org/opencv2). Any help would be greatly appreciated.

Asked by K. Zeng on 2014-10-07 14:38:07 UTC

Comments

Answers

  • You have to declare opencv2 as an external dependency in your CMakeLists.txt. Similar to eigen and opencv in this example: http://wiki.ros.org/catkin/CMakeLists.txt#catkin_package.28.29
  • You will have to add opencv2 also to the target_link_libraries (in CMakeLists.txt)

Hope this helps
