You should always initialize your Eigen matrices to Identity, this will avoid nasty errors: Eigen::Matrix3d R(Eigen::Matrix3d::Identity()); (this is not the problem here). And also, use http://docs.ros.org/melodic/api/tf_conversions/html/c++/tf__eigen_8h.html to convert between tf/Eigen, again, this will avoid nasty errors when working on more complex transforms.
