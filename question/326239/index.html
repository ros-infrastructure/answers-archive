Answers

I suppose you are working in C++. With getCurrentRPY() you will get Roll, Pitch and Yaw orientations in radians.

You can get quaternion orientation with getCurrentPose().orientation.

Hope it helps

I solved it! I used " std::vector orientation = move_group.getCurrentRPY(); " to get vector 3 elements roll pitch yaw. After that convert RPY to Quaternion and set this values to target_pose.
