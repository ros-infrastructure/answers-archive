rosinstall ~/ros "http://packages.ros.org/cgi-bin/gen_rosinstall.py?rosdistro=electric&variant=desktop-full"

Answers

The list of variants for Electric are described in REP 113, and those for Diamondback in REP 108. I couldn't find a corresponding list for Fuerte.

The robot-specific variants are listed only in REP 108; the variant you are looking for is called pr2_desktop (also in Electric).
