Can I add fixed camera at the top of the world?

I have a robot simulation and I want to add the camera to the world facing down over the simulation. Can I do this without attaching the camera to anything? Thanks!

Answers

Yes, you can use a joint to the world for that. Something like this should work:

<?xml version="1.0" ?>
<sdf version="1.5">
  <world name="default">
    <include>
      <uri>model://ground_plane</uri>
    </include>
    <include>
      <uri>model://sun</uri>
    </include>
    <include>
      <pose>0 0 10 0 1.57 0</pose>
      <uri>model://camera</uri>
    </include>
    <joint name = "joint_to_world" type="fixed">
      <parent>world</parent>
      <child>camera::link</child>
    </joint>
  </world>
</sdf>
