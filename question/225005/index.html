wheel odometry and laser_scan_matcher

This question has also been migrated to Robotics StackExchange as https://robotics.stackexchange.com/questions/71575/wheel-odometry-and-laser-scan-matcher.

Hello . I am using rosarduinobridge. https://github.com/hbrobotics/ros_arduino_bridge

Can I connect directly the ouput of rosarduinobridge (odom message) to laserscanmatcher ( http://wiki.ros.org/laser_scan_matcher ) ???

Asked by osmancns on 2016-01-25 10:40:20 UTC

Comments

Answers

Assuming you have a supported motor controller and encoder interface, the ros_arduino_bridge package publishes wheel odometry data on the /odom topic. Since the laser_scan_matcher has the option of subscribing to the /odom topic to augment the laser scan matching, then the answer is "yes".
