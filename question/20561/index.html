I had a similar problem. I just bought a new acer PC and many programs were not "working properly" - Gazebo was one of those, with the black ground problem (actually, many colors seemed darker). From terminal, however, no problem was reported.

Long story short: the "problem" was that my nvidia GPU was not using the correct drivers. I had installed them, but they were not loaded properly (in my case this was due to the secure boot). Now that drivers are running colors look better.
