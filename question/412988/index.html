Asked by Davies Ogunsina on 2023-03-02 09:47:36 UTC

Solved it!

The Husky simulations need the LMS1xx support enabled. It was enough to do

echo "export HUSKY_LMS1XX_ENABLED=1;" >> ~/.bashrc
