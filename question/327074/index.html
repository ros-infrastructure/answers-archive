How can I use radar for the obstacle detection?

This question has also been migrated to Robotics StackExchange as https://robotics.stackexchange.com/questions/92218/how-can-i-use-radar-for-the-obstacle-detection.

How can I use the radar in my localcostmapparams to detect the obstacles? I am displaying data in mm in topic /radar.

Asked by EdwardNur on 2019-06-26 16:00:29 UTC

Comments

Answers

Check the costmap configuration part of this tutorial. You will need to convert the radar data into LaserScan or PointCloud and then add the sensor under the obstacle_layer in your costmap config files. Should look something like this:

point_cloud_sensor: {sensor_frame: frame_name, data_type: PointCloud, topic: topic_name, marking: true, clearing: true}
