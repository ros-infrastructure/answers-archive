I m even new to ROS and I`d start implementing ethzasl_ptam.

I`ve proceeded as the tutorial explains, but please be aware of the dependencies mentioned. On GitHub, you may find at this location ethz-asl/ethzasl_ptam/ptam a readme.txt file that accurately explain what the program needs to work; this file has been made by Klein and Murray, the authors of the original PTAM.

I had some problem with my FireWire camera that need the ROS camera1934 that make my camera works as a topic to communicate with PTAM. Anyway there s an open issue in ethasl_ptam that involves ATI graphic cards, OpenGL and Linux. I don t know how to exactly explain that problem but it won`t allow you to see image from your camera in PTAM, whether your node is correctly runned. Please find "Nor Ptam or Cameracalibrator show image from camera" topic in GitHub.
