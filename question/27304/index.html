Velodyne lidar lasers see through checkerboards

This question has also been migrated to Robotics StackExchange as https://robotics.stackexchange.com/questions/29211/velodyne-lidar-lasers-see-through-checkerboards.

I created a velodyne lidar with this tutorial: http://gazebosim.org/tutorials?tut=guided_i1 In my tests I also need checkerboards to be present, but lidar laseds just pierce then and return inf range value. Any suggestions how to fix this?

Answers

Is your Velodyne a ray sensor or a gpu_ray sensor?

If it's ray, the sensor detects objects by their collisions, so you need to add collision of the same shape as your checkerboard. If it's gpu_ray, the sensor detects objects by their visuals, and collision is not needed.
