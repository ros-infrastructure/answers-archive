libignition-physics-dev correspond to the 1.x series of ignition-physics which is currently end of life. I would recommend to use any other supported versions:

  • Latest (at the moment of writing) is 4.x (Ignition Edifice):
    • libignition-physics4-dev https://ignitionrobotics.org/api/physics/4.1/installation.html
  • Also supported are versions 2.x (Ignition Citadel) and 3.x (Ignition Dome)
