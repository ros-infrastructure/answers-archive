What distribution you should use depends on your needs. If you're just doing it for fun, then check out foxy with these tutorials.

Because this answer will age fast, the "for-fun" or "I-don't-know-what-my-needs-are" answer will probably always be the latest Long Term Support (LTS) release. That general advice probably applies to most software packages.
