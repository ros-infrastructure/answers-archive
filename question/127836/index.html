Hi, I have a question about hectorslam. This link link text say "Use in place of gmapping". In this case, I edit my launch file how say the text: "param name="odomframe" value="odom" . My question is, What do hector_slam with this topic (/odom)? In case that hector do something. Someone have a example tf.? Thanks.

Answers

The odom frame is used for providing compatibility with REP-105 (and thus, gmapping). Currently, odometry information is otherwise ignored internally by hector_mapping, as in the original application it´s use actually lead to degradation of performance (due to severe slippage of robot platforms).
