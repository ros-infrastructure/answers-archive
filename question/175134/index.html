Asked by 2ROS0 on 2014-07-03 16:36:55 UTC

In my experience there are two reasons for questions not being answered.

  1. The question is not done very well, e.g. missing basic information, formatting, structure, answer is in docs/tutorials/google, etc. The guidelines help and in this case an update is a good idea. Also when new information was discovered.

  2. No one that has seen the question knows an answer. To trigger relevant people choose a valid title and use correct tags. Ideally reuse tags that are already in use as some people might selectively only look at tags they care about.
