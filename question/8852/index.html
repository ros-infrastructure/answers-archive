How to perform a cube drop experiment?

I wish to drop a cube from a random initial pose(I'd like to constrain the position here, not the orientation) and make it fall on the ground and record the final pose, and run this over a loop a lot of times. Similarly, I'd like to add initial impulses or forces on the cube.

Asked by Ratnesh Madaan on 2015-06-02 14:10:52 UTC

Comments

Answers

You could do it with a model plugin. Set the pose with SetWorldPose, apply an impulse to a link with AddForce, then monitor the pose until it stops, register it and repeat the process again.
