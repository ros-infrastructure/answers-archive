Asked by mgruhler on 2018-04-26 04:18:17 UTC

Answers

I don't know how much you are expecting from autonomous map building, it is an active field of research but there are several packages for this purpose, You can use frontier_exploration or rrt_exploration but you need to configure your robot in order to get this exploration packages working, for example properly configured navigation stack that can accept action goals to move_base needed by frontier. You can find requirement details on their wiki packages. Hope this helps
