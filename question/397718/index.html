use the linear acceleration data from imu for the position

I assume you mean integrating just the acceleration readings to obtain the position estimate? Acceleration readings are usually very noisy and will alone not be sufficient; you will notice a lot of drift in the position estimates even after filtering and removing the bias in the accelerometer readings.

You need to fuse the encoder/lidar readings with the accelerometer readings.
