Answers

RVIZ markers specify their frame_id in the message, along with their pose. So if you specify a point at (0, 0, 0) then it will be shown at the origin of the given pose defined within the coordinate system of ''

I usually set the pose to identity to keep things simple, and calculate the marker points directly within the specified frame, but that isn't always appropriate.
