To fix your "undefined reference" compile errors, add "bondcpp" in your find_package list in CMakeLists.txt. eg: 

...
find_package(catkin REQUIRED COMPONENTS
  geometry_msgs
  roscpp
  std_msgs
  bondcpp
)
...
