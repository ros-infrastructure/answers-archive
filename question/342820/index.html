Asked by VivosLespagnos on 2020-01-29 09:27:10 UTC

My comment was not meant as a full answer (hence, it was a comment).

The issue was that you were creating a Publisher in a callback. That will not work.

You'll need to make sure to initialise your publishers and subscribers in main(..) and then in your callback you use the publisher you created earlier to publish the result.

i thought it was impossible to declare two node in the same main.

you mean NodeHandles? You don't need two NodeHandles. One is sufficient. But you can create as many NodeHandle instances as you'd want/need.

The reason you don't see any output is that you're not publishing.

You'll need to fix that.
