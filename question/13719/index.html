I am writing here instead of reporting a Gazebo issue because I am not sure if I am doing something wrong.

REP117 states that: "Detections that are too close to the sensor to quantify shall be represented by -Inf. Erroneous detections shall be represented by quiet (non-signaling) NaNs. However, if I simply insert a Hokuyo laser and place and object too close, we see that the measurements below minimum range are incorrect (they go within the obstacle) and ranges with maxrange are reported as maxrange:

Furthermore, using the gazeboros plugin libgazeboroslaser.so I can see that it is reported exactly the same as in the topic visualization (wrong minrange instead of -inf and max_range instead of inf).

Is this a bug or I am doing something wrong?
