Hello,

I don't know what's your particular interest: ROS or ROS 2. There are some interesting books:

  • Programming Robots with ROS: A Practical Introduction to the Robot Operating System Novel by Brian Gerkey, Morgan L. Quigley, and William D. Smart.
  • ROS Robotics Projects Book by Lentin Joseph
  • ROS Robotics By Example Book by Carol Fairchild and Thomas Harman

For ROS 2 you may want to review the official documentation:

  • https://index.ros.org/doc/ros2/Tutorials/

You can easily find more book typing in Google: "book ROS".
