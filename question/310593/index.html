x1, y1, a1, x2, y2, a2, X1, Y1, A1 are all known, but I am not sure how to calculate X2, Y2, A2. And I do not think the fomular below are correct:

X2 = X1 - x1 + x2

A2 = A1 - a1 + a2

Can anyone help?
