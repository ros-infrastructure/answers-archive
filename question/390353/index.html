I don't have experience with Yocto, but it seems that your ROS 2 installation is not sourced. Or, in your context, probably some paths are not configured (CMake should be able to find the ROS 2 installation). For example, in my Desktop installation, the ament_cmake files are located:

/opt/ros/foxy$ find -name *.cmake | grep -i '/ament_cmake/' ./share/ament_cmake/cmake/ament_cmake_export_dependencies-extras.cmake ./share/ament_cmake/cmake/ament_cmakeConfig.cmake ./share/ament_cmake/cmake/ament_cmakeConfig-version.cmake
