Are you sure you are running ROS Kinetic? What is the version of your OS?

Additionally (but unrelated): the joint_state_publisher is not for controlling robots. It's for publishing fake JointState messages. As the name implies, it publishes state, it is not meant to control.

Asked by Anubhav Singh on 2020-05-14 07:35:47 UTC

As you can see in your screenshot and in the output of rostopic info, you have multiple publishers to /ow5/joint_states. That is probably going to conflict.

I believe this relates to my earlier comment, where I wrote the JointStatePublisher is not for controlling a robot. By including the JSP in your node graph you're essentially publishing fake (and conflicting) JointState messages, which are both consumed by the robot_state_publisher (and RViz).

There is a chance this is the cause of your problem.
