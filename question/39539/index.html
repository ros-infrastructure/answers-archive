Just checked, comp_ros does not exist any more. We have moved the functionality into other packages such as [[comp_cop]] for reading perception results or [[tf_prolog]] for reading coordinate transformations from tf. The main reason was that the ROS access was only a minor part of the respective functionality, while the core part was what to do with the information obtained from ROS (which can, obviously, be of very different kind).
