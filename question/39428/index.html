Answers

1) I suggest running "sudo /usr/sbin/turtlebot-start" and checking out the errors. That's what "sudo service turtlebot start" actually runs.

2) What does running "rostopic bw /camera" say? You could try using the compressed image topic instead - use a republish node on your local machine to subscribe to the compressed topic and uncompress it on your local machine.
