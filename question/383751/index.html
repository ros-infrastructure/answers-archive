But a quick glance through autonomy_bus.py shows you're setting up subscribers for every iteration of while not rospy.is_shutdown(). That's not correct.

Subscribers should be created only once, typically in __init__, and the callbacks _communication_ops_callback() and _mission_mgmt_callback() will then get called every time something arrives on the subscribed topics.
