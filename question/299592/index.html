But if the one-shot timer duration is less than the time-diff change on the linux system, then the one-shot timer will blocked at the timer.stop() call function and it always return true of the timer.hasPending() function, meanwhile it also block the mavros component. I checked the sourcecode of timer.cpp and timermanager.cpp, seems it block at the mutex(waitingmutex_), but I have no way to debug it due to I do not know how to change this sourcecode. As we know that, a timesync event of change linux time is a normally operation, seems such one-shot timer issue is too dangerous since it blocked the whole mavros process. Is anyone could help such issue? Many thanks forward.
