Quick comment: if you're building ROS from source, you can create your workspace wherever you want. There are no assumptions about the workspace being in any particular location.

If you're not building from source: it's likely/possible at least some packages have /opt/ros/$ROS_DISTRO hard-coded as the path of the ROS installation. That's not something easily changed.
