Sorry, I found the bug. See line 11 and 12 of the obstacle_detector.py:

    self.sub = rospy.Subscriber('/scan', LaserScan, self.scan, queue_size=1)
    self.pub = rospy.Publisher('obstacle', Obstacle, self.scan, queue_size =10)
