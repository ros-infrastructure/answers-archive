Answers

Check out Pgs. 17-20 of our TurtleBot hardware manual. It assumes that you've got the AC power adapter for your Kinect (which IIRC is optional). If you've got the adapter, you can modify it in a way where you can put it back together (shown in the manual). If you don't have the adapter, you'll need to get one.
