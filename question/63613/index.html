Answers

I think that your kinect_laser is publishing on topic /scan but move_base and gmapping expect it to be on topic /base_scan. So first undo your change on costmap_common.yaml, and add this remap on your <!-- Fake laser --> node:

<remap from="scan" to="/base_scan"/>

after the

<remap from="cloud" to="/cloud_throttled"/>

Then type 

rostopic info /scan

and

rostopic info /base_scan

to check that everybody is subscribed to /base_scan

Update Yeap, you must remap everything to /scan or to /base_scan. Let's use /scan:

  • Remove the on Fake laser
  • Rename base_scan as scan on costmap_common.yaml
  • Add in the explore launch file

/base_scan topic should disapear, and /scan should have 1 publisher and 3 subscribers
