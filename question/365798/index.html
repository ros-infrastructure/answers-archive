Maintain mobile robot center of gravity with heavy arm controlled by MoveIt

I've developed a mobile robot base and am planning on constructing an articulated arm that I intend to use MoveIt for path planning. My concern is that the arm I am building can conceivably put the center of gravity of the robot past the wheels resulting in the robot falling over. I'm not sure how to prevent this from happening and I haven't found anyone trying to solve such problem. Ideally, MoveIt would have center of gravity calculations/physics built-in, but it doesn't appear that it does (not surprisingly.. seems pretty complicated to do).

Besides redesigning the robot base for more stability, I see a couple possible means to solve this problem, but not sure if they are feasible and seek advice and whether I'm heading down a rabbit hole (been down plenty already building the robot base).

  1. Add constraints to the joints such the arm cannot get in a position that would cause the robot to fall. The problem with this is that the joint constraints will be based upon other joints (i.e., cannot extend elbow straight out when shoulder joint is straight out). To me this would be rather complex to put into MoveIt and I don't know if you can have such constraints (without perhaps adding a user-defined constraint that does it all).

  2. Add position limits such that some or all links have to stay within a predefined area. To me, this is likely the simplest solution and probably the safest, but perhaps the least flexible as it may be ok for a elbow joint to be outside a boundary if the hand joint is within the boundary, and vice versa, but not both. Again, this might need to be a user-defined constraint.

If there are other solutions or ideas, I would greatly appreciate the feedback.
