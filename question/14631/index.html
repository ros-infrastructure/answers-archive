Help getting vehicles to locomote

Maybe I'm doing something stupid, but I've build two 4-wheel vehicles and can't get them to move forward/backward properly. I've tried everything I can think of, from changing model weight to friction to different kinds of plugin actuation and nothing works right. They try to move, but it's as if they're in a tar-pit or on a glass floor (stuck or no traction). The driven wheels move, but the vehicle has problems trying to move.

cart3 is a 1m long cart derived from gazebomodels/cartfrontsteer. It will move forward, though when starting the front wheels initially move slower than the vehicle speed, but they catch up. However, it won't go backward - the rear (driven) wheels just spin and the front (steered) wheels don't roll and get dragged backward slowly. Unfortunately there was no ROS plugin to drive it & make it steer, so I modified gazeborostricycledrive plugin to operate the rear wheels and steer the front wheels. The front wheels were independent so I linked them with a steering linkage. Instead of the universal joint in cartfrontsteer it has two revolute joints in series.

The other is a 30 ton truck with suspension arms. It almost won't go forward or backward - the rear wheels break loose and spin, while the front wheels stay almost stationary. If I apply low force I can push the front wheels forward, and they'll rotate, but only slowly, and the cart will only move slowly. The rear wheels are driven with Joint->SetForce by the plugin, and the front wheels are just revolute joints with no plugins attached.

I've put the models and the plugin code that turns their joints in my github repo at https://github.com/PaulBouchier/truck The source code will only build in the gazeborospkgs/gazebo_plugins package, which I downloaded as source and added my plugins into, but hopefully you can see what's going on. The wheel spin joint axes look correct but the joints act as if they've got very high friction, comparable to ground friction.

I'm at the end of my wits on this, and would very much appreciate any suggestions.

Thanks

Paul

Answering my own question:

The solution was to use Joint->SetVelocity on the joint to the driven wheel and gradually ramp it up & down.

It seems that using Joint->SetForce on a joint to drive a wheel and thereby move the vehicle is not the right technique to use. I don't know why - maybe because the torque acts with zero radius, so doesn't result in a force applied by the wheel to the ground. The result seemed to be that torquing the rear (driven) wheels would attempt to lift the front of the vehicle, and with enough force, would spin the wheels, but that didn't produce thrust on the vehicle.
