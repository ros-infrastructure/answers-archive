This is not ROS related and documented a few times in the web, but I will try it in a few sentences. Lets say you have a motor like this one. With full speed at 170RPM a gear ratio of 1:30 and 360 ticks per turn gives you 170/60*30*360=30600 encoder ticks per second, or one every 32µs. (TODO: check math when awake again). Your ARM µC runs multiple threads and the scheduler decides when a thread gets CPU time, with a GHz CPU and Linux this might be a few 1000 times a second so you can get a task to read a GPIO every few ms and thats not guaranteed and still for from µs range.
