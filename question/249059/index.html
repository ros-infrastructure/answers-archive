Take a look at In ros gmapping, how to use Intel Research Lab dataset? for a related question.

You don't need to create a bag per se (an alternative would be writing a node that reads in the csvs, creates appropriate messages and publishes those), but it's probably a very good idea, as it lets you reuse a bunch of tools that either work directly with bags or by relying on rosbag play .. etc.
