If I'm lucky, it will eventually succeed open-loop, but this of course takes a long time.

The poses I'm using in set_place_area look reasonable in rviz - they're within reach, and there's plenty of open space for the arm to move through. The collision map doesn't show any spurious obstacles that could be causing problems.

How robust is the arm planning to the distance the place pose is from the surface of the table? Any suggestions on how I can make placing more reliable?
