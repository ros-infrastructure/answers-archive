This question has also been migrated to Robotics StackExchange as https://robotics.stackexchange.com/questions/27056/how-to-calculate-pose-of-a-component-quickly.

Answers

The tag is a space separated 6-tuple: x y z roll pitch yaw.

For example, a pose of x=1 y=2 z=3 roll=0 pitch=45degrees and yaw=90degrees:

<pose>1 2 3 0 0.785 1.571</pose>
