Answers

The problem could be that the car starts in the middle of the terrain. Gazebo will then correct for this by pushing the car up.

Could you try the following:

  1. Pause the simulator.
  2. Insert your car.
  3. Move your car so that it starts a bit above the heightmap.
  4. unpause the simulator.
