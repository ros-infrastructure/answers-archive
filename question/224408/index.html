hector_slam uses LIDAR data for SLAM. One can emulate LIDAR data with a RGB-D or stereo camera, but the results will be inferior to those with a proper LIDAR as the field of view is much smaller and noise much higher. See also this Q/A. Interestingly, the results presented here however look quite nice.
