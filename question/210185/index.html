Answers

The Ping command and other utilities are no longer released with the Prosilica driver due to licensing issues. See: https://github.com/ros-drivers/prosilica_driver/issues/4

You'll have to download the PvAPI binaries directly from AVT if you'd like to use the Ping program; or just skip that step in the setup tutorial.
