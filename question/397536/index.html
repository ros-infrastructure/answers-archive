Why does rclcpp have a make_shared function?

Was the idea here that at some point rclcpp might want to transition to `boost::makeshared? The stlmake_shared` was added to the stl in C++11.

There are also the SharedPtr and similar using statements that also seem odd to me. If the intention would be that you could more easily port rclcpp to some system without the stl where are the abstractions for the rest of the stl?
