Also asked here. https://github.com/ros-visualization/rviz/issues/1534 Turns out that mesh links can not be relative. Must be either "package:// or "file:/// (note three / ) I was just placing the CAD files in the same folder as the urdf. The error messages make NO mention of the files not being found. They say the "File came out empty" or "could not be loaded" but never mention the fact that the reason is they weren't found.
