Where is /tf messages generated in DRCSIM?

This question has also been migrated to Robotics StackExchange as https://robotics.stackexchange.com/questions/26086/where-is-tf-messages-generated-in-drcsim.

When I do 

roslaunch atlas_utils atlas.launch

and 

rostopic list

I see /tf messages. Can someone point me to where in gazebo land (source code) is tf messages generated for atlas? Many thanks!

Asked by sfeng on 2013-03-21 11:21:01 UTC

Comments

Answers

tf is generated outside gazebo using the robot_state_publisher(s) (One for the robot, one for the multisense head). Gazebo publishes joint states of the robot. The robot_state_publisher nodes receive those, generate the tf transforms based on them and publish them to tf. By running

rxgraph

You can look at the computation graph and see the topic connections between gazebo, robot_state_publisher and all other nodes.

See also the robot_state_publisher wiki page.
