Bouncy is really old and also EOL (for quite some time now), see: REP 2000: ROS 2 Releases and Target Platforms.

The likely cause of your problem is that rosbag2 just wasn't around when Bouncy was released.

If you don't have an absolute need to install Bouncy, I would recommend you upgrade to at least Eloquent, which can also still be installed on 18.04.
