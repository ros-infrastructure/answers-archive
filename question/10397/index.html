Answers

My T410 Thinkpad has an optimus card. In order to get the NV drivers working under Ubuntu 10.10 I had to disable the Intel graphics in BIOS, after which everything worked fine (unfortunately, you don't get the battery savings that optmius integrated graphics would offer). I've had no problems with rviz + kinect point clouds on my laptop.

1 thing to note - you need to play around with the color parameter in optirun (rgb, yuv, proxy etc) to see what gives you best performance
