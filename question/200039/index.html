A RGB stream registered to the depth stream is required. I’m pretty sure that the Structure sensor can register the depth image with the RGB image from the camera of the iPad.

You may want to look at the Asus Xtion live Pro for a more compact design of the Kinect. The Asus is powered by USB too!
