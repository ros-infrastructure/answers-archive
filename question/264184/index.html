rosbag play from time1 end time2

This question has also been migrated to Robotics StackExchange as https://robotics.stackexchange.com/questions/81350/rosbag-play-from-time1-end-time2.

Hi, guys: i just wander is there a command in rosbag play, could let us play a bag from time1 to time2? BTW, i did not find such command in wiki: http://wiki.ros.org/rosbag/Commandline#play

Answers

You can use the --start and --duration options to start playback at a specific offset into a bag file, and to play for a specific period of time. This isn't quite the same as specifying absolute start and end timestamps, but with a bit of math you can use it to do the same thing.
