This question has also been migrated to Robotics StackExchange as https://robotics.stackexchange.com/questions/95065/how-important-it-is-to-sync-the-timing-for-gnss-and-lidar-in-localization.

I have not synced the timing for gnss and lidar yet. Will it affect the localization accuracy a lot? I found gnsspose is published based on the GPS time and ndtpose is published based on the current timing. Why are they different?

Answers

Of course, GNSS and LiDAR should be time synchronized.

However, since Autoware Localization does not perform processing that requires strict time synchronization between GNSS and LiDAR, I think that even if the timing is not synchronized, it will not affect the position estimation much.
