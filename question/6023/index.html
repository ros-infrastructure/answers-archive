This is probably caused because of wheel slip. The command velocities sent by your keyboard driving could be higher than those used by the navigation stack. The wheels would slip and in turn the robot would think it's in a different location than it actually is. You'll notice that the robot pose in rviz starts to diverge from the pose in gazebo.

Try driving the robot at using a lower velocity.
