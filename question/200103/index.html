relax a subset of PR2 controllers

This question has also been migrated to Robotics StackExchange as https://robotics.stackexchange.com/questions/64868/relax-a-subset-of-pr2-controllers.

After bringing up a PR2 robot, the controllers are all fixed. I wonder is there a way that I can relax one arm and have other controllers still fixed. Anyone who knows how to do this? Thanks a lot!

Asked by guodi on 2014-12-26 18:43:13 UTC

Comments

Answers

You can stop arm controllers using:

rosrun pr2_controller_manager pr2_controler_manager stop l_arm_controller

A list of PR2's controller names can be show by

rosrun pr2_controller_manager pr2_controller_manager list

You can see how to use the controller manager by

rosrun pr2_controller_manager pr2_controller_manager

Cheers.
