How does ROS2 handle several routes to the same destination

Basically our problem is having ROS2 communication over 2 routes that reach the same destination.

For instance our publisher is 192.168.10.11 & 10.10.10.11 our subscriber is 10.10.10.10 and our router is 192.168.10.12 & 10.10.10.12. We configured our publisher and subscriber's machine to make a bridge via the router. We can ping each other via that router.

Therefore from the publisher we can reach the subscriber directly (same subnet) OR we can pass through 10.10.10.12 (i.e the router) via 192.168.10.12 to reach 10.10.10.10. How can we publish to those 2 links in the same time ?
