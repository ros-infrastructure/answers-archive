I tried to change "movearmsimpleposegoal.cpp" http://www.ros.org/wiki/move_arm/Tutorials/MoveArmPoseGoal

I wanted to specify pos and orientation and changed desired_pose.pose.orientation.w = 1.0 -> 0.5 (line31)

Then there's error message

[ERROR] [1311149512.607969220, 1281.101000000]: Unable to transform pose constraint on link 'rwristrolllink' to frame 'odomcombined' [ERROR] [1311149512.608135351, 1281.101000000]: Could not prepare planning environment

How can I fix it or change rwristroll_joint's orientation?

Answers

You need to provide a proper quaternion. Just changing one value will obviously leave the quaternion unnormalized.

There are some convenience functions that create quaternions from roll,pitch,yaw depending on what you need.
