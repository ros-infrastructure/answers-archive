Comments

You may want to adjust the collision and contact parameters for ODE (Physics Engine used in Gazebo). Particularly parameters mu, mu2, kp and kd. A value of 1000 for mu and mu2 is huge and a min_depth generally speaking is not suitable for most Robot models.

As you may have noticed I have not included a world link on my URDF. Do you think this is relevant to my issue?

Asked by John999991 on 2020-03-05 06:21:48 UTC

I do not think that is an issue. The "world" frame should be out of the robot description. All links provided in the URDF shall be own by the Robot model itself. The world link is a frame out of the scope of the robot description. The basic URDF tree would be base_footprint->base_link->robot_parts...
