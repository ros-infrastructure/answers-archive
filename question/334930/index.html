Answers

The problem of merging maps between robots is a slam problem. There are different solutions to do slam in ROS. (Amongst others gmapping, hector slam and cartographer)

One solution that is known to support multiple agents is cartographer: https://google-cartographer-ros.readthedocs.io/en/latest/going_further.html#multi-trajectories-slam
