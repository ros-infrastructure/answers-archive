Quick comment: catkin_make does not exist in ROS 2. You can't install it using apt, but even if you could, you can't really use it to build ROS 2 packages.

You may want to take a step back and check why you believe you want/need catkin_make.

"Creating a workspace" is just a matter of creating a directory. It seems that works.
