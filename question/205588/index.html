Answers

The problem comes from the WorldToROSTF element:

<WorldToROSTF>
  <rootName> world </rootName>
  <enableObjects> 0 </enableObjects>
  <rate>10</rate>
</WorldToROSTF>

After this is removed, the simulation works fine.
