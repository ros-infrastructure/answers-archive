Asked by zadorpataki on 2021-10-02 02:50:01 UTC

Problems like this have nothing to do with what's in the .bashrc, the workspace, the .ros directory or anything in $HOME.

apt manages a system-wide database, and that database tells it something is already installed which is incompatible with what the ROS .deb packages need. That's why apt refuses to install Noetic.

The typical way to diagnose this is using aptitude (or apt with the appropriate CLAs) and then drilling down until it's clear which package specifically is being held back, or is pinned, or is a requirement of something else (and therefore cannot be down or upgraded, or removed).

aptitude is not a magic tool. It will still need someone to understand what it shows you.
