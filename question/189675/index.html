Adding one more g500 in uwsim?

This question has also been migrated to Robotics StackExchange as https://robotics.stackexchange.com/questions/61672/adding-one-more-g500-in-uwsim.

How to add 1 or more robots in uwsim ? Can we simply do it by editing cirs.xml ?

Asked by Mind_hunter on 2014-08-11 06:35:58 UTC

Comments

Answers

Yes, you can take the scene 'shipwreck.xml' scene as an example: rosrun uwsim uwsim --configfile shipwreck.xml
