The launch is failing on c2 because your environment loader is not setting your ROS_PACKAGE_PATH up properly on c2.

Please see: http://pr2support.willowgarage.com/wiki/Tutorials/Setting%20up%20the%20ROS_ENV_LOADER

Asked by HanXiao on 2013-08-05 10:46:40 UTC

c1 and c2 have the same filesystem; anything you do on one machine is also on the other. Your ROS environment variables are not automatically transferred from one to the other when you do a roslaunch; this is why you must set up an environment-loader.

Asked by ahendrix on 2013-08-05 11:45:32 UTC

I STRONGLY recommend running move_base on c2, because the laser drivers are on c2, and it will significantly decrease the amount of traffic and latency between servers if move_base and the laser drivers run on the same machine.

You can specify which machine a node runs on by using the machine="" property of the tag in your launch file. You do not need to copy anything from c1 to c2.
