I looked into this for a bit and couldn't find a way to add a net-like material, similar to a soccer goal. The goal is to have a material you could see on the other side of the material, but it would partially obstruct vision (like a soccer goal net). Does anyone know of an example of this in Gazebo? Would a half-transparent material work in your case? Just make a block and set the visual material to Gazebo/RedTransparent to see how it looks like. It obviously wouldn't look like a soccer goal net but it would satisfy the see-through and partial obstruction criteria.
