After elevation_mapping transforms clouds to your specified sensor_frame_id, it applies a cleaning step where you could theoretically do any filtering you want. I'm still doing this in my own node because I want it on a separate thread. Threading elevation_mapping is another option to consider.

Asked by mogumbo on 2018-11-15 17:26:23 UTC

After cleaning, elevation_mapping computes variances using your choice of SensorProcessor class. This might be a problem for advanced applications that want to compute variances differently each sensor type. I believe you can only choose one. I might investigate further in the future.
