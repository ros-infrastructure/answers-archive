This question has also been migrated to Robotics StackExchange as https://robotics.stackexchange.com/questions/25491/gpuraysensor-hh-missing-in-1-3.

Looks like GpuRaySensor.hh went missing in Gazebo 1.3. I checked out the lastest source from the repo:
