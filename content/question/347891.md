  body_markdown: "Hi, <br/><br/>\nI am not sure if you only have this node. My assumption\
    \ is that you probably have some misconceptions about *frame* in ROS.\n\nPublishing\
    \ a `visualization_msgs/Marker`\_message does not create a frame. In fact, you\
    \ don't create individual frames in ROS. You define relationship between coordinate\
    \ frames by broadcasting a transform to `tf` or `tf2`.\n\n>  it keeps sending\
    \ me a warning saying that the frame doesn't exist.\n\nSo it literally means that\
    \ frame does not exist in the `tf`. When you use `lookupTransform()` , `target_frame`\
    \ and `source_frame` must both\_already exist\_in the\_`tf`\_tree. \n\nI suggest\
    \ you look through [Writing a tf2 broadcaster](http://wiki.ros.org/tf2/Tutorials/Writing%20a%20tf2%20broadcaster%20%28C%2B%2B%29)\
    \ and [Writing a tf2 listener](http://wiki.ros.org/tf2/Tutorials/Writing%20a%20tf2%20listener%20%28C%2B%2B%29)\
    \ to see how to write a proper tf broadcaster/listener."
