    Hi @sinou00,

In that post will find a Gazebo [system plugin](http://gazebosim.org/tutorials?cat=&tut=system_plugin&ver=3.0-4.0) that will allow you to get the position clicked in Gazebo world.

    Furthermore, you can enhance the plugin by reading all mouse events and storing the positions in a list that can be published by a ROS topic that would be used by your control node.
