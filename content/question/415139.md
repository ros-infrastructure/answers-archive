    About the setuptools deprecation you can find a quick workaround [here](https://answers.ros.org/question/396439/setuptoolsdeprecationwarning-setuppy-install-is-deprecated-use-build-and-pip-and-other-standards-based-tools/). Basically you need to downgrade it.
