    If you already have created a map then you don't have to use `gmapping` anymore. `gmapping` is for SLAM : Simultaneous Localization and **Mapping** so you just need to localize the robot in your map.

    What exactly have you run to get this result ? I assume you've just launched `map_server`to get your map and then `gmapping` ? Instead take a look at the file [navigation.launch](https://github.com/gaitech-robotics/RIA-E100/blob/master/e100_navigation/launch/navigation.launch) from your package, they launch the `map_server` node and then they use the package `AMCL` for the localization.
