  body_markdown: "Yes I have had the same problem.\n\nIt is the libcwiid that comes\
    \ with ROS Electric that is the problem. You can solve it be doing the following:\n\
    \ninstall libcwiid1 and libcwiid-dev with synaptic or apt.\n\ngo to\n\n/opt/ros/electric/stacks/joystick_drivers/cwiid/cwiid/lib/\n\
    \nremove or rename\n\nlibcwiid.so\nlibcwiid.so.1\nlibcwiid.so.1.0\n\nCreate the\
    \ following symbolic links with \u201Csudo ln -s\u201D\n\nlibcwiid.so -> /usr/lib/libcwiid.so\n\
    libcwiid.so.1 -> /usr/lib/libcwiid.so.1\nlibcwiid.so.1.0 -> /usr/lib/libcwiid.so.1.0\n\
    \nexample: sudo ln -s libcwiid.so /usr/lib/libcwiid.so\n\nThis will solve your\
    \ problem."
