    Yes, this was changed, in 2013 already actually. From the [CHANGELOG.rst](https://github.com/ros/ros_comm/blob/52b0556dadf3ec0c0bc72df4fc202153a53b539e/tools/roslaunch/CHANGELOG.rst#1950-2013-10-04):

    > add option to disable terminal title setting

    the option referred to would be `--disable-title`:

        $ roslaunch --help
        ...
          --disable-title       Disable setting of terminal title
        ...

    This was added by [ros/ros_comm#288](https://github.com/ros/ros_comm/pull/288).
