    \nSUBSYSTEM==\"usb\", ATTR{idVendor}==\"045e\", ATTR{idProduct}==\"02c4\", MODE=\"\
    0666\"\nSUBSYSTEM==\"usb\", ATTR{idVendor}==\"045e\", ATTR{idProduct}==\"02d8\"\

    02d9\", MODE=\"0666\" \n\nand save it in `/etc/udev/rules.d`. But do lsusb and\
    \ make sure the id of your kinect corresponds to the ones above otherwise substitute\
