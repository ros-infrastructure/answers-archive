  body_markdown: "Assuming the x and y axes of your IMU are aligned with your robot\
    \ and you want the x and y velocities with respect to the robot:\n\n    delta_t\
    \ = time between accelerometer readings\n    \n    loop:\n    velocity_x += (accel_x\
    \ * delta_t) \n    velocity_y += (accel_y * delta_t)"
