New users start with 1 karma point, which means that they can't post comments right away. This leads to "comment answers", because new users have no other choice. We have this issue already over at ROS Answers (see [here](http://answers.ros.org/question/54550/reduce-karma-required-for-commenting/)) and it is starting [here](http://answers.gazebosim.org/question/1474/weird-texture-mapping-in-collada-meshes-130/) as well.

I also found out that on AskBot's Q&A this limit is set to 1 (see [here](http://askbot.org/en/faq/)).

Hence, I suggest we lower it to 1 as well.
