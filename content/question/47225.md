    While you created a fluent object you didn't bind it to a variable. Try:

        (defvar fl (make-fluent :name 'fl :value 1))

    Have a look at [this tutorial](http://ros.org/wiki/cram_core/Tutorials/ControllingTurtlesimFromLisp) for a more sophisticated example that uses fluents.
