  body_markdown: "alas, Int32 =/= Int32[] \n\nYou'll need to define your publisher\
    \ to publish arrays (in your case, you can use [Int32MultiArray.msg](https://github.com/ros2/common_interfaces/blob/master/std_msgs/msg/Int32MultiArray.msg)).\n\
    \nIn python, (without knowing the contents of `self.create_publisher()`) that\
    \ could look something like:\n\n    from std_msgs.msg import Int32MultiArray\n\
    \    \n    ...\n    \n    self.publisher = self.create_publisher(\n        Int32MultiArray,\n\
    \        'wheel_settings',\n        10)\n    ...\n    \n    msg = Int32MultiArray()\n\
    \    msg.data = [1, 1, 1]\n    pub.publish(msg)"
