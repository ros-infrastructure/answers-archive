- body_markdown: |-
    Have you sourced your ROS install and the workspace where the `baxter_description` package is? Are you keeping the backticks in the command? ``rosrun xacro xacro.py --inorder `rospack find baxter_description`/urdf/baxter.urdf.xacro`` is not the same as``rosrun xacro xacro.py --inorder rospack find baxter_description/urdf/baxter.urdf.xacro``

    EDIT: also xacro.py is deprecated and inorder is default, use the following command instead: ``rosrun xacro xacro `rospack find baxter_description`/urdf/baxter.urdf.xacro``
