  body_markdown: "In general messages do not have methods on them. It's recommended\
    \ if you want to do operations on the objects to use a linear math library like\
    \ bullet or eigen. You'll find that there are packages which provide automatic\
    \ conversions such at http://wiki.ros.org/tf2_eigen and http://wiki.ros.org/tf2_bullet\n\
    \nThe geometry_msgs datatypes also don't provide the operations on objects so\
    \ if you're going to apply the transform you need to convert it anyway. \n\nAs\
    \ an aside I would also caution against premature optimization. If you're truely\
    \ using the exact same transform at the exact same time and it will never change\
    \ the inverse transform is truely correct. However if you're potentially dealing\
    \ with different timestamps or different frames it may be worth keeping the generality\
    \ of just looking up the coordinate frames. It's not a very expensive query. Also\
    \ you can greatly simplify your interface by directly applying the `.transform(...)`\
    \ family of methods instead of computing the transform and then manually applying\
    \ it: http://docs.ros.org/en/noetic/api/tf2_ros/html/c++/classtf2__ros_1_1BufferInterface.html#a667f0a36e15ee8b1fd50c871a70d35d5"
