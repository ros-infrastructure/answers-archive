    The project's [API Samples page](https://code.google.com/p/tesseract-ocr/wiki/APIExample) gives the proper command-line arguments for building and linking against the tesseract_ocr library: "`g++ -o myprogram myprogram.cpp -llept -ltesseract`"

    In cmake, you can link against libraries using `target_link_libraries()`; cmake will automatically add the `-l` flag for you, so all you have to do is:

    `target_link_libraries(my_tesseract_node lept tesseract)`
