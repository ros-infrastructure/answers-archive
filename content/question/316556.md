    I'd recommend reading the [Callbacks and Spinning page](http://wiki.ros.org/roscpp/Overview/Callbacks%20and%20Spinning) page. Since it sounds like you are calling `ros::spin()`, then all callbacks get put into a callback queue in the order in which they are received. Then every call to `ros::spin()` will handle the callback at the front of the queue. So the answer to your question depends on the order in which the service request and the topic were received.
