    frontier_exploration claims to work with or without the `/map` topic available. RTABMAP can publish a 2D map that could be used by frontier_exploration (the [Turtlebot demo](http://wiki.ros.org/rtabmap_ros/Tutorials/MappingAndNavigationOnTurtlebot) does this). Have a look at the [frontier_exploration docs, section 2.2](http://wiki.ros.org/frontier_exploration#Exploration_with_.2BAC8-map_data), and the [Turtlebot demo for RTABMAP](http://wiki.ros.org/rtabmap_ros/Tutorials/MappingAndNavigationOnTurtlebot).
