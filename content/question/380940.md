  body_markdown: "In the [navigation tutorial](http://wiki.ros.org/navigation/Tutorials/RobotSetup#Creating_a_Launch_File_for_the_Navigation_Stack),\
    \ notice that `costmap_common_params.yaml` is loaded twice but in different namespaces:\n\
    \n    <rosparam file=\"$(find my_robot_name_2dnav)/costmap_common_params.yaml\"\
    \ command=\"load\" ns=\"global_costmap\" /> \n    <rosparam file=\"$(find my_robot_name_2dnav)/costmap_common_params.yaml\"\
    \ command=\"load\" ns=\"local_costmap\" />\n\nIn your case, you load the common\
    \ parameters once without a namespace, so the parameters aren't where the two\
    \ costmaps are looking for their parameters.  You can check the parameter definitions\
    \ with:\n\n    rosparam list\n\nWith a correct setup, you should see the common\
    \ parameters listed under each costmap's namespace, like:\n\n    ...\n    /move_base/global_costmap/obstacles_layer/scan_1/clearing\n\
    \    /move_base/global_costmap/obstacles_layer/scan_1/data_type\n    ...\n   \
    \ /move_base/local_costmap/obstacles_layer/scan_1/clearing\n    /move_base/local_costmap/obstacles_layer/scan_1/data_type\n\
    \    ..."
