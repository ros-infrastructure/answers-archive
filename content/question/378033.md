  body_markdown: "Presumably the [`pointcloud_to_laserscan`](https://github.com/ros-perception/pointcloud_to_laserscan/blob/foxy/README.md)\
    \ package would do what you're looking for. As long as your laser scan data is\
    \ being published to a frame that has the current orientation of your 2D lidar,\
    \ it should map to the appropriate 3D coordinates. \n\nImportant topics to understand\
    \ will be [transforms and coordinate frames in ROS](https://www.ros.org/reps/rep-0105.html),\
    \ and [`robot_state_publisher`](https://docs.ros.org/en/foxy/Tutorials/URDF/Using-URDF-with-Robot-State-Publisher.html)\
    \ will likely also be your friend here."
