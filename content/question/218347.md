  body_markdown: "The three primary coordinate frames are described [here](http://www.ros.org/reps/rep-0105.html).\
    \ I haven't looked too deeply, but from a cursory glance at your launch file,\
    \ this looks fine.\n\nIn addition to those frames, when you use `navsat_transform_node`,\
    \ you get a transform (in your case) from *map*->*utm*. If you want to issue a\
    \ goal in the global frame, you need to first convert your lat/lon into UTM coordinates\
    \ (there's a header in robot_localization that exposes this functionality), and\
    \ then use the *map*->*utm* transform to transform that UTM position into your\
    \ robot's world frame. You can then send that goal to your planner.\n\n "
