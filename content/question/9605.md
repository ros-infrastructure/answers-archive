    Having fiducial markers on the test images lets one recover pose from those test images. We can use these pose estimates as ground truth to compare the performance of an object & pose detector. See also [http://opencv.willowgarage.com/wiki/SolutionsInPerceptionChallenge](http://opencv.willowgarage.com/wiki/SolutionsInPerceptionChallenge).
