    \ this resolved. Here is the step you need to follow,\n\nOnce  [pylon_camera][1]\
    \ is installed, there is a lib called pylon_camera_write_device_user_id_to_camera\
    \ which can be found in the <path>\n/ros/lib/pylon_camera/ directory. Just plug\
    \ one camera at a time and assign a device id to the selected camera as follow,\
    \ \n\n    ros/lib/pylon_camera/pylon_camera_write_device_user_id_to_camera traffic_left<this\
    \ is the id of the camera>\n\nThen in the config file, define the device which\
    \ was assigned. \n\n \n\n\n  [1]: http://wiki.ros.org/action/fullsearch/pylon_camera"
