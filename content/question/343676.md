  body_markdown: "You can think of base_footprint as ; \nprojection of `odom` frame\
    \ onto the ground plane, which is mostly used for object collision detection/avoidance\
    \ algorithms. `odom`'s roll and pitch angles can be changed during the movements\
    \ of robot, however in base_footprint \n\n    With respect to the odom frame,\
    \ the roll and pitch angles should be zero and the yaw angle should correspond\
    \ to the base_link yaw angle.\n    \n    Rationale: base_footprint provides a\
    \ fairly stable 2D planar representation of the humanoid even while walking and\
    \ swaying with the base_link.\n\nyou can also refer to [here](https://www.ros.org/reps/rep-0120.html#base-footprint)\
    \ for some more details. \n\nalso have look at the similar discussion in this\
    \ topic; [here](https://answers.ros.org/question/208051/what-is-the-purpose-of-base_footprint/)"
