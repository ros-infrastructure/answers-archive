  body_markdown: "I see two potential problems.  First, did you generate your stl\
    \ files from solidworks?  Solidworks seems to prepend their binary STL files with\
    \ 'solid' , which messes up assimp.  If you manually edit the files and change\
    \ that to any other five letter word, such as robot, then Assimp will load the\
    \ mesh file.  \n\nFor the second error, OMPL is not able to configure the search\
    \ space.  I've seen this happen when your robot consists entirely of continuous\
    \ joints with no joint limits.  We can't currently plan for such robots - if you\
    \ change at least one joint in your URDF to a regular Revolute joint and set joint\
    \ limits and re-run the wizard I think things will work."
