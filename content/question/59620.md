    From the [CameraInfo](http://ros.org/doc/api/sensor_msgs/html/msg/CameraInfo.html) message declaration:

    > Rectification matrix (stereo cameras
    > only) A rotation matrix aligning the
    > camera coordinate system to the ideal
    > stereo image plane so that epipolar
    > lines in both stereo images are
    > parallel.

    Alternatively, ROS has a tool built in to do stereo calibration and write it out in the correct format: http://www.ros.org/wiki/camera_calibration/Tutorials/StereoCalibration
