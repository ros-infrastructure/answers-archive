    If you've installed OpenNI 1.52 you might want to check your manifests.xml for the OpenNI packages in ROS and include the following:

      <rosdep name="libopenni-dev"/>
      <rosdep name="libusb-1.0-dev" />
      <rosdep name="libopenni-nite-dev"/>
      <rosdep name="libopenni-sensor-primesense-dev"/>
