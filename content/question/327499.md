In some datasets ORB-SLAM2 losses track when the ROS bag is played in normal speed.
However, if I play the ROS bag in 20% of its original speed the algorithm finishes the trajectory estimation without any issue.

I really can't figure out what the issue is, and by the time my processor does not choke, I can't understand why it looses track if I play the ROS bag in normal speed.
