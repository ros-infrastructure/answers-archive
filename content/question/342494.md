    The relevant tutorials are all out there. However, I agree that you have to find your way through all of those. So I'll give a shot about how I'd approach this. (No guarantees I didn't forget anything ;-) And, as I don't know your specific requirements, it'll be very high-level. I recommend to follow-up with specific questions once they arise.)

    1. model your car in GAZEBO (I'm assuming, this is what you want to use for simulation: [Tutorials](http://gazebosim.org/tutorials?cat=build_robot) (plural, you'll need to walk through quite a lot here). Goal: being able to move the simulated car with remote control (e.g. keyboard or a joystick).
    1. Get your car localized. Use [`fake_localization`](http://wiki.ros.org/fake_localization) for this (as suggested by @gvdhoorn ). Goal: having the full tf tree set up
    1. set up navigation. you can follow the [standard navigation tutorials](http://wiki.ros.org/navigation/Tutorials). If your car has an ackermann-steering, you need to choose an appropriate local planner. I'd suggest the [teb_local_planner](http://wiki.ros.org/teb_local_planner/Tutorials), but there are probably others that you could use as well. Goal: being able to send the car to a single point (from `rviz`).
    1. Implement a node that creates and services your random points to the navigation using an [action client](http://wiki.ros.org/actionlib/Tutorials). Goal: full functionality.

    I hope this makes sense. Godspeed :-)
