  body_markdown: "Chances are your serial device is not named `/dev/sbg` \n\nTry:\n\
    \n 1. Unplugging your usb->serial\n    converter. \n 2. Run `ls /dev` in a terminal\n\
    \ 3. Plug in the usb->serial converter\n 4. Run `ls /dev` again.\n 5. Look for\
    \ a newly added device (probably something like `/dev/ttyUSB0`)\n 6. Edit `config/sbg_device_uart_default.yaml`\
    \ to use the new device instead of `/dev/sbg`\n\n"
