Hi all, 

I have a robot with dimensions (LxWxH) in cm: 82x64x104 and the rotational center is 8 cm from the back of the robot. I am using rotary encoders to get the odometry message. I am using [amcl](http://wiki.ros.org/amcl?distro=indigo) for localization and [move_base](http://wiki.ros.org/move_base?distro=indigo) for planning with `TrajectoryPlannerROS` as the local planner and `sbpl_lattice_planner` as global planner.

I will prefer that the robot stops, goes in reverse and then continues on its path. What changes do I have to make to do something like this? Let me know if you need more information from me.
