    Here's what I would do:

    1. Subscribe to the local costmap topic being generated by your obstacle avoidance stack
    1. Get the range of points within this local costmap
    1. Pick an x value and a y value at random between these points
    1. Check if that point is marked as 'clear' by the costmap
    -  If it is, go there
    -  If it isn't, pick a new point
