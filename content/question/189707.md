    There are other methods for calculating the relative distance between two lat/lon coordinates that don't require knowledge of the individual pseudoranges to satellites. Also, if you use the utm_odometry_node in the [gps_common](http://wiki.ros.org/gps_common) package, you'll get UTM coordinates for each of your GPS positions. The UTM coordinates are given in meters, so computing the distance between two of them should be trivial. Are those methods insufficient for your needs?
