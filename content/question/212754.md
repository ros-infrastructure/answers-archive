  body_markdown: "For the Hokuyo URG-04LX-UG01 (as an example), the angular resolution\
    \ is `0.36\xB0` which is what you would get if you do `240/ranges.size()`.\n\n\
    Therefore, assuming the X axes (phi=0) is following ROS convention, you have to\
    \ set -120\xB0 to `ranges[0]` and then increase it by `0.36\xB0`. In summary:\
    \ `phi = (i * 0.36)-120`. And then, convert to radians."
