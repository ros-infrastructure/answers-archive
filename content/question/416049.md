    Hi there,
    Micro-ROS does not support Arduino UNO due to its resource constraints. Please go through the list of supported boards by micro-ROS here [micro-ROS Supported Hardware](https://micro.ros.org/docs/overview/hardware/)

    As a beginner, you can go through the steps of setting up micro-ROS in Arduino IDE using the [micro_ros_arduino repo](https://github.com/micro-ROS/micro_ros_arduino) for setting up supported hardware.

    Also, head to the [micro-ROS Tutorials page](https://micro.ros.org/docs/tutorials/core/overview/) for more detailed tutorials.

    Hope it helps
