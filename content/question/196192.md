  body_markdown: "buildtool_depend must only be catkin. The others mentioned there\
    \ have to be build_depend\n\nE.g.:\n\n      <buildtool_depend>catkin</buildtool_depend>\n\
    \    \n      <build_depend>roscpp</build_depend>\n      <build_depend>rospy</build_depend>\n\
    \      <build_depend>std_msgs</build_depend>\n      <build_depend>image_transport</build_depend>\n\
    \      <build_depend>cv_bridge</build_depend>\n      \n      <run_depend>roscpp</run_depend>\n\
    \      <run_depend>rospy</run_depend>\n      <run_depend>std_msgs</run_depend>\n\
    \      <run_depend>image_transport</run_depend>\n      <run_depend>cv_pridge</run_depend>\n\
    \nFurther, you have to find_package them in your CMakeLists.txt (in case you not\
    \ yet have):\n\n    find_package(catkin REQUIRED COMPONENTS roscpp rospy std_msgs\
    \ image_transport cv_bridge)\n\n"
