    \ in the realsense's model.sdf:\n\n    <!--horizontal_fov>1.211</horizontal_fov-->\n\
    \     <horizontal_fov>1.571</horizontal_fov>\n\nThen I set the rtab_map's camera\
    \ info topic to the depth cam info topic:\n\n<arg name=\"camera_info_topic\" \
