    \nUnless you add your workspace to the package path in your `~/.bashrc` file,\
    \ each time you open a new terminal you will need to `source` your workspace (see\
    \ [this tutorial](http://www.ros.org/wiki/ROS/Tutorials/InstallingandConfiguringROSEnvironment)).\n\
    \nTo set your workspace permanently, open your `.bashrc` file in a text editor,\
    \ for example -- `gedit ~/.bashrc` -- and add \n\n    export ROS_PACKAGE_PATH=/your/path/to/workspace:$ROS_PACKAGE_PATH\n\
