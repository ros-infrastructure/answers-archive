    \ only Nvidia cards really work (and only with the restricted Nvidia driver),\
    \ ATI/AMD and Intel cards will not work, since the drivers do not have all the\
    \ OpenGL/FireGL capabilities that Gazebo requires. In case of ATI drivers, we\
    \ observed error messages about missing GL features, but with the Intel drivers,\
