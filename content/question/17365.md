  body_markdown: "You're best bet would be to write your own custom plugin that \"\
    fakes\" the physics of an Ornithopter.   \n  \nFor reference you might want to\
    \ check out this paper:  \nhttp://www.cim.mcgill.ca/~dmeger/ICRA2015_GaitLearning/icra2015_gait_learning.pdf,\
    \ page 4  \nThe authors used Gazebo plugins to implement empirical equations for\
    \ a swimming robot's buoyancy, drag, and flipper thrust underwater.\n\nFlexible\
    \ surfaces can be approximated by using smaller rigid surfaces connected with\
    \ joints.\n\nFinally, Gazebo may not be the best option for your particular design.\
    \ You may be better off looking for another simulator that's designed to handle\
    \ soft-bodies / fluids.  "
