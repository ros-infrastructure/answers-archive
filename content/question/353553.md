    Then the question is do they fit your sensor? Specially the min height paramter can be difficult, the realsense that I use for example doesnt give exact "Zero"/Floor, so I sample from some centimeters upwards. Otherwise you get all the floor noise in the data. Also the max hight paramter might be tricky, as instead of a slice you get a projection of a one meter hight cut of the cloud with the standard parameters, this is different then PtoL wich gives just a slice 1 "pixel" wide at the center of the d-image. Then there is the min/max scan angle its set to +-Pi/2 radians thats nearly 180 degrees, rgbd cameras dont have such a wide field of view and I dont know how this effects the generated scan.
