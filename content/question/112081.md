  body_markdown: |
    It would seem that commit [e27ac3ba](https://github.com/ros/urdfdom/commit/e27ac3bacd42a89e6ae9a62cd64d9be2938b18c9) has changed the directory where the binaries in `urdfdom` end up in during installation.

    They are now installed as 'normal' executables in `/opt/ros/DISTRO/bin`, meaning that after you have sourced your ROS environment, you can just do `check_urdf /path/to/your/urdf` in a terminal. You don't need to use `rosrun ..` anymore.

    PS: I'm not sure if this was intended, so I've opened an [issue](https://github.com/ros/urdfdom/issues/20).
