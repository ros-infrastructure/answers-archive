    In RViz->Displays->Laser Scan->Color Transformer, you can set the laser scan colours to reflect the intensity of the laser scan. The colour depends on the intensity of the laser scan message received; first, the intensity at each point is normalised, and then a colour is computed based on that intensity. You can read more on the [rviz wiki](http://wiki.ros.org/rviz/DisplayTypes/LaserScan).
