In ROS2, how can I set CFLAGS and LDFLAGS to build a package?

XENO_CONFIG := /usr/xenomai/bin/xeno-config 

CFLAGS := $(shell $(XENO_CONFIG)   --posix --alchemy --cflags)

LDFLAGS := $(shell $(XENO_CONFIG)  --posix --alchemy --ldflags)

...

just like [this example.](http://www.cs.ru.nl/J.Hooman/DES/XenomaiExercises/Exercise-1.html)

How can guarantee that this parameters are being used in "ament buld --symlink-install --only-package PACKAGE_NAME"?
