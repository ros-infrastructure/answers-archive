  body_markdown: "Solved here: https://github.com/ros2/rclc/issues/200:\n\n> `rclc_executor_spin_some`\
    \ -> `rcl_wait` -> `rmw_wait` -> `uxr_run_session_until_data` and the former will\
    \ call your transport read and write functions.\n> \n> In general the spin function\
    \ in the executor is in charge of waiting for the middleware: in this step the\
    \ middleware is ran and if there is any data it is stored in RMW layer static\
    \ buffers. Then the executor performs a `rcl_take` that retrieves that data.\n\
    > \n> Let me know if you have further questions."
