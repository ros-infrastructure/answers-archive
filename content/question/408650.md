    There are two synchronizations going on. One for the d400 topics, make sure they are all published:

        rostopic hz /d400/color/image_raw \
           /d400/aligned_depth_to_color/image_raw \

        rostopic hz /t265/odom/sample \
           /rtabmap/rgbd_image
