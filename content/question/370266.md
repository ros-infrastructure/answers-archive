You can see that you may associate as many targets to an export as you like, and install as many exports as you like. All will fall under the same package-wide namespace, to be picked up by the [extras file](https://github.com/ament/ament_cmake/blob/0cf1d91c7031cc71caf0df85de4e67695740ff3a/ament_cmake_export_targets/cmake/ament_cmake_export_targets-extras.cmake.in#L8-L9) specific to this part of ament_cmake.
