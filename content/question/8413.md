    The `<include>` tag can be used as a [child of world](https://bitbucket.org/osrf/sdformat/src/bdd56d02553e51749ea56fb23e81f50a857250d2/sdf/1.5/world.sdf?at=default#cl-16)
    or as a [child of model](https://bitbucket.org/osrf/sdformat/src/bdd56d02553e51749ea56fb23e81f50a857250d2/sdf/1.5/model.sdf?at=default#cl-30). It currently accepts 4 sub-tags: `uri`, `name`, `pose`, and `static` (used for specifying models as static). Ideally, there would be more options there, but that's all we have implemented so far.
