    This sounds like a network/ROS environment configuration issue. My first thought would be to make sure that on your UAV, ROS_IP is set to the externally accessible IP address of the UAV. Take a look at the [NetworkSetup](http://www.ros.org/wiki/ROS/NetworkSetup) wiki page for more troubleshooting suggestions.

    It's generally not a good idea to mix versions of ROS, though I doubt that is the cause of this particular problem.
