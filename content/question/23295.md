  body_markdown: "Try setting friction parameters to the wheel links and ensuring\
    \ that your wheels have contact with the ground. Otherwise, there is nothing to\
    \ stop your wheels from rotating. It would be helpful to visualize the wheel contacts\
    \ in Gazebo and make sure that they are not jittery. If they are, you need to\
    \ tune your minDepth, kp, and kd tags.\n\nFor **example**:\n\n\t<gazebo reference=\"\
    left_wheel\">\n\t\t<mu1>0.9</mu1>\n\t\t<mu2>0.9</mu2>\n\t  <minDepth>0.001</minDepth>\n\
    \t\t<kp>100000000</kp>\n\t\t<kd>1</kd>\n\t</gazebo>\n\n\t<gazebo reference=\"\
    right_wheel\">\n\t\t<mu1>0.9</mu1>\n\t\t<mu2>0.9</mu2>\n\t  <minDepth>0.001</minDepth>\n\
    \t\t<kp>100000000</kp>\n\t\t<kd>1</kd>\n\t</gazebo>\n\nGuide on Friction: http://gazebosim.org/tutorials?tut=friction\n\
    \nI would also recommend setting `<implicitSpringDamper>` to true to get better\
    \ performance.\n\n\t<gazebo reference=\"left_wheel_hinge\">\n  \t       <implicitSpringDamper>true</implicitSpringDamper>\n\
    \t</gazebo>\n\n\t<gazebo reference=\"right_wheel_hinge\">\n  \t       <implicitSpringDamper>true</implicitSpringDamper>\n\
    \t</gazebo>"
