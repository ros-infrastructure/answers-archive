    The ROS wiki uses [MoinMoin](https://moinmo.in), not Markdown, so the page you link to isn't relevant.

    You can check [wiki/HelpOnEditing](http://wiki.ros.org/HelpOnEditing) and [wiki/HelpOnMoinWikiSyntax](http://wiki.ros.org/HelpOnMoinWikiSyntax) to see what is supported.

    Beyond that, I'm afraid you're going to have to resort to 'tricks' (ie: including images with your equations rendered (by something else) or embedding an html snippet that uses some service to render the equations).
