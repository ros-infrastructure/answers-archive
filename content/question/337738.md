    > How do I solve this issue?

    Use the correct name ;)

    [builtin_interfaces/msg/Time.msg](https://github.com/ros2/rcl_interfaces/blob/93cedce2dacd25fa4f2969d022ccbe3f2903e3fe/builtin_interfaces/msg/Time.msg) only has a `sec` field, not `seconds`:

        int32 sec
        uint32 nanosec

    And unless I'm mistaken:

    >     double cur_time = img_msg->header.stamp.seconds();

    It's also not a method/function, but a plain `public` member variable. You can't invoke it, so the `()` are also incorrect/not needed.
