    We are in the process of implementing many features in Gazebo. Bullet's soft-body dynamics are on our minds, though there is not yet a feature request for it on the [Gazebo issue tracker](https://bitbucket.org/osrf/gazebo/issues?status=new&status=open). Please feel free to submit one and vote for it.

    As a side note, we are actively working to implement deformable body physics using the [DART physics engine](https://github.com/dartsim/dart). Currently we have [added some sdf parameters](https://bitbucket.org/osrf/sdformat/issue/32/add-deformable-properties-to-sdf) and are working with the DART developers on the implementation.
