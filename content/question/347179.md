    Hi @me_saw,

    A Gazebo plugin is added with this lines in the URDF:

          <plugin filename="plugin_name.so" name="some_name" >
            plugin args...
          </plugin>

    However you will need to export the plugins path to advertise Gazebo where to find custom plugins. Take a look at this [tutorial](http://gazebosim.org/tutorials?tut=plugins_model&cat=write_plugin), you whould be able to follow it until the end, where you will find how to export and launch the plugin.

      \ not the link. For instance, imagine you have a basic robot with a `base_link`\
      \ and two `wheel_links` the descriptino will be somewhat to: \n`base_link -->\
      \ wheel_left_link` (joined by `left_wheel_joint`: `continuous`) \n`base_link\
      \ --> right_wheel_link` (joined by `right_wheel_joint`: `continuous`)\nThen\
      \ you need to load a plugin that uses that joints setting the velocity to move\
      \ the model. Take a look at the [diff_drive_plugin](http://docs.ros.org/melodic/api/gazebo_plugins/html/gazebo__ros__diff__drive_8cpp_source.html)\
