Hi,

I am working with Ignition-Edifice to simulate an environment for my Kinova robot. For doing so I downloaded the [realsense camera from Ignition-fuel ](https://app.ignitionrobotics.org/OpenRobotics/fuel/models/Intel%20RealSense%20D435)to have some vision connected with ROS2. `<plugin name="ignition::gazebo::systems::Sensors" filename="libignition-gazebo-sensors-system.so"> </plugin>`

Is this some kind of bug due to ignition version or is some other solution?
