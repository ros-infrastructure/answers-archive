    I believe the repo in your link was developed for an older ROS distro.

    `xacro.py` is deprecated.

    In `autonomous_nav.launch` file found [here](https://github.com/bandasaikrishna/Autonomous_Mobile_Robot/blob/main/mobile_robot_autonomous_navigation/launch/autonomous_nav.launch) replace the following:

        <param name="robot_description" command="$(find xacro)/xacro.py $(arg model)"/>

    to:

        <param name="robot_description" command="$(find xacro)/xacro $(arg model)"/>
