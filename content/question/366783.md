    A few things you might try:

     - change the fixed frame from map to odom and perhaps back again
     - try opening rviz before you start your application
     - try opening rviz after you start your application
     - try a different rmw implementation:
       - see: https://index.ros.org/doc/ros2/Tutorials/Working-with-multiple-RMW-implementations/
       - basically just add `RMW_IMPLEMENTATION=rmw_cyclonedds_cpp` before your commands

    If any of those things help, it may indicate what the actual problem is, or if it is a bug in rviz perhaps.
