    The format is documented here: [wiki/Bags/Format/2.0](http://wiki.ros.org/Bags/Format/2.0).

    Instead of writing something from scratch, you may want to look at something like [ros/ros_comm/tools/rosbag_storage](https://github.com/ros/ros_comm/tree/melodic-devel/tools/rosbag_storage), which is the C++ library underlying the `rosbag` set of tools.

    It does have some dependencies on other pieces of ROS, but those could potentially be removed.

    See also #q267309 for a previous Q&A about this topic.
