    I believe the best approach is to use namespaces (why are you trying to avoid this?). Even if you manage to use multiple masters, you'll need namespacing as Gazebo cannot provide the data for each robot in topics without namespace. How would you differ the odom of robot A from robot B, for example?

    EDIT1: You don't need to double the number of subscribers/publishers if you have twice the number of topics in different namespaces. The correct approach is to launch your controller node twice, under different namespaces, and it will handle the subs/pubs in a transparent way.

    Regarind your obstacle avoidance, do a `rostopic list | grep scan` and check how many scan topics you have. I guess you are subscribing to a scan topic that does not belong to the robot you are controlling.

    Check these examples of multi robot simulation: http://wiki.ros.org/Robots/TIAGo/Tutorials#Multi-TIAGo
