  body_markdown: "The syntax for `rosrun` is\n\n    rosrun <package-name> <executable>\n\
    \nIt looks like, in your case, `<package-name>` is `mobile_robot_pkg` and `<executable>`\
    \ is `robot_bt_driver.py`. So, the command should be \n\n    rosrun mobile_robot_pkg\
    \ robot_bt_driver.py\n\nI'm not sure what `Keyboard` is."
