    \ a *lookupTransform* is called at time:\n\nscan_in.header.stamp + ros::Duration\
    \ ().fromSec (scan_in.ranges.size () * scan_in.time_increment)\n\nMy quick-fix\
    \ solution was to call *waitForTransform* and include that added time before calling\
    \ *transformLaserScanToPointCloud*. \n\nSource: https://github.com/ros-perception/laser_geometry/blob/hydro-devel/src/laser_geometry.cpp\n"
