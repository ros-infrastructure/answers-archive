  body_markdown: "The TurtleBot is a flexible system which can work at many levels.\
    \  Controlling the base can be done by almost any processor.  To process the Kinect\
    \ you need at least an Atom processor from 2011 or newer.  Every computer is different\
    \ and we can't test all of them, we don't use the graphics card much for we usually\
    \ keep the laptop shut.  We recommend the [Asus EeePC 1215N](http://turtlebot.com/buy-parts.html)\
    \ as it can process the Kinect with one core and there's a second core available\
    \ for other processing.  And we've tested extensively with it.  \n\nOther things\
    \ the laptop will need include: \n\n - At least 2 USB buses with 1 USB 2.0 bus\
    \ to dedicate solely to the Kinect. \n - A wifi adapter compatible with your network.\
    \  \n\n "
