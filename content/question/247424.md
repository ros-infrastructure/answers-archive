    As you are probably on a recent ROS version, you will need to convert the package to the catkin build system. The [catkin migration guide](http://wiki.ros.org/catkin/migrating_from_rosbuild) should contain all the relevant information. The [catkin docs](http://docs.ros.org/indigo/api/catkin/html/) will also be useful.

    edit: Whoops, my bad, I was just attended to the fact that you *can* actually use rosmake to build ros packages for the more recent distributions. Have a look at the [rosbuild wiki](http://wiki.ros.org/rosbuild) and [tutorials](http://wiki.ros.org/rosbuild/Tutorials) for more info.
