    Hi David,

    PR2 odometry information is published by a [ROS odometry controller](https://code.ros.org/svn/wg-ros-pkg/stacks/pr2_controllers/trunk/pr2_mechanism_controllers/src/pr2_odometry.cpp).  All controllers in gazebo are loaded through a [gazebo plugin](http://ros.org/wiki/pr2_gazebo_plugins?distro=groovy#gazebo_ros_controller_manager) that mimics the [PR2 controller manager](http://ros.org/wiki/pr2_controller_manager) on the robot.  Gazebo itself does not keep odometry information.

    John
