  body_markdown: "As per ROS REP 103: Standard Units of measure and Coordinate Conventions,\
    \ units in Gazebo should be specified in meters and kilograms. (...)\n\nhttps://www.ros.org/reps/rep-0103.html\n\
    \nIt seems to depend on the controller:\n\nhttp://wiki.ros.org/ros_control#Controllers\n\
    \nhttps://answers.gazebosim.org//question/4205/joint_state-ros-topic-from-gazebo-and-ros_control/\n\
    \nand the joint type being controlled (http://wiki.ros.org/urdf/XML/joint):\n\n\
    > <limit> (required only for revolute\n> and prismatic joint)\n> \n> An element\
    \ can contain the following\n> attributes:\n> \n> - lower (optional, defaults\
    \ to 0) \n>\n> An attribute specifying the lower joint\n> limit (radians for revolute\
    \ joints,\n> meters for prismatic joints). Omit if\n> joint is continuous. \n\
    > \n> - upper (optional, defaults to 0)\n>\n> An attribute specifying the upper\
    \ joint\n> limit (radians for revolute joints,\n> meters for prismatic joints).\
    \ Omit if\n> joint is continuous."
