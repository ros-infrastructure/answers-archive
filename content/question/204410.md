  body_markdown: "The costmap_2d package does not support rotating costmaps. \n\n\
    The rolling_costmap forces the orientation of the costmap to remain stable. This\
    \ makes it easier to shift the data as the window \"rolls\". The orientation is\
    \ a lot more difficult to transform the data, and the costmap does not currently\
    \ support it. "
