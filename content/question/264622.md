    These are not files that are part of the source or binary distribution, as they are unique to each camera: they contain calibration values for the *intrinsics* of your sensor.

    To generate them, you'll have to calibrate the camera. See [wiki/openni_launch/Intrinsic calibration of the Kinect cameras](http://wiki.ros.org/openni_launch/Tutorials/IntrinsicCalibration) for a tutorial (it doesn't completely apply though, according to [this](https://3dclub.orbbec3d.com/t/astra-pro-rgb-camera-calibration-file/761) post on the ORBBEC forum).
