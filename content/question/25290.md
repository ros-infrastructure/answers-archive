  body_markdown: |-
    `/clock` publishes a message once per every new state, i.e. at 1/[max_step_size](http://sdformat.org/spec?ver=1.6&elem=physics#physics_max_step_size) Hz. You should rather focus on why your simulation runs so slow than the clock publish rate (did you perhaps tweak max_step_size?).

    Nonetheless, if you still want to change the frequency, you can do that with the `/gazebo/pub_clock_frequency` parameter on the parameter server.
