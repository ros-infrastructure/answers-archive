  body_markdown: |-
    After you brings up RGBDSLAM by using Kinect or other RGBD sensors, you can use `rqt` to check what are the input topics of RGB and depth image for RGBDSLAM.

    Let's say the topic names are /rgb_image and /depth_image, then you need to publish images in your dataset to /rgb_image and /depth_image. You can do it by reading the images from OpenCV and publish it to topics. (You might need this [ref](http://wiki.ros.org/cv_bridge/Tutorials/UsingCvBridgeToConvertBetweenROSImagesAndOpenCVImages).)

    Due to some problem I cannot indicate the precise topic name. But I think you can find out by yourself.
