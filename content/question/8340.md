  body_markdown: "The function is probably badly named, but I believe `GetAngle` for\
    \ a prismatic joint returns the joint's displacement in meters. See [this issue](https://bitbucket.org/osrf/gazebo/issue/1086/better-documentation-needed-for-joint).\n\
    \nI did a quick test with the GUI. Insert the `Simple Arm` model and on the World\
    \ tree, select the `arm_wrist_lift_joint`, which is prismatic. Expand `axis1`\
    \ and you'll see that the joint's lower and upper limits are -0.8 m and 0.1 m\
    \ respectively. Now expand the joint control panel on the right and apply some\
    \ forces to move this joint up and down, and you'll see the value of `angle_0`\
    \ go from one joint limit to the other.\n\n![image description](https://ftp.osuosl.org/pub/ros/download.ros.org/downloads/se_migration/gazebosim/14275855139653644.png) "
