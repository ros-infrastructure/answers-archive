    MoveIt! is a popular choice for inverse kinematics. Take a look at the [documentation](http://docs.ros.org/indigo/api/moveit_tutorials/html/doc/ros_visualization/visualization_tutorial.html). The tutorials there will help you to configure a solver and use RViz for controlling your arm.

    Once that's working, it's just a small step to use InteractiveMarkers in ros3djs to do the same from a web browser.
