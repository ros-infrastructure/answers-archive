 Hi all,

I new to ROS2 and try to follow the ROS2-tutorial about [Service](https://index.ros.org/doc/ros2/Tutorials/Writing-A-Simple-Py-Service-And-Client/). I tried to implement a  Client-node in ROS2-Ardent platform. The [reference](https://index.ros.org/doc/ros2/Tutorials/Writing-A-Simple-Py-Service-And-Client/#write-the-client-node) uses `call_async()` function, which are not support in ROS-Ardent (https://github.com/ros2/rclpy/blob/018b9fbf1c66f1c1c488510e0caa299fb0223277/rclpy/rclpy/client.py#L71), only `call()` method. Follow this [link](https://index.ros.org/doc/ros2/Tutorials/Sync-Vs-Async/#introduction), it is said that not recommeded to work with `call()`.  

Is there any alternative way to work-around this confliction?  
The `Thread` syntax too complicate while `call_async()` not supported.
