    The essential problem is that `vision_opencv` is a [metapackage](http://wiki.ros.org/catkin/package.xml#Metapackages), not a package. A package should never depend, actually, it cannot depend on a metapackage. There is a great discussion about why this is the case [here](https://groups.google.com/forum/#!msg/ros-sig-buildsystem/mn-VCkl2OHk/-9FoB15ixE0J).

    In your package, depending on `cv_bridge` should be enough. Note that the [package.xml](https://github.com/ros-perception/vision_opencv/blob/indigo/cv_bridge/package.xml) in `cv_bridge` adds a bunch of system dependencies (such as `libopencv-dev`).
