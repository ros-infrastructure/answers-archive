  body_markdown: "The camera_link-frame is the parent frame of your camera-subtree\
    \ so you should use this one to attach your camera to the tf-tree. \nYou use the\
    \ world->IR Transformation only as an intermediate result to compute the pose\
    \ of the camera_link-frame relative to the world and then add the world->camera_link-Transformation\
    \ to the tf-tree. \nso roughly T(world->camera_link)        =    T(world->IR)\
    \  *  T^-1(camera_link->IR) \n"
