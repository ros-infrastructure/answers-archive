  body_markdown: "This is an old post but for anyone may looking for it:\n\nTuning\
    \ Hector is related to the movement of the LiDAR sensor, the resolution of the\
    \ LiDAR and sometimes the environment. \n\nTypically, I feel that if LiDAR is\
    \ not fixed on a moving platform or if the platform is moving brutally, you need\
    \ to watch carefully with the `laser_max_dist` and `laser_min_dist` as well as\
    \ `update_factor_free` and `update_factor_free`. You need to think about how many\
    \ points you need to include in each scan and if they are in a valid sweep at\
    \ all.\n\nSometimes `map_resolution` helps too if you know the feature of your\
    \ environment. lower it to a reasonable size definitely contribute to the processing\
    \ speed. you can pair it with `map_size` to manage the total map size yet still\
    \ remain reasonable features.\n\nIn case you find your mapping twisted or kidnapped\
    \ when the pose turning, then play with `map_update_angle_thresh` and `map_update_distance_thresh`.\n\
    \nafter all, `map_multi_res_levels` is the key if your system poses joggling back\
    \ and forth. but I feel you should not go more than 3."
