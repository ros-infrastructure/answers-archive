  body_markdown: "If you use a single-threaded spinner you don't need to use mutex\
    \ because the callbacks will be called sequentially, therefore only one callback\
    \ will be reading or writing the variable at a time.\n\nYou can read more about\
    \ it here:\n \nhttp://wiki.ros.org/roscpp/Overview/Callbacks%20and%20Spinning"
  comments:
  - body_markdown: |-
      Importantly, rospy has a completely different threading model. Every subscriber and timer in rospy spawns its own thread, and rospy.spin() is jut an infinite loop that lets those threads operate. For that reason, mutexes are very important in rospy when globals or class variables are used.
