    \ was messing up with my path (as mentioned in the comments).\nSo I commented\
    \ the `export PATH=\"/home/usp/anaconda2/bin:$PATH\"` in my `~/.bashrc` ,then\
    \ close the terminal & reopen it. And everything was running smooth."
