    \ or `/usr/lib/libpoencv_core.so.2.3.1` and ~-l/usr/lib/libopencv_core.so.2.3.1`\
    \ is not supported.\nAt this moment, we need following script to modify output\
    \ of pkg-config to gcc compliant.\n\n$ pkg-config --libs opencv-2.3.1 | sed 's#-l\\\
    S*lib\\([^\\.\\/]*\\).so.2.3.1#-l\\1#g'\n-lopencv_contrib -lopencv_legacy -lopencv_objdetect\
    \ -l3d -lopencv_features2d -lopencv_video -lopencv_highgui -lopencv_ml -lopencv_imgproc\
    \ -lopencv_flann -lopencv_core  \n\n$ pkg-config --libs opencv-2.3.1 | sed 's#-l##g'\n\
