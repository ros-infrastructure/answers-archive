    I'm not aware of a way to generate DH parameters directly from URDF.

    You can however convert your robot the OpenRAVE format (see for example [here](http://ros.org/wiki/Industrial/Tutorials/Create_a_Fast_IK_Solution) on how to do that). Then, you can generate a table of DH parameters using OpenRAVE's [GetDHParameters](http://openrave.org/docs/latest_stable/openravepy/openravepy_int/#openravepy.openravepy_int.planningutils.GetDHParameters) function.
