  body_markdown: "Actually, I wouldn't say this is fixed. It does raise some issues\
    \ as there's a bit more to this than it seems at first glance. Two packages are\
    \ involved on lucid:\n\n * libusb-dev (this is pointer to libusb-0.1)\n * libusb-1.0.0-dev\n\
    \nFrom nhanndt's experience, it turns out ni won't compile if libusb-dev is not\
    \ installed, which may quite often be the case on a fresh install.\n\nWhich begs\
    \ the question - does ni need libusb-dev as an rosdep? Is ni even linking with\
    \ libusb-1.0? Does it need libusb-1.0?\n\nLooking at the CMakeLists.txt in openni_camera\
    \ - it's pulling in libusb-0.1 into the example binaries. You can take out the\
    \ usb reference there and still compile as libusb-1.0 is getting pulled in libraries\
    \ in the openni package. \n\nSo probably the usb linking in target_link_libraries\
    \ in openni_camera/CMakeLists.txt can be dropped and it will resolve the problem\
    \ for future users. Can this be confirmed (should probably ticket this)?\n\n"
