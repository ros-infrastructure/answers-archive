    It seems that you do not use rectified images. Please [calibrate your camera](http://www.ros.org/wiki/camera_calibration/Tutorials/MonocularCalibration) and make sure the camera driver pubishes the accurate [CameraInfo](http://www.ros.org/wiki/image_pipeline/CameraInfo) messages. Run [image_proc](http://www.ros.org/wiki/image_proc) in the camera's namespace to generate rectified images from your raw input.
    Then let mono_odometer subscribe to `<your camera>/image_rect`. If you still have problems, please modify your question to include the full console output of `mono_odometer`.
