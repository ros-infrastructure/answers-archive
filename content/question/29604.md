    You can also use the [Polygon DisplayType](http://www.ros.org/wiki/rviz/DisplayTypes/Polygon).

    It accepts a [PolygonStamped](http://www.ros.org/doc/api/geometry_msgs/html/msg/PolygonStamped.html) message and displays it in rviz for you.

    I have used this before to demonstrate a boundary of a field.  This will not fill in the polygon for you, though.
