    As far as I know, there's no complete documentation like the ROS REP 103.

    In general, Gazebo uses [SI](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_System_of_Units) units, for example:

     - length: meter (m)
     - mass: kilogram (Kg)
     - time: second (s)
     - angle: radian ([gazebo::math::Angle::Degree()](http://osrf-distributions.s3.amazonaws.com/gazebo/api/dev/classgazebo_1_1math_1_1Angle.html#a10a0c78fdec2745bd59d1330ab50548f) to convert to degrees)
     - force: Newton (N)

    Coordinate frames are right-handed, with `Z` up, `X` forward and `Y` to the left.

    Euler angles follow the roll (about X axis), pitch (about Y axis), yaw (about Z axis) order about a fixed reference frame.
