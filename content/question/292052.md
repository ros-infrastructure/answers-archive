  body_markdown: |-
    The answer to your *title* is "yes, as long as it's a robot frame that is statically connected to your IK eef".

    But you are actually asking about attached objects which are handled differently from robot frames.
    Using those as eef links does not work out of the box as far as I know.
    But I, as one of the maintainers, would really like it to work and in theory it's not even difficult.
    If I remember correctly the problem was that an intermediate representation only gets to know about the `RobotModel` and not the `RobotState`, and the former does not know anything about attached objects.

    (attempted) Patches are very welcome!
