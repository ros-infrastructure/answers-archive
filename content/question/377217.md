  body_markdown: "Hello, \n\n1- when you download the carla simulator and extract\
    \ the binary , find the OpenDRIVE maps Town01 .. 10 (.xodr) \n\n2- use conversion\
    \ tool like [assure mapping tool](https://github.com/hatem-darweesh/assuremappingtools)\
    \ to load the .xodr and make sure it include traffic lights. \n\n3- you can use\
    \ the same tool to save the .xodr into either KML map for OpenPlanner , or Lanelet2\
    \ map . \n\n4- you can use a [converter node](https://github.com/Autoware-AI/utilities/tree/master/lanelet_aisan_converter)\
    \  inside Autoware.AI to convert the lanelet2 map to vector map. \n\n"
