The PR2's [environment server launch file](https://code.ros.org/svn/wg-ros-pkg/stacks/pr2_arm_navigation/trunk/pr2_arm_navigation_actions/launch/environment_server.launch) sets some parameters that are undocumented in the [planning_environment package wiki page](http://www.ros.org/wiki/planning_environment), in particular:

 - object_padd
 - pointcloud_padd
 - joint_state_cache_allowed_difference

Of these, the first two appear in the [API docs of the package]( http://www.ros.org/doc/api/planning_environment/html/). My intention is to edit the wiki and document them. My question is mostly concerned with the third (undocumented?) parameter. Does it refer to the maximum allowed (past/future) extrapolation time when querying the joint states cache?.
