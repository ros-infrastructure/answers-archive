    Quaternions are a representation of orientation [wikipedia](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quaternion)

    For advanced concepts there's an extensive reference written by [Erik Dam](http://www.itu.dk/people/erikdam/DOWNLOAD/98-5.pdf)

    There's a good [primer](http://www.ogre3d.org/tikiwiki/Quaternion+and+Rotation+Primer) on the ogre website.

    O.Khatib lectures (from Stanford) on introduction to robotics are freely available from the iTunes U store and can be accessed [here](https://see.stanford.edu/Course/CS223A). There is a part elaborating on rotation representations.

    There's also a great tutorial at https://eater.net/quaternions
