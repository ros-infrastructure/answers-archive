    My gmapping.launch file that solved the problem:

        <launch>
          <node pkg="gmapping" type="slam_gmapping" name="slam_gmapping" output="screen">
            <rosparam>
              odom_frame: odom
              map_update_interval: 0.5
              maxUrange: 30.0
              maxRange: 60.0
              sigma: 0.05
              kernelSize: 1
              lstep: 0.05
              astep: 0.05
              iterations: 5
              lsigma: 0.075
              ogain: 3.0
              lskip: 0
              linearUpdate: 0.5
              angularUpdate: 0.436
              temporalUpdate: -1.0
              resampleThreshold: 0.5
              particles: 80
              xmin: -1.0
              ymin: -1.0
              xmax: 1.0
              ymax: 1.0
              delta: 0.025
              llsamplerange: 0.01
              llsamplestep: 0.01
              lasamplerange: 0.005
              lasamplestep: 0.005
              base_frame: base_footprint
            </rosparam>
          </node>
        </launch>

    $ rosrun gmapping slam_gmapping scan:=base_scan

I am using a youbot with ubuntu 12.04 and ROS hydro. My the laser i am using is a hokuyo laser.
