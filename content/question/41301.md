    Sorry, the wiki is not necessarily 'a mess' just because there is no tutorial for exactly your use case. All relevant tutorials and more in-depth descriptions are directly linked from the [knowrob wiki page on ros.org](http://www.ros.org/wiki/knowrob).

    Effectively using the system and adapting it to your needs won't work without some learning from your side. I'd recommend to go through the basic tutorials and have a closer look at those about methods for [reasoning about objects](http://ias.in.tum.de/kb/wiki/index.php/Reasoning_about_objects) or the [representation of map data](http://ias.in.tum.de/kb/wiki/index.php/Map_data_representation).

    Besides, there are quite some [publications](http://ias.cs.tum.edu/people/tenorth/publications) describing different aspects of the system.
