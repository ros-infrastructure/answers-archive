- body_markdown: |-
    move_base works with both `amcl` and `robot_localization`. You can check [this](https://answers.ros.org/question/259084/basic-robot_localization-example/) answer for more info and examples regarding `robot_localization`. As for the message, if it comes as [std_msgs/String](http://docs.ros.org/melodic/api/std_msgs/html/msg/String.html) in the form you explained then you can convert it into [nav_msgs/Odometry](http://docs.ros.org/melodic/api/nav_msgs/html/msg/Odometry.html) or [geometry_msgs/PoseWithCovarianceStamped](http://docs.ros.org/melodic/api/geometry_msgs/html/msg/PoseWithCovarianceStamped.html) msg in the callback by simply parsing the string and then feed that message into `robot_localization`
