  body_markdown: "The Camera Info Message will not be published by USARSim, it describes\
    \ the distortion of the Camera. What it is supposed to contain, can be found in\
    \ the [Camera Info Message Api](http://www.ros.org/doc/api/sensor_msgs/html/msg/CameraInfo.html)\n\
    \n>  If the camera is uncalibrated, the matrices D, K, R, P should be left \n\
    > zeroed out. In particular, clients may assume that K[0] == 0.0  indicates an\n\
    > uncalibrated camera."
