  body_markdown: "It can do the same job, however the one under ROS uses a different\
    \ model structure URDF(is beeing deprecated though). Whereas current gazebo uses\
    \ *.SDF. Some standard settings might be different. However the main difference\
    \ is that ROS comes with a large library of other robotics tools, and gazebo plugins,\
    \ which you can couple to gazebo.\nHence controlling your robots in under ROS\
    \ is somewhat simpler(using nodes, services etc.), as what most people need is\
    \ already programmed by others and added to the package. I prefer just plain gazebo,\
    \ as i have no use for the ROS control. "
