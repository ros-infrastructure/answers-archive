  body_markdown: "Hi,\n\nPlease have a look into [yaml](http://wiki.ros.org/map_server#YAML_format)\
    \ of map you are using. \n\nusing `origin` and `resolution`  you can find position\
    \ of each cell.\n\n\n----------\n   \n\n> **origin** : The 2-D pose of the\n>\
    \ lower-left pixel in the map, as (x, y,\n> yaw), with yaw as counterclockwise\n\
    > rotation (yaw=0 means no rotation).\n> Many parts of the system currently\n\
    > ignore yaw.\n\n\n----------\n\n>**resolution** : Resolution of the map, meters\
    \ / pixel\n\n\n----------\n**Edit**\n\n[Occupancy Grid msg](http://docs.ros.org/indigo/api/nav_msgs/html/msg/OccupancyGrid.html)\
    \ member `data` is having \n\n> Occupancy probabilities are in the\n> range [0,100].\
    \  Unknown is -1.\n\nUse `free_thresh` param to get Unoccupied cells (i.e free\
    \ cells). \n\n\n\n\n> **free_thresh** : Pixels with occupancy\n> probability less\
    \ than this threshold\n> are considered completely free.\n\nif `free_thresh: 0.196`\
    \ then all cells with occupancy lessthan 19.6 (and not -1 ) are free . \n"
