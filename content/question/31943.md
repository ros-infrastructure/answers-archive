  body_markdown: |-
    It looks like you may be trying to use the [old Diamondback `eigen` package](http://www.ros.org/wiki/eigen/diamondback), which downloads and builds the library from source.

    [Starting with Electric](http://www.ros.org/wiki/eigen), you should replace `<depend eigen>` with `<rosdep eigen>` in your `manifest.xml`, and make some [updates to your `CMakeLists.txt`](http://www.ros.org/wiki/eigen#Electric_CMakeLists.txt). That will use the system version of the eigen library (after the rosdep is installed).

    UPDATE: The Debian entry for the eigen rosdep may be broken. Rosdep *should* detect that eigen is already installed and not try to download it again. What does this say?

        rosdep check eigen

    UPDATE2: Clearly, the Debian rosdep for eigen is broken. I am opening a [Trac ticket](https://code.ros.org/trac/ros/ticket/3927) to get it fixed. On Fuerte rosdep works differently, but the same problem appears to be present there, too.
