    Quick comment (and not an answer): the error you quote is actually not from `move_group`. It's logged by the `joint_trajectory_controller`. See [here](https://github.com/ros-controls/ros_controllers/blob/dce87855e1adf3a66271db8243d58561da9f2cdb/joint_trajectory_controller/include/joint_trajectory_controller/joint_trajectory_controller_impl.h#L574).

    I'd suggest to update your `ROSCONSOLE_FORMAT` to something like: `'[${severity}] [${time}] [${node}]: ${message}'` so it's clear which node prints what. You could add the following to your `.bashrc` (if using Bash):

        export ROSCONSOLE_FORMAT='[${severity}] [${time}] [${node}]: ${message}'

    doesn't change your question of course, but thought I'd mention it.
