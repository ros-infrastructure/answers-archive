    \nOpenSplice is indeed a supported DDS vendor in ROS 2. The RMW layer is hosted\
    \ at [ros2/rmw_opensplice](https://github.com/ros2/rmw_opensplice).\n\n> and what\
    \ extend?\n\nLooking at the [issue tracker](https://github.com/ros2/rmw_opensplice/issues)\
    \ it would appear there are a nr of outstanding issues.\n\nREP 2000 lists OpenSplice\
    \ support as \"Tier 2\" for Crystal ([here](http://www.ros.org/reps/rep-2000.html#crystal-clemmys-december-2018-december-2019)),\
    \ which implies that OpenSplice does not see the same level of support as FastRTPS\
    \ or RTI Connext (the exact difference between the various tiers should get clarified\
    \ by [ros-infrastructure/rep#192](https://github.com/ros-infrastructure/rep/pull/192)).\n\
    \n\n\n"

      "support" is a bit ambiguous of course: at one level it may mean that there is a way to use OpenSplice as your middleware, which is what the RMW repository makes possible. But then there are things like non-functional requirements and whether you receive the same kind of support when reporting issues.

      The fact that it works for you on Debian doesn't necessarily mean that it makes sense to state that (in REP 2000 fi), as it may still be too costly or involved to actually support it properly.
