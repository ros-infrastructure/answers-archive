  body_markdown: "From your question, it seems like you may be confused about the\
    \ confusing acronym \"IPC\".\n\nIn general, IPC refers to \"inter-process communication\"\
    , communication between processes.\n\nThe link you're referring to, points to\
    \ \"**intra**-process communication\", which is communication between nodes in\
    \ a single process. Note that there's a mistake in your question, you should be\
    \ passing ``use_intra_process_comms``.\n\nROS2 communication uses inter-process\
    \ by default, but by enabling intra-process, you can use zero-copy transport for\
    \ faster \nmessage transport.\n\nIf you have two separate ``ros2 run`` or ``ros2\
    \ launch``, then they are running in separate processes, and you won't be able\
    \ to have intra process communication between them.\n\nTo your other questions:\n\
    \n* Having multiple executors in one node:  intra-proces communication should\
    \ work (although I haven't actually tried this out).\n* Does IPC support all QoS:\
    \ yes, it should."
