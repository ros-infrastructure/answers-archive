  body_markdown: "Lets take `add_point()` line by line.\n\n    point = JointTrajectoryPoint()\n\
    Get a new `JointTrajectroty` point. \n\n    point.positions = copy(positions)\n\
    Copy the arg `positions` into the newly created `point`'s `positions` attribute.\n\
    \n    point.time_from_start = rospy.Duration(time)\nSet `time_from_start` of `point`\n\
    \n    self._goal.trajectory.points.append(point)\nAppend this newly created `point`\
    \ to the list `self._goal.trajectory.points`\n\n`copy(positions)` is used to simply\
    \ copy the lists to the new `JointTrajectory` point. You can't `append()` positions\
    \ directly to `self._goal.trajectory.points`. You can only `append()` `point`s.\
    \ \n\nHope it's clear!"
