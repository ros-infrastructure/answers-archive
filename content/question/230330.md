    There is [uml-robotics/ROS.NET](https://github.com/uml-robotics/ROS.NET), a 3rd-party client library written in C#, explicitly targeting Windows. I have not used this myself, so I can't comment on performance/stability/compatibility.

    An alternative might be to use [rosbridge](http://wiki.ros.org/rosbridge_suite): it's essentially a json-over-websockets (by default) bridging solution that might be enough, depending on the requirements of your application.
