    The controller requires `JointState` handles to read the current joint state (which it gets from your `hardware_interface`), and as everything in ROS having to do with positions, velocities, accelerations, etc (see [REP-103: Standard Units of Measure and Coordinate Conventions](https://www.ros.org/reps/rep-0103.html)), those handles expect to be accessing data in appropriate units. So for revolute type joints, that would be `rad`, `rad/s` and `rad/s^2`. For linear joints, `m`, `m/s` and `m/s^2`.

    There is no single answer to your question, as we do not know what type of robot you are trying to configure the `diff_drive_controller` for.
