    > Is that the only way ? or rather the recommended way ?

    I'd say that is the recommended way, as hanging in a system call is never really nice / robust (unless you use a blocking read on a suitable configured file descriptor as a timing device, or waiting for an event). You could look into `SO_RCVTIMEO` (see [Linux: is there a read or recv from socket with timeout?](http://stackoverflow.com/a/2939145)) on SO).

    Another option would be using `select(..)` or Boost asio, but that would be (slightly) more involved.

    > I guess I will go with timeout then. I am looking if I could use the SIGINT signal in any way

    Well, at least according to [this](http://stackoverflow.com/a/3800915) (and others), `SIGINT` might not make it to `recvfrom(..)` and friends. Which could be why you are hanging in `recvfrom(..)` when you `ctrl+c` the `roslaunch` session. But I haven't verified this.
