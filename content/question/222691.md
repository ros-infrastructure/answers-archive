  body_markdown: "The fixed frame is what you consider not moving in the world, i.e.,\
    \ fixed. It is, what rviz projects all data into. For your example, you might\
    \ just choose the frame that the laser is published in. However, if it is attached\
    \ to a moving base, this should be a frame in the world, e.g., odom or map (if\
    \ these are available). "
