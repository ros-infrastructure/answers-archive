  body_markdown: "It depends, at least for me. \n\nIf it's an important part of your\
    \ research (e.g. you prepared a special protocol for experimental validation of\
    \ results, used some packages from ROS toolkit, etc.), then you should mention\
    \ it by citing the [ICRA 2009 article](http://robotics.stanford.edu/~ang/papers/icraoss09-ROS.pdf)\
    \ \n\nor at least by giving a site link like this: \n\n`Open Source Robotics Foundation.\
    \ Robotic Operating System.\\url{https://www.ros.org}`\n\nIf it was used somewhere\
    \ on your robot, but wasn't important to the overall publication, I would skip\
    \ it (but it should be in the project documentation beyond those few pages in\
    \ the journal)."
