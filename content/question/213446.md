    \ \n\nCMakeLists.txt:\n\n    find_package(catkin REQUIRED COMPONENTS\n       ...\
    \ \n       your_old_package\n    )\n\npackage.xml:\n\n       <run_depend>your_old_package</run_depend>\n\
    \       <build_depend>your_old_package</build_depend>\n\nI guess you are also\
    \ missing to link your node inside CMakeLists.txt with roscpp by:\n\n    add_executable(<executable_name>\
    \ <dependencies>)\n    target_link_libraries(<executable_name> ${catkin_LIBRARIES})\n\
    \nHave you checked `rqt_graph` if the topics are connected?\n\n(deselect hide\
    \ checkboxes to see unconnected topics rectangular boxes)\n\nIf not you might\
