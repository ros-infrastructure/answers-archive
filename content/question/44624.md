    Great references (and hopefully they clarify your questions too) are on the wiki and the REPs, e.g. [distances are in meters, angles are in radians ](http://www.ros.org/wiki/geometry/CoordinateFrameConventions) or more broadly, [SI or SI-derived units](http://www.ros.org/reps/rep-0103.html) are to be used (by everyone).

    Remember that the [twist message](http://ros.org/doc/api/geometry_msgs/html/msg/Twist.html) "expresses velocity in free space broken into it's linear and angular parts." So angular.z is the angular velocity (not an angle).
