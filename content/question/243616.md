    The publish and subscribe queues are both per-subscriber.

    The [topics page](http://wiki.ros.org/Topics) describes the Topic Statistics feature which measures topic frequency, bandwidth and dropped messages, and from the message definition it looks like those statistics are per-connection. I haven't used these myself, but you should be able to view the topic statistics in [rqt_graph](http://wiki.ros.org/rqt_graph)

    You may also want to [read the paper](http://nerve.uml.edu/ros-2014/ROS%20System%20Profiling%20and%20Visualization%20-%20Dan%20Brooks.pdf) that was written when the topic statistics feature was added.
