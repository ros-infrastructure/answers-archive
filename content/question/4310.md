    The ros\_control project has a trajectory controller that includes an FollowJointTrajectoryGoal action server. It can then connect to your simulated robot using the gazebo\_ros\_control plugin by adding <transmission> tags to your robot's URDF. This is documented [here](http://gazebosim.org/wiki/Tutorials/1.9/ROS_Control_with_Gazebo) and the trajectory controller is documented [here](https://github.com/ros-controls/ros_controllers/tree/hydro-devel/joint_trajectory_controller).
