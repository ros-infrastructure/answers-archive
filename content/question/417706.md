    The coordinate frames for mobile platforms are defined in [REP 105](https://www.ros.org/reps/rep-0105.html) including `base_link`. Which you appear to be following correctly. The most common location on ackermann vehicles is the center of the rear axle.

    I don't think that the problem that you're having is related to your coordinate frames.
