    The `rosdep` key for `rospkg` is `python-rospkg` not `rospkg`.

    Your package must use `python-rospkg` not `rospkg` when declaring a dependency.

        % rosdep --rosdistro groovy resolve python-rospkg
        #pip
        rospkg
