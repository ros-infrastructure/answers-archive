title: uvc_camera applies max values for brightness and contrast
upvote_count: 0
---
I have been using <code>uvc_camera</code> to capture image streams in ROS before. It has changed its behavior recently and I can't figure out why or how to revert it. 

When opening my capture device, <code>rosrun uvc_camera camera_node _device:=/dev/video1</code>, it will print out its parameters as usual. The parameters of interest are:
<pre>  int (Brightness, 0, id = 980900): -16 to 16 (1)
  int (Contrast, 0, id = 980901): 1 to 32 (1)</pre>

Looking at the stream with <code>rosrun image_view image_view image:=image_raw</code> shows an image that is by far too white, contradicting the zero values for both brightness and contrast. Looking at the camera device, after turning of the <code>uvc_camera</code> node, with <code>guvcview</code> shows that the Brightness and Contrast parameters are set to their max values, 16 and 32, respectively.

Using the same application, <code>guvcview</code>, I can revert the parameters to their default values. However, when turning on the <code>uvc_camera</code> node again, the image will again be too white. 

As I mentioned, I have used <code>uvc_camera</code> before on the same and other webcams. The faulty behavior does not change when changing webcams.

I am stuck here, any suggestions on how to get a proper image stream are more than welcome.
