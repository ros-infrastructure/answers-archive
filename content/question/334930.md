  body_markdown: "The problem of merging maps between robots is a slam problem. There\
    \ are different solutions to do slam in ROS. \n(Amongst others [gmapping](https://wiki.ros.org/slam_gmapping),\
    \ [hector slam](https://wiki.ros.org/hector_slam) and [cartographer](https://wiki.ros.org/cartographer))\n\
    \nOne solution that is known to support multiple agents is  **cartographer**:\
    \ https://google-cartographer-ros.readthedocs.io/en/latest/going_further.html#multi-trajectories-slam"
