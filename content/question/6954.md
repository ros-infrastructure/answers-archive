    `SetVelocity` bypasses physics (see the call to [SetVelocityMaximal](https://bitbucket.org/osrf/gazebo/src/e7aded6140484b7adccae3c612453deb798769f0/gazebo/physics/Joint.cc?at=default#cl-749) underneath), so forces were not computed based on target velocity.  To do this in a physically realistic fashion, write a force based PID controller that takes velocity target as feedback, and `GetForceTorque` should give you more physically meaningful readings.
