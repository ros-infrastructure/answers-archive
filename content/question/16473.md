    \ using one.\n\nFor C++, you can use the [sdformat](https://bitbucket.org/osrf/sdformat/overview)\
    \ library.\n\nNot sure if you've seen this, but the SDF spec is [here](http://sdformat.org/spec).\n\
    \nA clarification on reference frames in SDF:\n\n* top level `model` poses are\
    \ expressed in the `world` frame\n* nested `model` poses are expressed in their\
    \ parent `model` frame\n* `link` poses are expressed in their parent `model` frame\n\
    * `visual` and `collision` poses are expressed in their parent `link` frame\n\
    * `joint` poses are expressed in their **child** `link` frame \n\n"
