 Working with ROS can be challenging, but don't be discouraged! It's entirely normal to face difficulties when learning something new. Remember that learning ROS is a journey, and "ah-ha" moments will come with persistence and practice.

As you continue to work with ROS, you'll gradually gain a deeper understanding of its concepts and functionalities. The fact that you've already learned to create and spawn URDF robots and navigate Linux shows that you're making progress.

The beauty of ROS lies in its versatility and power. As you gain more experience, you'll discover how ROS enables seamless communication between different nodes, allowing you to create complex robotic systems.

Be patient with yourself and don't shy away from exploring different aspects of ROS. Seek out resources, tutorials, and examples to expand your knowledge. Embrace the learning process and don't be afraid to experiment.

The "ah-ha" moments will come when you least expect them. It might be understanding a tricky concept, successfully remapping nodes, or creating a functional robot system. These moments of clarity and achievement will gradually build up, and you'll find yourself feeling more confident in your ROS skills.

Celebrate your progress, no matter how small, and take pride in each milestone you achieve. Learning ROS opens up exciting possibilities in robotics, and your dedication will undoubtedly pay off.

Stay persistent, stay curious, and keep challenging yourself. You'll soon discover what makes ROS so cool and find yourself considering your expertise in ROS with a sense of accomplishment. You're on the right path, and we believe in your potential to excel in ROS!
