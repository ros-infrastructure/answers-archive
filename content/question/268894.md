  body_markdown: "> I don't think that editing the accepted answer is the correct\
    \ way to go about it, [..]\n\nEditing the answer to remove whatever was there\
    \ - updating typically includes removing parts - is probably not very nice.\n\n\
    What could be acceptable is to post a new answer, and then edit the accepted one\
    \ to *add* a note that there is a new answer. The note could be placed at the\
    \ top of the original answer, to make sure it has maximum visibility.\n\nSomething\
    \ like:\n\n> Edit YYYY-MM-DD: see answer by @user_who_posted_new_answer for an\
    \ updated answer.\n> \n> ---\n> \n> The original answer text ..\n\n\n---\n\nAnd\
    \ with enough karma it's also possible to *unaccept* the answer and then accept\
    \ the new one. Personally I would not do that, as it implies that the original\
    \ poster accepted the new answer, which he hasn't (and with really old questions\
    \ most likely never will do)."
