    \ mesh). \nFor those who are beginners, to spawn a simple SDF you need just to:\n\
    \n    <param name=\"robot_description_sdf\" textfile=\"$(find somebot_urdf)/Table/v140/model.sdf\"\
    />\n    <node name=\"spawn_model\" pkg=\"gazebo_ros\" type=\"spawn_model\" output=\"\
    screen\" args=\"-sdf -param robot_description_sdf -model model -x 0.0 -y 0.0 -z\
    \ 0.0\"/>\nThe second .urdf file in simple cases is not necessary. \nNow Gazebo\
    \ works perfectly without crashes."
