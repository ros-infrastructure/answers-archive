    You'll want to add an extra `args=""` parameter to your `<node>` tag. For more information, take a look at the [XML docs for `<node>`](http://www.ros.org/wiki/roslaunch/XML/node)

    **EDIT**: Try something like:

    `<node pkg="rosjava_bootstrap" type="run.py" args="bumperCar org.lejos.ros.nodes.bumpercar.BumperCarControl" name="BumperCarControl" />`
