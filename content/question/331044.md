    \ a duplicate question? Please do not flood the forums with duplicates.\n\nIf\
    \ not, you could estimate your velocity by deriving your position with respect\
    \ to time.\nEstimate pose from velocity\n\n    x += v_x * dt\n    y += v_y * dt\n\
    \    theta += v_theta * dt\n\nand so therefore your velocity in the odom frame\
    \ can be estimated as\n\n    v_x = delta_x / dt\n    v_y = delta_y / dt\n    v_theta\
    \ = delta_theta /dt\n\nGiven this you can extrapolate a robot frame velocity of\
    \ \n\n    v.x = v_x/cos(theta)\n    omega = v_theta (due to the nature of differential\
    \ drive kinematics).\n\nAlso keep in mind that due to kinematic constraints\n\n\
    \    v.y=0"
