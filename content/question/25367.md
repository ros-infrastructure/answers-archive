  body_markdown: |-
    Add this description to your world file.

        <scene>
          <ambient>1 1 1 1</ambient>
          <background>1 1 1 1</background>
          <shadows>0</shadows>
        </scene>

    For the full specification, see [here](http://sdformat.org/spec?ver=1.7&elem=scene).

    You could optionally try to play with the sun (see [here](https://github.com/osrf/gazebo/blob/gazebo11/models/sun/model.sdf)).
