![image description](https://ftp.osuosl.org/pub/ros/download.ros.org/downloads/se_migration/ros/13491825887758888.png)

    roscd gscam
    cd bin
    rosrun gscam gscam &
    rosrun camera_calibration cameracalibrator.py --size 9x7 --square 0.039 /image:=gscam/image_raw &
