    You need to specify the action.
    Do this (if you want to call the action 'follow_joint_trajectory'):

        Client_t client("follow_joint_trajectory", true);

    [This actionlib tutorial](http://wiki.ros.org/actionlib_tutorials/Tutorials/SimpleActionClient) is very easy and well written.
