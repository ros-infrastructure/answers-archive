    the gazebo_ros_factory was deprecated as we wrapped the functionalities into `gazebo/src/gazeboros.cpp`.

    Look for and remove the plugin referring to <strong>libgazebo_ros_factory</strong> in your `.world` file.

    Once [simulator_gazebo 1.4.5](http://ros.org/wiki/simulator_gazebo/ChangeList/1.4) debians are out, gazebo should continue to run without throwing and exiting (see [this question](http://answers.ros.org/question/2176/care-o-bot-simulator-crashing-gazebo)).

    On top of that, there are other bugs.  I've opened a [ticket](https://code.ros.org/trac/ros-pkg/ticket/5172) with a patch that makes things work.
