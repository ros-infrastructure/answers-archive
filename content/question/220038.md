  body_markdown: "Since ```orocos_kdl``` is not a catkin package you need to find\
    \ it separately and not as part of the ```find_package(catkin)``` \n\nSo try\n\
    ```\nfind_package(orocos_kdl REQUIRED)\n```\ninstead of \n```\nfind_package(catkin\
    \ REQUIRED COMPONENTS orocos_kdl)\n```"
