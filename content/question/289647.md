    I don't know how much you are expecting from autonomous map building, it is an active field of research but there are several packages for this purpose, You can use [frontier_exploration](http://wiki.ros.org/frontier_exploration) or [rrt_exploration](http://wiki.ros.org/rrt_exploration) but you need to configure your robot in order to get this exploration packages working, for example properly configured navigation stack that can accept action goals to move_base needed by frontier. You can find requirement details on their wiki packages. Hope this helps
