  body_markdown: " Sounds like RVIZ view is set to TopDownOrtho. You want to change\
    \ it to Orbit.\n\nFirst make sure that the panel \"Views\" is visible. Next to\
    \ \"File\" is menu called \"Panels\". Click \"Panels\" and click \"Views\" so\
    \ that it has a check mark next to it. \n\nNext on the view panel there is a drop\
    \ down box labelled \"Type:\". Change it to \"Orbit (rviz)\"."
