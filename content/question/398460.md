    \ version of ROS or ROS2. Try running\n\n * ``sudo apt-key del F42ED6FBAB17C654``\
    \ to remove the key you've added, then run\n\n * ``sudo apt update``, then\n\n\
    \ * ``sudo curl -sSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ros/rosdistro/master/ros.key\
    \ -o /usr/share/keyrings/ros-archive-keyring.gpg``, then\n\n * ``echo \"deb [arch=$(dpkg\
    \ --print-architecture) signed-by=/usr/share/keyrings/ros-archive-keyring.gpg]\
    \ http://packages.ros.org/ros2/ubuntu $(source /etc/os-release && echo $UBUNTU_CODENAME)\
    \ main\" | sudo tee /etc/apt/sources.list.d/ros2.list > /dev/null``"
