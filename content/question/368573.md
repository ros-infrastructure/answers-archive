    To get you started check out [roslib](https://www.npmjs.com/package/roslib) to connect to your ROS server. It's important you have [Ros3djs](https://github.com/RobotWebTools/ros3djs) installed to provide the visualization in the browser.

    These docs provide [a decent tutorial](http://wiki.ros.org/ros3djs/Tutorials/VisualizingAURDF) to get you started. Also make sure you have the [rosbridge websocket](http://wiki.ros.org/rosbridge_suite/Tutorials/RunningRosbridge) to connect to your ROS instance in the web browser.

    Finally if your robot is not already moving you can use the [joint state publisher gui](http://wiki.ros.org/joint_state_publisher) to provide a quick and easy way to update the joint positions.
