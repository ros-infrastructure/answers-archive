  body_markdown: "Hi there,\n\nIf you are planning with MoveIt! using the MoveGroupCommander\
    \ (python/C++), you can inspect the trajectory that was planned. I do this by\
    \ the following:\n\n    group_manipulator.set_pose_target(pose_target)  \n   \
    \ plan_manipulator = group_manipulator.plan()\n    \n    print plan_manipulator.joint_trajectory.points.positions\n\
    \nMore information about joint_trajectory msgs can be found [here](http://docs.ros.org/kinetic/api/trajectory_msgs/html/msg/JointTrajectory.html).\
    \ \n\nThis will allow you to inspect the positions of each way point in the trajectory.\
    \ Rather than printing them to screen, you can plot them with your favorite tool."
