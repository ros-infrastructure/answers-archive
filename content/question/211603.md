     Put launch files into the package you pass as the 1st argument.

        $ roslaunch [package] [filename.launch]

    As [noted in the tutorial](http://wiki.ros.org/ROS/Tutorials/UsingRqtconsoleRoslaunch#Using_roslaunch), roslaunch command automatically looks into the passed package and detect available launch files.
