    Although more experienced users might answer differently, I don't think what you're asking is possible on `RViz` by itself since it's not an image processing/manipulating tool per se.

    `OpenCV` has [a nice stitching capability](http://docs.opencv.org/modules/stitching/doc/introduction.html). You should be easily able to transfer the resulting image to ROS by [image_transport](http://www.ros.org/wiki/image_transport).
