    Try using the [DiffDrivePlugin](https://bitbucket.org/osrf/gazebo/src/9e998023acbb0417ca6214047d0a102c9984b927/plugins/DiffDrivePlugin.hh?at=default), of [SkidSteerPlugin](https://bitbucket.org/osrf/gazebo/src/9e998023acbb0417ca6214047d0a102c9984b927/plugins/SkidSteerDrivePlugin.hh?at=default).

    Like so:

        <plugin filename="libDiffDrivePlugin.so" name="diff_drive">
          <left_joint>left_wheel_hinge</left_joint>
          <right_joint>right_wheel_hinge</right_joint>
          <torque>5</torque>
        </plugin>
