    I am not aware of any reports of using the Ladybug with [camera1394](http://www.ros.org/wiki/camera1394). It is not included in the [list of cameras tested](http://www.ros.org/wiki/camera1394/CameraList).

    That driver currently does not support the IIDC Pan feature. A patch to support it should be simple, and could be officially supported in the next release, if you need it.
