    All joints in Gazebo are passive until you send commands for them to move. So if you don't send any commands, the joints can move freely.

    You can check out [this](http://gazebosim.org/tutorials?cat=guided_b&tut=guided_b3) tutorial on how to make a simple mobile robot with two actuated front wheels and a passive caster wheel in the rear.
