    You need to sense the obstacles (for example with a laser scanner) and then mark the cells in your grid as not-walkable.

    Take a look at [SLAM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simultaneous_localization_and_mapping).

    The ROS-packages [hector-mapping](http://wiki.ros.org/hector_mapping) or [gmapping](https://wiki.ros.org/gmapping) can do this for you.
