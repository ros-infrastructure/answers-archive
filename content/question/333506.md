  body_markdown: |-
    ROS Kinetic is only supported on Ubuntu Xenial (ie: `16.04`), see [REP-3 - Platforms by Distribution: Kinetic Kame (May 2016 - May 2021)](http://www.ros.org/reps/rep-0003.html#kinetic-kame-may-2016-may-2021).

    You cannot install Kinetic on Ubuntu `18.04`.

    You'll need to either downgrade to Ubuntu Xenial, or [install ROS Melodic](http://wiki.ros.org/melodic/Installation/Ubuntu).
