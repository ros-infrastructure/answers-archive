  body_markdown: "Gazebo is not setup to consume an external clock. You could write\
    \ a plugin to Gazebo that controls simulation based on a different clock. For\
    \ example, your plugin could start simulation paused and subscribe to your custom\
    \ clock topic. Then, you'd have to step simulation based on the clock information.\
    \ This will only work if your simulation can run at the same speed or faster than\
    \ your clock. "
