  body_markdown: "To get it to recognize `ign-cmake0`, edit `ign-math\\configure.bat`\
    \ `L22` by adding the `-DCMAKE_PREFIX_PATH`:\n\n    cmake .. -G \"NMake Makefiles\"\
    \ -DCMAKE_INSTALL_PREFIX=\"%WORKSPACE_INSTALL_DIR%\" \n    -DCMAKE_BUILD_TYPE=\"\
    %build_type%\" -DBUILD_TESTING:BOOL=False\n    -DCMAKE_PREFIX_PATH=\"path\\to\\\
    gz-ws\\ign-cmake\\build\\install\\Release\"\n\nIn your case, I think it would\
    \ be : `-DCMAKE_PREFIX_PATH=\"D:\\gz-ws\\ign-cmake\\build\\install\\Release\"\
    `\nYour second `.\\configure`failed because the script leaves you in the `build`directory,\
    \ so you need to move back to \xECgn-math`."
