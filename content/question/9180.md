    We just released our ROS-Wrapper for libviso2, called [viso2_ros](http://www.ros.org/wiki/viso2_ros). It contains nodes for monocular and stereo odometry. Every parameter from libviso2 can be set through ROS. Odometry information is published as [nav_msgs/Odometry](http://www.ros.org/doc/api/nav_msgs/html/msg/Odometry.html) and [geometry_msgs/PoseStamped](http://www.ros.org/doc/api/geometry_msgs/html/msg/PoseStamped.html) as well as through a [tf](http://www.ros.org/wiki/tf) for easy visualization through [rviz](http://www.ros.org/wiki/rviz). Have a look at the corresponding [ros wiki page](http://www.ros.org/wiki/viso2_ros) for further information.
