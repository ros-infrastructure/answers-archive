    Have a look at the documentation in the [ros wiki: rosbag](http://wiki.ros.org/rosbag/Commandline):

        -l NUM, --limit=NUM
             Only record NUM messages on each topic.

    This is however not(!) what you really want to use as you will most likely not record the imu-message that belongs to the image-data as the imu (probably) will have a much higher frequency. A [Time Synchronizer](http://wiki.ros.org/message_filters#Time_Synchronizer) is a better choice.
