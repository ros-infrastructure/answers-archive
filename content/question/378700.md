    See this page, under "How to Edit a ROS Wiki Page" http://wiki.ros.org/EditingTheWiki#How_to_Edit_a_ROS_Wiki_Page

    In short, you have to [create an account on wiki.ros.org](http://wiki.ros.org/action/newaccount/Documentation?action=newaccount), then request to be added to the ROS wiki whitelist by [commenting on a specific issue](https://github.com/ros-infrastructure/roswiki/issues/258) (mention your username and why you want to edit the wiki/what page you want to edit). Once this is done, go back to the wiki page you want to modify, and click "Edit" on the right.
