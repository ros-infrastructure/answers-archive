  body_markdown: "Here is a good overview of [creating your own ROS repository](http://www.ros.org/wiki/Get%20Involved#Create_Your_Own_.2A-ros-pkg_Repository).\n\
    \n 1. At a minimum, send a message to the `ros-users` mailing list containing\
    \ the repository address.\n\n Since `git` and `hg` repositories generally require\
    \ separate source URLs for each stack, you may also wish to provide a `.rosinstall`\
    \ file, allowing fine-grained control over your part of the index. (For `svn`\
    \ repositories, that is often not needed.)\n\n 2. Wiki pages are not created automatically.\
    \ You are free to create them before or after announcing the new repository. \n\
    \n Please do create them, using the appropriate wiki templates. Some fields will\
    \ not appear until the indexer finds your code. That's OK.\n\n Personally, I like\
    \ to create package and stack pages **before** they appear in the repository.\
    \ Then, I can easily tell when the indexer has visited them.\n\n 3. Binary ROS\
    \ releases are not required, but I would recommend them. A good general open source\
    \ practice is to \"release early and often\". "
