    Hi @hojeong,

    That is not totally true since the [Qt project](https://www.qt.io/download-open-source?hsCtaTracking=9f6a2170-a938-42df-a8e2-a9f0b1d6cdce%7C6cb0de4f-9bb5-4778-ab02-bfb62735f3e5) have a (L)GPL part that can be used for free.

    As for other free tools to generate GUI it depedns on the language you plan to use and the target OS platform.

    For pyhton you have [PyQt](https://pypi.org/project/PyQt5/), [PyGTK](https://python-gtk-3-tutorial.readthedocs.io/en/latest/), [wxPython](https://www.wxpython.org/)...

    For C++ you have plenty as well, like [Qt](https://www.qt.io/), [wxWidgets](https://www.wxwidgets.org/), [GTK+](https://www.gtk.org/), [JUCE](https://juce.com/), [gtkmm](https://www.gtkmm.org/en/)...

    Finally, of course that you will be able to produce nice GUI with  Visual Studio mfc, but you need to bear in mind that ytou will need to integrate its framework into ROS enviroment.

    There are plenty of possibilities and all of them are free to use or have a free license part.
