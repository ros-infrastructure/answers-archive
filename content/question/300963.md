    There is not yet any defined best practice on documenting ROS2 packages. The ROS2 style guide does [give some information](https://github.com/ros2/ros2/wiki/Developer-Guide#documentation) on what should be in the documentation, but it does not prescribe a format. Use what you are familiar with, what is easy to maintain, and what produces good, readable documentation.
