    You could have a look at how the PR2 does it: [pr2_head_action](http://www.ros.org/wiki/pr2_head_action). Basically, they use [tf](http://www.ros.org/wiki/tf) to transform the 3D point into the pan and tilt frames, and then use two lines of trigonometry to get the corresponding pan and tilt angles.
