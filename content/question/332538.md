    [Probably your odometry is inaccurate.](http://wiki.ros.org/navigation/Tutorials/Navigation%20Tuning%20Guide#Odometry)

    Improving odometry typically involves tweaking the wheel odometry calculation by using a better model or by adapting model parameters. Or by involving additional sensors, like IMU or some kind of visual odometry, potentially fused by a [Kalman filter](http://docs.ros.org/lunar/api/robot_localization/html/index.html).

    Alternatively, or in addition you could try to find another SLAM implementation that maybe works more robust with respect to bad odometry, e.g., [hector_slam](https://wiki.ros.org/hector_slam) or [nav2d](http://wiki.ros.org/nav2d) or [Cartographer](https://google-cartographer-ros.readthedocs.io/en/latest/index.html).
