    You have a few options:

    1. Since you're using ROS, you can publish to `/gazebo/set_model_state`, [here](http://gazebosim.org/tutorials?tut=ros_comm#SetModelPoseandTwistinSimulationviaTopics)'s a tutorial.

    1. Or you could use Gazebo transport and publish a message to the `~/model/modify` topic

    1. Finally, you can always write a world plugin which sets the pose of objects in the world. [Here](http://gazebosim.org/tutorials?tut=plugins_world_properties&cat=write_plugin)'s a tutorial.
