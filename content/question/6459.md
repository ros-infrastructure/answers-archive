  body_markdown: "Hi,\n\nyour is not the problem. The sky is just optional and has\
    \ to be enabled explicitly.\n\ngazebo world files can contain a scene/sky block\
    \ like:\n\n    <scene>\n      <sky>\n        <clouds>\n          <speed>12</speed>\n\
    \        </clouds>\n      </sky>\n    </scene>\n \n\nThis enables SkyX...(taken\
    \ from `empty_sky.world` example)\nhave a look at the corresponding SDF regions\
    \ for more information\n[sdf1.5 sky](http://sdformat.org/spec?ver=1.5&elem=scene#scene_sky)\n\
    \n"
