    There are multiple RGBD cameras supported by as many ROS drivers/nodes (see [wiki.ros.org/Sensors - 3D Sensors](http://wiki.ros.org/Sensors#A3D_Sensors_.28range_finders_.26_RGB-D_cameras.29) for an incomplete listing), but the Kinect v2 is a popular one. Well supported by [code-iai/iai_kinect2](https://github.com/code-iai/iai_kinect2) and [ethz-asl/kinect2-ros](https://github.com/ethz-asl/kinect2-ros).
