    According to [ros/rosdistro/rosdep/base.yaml](https://github.com/ros/rosdistro/blob/8410f503ccb5769a46a1e4df609cfc9848fdcfe3/rosdep/base.yaml#L1811), the `libfcl-dev` key resolves to `libfcl-dev` for Ubuntu Bionic (which is what ROS Melodic is supported on).

    So it would seem to be `0.5.x` for both.
