  body_markdown: "The [usual practice](http://ros.org/wiki/Packages#Common_Files_and_Directories)\
    \ is to place your headers in `include/package_name`, referencing them like this:\n\
    \n    #include <package_name/header_name.h>\n\nYou would fill in your own `package_name`\
    \ and `header_name`, as appropriate. \n\nThey only need to be exported in the\
    \ `manifest.xml` if other packages include them. Your question implies no need\
    \ for that."
