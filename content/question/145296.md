    \ is to use one of the Ubuntu version stated on the [Installation page](http://wiki.ros.org/hydro/Installation/Ubuntu)\

    \ speed of learning will depend on previous knowledge, but following the tutorials\
    \ and trying out things yourself generally will allow you to make progress best."
