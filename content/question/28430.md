  body_markdown: "SDFormat models have to be self contained, i.e, a joint cannot reference\
    \ a link/frame outside of the model (except `world`). So when you tried to spawn\
    \ your model, it's failing at the SDFormat parsing stage. \n\nThis kind of thing\
    \ is best accomplished using a plugin. My suggestion is to try using the [DetachableJoint](https://gazebosim.org/api/gazebo/7/detachablejoints.html)\
    \ plugin. You can put the plugin in the object you're spawning at runtime. By\
    \ default, the DetachableJoint plugin creates a fixed joint between two links\
    \ when it gets loaded. "
