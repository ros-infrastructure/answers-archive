  body_markdown: "I found my own solution.\n\nWhen defining a publisher, if you're\
    \ trying to publish to the top level you must add a '/' to the front of the path.\
    \ For example;\n\n    nh.advertise<diagnostic_msgs::DiagnosticArray>(\"/diagnostics\"\
    , 10);\npublishes to the topic;\n\n    /diagnostics\nWhile \n\n    nh.advertise<diagnostic_msgs::DiagnosticArray>(\"\
    diagnostics\", 10);\n\npublishes to the \n\n    node_name/diagnostics\n\n"

    nh.advertise<diagnostic_msgs::DiagnosticArray>("diagnostics", 10);
