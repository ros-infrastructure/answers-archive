  body_markdown: "Assuming you have an IMU, look at the pitch angle:\n\n1. should\
    \ be below a flat surface threshold, e.g. 5\xB0\n2. wait until above threshold\
    \ for ramp angle, e.g. 20\xB0 -> on ramp\n3. wait until below flat surface threshold,\
    \ e.g. 5\xB0 -> off ramp\n\nIt's basically like a Schmitt-Trigger if you are familiar\
    \ with that."
