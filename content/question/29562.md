  body_markdown: "Are you talking about setting the initial position? \nYou can set\
    \ this by modifying your .world file. Example:\n\nroomba( pose [ 3.2 8 0 0 ] name\
    \ \"roomba_0\" color \"red\")\n\nwhere roomba is the name of the model of my robot.\
    \ The first argument of pose is the x position, the second is y position, third\
    \ z position and the last is the orientation of the robot.\n\nAnother way to set\
    \ the robot's position is to click on it and drag to the desired position."
