  body_markdown: "viso2 works only with mono or stereo cameras. If you want to use\
    \ the depth information of your kinect, then viso2 is not for you. \n\nOn the\
    \ other hand, fovis works with both stereo cameras or depth cameras. To use it\
    \ with your freekinect, use the rectified topics\n\n - <camera>/rgb/image_rect\
    \ (sensor_msgs/Image)\n - <camera>/depth_registered/image_rect (sensor_msgs/Image)\n\
    \nto publish/subscribe to your camera. Then open a dynamic reconfigure gui or\
    \ a launchfile and play with the parameters until you get the results you expect.\n\
    \n[Here](http://docs.fovis.googlecode.com/git/group__FovisCore.html#ga113578b67d3e37bc78f1fffd8440e1ff)\
    \ you have all the parameters explained."
