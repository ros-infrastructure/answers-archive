    It looks like the contacts between the banana and the table may be unstable. You can inspect them by choosing `View -> Transparent` and `View -> Contacts`.

    One solution would be to tweak the [surface](http://sdformat.org/spec?ver=1.6&elem=collision#collision_surface) parameters of the banana's collision. Something like this:


            <surface>
              <friction>
                <ode>
                  <mu>0.5</mu>
                </ode>
              </friction>
              <contact>
                <ode>
                  <min_depth>0.001</min_depth>
                  <kp>1e9</kp>
                </ode>
              </contact>
            </surface>
