    I guess I was right, after looking a little deeper into CMakeLists and tinkering around a bit I found that I have to use INSTALL(DIRECTORY ...) to make copies of the .yaml file to the right destination.

        install(
          DIRECTORY
            launch
            config
          DESTINATION
            share/${PROJECT_NAME}/
        )
