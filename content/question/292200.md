How would you recommend one use vision to determine the pose of an object for which there is a CAD model (and mesh)?  One option is [visp](http://wiki.ros.org/visp), but I think that only works for a 2D camera and I don't want to have to depend on camera calibration.  Another option, I would think, would be to use a depth camera and determine pose by somehow comparing the model to the point cloud.  (This depends on camera calibration, too, but it would be done at the factory.)  Do you know of any methods, preferably ROS-based but not necessarily, for getting pose from a point cloud?

tl;dr --- How do you suggest getting pose from a camera?  visp, depth camera, or other?
