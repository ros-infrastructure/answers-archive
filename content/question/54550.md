Apparently there is the issue, that new users with only few karma can't post comments (on other people's answers)([example](http://answers.ros.org/question/51908/xtion-empty-device-id/#54333)).
As a result some post comments as answers, since there is no other way.

If this is true, could we lower the amount of karma required for commenting?
