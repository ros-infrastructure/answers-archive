    I'm not sure you understand the concept of [topics](http://www.ros.org/wiki/Topics) and [messages](http://www.ros.org/wiki/Messages) quite correctly. It might help you to go through the main tutorials again, in particular [UnderstandingTopics](http://www.ros.org/wiki/ROS/Tutorials/UnderstandingTopics) and [WritingPublisherSubscriber(c++)](http://www.ros.org/wiki/ROS/Tutorials/WritingPublisherSubscriber%28c%2B%2B%29).

    Messages are always broadcast on topics. To create a topic, one node needs to advertise that they will broadcast messages on that topic. Then another node subscribes to that topic and receives all messages broadcast on that topic.
