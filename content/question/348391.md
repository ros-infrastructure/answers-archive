    As @gvdhoorn commented,

    This is a compilation issue regarding the definition of the `get` method in the `ros::param` namespace. If you take a look at the class documentation [here](http://docs.ros.org/diamondback/api/roscpp/html/namespaceros_1_1param.html), there is no `get` function matching the `uint32_t` data type. You way want to change that to `int` and then perform a static_cast to `uint32_t`.
