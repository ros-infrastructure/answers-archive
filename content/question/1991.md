    \ wich is the one being updated and developed. \n\nYou can follow the tutorials\
    \ here to learn how to use sensor data and move models and then use a plugin to\
    \ send data to ROS. You will need some knowledge of ROS publishing/subscribe to\
    \ topics and types of messages, but once this \"bridge\" is set up you can use\
    \ ROS to send vel commands to your gazebo robot.\n\nAt the moment i have a robot\
    \ in gazebo that sends laser scans and Pose to ROS and receives geometry_msgs/Twist\
