  body_markdown: |-
    The easiest way should be to use the StateValidationService of the `move_group` node, by calling the service `/check_state_validity` with [this message](http://docs.ros.org/api/moveit_msgs/html/srv/GetStateValidity.html). You will need the RobotState, which you can get from the `/get_planning_scene` service with [this message](http://docs.ros.org/api/moveit_msgs/html/srv/GetPlanningScene.html).

    In the [definition of the StateValidationService](https://github.com/ros-planning/moveit/blob/melodic-devel/moveit_ros/move_group/src/default_capabilities/state_validation_service_capability.cpp), you also find a pretty good example of how to do the checks yourself, in case you want to avoid calling the service too often.
