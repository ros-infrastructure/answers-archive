    That likely means that something else was wrong and caused the process to terminate. I have seen that message many times when nodes die (whether because I killed them or something else caused them to crash). A technical explanation of the kind of situation that leads to that error and how to avoid it is given in [this answer](https://stackoverflow.com/a/30090695). That being said, it sounds like your situation is similar to [this one](https://answers.ros.org/question/339013/boost-thread-error/), so debugging it may not be straightforward.

    Personally, I interpret the message as saying 'the node died', so as long as the node only dies when I kill it, I don't worry about it. If I didn't kill it, then I look for what really caused the node to die.
