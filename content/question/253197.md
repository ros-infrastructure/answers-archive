    C++'s `std::string` class provides an indexing operator implementation that lets you access individual characters in the string. See [std::string::operator[]](http://www.cplusplus.com/reference/string/string/operator[]/) for details.

    In your particular case, you could do something like:

        size_t my_index = ...;
        char the_byte = msg->data[my_index];

    Now `the_byte` contains the `char` at position `my_index` in `msg->data` (which is of type `std::string` ([wiki/msg](http://wiki.ros.org/msg))).

    As I wrote in my comment on your OP though: `std_msgs/String` is really not a good message to use for interfacing with CAN wrappers (or for storing byte buffers in, period).
