    Here are the relevant `rosdep` rules for `libsensors4-dev` (from [rosdep/base.yaml](https://github.com/ros/rosdistro/blob/252865de3f08613925b786e40e4a9242761ed4fe/rosdep/base.yaml#L1880-L1883)):

        libsensors4-dev:
          arch: [lm_sensors]
          fedora: [lm_sensors-devel]
          ubuntu: [libsensors4-dev]

    and for `mongodb` (again from [rosdep/base.yaml](https://github.com/ros/rosdistro/blob/252865de3f08613925b786e40e4a9242761ed4fe/rosdep/base.yaml#L2324-L2326)):

        mongodb:
          fedora: [mongodb]
          ubuntu: [mongodb]

    As you can see, there are no stanzas for `debian`, which is why `rosdep` is failing. The fact that you installed them via `apt-get` is irrelevant, as `rosdep` does not know what the package name is on your OS, and thus cannot find it in the list of installed packages.

    The proper course of action here would be to contribute those rosdep rules. For that, see [Contributing rosdep rules](http://docs.ros.org/independent/api/rosdep/html/contributing_rules.html).
