    Creating collision meshes is relatively simple: in most ROS description or ROS-Industrial support packages they are simple the *convex hulls* of the normal (or `visual`) messages.

    You can create convex hulls using a nr of meshing tools. Two frequently used ones are Meshlab and Blender.
