    \ like this \n\n    <model name='test'>\n      <link name='test_link'>\n\n   \
    \     <collision name='collision'>\n          <geometry>\n            <mesh>\n\
    \              <uri>model://test_pkg/meshes/test_mesh.dae</uri>\n            </mesh>\n\
    \          </geometry>\n        </collision>\n\n        <visual name='visual'>\n\
    \          <geometry>\n            <mesh>\n              <uri>model://test_pkg/meshes/test_mesh.dae</uri>\n\
    \            </mesh>\n          </geometry>\n        </visual>\n\n      </link>\n\
    \    </model>\n\nFor your other problem I don't have an answer. I don't really\
