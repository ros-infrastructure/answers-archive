    Your plugin needs a node handle to publish ROS messages. Here is an example of a [GPS plugin](https://github.com/tu-darmstadt-ros-pkg/hector_gazebo/blob/indigo-devel/hector_gazebo_plugins/src/gazebo_ros_gps.cpp) for Gazebo.

    Especially look at the following lines:

    <pre><code>152. node_handle_ = new ros::NodeHandle(namespace_);
    153. fix_publisher_ = node_handle_->advertise&lt;sensor_msgs::NavSatFix&gt;(fix_topic_, 10);
    </pre></code>

    <pre><code>236. fix_publisher_.publish(fix_);</pre></code>
