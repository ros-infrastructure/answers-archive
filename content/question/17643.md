    When you click `File -> Save World As`, Gazebo saves the current SDF representation of the world, which includes the SDF description for all entities in the world.

    However, models often refer to assets outside of the SDF file, like meshes, textures, plugins, etc. When you open the saved wotld on a different computer, you need to make sure all those resources are available and have the same path. [This](http://gazebosim.org/tutorials?tut=components&cat=get_started) tutorial explains these concepts.
