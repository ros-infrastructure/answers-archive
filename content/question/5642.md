    Unfortunately, not all the reference frames are viewable. In gazebo 2.2 and higher, if you select the `View Center of Mass / Inertia` from the View menu, then it will display pink boxes representing the size of a box of uniform density with equivalent mass properties, and green lines that represent the link frame. Also, you can `View Joints` to see axis frames for the joints. Please make a [feature request](https://bitbucket.org/osrf/gazebo/issues/new) on bitbucket if you need additional functionality.
