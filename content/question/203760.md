    > If I do ros::Duration::sleep does ros spin and are callback functions called?

    No. Sleep just sleeps the thread for the specified duration.

    > If not is there a way to pause the program while still managing callbacks?

    You can have a loop that alternates sleeping and spinning, as exemplified in [this roscpp tutorial](http://wiki.ros.org/ROS/Tutorials/WritingPublisherSubscriber%28c%2B%2B%29#roscpp_tutorials.2BAC8-Tutorials.2BAC8-WritingPublisherSubscriber.CA-2f0de5afcea33b164ff81dfbdb2ca623dfa24042_82), or you can have separate spinner threads. I recommend reading the ROS wiki page on [callbacks and spinning](http://wiki.ros.org/roscpp/Overview/Callbacks%20and%20Spinning).
