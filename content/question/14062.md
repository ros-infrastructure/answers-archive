  body_markdown: "You could not directly subscribe to ROS topics and use ROS messages\
    \ by a transport node. You have 2 solutions to choose:   \n1. Declare a ROS node\
    \ in your plugin and use it to subscribe to your ROS messages. And then fill your\
    \ transport messages one field by one field.   \n2. Declare a transport node in\
    \ your ROS node and publish your transport messages.    \n\nThe second solution\
    \ seems more convenient. I have not tried this way but I suggestion you tried\
    \ it and told us whether it is feasible or not."
