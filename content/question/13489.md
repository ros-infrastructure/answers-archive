  body_markdown: "I'm not familiar with the PX4 project, but you can add a camera\
    \ to the SDF file, and put it on a revolute2 or ball joint (so it can rotate X\
    \ and Y, or Z for ball joint if you want), and give them no friction or damping.\
    \ Gravity should just let it hang perfectly assuming you don't have any wind plugins\
    \ running.\n\nHere's a demo:\n[gimbel_demo.world](https://gist.github.com/PeterMitrano/a645f8047c353d2696a2de38053ab9e0)\n\
    \nUse the joint panel on the left to move the red and green links to see that\
    \ it does work.\n\nYou can add a camera to the blue cube. In your case, just make\
    \ the joint like I did directly to your quadcopter chassis. I only made the other\
    \ links to demonstrate the motion. "
