- body_markdown: "Just an observation: there is no \"world\" or \"environment\" in\
    \ RViz. RViz does not maintain any state. It directly visualises whatever it receives\
    \ on the topics it has subscribed to (or: the topics your Display plugins have\
    \ subscribed to).\n\nSee also #q382149 for a similar discussion.\n\n> Afterwards\
    \ the representations of the environment caught by the sensors are disjunct. \n\
    \nthis is not necessarily something you can say, as RViz does not contain any\
    \ \"environment\".\n\nWhat *can* happen is your localisation node(s) get(s) confused/stops\
    \ working properly due to wheel sleep (for instance). It reports forward motion,\
    \ but the wheels are slipping, leading the data visualisation in RViz to give\
    \ the impression \"your robot\" (really: a bunch of meshes rendered in a 3D space\
    \ by some visualisation tool) is still moving forward, while in reality (or in\
    \ Gazebo), it's actually stuck.\n\nThat's not an RViz problem. A 1D/2D/3D plot\
    \ would show the same thing."
