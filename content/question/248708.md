  body_markdown: "This error is usually thrown because the mico controller isn't seen\
    \ on your USB bus.\n\nFirst, make sure you've put the udev rule in your udev folder\
    \ and reboot.\n\nSecond, if you have a graphical interface on your machine you\
    \ could try to install Kinova SDK. It comes with a software called DevelopmentCenter.\
    \ Launch the DevelopmentCenter and try to connect to your robot. \n\nIf that's\
    \ also not a success. Try the following `lsusb` or `usb-devices` to check if the\
    \ controller is seen on the USB bus. If the controller isn't seen the most probable\
    \ cause is that your Linux config allows your computer to auto-sleep the USB ports\
    \ for power saving. You could try disabling this feature.\n\nIf all of the above\
    \ fails, I'd suggest you try the software on another computer to confirm that\
    \ the issue comes from your computer or the robot itself."
