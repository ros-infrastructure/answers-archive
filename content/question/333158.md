    You can take a look at [ndt_fuser](http://wiki.ros.org/perception_oru/Tutorials/Using%20NDT%20Fuser%20to%20create%20an%20NDT%20map), you can feed this information about odometry and that will help in mapping. To get odometry with IMU and encoder, you can use [robot_localization](http://wiki.ros.org/robot_localization).

    NOTE: You cannot use your encoder data directly for robot_localization. You will need to convert it into a twist message or Odometry message.
