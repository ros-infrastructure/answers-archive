    >     rawSignalArray = Float32MultiArray(data=targets)

    what is the contents of `targets`? `Float32MultiArray` is not a simple msg, it needs all of its fields properly setup for it to work. Refer to the [documentation](http://docs.ros.org/api/std_msgs/html/msg/Float32MultiArray.html). Only setting `data` is probably not going to work.

    Please also note: usage of msgs from `std_mgs` is discouraged. It would be better to use a more semantically meaningful message type (a `Float32MultiArray` could carry *anything*, not just a scan of a GPR sensor).
