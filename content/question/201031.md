    I also encounter the same situation. This is how I solved it :

    1) Convert your image to grayscale:
                    frame = cv2.cvtColor(frame1, cv2.COLOR_BGR2GRAY)
    2) Give the correct encoding to cv_bridge(in case you are using cv_bridge) :
                   pub.publish(bridge.cv2_to_imgmsg(frame, "mono8"))
    3) know your image size with : (frame.shape) and give that frame shape to your camera model yaml file.
    Hope this helps!!
