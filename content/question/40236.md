    The usual robot localization data is in the transform map->base_link. You need to [listen to this tf](http://www.ros.org/wiki/tf/Tutorials/Writing%20a%20tf%20listener%20%28C%2B%2B%29)

    Do note that the path traced out using this localization data may have discrete jumps as explained in the [REP105](http://ros.org/reps/rep-0105.html#coordinate-frames)
