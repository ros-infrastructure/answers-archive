  body_markdown: "Assuming you have the starting position `p_0 = (x,y,z)` [from ROS\
    \ message called `Pose` and it's field Position](https://docs.ros.org/en/noetic/api/geometry_msgs/html/msg/Pose.html)\
    \ and a list of `coordinates = [(x_1, y_1, z_1), (x_2, y_2, z_2), ..., (x_n, y_n,\
    \ z_n)]`, you can get the closest point by calculating a list of the lengths,\
    \ e.g. \n\n`lengths = [vector3_length(p_0 - p) for p in coordinates]`, \n\nwhere\
    \ you have your function for `vector3_length` \n(2nd root of the sum of squared\
    \ differences `x_0-x_i `, `y_0-y_i` and `z_0-z_i`)), then taking the minimum value\
    \ with its index (`enumerate` etc.) - use it to choose the correct closest point.\n\
    \nIf you want to get a more ROS-based answer, you should give us more material\
    \ - as mentioned by @Ravi Joshi in the comments, ROS Tutorials is an excellent\
    \ place to start."
