    Know it's been a long time but:
    You can't have an array of values, however, you can use a string with a comma deliminator between the values.

    For example

        gen.add("vals", str_t, 0, "A comma delimited list", "1,2,3,4")
    later on you can [break it into an array](http://stackoverflow.com/questions/14265581/parse-split-a-string-in-c-using-string-delimiter-standard-c) in your code. In python it's really ease, just use `you_string.split(',')`
