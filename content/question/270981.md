    Ubuntu 14.04 is not a target platform for ROS Kinetic. [Platforms targeted by Kinetic are Ubuntu Wily (15.10) and
    Ubuntu Xenial (16.04)](http://www.ros.org/reps/rep-0003.html#kinetic-kame-may-2016-may-2021). You have a couple of options:

    1. Use ROS Indigo or Jade with Ubuntu 14.04
    2. Upgrade to Ubuntu 15.10 or 16.04
