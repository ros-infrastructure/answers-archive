  body_markdown: "You have to enter 50,000 because you are running a while loop which\
    \ adds the current velocity to a \"distance\", publishes a command, and then immediately\
    \ repeats, with no delay. The while loop will run as fast as it can, repeating\
    \ `distance/linearVel` times (ie, 100,000 times) and then exiting, no matter how\
    \ far the turtle actually traveled. It just so happened that 50,000 was the magic\
    \ number for you, and it has no real relationship to the desired 5 metres.\n\n\
    I don't know where your idea came from to take time out of the equation, but please\
    \ forget it. Distance is always speed integrated over time. Never use anything\
    \ else. "
