    Note that `navfn` is a global planner, i.e. it computes that path from a start point to an end point, whereas `dwa_local_planner` is a local planner (as the name suggests) , which is used to avoid obstacles while following a pre-planned (from the global planner) path.

    Thus, you cannot substitute one with the other.

    Alternatives would be (amongst others):

    - for `navfn`: [`global_planner`](http://wiki.ros.org/global_planner?distro=lunar)
    - for `dwa_local_planner`: [TrajectoryPlannerROS](http://wiki.ros.org/base_local_planner?distro=lunar#TrajectoryPlannerROS), [`teb_local_planner`](http://wiki.ros.org/teb_local_planner) and others.

    You can change them by replacing the respective `base_global_planner` and `base_local_planner` [parameters of `move_base`](http://wiki.ros.org/move_base#Parameters) to the respective planner plugin name.

    About what plan are you talking now? Global or local?

    Anyways, the answer is the same. Choose your appropriate planner algorithm and implement that as the respective LocalPlanner/GlobalPlanner plugin to `move_base`. There is a tutorial about [how to do this for a global planner](http://wiki.ros.org/navigation/Tutorials/Writing%20A%20Global%20Path%20Planner%20As%20Plugin%20in%20ROS) on the wiki.
    You can also have a look on other planners how they are implemented.
