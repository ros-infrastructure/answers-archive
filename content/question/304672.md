  body_markdown: "Let say you want Link1 to have a ball joint with Link2. In this\
    \ case you have to add 3 additional links lets call them (temp1, temp2, and temp3).\
    \ These links should not have transmission (unless you want to control ball joint),\
    \ also you should consider very small mass for them, then it won't impact your\
    \ model.\n\nYou have to connect them in this way: Link1--> temp1 --> temp2 -->\
    \ temp3 --> Link2\n\nAll joints are revolute except the last one is fixed. Revolute\
    \ joints connected to other and can rotate over 1 axis only. "
