    Check out the **Changing driver settings** section of the [openni_camera](http://www.ros.org/wiki/openni_camera) wiki. The parameter you'd want to tweak is **depth_mode**. The Kinect openni_node driver supports VGA(640x480), QVGA(320x240), and QQVGA(160x120) resolutions of the point cloud, all at 30Hz.
