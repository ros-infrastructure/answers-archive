    [dynamic reconfigure](http://www.ros.org/wiki/dynamic_reconfigure) lets you turn off registration of the two images. Then you can use [rosbag](http://www.ros.org/wiki/rosbag/) to save the rgb and depth image topics for later processing. Or you could edit openni_node.cpp in the openni_camera package to store the images instead of publishing, but this is far more involved.
