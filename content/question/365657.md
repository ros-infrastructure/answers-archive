    I got the same error message (although in Noetic), and in my case the problem was caused by the system being unable to find the shared library, despite it being installed. I suspect it could be the same for you. I solved it by running `sudo ldconfig /opt/ros/noetic/lib/`. The problem is listed in a [troubleshooting page](http://www.youbot-store.com/wiki/index.php/Error_while_loading_shared_libraries) of the Youbot Wiki, but unfortunately it doesn't easily come up on Google.
