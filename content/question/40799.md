    The tf tree of the robot is not published by gazebo directly but by the [robot_state_publisher](http://ros.org/wiki/robot_state_publisher) node. Have a look at its wiki page. It explicitly states that all static transforms are future dated by 0.5 seconds. See [here](http://ros.org/wiki/tf/FAQ#Do_transforms_expire.3F) and [here](http://answers.ros.org/question/35908/using-tf-to-transform-a-point/?answer=35981#post-id-35981) for an explanation of the reasons for future-dating transforms.
