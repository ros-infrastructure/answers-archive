    It's a bit indirect and your package(s) may not be listed, but from [ROS Metrics Report extension](https://discourse.ros.org/t/ros-metrics-report-extension/4544) on ROS Discourse you can get to [awstats.osuosl.org/list/packages.ros.org](https://awstats.osuosl.org/list/packages.ros.org) which is the Awstats page for `packages.ros.org`.

    From there to [this page for December, 2018](https://awstats.osuosl.org/reports/packages.ros.org/2018/12/awstats.packages.ros.org.html) and then finally to the [full list](https://awstats.osuosl.org/reports/packages.ros.org/2018/12/awstats.packages.ros.org.downloads.html) for the *Downloads (Top 10)* section.

    `ctrl+f` on your package name should give you *some* statistics.
