    \ around and found this page [Rviz Troubleshooting](http://www.ros.org/wiki/rviz/Troubleshooting)\
    \ that explains two possible causes to your problem. Either your card does not\
    \ support OpenGL or you do not have the correct graphics drivers installed (or\
    \ both). For a new laptop, I would bet that you'd be safe with most modern GPUs.\
    \ If the GPU supports OpenGL, you're probably ok. You can get a laptop with a\
    \ dedicated GPU for very little money nowadays.\n\nIf it helps, I can run Rviz\
    \ on Intel Integrated GMA HD graphics in my laptop, but I also run it on other\
