    The `DiagnosticArray` message is defined in the [diagnostic_msgs](http://www.ros.org/wiki/diagnostic_msgs) package, which is contained in the [common_msgs](http://www.ros.org/wiki/common_msgs) stack.

    So, the reference should be to `diagnostic_msgs/DiagnosticArray.h`
