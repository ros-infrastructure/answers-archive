    you can try setting [`max_vel` parameter](http://gazebosim.org/sdf/dev.html#max_vel179) to 0, e.g.:

        <collision name="...">
          <surface>
            <contact>
              <ode>
                <max_vel>0</max_vel>
              </ode>
            </contact>
          </surface>
        </collision>

    this will prevent/reduce interpenetration error from injecting energy into the system.
