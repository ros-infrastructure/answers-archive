    You can find out how many nodes are publishing and listening to a topic.

    You'll need to create a subscriber to the topic, wait for a second or so for any publishers to connect then use the [getNumPublishers() method](http://docs.ros.org/indigo/api/roscpp/html/classros_1_1Subscriber.html#a05dda149862067795485d1ef7e5e390b) to check that it's being published.

    You can do the same trick with a subscriber and it's [getNumSubscribers() method](http://docs.ros.org/indigo/api/roscpp/html/classros_1_1Publisher.html#ad56c8e954020290dcb87cd6bbd330a2b).

    I don't know if there is a more direct approach, but I know this way will work.
