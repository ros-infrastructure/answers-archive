    Yes, the `ros1_bridge` package depends on a number of ROS 1 packages. These are not listed in the main ROS 2 installation instructions that you linked to, but in the ros1_bridge-specific instructions at: https://github.com/ros2/ros1_bridge/blob/master/README.md
