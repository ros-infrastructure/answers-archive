  body_markdown: "From [the wiki page, section 2.3](http://wiki.ros.org/ueye_cam):\
    \ \n\nIf you want to downsize the image while preserving the field of view, change\
    \ one or multiple of the 'sensor_scaling' / 'binning' / 'subsampling' ROS parameters\
    \ (e.g. set sensor_scaling=2 works in all uEye cameras that I've used).\n\nIf\
    \ however you want to zoom digitally into a portion of the image, you can tweak\
    \ the 'image_height', 'image_width', 'image_left', and 'image_right' ROS parameters.\n\
    \nNote that you can play with all of these parameters, and more, while running\
    \ ueye_cam live, using [dynamic_reconfigure](http://wiki.ros.org/dynamic_reconfigure)."
