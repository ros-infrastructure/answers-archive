What have I changed on top of the original files: 

 

In navsat_transfor_template.yamal: 

I set magnetic_declination_radians: 0.1717 

broadcast_utm_transform: true 

Why making the change? Unfornately, earth is not flat, magnetic fields come at an angel 

 

In navsat_transform_template.launch: 

I added 

<remap from="gps/fix" to="/fix"/> 

<remap from="imu/data" to="/imu"/> 

<remap from="odometry/filtered" to="/odom"/> 

Why making the change? Navsat transform publish GPS topic to /fix 

 

In turtlebot3_gmapping.launch: 

I added 

<!-- AMCL --> 

<include file="$(find turtlebot3_navigation)/launch/amcl.launch"/> 

Why making the change? I felt like I really need AMCl to navigate to unknown regions, I could be horribally wrong here. 

 
In turtlebot3_slam.launch: 

I added 

<!-- move_base --> 

<include file="$(find turtlebot3_navigation)/launch/move_base.launch"> 

<arg name="model" value="$(arg model)" /> 

</include> 

Why I made the change? I felt like I really need movebase
