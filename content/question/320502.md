    Unfortunately, Melodic doesn't support Ubuntu 18.10, it only supports Ubuntu 18.04 and 17.10. So, it looks like you will have to resinstall a different OS that is supported by ROS. As stated in [this answer](https://answers.ros.org/question/315206/installing-ros-melodic-in-ubuntu-1810/?answer=315209#post-id-315209)

    > [...] you can of course try building from source on 18.10 and/or use the Docker images available.
    >
    > It's just that no binary packages are provided for 18.10 by the ROS buildfarm.

    You can check [REP 3](http://www.ros.org/reps/rep-0003.html) to see what operating systems are supported by the different versions of ROS.
