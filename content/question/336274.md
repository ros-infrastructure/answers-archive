  body_markdown: "Hello !\n\nThis question is asked [here](https://github.com/Kinovarobotics/kinova-ros/issues/281)\
    \ and [here](https://github.com/Kinovarobotics/kinova-ros/issues/280), this question\
    \ is too focused on the Kinova arm and shouldn't be asked here but I might take\
    \ a shot :\n\nAs pointed out by @choco93 , you can lower the rate with [topic_tool\
    \ throttle](http://wiki.ros.org/topic_tools/throttle)\n\nTo go faster :\n\nThe\
    \ rate of the publisher is defined here : https://github.com/Kinovarobotics/kinova-ros/blob/master/kinova_driver/src/kinova_arm.cpp#L220\
    \ it's 0.1 by default.\n\nThe function here : https://github.com/Kinovarobotics/kinova-ros/blob/master/kinova_driver/src/kinova_arm.cpp#L690\
    \ should be called at the rate defined above.\n\nThe USB of the Kinova Arm is\
    \ limited, so you cannot go much faster (I had problem going above 15hz), you\
    \ can set the parameter `\"status_interval_seconds\"` to something lower than\
    \ the default 0.1 but you will hit the limitation of the USB pretty quickly\n\n\
    So you also might want to throttle the publication of other topic (I went to 50hz\
    \ with tool_pose and 1hz with the other topic without problem) but that require\
    \ you to setup 4 timer for the 4 publisher with different rate (and also a dynamic\
    \ configuration if you need to change the rate of different topic for different\
    \ use cases). \n\nI cannot say much for the ethernet connected Kinova Arm since\
    \ I didn't tested it.\n"
