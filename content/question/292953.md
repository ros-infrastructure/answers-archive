  body_markdown: "Make sure you are cloning the repository inside the `src` folder\
    \ of your workspace. \nFor instance, if your workspace is called `catkin_ws` and\
    \ that folder resides in your home directory, then the place to clone your repository\
    \ is `~/catkin_ws/src`. Then you have to invoke `catkin_make` from inside `catkin_ws`\
    \ and not `catkin_ws/src`. \n\nI suggest that you use `catkin build` instead.\
    \ I find it nicer and you can invoke it from within any subfolder of your workspace.\
    \ I like to think that it works somewhat like `git` in that particular respect:\
    \ works from any subfolder."
