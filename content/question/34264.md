  body_markdown: "You lose only the first few messages. This probably doesn't have\
    \ anything to do with buffer sizes but that subscribing takes a while. Your publisher\
    \ node always publishes something although the connection to your subscriber is\
    \ not completely established yet. This behavior directly follows from the way\
    \ ros works.\n\nNormally nodes are written in a way that this doesn't matter.\
    \ However, roscpp provides callbacks when nodes subscribe or unsubscribe (connect_cb\
    \ and disconnect_cb as shown [here](http://ros.org/doc/electric/api/roscpp/html/classros_1_1NodeHandle.html#a2d81fe5562417931bd7634490c364473))\
    \ and you can get the [number of subscribed nodes](http://ros.org/doc/electric/api/roscpp/html/classros_1_1Publisher.html#ad56c8e954020290dcb87cd6bbd330a2b)\
    \ from the publisher object. That way, the publisher can start publishing only\
    \ when the subscriber is present. \n\nLatched topics are used more commonly though.\
    \ With a latched topic, the subscriber always receives the newest message that\
    \ has been published right after the connection to the publisher is established."
