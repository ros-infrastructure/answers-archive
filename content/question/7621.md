    I confirm you that Gazebo 2.2 doesn't have DEM support. It started in Gazebo 3.0. You have a few options:

    * Implement the terrain using heightmaps. [Here](http://wiki.gazebosim.org/wiki/Tutorials/beginner/heightmap) you have an old tutorial that might be useful. Also, you can take a look at the heightmaps that we created for the VRC. [Here](https://bitbucket.org/osrf/drcsim/src/55bddb26dda1672eac2bc134f2f06b9327e26ad0/drcsim_model_resources/gazebo_models/vrc_heightmap_1/?at=default) you can see an example.
    * Install a newer Gazebo version. Take a look at [this tutorial](http://gazebosim.org/tutorials?tut=install&cat=get_started) because maybe it's not so difficult to update your Gazebo and you will be able to use DEMs.
