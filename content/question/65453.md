    To really understand how to use ROS for higher level functions, it is best to go through **ALL** the links in [http://www.ros.org/wiki/ROS/Tutorials](http://www.ros.org/wiki/ROS/Tutorials)

    I believe you have understood what gmapping [requires](http://www.ros.org/wiki/gmapping#Required_tf_Transforms) correctly. What you are missing now is the tf, so the logical next step is to look into the [tf tutorials](http://www.ros.org/wiki/tf/Tutorials) (which is under "Tutorials for other libraries" in the main tutorials page). After that, you might want to further understand how to use the [navigation stack](http://www.ros.org/wiki/navigation/Tutorials).

    A "shortcut" for learning how to use ROS for robots is to look for [robots](http://www.ros.org/wiki/Robots) with good documentation, like [turtlebot](http://www.ros.org/wiki/turtlebot_navigation/Tutorials/Build%20a%20map%20with%20SLAM). Then go into the launch files to see how things are done.
