  body_markdown: "This typically indicates that you haven't set up you CMakeLists.txt\
    \ correctly.\n\nYou are missing the Boost-related directives, try adding `rosbuild_add_boost_directories()`\
    \ and `rosbuild_link_boost()` to your CMakeLists.txt\n\nIt should look something\
    \ like this:\n\n    cmake_minimum_version(VERSION 2.4.6)\n    include($ENV{ROS_ROOT}/core/rosbuild/rosbuild.cmake\n\
    \    \n    rosbuild_init()\n    rosbuild_add_boost_directories()\n    set(EXECUTABLE_OUTPUT_PATH\
    \ ${PROJECT_SOURCE_DIR}/bin)\n    set(LIBRARY_OUTPUT_PATH ${PROJECT_SOURCE_DIR}/lib)\
    \ \n    \n    rosbuild_add_executable(simple_gripper src/simple_gripper.cpp)\n\
    \    rosbuild_link_boost(simple_gripper thread)\n\nFor more information on using\
    \ the CMakeLists.txt file, check out the [rosbuild wiki page](http://ros.org/wiki/rosbuild/CMakeLists).\n\
    \nThere are also some good examples on the [examples page](http://ros.org/wiki/rosbuild/CMakeLists/Examples).\n\
    \n\n"
