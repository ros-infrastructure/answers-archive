    \ comes from\n- `DEPENDENCIES std_msgs` in the `rosidl_generate_interfaces` command\
    \ (see [here](https://docs.ros.org/en/foxy/Tutorials/Single-Package-Define-And-Use-Interface.html#use-an-interface-from-the-same-package))\n\
    - Put `std_msgs/Header` in the `.msg` file instead of just `Header`\n\nThe relevant\
    \ piece of your CMakeLists should be then:\n\n    find_package(rosidl_default_generators\
    \ REQUIRED)\n    find_package(std_msgs REQUIRED)\n    \n    rosidl_generate_interfaces(${PROJECT_NAME}\n\
    \      \"msg/Num.msg\"\n      \"msg/Complex.msg\"\n      DEPENDENCIES std_msgs\n\
    \     )\n\nThen, also put `<depend>std_msgs</depend>` in `package.xml` if you\
    \ haven't already."
