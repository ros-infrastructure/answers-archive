  body_markdown: "I am not completely sure, but from what I could understand, the\
    \ registered data matches the depth information and relates to the **RGB** camera.\
    \ \n\nBy taking a look at the **/camera/depth_registered/disparity**   we can\
    \ see that it is slightly different from **/camera/depth/disparity**\n\n\n**/camera/depth_registered/disparity**\
    \ seems to be the image got from the rgb camera, showing only the part that is\
    \ visible by the depth sensor. It is useful when you want to relate the RGB image\
    \ and the depth points. Imagine you want to 3D scan an object. If you need to\
    \ know the color of that object, you should use **/camera/depth_registered** .\
    \ That is how the **/camera/depth_registered/points** might be built, as it kind\
    \ of mix the information from RGB camera and depth sensor.\n\n**/camera/depth/disparity**\
    \ contains the raw depth information from the depth sensor, though."
