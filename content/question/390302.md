    \nAt a minimum at the lowest rate you expect messages to come in / events to be\
    \ scheduled -- if you care about delays.\n\nIdeally at a much higher rate, as\
    \ delaying processing the event queue (ie: calling `spinOnce()`) will introduce\
    \ artificial delays in your event processing.\n"
