  body_markdown: "I believe that your problem is that you're not specifying the correct\
    \ namespaces for your parameters. TrajectoryPlannerROS will be in the correct\
    \ namespace, but your costmaps have no idea where to look for their parameters.\
    \ \n\nThe local planner is typically sourced with the rolling_window parameter\
    \ set to true, and the global planner's is static. Additionally, it might be wise\
    \ to specify the sensor frame of your laser scanner so that the robot can do the\
    \ necessary transforms for the incoming data. \n\nI would suggest looking at an\
    \ example such as the [erratic robot](http://www.ros.org/wiki/Robots/Erratic)\
    \ to get an idea of how to set it up more clearly. That way, you can be sure all\
    \ of the parameters are specified and in the correct namespaces. "
