    \ /etc/udev/rules.d/orbbec-usb.rules\n    \n    $ sudo udevadm control  --reload\
    \ -rules\n\nunplug and replug the camera to the usb port of the pi\n\n    $ source\
    \ devel/setup.bash\n    \n    $ roslaunch astra_camera astra.launch\n\nThe udev\
    \ rules of the astra camera has to be in the /etc/udev/rules.d/ folder with chmod\
