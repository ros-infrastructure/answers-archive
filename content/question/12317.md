    Max,

    Do you *require* it being a gazebo plugin?. If you can settle for a controller implementing the pr2_controller_interface, the [JointPositionController](http://www.ros.org/wiki/robot_mechanism_controllers/JointPositionController) class implements a single-joint PID position controller. Also, some time ago I extended the JointPositionController to a group of joints for convenience reasons ([code](https://github.com/pal-robotics/reem_controllers), not tested extensively). A word of caution: In these implementations updating the reference signal is non-realtime (plain ROS topic).
