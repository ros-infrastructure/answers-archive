Hi, I have a small (RPI based) robot with wheels that I have a RPI camera mounted on its body rigidly (although it does have a module for a camera mount with 2 servos). I have pushed the video feed to openvslam (monocular) so I can get a map of my play area. I have noticed since I drive my robot and feed the video to openvslam I see a lot of jitter when the car moves often causing the tracking to be lost. I am looking for a way to stabilize my video stream so I can still get good tracking and reduce the jitter when the car moves. Here is the actual robot: ![ROBOT image](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Freenove/Freenove_4WD_Smart_Car_Kit_for_Raspberry_Pi/master/Picture/icon.png)

Here are somethings I have tried:

 - Rigidly attaching the camera to body
 - Using sports mode in camera (works, but difficult in low light)
 - used vidgear stabilization: https://github.com/abhiTronix/vidgear/wiki/Real-time-Video-Stabilization#real-time-video-stabilization-with-vidgear (latency too high even on PC)
 - Set FPS to 30, 60, and 90 with marginal decrease in jitter (also set rate = the same value FPS)
 - Increased resolution to 640, 480 (I can do up to 720p but there is a small latency and I don't see much of a difference in feature detection)
 - Set video_stabilization to true
 - I even made some garbage shot absorbers using some springs on the wheels.

Is there any advice you can give so I can reduce the jitter from the frames. What else can I do to stabilize my camera such that it reduces the jitter?

Somethings I can try is maybe mounting either a light on the robot and using anti-shake mode?
