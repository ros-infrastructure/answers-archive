    I think you hit a bug in roscpp introduced 10 days ago. The `const char *` result of `mt::md5sum<M>(message)` is compared with the string literal `"*"` in [publisher.h:112](https://github.com/ros/ros_comm/blob/aa3adf0020e451005e5fea9ba9d9085fe87c676c/clients/roscpp/include/ros/publisher.h#L112). The warning also appears in newer versions of gcc (like 4.6.3 in a standard Ubuntu precise installation).

    Created a ticket: [https://github.com/ros/ros_comm/issues/165](https://github.com/ros/ros_comm/issues/165).
