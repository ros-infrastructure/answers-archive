    Yes, it is possible but you'll have to do the calculation. You can write a plugin that creates a [RayShape](http://osrf-distributions.s3.amazonaws.com/gazebo/api/9.0.0/classgazebo_1_1physics_1_1RayShape.html). The RayShape can tell you what entity the Ray intersects. Next, you would have to get the [entity](http://osrf-distributions.s3.amazonaws.com/gazebo/api/9.0.0/classgazebo_1_1physics_1_1World.html#a41eda6d6656a228d4a3f4b5b7d8e11f4), and use its pose information along with the entity's collision shapes to figure out the incident angle.
