    Changing major versions of a software package will likely come with changes to the API. It's not guaranteed that you will have to change your code, but you should be prepared.

    Take a look at our [versioning methodology](http://gazebosim.org/#status), and the [tick-tock model](http://gazebosim.org/#status) we use when changing the API.

    The latest major version of Gazebo is 6, and it comes with a slew of bug fixes and new features. A [migration guide](https://bitbucket.org/osrf/gazebo/src/default/Migration.md) is available to help upgrade your code.


    Without any more information about your project, we can only suggest to use the latest released versions of ROS and Gazebo.
