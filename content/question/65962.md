    You just start the [hokuyo_node](http://www.ros.org/wiki/hokuyo_node) laser driver on the Nao and subscribe to the topic `scan`, that's all. For that to work you will have to crosscompile ROS (or at least the relevant parts including the hokuyo_node) for the Nao.

    We also had some problems with the serial connection of the Nao's laser, so we connected the Hokuyo via USB instead.
