  body_markdown: "**Octomap is a compact way to encode the environment about obstacles\
    \ and even colors. These maps are very useful for self-localization, although\
    \ not so much to navigate when the robot is on the ground and there are no obstacles\
    \ in height that prevent the robot from passing through.**\n\n*Source:   \nJ.\
    \ Gin\xE9s, F. Mart\xEDn, V. Matell\xE1n, F. J. Lera, and J. Balsa, \u201CDynamics\
    \ maps for long-term autonomy,\u201D in 2017 IEEE International Conference on\
