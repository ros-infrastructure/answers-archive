    Hi @pmuthu2s,

    I've seen that you've tried to see it using  Markers in RViz in your [another question](https://answers.ros.org/question/279558/visualising-the-real-time-trajectory-path-using-markers/).

    By checking the answer in that question we can see that the Markers work, but you have to select the right `frame_id` when publishing it.

    Also, you have to make sure you are really receiving the messages of type `geometry_msgs/TwistStamped`. You can do that with `rostopic echo /TOPIC_THAT_PUBLISHES_TWISTSTAMPED_MESSAGE`

    Please, that a look in the [answer of that your another question](https://answers.ros.org/question/279558/visualising-the-real-time-trajectory-path-using-markers/?answer=279739#post-id-279739).
