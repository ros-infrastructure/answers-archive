  body_markdown: "The tf2 broadcaster is publishing the coordinate frames of the lead\
    \ turtle. A tf2 listener calculates the difference between the coordinate frames\
    \ of the lead turtle and the follower turtle. It then moves the follower turtle\
    \ so that it follows the lead turtle.\n\n      30         msg = geometry_msgs.msg.Twist()\n\
    \      31 \n      32         msg.angular.z = 4 * math.atan2(trans.transform.translation.y,\
    \ trans.transform.translation.x)\n      33         msg.linear.x = 0.5 * math.sqrt(trans.transform.translation.x\
    \ ** 2 + trans.transform.translation.y ** 2)\n\nWhere `trans` is tf from turtle1.\
    \ There is a good tutorial that explains this in more detail: https://automaticaddison.com/working-with-tf2-in-ros-noetic/"
