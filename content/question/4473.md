    The <image> geometry model is broken in Gazebo, see [this issue](https://bitbucket.org/osrf/gazebo/issue/847/geometry-does-not-work).

    Temperature is currently no implemented in Gazebo. The easiest solution is to write a WorldPlugin that outputs temperature data. [Example world plugin](http://gazebosim.org/wiki/Tutorials/1.9/make_world/control). Every update cycle, your world plugin could get the position of you robot, and then output a temperature value to a topic.
