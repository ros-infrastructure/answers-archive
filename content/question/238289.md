  body_markdown: "In short words: \n\nYes you can use an arduino to work as a ROS\
    \ node, there are good examples here [wiki.ros.org/rosserial_arduino/Tutorials](http://wiki.ros.org/rosserial_arduino/Tutorials)\
    \ showing you, how you can use an arduino to subsribe and publish to messages\
    \ f.e.\n\nWhat you want to do if your motors are driven by an arduino, is to programm\
    \ the arduino as a, i call it \"base_controller\". This way the arduino (running\
    \ as ros-node) could subsribe to the topic \"/cmd_vel\" and then \"translate the\
    \ subsribed message into commands to steer your drives connected to it.\n\nwhat\
    \ i expect you to do first, is to go through the [basic tutorials for ROS](http://wiki.ros.org/ROS/Tutorials),\
    \ so you understand the ROS infrastructure and what f.e. nodes are, what they\
    \ do (subscribe and publish messages, in generall) and so on.\n\nHope this helps\
    \ a bit, Fabian"
