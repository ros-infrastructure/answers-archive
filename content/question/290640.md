    There are two very mature packages I'm aware of that implement both visual odometry and SLAM using depth cameras such as the realsense. [Rtabmap](http://wiki.ros.org/rtabmap_ros) and [rgbdslam](http://wiki.ros.org/rgbdslam), both of these work with the 3D information that is produced by the realsense.

    You don't need to perform your own stereopsis calculations with the Realsense, they calculate depth themselves in a different way.
