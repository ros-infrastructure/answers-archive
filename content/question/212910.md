  body_markdown: "Hey I am also new to ROS, but I can answer to some of these questions:\n\
    \n1. It is not necessary to create a real kinect, you can just realize a simple\
    \ box. But the origin is important for your future tf, and to correctly link the\
    \ kinect to the rest of the robot.\n\n2. Give more details about what is the output\
    \ of your encoders.\n\n3. No, you still need the kinect to make it run on an known\
    \ map, because amcl node subscribes to this /scan topic provided by the Kinect\
    \ or laser you use.  ( take a look to http://wiki.ros.org/amcl )\n\n4. After mapping\
    \ you can give a position on your 2D map but you have to specify where is your\
    \ initial position on the map first. Look at the autonomous navigation tutorial\
    \ (http://wiki.ros.org/turtlebot_navigation/Tutorials/Autonomously%20navigate%20in%20a%20known%20map)\
    \ and the amcl_demo.launch for more details. \n\nHope it would help."
