    If you want to synchronize these topics by time, you need to use something [like `message_filters` package](https://wiki.ros.org/message_filters).

    These libraries are popular with working with point clouds:

    - http://wiki.ros.org/perception_pcl
    - http://wiki.ros.org/pcl_ros

    Especially [this one](http://wiki.ros.org/pcl_ros#pointcloud_to_pcd) and [this one](http://wiki.ros.org/pcl_ros#convert_pcd_to_image) are about converting point clouds to images.
