  body_markdown: "On shutdown, the `camera1394` driver sets video transmission off,\
    \ stops capturing frames, and frees the `libdc1394` resources. It currently does\
    \ not attempt to power off the device.\n\nAlthough `coriander` documentation says\
    \ \"few cameras support the on/off feature\", `camera1394` could support it for\
    \ those that do. If you think it should, please [open a Trac ticket](https://code.ros.org/trac/ros-pkg/)\
    \ for `camera_drivers`. There is time to make that change for the coming Fuerte\
    \ release. \n"
