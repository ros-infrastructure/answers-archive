    This is a non-trivial problem, two approaches doing this are implemented in [open_karto](https://github.com/ros-perception/open_karto) and [cartographer](https://github.com/googlecartographer/cartographer). For the latter, you can read about the clever branch-and-bound approach used in [this paper](https://scholar.google.com/scholar?cluster=1316991902388640452&hl=en).

    In gmapping, there is no explicit detection of loop closures, but map hypotheses with "badly" matched parts get a low weight and get sampled out.
