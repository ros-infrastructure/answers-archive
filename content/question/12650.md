    \ so when you launch the TurtleBots do something like this in your rolsaunch file\n\
    \n    <launch>\n      <group ns=turtlebot1>\n    \n        all your regular launch\
    \ stuff.....\n    \n      </group>\n    </launch>\n\nsee this page for more roslaunch\
