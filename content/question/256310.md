    Please see [wiki/Sensors](http://wiki.ros.org/Sensors) for a start.

    That is not an exhaustive list (read: there are tons more sensors that have ROS drivers), but should give you an idea.

    To quickly find out if something is usable with ROS: use Google, keywords: `ros $name_of_your_sensor`.

    > And in particular, how can I use a random USB Camera in my robot?

    Most (if not all these days) USB cameras are [uvc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USB_video_device_class) compatible, meaning that your Linux distro will most likely be compatible with them OOTB. Packages like [libuvc_camera](http://wiki.ros.org/libuvc_camera) and [usb_cam](http://wiki.ros.org/usb_cam) should be able to interface with such cameras.
