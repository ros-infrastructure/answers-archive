It appears that the method `virtual bool solve (const base::PlannerTerminationCondition &ptc)` is missing from `ompl::geometric::SimpleSetup` in the ROS package. [Here](http://ompl.kavrakilab.org/classompl_1_1geometric_1_1SimpleSetup.html) is the OMPL reference, and [here](http://ros.org/doc/electric/api/ompl/html/classompl_1_1geometric_1_1SimpleSetup.html) is the ROS reference without this method.

I am trying to run several planners in parallel, so I am modifying the call to solve in `ompl_ros_planning_group` (in ompl_ros_interface) to take a PlannerTerminationCondition object  as an argument so that all planners can be terminated as soon as one finishes planning.
