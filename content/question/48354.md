  body_markdown: "Hi, if I were you I'd look at the sr_remappers code. Basically you're\
    \ getting a vector of data from your glove and want to remap it to the shadow\
    \ hand. To do this you need to remap the glove space to the hand space (-> write\
    \ a matrix which will transform from one to the other). \nThe remapper should\
    \ subscribe to your glove topic and republish to the hand controllers:\n\n - /sh_ffj0_mixed_position_velocity_controller/command\n\
    \ - /sh_ffj3_mixed_position_velocity_controller/command\n - /sh_ffj4_mixed_position_velocity_controller/command\n\
    \ - etc...\n\nOnce you've written this remapper, you can just start the simulated\
    \ hand:\n\n    roslaunch sr_hand gazebo_hand.launch\n    rosrun gazebo gui\n\n\
    Finally start your glove node and start your remapper node.\n\nLet me know how\
    \ it goes. \n\nMay I ask how many DOFs does your glove have? At what kind of frequency\
    \ are you polling the data?"
