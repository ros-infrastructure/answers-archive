  body_markdown: |-
    It is not possible to subscribe at a given frequency, you have to filter the incoming messages yourself:

    If the topics have to be synchronized, use a [TimeSynchronizer](http://www.ros.org/wiki/message_filters#Example_.28Python.29-1) for your subscription.
    In your callback, check the last time you stored the messages and if it is more than 2 seconds ago, store again and store the timestamp.

    If your topics do not have to be synchronized and you just want the output to be logged to a textfile you can use [throttle](http://ros.org/wiki/topic_tools/throttle) to republish your topic at the desired rate and [rostopic](http://www.ros.org/wiki/rostopic#rostopic_echo) for logging:

        rosrun topic_tools throttle messages /topic 2.0
        rostopic echo -p /topic_throttled > topiclog.txt
