  body_markdown: "Your code seems correct, as long as the life time of your R_bc Matrix\
    \ is longer than the life time of your node. \n\nTo clarify this: if your create\
    \ the R_bc as local object in a small function and take the take its ref and than\
    \ leave the function your R_bc will get destructed as you leave the function.\
    \ If your subscriber later on calls the function with the pointer pointing the\
    \ destructed object, this will cause segfault.\nBut if you create your R_bc as\
    \ global object or in your main any its life time is longer than ros::ok() this\
    \ should work.\n\nHowever, you might also consider putting your R_bc in a class\
    \ along with your subscriber, see [http://answers.ros.org/question/121831/boostbind-errors-in-subscriber-callback-functions/]"
