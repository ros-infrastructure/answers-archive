  body_markdown: "answering my own question 2) :\nit turns out that it -is- possible\
    \ to hard-set the location and orientation of the turtle. This can be accomplished\
    \ by issuing the following command:\n\n> $ rosservice call /turtle1/teleport_absolute\
    \ -- [X] [Y] [theta]\n\nwhere [X],[Y], and [theta] are float numbers. \n\nFor\
    \ example:\n\n> $ rosservice call /turtle1/teleport_absolute -- 0.0 0.0 90.0\n\
    \nputs the turtle in the lower left corner pointed upward."
