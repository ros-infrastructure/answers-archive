    Error reads that a binary `rosrun` cannot find an executable `run_emvs`. In your `CMakeLists.txt` (Btw thanks for posting a detail of your setting. That helps):

        cs_add_executable(run_emvs src/main.cpp)

    This line is probably what you meant to define an executable. I've never seen `cs_add_executable`, then Google says it's for `catkin_simple`, an early variant of catkin, which I don't expect the tool you use, `catkin_tools`, supports. See [catkin's document for building executable (docs.ros.org)](http://docs.ros.org/kinetic/api/catkin/html/howto/format2/building_executables.html). Replace with `add_executable`.
