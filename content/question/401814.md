  body_markdown: "Okay so thanks to Joe, i found a solution to launch the slam program\
    \ with multiple turtlebots:\n\n1) Check that the TB clock is sync with your computer\
    \ one, if not use the command:\n\n    ssh [user_name]@[tb3_ip_adress] sudo date\
    \ -s $(date -Ins)\n\n2) On the turtlebot, go to `~/catkin_ws/src/ld08-driver/src`\
    \ and open the file `ld08_driver.cpp`. Find the line with `scan.header.frame.id\
    \ = \"base_scan\"` and change it to  `scan.header.frame.id = \"tb3_1/base_scan\"\
    `. Do a catkin_make after\n\n3) On your computer go to to your ROS repository\
    \ (in my case `/opt/ros/noetic`) and open `share/turtlebot3_slam/launch/turtlebot3_slam.launch`.\n\
    \nUnder: `<arg name=\"multi_robot_name\" default=\"\"/>`\n\nAdd: \n \n`<arg name=\"\
    get_base_frame\" default=\"base_footprint\"/>`\n\n`<arg name=\"get_odom_frame\"\
    \ default=\"odom\"/>`\n\n` <arg name=\"get_map_frame\" default=\"map\"/>`\n\n\
    Under: `<arg name=\"configuration_basename\" value=\"$(arg configuration_basename)\"\
    />` (in the include SLAM )\n\nAdd:\n\n `<arg name=\"set_base_frame\" value=\"\
    $(arg get_base_frame)\"/>`\n\n `<arg name=\"set_odom_frame\" value=\"$(arg get_odom_frame)\"\
    />`\n\n `<arg name=\"set_map_frame\"  value=\"$(arg get_map_frame)\"/>`\n\nAfter,\
    \ you just need to do:\n\n1) (on your computer) Launch `roscore`\n\n2) (On each\
    \ Turtlebot) Launch `ROS_NAMESPACE=tb3_1 roslaunch turtlebot3_bringup turtlebot3_robot.launch\
    \ multi_robot_name:=tb3_1 set_lidar_frame_id:=tb3_1/base_scan` (change tb3_1 with\
    \ your Tb name)\n\n3) (on your computer) Launch `ROS_NAMESPACE=tb3_1 roslaunch\
    \ turtlebot3_slam turtlebot3_slam.launch get_base_frame:=tb3_1/base_footprint\
    \ get_odom_frame:=tb3_1/odom multi_robot_name:=tb3_1` (change tb3_1 with the Tb\
    \ name you want to control)\n\nIt should launch Rviz and display the mapping.\
    \ There will be an error with the Robot Model, just go to Robot Model>TF prefix\
    \ and add tb3_1. \nWith this, i managed to do simultaneously a mapping with my\
    \ Tb #1 and my Tb #2 without any trouble (as for the Tb #1 don't forget to modified\
    \ the TF prefix in the robot model in Rviz for the Tb#2 !"
