    > when a node abnormal exited, that node still can be seen on the master ?

    yes.

    [rosnode cleanup](http://wiki.ros.org/rosnode#rosnode_cleanup) is a way to try and rectify such a situation, but it's not entirely without faults. See the linked discussion in #q9521.

    > How can I check the active status of that node?

    I would say the same way `rosnode cleanup` does it: [rosnode_cleanup()](https://github.com/ros/ros_comm/blob/94aaaec56e51840d8a39de383c6e89054af97e1e/tools/rosnode/src/rosnode/__init__.py#L454-L477). It essentially "pings" (xmlrpc) all nodes it knows about and any node that did not respond are considered dead, and deregistered.
