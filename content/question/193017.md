  - body_markdown: |-
      You just need to integrate the values into velocities and then again into position. The data is in the sensor frame, so you'll need to transform the velocities into the world frame before you integrate them into position. Double integration won't work well, though, just be warned. Trying to get position data from accelerometers and gyros alone is, IMO, a waste of time, unless you only need it over a very short period. But YMMV.

  body_markdown: |-
    I'm guessing your IMU puts out linear acceleration, angular velocity, and absolute orientation. While I have a branch of [robot_localization](http://wiki.ros.org/robot_localization) that will take in linear acceleration data, I should point out that the double integration of linear acceleration as your only source of absolute position will produce a *really* poor estimate. If you had some estimate of your vehicle's linear velocity, such as from your robot's wheel encoder odometry, then you'd definitely benefit from the linear acceleration data.
