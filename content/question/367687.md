    As explained in the [Building a Movable Robot Model with URDF tutorial](http://wiki.ros.org/urdf/Tutorials/Building%20a%20Movable%20Robot%20Model%20with%20URDF), `revolute` joints have strict motion limits.  For something like a wheel, you probably want a `continuous` joint.

    ----------
    UPDATE

    It's moving, so you almost got it!  However, it appears your wheels operate on the wrong axis.  When you give it a linear x motion, the wheels tumble (rotation about the wrong axis); when you give it a linear y, there's no motion (skid steer can't move sideways).  If you give it an angular z, I expect the wheels to tumble in opposite directions (correct command, wrong axis).  As suggested in [this Gazebo answer](https://answers.gazebosim.org//question/21826/how-to-use-the-gazebo_ros-skid-steer-drive-plugin/), it may help to read [the controller's source code](http://docs.ros.org/en/melodic/api/gazebo_plugins/html/gazebo__ros__skid__steer__drive_8cpp_source.html) and take an incremental approach to testing.  In this case, the controller is actually behaving as expected.

    The missing piece is that you can change the axis of rotation for a `joint` with the `<axis>` element as mentioned in the [`URDF/XML/joint` documentation](http://wiki.ros.org/urdf/XML/joint).  The default is rotation about the x-axis, which you observed.  What you want is rotation about the y-axis, so add `<axis>0 1 0</axis>` to your wheel `joint` definition.
