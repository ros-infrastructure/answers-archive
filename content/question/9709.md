I notice that some wiki pages using the `<<Version()>>` macro still display the Cturtle version by default. Since Diamondback is the current official release, I'd prefer that version, instead.

Examples:

 * [http://www.ros.org/wiki/image_view](http://www.ros.org/wiki/image_view)
 * [http://www.ros.org/wiki/camera1394](http://www.ros.org/wiki/camera1394)
