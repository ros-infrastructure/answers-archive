    \ reconsider your software design. In most cases is not needed publisher and subscriber\
    \ at one place (doesn't have to be your case).\nMostly: publisher is for sensor\
    \ and subscriber is for the actuator, for example you may want to publish the\
    \ data from rotary encoder from motor, and then you may want to send the data\
    \ to the subscriber to the driver of the motor. \nAt the very beginning the motor\
    \ is considered as an one part. But in the detailed view there are two parts:\
    \ the sensor and the actuator. And they can be launched via launch file in two\
    \ separate threads.\nBut again I don't know your intentions so doesn't have to\
