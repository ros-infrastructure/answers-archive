    **Finally a nice option exists a few years later :)**

    This is covered by [this issue](https://github.com/ros/ros_comm/issues/706) on Github. The [corresponding PR](https://github.com/ros/ros_comm/pull/1850) has been merged over a year ago and `Noetic` is shipped with the feature. It does so by repeating the latched topics (e.g. static TFs) for each bag.

    The option you are looking for while recording is `--repeat-latched`, so e.g.:

         rosbag record -a --repeat-latched --duration 5 --split
