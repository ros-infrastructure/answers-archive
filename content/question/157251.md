    Brian Axelrod wrote a dynamixel pro driver and controller. It isn't documented on the ROS wiki, but it is available on his [github account](https://github.com/baxelrod?tab=repositories), and it should be released into hydro as `ros-hydro-dynamixel-pro-driver` and `ros-hydro-dynamixel-pro-controller`.

    I haven't used these drivers personally, so I can't help you use them, and I'm not sure how well they work.
