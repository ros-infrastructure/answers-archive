    Hello, I'm the author of  [trac_ik_python](http://wiki.ros.org/trac_ik). In the Python wrapper we are just exposing the C++ interface. As far as I know, there is no documentation on what exactly the boundaries are, but, commonsense (and a bit of testing when I made the wrapper) makes me think it's the error/tolerance as you name them. Also the units should be the  inherent units of the position/orientation.

    So, in short, *bx, by, bz* should be in **meters** and *brx, bry, brz* should be **radians** (as it's the standard unit), and yes, the boundaries are the allowed errors.

    Also *brx* should be boundary on rotation on X, so **roll**. In the same case *bry*, **pitch** and *brz* **yaw**.
