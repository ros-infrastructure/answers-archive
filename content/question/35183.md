    You can use the [republish](http://www.ros.org/wiki/image_transport#republish) node to convert the transport from compressed to raw. Your call will look something like this:

        rosrun image_transport republish compressed in:=(in_base_topic) raw out:=(out_base_topic)

    I know this is an old question, but if somebody is looking for how to do this in a launch file the answer is here: http://answers.ros.org/question/12216/launch-file-how-to-write-it/

        <node name="republish" type="republish" pkg="image_transport" output="screen" args="compressed in:=/axis_camera raw out:=/axis_camera/image_raw" />
