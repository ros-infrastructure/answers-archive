    *Everything* in ROS is required/supposed/assumed to follow [REP-103: Standard Units of Measure and Coordinate Conventions](https://www.ros.org/reps/rep-0103.html) when it comes to things like units.

    So in the case of dimensions, the units would be metres (see [Units: Base Units](https://www.ros.org/reps/rep-0103.html#units)).

    Everywhere in ROS, so also in MoveIt.
