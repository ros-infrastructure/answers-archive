    \ what state information are you \"fusing\"? are you running object detection\
    \ or visual slam using the camera? the overlap between a 2D lidar and an RGB image\
    \ would be a line of pixels (or more likely due to the angular resolution of each\
    \ a dotted line of pixels). \n\nIf you have a feature detection in the RGB image\
    \ that corresponds with that overlap, then yes, its presumably possible to use\
    \ the LIDAR information to fuse or register the detection with the pointcloud\
    \ or scan. \n\nif the goal is just camera calibration, there are usually ways\
    \ that don't involve direct depth measurement (such as printing out a checkerboard)"
