  body_markdown: |-
    Non-fixed joints are not published to tf-tree by default.  One way to do this is to rely on the [gazebo_ros_controller_manager plugin](http://ros.org/wiki/pr2_gazebo_plugins#gazebo_controller_manager).  To make this work you have to provide [`<transmission>` elements](http://ros.org/wiki/urdf/XML/Transmission) to your model for each non-fixed joints.

    Or you can write a gazebo plugin to publish tf transforms for non-fixed joints, which might be a handy plugin to have around (or ticket this as a feature request).
