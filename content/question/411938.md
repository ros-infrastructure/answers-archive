My ros2 version is foxy, and I have installed [realsense_ros, ](https://github.com/IntelRealSense/realsense-ros)[slamtool_box](https://github.com/ros-perception/depthimage_to_laserscan/tree/foxy-devel) and [depthimage_to_laserscan](https://github.com/ros-perception/depthimage_to_laserscan).    

FIrst I start D400 and T265 node by

> ros2 launch realsense2_camera rs_d400_and_t265_launch.py  

Then I convert depth to scan:  
  modify depthimage_to_laserscan/launch/depthimage_to_laserscan-launch.py:

> remapping=[('depth', '/D400/depth/image_rect_raw'),
                      ('depth_camera_info', '/D400/depth/camera_info')]  

  and  in terminal

> ros2 launch depthimage_to_laserscan depthimage_to_laserscan-launch.py

Then start slamtool box

> ros2 launch slam_toolbox online_async_launch.py

But there is no /map data published.
