    \ with Robotiq grippers to perform assembly tasks. You can check [its moveit_config](https://github.com/o2ac/o2ac-ur/tree/main/catkin_ws/src/o2ac_moveit_config)\

    \ to see how to connect the robot and gripper.\n\nThe steps in short:\n\n- Add\
    \ the robot and gripper into a URDF scene \n- (Optional) In the URDF, define a\
    \ frame called `gripper_tip` as a child of the robot's end-effector or flange\
    \ link, offset it to where the tip of the gripper would be when closed. This is\
    \ useful to define your IK chain later. \n- Run MoveIt Setup Assistant, following\
    \ the tutorial and setting the end effector link to your `gripper_tip` frame"
