  body_markdown: "Hi!\n\nTo solve this, I use the following in the header of the CMakeLists.txt\
    \ of the package .. \n\ncmake_minimum_required(VERSION 2.8)\n\nproject(Name)\n\
    \ncmake_policy(SET CMP0054 OLD)  \ncmake_policy(SET CMP0045  OLD)\n\n"
