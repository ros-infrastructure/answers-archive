    I'm in the Boston area, and I have a Turtlebot2 with kobuki base and Asus 1025c laptop (teeny laptop).  Email me and you can come visit.

Same thing with my new apartment, but for a different reason.  This time, the moderate shag carpet hangs up the front and rear roller when it turns.  I'm working on this.
