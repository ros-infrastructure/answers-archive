    For the keyboard, you could load the [KeyboardGUIPlugin](https://bitbucket.org/osrf/gazebo/src/default/plugins/KeyboardGUIPlugin.hh), which publishes key presses on Gazebo topic `~/keyboard/keypress` and make a plugin which subscribes to it.

    For the mouse, you can add filters to catch when mouse events are registered on the GUI. [Here](https://bitbucket.org/chapulina/gazebo_plugins/src/dd9c7d7e7291b469984f22d5cbe3a13c1a905c39/Dominoes/DominoesPlugin.cc?at=default&fileviewer=file-view-default#DominoesPlugin.cc-101)'s an example.
