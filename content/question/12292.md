  body_markdown: "This error can occur when you have cyclical dependencies. Does your\
    \ package depend on a package that also depends on your package? Sorry for the\
    \ confusing wording, but I don't know of a better way to say it. \n\n**EDIT**\n\
    \nHere's an example of a cyclical dependency:\n\nPackage A is called `my_robot_config`.\
    \ Package B is called `my_robot_sensors`. \n\nIn the manifest.xml files:\n\n \
    \   my_robot_config\n\n    <depend package=\"my_robot_sensors\" />\n\n---\n  \
    \  my_robot_sensors\n\n    <depend package=\"my_robot_config\" />\n\nAll you have\
    \ to do is look through the manifest.xml files in your packages to make sure that\
    \ two packages don't depend on one another. "
