    You also have to specify rosbag as a dependency of your package correctly. The first example package I found where this is done is https://github.com/ros-perception/perception_pcl/tree/hydro-devel/pcl_ros. Have a look at the package [package.xml](https://github.com/ros-perception/perception_pcl/blob/hydro-devel/pcl_ros/package.xml) and [CMakeLists.txt](https://github.com/ros-perception/perception_pcl/blob/hydro-devel/pcl_ros/CMakeLists.txt) files, search for "rosbag" and add the analogous (basically the same) lines to your package.

    Also have a look at the related [catkin wiki pages](http://wiki.ros.org/catkin/CMakeLists.txt).
