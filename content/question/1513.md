    I think the minimum includes you'll need are:

        #include <gazebo/physics/Joint.hh>
        #include <gazebo/physics/JointWrench.hh>

    and use the function this way:

        physics::JointPtr joint = this->model->GetJoint("my_joint_name");
        physics::JointWrench wrench = joint->GetForceTorque(0);

    It's not a good example as it's convoluted, but the [AtlasPlugin](https://bitbucket.org/osrf/drcsim/src/ed05dbae3f24/ros/atlas_msgs/AtlasPlugin.cpp?at=AtlasSimBehaviorLibrary_1.0.3) makes use of the `GetForceTorque` calls to get forces and torques at wrist and ankle.
