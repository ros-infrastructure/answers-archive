    If you're looking to store 2D positions or poses, perhaps the [Point](http://docs.ros.org/kinetic/api/geometry_msgs/html/msg/Point.html) or [Pose2D](http://docs.ros.org/kinetic/api/geometry_msgs/html/msg/Pose2D.html) types in `geometry_msgs` could work?

    For `Point`, just ignore the `z` member. If you don't care about orientation, ignore `theta` in `Pose2D`.
