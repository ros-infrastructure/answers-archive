Hello all,

If I use the command line to publish a topic to an arduino, I notice that occasionally it seems to not work. For example, for the sake of simplicity, let's suppose that I am trying to control an LED via topic ```/led``` and message type ```std_msgs::UInt8```. Where ```rostopic pub /led std_msgs/UInt8 1``` is to turn on the LED and ```rostopic pub /led std_msgs/UInt8 0``` to turn off the LED. Usually, these command work no problem, and the arduino successfully toggles the LED. However occasionally, the LED is not toggled as expected right away. I have to publish the topic a second time, or even a third time, before it seems that the published method actually "goes through" and toggles the LED. Of course, the easy fix is to recursively publish the topic through the CL with the flags ```-r 10``` for example, but I do not want to have to do this. Any ideas what could be going on?

Thanks

Follow-up: I believe it has to do with the communication between my PC and the arduino. If I echo the published topic I can see that my PC is always publishing successfully, but the arduino does not always respond to this. Does anyone know if there is a certain way I can configure the rosserial communication such that this does not happen?
