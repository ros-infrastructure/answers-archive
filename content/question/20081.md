  body_markdown: "I figured out what I was doing wrong here. When I tried to specify\
    \ a link to the world, I didn't specify the model name. \n\nA link like the following\
    \ will prevent the link named `base` of a model named `arm` from moving relative\
    \ to the world: \n\n    <link name=\"world_fix\">\n        <parent>world</parent>\n\
    \        <child>arm::base</child>\n    </link>\n\nPlacing this in the root of\
    \ your `<model>` will then prevent the base from moving. "

I figure that there must be a method to hold the base of the arm static relative to the default ground_plane in the gazebo world but can't seem to figure it out.
