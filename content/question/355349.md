    \ figured your way out of this problem.\nI was facing the same challenge and I\
    \ will tell you the solution that is working for me.\n\nFirstly, rosbridge websocket\
    \ is compatible with ros/non-ros web interfaces. \nIf everything is working well\
    \ on your localhost, all you need to do is setup a VPN on the client side and\
    \ join to your bots network. For this, I assume your bot and your client side\
    \ has a stable internet connection.\n\nAfter VPN connection is successful, If\
    \ you are able to:\n\n    ping <your_bots_ip>\n\nThen, everything will work fine\
    \ for you\n\nyou can make use of [roslibpy](https://roslibpy.readthedocs.io/en/latest/)\
    \ to send/receive the data from the web interface. Remember the IP address you\
    \ will be sending/recieving will be the bots IP.\n\nTalking about the vpn, since\
    \ my client side was windows, I used [wireguard VPN](https://www.wireguard.com/).\
    \ However, if your client side is Linux/arduino, I suggest you to checkout [husarnet](https://husarnet.com/)\
    \ which is very easy to implement even if you have a non-static internet connection.\
    \  Hope this helps!\n"
