    Both gmapping/laser_scan_matcher and hector_slam are viable options that have been demonstrated to work well in different scenarios (see also this [Q/A](http://answers.ros.org/question/11945/ros-mapping/)). It might make sense to just try both for your setup and see what works best. Depending on LIDAR type, size/characteristics of the enviroment, available computing resources and other factors you might get better results with one approach or the other.
