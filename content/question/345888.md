    You may want to adjust the [collision and contact](http://sdformat.org/spec?ver=1.7&elem=collision) parameters for ODE (Physics Engine used in Gazebo).
    Particularly parameters `mu`, `mu2`, `kp` and `kd`. A value of 1000 for `mu` and `mu2` is huge and a `min_depth` generally speaking is not suitable for most Robot models.
