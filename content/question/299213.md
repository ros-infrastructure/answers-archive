    \ I would recommend looking into resources to better your knowledge of them before\
    \ attempting what you're trying to do (this is a good learning experience!). What\
    \ you likely want to do is convert the quaternion to RPY with TF or another equivalent\
    \ tool and base your logic for your application based on them to give yourself\
    \ a physical understanding of what's going on.\n\nAfter that, I would just make\
    \ a quaternion of the map frame orientation and compare against the robot's to\
    \ determine the relative difference to know if \"to\" or \"away\" from the origin.\
