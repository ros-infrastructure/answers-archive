  body_markdown: "Hi, \n\nUWSim is more of a nice renderer that takes either your\
    \ robot current pose or cmd_vel as an input.\n\nIn order to do path planning you\
    \ have to get a representation of the robot and its surrounding environment in\
    \ a dedicated planner.\nHow you fill this environment with information is up to\
    \ you. UWSim can publish 3D point clouds but you could just use the ground truth\
    \ when testing the planner itself.\n\nTo my point of view the resulting trajectory\
    \ should be visualized in RViz and not UWSim."
