  body_markdown: "Some debugging steps which might be helpful to pinpoint where the\
    \ system breaks:\n\n 1. **Verify your laser data**: Start rviz, add a LaserScan\
    \ display type, and set the \"fixed frame\" to `base_laser` (or whatever the frame\
    \ of your laser is). Are you seeing data?\n 2. **Verify your odometry**: Set the\
    \ \"fixed frame\" in rviz to `odom`, and add a \"tf\" display type. Is the `base_link`\
    \ frame moving as you would expect, reported by your odometry?\n 3. **Debug your\
    \ tf tree**. You can do that by running\n\n    rosrun tf tf_monitor\n    \nOr\
    \ even better, start `rviz`, and add a \"tf\" display type.\n\nThis is what your\
    \ tree should look like:\n\n - `map->odom` (published by amcl or gmapping) \n\
    \ - `odom->base_link` (published by robot_pose_ekf)\n - `base_link->base_laser`\
    \ (static, published by static tf published in one of your launch files)\n\nIf\
    \ any of these are missing, you need to address that specific node.\n"
