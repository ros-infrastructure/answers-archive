  body_markdown: |+
    In [fovis_ros wiki](http://wiki.ros.org/fovis_ros) it's stated that pose and twist covariance is not published for mono depth odometer.  That's why robot_pose_ekf is telling you that the covariance for the "vo" topic is zero. The library libfovis itself does not provide that information, and so does the wrapper.

    If you want, you can change *fovis_ros/src/odometer_base.hpp* to set a fixed covariance to do some tests.
