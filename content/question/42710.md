    The yaw for base_link and base_stabilized should be the same, it's only the roll and pitch angles that differ between both, as also described [in the setup tutorial](http://www.ros.org/wiki/hector_slam/Tutorials/SettingUpForYourRobot).
    You can use the [hector_imu_attitude_to_tf](http://www.ros.org/wiki/hector_imu_attitude_to_tf) node to conveniently publish the base_stabilized->base_link transform using a IMU message. I just added this to the tutorial, too.
