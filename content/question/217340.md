  body_markdown: |
    You can use [tf](http://wiki.ros.org/tf) for this. After you've received the marker pose, just apply a transform that translates it over the desired distance in the desired direction (API docs [here](http://wiki.ros.org/tf/Overview/Transformations), tutorials [here](http://wiki.ros.org/tf/Tutorials)).

    It might be worthwhile to immediately start using [tf2](http://wiki.ros.org/tf2) though, the successor to `tf`.
