  body_markdown: |-
    MoveIt is a motion planning framework not a full manipulation solution. That said,  I would recommend that you check out [moveit_grasps](https://github.com/ros-planning/moveit_grasps) as one option. Alternatively, I would recommend you look into GPD. There are a number of projects floating around that use that library with MoveIt including one from the [TAMS-group](https://github.com/TAMS-Group/moveit_gpd_pick_object) and a [ROS2 GPD + moveit project](https://discourse.ros.org/t/ros2-grasp-library-moveit-intel-openvino-initial-release-0-4-0/8285) from Intel.

    You may also want to explore [moveit_example_apps](https://github.com/ros-planning/moveit_example_apps) which has a pick and place application in it that uses MoveIt.
