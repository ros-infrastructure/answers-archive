  body_markdown: "Setting `LD_LIBRARY_PATH` will likely not do anything. That's a\
    \ runtime setting.\n\nThis is purely a \"CMake thing\". According to the [FindBoost.cmake](https://cmake.org/cmake/help/latest/module/FindBoost.html#hints)\
    \ documentation:\n\n> This module reads hints about search locations from variables:\n\
    > \n>  - `BOOST_ROOT`, `BOOSTROOT`\n> \n>    Preferred installation prefix.\n\n\
    You should be able to do something like:\n\n    catkin_make [all your other args]\
    \ --cmake-args -DBOOST_ROOT:PATHNAME=/path/to/your/boost\n\nThis might or might\
    \ not work, depending on whether the package(s) you're trying to build are the\
    \ first to look for Boost. If one of the dependencies have already done that,\
    \ and the result gets cached or exported, your dependents will most likely not\
    \ evaluate `FindBoost` again, leading to your `BOOST_ROOT` being ignored.\n\n\
    **But** in all cases: please be aware that mixing different Boost versions is\
    \ fraught with potential problems.\n\nMostly `SEGFAULT`s.\n\nYou cannot link a\
    \ ROS node to multiple versions of Boost, which is what will happen when you link\
    \ `abb_robot_driver`'s nodes against `roscpp` (which it must) and your custom\
    \ Boost version.\n\nUnless you've built the whole of your ROS Noetic from source\
    \ with that specific Boost version, I wouldn't recommend doing what you're trying\
    \ to do."
