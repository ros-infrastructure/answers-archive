    As an answer to your original question: the MoveIt setup assistant and rviz most likely crash because the STL files your URDF is referring to are only 80-bytes (most likely only STL header) and contain only '\0' bytes.

    This could be caused by the fact that you are using the student version of SolidWorks, which has been known to be incompatible with the exporter plugin. See [brawner/sw2urdf/issues](https://bitbucket.org/brawner/sw2urdf/issues?status=new&status=open) on bitbucket for a list of known issues.
