    There is no convenience function to get the CoG of a whole robot. You'll have to iterate over all the links in the robot, get the CoG, and compute the overall CoG.

    [Get all links in a model](http://gazebosim.org/api/dev/classgazebo_1_1physics_1_1Model.html#ae5db568f6f71777b45c36fdcb427956e)

    [Get the interial properties of a link](http://gazebosim.org/api/dev/classgazebo_1_1physics_1_1Link.html#a1f1f68da2f18d386111a7521c9aebcf1)

    [Get the CoG](http://gazebosim.org/api/dev/classgazebo_1_1physics_1_1Inertial.html#a6388ffbd1a49f64156209463c5fa2f8e)
