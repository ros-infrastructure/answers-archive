    If I am understanding what you are trying to do, there's a much easier way.

    If your IMU is mounted at the front of your robot, then take that mounting pose and [publish a static transform](http://wiki.ros.org/tf2_ros#static_transform_publisher), with the parent given as `base_link` and the child frame as `imu`. Then just make the EKF subscribe directly to your IMU data. The filter already knows how to apply those static transforms to the raw data.
