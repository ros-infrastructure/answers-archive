    I don't have a definite answer for you, but I would say anything that works for a computer should work on the Pioneer 3-DX.

    So I would tell you to maybe look into the [Microphone suggestions by MobileRobots/Adept](http://robots.mobilerobots.com/wiki/Microphones_for_Peoplebot_and_Pioneer_Audio_Systems), using the [Kinect Microphone Array](http://answers.ros.org/question/43143/capturing-kinect-audio/) or popular choices for that kind of application on PCs. You can also look into specialized Speech Recognition software, such as [CMU Sphinx](http://cmusphinx.sourceforge.net/).

    Good luck!
