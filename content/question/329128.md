I open a udp port on my ROSBerryPi 3B (kinetic) by :
> roslaunch rosbridge_server rosbridge_udp.launch port:=11000

and then I get connected with it using a Windows 10 laptop on which there is no ROS running.
I can succesfully communicate with the pc and raspberry pi afterwards. The problem is that when I disconnect my PC (by directly closing the terminal) I get the warning:
