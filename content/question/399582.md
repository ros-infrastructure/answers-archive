  body_markdown: "It's likely that you're starving your listener of time to subscribe\
    \ and receive incoming messages as you're continuously calling lookup_transform\
    \ and never letting the node process incoming messages. If they are coming in\
    \ enough you definitely won't get them all in the first pass while the buffer\
    \ is filling and you need to make it robust to retrying the queries after the\
    \ connections have been established to the different sources of the tf streams.\
    \ \n\nTake a look at the tutorials where there's a try/except block and a sleep.\
