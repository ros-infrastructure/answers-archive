I've been playing with the camera track_visual element.
I've setup to position the camera at a fixed position when follow is toggled: 

        <track_visual>
          <static>1</static>
          <use_model_frame>1</use_model_frame>
          <xyz>8 0 5</xyz>
          <inherit_yaw>1</inherit_yaw>
        </track_visual>

However, once the follow mode exited, the camera orbit mode (middle mouse button) behaviour changes.
"Normally" the middle mouse button let you ajust the camera yaw and pitch angle but once I enter and exit follow mode orbit also affects the camera roll angle.
