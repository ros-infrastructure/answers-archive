    While `rosserial` has been released into Indigo (see [debbuild/indigo.html?q=serial](http://www.ros.org/debbuild/indigo.html?q=serial) for instance), it appears it hasn't yet been synced with the main repositories, which could be why apt-get cannot find it.

    It *is* currently available from the `ros-shadow-fixed` repository, see [ros-shadow-fixed/pool/ros-indigo-rosserial](http://packages.ros.org/ros-shadow-fixed/ubuntu/pool/main/r/ros-indigo-rosserial/).

    So it appears we'll have to wait for the next 'sync', which will be announced on the mailing lists.
