    > I need to send information to ROS through a Service.

    Do you mean sending information to your ROS Service?

    Let's say you have a ROS Service server that needs a text message to serve a request. You can use the [ZeroMQ](https://zeromq.org/) library to send the information from your C++ code. There are various tutorials available online. You may also check [ros_zeromq_tutorial](https://github.com/ravijo/ros_zeromq_tutorial).
