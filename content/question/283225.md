  body_markdown: "The bare minimum is to get images from your camera and then publish\
    \ them as sensor_msgs/Image.\n\nOpenCV is good as a data intermediate format,\
    \ if you can get images from your camera and display them with cv::imshow or save\
    \ them to disk with imwrite and they look good then converting them to ros with\
    \ cv_bridge should be painless.\n\nThe next thing to do is use image_transport\
    \ CameraPublisher and use a CameraInfoManager along with it and you will be able\
    \ to get compressed image outputs and synchronized camera_info messages along\
    \ with the standard image. \n\nFor performance it is good to have the driver be\
    \ a nodelet.\n\nDynamic reconfigure support is nice for changing parameters that\
    \ should be adjustable live- exposure, frame rate, and anything else the camera\
    \ supports.\n\nBeyond that look at examples of existing cameras:\n\nhttps://github.com/ros-drivers/usb_cam\n\
    \nhttps://github.com/magazino/pylon_camera\n\nhttps://github.com/ros-drivers/pointgrey_camera_driver\n\
    \nhttps://github.com/ros-drivers/axis_camera\n\n(Is there a generic network or\
    \ security camera driver?)"
