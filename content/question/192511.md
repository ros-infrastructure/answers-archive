    You should also target at least ROS Hydro, since that is where rosjava/android really matured and became useable.

    You can use a [rosbuild workspace together with a catkin workspace](http://wiki.ros.org/catkin/Tutorials/using_rosbuild_with_catkin), or you can [catkinize](http://wiki.ros.org/catkin/migrating_from_rosbuild) the provided Dr. Robot package by rewriting the manifest.xml->package.xml and updating CMakeLists.txt.

    There may also be some dependency issues with migrating the code from fuerte to hydro, but from secondhand experience it's not bad, just stick to the migration [guides](http://wiki.ros.org/hydro/Migration).
