Hi there.
I'm using Ros Indigo with Gazebo2.

I modeled a car with two fixed rearwheels and two steerable (revolute joints) frontwheels using URDF.
When the simulation starts the car falls down in the front. If i reduce the mass of the chassis the car won't fall down in the front anymore but starts to bounce.
If I reduce the chassis mass to a small number (1kg) it almost stops bouncing.

My guess is, that this joint can only take a certain amount of weight.

How can I resolve this issue? After all my chassis weighs over 500 kg.

Thanks.
