    I made a very simple example of a 2WD robot with a Pi2 on it than can be controlled via ROS some time ago, see here: [simple_2wd_robot](https://github.com/skohlbr/simple_2wd_robot).

    On the ROS side, it listens to '/cmd_vel' [here](https://github.com/skohlbr/simple_2wd_robot/blob/master/simple_arduino_control/src/joystick.cpp#L60). The left and right motor commands are then computed on the ROS side and sent via rosserial_arduino. On the Arduino side, they are received and used [here](https://github.com/skohlbr/simple_2wd_robot/blob/master/simple_2wd_control_arduino/firmware/controller.cpp#L26). This is a very simple implementation that runs open loop. For accurate control, you'd probably want encoders on your wheels.
