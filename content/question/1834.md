    1) Yes, all of those topics will be available in the VRC.  The [User Guide](http://gazebosim.org/wiki/DRC/UserGuide#Published_topics) has been updated to include them.

    2) The `tf_static` and `tf2_buffer_server/*` topics are provided by the `tf2_buffer_server` tool, part of [tf2](http://ros.org/wiki/tf2).  This is an experimental tool, without any user-facing documentation.  In a nutshell, `tf2` is a more flexible, and, in some cases, more efficient implementation of `tf`.  The `tf2_buffer_server/*` topics constitute an [actionlib](http://ros.org/wiki/actionlib) interface, which is a sort of asynchronous request/reply mechanism.  You can use this interface to ask the `tf_buffer_server` for coordinate transforms without having to subscribe to `/tf` yourself.
