    It's because your gcc is not new enough. You either have to [fix some compiler flags](https://github.com/raulmur/ORB_SLAM/issues/108), [install a newer version of gcc](https://solarianprogrammer.com/2016/06/24/raspberry-pi-raspbian-install-gcc-compile-cpp-14-and-cpp-17-programs/), or [use Ubuntu Mate which comes with a (new enough) gcc 5.4](https://gist.github.com/nickoala/8d7e0bc24be3cec459e63bc1eb8cc858).

    I actually share my experience [building ORB-SLAM2 on Pi 3 here](https://gist.github.com/nickoala/8d7e0bc24be3cec459e63bc1eb8cc858). I am not sure how relevant ORB-SLAM2 is to ORB-SLAM the original, but I suppose they should not be too different. And I did not use ROS. If you stick with ORB-SLAM and/or Raspbian Jessie and/or ROS, then you will have to adapt. I hope my sharing will help lead you in the right direction, even though it may not be totally applicable.
