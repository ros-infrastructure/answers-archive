  body_markdown: "It appears that \"track\" substitutes yaw, or heading, hence it\
    \ would be more intuitive if that quantity were moved to the section \"# Device\
    \ orientation (units in degrees)\". I believe what is meant here is not the dip\
    \ angle as used by geologists - which would just be the complementary (depending\
    \ on the definitions) angle of pitch - but the local magnetic inclination, i.e.\
    \ the angle of the Earth's magnetic field lines relative to the horizontal, again\
    \ see [here](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dip_angle). This could be reinforced\
    \ by looking at [this explanation](https://gpsd.gitlab.io/gpsd/gpsd_json.html).\
    \ \n<br><br>Most GNSS receivers do not have a \"dip circle\" which would measure\
    \ this quantity. Nevertheless, this angle is frequently used in aviation since\
    \ it might cause the airplane's compass to give erroneous readings."
