    The easiest way to do this is to make your own node which [subscribes](http://wiki.ros.org/image_transport/Tutorials/SubscribingToImages) to the image topic, draws the markers on top of the image and [publishes](http://wiki.ros.org/image_transport/Tutorials/PublishingImages) it on a new topic. I do this for debugging vision algorithms on a regular basis.

    The image transport package can be used to receive and publish openCV images, openCV's basic drawing functions are described [here](https://docs.opencv.org/2.4/modules/core/doc/drawing_functions.html).
