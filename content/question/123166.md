    A few years ago when we were taking the PR2s to the Hack the Future event for kids, a few of us at Willow wrote the [pr2_hack_the_future](https://github.com/PR2/pr2_hack_the_future) stack, which includes a simple web interface, a script queue, and a simplified interface for interacting with the PR2: [pr2_simple_interface](https://github.com/PR2/pr2_hack_the_future/tree/master/pr2_simple_interface).

    It's currently undocumented, unmaintained, and out of date, but it's probably still better than starting from nothing.
