    [rqt](http://wiki.ros.org/rqt) is a framework for building ROS visualization tools.

    You may wnat to bring up the base rqt UI and play with [available plugins](http://wiki.ros.org/rqt/Plugins) to get a sense for what they can do.

    You may also want to go through the [ROS Tutorials](http://wiki.ros.org/ROS/Tutorials), one of the last and most-overlooked tutorials is [Navigating the Wiki](http://wiki.ros.org/ROS/Tutorials/NavigatingTheWiki), but it's also one of the best places for learning about specific ROS tools and packages.
