    When it's said that `odom` is useless as global frame it's because of the drifts. If your robot drifts then odom will know that you drifted but since `odom`is your global frame then "all your world have drifted" too. It's just a problem of frame of reference.

    That's why the frame `map` is used as global frame. Each drift caught by `odom` is sent to `map` thanks to `tf` and the transform between `map` and `odom` will tell you how the robot drifted so you will know where you actually are.
