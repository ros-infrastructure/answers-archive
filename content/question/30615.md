    \ will be filtered, but to what end? I am not sure you have enough information\
    \ (2D accelerations) to do much with. You will also need some method to sense\
    \ orientation, which is the most important. That is usually done with gyros and\
    \ a 3D accel or perhaps a camera (could give you a rough estimate).\n\nIf you\
    \ are trying to do some strap on inertial nav stuff, this won't be good enough.\
    \ The accel errors will grow without bounds and your pose will be junk. You said\
    \ you don't have wheel encoders, but what about [video odometry](http://www.ros.org/wiki/srv_vision)\
    \ or GPS to attempt to correct the drift. "
