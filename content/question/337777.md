    This functionality doesn't currently exist in moveit, but it is being implemented.

    As a workaround if you want an extremely simple PTP-like function, simply assemble a trajectory with the start and end points in joint space yourself, [use one of the time parameterization methods](http://docs.ros.org/api/moveit_tutorials/html/doc/time_parameterization/time_parameterization_tutorial.html) to time it, and then pass it to the move group to execute it.
