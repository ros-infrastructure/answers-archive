    \ this chip to the board before adding other parts. \nIt also has a different\
    \ scale (250 deg/s instead of 150 deg/s).\n\nThere are also a few other support\
    \ components (caps and a resistor) that are required. We're getting those fixes\
    \ into the latest parts package soon.\n\nAt the moment, we haven't found anything\
    \ which is pin-compatible and easy to hand solder."
