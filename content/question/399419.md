  body_markdown: "First MoveIt differs between pick and place, but they are similar.\
    \ For picking MoveIt adds a Step between 1 and 2 and after 3. In these steps,\
    \ you can define an approach and retreat movement in which the robot moves in\
    \ a linear motion to make sure to not touch the object in an unforeseen movement.\
    \ \n\nYou can find an example [here](https://python.hotexamples.com/examples/moveit_msgs.msg/Grasp/-/python-grasp-class-examples.html)."
