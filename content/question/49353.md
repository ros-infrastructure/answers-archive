  body_markdown: "An IMU is one of many sensors which can be used to estimate robot\
    \ motion through integration at high data frequency (dead reckoning) while external\
    \ pose corrections are not available (via GPS, landmarks, etc). \n\nSpecifically,\
    \ if your IMU contains an accelerometer and a gyro you may use it to estimate\
    \ linear displacement (by integrating accelerations twice in all three axes) and\
    \ angular displacement (by integrating angular velocities once in all three axes).\n\
    \nAs an added bonus, if your IMU also contains a magnetometer, you may use it\
    \ to correct the pose estimate obtained by the process mentioned earlier, if you\
    \ make some considerations about your own acceleration and the local gravitational\
    \ and magnetic fields.\n\nThe reason why you need external pose corrections is\
    \ that these sensors contain errors that, when integrated, generate a drift in\
    \ your estimate (i.e. your estimate eventually diverges because of random fluctuations\
    \ around the mean). So external pose corrections, even if not always available,\
    \ allow you to bound the pose uncertainty."
