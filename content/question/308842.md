    The [VelodyneScan](http://docs.ros.org/api/velodyne_msgs/html/msg/VelodyneScan.html) is just raw UDP packets, and the interpretation depends on which type of sensor generated the packets. There is nothing in ROS to convert backwards from [PointCloud2](http://docs.ros.org/api/sensor_msgs/html/msg/PointCloud2.html) to [VelodyneScan](http://docs.ros.org/api/velodyne_msgs/html/msg/VelodyneScan.html) because that's not really useful.

    I am not aware of anything that simulates the Velodyne laser scanners at the UDP packet level. It is much simpler to simulate the [PointCloud2](http://docs.ros.org/api/sensor_msgs/html/msg/PointCloud2.html) directly, and that is the standard format for 3D data. I recommend comparing real and simulated data using the [PointCloud2](http://docs.ros.org/api/sensor_msgs/html/msg/PointCloud2.html) format.

So cloud anyone help to told me that how can I transform or convert the [`PointCloud2`](http://docs.ros.org/melodic/api/sensor_msgs/html/msg/PointCloud2.html) **back to** [`VelodyneScan`](http://docs.ros.org/api/velodyne_msgs/html/msg/VelodyneScan.html)? Or is there already any ROS package or codes could do this conversion?
