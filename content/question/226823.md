    hector_slam performs localization only in 2D, but if you combine that information with IMU data (for roll and pitch) and additional data about the z position (from prior knowledge, odometry or other sensors), it can be used for 3D mapping. This approach is used for building a 3D octomap-based map via mapping with known poses using a RGB-D sensor with our autonomous USAR robots as [visible in this video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jgNFeDH3qFk).

    Depending on your accuracy requirements, sensor motion and scale of environment other solutions might be worth a look. See for instance [this recent video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jlAArM_6N-I) made using a Velodyne HDL-64e. [ethzasl_icp_mapping](https://github.com/ethz-asl/ethzasl_icp_mapping) also uses libpointmatcher and you could try that.
