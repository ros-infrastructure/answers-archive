 Hi all,

I know that this is a multi-times repeated question but unfortunately I didn't find a solution to my problem. I tried both teb_local_planner and neo_local_planner for my differential drive, back-wheeled robot, but when it reaches the goal, even though it may outputs "Goal Reached", move_base doesn't recognize the goal as being reached successfully, and moves on to the recovery behaviors before aborting the mission. I tried with less xy_tolerance but in my opinion it should work with 0.1m tolerance. I get the feeling that the reason may be that I send the goals in the base_footprint frame instead of map, but it would make it complicated to send it in the map frame. Any suggestion-opinion is welcome.
A similar problem identified here: https://answers.ros.org/question/240877/under-what-circumstances-will-actionlib-not-mark-a-movebasegoal-as-successful/

PS: As a side-problem and since I already uploaded my files, I would be glad if anyone identifies why my neo_local_planner periodically accelerates and decelerates instead of doing a smooth move. I believe it has something to do with the frequencies.
