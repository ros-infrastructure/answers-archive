  body_markdown: "Could You be more specific on the system You're running, please?\
    \ \n\nIn general case, after adding `source path_to_your/catkin_ws/devel/setup.bash`\
    \ to `~/.bashrc` it should work automagically on each start-up or new terminal\
    \ run. More on this topic: [step 8](http://nootrix.com/2012/05/ros-installation/)\n\
    \nAnother solution would be adding an [`alias`](http://www.thegeekstuff.com/2010/04/unix-bash-alias-examples/#more-4009)\
    \ for mentioned `source` call. Something like:\n\n    alias robot1_ws=\"source\
    \ path_to_your/catkin_ws/devel/setup.bash\"\n    alias robot2_ws=\"source path_to_your/catkin_ws2/devel/setup.bash\"\
    \n    alias robot3_ws=\"source path_to_your/catkin_ws3/devel/setup.bash\" \nin\
    \ Your `~/.bash_aliases` file. To `source` one of those workspaces You'd have\
    \ only to type `robot1_ws`, `robot2_ws` or `robot3_ws` in the terminal."
