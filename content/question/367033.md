    > I want to use RViz as my simulation [..]

    Just an observation: RViz is not a simulator. It's purely a visualisation tool. It visualises / renders whatever information it receives from other external components.

    From the rest of your question I believe that would be MoveIt in this case.

    > [..] and the only task it should have is to interpolate a planned trajectory

    RViz is also not a motion planner, so it won't do this either.

    It would probably again be MoveIt in your case.

    Suggestion: update the title of your question to better describe what your problem is. I believe you are actually wondering why your motion planner does not appear to take collision objects into account when planning a motion. That has nothing to do with RViz.
