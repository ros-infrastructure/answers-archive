I encountered some difficulty with installing ROS dependencies on Archlinux (via --dep-install). The output of the interactive `pacman` and `yaourt` sessions does not appear in full length in the terminal (the questions are not printed to the terminal). I managed to blindly answer the questions  by `yaourt` and `pacman` and thus install ROS diamondback on arch. I tested this on two different Arch installations.

Did anyone else notice this phenomenon, or even knows of a workaround?

Here is a screenshot of the output, notice the questions for the root password, and the questions whether to proceed with the installation - they are both not printed (pasting terminal output to another window shows that it's not just an issue of terminal colors):
