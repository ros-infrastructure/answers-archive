    ### Ignore dynamics

    If dynamics is not important to your application, you could try creating an animation. Check out [this tutorial](http://gazebosim.org/tutorials?tut=animated_box&cat=build_robot).

    Another option which disregards dynamics is [Joint::SetPosition](http://osrf-distributions.s3.amazonaws.com/gazebo/api/dev/classgazebo_1_1physics_1_1Joint.html#a5dd5194c47634dde3daf11344b120c32).

    ### With dynamics

    If dynamics are important and you don't want to do the force computation, your best bet is probably to do PID using the [JointController](http://osrf-distributions.s3.amazonaws.com/gazebo/api/dev/classgazebo_1_1physics_1_1JointController.html), but that requires tuning.
