  body_markdown: "Costmap filters are no longer applied with the inflation layer.\
    \ As the keepout / speed zones are meant to place a mask on specific no-go or\
    \ high cost areas, it didn\u2019t make sense for us to inflate them since they\
    \ themselves should be representative of the areas the robot should not enter.\
    \ \n\n"
