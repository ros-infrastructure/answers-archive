    Well first you can look at the [tf tree](http://wiki.ros.org/tf#view_frames) by running the following command:

        rosrun tf view_frames

    Than you can use `lookupTransform` from tf [here](http://docs.ros.org/indigo/api/tf/html/c++/classtf_1_1Transformer.html#ac01a9f8709a828c427f1a5faa0ced42b). This will give you the [stamped transform](http://docs.ros.org/indigo/api/tf/html/c++/classtf_1_1StampedTransform.html).
