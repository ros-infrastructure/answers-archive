    You can try using the `<collide_without_contact>` tag. [Here](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/arpg/Gazebo/master/test/worlds/collide_without_contact.world)'s a nice example world.

    You could also look at [collide bitmasks](http://gazebosim.org/tutorials?tut=collide_bitmask&cat=physics), in case you still want some models to collide with each other.

    (by the way, Gazebo 2 is not supported anymore, it's recommended you move to Gazebo 7)
