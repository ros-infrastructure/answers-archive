    The joint velocity limits can be set in the URDF like [here](https://github.com/tu-darmstadt-ros-pkg/taurob_tracker_common/blob/master/taurob_arm_description/urdf/taurob_arm_macros.urdf.xacro.xml#L14). They additionally can be overwritten in the MoveIt [joint_limits.yaml](https://github.com/tu-darmstadt-ros-pkg/hector_tracker_planning/blob/master/hector_tracker_robot_moveit_config/config/joint_limits.yaml) file. To change the speed on a per motion plan basis, you can set the two scaling factors for velocity and accelerations as described in [MotionPlanRequest.msg](https://github.com/ros-planning/moveit_msgs/blob/jade-devel/msg/MotionPlanRequest.msg#L37). Spinboxes for setting both of those are also available in the MoveIt! MotionPlanning rviz plugin.
