  body_markdown: "what you have will work. \n\nThe essential transforms are the map\
    \ to odom transform:\n\n*map -> odom* \n\nthe odom to robot transform:\n\n*odom\
    \ -> base_footprint*\n\nand then robot transform to the published frames of any\
    \ sensor data, which in your case is just:\n\n*base_link -> laser*\n\n\nMost robots\
    \ end up with a lot of static transforms, because their are usually many small\
    \ parts seperating the transform from base_footprint to whatever sensors the robot\
    \ has, so instead of *base_link -> laser*, you end up with transforms that have\
    \ more degrees of seperation, like this:\n\n*base_link ->chassis->laser_mount->laser*\n\
    \n\nBut those degrees of seperation aren't necessary, all that matters is that\
    \ navigation has a way to transform from the sensor frame to your robot's base\
    \ frame."
