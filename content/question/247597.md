  body_markdown: "**How to check your Xacro / urdf model**\n\nTo be able to check\
    \ your model, you have to convert your xacro file to plain urdf: \n\n    rosrun\
    \ xacro xacro.py -o model_out.urdf model_in.urdf.xacro\n\nThen you can use:\n\n\
    \ - **check_urdf:** `check_urdf tmp.urdf` see here for more details: [How to check\
    \ xacro syntax like check urdf](http://answers.ros.org/question/9208/how-to-check-xacro-syntax-like-check_urdf/)\n\
    \n - **gzsdf:** Gazebo comes with this tool to check the validity of an urdf model.\
    \ See here: [Gazebo ROS urdf](http://gazebosim.org/tutorials?tut=ros_urdf), part\
    \ \"Verifying the Gazebo Model Works\"\n\n\n**How to use your Xacro model**\n\n\
    To use your xacro model, you have to convert it first to plain urdf. The best\
    \ way is to convert it at the time you load you model into a ros parameter, so\
    \ your urdf model will always be up to date, as any modifications on the xacro\
    \ will be applied on next launch.\n\nSee here: [Using Xacro to clean urdf file](http://wiki.ros.org/urdf/Tutorials/Using%20Xacro%20to%20Clean%20Up%20a%20URDF%20File),\
