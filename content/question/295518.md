In ROS1, you could [retrieve a list of parameters](http://wiki.ros.org/roscpp/Overview/Parameter%20Server#Retrieving_Lists)

The documentation shows [get_parameter returning an ParameterVariant](http://docs.ros2.org/ardent/api/rclcpp/classrclcpp_1_1_node.html#adb7c3b1a45898860713d4f7e892a5fe7) but [ParameterVariant](http://docs.ros2.org/ardent/api/rclcpp/classrclcpp_1_1parameter_1_1_parameter_variant.html) doesn't seem to have an `as_vector` option.
