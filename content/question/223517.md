  body_markdown: |-
    `imu0_pose_covariance` is not a [setting](http://wiki.ros.org/robot_localization) for `ekf_localization_node`.  The covariances for measurements, including IMU data, come from the [sensor_msgs/Imu message](http://docs.ros.org/api/sensor_msgs/html/msg/Imu.html) itself. Check your IMU driver.
