    \ it out, so everybody with the same problem: here is my solution.\n\nThe file\
    \ \n\n> your_catkin_ws/sc/fmauch_universal_robot/\"your_ur\"_moveit_config/config/controllers.yaml\n\
    \nhas to look as follow:\n\n        controller_list:\n      - name: \"\"\n   \
    \     action_ns: /scaled_pos_joint_traj_controller/follow_joint_trajectory\n \
    \       type: FollowJointTrajectory\n        joints:\n          - shoulder_pan_joint\n\
    \          - shoulder_lift_joint\n          - elbow_joint\n          - wrist_1_joint\n\
    \          - wrist_2_joint\n          - wrist_3_joint\n\nYou have to change \"\
    your_ur\" with your robot (in this case UR5)\n\nHope that helps other people starting\
    \ with ros!"
