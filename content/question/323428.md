  body_markdown: "You don't actually need actionlib to do drive using only odometry.\
    \ You can subscribe to your vehicle's odometry, and publish a command velocity\
    \ based on how much your odometry thinks the vehicle moved. But beware that this\
    \ is subject to odometric errors like wheel slip, differences in the diameter\
    \ of both the wheels, quantization errors, etc. \n\n[Here](http://www.theconstructsim.com/move-certain-distance-turn-move-using-odometry-topic/)\
    \ is a tutorial that might help you get started."
