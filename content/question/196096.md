    Hello,

    I posted a similar question  a while ago, and not having found a good solution, i coded one myself (unaware of ros industrial package).
    My solution needs a way to recover a frame through the camera, like ar_pose, plus a marker that is attached to the end effector (no need to know where, only that does not move during calibration).
    I checked it with Asus Xtion + ar_kinect
    the solution gives the camera to base frame transformation (plus the position of marker w.r.t. the robot link where the marker is attached, a data that is normally discarded)

    see [camera_robot_calibration](https://github.com/gborghesan/camera_robot_calibration)

I was trying to calibrate my camera mounted on the robot base so that I can get the transform from base_link to camera_link. i was looking for efficient and fast ways to do this and I found the following [calibration algorithm](http://vision.kuee.kyoto-u.ac.jp/~nob/proj/mirror/) which was useful because I could fix my pattern on the robot base directly and use a mirror to make it visible to the camera.

After doing some tests with that, the results were totally wrong. I also tested with a webcam mounted on my pc and I couldn't get good results. 

How do the ROS community proceed with the extrinsic calibration of a monocular camera?
