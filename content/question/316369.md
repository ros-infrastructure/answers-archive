  body_markdown: "Assuming that you are running `roscore` on the local machine, open\
    \ a terminal and try the following:\n\n    $ ifconfig\n\nThis will show you your\
    \ ip address, among other info.\n\nOnce you find out what your ip address is,\
    \ in terminal type the following at the bottom:\n\n    $ echo $ROS_MASTER_URI\n\
    and\n\n    $ echo $ROS_IP\n\nEnsure that these match what you found in the first\
    \ step.\n\nIf not, go in to your /.bashrc and type the following\n\n     export\
    \ ROS_MASTER_URI=http://**your_ip_address**:11311 \n     export ROS_IP=**your_ip_address**\
    \  \n\nEither re-source your .bashrc or close terminal and re-open a new one before\
    \ running another `roscore`.\n\n   \n\n"
