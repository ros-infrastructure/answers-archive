    \ your function call is incorrect.\n\nHere's what the accepted answer says\n\n\
    > A shared_ptr to an object owning the\n> `sensor_msgs::Image` and the other\n\
    > \n> A shared_ptr to a `sensor_msgs::Image`\n> message You are providing neither\
    \ one\n> nor the other.\n> \n> In your case, the first option should\n> work,\
    \ which expects the image as first\n> parameter and the shared_ptr to the\n> object\
    \ owning the image (which is msg)\n> as second parameter:\n> \n> `cv_bridge::toCvShare(msg->color,\
    \ msg, \"bgr8\")`\n\nIn short, try changing\n\n    cv::imshow(\"view\", cv_bridge::toCvShare(srv.response.img,\
    \ \"bgr8\")->image);\n\nto\n\n    cv::imshow(\"view\", cv_bridge::toCvShare(srv.response.img.color,\
    \ srv.response, \"bgr8\"));\n\n(Change `srv.response.img.color` and `srv.response`\
    \ to whatever the equivalent is for your service message, since I don't know exactly\
    \ how yours is formatted.)"
