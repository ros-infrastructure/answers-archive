  body_markdown: |-
    [geometry_msgs/Twist](http://www.ros.org/doc/api/geometry_msgs/html/msg/Twist.html) is for velocities, [geometry_msgs/Pose](http://www.ros.org/doc/api/geometry_msgs/html/msg/Pose.html) is for position and orientation.

    [nav_msgs/Odometry](http://www.ros.org/doc/api/nav_msgs/html/msg/Odometry.html) contains both together with covariances (whereas designed for other purpose).

    If you just need Twist and Pose together, you can [easily design your own message](http://www.ros.org/wiki/ROS/Tutorials/CreatingMsgAndSrv).
