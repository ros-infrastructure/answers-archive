  body_markdown: |-
    Bullet is now a system dependency and is registered in rosdep as such. If you look at [rosdep/base.yaml](https://github.com/ros/rosdistro/blob/master/rosdep/base.yaml) you can see there is a key for bullet.

    Here is [the commit](https://github.com/laas/visp_tracker/commit/e7a93d69dc0ce415e57f3c02e638e92f2e1d53fa) adapting `vision_visp` to Hydro (removing the ROS dependency and detecting Bullet with pkg-config instead).

    Chances are that you will probably need an equivalent commit to adapt your own software to Hydro.
