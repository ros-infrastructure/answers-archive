    \ msg) \n {\n \n    for(i=0;i<16;i++)\n    {\n        datax=msg->points[i].x;\n\
    \        datay=msg->points[i].y;\n        ROS_INFO(\"[%f]\",datax);\n        ROS_INFO(\"\
    [%f]\",datay);\n    }\n    ROS_INFO(\"....\");\n}`\n you can refer to the sensor_msgs/PointCloud\
    \ for details.  \n"
