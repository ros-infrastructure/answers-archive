Inspired by [this thread](http://answers.ros.org/question/101441/can-catkincmake-detect-the-version-of-a-dependency/), I'm trying to utilize `version_gte` defined in [REP-0127](http://www.ros.org/reps/rep-0127.html#various).
I created an empty package beginner_tutorials from [a tutorial](http://wiki.ros.org/ROS/Tutorials/catkin/CreatingPackage), then placed in `package.xml`:

      <build_depend version_gte="1.10.3" version_lt="2.0">roscpp</build_depend>

where `$ rosversion roscpp` returns `1.10.2`, expecting build would fail. But `catkin_make`-ing (tried `install` too) doesn't not even yield warning and just passed normally. Am I missing something?

---
**Update**) Answer from @Dirk Thomas gong something in me, and it was actually the following that was missing.

    catkin_package(
      CATKIN_DEPENDS roscpp
    )
