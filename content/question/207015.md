  body_markdown: "You must push down the nodes publishing odometry data into their\
    \ own namespaces.\n\nIt sounds to me that you have two turtlebots. Let's call\
    \ them turtle1 and turtle2. They are both publishing odometry data to the same\
    \ topic. Let's call that topic \"odom\" in the global namespace /odom. What you\
    \ want is each turtlebot to publish on that topic in its own namespace. /turtle1/odom\
    \ and /turtle2/odom, respectively. \n\n So in your launch file do something to\
    \ the effect of: \n\n \n\n     <node ns=\"turtle1\" name=\"odom_pub_turtle1\"\
    \ pkg=\"your_package\" type=\"your_node\" />\n     <node ns=\"turtle2\" name=\"\
    odom_pub_turtle1\" pkg=\"your_package\" type=\"your_node\" />\n"
