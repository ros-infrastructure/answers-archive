    I tend to create another xacro that describes the assembly

    The assembly creates just the two objects/robots, and one joint with `type="fixed"`.

    You need to choose one link on each part where they will connect, and use the `<origin ...` tag to set the orientation of the camera.
    I'll take a guess that the `<child link= ...` will be `camera_base`

    Doing it this way allows you to use unmodified parts in separate packages, and  maintain very small custom code that brings everything together
