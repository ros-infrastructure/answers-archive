I am working with ros-melodic. I was trying to use tf_remap to change the frame_id "odom" from a bagfile I am running to something like "odom_ignore". In a launch file of mine, I have the following lines:

      <node pkg="tf" type="tf_remap" name="tf_remapper" output="screen">
          <rosparam param="mappings">
              [{old: odom, new: odom_ignore}]
          </rosparam>
      </node>

As well, when running my bagfile I make sure to remap the /tf topic to /tf_old:

    rosbag play --clock my_bag.bag /tf:=/tf_old

After starting the bagfile and running my launch file, I even get the output:

    Applying the following mappings to incoming tf frame ids {'odom': 'odom_ignore'}

However, running `rosrun tf view_frames` shows no such remapping in my tf tree. A related ticket on the issue [here](https://github.com/dfki-ric/mir_robot/issues/22) unhelpfully assumes that the /tf topic is simply not being remapped properly. Has anyone else encountered a similar issue and might have a fix?
