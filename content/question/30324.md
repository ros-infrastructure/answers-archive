    If you use [gmapping](http://www.ros.org/wiki/gmapping), the robot's pose is published via a tf. You can use [tf_echo](http://www.ros.org/wiki/tf#tf_echo) to display this transformation:

        rosrun tf tf_echo /map /base_link

    You may also try out [hector_trajectory_server](http://www.ros.org/wiki/hector_trajectory_server) for saving and retrieving the traveled trajectory.
