    I found the secret sauce:

    in package.xml:
    <code>

        <run_depend>gazebo_ros</run_depend>
        ...
        <export>
          <gazebo_ros gazebo_media_path="${prefix}" />
        </export>

    </code>

    then in the world file:

    <code>

        <geometry>
          <mesh>
            <uri>file://name_of_mesh.dae</uri>
          </mesh>
        </geometry>

    </code>

    I had to change model:// to file:// here.
