    One option would be to add use the [initialpose topic](http://wiki.ros.org/amcl#Subscribed_Topics) to completely re-initialize your particle filter whenever you see a known fiducial.

    Perhaps a better choice would be to include fiducial measurements into [robot_localization](http://wiki.ros.org/robot_localization) as a measurement source. Then the filtered estimate from `robot_localization` will produce a much more accurate tf tree that `amcl` can use.
