    \ at in the error messages you're getting. \n\n    (device disconnected or multiple\
    \ access on port?)\n\nThere are specific commands for [logging inside arduino\
    \ code](http://wiki.ros.org/rosserial/Overview/Logging) using rosserial to solve\
    \ this exact problem. As shown below:\n\n    nh.loginfo(\"Program info\");\n\n\
