    This can happen when ROS_IP or ROS_HOSTNAME (only one of the two needed) are not set correctly on both machines. See also [lEnvironmentVariables](http://wiki.ros.org/ROS/EnvironmentVariables) or this [Q/A](http://answers.ros.org/question/9915/ros-mutiple-machine/), Searching for "ROS_IP" or "ROS_HOSTNAME" here or on google should also provide sufficient info to fix this issue.
