  body_markdown: |-
    In your launch file, just add:

    arg name="model" default="$(find your_package)/urdf/your_file.urdf.xacro"/

    param name="robot_description" command="$(find xacro)/xacro.py '$(arg model)'" /

    This should work.

    Or if you have just urdf file and not a xacro, add:

    param name="robot_description" textfile="$(find your_package)/urdf/your_file.urdf" /
