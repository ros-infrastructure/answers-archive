    Try [fovis_ros](http://wiki.ros.org/fovis_ros). It's a visual odometer that can use stereo cameras or a kinect.

    If not, [viso2_ros](http://wiki.ros.org/viso2_ros) can use either mono or stereo cameras to produce odometry.
