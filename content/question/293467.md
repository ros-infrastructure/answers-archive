    I also struggled with the RT_LIBRARY-NOTFOUND issue and managed to solved it thanks to @duviros 's answer from [#q207608](https://answers.ros.org/question/207608/error-while-cross-compiling-rospkg-for-arm/) (check it out his little tutorial helped me a lot).

    In ordrer to solve the RT_LIBRARY you have to add this line in your toolchain.cmake file :

        # Set the target architecture
        set(CMAKE_LIBRARY_ARCHITECTURE arm-linux-gnueabihf)

    Replace `arm-linux-gnueabihf` with your compiler (but it should be the same if you are cross compiling for raspberry) and you shouldn't get the error anymore.
