    Maps live in and are accessed through the [map_store](http://ros.org/wiki/map_store) node, which is backed by a mongodb instance. The map_store node has some service calls you can make to extract maps, but they aren't documented.

    Once you've made a map and loaded it with one of the android applications, you should be able to use the map_saver node in map_server to save your map to a file; take a look at the documentation for [map_saver](http://ros.org/wiki/map_server#map_saver)
