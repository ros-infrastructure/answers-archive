- body_markdown: "Glad you solved, you can post the solution as an answer to your\
    \ question for future readers.\n\nAbout the \"the reverse order should give the\
    \ opposite result\", the key to understand this in the [tf docs](wiki.ros.org/tf/Overview/Transformations),\
    \ where it says\n \n> \"The relationship between two frames is represented by\
    \ a 6 DOF relative pose, a translation followed by a rotation.\"\n\nIn your case,\
    \ to go from `base_link` to `odom` you apply a translation expressed in `base_link`\
    \ RF and then rotate. To go back, you first apply a rotation **in `odom` RF**\
    \ and then rotate. The two transforms are indeed \"opposite\", but as they are\
    \ not expressed wrt the same RF, it is not just a matter of putting a minus sign\
    \ in front."
