    \ accordingly and stop throwing the errors.  These may not all be necessary.\n\
    \nedit your bashrc to export the following.\n    \n    #In my case the boost 1.46.1\
    \ library was located in /usr/local/lib\n    export LD_LIBRARY_PATH=\"/usr/local/lib:$LD_LIBRARY_PATH\"\
    \ \n    #I also wanted to explicitly tell the ROS environment my boost location\n\
    \    export ROS_BOOST_ROOT=\"/usr/local/\"\n    export ROS_BOOST_VERSION=\"1.46.1\"\
    \n\nI then followed the intructions for compiling from source at [ros.org/wiki/electric/Installation/Ubuntu/Source](http://www.ros.org/wiki/electric/Installation/Ubuntu/Source)\n\
    \nYou may also want to preclean any package that you are recompiling.\n\n    rosmake\
