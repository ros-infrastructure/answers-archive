@nkoenig announced [in this thread](http://code.ros.org/lurker/message/20120925.160824.7c8d5f5f.es.html). 

To me, however, since `Gazebo` can work standalone without `ROS`, it makes more sense to delegate `ROS` related question to [answers.ros.org](http://answers.ros.org). Indeed there have been recently [many questions that are related to `gazebo` on answers.ros.org](http://answers.ros.org/questions/scope:all/sort:activity-desc/tags:gazebo/page:1/) that might have not captured enough attention from people in gazebosim community.
