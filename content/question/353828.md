    [`rclcpp::Node` inherits from `std::enable_shared_from_this<>`](https://github.com/ros2/rclcpp/blob/bf70ce15bf5e80a083b59bce39fa860ac47f4a8f/rclcpp/include/rclcpp/node.hpp#L72). You can get a shared pointer to a node by calling `shared_from_this()` in your subclass. There's more info about [`std::enable_shared_from_this<>` here](https://en.cppreference.com/w/cpp/memory/enable_shared_from_this/shared_from_this).
