  body_markdown: "The pose_follower has two parameters that control the goal tolerance:\
    \ \n\n`tolerance_trans` (default 0.02) and \n\n`tolerance_rot` (default 0.04).\n\
    \n\nSo if you have not changed these parameters, it is normal that your robot\
    \ is 5-10 cm apart from a goal when it \"reaches\" it.\nAnyway, keep in mind that\
    \ an error of 10cm is quite small and acceptable in many applications."
