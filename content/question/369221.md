For ROS2 Foxy Fitzroy:

In the following ROS2 documentation page (Concepts) https://index.ros.org/doc/ros2/Concepts/About-Quality-of-Service-Settings/  
it is said that "At the same time, developers are given the flexibility to control specific policies of the QoS profiles." 

So, does this mean that only the developers, who are making some changes in the code, would get the provision to control specific policies of the QoS profiles and NOT the end-users of ROS2 ? 
I mean, if I am using ROS2 for my robotic application, (and I am not modifying any ROS2 code) will I have the flexibility to change the QoS policies of specific QoS profiles ? Or I get only to choose the profiles and not touch the policies within those profiles ?
