    As documented [here](http://osrf-distributions.s3.amazonaws.com/sdformat/api/dev/classsdf_1_1Element.html#a061de481194c1f8c5d5ef5b99a85dada), these are the possible values for `required`:

    * `1`: Exactly one is required
    * `+`: At least one is required, may have more
    * `*`: Zero or more elements are accepted
    * `0`: Optional, not required, If present, there should be only one.
