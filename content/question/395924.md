  body_markdown: "> I write `gedit -/.bashrc` and terminator says `Unknown option\
    \ -/.bashrc`. \n\n`.bashrc` exists in your `$HOME` directory.\n\nBash recognises\
    \ a shorthand which refers to that directory: the `~` character (or: *tilde*).\n\
    \nSo the correct command would be:\n\n    gedit ~/.bashrc\n\nSo it's not a `-`\
    \ (ie: dash), but a `~`.\n\n"
