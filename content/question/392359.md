  body_markdown: "That's 3 very broad questions. I haven't seen any mechanism like\
    \ that ready-made. The differential drive you mention is by far the closest one.\
    \ I don't know if you have much more choice than to dive in and tinker with the\
    \ parts. \n\nIf I was in your shoes and wanted to simulate that robot, I would\
    \ probably try to find a working wheeled robot setup in Gazebo and change the\
    \ links so it looks more like yours. At least you'll have a starting point. I\
    \ can't recommend anything, but there are multiple [ROSCon](https://vimeo.com/378682713)\
    \ [talks](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lneQskNqhFQ&ab_channel=GoGoGadgetFlow)\
    \ about racecars that seem to have shared their work. It would be helpful for\
    \ future readers if you added links to what you find to this question (or answer\
    \ it later when you feel more confident).\n\nNote that MoveIt is for planning\
    \ and does not simulate your passive wheels. You will need to use Gazebo for that\
    \ (and don't expect much in terms of precision \u2013 contacts are difficult to\
    \ simulate)."
