    hector_slam uses LIDAR data for SLAM. One can emulate LIDAR data with a RGB-D or stereo camera, but the results will be inferior to those with a proper LIDAR as the field of view is much smaller and noise much higher. See also [this Q/A](http://answers.ros.org/question/33933/kinect-doing-hector-slam/). Interestingly, the results presented [here however look quite nice](https://defendtheplanet.net/2014/10/26/low-cost-hector_mapping-xtion-9drazor-imu-hardware-odometry/).
