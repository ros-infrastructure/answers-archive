    It seams that you are setting parameters in the wrong name-space for your node.

    If you look at [launch file of rtabmap](https://github.com/introlab/rtabmap_ros/blob/master/launch/rtabmap.launch#L247) you can see that parameters are set depending on `localization` parameter and it seams they should not be used by the user.

    Also you are setting `bool` parameters, but they are actually strings.
