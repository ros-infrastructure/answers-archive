    We don't have support for the R1610 variant yet I believe, but if you have RSI on your controller (and either a C2 or a C4) then the [wiki/kuka_rsi_hw_interface](http://wiki.ros.org/kuka_rsi_hw_interface) could be one option (readme [here](https://github.com/ros-industrial/kuka_experimental/tree/indigo-devel/kuka_rsi_hw_interface/krl)).

    Adding support for a new robot variant is relatively trivial, and consists of converting the meshes to a usable format (`stl` or `dae`) and modeling the kinematics using xacro/urdf.

    You can probably use the existing [kuka_kr16_support](http://wiki.ros.org/kuka_kr16_support) package as a template.
