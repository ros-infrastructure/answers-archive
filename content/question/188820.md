Looking around I have seen more people interested in the use of the Kinect 2 sensor, also thinking on the "Windows sending the Kinect data to Linux" solution, but I suppose that if it was done directly with ROS, this would simplify a lot of things.

I would like to ask if there is anyone who has the knowledge and may be interested in making a built to be used with the new versions of VS.
