  body_markdown: "Gazebo 1.4 will first attempt to find a `model.config` file. If\
    \ a `model.config` doesn't exist, then Gazebo will attempt to find and use `manifest.xml`.\
    \ \n\nThis is the behavior in the released version of Gazebo 1.4.\n\n`manifest.xml`\
    \ is being deprecated because it can be confounded with `manifest.xml` from ROS."
