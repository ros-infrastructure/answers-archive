  body_markdown: "Dependencies go in the sub-project build.gradle files.\n\nDependencies\
    \ for fellow sub-projects have the format (note you don't need any version specification):\n\
    \n```\ndependencies {\n  compile project(':fellow_sub_project_name')\n}\n```\n\
    \nDependencies for artifacts (jars) that are outside your gradle super project\
    \ (typically repo) have the format:\n\n```\ndependencies {\n compile 'org.ros.rosjava_messages:rosjava_test_msgs:[0.1,0.2)'\n\
    }\n```\n\nThe uniquely qualified name here (org.ros.rosjava_messages) is the directory\
    \ the artifact can be found. The name (rosjava_test_msgs) is the jar name. The\
    \ [) brackets tell it to pick up the latest release in that range (e.g. 0.1.6).\
    \ \n\nArtifacts can usually be found either in /opt/ros/hydro/share/maven or at\
    \ https://github.com/rosjava/rosjava_mvn_repo.\n\n"
