    That particular node is actually pretty well documented. From [wiki/depthimage_to_laserscan - Node - depthimage_to_laserscan - Published topics](http://wiki.ros.org/depthimage_to_laserscan#Published_Topics):

    > 2.1.2 Published Topics

    > `scan` ([sensor_msgs/LaserScan](http://docs.ros.org/api/sensor_msgs/html/msg/LaserScan.html))

    > &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; The output laser scan. Follows [REP 117](http://ros.org/reps/rep-0117.html), and will output range arrays that contain NaNs and +-Infs.

    If you follow the link to the `sensor_msgs/LaserScan` documentation, it should be pretty clear.
