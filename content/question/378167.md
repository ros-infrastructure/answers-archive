  - body_markdown: |-
      `turtlesim_node` only publishes the [turtle's pose](https://docs.ros.org/en/api/turtlesim/html/msg/Pose.html) to a topic--not an actual [transform](https://docs.ros.org/en/api/geometry_msgs/html/msg/TransformStamped.html).  When you run the launch file, `turtlesim_node` spawns one turtle automatically (use the [`spawn` service](https://wiki.ros.org/turtlesim#Services) to make more) and publishes the pose for `turtle1` (default name in the [source code](https://github.com/ros/ros_tutorials/blob/74cc182dce2bb7d221052d1d45fe4ad0e7705843/turtlesim/src/turtle_frame.cpp#L114)) on the `/turtle1/pose` topic.  The `turtle1_tf_broadcaster` node subscribes to that topic, gets the pose, and publishes the corresponding transform to the tf tree.

      Until you spawn a second turtle, `turtlesim_node` doesn't publish on the `/turtle2/pose` topic, so the second broadcaster won't get a pose and won't publish the transform for `turtle2`.  Therefore, the transform won't exist in the tf tree.
