Hi, 

I am using ROS Indigo on Ubuntu 14.04 for controlling a Kuka youBot. The robot, using the available [drivers](http://wiki.ros.org/youbot_driver), typically works okay. However, upon starting [sound_play](http://wiki.ros.org/sound_play) (a package for converting text-to-speech), through the following command,

    rosrun sound_play say.py 'Hello World'

the robot computer looses connection to the robot hardware and the driver stops responding (similar to the error reported [here](http://answers.ros.org/question/222172/))! 

I am wondering what might be the reason for this behavior, and the steps that I could take to resolve this issue.
