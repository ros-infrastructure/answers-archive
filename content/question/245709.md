But during this process it somehow fucked up my workspace. I have these two lines in my .bashrc 

    source /opt/ros/indigo/setup.bash
    source ~/catkin_ws/devel/setup.bash

Now the later overwrites the first one and if I change the order the same happens. This didn't happen before.
I could do 

    rosrun rviz rviz
and

    roscd 'some_package_in_my_workspace'
but now I can only do one or the other and if I want to do the other for example for

    rosrun rviz rviz

and for roscd I have to source my workspace.
