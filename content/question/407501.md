    \ for running chosen global planner on Turtlebot.\n\n> There are a few steps to\
    \ follow to run\n> your planner in turtlebot. First, you\n> need to copy the package\
    \ that contains\n> your global planner (in our case\n> global_planner) into the\
    \ catkin\n> workspace of your Turtlebot (e.g.\n> catkin_ws). Then, you need to\
    \ run\n> catkin_make to export your plugin to\n> your turtlebot ROS environment.\n\
    > Second, you need to make some\n> modification to move_base\n> configuration\
    \ to specify the new\n> planner to be used. For this, follow\n> the three steps(...)\n\
    \nYou have to modify `move_base.launch.xml` and:\n\n> (...) add the new planner\
    \ as\n> parameters of the global planner, as\n> follows: \n> \n>     <node pkg=\"\
    move_base\" type=\"move_base\" respawn=\"false\" name=\"move_base\" output=\"\
    screen\"> \n> <param name=\"base_global_planner\"\n> value=\"global_planner/GlobalPlanner\"\
    />\n> \n> \n> \n> Save and close the\n> `move_base.launch.xml`. Note that the\n\
    > name of the planner is\n> `global_planner/GlobalPlanner` the\n> same specified\
    \ in\n> `global_planner_plugin.xml`. Now, you\n> are ready to use your new planner.\n\
    \nYou have to modify it to use `carrot_planner`in the XML file -  `carrot_planner`\
    \ is already there in example plugins."
