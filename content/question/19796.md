  body_markdown: "The WorldPose() function return ignition::math::Pose3d which consists\
    \ of the position and orientation. You can get these like this:\n\n    ignition::math::Vector3\
    \ pos = this->model->GetLink(\"link1\")->WorldPose()->Pos();\n\n    ignition::math::Quaternion\
    \ rot = this->model->GetLink(\"link1\")->WorldPose()->Rot();\n\nSince the twist\
    \ is a combination of angular_vel + linear_vel you just combine the WorldLinearVel()\
    \ and WorldAngularVel() and you have it.\n\n "
