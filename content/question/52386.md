The [SimpleActionServer(ExecuteCallbackMethod) tutorial](http://www.ros.org/wiki/actionlib_tutorials/Tutorials/SimpleActionServer%28ExecuteCallbackMethod%29) has a CMakeList.txt inconsistency for Catkin/Groovy.

At one place it says:

> find_package(catkin REQUIRED
> COMPONENTS actionlib_msgs) Note that
> **CMake needs to find_package only
> actionlib_msgs** (not message_generation
> that is referenced implicitly by
> actionlib_msgs).

Later it says:
>find_package(catkin REQUIRED COMPONENTS actionlib message_generation)

I assume the second one was not updated and the first example is correct. Please confirm.
