    @TheHappyIrishman, It seems to me after reading your post that you are launching the sensor driver in your machine and it is searching the lidar in a port that does no exist cause you have connected the sensor. If you want to add a lidar to your simulation you can check [this](http://gazebosim.org/tutorials?tut=guided_i1&cat=) and [this](http://gazebosim.org/tutorials?tut=add_laser) that shows you the how-to. Furthermore you can also take a look at [this](http://gazebosim.org/tutorials?tut=ros_gzplugins#GPULaser) in which you can find they are using the hokuyo.

    Finally if you want to have a more realistic output from the plugin, instead of using the basic gazebo plugin you can use this [one](https://bitbucket.org/DataspeedInc/velodyne_simulator/src/master/) that produces proper pointclouds for your simulation.
