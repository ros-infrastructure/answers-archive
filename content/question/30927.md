    <p>I figured it out, the line</p>
    <p><em>tf.ExtrapolationException: Lookup would require extrapolation into the past. Requested time 431.621000000 but the earliest data is at time 431.960000000, when looking up transform from frame [/r_wrist_roll_link] to frame [/base_link]</em></p>
    <p>was causing the error, and I fixed it using the suggestion <a href="http://correll.cs.colorado.edu/?p=2069">here</a> where the steps they outlined were:</p>
    >I ran into this even though I am running a native Ubuntu install.  This can be fixed by adding a wait to the start of the call to return_cartesian_pose:

    >roscd pr2_object_manipulation
    >cd manipulation
    >cd pr2_gripper_reactive_approach
    >cd src
    >cd pr2_gripper_reactive_approach
    >sudo gedit controller_manager.py

    >Around line 1430, there is the line:
    >(trans, rot) = self.tf_listener.lookupTransform(frame, >self.whicharm+'_wrist_roll_link', rospy.Time(0))

    >Above this add:
    >rospy.sleep(0.5)

    >Save, and make the package again
    >rosmake pr2_object_manipulation

    >Since there's a sleep every time that method is called, the simulation will be a little slower.  You can adjust the wait duration to be just larger than the delay that you see on your machine. 0.5 works for me most of the time.

    This worked for me, however I also had to edit simple_pick_and_place_example.py around line 35
    <p><em>self.papm.call_tabletop_detection(take_static_collision_map = 1, update_table = 1, clear_attached_objects = 1)</em></p>
    should be
    <p><em>self.papm.call_tabletop_detection(update_table = 1, clear_attached_objects = 1)</em></p>

        except tf.Exception:

    instead of separately doing pr2 + table + coffee cup.  (The coke can in that world has our grasping hack on it, which makes it easier for the robot to not drop it once it's picked up.)
