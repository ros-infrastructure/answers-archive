    According to [REP 2000: Foxy Fitzroy (May 2020 - May 2023)](https://www.ros.org/reps/rep-2000.html#foxy-fitzroy-may-2020-may-2023), `armhf` (or `arm32` I believe it's called in that REP), is *Tier 3* on all supported OS (Ubuntu, Debian and WebOS).

    That means there are no binary packages available, and the `S` in the table means users are required to build them from source.

    > There are just a few ament_cmake ones

    that's probably because those are Python/CMake packages, which don't require any additional builds to be usable on `arm32`.

    You can also see this on [repo.ros2.org/status_page/ros_foxy_ubhf.html](http://repo.ros2.org/status_page/ros_foxy_ubhf.html): note there are almost no green squares in the `Fhf` column.
