  body_markdown: "With the current navigation stack, you have to either convert to\
    \ PointCloud, PointCloud2, or LaserScan (I would avoid PointCloud as that message\
    \ might go away some day and is superceded by PointCloud).\n\nThe sensor_msgs/Range\
    \ message is actually fairly new, and while you could certainly go add that capability\
    \ to the navigation stack, you would have to edit the costmap_2d, possibly AMCL,\
    \ etc, and so it is more straightforward to just write a node that creates a laserscan\
    \ out of your sensor data. \n\nNote however, that you may actually want to interpolate\
    \ in between your range finders or tune the costmap grid size, otherwise you may\
    \ have serious issues clearing the costmap if you don't have enough other sensors\
    \ on the robot.\n"
