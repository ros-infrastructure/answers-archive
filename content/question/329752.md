    Yes, if you add a RobotModel display in rviz and expand its options in the Displays panel, you should see a checkbox for "Collision Enabled". If you check it, you can see the collision models for your robot that were read from the URDF. A nice example of that can be found [here](http://sdk.rethinkrobotics.com/wiki/a/images/1/1d/Collision_model.png).
