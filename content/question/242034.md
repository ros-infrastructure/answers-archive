  body_markdown: "The problem is, that you never call `ros::spin` or `ros::spinOnce`.\n\
    \nTry replacing`gtk_main();` with\n\n\tauto t = std::thread([&]() {\n\t\tros::spin();\n\
    \t});\n\n\tgtk_main();\n\n\t// clean up the thread\n\tif (t.joinable()) {\n\t\t\
    t.join();\n\t}\n\nThat's needs at least c++11, otherwise you need to figure some\
    \ other syntax for running `ros::spin()` in it's own thread.\n\nBasically gtk\
    \ and ros both need a dedicated thread to loop and process events. Pay attention\
    \ on racing conditions between the two threads!"
