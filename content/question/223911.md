  body_markdown: |2-
     The timestamp is the the time of the first ray/distance measurement in a scan. Note this is also stated in the comments of the [sensors_msgs/LaserScan](http://docs.ros.org/api/sensor_msgs/html/msg/LaserScan.html) message definition.

    The time calibration performs some sending of messages back and forth between scanner and host similar to [NTP time synchronization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Network_Time_Protocol#Clock_synchronization_algorithm). There is some more info [here on page 27 of the UTM-30LX-EW protocol documentation](https://www.hokuyo-aut.jp/02sensor/07scanner/download/pdf/UTM-30LX-EW_protocol_en.pdf).
