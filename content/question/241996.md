  body_markdown: "Okay, I just accidentally found out how to get it while searching\
    \ for a way to get the **ViewController**.  \nYou get it as follows:\n\nYour class\
    \ should have a **DisplayContext *** property called ***context_***.  \nThrough\
    \ this property we can access the **ViewManager** by calling `context_->getViewManager()`\
    \ that can give us a reference to the **RenderPanel** by calling `getRenderPanel()`.\n\
    \nSo in short to grab the **RenderPanel** you simply call:  \n\n`rviz::RenderPanel*\
    \ panel = context_->getViewManager()->getRenderPanel();`"
