  body_markdown: |
    Try the following and see if it improves your situation:

     1. remove all `source ..` lines that you added from your `.bashrc`
     1. delete the `build` and `devel` folder from your first workspace (ignore your second one for now)
     1. in a new terminal, `source /opt/ros/indigo/setup.bash`
     1. run `catkin_make` (or `catkin build`, whichever you prefer)
     1. `source devel/setup.bash`

    Now see whether you end up in the correct location.

    The setup files are not magic, they basically just set some environment variables, and depending on what you `source`d before, those variables could / may include additional paths.

    By rebuilding your workspace you regenerate a fresh set of `setup.*` files, which, if you followed the proper procedure, should work correctly.
