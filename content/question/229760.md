title: Are Joint Lower and Upper Limits Required in SDF?
upvote_count: 0

According to [SDF v 1.6's joint limit syntax](http://sdformat.org/spec?ver=1.6&elem=joint#axis_limit), a joint limit's `<lower>` and `<upper>` elements are required ("Required: 1") but its text description states "Omit if joint is continuous".

![image description](https://ftp.osuosl.org/pub/ros/download.ros.org/downloads/se_migration/ros/14585718197335891.png)

Isn't this a contradiction? If the `<lower>` and `<upper>` elements can be omitted to support continuous joints, shouldn't the "Required: 1" be changed to "Required: 0"?
