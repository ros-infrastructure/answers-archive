  body_markdown: "ROS contains functionality for converting LaserScans into PointClouds.\
    \ Those functions are located in the [laser_geometry](http://www.ros.org/wiki/laser_geometry)\
    \ package. The function `transformLaserScanToPointCloud()` takes in a LaserScan,\
    \ a tfListener, and a desired frame_id and converts that LaserScan into a PointCloud\
    \ in that frame. "
