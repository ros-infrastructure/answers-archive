    The warning message is due to a change in transform-handling behavior. In short, it used to be the case that a redundant transform (one with the same timestamp and frame_ids as a previously received transform) would be added to the transform cache alongside the original one. This was apparently not intended behavior, and as of noetic redundant transforms are ignored and a warning is printed (see this [issue](https://github.com/ros/geometry2/issues/414) for more details).
    You are definitely not the only one to encounter this, as evidenced by this [discussion](https://github.com/ros/geometry2/issues/467).
    It's up to you whether to try to hunt down the source of the redundant transforms in your setup and fix it or to just ignore the warnings.
