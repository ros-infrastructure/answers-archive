  body_markdown: |-
    Contacts usually stabilize when you set a non-zero min_depth.

    Adding this to your base link does the trick:

        <collision name="collision">
          ...
          <surface>
            ...
            <contact>
              <ode>
                <min_depth>0.001</min_depth>
              </ode>
            </contact>
          </surface>
        </collision>
