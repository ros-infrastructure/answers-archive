    [cereal_port](http://ros.org/wiki/cereal_port) is part of the [serial_communication](http://ros.org/wiki/serial_communication) stack.

    From the documentation, it looks like the serial_communication stack is no longer maintained, and is not available in Groovy. You may be able to build it from source, or contact the maintainers to get it released into Groovy.

    As an alternative, the [serial](http://williamjwoodall.com/serial/) library is available in Groovy and should do what you need.
