  body_markdown: "You can use the old way they used to do things.  I was also in the\
    \ situation where I absolutely could not use RosActivity (since I was already\
    \ extending).  I dug in to their ROSActivity and a few of their tutorials to find\
    \ the below.  Hope it helps.  \n\nThe method I currently use is:\n\n    nodeConfiguration\
    \ = NodeConfiguration\n\t\t\t\t\t\t\t.newPublic(InetAddressFactory.newNonLoopback()\n\
    \t\t\t\t\t\t\t\t\t.getHostAddress());\n\n\tString hostMaster = prefs.getString(\"\
    ros_IP\", \"\");\n\tInteger port = Integer.parseInt(prefs.getString(\"ros_port\"\
    ,\n\t\t\t\t\t\t\t\"\"));\n\tURI uri = URI.create(\"http://\" + hostMaster + \"\
    :\" + port);\n\tnodeConfiguration.setMasterUri(uri);\n\n\tnodeMainExecutor = DefaultNodeMainExecutor.newDefault();\n\
    \    nodeMainExecutor.execute(<NAMEOFNODECLASS>, nodeConfiguration);\n\nI am using\
    \ a preference menu to hold the hostMaster IP and port, but you can change those\
    \ directly.\n\nI have been working with ROSJava a lot lately and have been looking\
    \ for some people who may be more involved in it to hit questions back and forth.\
    \  I hope this helps you."
