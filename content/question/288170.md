    You need to have the ROS Environment sourced in the terminal where you run this.
    Follow the tutorial about  [Setting up a ROS Workspace](http://wiki.ros.org/ROS/Tutorials/InstallingandConfiguringROSEnvironment#Create_a_ROS_Workspace).

    Remember to call `source <PATH_TO_YOUR_WORKSPACE>/devel/setup.bash` in every new terminal you open.
