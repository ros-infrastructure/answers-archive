  body_markdown: "Here is what I found \n1.For Links the MI is centered at the COG\
    \ and hence we need to find the MI at the COG w.r.t. the base frame.\n2.For links\
    \ connected at joints we must find the MI at the origin of the Joint of the child\
    \ link. \n3. Also after entering the MI values do not forget to set unit mass\
    \ AND divide the MI matrix by the mass. After this [C:\\fakepath\\Hex_Mass_Correct.png](https://ftp.osuosl.org/pub/ros/download.ros.org/downloads/se_migration/gazebosim/15241673269052774.png)\
