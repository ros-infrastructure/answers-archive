    You cannot use `ur_modern_driver` with e-series controllers right now.

    There is an open pull request ([ros-industrial/ur_modern_driver#216](https://github.com/ros-industrial/ur_modern_driver/pull/216)) which adds preliminary support, but the current focus is on the new driver for CB3 robots and e-series robots announced last year ([presentation](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gjrnOdymUUE)).

    Either apply for the closed beta test, or use the PR with your UR5e.
