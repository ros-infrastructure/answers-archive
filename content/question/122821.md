    [hector_slam](http://wiki.ros.org/hector_slam) provides localization and mapping capabilities and does not perform detection and tracking of QR codes. These two tasks are performed mainly by the [hector_qrcode_detection](https://github.com/tu-darmstadt-ros-pkg/hector_vision/tree/master/hector_qrcode_detection) and [hector_object_tracker](https://github.com/tu-darmstadt-ros-pkg/hector_worldmodel/tree/master/hector_object_tracker) packages. We have not released extensive documentation or tutorials for those, but launch files used on our Hector UGV robot for [this video](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PKI378kadp8) are available ([here](https://github.com/tu-darmstadt-ros-pkg/hector_worldmodel/blob/master/hector_object_tracker/launch/object_tracker.launch) and [here](https://github.com/tu-darmstadt-ros-pkg/hector_vision/blob/master/hector_qrcode_detection/launch/qrcode_detection.launch)). See also the [Hector Open Source Modules](https://www.sim.informatik.tu-darmstadt.de/publ/download/2013_RoboCup_Kohlbrecher_open_source_tools.pdf) overview paper for some high level information on which components are responsible for which task.
