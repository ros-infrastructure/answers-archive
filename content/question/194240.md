    I'm not familiar with how hector_slam works, but you may be able to get away with publishing both scans on the same topic but with different tf frames for orientation.

    Otherwise, you could create an intermediary node using a [time sync](http://wiki.ros.org/message_filters#ApproximateTime_Policy) message filter, [convert both to pointclouds](http://wiki.ros.org/laser_geometry#C.2B-.2B-_Usage), add the two clouds, and then turn the combined cloud [back into a laserscan](https://github.com/ros-perception/perception_pcl/tree/indigo-devel/pointcloud_to_laserscan).
