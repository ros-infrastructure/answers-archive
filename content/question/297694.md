  body_markdown: |-
    This might change in future implementations, but it looks like at the moment you cannot set a tolerance because the pick/place pipeline uses `computeCartesianPath` [internally](https://github.com/ros-planning/moveit/blob/melodic-devel/moveit_ros/manipulation/pick_place/src/approach_and_translate_stage.cpp), which does not take tolerances as input parameters.

    As a workaround, you could generate grasps in more orientations and pass a vector of `Grasp` messages to the pick function. Depending on your needs, this might be sufficient. Other solutions I can think of would only get more complex.
