  body_markdown: "It would help if you also posted your costmaps configurations.\n\
    \nNormally, when you setup your [ costmap layers](http://wiki.ros.org/costmap_2d/Tutorials/Configuring%20Layered%20Costmaps),\
    \ you can also choose the `combination method` which is how you are going to combine\
    \ that layer (the laser one in your case) with the \"master costmap\". Setting\
    \ this parameter to `0` will make your \"obstacle layer\" to overwrite the \"\
    static_map layer\", clearing obstacles that are not detected by the laser scan.\n\
    \nfor example:\n\n     plugins: \n            - {name: static_map,       type:\
    \ \"costmap_2d::StaticLayer\"}\n            - {name: obstacles,        type: \"\
    costmap_2d::VoxelLayer\"}\n        publish_frequency: 1.0\n        obstacles:\n\
    \            observation_sources: base_scan\n            combination_method: 0\n\
    \            base_scan: {data_type: LaserScan, sensor_frame: /base_laser_link,\
    \ clearing: true, marking: true, topic: /base_scan}\n\ncheck slide 16 [here](http://roscon.ros.org/2014/wp-content/uploads/2014/07/ROSCON2014_DLu.pdf)\
    \ for more info on that."
