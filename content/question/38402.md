    \ then, do the follow:\n\nDisable the ntp service to update\n\n    sudo service\
    \ ntp stop\n\nUpdate\n\n    sudo ntpdate 0.ubuntu.pool.ntp.org\n\nEnable again\n\
    \n    sudo service ntp start\n\nDo this on both computers\n\nMaybe its not necessary\
    \ to start and stop\n\nVerify again if the time are now ok\n\n    timedatectl\n\
