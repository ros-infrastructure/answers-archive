  body_markdown: "Going to answer my question in case anybody else comes across this.\
    \ \nThe problem turned out to be that in my cmakefiles, I did not properly set\
    \ up my node's dependency on roscpp. As a result, it wouldn't build roscpp first,\
    \ and hence the conflict. \n\nissue was resolved by: \n\n- adding roscpp, and\
    \ some messages to find_package\n- adding roscpp as a CATKIN_DEPENDS to catkin_package()\n\
    - adding roscpp_generate_messages_cpp as an add_dependency \n- adding msgs & roscpp\
    \ as build_depends & run_depends in package.xml"
