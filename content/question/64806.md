  body_markdown: "I am not using Xtion Pro, but for kinect, but it is the same openni_tracker.\
    \ \n\nI would say that in fact you make two mistakes :\n\n -  as soon as you live\
    \ the frame, the transformation \"/openni_depth_frame\" to \"/right_hip_1\" disappears\
    \ in almost no time (less than two seconds). However, if you are using RVIZ, you\
    \ have a parameter you can change : In RVIZ, in the pannel Displays, under TF\
    \ you have \"Frame Timeout\". Try putting this to 0, and you will not see the\
    \ transforms anymore, as soon as you left the frame.\n - The second mistake concerns\
    \ your use of listener.waitForTransform : as you can see on [this tutorial](http://www.ros.org/wiki/tf/Tutorials/tf%20and%20Time%20%28C%2B%2B%29#Wait_for_transforms),\
    \ the third argument is the moment at which you want to know the transform was.\
    \ Here, it seems that you always ask for a transform in the past, at the last\
    \ moment you found the right hip. Thus leading to a seemingly always existing\
    \ transform. Try to replace right_hip_point_1.header.stamp by ros::Time::now(),\
    \ and you should not have it anymore.\n\nThe combination of the two thus lead\
    \ you to think the transform was still here after you left the frame, whereas\
    \ in fact it is not :-)\n\nLet me know,\n\nBests regards,\n\nSteph\n\n"
