    As a general note, the best source for Stage details is github, but it's embedded in [model_draw.cc](https://github.com/rtv/Stage/blob/f9c59ca77f203049979dcf32223e5d2b26c03bf2/libstage/model_draw.cc#L378).
    stageros doesn't publish this information. The condition is identified in [model_position.cc](https://github.com/rtv/Stage/blob/f9c59ca77f203049979dcf32223e5d2b26c03bf2/libstage/model_position.cc#L602).

    You could modify [stageros.cpp](https://github.com/ros-simulation/stage_ros/blob/master/src/stageros.cpp#L428) to detect when a model is stalled (collisions) and to publish a message on a topic.

    As a final note, you could also just evaluate the <em>/robot_X/odom</em> message and compare the observed velocity to the intended velocity of the robot. (Compare the embedded twist message to the twist command that you issued.)
