    \ of strings formatted like trajectory_msgs::JointTrajectory. You could try writing\
    \ [rostopic](http://www.ros.org/wiki/ROS/YAMLCommandLine) messages to your node.\
    \ Or write your own [YAML](http://www.ros.org/wiki/rosparam) file that contains\
    \ the information about your trajectory and upload it to the parameter server.\
    \ Then using a node to read the parameter server and format a JointTrajectory\
    \ msgs from the information and publishing it. \n\nAs for converting an integer\
    \ or float to a duration type you can save your duration as a float or integer\
    \ and [use](http://www.ros.org/wiki/roscpp/Overview/Time):\n\n    ros::Duration(24*60*60);\n\
    \nwith the time in seconds in the brackets. And your stamp values in your header\
    \ you just use:\n\n    header.stamp = ros::Time::now();\n\nbefore you publish\
    \ it. \n\nhope it helps\n "
