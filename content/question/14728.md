  body_markdown: "Floating joints only exist on URDF, not on [SDF](http://sdformat.org/spec?ver=1.6&elem=joint#joint_type)\
    \ (which is the primary description format used by Gazebo). \n\nAccording to URDF\
    \ [documentation](http://wiki.ros.org/urdf/XML/joint), a floating joint \"allows\
    \ motion for all 6 degrees of freedom\". For SDF, that would be no joint at all."
