    The PR2 is a fairly unique case because it uses a Linksys WRT610Nv2 access point as its wireless client, and signal information isn't reported to the dashboard.

    You may be able to find useful code, frameworks or ideas in the [linux networking](http://ros.org/wiki/linux_networking) stack, but the documentation there is pretty sparse. I would start by digging through the wpa_supplicant_node to see if it's useful or not.

    I don't know of anyone else that is working on this at the moment.
