    You need to compile your package with debug symbols.

        catkin_make --pkg <package_name> --cmake-args -DCMAKE_BUILD_TYPE=Debug

    Or for all packages:

        catkin_make --cmake-args -DCMAKE_BUILD_TYPE=Debug
