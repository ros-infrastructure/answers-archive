  body_markdown: "If you're detecting the center of the hole in both images, you can\
    \ draw a ray from each camera through the hole based on which pixel the hole corresponds\
    \ to and the image distortion model. Then you can find where the two rays intersect\
    \ to determine the 3D position of the hole. The [image_geometry](http://wiki.ros.org/image_geometry)\
    \ library has tools to help out with these types of problems. "
