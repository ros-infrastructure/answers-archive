  body_markdown: |
    ROS uses SI units for everything, so also in URDF (for link lengths) and for meshes. See [REP-003](http://www.ros.org/reps/rep-0103.html), section *Units*.

    You'll have to make sure your meshes use *metres* as scale. Meshes exported by CAD tools often use *millimetres*, so they will be a 1000 times too large.

    ---

    If you don't want to or can't scale your meshes, you could use the `scale` attribute on the `mesh` element in your urdf. See [wiki/urdf/XML/link - Elements](http://wiki.ros.org/urdf/XML/link#Elements) for more information.
