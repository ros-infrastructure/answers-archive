    Actually, [wstool]( http://wiki.ros.org/wstool) replaces rosws. See the link for details about that. And both rosws and wstool use [rosinstall files](http://docs.ros.org/independent/api/rosinstall/html/rosinstall_file_format.html) for configuring workspaces. In general I have only seen wstool used with catkin workspaces and would recommend you use wstool instead of rosws.
