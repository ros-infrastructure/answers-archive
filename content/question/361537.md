  body_markdown: "As discussed on the MoveIt Discord: They are just pointers to RobotModel\
    \ and RobotState* (see [robot_model.h](http://docs.ros.org/api/moveit_core/html/classmoveit_1_1core_1_1RobotModel.html)\
    \ and [robot_state.h](http://docs.ros.org/api/moveit_core/html/classmoveit_1_1core_1_1RobotState.html)\
    \ respectively). All the `xxxPtr`, `xxxConstPtr` etc. pointers are defined via\
    \ `MOVEIT_CLASS_FORWARD` at the start of those header files. See [class_forward.h](http://docs.ros.org/api/moveit_core/html/class__forward_8h_source.html)\
    \ and [declare_ptr.h](http://docs.ros.org/api/moveit_core/html/declare__ptr_8h_source.html)\
    \ for the definition of that macro:\n\n    #define MOVEIT_DECLARE_PTR_MEMBER(Type)\n\
    \    typedef std::shared_ptr<Type> Ptr;\n    typedef std::shared_ptr<const Type>\
    \ ConstPtr;\n    typedef std::weak_ptr<Type> WeakPtr;\n    typedef std::weak_ptr<const\
    \ Type> ConstWeakPtr;\n    typedef std::unique_ptr<Type> UniquePtr;\n    typedef\
    \ std::unique_ptr<const Type> ConstUniquePtr;\n\nSo not only RobotModelPtr, but\
    \ also RobotModelConstPtr, RobotModelWeakPtr etc. are defined, but there is no\
    \ explicit definition your IDE displays, and you won't find them with simple Ctrl+F.\
    \ \n\nI found this confusing as well, and it's not easy to figure out if you don't\
    \ know the pattern. I'm not sure how to make it easier to discover when you read\
    \ the code, but feel free to submit a PR with documentation."
