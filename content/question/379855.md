    Quick comment: Gazebo *requires* intertias, masses and `dynamics` elements for all `link`s and `joint`s. If those are not present, it will filter those elements out of the model it loads.

    Afaik, the HC10 model you use doesn't have those elements, which could very well be the cause of the errors you describe.
