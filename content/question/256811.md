    This looks vaguely like a network issue; I advise that you read the [ROS Network Setup](http://wiki.ros.org/ROS/NetworkSetup) and [ROS Multiple Machines](http://wiki.ros.org/ROS/Tutorials/MultipleMachines) tutorials and make sure that the ROS_MASTER_URI and hostnames are configured correctly on your machines, and that you can do a `rosnode ping` from the computer running matlab to the urg_node on your Raspberry Pi. I don't know much about the ROS bindings for Matlab, so the commands in matlab may be a little different from the tutorials.
