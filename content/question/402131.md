    There are a couple tricks out there that can be used:

     - Do you really need full frame rate for the sensors? If not, you can insert something like the [throttle node from topic_tools](http://wiki.ros.org/topic_tools/throttle) in between your actual sensors and the rosbag recorder to downsample the frame rate to a more manageable amount of data.
     - I've personally always had better luck compressing the bagfiles as a separate step AFTER recording (so, record uncompressed, then when the bagfile finishes or splits you compress it).

        rosbag record -O "$NAME"_"$cnt" --split --size=4096 /topics
