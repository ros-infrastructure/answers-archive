  body_markdown: |-
    You cannot install `rosbash` for ROS Foxy.

    `rosbash` is a ROS 1 package, it's not available for ROS 2.

    The cause for your error however is likely the fact that `rosbash` is the [UpstreamPackages](https://wiki.ros.org/UpstreamPackages) version of `python-$ROS_DISTRO-rosbash`, which would be the correct package name.

    You cannot mix the two streams of packages (well, it can be done, but I would not attempt that if you're just starting out).
