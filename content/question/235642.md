 I was using turtlebot to do navigation experiments based on `ROS_navigation` stack. **I found that the turtlebot was much hesitating when it met some obstacles in the front.** Even though the obstacle(which has been stayed there for a while) has been removed, the turtlebot still rotating at the place where it first see the obstacles.
