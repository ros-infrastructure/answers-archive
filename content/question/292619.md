    Any `rosbridge` packages don't seem to be released yet at the moment (see [repositories.ros.org](http://repositories.ros.org/status_page/ros_melodic_default.html?q=rosbridge)). Some of its dependent package isn't released yet either (see [another page on repositories.ros.org](http://repositories.ros.org/status_page/blocked_releases_melodic.html?q=rosbridge&s=6&r=1)), which means it'll become release-able once dependent packages are released.

    Looks like there's already [a request](https://github.com/RobotWebTools/rosbridge_suite/issues/333). Voicing up there is the best way to communicate with the maintenance team of the package in question.
