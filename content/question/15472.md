  body_markdown: "Calculating the odometry is typically done via a plugin, that is\
    \ a piece of code inserted into the model that can do calculations and change\
    \ the model based on an interface defined [here](http://gazebosim.org/tutorials?tut=plugins_model&cat=write_plugin)\
    \ \n\nA Model plugin will give you access to joint information usually useful\
    \ for calculating your odometry. For special arrangements its usually better to\
    \ calculate your odometry and publish as you see fit, typically via a ros odometry\
    \ message. \n\nAlternatively for common arrangements there are some existing plugins\
    \ [available](http://gazebosim.org/tutorials?tut=ros_gzplugins) that might suit\
    \ your purpose. I am thinking specifically of the differential drive ros plugin,\
    \ skid steer plugin and planar drive plugin. They will give you a control interface\
    \ (for issuing commands) as well as output odometry information."
