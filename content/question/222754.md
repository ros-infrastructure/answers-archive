  body_markdown: " Hi,\n\nRGBDSLAM and RTABMAP are visual features-based SLAM, so\
    \ if the ceiling has enough visual features and there are some discrimitive area\
    \ (for localization/loop closure), they may work. The ceiling must be also in\
    \ the depth range of the camera.\n\nNote that RTABMAP can also work with only\
    \ a RGB camera to detect loop closures (localization). This mode could be used\
    \ to localize the robot based purely on the location appearance (e.g., it will\
    \ tell that you are \"near\" a location in the map, without transformation estimation).\
