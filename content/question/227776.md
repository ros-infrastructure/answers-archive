  body_markdown: |-
    You need to [remap](http://wiki.ros.org/Remapping%20Arguments) the `map` and `map_metadata` topics from [`map_server`](http://wiki.ros.org/map_server) to what you need, i.e. to `world` and `world_metadata`.

    Depending on whether you are using `roslaunch` or `rosrun`, there are different ways.
    Use the [`<remap ...>` tag](http://wiki.ros.org/roslaunch/XML/remap) for roslaunch.
    If you use rosrun, add the following:
    `rosrun map_server map_server mymap.yaml /map:=/world /map_metadata:=/world_metadata`.
