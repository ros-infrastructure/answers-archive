    There should be a valid [URI](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uniform_resource_identifier) specified after the 'uri': identifier in your rosinstall file. Turns out there is a [nao_extras](https://github.com/ros-nao/nao_extras) repository, so the correct rosinstall entry would probably be:

        {'git': {'local-name': 'nao_extras', 'uri': 'https://github.com/ros-nao/nao_extras'}}
