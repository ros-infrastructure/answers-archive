  body_markdown: "You can launch the `roscore` on your laptop (or any ROS device that\
    \ stays connected during the experiment. it could be one of the robots) and then\
    \ launch your two robots with `roslaunch` commands. If they are on the same network\
    \ and you have correctly specified the `ROS_MASTER_URI` you should have no issues.\n\
    \nBy default, the current version of ROS does not allow to have multiple ROS masters\
    \ on the same network, but efforts have been made to implement this feature, you\
    \ can have a look at the following packages :\n\n - http://wiki.ros.org/multimaster_fkie\n\
    \ - http://wiki.ros.org/rocon_launch\n\nThis being said you might want to start\
    \ working with a single master first and transition to multiple master only if\
    \ needed. If you are working with a reliable network you probably don't need multiple\
    \ masters.\n\nAlso, if you have multiple robots, make sure that you launch them\
    \ in separate namespaces (e.g `/robot1/`,`/robot2/` to avoid confusions and conflicts\
    \ ... "
