  body_markdown: "Please post your configuration and launch files. It makes answering\
    \ such questions easier.\n\nI can say that this is a typical setup for GPS data:\n\
    \nEKF1: world frame is odom, fuse IMU and wheel encoders.\n\nEKF2: world frame\
    \ is map, fuse GPS, IMU, and wheel encoders\n\n`navsat_transform_node` should\
    \ listen to the output of EKF2.\n\nAs to which output to use, it doesn't really\
    \ make sense to use the output of `navsat_transform_node`, unless you want to\
    \ plot it in Google Earth or something. The output of EKF2 will be the most accurate\
    \ pose, but is obviously subject to discontinuities from the GPS.\n\nEDIT: your\
    \ second solution is more in line with what I was thinking, yes. The example in\
    \ the package isn't prescriptive; that is, I don't encourage you to stick to it\
    \ necessarily, but it gives an idea of what's required to work with GPS data.\
    \ \n\nWhat is your second odometry source? The `odom_gps` data? Or another one?\n\
    \nI'll give you the same advice I give everyone: get your filters working with\
    \ just IMU and wheel encoders. When that's done, add the GPS data.\n\nI'm not\
    \ sure how well this will work in a mapping context, but I know nothing about\
    \ your setup."
