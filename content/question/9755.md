  body_markdown: "All threading is local to processes.  \n\nAll callbacks can be associated\
    \ with a specific callback queue.   All callbacks go into the default queue if\
    \ not specified.  A NodeHandle can have a custom callback queue associated with\
    \ it, or specific callback registrations. \n\nIf you have a single thread servicing\
    \ a callback queue.  All will be serial.  \n\nAny specific callback will only\
    \ be called after the last instance has finished, regardless of how many threads\
    \ there are in the queue.  \n"
