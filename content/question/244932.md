  body_markdown: "Before using Gazebo, you need to create a URDF model of you 7 DOF\
    \ robot. You can follow through the tutorials of [Gazebo and ROS](http://gazebosim.org/tutorials?cat=connect_ros).\n\
    \nTest your model works with Gazebo by using [ros_control](http://wiki.ros.org/ros_control)\
    \ for the controllers. \n\nThen you will have to map the geomagic touch device\
    \ to some sort of control input for ros_control controllers. \n\nIf you are satisfied\
    \ with your simulation in Gazebo, you will need to replace the simulated robot\
    \ with your robot hardware. This includes creating a joint state publisher and\
    \ controllers for your 7 DOF robot. "
