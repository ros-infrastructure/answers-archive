  body_markdown: "You just have to set your Environment Variables correctly in your\
    \ case the `ROS_MASTER_URI` where you specify where your PC can find the Master\
    \ documented [here.](http://wiki.ros.org/ROS/EnvironmentVariables)\n\nSo you just\
    \ have to open a new Terminal Window and enter `export ROS_MASTER_URI=http://mia:11311/`\
    \ (mia: my ip address). In your case enter the ip address of the raspberrypi instead\
    \ of mia.\n\nThan starting RVIZ with `rosrun rviz rviz`. (you also can check with\
    \ `rostopic list` if your topics are available now).\n\n\n--- Update -- \n\nCorresponding\
    \ to your comment you have also set your ROS_IP. Sorry i forgot to tell you that.\n\
    So just find out your IP-Address with \n\n    hostname -I\n\nand export it with\n\
    \n    export ROS_IP=192.168.xxx.xxx\n\nSo you have to do both. Setup the ROS_MASTER_URI\
    \ and the ROS_IP.\nPlease don\u2019t forget to set the ROS_IP at your PC and the\
    \ RaspberryPi."
