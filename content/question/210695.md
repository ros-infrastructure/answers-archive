    This is more a Gazebo question than a ROS question (so I should refer you to the [gazebosim.org](http://gazebosim.org) site), but what you ask is described in one of the [Gazebo tutorials](http://gazebosim.org/tutorials), namely the [Connect to ROS](http://gazebosim.org/tutorials?tut=ros_gzplugins&cat=connect_ros) one.

Everything will seem really convoluted and incomprehensible otherwise (at least, that is what many students/people tell me is their experience).
