    \ \n\nFirst, it is important to differentiate between a global planner and a \"\
    local\" planner. This is a confusing term, it is better to refer to the former\
    \ as a planner and the latter as a controller, simpler. \nYou can write your own\
    \ planner or controller as a plugin. Your controller always will need a reference\
    \ trajectory so that it can follow it by producing proper twist messages. \n\n\
    So make sure your robot and PC are on the same network, then launch `planner_server`\
    \ in your PC, and the rest of the nav stack nodes on the robot, not exactly sure\
    \ what did not work for you"
