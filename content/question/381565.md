  body_markdown: "Catkin does not replace CMake.\n\nCatkin only drives CMake (ie:\
    \ starts it at the right moment).\n\nSo whatever the CMake version you use supports,\
    \ you can use in `CMakeLists.txt`.\n\nCatkin does not change anything there.\n\
    \n> Note : Also as per the http://wiki.ros.org/catkin/CMakeLists.txt the option\
    \ is not supported. \n\nthat page documents how the Catkin-contributed CMake *macros*\
    \ should be used.\n\nIt does not say anything about whether or not certain CMake\
    \ functionality is supported or not.\n\nTo make it extra clear: Catkin does not\
    \ interpret nor execute your `CMakeLists.txt`. That's CMake's job. Catkin just\
    \ starts CMake.\n"
