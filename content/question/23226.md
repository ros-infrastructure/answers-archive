 Hello, I read the LiftDragPlugin.cc carefully， and I think the 225 line may have mistakes.

This line looks like :
ignition::math::Vector3d velInLDPlane = vel - vel.Dot(spanwiseI)*velI;

I think this line should be 

ignition::math::Vector3d velInLDPlane = vel - vel.Dot(spanwiseI)*spanwiseI;

because "vel - vel.Dot(spanwiseI)*velI;" does not change the direction but only the norm of vel. Therefore, the vel should 
substract the spanwise veciloty.
