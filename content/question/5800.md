    There are some scripts in drcsim that enable this behavior. See the [atlas\_no\_controllers.launch file](https://bitbucket.org/osrf/drcsim/src/13354da3a41bfb74d908719cdb51e7af326329b0/drcsim_gazebo/launch/atlas_no_controllers.launch?at=default)
    and the [scripts folder](https://bitbucket.org/osrf/drcsim/src/13354da3a41b/drcsim_gazebo/scripts/?at=default),
    especially [run\_gzserver\_gdb](https://bitbucket.org/osrf/drcsim/src/13354da3a41bfb74d908719cdb51e7af326329b0/drcsim_gazebo/scripts/run_gzserver_gdb?at=default). I believe it can be invoked by appending `gzname:=gzserver_gdb` to the roslaunch command, for example

     `roslaunch drcsim_gazebo atlas.launch gzname:=gzserver_gdb`
