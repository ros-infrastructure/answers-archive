    In order for a model to appear in the list, it needs contain a `model.sdf` and `model.config` file.  See [this tutorial](http://gazebosim.org/tutorials?tut=model_structure&cat=build_robot) and also [this repo](https://github.com/osrf/gazebo_models) for examples.  The `meshes` and `materials` directory structure is optional (provided your `dae` files, textures, etc. reference each other correctly), but `model.sdf` and `model.config` is required
