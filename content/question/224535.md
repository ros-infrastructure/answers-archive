    Just publish on /pan_controller/command messages of type std_msgs/Float64. [Here](http://wiki.ros.org/ROS/Tutorials/WritingPublisherSubscriber(python))'s how to.

    Line 7 would look like:

        pub = rospy.Publisher('/pan_controller/command', Float64, queue_size=10)

    and 13 like:

        pub.publish(1.5)
