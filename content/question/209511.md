I have a mobile base which is navigating around a room (already have a map of the room). I am using rotary encoders for odometry and I am feeding [phidgets 3/3/3](http://www.phidgets.com/products.php?product_id=1056_0) IMU data along with rotary encoders data to the [robot_pose_ekf](http://wiki.ros.org/robot_pose_ekf). I am using [amcl](http://wiki.ros.org/amcl) for localization and [move_base](http://wiki.ros.org/move_base?distro=indigo) for planning. I am a little surprised because the local planner is not sending any velocities, `rostopic echo /cmd_vel` does not show anything whereas `rostopic echo /odom` obviously shows the robot pose and twist.
