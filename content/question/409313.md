  body_markdown: "A ROS node will be responsible for controlling the LiDAR, reading\
    \ the scan data and publishing /scan topics which other nodes can subscribe to.\
    \  Also the /scan topics (along with other desired topics) can be \u201Crecorded\u201D\
    \ with a ROS utility, for later \u201Cplayback\u201D.  \n\nSo no, ROS cannot use\
    \ .LAS format scans.   The manufacturer of your LiDAR will have a ROS and ROS2\
    \ node that you will install and run on your ROS robot."
