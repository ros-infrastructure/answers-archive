    Hi,

    The friction force with the water (drag) is already simulated. You can change the paremeters modifying the [.yaml file](https://github.com/uji-ros-pkg/underwater_simulation/blob/indigo-devel/underwater_vehicle_dynamics/config/dynamics_g500.yaml). The parameters used are based on the Fossen dynamics model described in [guidance and control of ocean vehicles](https://books.google.es/books/about/Guidance_and_control_of_ocean_vehicles.html?id=cwJUAAAAMAAJ&redir_esc=y). You can also check the [UWSim paper](http://ieeexplore.ieee.org/stamp/stamp.jsp?arnumber=6385788&tag=1) where it is explained.
