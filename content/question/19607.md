  body_markdown: "I see you have a lot of fixed joints on your URDF. When converted\
    \ to SDF, links connected by fixed joints are lumped together into links for performance,\
    \ and as a consequence, the link names change.\n\nOne thing you could try would\
    \ be to use the `preserveFixedJoint` on your URDF, so that the fixed joints are\
    \ persisted, and link names and poses don't change, for example:\n\n\n    <joint\
    \ name=\"camera_joint\" type=\"fixed\">\n    ...\n    </joint> \n    <gazebo reference='camera_joint'>\"\
    \n        <preserveFixedJoint>true</preserveFixedJoint>\"\n    </gazebo>\"\n"
