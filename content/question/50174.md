  body_markdown: "Since kino and dvgrab work but coriander does not, this device apparently\
    \ conforms to the Digital Video (DV) and not the Digital Camera (DC) standard.\
    \ So, camera1394 would not be expected to work. \n\nTry the [gscam](http://ros.org/wiki/gscam)\
    \ driver. It uses gstreamer, which does support DV devices."
