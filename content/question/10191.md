    \ \n\nSome of the stacks supplied by other organizations include complete examples\
    \ of working ROS code including hardware drivers (PR2, wubble, erratic, turtlebot,\
    \ Pioneer P2OS), http://www.ros.org/wiki/Robots.\n\nThere is a partially completed\

    \ but that won't help. \n\nIf you provide some more information regarding what\

    Usually it is quite simple writing a hardware driver for ROS as it mainly is producing a message interface, and possibly diagnostics. That is, given, that you can write/modify a hardware driver not specific to ROS.
