  body_markdown: "Are you referring to the disparity image or the depth image published\
    \ by the driver?\n\nThe depth image contains the distance from the camera to the\
    \ scene along the z-axis (optical axis) of the camera. The ROS [openni_camera](http://ros.org/wiki/openni_camera)\
    \ driver publishes the depth image as a:\n\n * 16-bit unsigned integer, in millimeters,\
    \ on `camera/depth/image_raw`,\n * float, in meters, on `camera/depth/image`.\
    \ \n\n(see [depth_image_proc](http://www.ros.org/wiki/depth_image_proc))\n\nThe\
    \ ROS driver does not publish the nonlinear, 11-bit raw output of the Kinect."
