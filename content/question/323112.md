  body_markdown: "Your intuition is partially correct: OMPL and SPBL are planning\
    \ libraries, and STOMP, CHOMP, and specific sampling-based planners like RRT and\
    \ PRM are planners. What you are seeing on the 'Planners' tab are actually the\
    \ specific [planning plugins](https://moveit.ros.org/documentation/plugins/#plannermanager)\
    \ available in MoveIt. Some of those planning plugins only implement a single\
    \ planner, and others are entire libraries, so the simpler of the two terms (planners)\
    \ was used, probably for readability and clarity. "
