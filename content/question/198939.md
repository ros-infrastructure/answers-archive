    Seeing as [steered_wheel_base_controller](http://wiki.ros.org/steered_wheel_base_controller) is a [ros_control](http://wiki.ros.org/ros_control) plugin, I don't think you're supposed to load it like you do -- eg: directly using `pluginlib`.

    You might want to take a look at the [ros_control/Tutorials](http://wiki.ros.org/ros_control/Tutorials) and the [presentation](http://roscon.ros.org/2014/wp-content/uploads/2014/07/ros_control_an_overview.pdf) of Adolfo at ROSCon14 last September on `ros_control`.
