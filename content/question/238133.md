    You can use the normal [include_directories](https://cmake.org/cmake/help/v3.0/command/include_directories.html)/[target_link_libraries](https://cmake.org/cmake/help/v3.0/command/target_link_libraries.html) cmake macros for this:

        include_directories(${ADDITIONAL_INCLUDE_DIRS})
        orocos_component(foo foo.cpp)
        target_link_libraries(foo ${ADDITIONAL_LIBRARIES_TO_LINK_WITH})
