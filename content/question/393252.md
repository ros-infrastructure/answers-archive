    Hi @infiniteGo, take a look at the gazebo plugins for `RotorS` for a MAV gazebo simulator: https://github.com/ethz-asl/rotors_simulator, you can adapt them to Ardrone.

    Under `rotors_gazebo_plugins/src` [folder](https://github.com/ethz-asl/rotors_simulator/tree/master/rotors_gazebo_plugins/src) you can see examples of GPS and odometry plugins:

    namespace gazebo {

    GazeboGpsPlugin::GazeboGpsPlugin()
        : SensorPlugin(),
          // node_handle_(0),

          pubs_and_subs_created_(false) {}

    GazeboGpsPlugin::~GazeboGpsPlugin() {
    }
