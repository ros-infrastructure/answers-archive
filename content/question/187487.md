  body_markdown: "You do not have to edit openni2.launch.\n\nTo push the topics/frames\
    \ to a namespace just do:\n\n    roslaunch openni2_camera openni2_camera_node\
    \ camera:=xyzrgb_camera\n\nwhere xyzrgb_camera is the name of your namespace.\
    \ /camera/depth/points will now be, \n\nxyzrgb_camera/depth/points"
