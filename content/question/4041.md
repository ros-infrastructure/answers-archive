  body_markdown: "If you take a look into the [hokuyo model's SDF](https://bitbucket.org/osrf/gazebo_models/src/6cd587c0a30e/hokuyo/model.sdf?at=default)\
    \ you can see, that the laser takes 640 samples (`<samples>640</samples>`). With\
    \ the resolution (`<resolution>1</resolution>`)  and the min angle and max angle\
    \ (`<min_angle>-2.26889</min_angle>`, `<max_angle>2.268899</max_angle>`) you have\
    \ everything you need. \n\nThen for example in a plugin go through the samples\
    \ ([for this you should take a look in the control model tutorial](http://gazebosim.org/wiki/Tutorials/1.3/intermediate/control_model))\
    \ and check the distances:\nIf sample 0 detects a low range then you know, that\
    \ from your sensor direction in the angle of -2.26889 there is an object.\n\n\
    Edit: perhaps its easier to connect a simple ray sensor to your mobile robot.\
    \ Then you can control things like angle and samples without editing the hokuyo\
    \ model."
