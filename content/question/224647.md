 Hi all,

I am using [dwa_local_planner](http://wiki.ros.org/dwa_local_planner) as the local planner and sometimes, it generates in place velocities towards the obstacle although the global plan is good. I have attached two images below which shows this clearly :    

**Before Planning:**

![image description](https://ftp.osuosl.org/pub/ros/download.ros.org/downloads/se_migration/ros/14533283315333197.png)

**After Planning:**  
![image description](https://ftp.osuosl.org/pub/ros/download.ros.org/downloads/se_migration/ros/14533284092323857.png)
