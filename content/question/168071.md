While testing turtlebot navigation with stage simulation I found interesting behavior. Robot gets stuck in empty space and starts to rotate to clear costmap. Could anybody explain why robot gets stuck in empty space?

> [ WARN] [1400749013.113075404, 136.300000000]: Clearing costmap to unstuck robot.

[move_base configuration](https://github.com/turtlebot/turtlebot_simulator/tree/hydro-devel/turtlebot_stage/param/move_base) and 
[video](http://files.yujinrobot.com/turtlebot/turtlebot_stuck.mp4)

You can test with [turtlebot_stage](https://github.com/turtlebot/turtlebot_simulator/tree/hydro-devel/turtlebot_stage) package.
