    \ and found it working:\n\n\n        <launch>\n            <node pkg=\"nodelet\"\
    \ type=\"nodelet\" name=\"my_nodelet_manager\" args=\"manager\" output=\"screen\"\
    \ />\n            <node pkg=\"nodelet\" type=\"nodelet\" name=\"pointcloud_to_laserscan\"\
    \ args=\"load pointcloud_to_laserscan/pointcloud_to_laserscan_nodelet my_nodelet_manager\"\
    >\n                <remap from=\"cloud_in\" to=\"/camera/depth/color/points\"\
    \ />\n            </node>\n        </launch>\n\n\n    Pay attention to the `<node\
