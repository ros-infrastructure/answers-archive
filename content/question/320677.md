Hello, I have a problem when running rtabmap SLAM algorithm in my real turtlebot. Specifically, when I visualize octomap in rviz, the map seems to have a limit at the top of the room. I suppose that it shows the room’s ceiling but it could also be sensor’s noise. I use: 
Turtlebot 2 with Ubuntu 16.04 and ROS kinetic, my camera is Asus Xtion Pro and my computer has Ubuntu 16.04 and ROS kinetic.

Firstly, how could I understand if this limit in octomap is room’s ceiling or sensor’s noise and furthermore how can I throw these nodes in octomap in order to see room’s details?
