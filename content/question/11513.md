    Here are some things I suggest:

    Increase the [--queue](http://www.ros.org/wiki/rosbag/Commandline#play) parameter
    Also, if you don't care about the time scale, decrease the [--rate](http://www.ros.org/wiki/rosbag/Commandline#play) parameter. You can do a combination of both - high queue and low rate will alleviate the strain on the system, as far as I can tell.

    Another thing to try is to load the bag using [rxbag](http://www.ros.org/wiki/rxbag) and play it from the "play" control in the GUI. This worked better for me when I was using huge (>4GB) bags. I assume its because rxbag loads everything in the memory at once, whereas rosbag reads it sequentially from disk, but that's a guess.

    Here's a [somewhat similar thread](http://answers.ros.org/question/10090/rosbag-keyboard-controls-unresponsive).

    Had a similar issue with a 1.5Gb bag file. Tried :

     - slowing the rate down to a tenth

    But at the end it was caused by empty messages in the bag.

        rosbag play --skip-empty=1 fat_file.bag
