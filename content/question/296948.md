    You may be able to achieve this by setting the `initial_pose_a` and `initial_cov_aa` parameters described on the [AMCL wiki page](http://wiki.ros.org/amcl) before starting the AMCL node. Obviously you'll need to choose a covariance (in radians) that corresponds to your compass's heading accuracy in order to generate the correct distribution of headings in the particle cloud.
