    No, this is not supported.

    Note also: `computeCartesianPath(..)` is not a planner. It performs a linear interpolation between two or multiple waypoints. That is rather different from a real planner.

    ---

    Edit: I don't know whether it will be helpful in your situation, but you could take a look at [descartes](http://wiki.ros.org/descartes) and / or [trajopt_ros](https://github.com/ros-industrial-consortium/trajopt_ros/)/[tesseract](https://github.com/ros-industrial-consortium/tesseract).
