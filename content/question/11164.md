  body_markdown: "I think there shouldn't be a problem with that; the real-world Kinect\
    \ just publishes its data on a normal ROS topic, Gazebo publishes and subscribes\
    \ also to normal topics, and your node can use both.\n\nThere is only one potential\
    \ snag: gazebo uses [simulated time](http://www.ros.org/wiki/Clock), while the\
    \ Kinect will probably use wall time. You'll have to be careful about where and\
    \ how you use timestamps.  "
