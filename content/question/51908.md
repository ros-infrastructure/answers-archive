  body_markdown: "**Update: solution suggestion**\n\nAfter fiddling around with the\
    \ options a bit, here is my current workaround to the problem of having no serial\
    \ available from the xtion.\n\nWith *lsusb* I determined that my PC has two separate\
    \ hubs available for external USB devices, allowing me to plug in one xtion for\
    \ each. Since the bus number doesn't chance, I can be sure that the xtion connected\
    \ to a specific bus is always the same.\n\nBy moving the calibration files (named\
    \ depth.yaml and rgb.yaml) into separate folders, I can assign the calibrations\
    \ with the corresponding xtion. The serial number naming for the folders are based\
    \ on a manual readning on the physical device.\n\nThis allows a fairly consistent\
    \ behaviour, and for now it is sufficient to solve all my current problems with\
