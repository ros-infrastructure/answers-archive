  body_markdown: "Record compressed stream via:\n\n    $ rosbag record /front_camera/image_raw/compressed\n\
    \nPlay back recording with:\n\n    $ rosbag play <bagfile>\n    $ rosrun image_view\
    \ image_view image:=/front_camera/image_raw compressed\n\nnote the space between\
    \ the raw topic and the transport type in image_view. \n\n"
