    It should absolutely work with mongodb-org. I would assume that the dependencies of the `mongodb_store` package are wrongly stated in that the dependency should be `(mongodb | mongodb-org)` and even that is not quite accurate, it should probably be just the server: `(mongodb-server | mongodb-org-server)`. So I think your issue is just the installation not the usage. You could get around that in several ways by [ignoring unmet dependencies](https://serverfault.com/questions/250224/how-do-i-get-apt-get-to-ignore-some-dependencies), or you might be able to build `mongodb_store` from source.
