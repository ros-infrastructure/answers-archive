  body_markdown: |-
    The "ranges" member of a [LaserScan](http://docs.ros.org/api/sensor_msgs/html/msg/LaserScan.html) message is represented by a `std::vector<float>` and can be used just like any other std::vector of floats.

    It might help to have a look at the laser callbacks in [slam_gmapping](https://github.com/ros-perception/slam_gmapping/blob/hydro-devel/gmapping/src/slam_gmapping.cpp#L476), [AMCL](https://github.com/ros-planning/navigation/blob/hydro-devel/amcl/src/amcl_node.cpp#L816) and [hector_mapping](https://github.com/tu-darmstadt-ros-pkg/hector_slam/blob/master/hector_mapping/src/HectorMappingRos.cpp#L229). For the latter, an example of accessing range data is the [rosLaserScanToDataContainer](https://github.com/tu-darmstadt-ros-pkg/hector_slam/blob/master/hector_mapping/src/HectorMappingRos.cpp#L229) function.
