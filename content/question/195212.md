  body_markdown: "There are some possiblities to publish outside the while loop, e.\
    \ g.\n\n--> publishing in a subcriber callback (suitable if your node dows only\
    \ few processing and your published out message only depends on the incoming messages,\
    \ e. g. for any kind of republishers)\n--> publishing in a timer callback (if\
    \ you want to publish at constant rate outside the while (ros::ok()) loop  )\n\
    \nNote that you should set up your publisher before initializing the Subscriber/Timer\
    \ and your publisher should be visible inside the callback (local publisher object\
    \ not possible anymore). "
