  body_markdown: "The 0.5 is coming from using an RK2 integration scheme. Specifically,\
    \ from a quick glance at the code, it seems they are using what is often referred\
    \ to as the [Explicit Midpoint Method](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Runge%E2%80%93Kutta_methods#Explicit_midpoint_method).\
    \ This is a fully explicit RK scheme that has two stages (one at the left node,\
    \ and one at the midpoint). The weight on the midpoint is 1/2 (that is where the\
    \ 0.5 you are seeing comes in), and the weight on the left node is 0. \n\nIn simple\
    \ terms the line mentioned in the original question is finding the midpoint orientation\
    \ between the original orientation and the final orientation, and this midpoint\
    \ orientation is being used for evaluating the sin and cos terms across the timestep."
