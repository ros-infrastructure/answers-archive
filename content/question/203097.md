    I don't know of any packages that specifically support this kind of device, but afaik, most *presentation devices* are actually just USB HID input devices (keyboards with just a few keys). I can imagine updating [teleop_twist_keyboard](http://wiki.ros.org/teleop_twist_keyboard) (or just remapping keys) would do the trick.

    There are multiple ways under Linux to determine which particular keycodes / keys a device sends, see [Extra keyboard keys](https://wiki.archlinux.org/index.php/extra_keyboard_keys#Keycodes) on the Arch wiki for some possible options.
