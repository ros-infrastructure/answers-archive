    At first I would advise you to check if you mounted the sensor in a "correct way" aka "correct" orientation (check your sensors documentation/manual on this one). Simple mistake of switching top and bottom side can be stressful and could be a cause of your problems. If this part is correct then the "problem" you are facing could be in coordinate system your IMU uses. There are left- and right-handed coordinate system, both of which are know to be used in robotics. Check [this](https://www.evl.uic.edu/ralph/508S98/coordinates.html) link for more information. Hope this helps you understand more about your problem.
