    \nYou can change parameters of multi-dynamixels using SyncWrite function.\n\n\
    If you look at line 56 in [torque control example](https://github.com/ROBOTIS-GIT/dynamixel-workbench/blob/master/dynamixel_workbench_controllers/src/torque_control.cpp),\
    \ 'Goal_Current' parameter are added to syncwrite handler. You can change it as\
    \ 'P_Gain' or 'D_Gain' and write gain to the dynamixels by syncWrite function\
    \ simultaneously.(line 152 in same example)\n\nBest regards\nDarby"
