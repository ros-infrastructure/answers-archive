    Hello, regarding the error with the STL file:
    I was facing the same problem and all I did was to download `top_plate.stl` from the official repository from [gazebo](http://models.gazebosim.org/husky/meshes/), delete the current located in `/husky_description/meshes` and replace it. After replacing make sure to go to the root of your catkin workspace and

           $ catkin_make

    After relaunching the RViz everything worked properly.
