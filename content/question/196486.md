    PointClouds come in many forms, with different sets of fields and different data layouts.

    The additional metadata in the [sensor_msgs/PointCloud2](http://docs.ros.org/api/sensor_msgs/html/msg/PointCloud2.html) message describe how many rows and columns there are, which fields are present in each pixel, and what order they're in.

    **EDIT**

    PCL has a [visualization library](http://pointclouds.org/documentation/overview/visualization.php). It should be possible to use it from .NET: http://stackoverflow.com/questions/18203173/net-and-pcl-point-cloud-library . It will take a little massaging to convert a ROS message into a PCL message, but the formats are basically the same.
