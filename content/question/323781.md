    The short answer: it's a mix. Whichever interface carries a particular piece of information is used.

    This includes the secondary and 'real-time' (or 'matlab') interfaces.

    RTDE and the primary interface are not used (the primary interface is used, but not for this kind of data).

    ---

    Edit: more information can be found in the [Remote Control Via TCP/IP - 16496](https://www.universal-robots.com/how-tos-and-faqs/how-to/ur-how-tos/remote-control-via-tcpip-16496/) article on the Universal Robot support site and in the thesis that describes the Kinetic refactor ([here](http://production.datastore.cvt.dk/oafilestore?oid=598aeb6f5010df755c717ee7&targetid=598aeb6f5010df755c717ee9)).
