    Couple of things to try / check:

     1. Does the file `/home/sanket/.rviz/config` exist?
     1. What are its permissions?
     1. Is it corrupted / damaged in any way?
     1. If it exists, remove it, try again.
     1. Check out one of the many other threads with 'rviz' and 'segfault' in their title: [1](http://answers.ros.org/question/11376/rviz-segmentation-fault-at-launch/), [2](http://answers.ros.org/question/12043/rosrun-rviz-rviz-segmentation-fault/), [3](http://answers.ros.org/question/11809/rviz-crashing-at-startup-with-baddrawable/), etc.
     1. Check the [rviz troubleshooting](http://www.ros.org/wiki/rviz/Troubleshooting#Segfault_during_startup) section on its wiki.

    ---

    Another option (perhaps the first) is to run rviz in GDB. Then get a backtrace. It seems you have coredumps enabled, so you could do a post-mortem debugging session (see [here](http://cgi.cse.unsw.edu.au/~cs3231/doc/debug.php) for some info (just a random link, just use a search engine for more)).
