    See [REP 103](http://www.ros.org/reps/rep-0103.html) for coordinate system conventions. In general, most coordinate frames follow those conventions, unless otherwise specified.

    The short answer is that counter-clockwise is positive change in yaw in a frame that follows REP103 conventions.
