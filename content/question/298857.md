    Not a complete answer, but: your "first way" is using the `skid_steer_drive_controller` plugin provided by Gazebo, while the "second way" is using the `DiffDriveController` plugin from `ros_control` (hosted by `gazebo_ros_control` in Gazebo).

    Those are definitely not the same.
