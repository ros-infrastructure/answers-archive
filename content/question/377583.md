  body_markdown: "I would strongly recommend applying your logo/texture to your model\
    \ in advance, prior to importing it to gazebo, rather than trying to mess with\
    \ things like material scripts. My preferred approach is using [Blender](https://www.blender.org/)\
    \ (free and open source, though admittedly a bit of a learning curve) to [add\
    \ a texture map](https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/2.79/editors/uv_image/uv/editing/applying_image.html)\
    \ to my mesh (e.g. .stl) files, then export as [collada (.dae) with textures](https://blender.stackexchange.com/questions/12340/export-to-dae-with-textures/12341).\
    \ This is a much stronger approach, especially for something like a logo where\
    \ the relative size, scale, and position of the logo matters. \n\nIf you absolutely\
    \ *need* to use ogre material scripts, its possible... I would recommend starting\
    \ by looking in `~/.gazebo/ogre.log` (or the equivalent location if you aren't\
    \ using Ubuntu) to see if there are any error messages (e.g. \"texture file not\
    \ found\") that might help guide you. And if you still need help, please clarify\
    \ your question first to make it easier for people to figure out where your problem\
    \ is. "
