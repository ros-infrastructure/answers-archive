    \ you want. Here's one of the key parameters to change:\n\n> `~delta` (`float`,\
    \ default: 0.05)\n> \n> Resolution of the map (in metres per occupancy grid block)\n\

    \ (occupying 25 cm\xB2 area) obstacle/wall. You will also need to change other\
