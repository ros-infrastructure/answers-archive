  body_markdown: "I do not know of any support for RADAR sensors in ROS at this time\
    \ (which doesn\xB4t mean it doesn\xB4t exist somewhere, only that their use is\
    \ very uncommon). LIDAR based sensing (SLAM, point cloud processing etc.) however\
    \ is ubiquitous in the ROS world, so from an availability standpoint LIDAR sensors\
    \ are the clear winners.\nIt also appears to me that Velodyne type sensors are\
    \ still the main sensor for 360deg coverage object detection, collision avoidance\
    \ etc. on most (highly) autonomous car projects (certainly the Google one). Caveats\
    \ due to cost or the desire to have a \"clean\" car configuration apply of course."
