    I stumbled upon the solution completely by accident.
    I ran a rospack list and I received this error:

        terminate called after throwing an instance of 'rospack::Exception'
          what():  error parsing manifest of package rosmake at /home/pi/ros_catkin_ws/install_isolated/share/rosmake/package.xml
        Aborted

    Text like this  was at the top of many of the package.xml files:

        <?xml version="1.0"?>
        <?xml-model
          href="http://download.ros.org/schema/package_format3.xsd"
          schematypens="http://www.w3.org/2001/XMLSchema"?>


    I had to go through and remove them all. rospack worked after that.
    So did my launch (well, there are other errors, but they are different than this)
