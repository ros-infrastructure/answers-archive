    The answer depends on the number of spin loops you are running. Have a look at [this page](http://ros.org/wiki/roscpp/Overview/Callbacks%20and%20Spinning).

    The short answer is: if you call `spin` only once in your applications, you shouldn't need to synchronize. If you have multiple spinners, synchronize.
