I would like to have a ROS2 Node that hosts a service which will be called from another machine. However it is very important for this node to know if the connection to the other machine is currently available. I have currently two different ideas and maybe you folks can share your opinions on my ideas and maybe other ideas on how to do this. My ideas are the following:  
1. using `system("ping IP_ADRESS -w 1");` inside the node to check if the other machine can be pinged in less than one second.  
2. Just continuously publish a heartbeat message on the other machine and check if those messages are being received.  
Is any of these two options viable or is there a better way to do this? Maybe a "ROS way" of doing it?
