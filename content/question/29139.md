    Why do you want it to be a revolute joint, and not a fixed joint? Is it because you want to simulate sometimes with the motor up and sometimes with the motor down?

    You could try to add the JointPositionController plugin, see [this demo](https://github.com/gazebosim/gz-sim/blob/gz-sim7/examples/worlds/joint_position_controller.sdf).
    It optionally has an [initial position field](https://github.com/gazebosim/gz-sim/blob/3bde4b5318f9bed37e2fc8dbe5877bbdc9b73ae1/src/systems/joint_position_controller/JointPositionController.hh#L97-L98).

    There's currently an [open issue](https://github.com/gazebosim/gz-sim/issues/1997) but this is likely irrelevant to your usecase.
