I have an issue I don't understand with tf in Python. Consider this code:

    rospy.init_node('test')
    l = tf.TransformListener()
    # rospy.sleep(0.5)
    # l.waitForTransform('BODY', 'manipulation_marker', rospy.Time(0), rospy.Time(10))
