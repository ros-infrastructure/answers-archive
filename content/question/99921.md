    I don't believe there is an official wired PS3 controller, see the complaints here:
    http://community.us.playstation.com/t5/PlayStation-3/I-am-extremely-angry-at-Sony-no-wired-controllers/td-p/30842367

    ROS does support PS3 controllers over bluetooth:
    http://wiki.ros.org/ps3joy/Tutorials/PairingJoystickAndBluetoothDongle

    See here for turtlebot's example launch file:
    https://github.com/turtlebot/turtlebot_apps/blob/hydro-devel/turtlebot_teleop/launch/ps3_teleop.launch

    If you need a wired controller, I would look at something that support's Linux's joystick API:
    http://wiki.ros.org/joy

    A new option would be to get a PS4 controller. Currently, these won't work over Bluetooth, but from our testing they are immediately recognized as a joystick by Ubuntu Linux 12.04 when connected over USB. You could then use the standard joy_node (http://wiki.ros.org/joy).

    Note that the button mappings appear to be entirely different  from the popular PS3 controller.
