    The simple answer is no. gmapping needs odometry.

    You can either use SLAM that doesn't require this as [hector_mapping](http://www.ros.org/wiki/hector_mapping) or find an intermediate node that can produce odometry from IMU or laser + imu. http://www.ros.org/wiki/laser_scan_matcher should provide this functionality.
