    The `frame_id` in the `PointCloud2` header names the relevant `tf` frame of reference.

    ROS handles transforms between coordinate frames using the [tf](http://ros.org/wiki/tf) package. That component is complex, not easily summarized here. Please consult the [tf design documentation](http://ros.org/wiki/tf/Design) and the [tf tutorials](http://ros.org/wiki/tf/Tutorials) for more information.
