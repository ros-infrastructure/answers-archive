  body_markdown: "On a real robot you probably can't have exactly the same trajectory\
    \ every time. \n\nThere is always delay in any sensor. Plus, there are random\
    \ odometry errors and actuator errors. There will also be random timing variation\
    \ in the arrival of various messages from independent sources.\n\nDepending on\
    \ how you implement your navigation algorithm, you may be able to minimize many\
    \ of those random factors with a feedback control loop."
