      * [Insert a Model from file](http://gazebosim.org/api/1.2.0/classgazebo_1_1physics_1_1World.html#a134428887e91b276a59cbd0a66196aa3)

    1.  Via a msgs::Factory message on the "~/factory" topic.
    1. Use the gzfactory command line tool.
