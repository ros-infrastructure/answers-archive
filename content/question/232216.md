    \ callbacks.\n3. Do other nodes need the data you publish on the `navStuff` topic?\

    \ easier ways to handle data inside your program e.g. store it in a global or\
    \ member variable.\n\nIf I understand you right you need data from both `/imu_um6/mag`\
    \ and `/gps/fix`. As soon as you got both you need to process it and publish to\
    \ `/husky_velocity_controller/cmd_vel`. What you have to do is, store the data\
    \ from these two topics in a global variable. In your `run` method you wait until\
    \ you got your data and then process it and publish it.\n\n\n----------\n\n\n\
