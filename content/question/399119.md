    I had a same problem and solved it by change the `bt_navigator:`session inside the .yaml param file.

        bt_navigator:
          ros__parameters:
            use_sim_time: False
            bt_xml_filename: "navigate_w_replanning_and_recovery.xml"

        bt_navigator:

            use_sim_time: True
