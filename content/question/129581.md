    Looking at the [documentation for the LaserScan message](http://docs.ros.org/api/sensor_msgs/html/msg/LaserScan.html), it looks like the `time_increment` value should be used for the time between measurements within a scan (in you case 3ms), and the `scan_time` should be set to the time between scans (in your case 100ms for a 10Hz scan rate).
