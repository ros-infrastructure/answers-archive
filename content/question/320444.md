    A more ROS/component based approach could be something like this: a node B that subscribes to the topics that carry the msgs that you want to "fake" and then use a [mux](http://wiki.ros.org/topic_tools/mux) that switches between the real topics and the ones from node B. Switching the mux is part of your (higher-level) coordination system.

    ---

    Edit: it's still not completely clear why "the most recent message" must be sent to Cartographer when the robot "isn't operating normally". *That* I believe is the xy-problem here. But let's not go into that now.

    If Cartographer is complaining then wouldn't it make sense to debug that? If the messages you are sending from your class `A` and class `B` are identical (save the timestamps most likely), then either you're violating some (implicit) assumption in Cartographer, or there is an actual bug in Cartographer. Both cases would seem to be something to look at in Cartographer.
