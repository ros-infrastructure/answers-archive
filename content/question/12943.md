    ROS is designed to run on full computers; not microcontrollers.

    You need a full computer with at least several hundred MB of RAM (1GB is better) and a reasonably fast processor (1GHz+) to run navigation.

    You may be able to port [rosserial](http://ros.org/wiki/rosserial) to your PIC and use it to pass control signals to and from a PC running ROS.

    **EDIT:**

    For Linux, I recommend downloading and installing Ubuntu. It's very easy and ROS is well-supported on ubuntu.

    For ROS in general, start with the [ROS Tutorials](http://ros.org/wiki/ROS/Tutorials).

    You'll want to either write a custom protocol and driver node to communicate with your PIC, or [port rosserial](http://ros.org/wiki/rosserial_client/Tutorials/Adding%20Support%20for%20New%20Hardware) to the PIC and use the rosserial driver to communicate over a serial port.
