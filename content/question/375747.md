The `robot_base_frame` parameter appears in both local and global costmaps and should match the robot.  Here are some helpful links, including tutorials and example files:

     - [Navigation tutorials](http://wiki.ros.org/navigation#Tutorials)
     - [TurtleBot3 tutorials](https://emanual.robotis.com/docs/en/platform/turtlebot3/simulation/)
     - [TurtleBot3 navigation files](https://github.com/ROBOTIS-GIT/turtlebot3/tree/master/turtlebot3_navigation) (useful reference)
