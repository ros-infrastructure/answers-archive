Hello,
I am working on the open-source ApolloAuto framework(https://github.com/ApolloAuto/apollo).
Previously they used ROS for their communication, but now they use CyberRT , from version 3.5 onwards.
For those of you who are not aware, CyberRT is Baidu's version of ROS, used for higher performance. 
There is also a migration guide from ROS to Cyber in their official page -  https://cyber-rt.readthedocs.io/en/latest/CyberRT_Migration_Guide.html

My query is : Have any of you tried to migrate CyberRT to ROS ?
