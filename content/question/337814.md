    Yes it is possible, the pc on your robot will master and your pc will be slave. You can follow this [tutorial](https://github.com/ut-ims-robotics/tutorials/wiki/Running-ROS-over-multiple-computers) to accomplish that. Once the communication is established you can run your lidar node on intel board and camera node on your own pc. But you have to keep bandwidth limitations in your mind.

      I would suggest you go over ROS beginner [tutorials](http://wiki.ros.org/ROS/Tutorials) and get a grasp of how ROS ecosystem works. From this you will get at least basic understanding of what you can do and how to do it and you will be better able to navigate your way instead of asking every small detail. To answer your question ROS distro comes with a lot of messages and you can also make your own messages if needed. Float is part of `std_msgs/Float32` and so is string.
      You can also use command line to publish a message like
      `rostopic pub /topic_name message_type message`. Where you can tab autocomplete message_type and message.
