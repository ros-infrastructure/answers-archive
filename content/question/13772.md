    I'll update this answer as you provide more info and I find more things, but for starters the mass and inertia on your rollers is wrong. You can approximate them with cylinders using an equation like the ones from [here](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_moments_of_inertia). You could also use meshlab or a cad program to give you them.

    [some useful info on friction](http://gazebosim.org/tutorials?tut=friction&cat=physics)

    I'd also recommend setting `implicit_spring_damper` true for all joints. Also, I'd add a very small amount of damping to all joints, `.01` or so.

    This URDF isn't much help without the meshes, so you should zip everything (including model.config) up and then we can test it out

    I was having the same problem.

    Can you see a side-view of your mecanum wheel? If the rollers are cylindrical, the side profile of the mecanum wheel is nearly octagonal, which causes the robot to move jaggedly. The mecanum rollers need to have a curvature to them, to cause the mecanum wheel to be nearly circular.

    Properly curved mecanum roller:
    ![image description](https://ftp.osuosl.org/pub/ros/download.ros.org/downloads/se_migration/gazebosim/16651781333827452.png)
