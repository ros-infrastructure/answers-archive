  - body_markdown: "Find the rest-state orientation (say `q0`), and rotate the accelerometer\
      \ reading by that amount.\n\n`accel_corrected =   q0*accel_measured*q0c`, where\
      \ `q0c` is the quaternion conjugate and `*` is the quaternion multiplication.\
      \ \n\nFor the given values, you have\n\n    q0 = [0.975320518017, 0.0196061972529,\
      \ -0.100331619382, 0.19570158422]\n    accel_meas = [0, 1.93930334473, 0.0191536132812,\
      \ 9.28710823975]\n    q0c = [0.975320518017,-0.0196061972529,0.100331619382,-0.19570158422]\n\
      \    accel_corrected = [0,-0.002,0.03047,9.4874]\n\nAlso, don't forget to normalize\
      \ and scale it to 9.81."
