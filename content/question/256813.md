  body_markdown: "It seems to me that a good place to start is to look at the occupancy\
    \ grid map message from the nav_msgs package\nhttp://docs.ros.org/api/nav_msgs/html/msg/OccupancyGrid.html\n\
    \nAlso look at the laser scan message from sensor_msgs\nhttp://docs.ros.org/api/sensor_msgs/html/msg/LaserScan.html\n\
    \nTo visualize it you will probably want to use rviz with the map display type\n\
    http://wiki.ros.org/rviz/DisplayTypes/Map\n\nNow assuming you have a stream of\
    \ LaserScan messages you just need to figure out the math to map each ray to a\
    \ grid cell. To do this you need to know the pose of the laser scanner. You will\
    \ have to translate the ray direction from the laser scanners coordinate frame\
    \ to the world coordinate frame. The tf package might help with that http://wiki.ros.org/tf\n\
    \nThe hard part will be to handle noise and to properly merge together multiple\
    \ laser scans over time to get a good probabilistic map. "
