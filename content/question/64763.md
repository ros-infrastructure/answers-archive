    The `ROS_PACKAGE_PATH` is wrong - it should also include the path to overlay, i.e. to your catkin workspace.

    If `ROS_PACKAGE_PATH` didn't change after `$ source ~/catkin_ws/devel/setup.bash` then there is something wrong with your overlay. Try to [recreate it](http://www.ros.org/wiki/catkin/Tutorials/create_a_workspace).

    **UPDATE**:

    This [tutorial](http://www.ros.org/wiki/catkin/Tutorials/using_a_workspace) may also help. In case you are using *install space* you have to source `setup.bash` from it.

    At any rate it will be better to recreate workspace and try again with `$ source devel/setup.sh`
