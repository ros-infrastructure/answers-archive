  body_markdown: "I'm fairly certain that they are referring to a situation where\
    \ 2 Kinects are both looking at the plane at the same time. The projected patterns\
    \ from the Kinects interfere with each other, and the data obtained from either\
    \ Kinect is likely to be noisier. [Here is a PDF](http://webee.technion.ac.il/~lihi/Teaching/2012_winter_048921/PPT/Roy.pdf)\
    \ of a presentation discussing the issue, and some possible remedies. Specifically,\
    \ see page 20 for examples of a scene with multiple Kinects interfering. Microsoft\
    \ published a paper on the *Shake \u2018n\u2019 Sense*, a very cool technique\
    \ for helping to eliminate the interference. Here's a [demo video](http://research.microsoft.com/apps/video/default.aspx?id=160616&r=1)."
