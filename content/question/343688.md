- body_markdown: "in gazebo , `world` is static coordinate system, all other existing\
    \ frames should be defined w.r.t this `world` frame. When your robot is moving\
    \ around in this world, you usually have `/odom` frame which provides continuous\
    \ transform from your robot center(`/base_link`) to `world`, Assuming your robot\
    \ is moving and exploring around, it will make more sense if camera is attached\
    \ to robot(could be base_link). If you attach the camera to base_link you do not\
    \ need to broadcast additional transform, and probably the reason you do not see\
    \ point cloud is, you have not defined a connection between base_link and camera_link,\
    \ so in RVIZ you would be seeing something like; \n\n    No transform from [blabla_link]\
    \ to [/some_link]"
