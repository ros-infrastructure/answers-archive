    Without digging into the essential reason, in [its CMakeLists.txt](http://alufr-ros-pkg.googlecode.com/svn/trunk/freiburg_tools/rosgui_paramedit/CMakeLists.txt), comment out these lines like below and `rosmake` again. Plugin seems to be working for me (Ubuntu Quantal), although I'm not aware of the effect so far.

        #rosbuild_include(pluginlib hide_plugin_symbols)
        #pluginlib_hide_plugin_symbols(rosgui_paramedit ParamEdit)
