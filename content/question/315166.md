    You need the `inertial` tag to properly load a model in Gazebo (but not for rivz, that's why you are able to visulaize the urdf with rviz), there is typos in your code : instead of `inertial` you wrote `inertia`. Also the `cylinder_inertia` or `box_inertial_matrix` don't exist, check the [wiki](http://wiki.ros.org/urdf/XML/link#Elements) to see the elements inside the inertial tag.
