    \ or just add it in the launch file:\n\n    <node pkg=\"leg_detector\" type=\"\
    leg_detector\" name=\"leg_detector\" args=\"scan:=scan $(find leg_detector)/config/trained_leg_detector.yaml\"\

    odom\" />\n    </node>\n\nIf you don't have an odometry frame just use the one\
    \ of the laser and see if that works for you.\n\n "
