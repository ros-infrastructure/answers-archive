  body_markdown: "First, I'm not sure it's a good idea to try and communicate between\
    \ two different versions of ROS. That could lead to some problems. \n\nSecond,\
    \ have you configured the network properly? Try taking a look [here](http://www.ros.org/wiki/ROS/Tutorials/MultipleMachines).\
    \ The general idea is that you choose a PC to be your \"master\". On this machine,\
    \ before starting roscore or a roslaunch, you must perform (where MASTER_IP is\
    \ the IP address of your \"master\" computer):\n\n    export ROS_MASTER_URI=http://MASTER_IP:11311\n\
    \    export ROS_IP=MASTER_IP\n\nOn your second machine, you perform the following\
    \ (where SLAVE_IP is the IP address of the second machine): \n\n    export ROS_MASTER_URI=http://MASTER_IP:11311\n\
    \    export ROS_IP=SLAVE_IP\n\nYou must do this in every terminal that you use."
