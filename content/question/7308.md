    Closed kinematic chains are not officially supported with URDF, though they are supported with SDF. Here is an [example of an SDF model with closed chain](https://bitbucket.org/osrf/gazebo/issue/863/tracksdriveplugin). Also, the PR2 gripper has a 4-bar linkage.

    We are planning to create a [unit test for closed chains](https://bitbucket.org/osrf/gazebo/issue/863/tracksdriveplugin), but haven't done it yet.
