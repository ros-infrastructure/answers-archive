    See [$(eval &lt;expr&gt;)](http://wiki.ros.org/roslaunch/XML).
    You may thus do it as:

        <launch>
          <arg name="n" />
          <param name="something" value="$(eval '/dev/video' + arg('n'))"/>
        </launch>
