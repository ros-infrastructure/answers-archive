    That's a bit of an esoteric need, so you'll probably have to write your own node to make the conversion.

    The simplest approach would be to use the tools in [laser_geometry](http://wiki.ros.org/laser_geometry) to initially convert the laserscan into a pointcloud. Then iterate through each point in the cloud and 'extrude' it by adding additional points with different z values, with whatever your required density is.
