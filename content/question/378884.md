    Looks like the /radar_scan topic is of type [/RadarScan](https://github.com/radar-lab/ti_mmwave_rospkg/blob/master/msg/RadarScan.msg). The [bag_to_pcd](http://wiki.ros.org/pcl_ros#bag_to_pcd) package requires sensor_msgs/PointCloud2 type.

    You will have to find a way to convert `RadarScan` to `PointCloud2` type and it should work fine.
