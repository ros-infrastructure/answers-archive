    I don't know of any gazebo plugin designed for a 6-wheeled vehicle. Fortunately, it's very easy to write your own plugin. You can start by copying the skid steer or diff drive plugin.

    There are lots of [tutorials](http://gazebosim.org/tutorials?cat=plugins), and [examples](https://bitbucket.org/osrf/gazebo/src/default/examples/plugins/).

    There is a 6 wheel plugin and a generalized multi wheel plugin as parts of [hector\_gazebo\_plugins](https://github.com/tu-darmstadt-ros-pkg/hector_gazebo/tree/indigo-devel/hector_gazebo_plugins). Those do not permit actuating the axis independently, but could be modified to do so. Note they are gazebo ROS plugins and not plain gazebo ones.

    A usage example is available [here starting in line 130](https://github.com/tu-darmstadt-ros-pkg/taurob_tracker_common/blob/master/taurob_tracker_description/urdf/tracker_chassis.gazebo.xacro.xml#L130).
