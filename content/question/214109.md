    Check out [this Tutorial on having a `rosbuild` workspace on top of a `catkin` workspace](http://wiki.ros.org/catkin/Tutorials/using_rosbuild_with_catkin#rosbuild_workspace_on_top_of_catkin_workspace).

    However, note the drawback:

    - You cannot depend from a `rosbuild` package on a `catkin` package.

    Thus, it would be worthwhile to switch to `catkin` for all packages.
