  body_markdown: "20mm is really really narrow. Depending on distance to the scanner\
    \ and the resolution, such a pole will result in only a few laser points, if at\
    \ all. \nMy suggestion would be to do some basic calculations first to see the\
    \ number of points the lidar will 'see' a snowpole with at what range. \n\nMy\
    \ bet is that that is relatively low and thus that a lidar is not really a great\
    \ way to achieve this goal. \nIf I'm wrong and you do get eg. tens of points on\
    \ a snowpole, you could do a Hough transform to fit circles on those points. Or\
    \ just clustering could be sufficient. \n\nIf lidar does turn out to *not* be\
    \ useful, a stereo-camera that filters out red blobs (which should be do-able\
    \ against a snowy background) could be an option worth considering."
