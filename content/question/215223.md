    You use messages for many-to-many communication over [ROS Topics](http://wiki.ros.org/Topics), whereas [Services](http://wiki.ros.org/Services) provide Request/Response communication.

    So both could be used what you try to do, depending on your actual use case.

    See the [Tutorials Page on the ROS wiki](http://wiki.ros.org/ROS/Tutorials), especially the Beginner tutorial sections 10. for creating msgs and srvs, 11-13 for Topic communication via Publisher/Subscribers and 14-16 for Services.
    From the explanation there, the differences should become clear.
