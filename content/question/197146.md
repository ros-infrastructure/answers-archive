    We have used the [Epics library](http://www.aps.anl.gov/epics/) for communicating with AB PLCs.  It's not a ROS interface exactly, but it can easily allow a ROS node (or any C++ program) to communicate with the PLC.  The libraries typically communicate at a *tag* level with the PLC (similar to most other methods).
