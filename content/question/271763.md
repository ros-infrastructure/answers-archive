    There seems to be a mismatch between joint names. In line 37 of the [dynamixel tutorial](http://wiki.ros.org/dynamixel_controllers/Tutorials/Creatingdynamixelactionclient) : ```arm = Joint('f_arm')``` you need to set the same name as in your motor driver, i.e. ```arm = Joint('joint0_position_controller')```

    here I post a snippet of our arm driver based on [dynamixel_motor](https://github.com/socrob/dynamixel_motor).

          <node pkg="dynamixel_controllers" type="controller_spawner.py" name="joint_position_controller_spawner" output="screen"
                args="--manager=cyton_manager
                      --port left_arm_port
                      --type=simple
                      joint0_position_controller" />
