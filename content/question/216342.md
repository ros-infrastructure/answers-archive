    One option is to use the [PCL ](http://wiki.ros.org/perception_pcl), as suggested by NEngelhard. There's a tutorial on how to compute an oriented bounding box [here](http://pointclouds.org/documentation/tutorials/moment_of_inertia.php). This algorithm is based on PCA (Principal Component Analysis), which works well for most "longish" objects; for more compact objects (like cubes), the computed bounding box is not optimal.

    Alternatively, you could use [octomap](http://wiki.ros.org/octomap) to generate a voxel map from your point cloud. It will give you a better estimate of the object's volume, and a much better approximation of its shape.
