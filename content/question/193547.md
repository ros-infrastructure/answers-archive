    PCL has tools to generate an image from the point cloud directly ([basic](http://wiki.ros.org/pcl_ros/Tutorials/CloudToImage) or [range image](http://pointclouds.org/documentation/tutorials/range_image_creation.php)), however not for dealing with two parallel objects. The above links will work if you're willing to take a hit in quality, but if you want to work with the original RGB image, you'll have to do some manual calculations.

    You could [project](http://www.pointclouds.org/documentation/tutorials/project_inliers.php) the pointcloud onto a plane representing the rectified* image, and use the points to mark which pixels you want to 'keep'. Or just project the bounding box and.

    *: I am not a vision expert, so this may not be rigorous
