    If you use a GPU ray sensor, you can remove collisions from your particles, because the sensor only detects the visuals.

    If you use a (CPU) ray sensor, you can try playing with [collision bitmasks](http://gazebosim.org/tutorials?tut=collide_bitmask&cat=physics).
