  body_markdown: "The closest thing to `gazebo_ros_control` for Ignition/ROS 2 is\
    \ [`ign_ros2_control`](https://github.com/ignitionrobotics/ign_ros2_control),\
    \ with its recently opened [PR](https://github.com/ignitionrobotics/ign_ros2_control/pull/1).\
    \ You can hear more information about it in [\U0001F525 Edifice Demos! Ignition\
    \ Community Meeting (March 2021)](https://youtu.be/aKpUSXgZXYM?t=2079).\n\nIf\
    \ you need something more simple/custom, you can also use [`JointController`](https://github.com/ignitionrobotics/ign-gazebo/blob/26832764749291b6ea22b525262136ecbb63fbb9/examples/worlds/joint_controller.sdf),\
    \ [`JointPositionController`](https://github.com/ignitionrobotics/ign-gazebo/blob/26832764749291b6ea22b525262136ecbb63fbb9/examples/worlds/joint_position_controller.sdf)\
    \ or [`JointTrajectoryController` (for multiple joints)](https://github.com/ignitionrobotics/ign-gazebo/blob/26832764749291b6ea22b525262136ecbb63fbb9/examples/worlds/joint_trajectory_controller.sdf)\
    \ in combination with [`ros_ign_bridge` (`ros2` branch)](https://github.com/ignitionrobotics/ros_ign/tree/ros2/ros_ign_bridge),\
    \ i.e. publish commands in ROS 2 and subscribe to them in the individual Ignition\
    \ `Joint*Controller` plugin(s)."
