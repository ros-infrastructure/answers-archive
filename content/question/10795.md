    \ Similarly, when AMCL is not well localized, it will generate more particles\
    \ per step, and therefore require more computation.\n\nThe 'A' in AMCL (for \"\
    adaptive\") refers to the algorithm's ability to choose the particle count automatically,\
    \ for exactly this reason. "
