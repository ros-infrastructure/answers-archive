  body_markdown: "> when I manually try to move the robot with the interactive marker,\
    \ the marker simply does not move towards y-axis. \n\nThe interactive marker,\
    \ as used by the MoveIt RViz plugin, is a 6 DoF marker.\n\nIt's inherently impossible\
    \ for a 5 DoF robot to reach all the poses a 6 DoF marker can assume.\n\nThat's\
    \ the immediate cause of the behaviour you observe.\n\n> I thought the marker\
    \ is not moving because I commanded the robot to move to an unreachable position,\
    \ \n\nIndeed.\n\nBut note: you're not *commanding* anything yet. The marker *only*\
    \ alters the Goal State.\n\n> however since the Query panel succeeded to bring\
    \ the robot to that position, I am clueless.\n\nUsing the `<random valid>` option\
    \ from the dropdown instructs the MoveIt RViz plugin to chose a random *joint\
    \ space pose* for the selected group.\n\nAs joint space poses will always be reachable\
    \ -- no matter the nr of DoF -- this will work.\n\nNo IK is involved there, which\
    \ would explain why you cannnot use the marker, but can set a random joint space\
    \ pose successfully.\n"

Hi all,

recently I am playing around with my custom 5-dof robot. In Rviz MotionPlanning panel, I can add `<random valid>` Goal State to my Query and the corresponding motion can be planned and executed.

However, when I manually try to move the robot with the interactive marker, the marker simply does not move towards y-axis. I thought the marker is not moving because I commanded the robot to move to an unreachable position, however since the Query panel succeeded to bring the robot to that position, I am clueless.

What could be other reasons for this problem?
