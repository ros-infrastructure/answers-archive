  body_markdown: "The values you are looking for are: \n\n    float32 angle_min  \
    \      # start angle of the scan [rad]\n    float32 angle_max        # end angle\
    \ of the scan [rad]\n    float32 angle_increment  # angular distance between measurements\
    \ [rad]\n\nThe i-th range corresponds to angle: angle_min + i * angle_increment.\
    \ Two consecutive ranges are always angle_increment apart. You should read those\
    \ out for the kinect specifics, but I doubt that they are full 360 deg as that\
    \ wouldn't make sense."
