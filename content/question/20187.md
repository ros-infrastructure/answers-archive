    From experiments, I can confirm that, for rotational joints, [`SetForce()'s`](http://osrf-distributions.s3.amazonaws.com/gazebo/api/dev/classgazebo_1_1physics_1_1Joint.html#ab2491053d1c5ebb97c377064797af494) `effort` argument is in **Nm** - at least with the ODE backend.

    As to why it's SetForce() instead of SetTorque(), the [devs wanted to cover both rotational and prismatic joints](http://answers.gazebosim.org/question/5515/setting-force-to-joint-not-torque/).
