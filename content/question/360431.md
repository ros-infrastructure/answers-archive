 As a continuation of this [question](https://answers.ros.org/question/233980/can-a-costmaps-inscribed_radius-and-circumscribed_radius-be-set-from-the-parameter-server/), I understand that inscribed_radius of a robot is calculated solely based on the [robot footprint](https://github.com/ros-planning/navigation/blob/indigo-devel/costmap_2d/src/layered_costmap.cpp#L176). Also, my understanding is that inscribed_radius is initialized [here](https://github.com/ros-planning/navigation/blob/indigo-devel/costmap_2d/src/layered_costmap.cpp#L66). 

If my understanding is correct, then inscribed_radius will be minimum of itself and the footprint calculation.  [See here](http://docs.ros.org/jade/api/costmap_2d/html/footprint_8cpp_source.html#l00057)

Since the inscribed_radius has been initialized as 0.1, then if the footprint inscribed radius is larger than that, inscribed_radius will always remain 0.1. This is consistent with what I have seen in my testing as well.
