After moving the robots for a bit around my room (manually pushing the robot gently or sending poses via. rviz) the distance and coordinates are sometimes correct and what happens is that it looks like the robots are jumping a meter away from each other. In rviz the visualisation of the robots in the map is correct, so they are definitely not jumping in rviz. This seems to be just the amcl pose and the recording  of the poses.
