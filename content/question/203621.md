  body_markdown: "If there are no obstacles in the world, the laser will not reflect\
    \ off of anything and give no response. This is an error condition which may have\
    \ several causes (such as absorptive bodies) and the data cannot be treated as\
    \ having been cleared to max range. Thus without any obstacles the laser is always\
    \ reporting no data and gmapping cannot build a map. You can update the configuration\
    \ to make this work, but it's a very specific edge case which is rarely encountered\
    \ in the real world and can lead to false negatives on obstacles. \n\nI expect\
    \ the reason the map is jumping around is that it's trying to optimize based on\
    \ close to zero data. Almost any point is equally likely so as gmapping samples\
    \ possible locations different ones win out due to noise in the system."
