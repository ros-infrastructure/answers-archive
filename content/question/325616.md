  body_markdown: |-
    I played back your bag file, and as far as I can see you are not publishing the transform for the laser. You need to tell the ROS world that your laser sensor is rotating by [publishing a transform for it](http://wiki.ros.org/tf/Tutorials/Writing%20a%20tf%20broadcaster%20%28C%2B%2B%29). Ideally this should be published at exactly the same time a frame of laser data comes in, but you should be able to get away with just publishing at a rate faster than the scan rate of the laser.

    If your laser itself (`laser_link`) is the bit that is rotating, then update the transform from `top_plate` to `laser_link`. If it is the top plate that is rotating then update the transform from `base_link` to `top_plate`.
