This is a follow up to [this question](https://answers.gazebosim.org/question/26198/does-setmodelstate-block-such-that-the-next-sensor-readings-account-for-the-modified-scene/).  If my suspicion is correct, I will need some way of checking when `World::Step` was called after `setModelState` in order to reliably get sensor readings that register the updated model positions.

Looking at [`World::RunLoop`](https://github.com/osrf/gazebo/blob/gazebo11/gazebo/physics/World.cc#L537), it seems `World::Step` is called as fast as possible and thus is subject to the computer load, making it nondeterministic.

Given that, is there any programmatic way of checking when `World::Step` has updated the scene?
