    I see that you have two different ros packages with the same name "objects", are you able to put all your objects and meshes into ros packages with different names or just one single ros package?

    Secondly, please take a look at the [gazebo media path section](http://ros.org/wiki/simulator_gazebo/GazeboConfiguration#simulator_gazebo.2BAC8-GazeboConfiguration.2BAC8-cturtle-1.Gazebo_Media_Path) and setup your `manifest.xml` accordingly.  Simply put, you need to put object1.stl in a ros package that depends on `gazebo` directly, and contains the `gazebo_media_path` export in its `manifest.xml`.
