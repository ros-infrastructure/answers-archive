UPDATE:
I created my model in /home/rhexlover/.gazebo/models/my_robot by following the tutorial. And I added the following to my model's sdf file,

    <sdf version='1.5'>
    <world name="default">
    <plugin name="hello_world" filename="libhello_world.so"/>
    </world>
    <model name="my_robot">
    ...

I click my model in Gazebo, and click again to put it in the world. It doesn't show up and in the terminal it prints the entire content of the model's sdf file.

but the plugin works when I call it directly with gzserver:

UPDATE #2:
I think I figured it out. The world tag should not be in model's sdf. When I put my model's sdf into a .world file and the model tag under the world tag, it all works now.
