    Perhaps the easiest solution is to write a node that converts bumper messages to a datatype that can be processed by a [costmap2d obstacle_layer](http://wiki.ros.org/costmap_2d/hydro/obstacles) ([this looks like a potential solution](http://answers.ros.org/question/58111/bumper-in-the-gmapping-costmap-turtlebot/)). I would also make a separate costmap layer for just the bumper sensor to avoid the Kinect marking the region as clear.
