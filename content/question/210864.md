    As a beginner, there are three things you really need to understand (IMHO):
    <ol>
    <li>ROS nodes as independent computational units</li>
    <li>Messages and how they provide an interface between/among nodes</li>
    <li>The callback queue(s) and how to use <code>spin</code>/<code>spinOnce</code> for internal node operation</li>
    </ol>
    With those in hand, you'll be well prepared to build fairly complex systems.
