    The easiest way I believe would be to open your raw data with python and use openCV to convert them into [sensor_msgs/Image](http://docs.ros.org/en/melodic/api/sensor_msgs/html/msg/Image.html). You can also have a look at the [cv_bridge](http://wiki.ros.org/cv_bridge/Tutorials/UsingCvBridgeToConvertBetweenROSImagesAndOpenCVImages).

    Then once you managed to convert your raw data into sensor_msgs/Image messages you will have to save them into a bag. You can use the [rosbag python api](http://wiki.ros.org/rosbag/Code%20API) for that. Have a look at the [rosbag cookbook](https://wiki.ros.org/rosbag/Cookbook). (Especially section 1.2).
