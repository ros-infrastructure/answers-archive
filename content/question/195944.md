  body_markdown: "I think i solved the problem.\n\nthe problem was rrbot.xacro\n\n\
    in the <transmission>state\n\nreplace\n\n`<joint name=\"joint1\"/>` \n\nwith\n\
    \n     <joint name=\"joint1\">\n      <hardwareInterface>EffortJointInterface</hardwareInterface>\n\
    \    </joint>\n\nand all error was cleared\n\nthanks for your help.\n\nRYO"
