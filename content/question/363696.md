Then, in the same node, I want to convert the coordinates from pixels to real 3D world coordinates, using for that the function *rs2_deproject_pixel_to_point* provided by the library *librealsense2* in the Intel Real Sense SDK.

terminate called after throwing an instance of 'rs2::error'
