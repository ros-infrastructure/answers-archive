  body_markdown: "I found the solution to this problem.\n\nI used transmission elements\
    \ for the joints (i.e the BLDC motors are modelled as joints with transmission\
    \ of type \"continuous\") and used the VelocityJointInterface as my hardwareInterface,\
    \ and used velocity_controllers/JointGroupVelocityController in my .yaml file,\
    \ to command all the motors **SIMULTANEOUSLY** .\n\nI also used the libLiftDragPlugin.so\
    \ from Gazebo aerodynamics, that generates thrust when my propellers (modelled\
    \ as thin plate in my .urdf (or) .xacro file) rotate about the joints.\n\nNo need\
    \ of writing complex plugins!\n\nHope this helps. \n\nThank you!"
