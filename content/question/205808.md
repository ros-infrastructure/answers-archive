  body_markdown: "The problem you are describing is one of robot localization. See\
    \ the [amcl](http://wiki.ros.org/amcl) package and [Robot Localization](http://wiki.ros.org/robot_localization)\
    \ packages. The latter supports multiple sensor types, while I believe amcl relies\
    \ on laser scan data. Usually localization happens concurrently with mapping in\
    \ the SLAM problem (simultaneous localization and mapping) and laser scan/GPS/IMU\
    \ sensors are very useful. \n\nThis problem will be easier if you can guarantee\
    \ a known start position on your map, and are able to collect odometry data from\
    \ the robot. The sensors you describe are difficult to use for this problem. A\
    \ laser scan can update the map reliably, then compare it to how far the robot\
    \ move based on its odometry data and it's GPS/IMU readings. Taken in totality\
    \ we then have a much better idea of where the bot is.\n\nIn your case it almost\
    \ seems easier to use dead reckoning for navigation and those sensors for obstacle\
    \ detection and avoidance. Hope this helps. "
