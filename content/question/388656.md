    \ the goal is too close to the wall for teb_local_planner to find any good maneuver.\n\
    \nI have just tested the same situation but with a smaller vehicle: I have simply\
    \ reduced footprint and wheelbase parameters by a factor 2. And now it works rather\
    \ nice:\n\n![image description](https://i.imgur.com/5XHh1hT.png)\n![image description](https://i.imgur.com/udfa5Dh.png)\n\
    ![image description](https://i.imgur.com/XZ4p1py.png)\n\nSo maybe I can solve\
    \ my problem just by moving the goal point a bit further away from the wall\n\n\
    EDIT:\nmaking it work for bigger robots was harder than I thought. There is not\
    \ just the distance to the wall that matters. Actually even without obstacles\
    \ around the planner was unable to find good turn around strategies for the bigger\
    \ vehicle. It always ended up making in-place star shaped turns like this:\n![inefficient\
    \ turn](https://user-images.githubusercontent.com/18645627/137458167-983115a9-5e8e-4556-96c5-347955d55f58.png)\
    \ \n\nI went through a lot tuning and still could not make it work properly. So\
    \ I ended up implementing 2 extras cost functions to the planner: \n\n - one that\
    \ introduces a penalty when the vehicle changes from driving forward to driving\
    \ backward (I described [here](https://github.com/rst-tu-dortmund/teb_local_planner/issues/323))\
    \ to encourage the planner to find 2-3 point turns instead of star shape plans\
    \ like above\n - another which introduces a penalty for not being oriented the\
    \ same than the local planner goal. Basically the idea with this one is to force\
    \ the planner to orient the vehicle properly as soon as possible. This fixed the\
    \ previous behavior I had most of the time: the planner would first want to reach\
    \ destination position and from there would start to wonder how to turn around\
    \ (and fail miserably at it)\n\nI have not tested much the individual contribution\
    \ of each new penalty but the conjunction of both works rather nicely.\n\nI settled\
    \ my weights like this:\n\n - weight_kinematics_nh: 1000\n - weight_same_orientation_than_goal,\
    \ weight_forward_backward_switch, weight_optimal_time, weight_obstacle, weight_dynamic_obstacle\
    \ : 50\n - weight_kinematics_turning_radius: 10\n - other weights: default\n\n\
    As for the Homotopy class planning I increased the area width, scale and number\
    \ of samples. I also reduced h_signature_prescaler to 0.75 which looks a bit magic\
    \ to me but had a positive effect on the quality of the planner generated paths.\
    \ In order to make this run fast enough, I had to reduce a bit the size and resolution\
    \ of my costmap. Most other parameters are defaults (or just scaled to the size\
    \ of the robot)\n\n"
