    You're mixing up two types of controller yaml files: MoveIt and ros_control (confusing I know)

    Examples:
    - A ros_control configuration yaml looks like this: [rrbot_controllers.yaml](https://github.com/PickNikRobotics/ros_control_boilerplate/blob/melodic-devel/rrbot_control/config/rrbot_controllers.yaml)
    - A MoveIt configuration yaml looks like this: [panda_gripper_controllers.yaml](https://github.com/ros-planning/panda_moveit_config/blob/melodic-devel/config/panda_gripper_controllers.yaml)

    They both have a `type` field, and you just have yours mixed up. For the `ElectricGripperController` the type should be "GripperCommand". Your other configs in that yaml are wrong also.

    Your MoveIt configuration by default uses the MoveItSimpleControllerManager. Its simple, [so it only supports two types](https://github.com/ros-planning/moveit/blob/master/moveit_plugins/moveit_simple_controller_manager/src/moveit_simple_controller_manager.cpp): "FollowJointTrajectory" and "GripperCommand"

    I hope this helps!
