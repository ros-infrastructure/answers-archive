    In the documentation for the [costmap_2d](http://wiki.ros.org/costmap_2d#Component_API) they mention that there is a subscription to the `footprint` topic. This led me to believe that you ought to be able to publish on one of the `<costmap>/footprint` and have everything be automatically updated. A quick test shows that this does appear to be the case (although I haven't thoroughly tested). In the [source code](https://github.com/ros-planning/navigation/blob/jade-devel/costmap_2d/src/costmap_2d_ros.cpp#L137-L138) you can see they do indeed subscribe to the topic, and that the subscribed footprint eventually [changes the local attribute](https://github.com/ros-planning/navigation/blob/jade-devel/costmap_2d/src/costmap_2d_ros.cpp#L337-L344), and further, this eventually is [published](https://github.com/ros-planning/navigation/blob/jade-devel/costmap_2d/src/costmap_2d_ros.cpp#L414-L437) back to the topic.

    Additionally, note that in the [Trajectory scoring parameters](http://wiki.ros.org/dwa_local_planner#Trajectory_Scoring_Parameters) of dwa_local_planner, they mention that there is a parameter to scale the size of the footprint based on how fast the robot is going. The actual implementation of this (which is easier to understand than the docs) is [here](https://github.com/ros-planning/navigation/blob/jade-devel/base_local_planner/src/obstacle_cost_function.cpp#L102-L114). This may also fit your use case.
