    Probably the display you are using in RViz is [TF](http://wiki.ros.org/rviz/DisplayTypes/TF). So have a look at [Tutorials](http://wiki.ros.org/tf/Tutorials). Particularly, you can use [tf::TransformListener](http://wiki.ros.org/tf/Tutorials/Writing%20a%20tf%20listener%20%28C%2B%2B%29) to get a coordinate frame transformation between '/world' and '/base_link' frames to acquire robot's pose in global frame.
