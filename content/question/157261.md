  body_markdown: "Hi b3l33\nThe gains of the controller and the inertia of the model\
    \ have default values.\nThis cause the the arm has a lot of inertia and the controller\
    \ is very slow and underdamped.\nThe origin of the oscilations is the gravity,\
    \ acting as a perturbation on the system.\nOther efect is a very large position\
    \ error. When the arm stops, the hand is very down the goal state. You can see\
    \ it in rviz.\n\nI want to develope a best modell next week, but now you can use\
    \ the following;\n\n(This values are tested for hydro-dev branch of  ros-industrial/universal_robot\
    \ package.)\nIn order to improve the behaviour, you should increase the gain of\
    \ the controller, reduce the inertia of the links and increase the damping in\
    \ the joins.\nYou can change all  p gain from 1.000 to 10.000 in the file,\n\n\
    >   ur_gazebo/controller/arm_controller_ur5.yam\n\n    shoulder_pan_joint: {p:\
    \ 10000.0, i: 0.0, d: 0.0, i_clamp: 0.0, i_clamp_min: 0.0, i_clamp_max: 0.0}\n\
    \    shoulder_lift_joint: {p: 10000.0, i: 0.0, d: 0.0, i_clamp: 0.0, i_clamp_min:\
    \ 0.0, i_clamp_max: 0.0}\n    elbow_joint: {p: 10000.0, i: 0.0, d: 0.0, i_clamp:\
    \ 0.0, i_clamp_min: 0.0, i_clamp_max: 0.0}\n\nReduce inertia in all links to inertia\
    \ to  ixx=\"0.002\" ixy=\"0.0\" ixz=\"0.0\" iyy=\"0.02\" iyz=\"0.0\" izz=\"0.002\"\
    \ in files\n>  ur_description/ur5_joint_limited.urdf.xacro   and   ur_description/ur5_joint_limited.urdf.xacro\
    \ \n \n      <inertia ixx=\"0.002\" ixy=\"0.0\" ixz=\"0.0\" iyy=\"0.02\" iyz=\"\
    0.0\" izz=\"0.002\"/>\n\nAnd increase the damping in all joints to 0.5 in files\n\
    >  ur_description/ur5_joint_limited.urdf.xacro   and   ur_description/ur5_joint_limited.urdf.xacro\n\
    \n      <dynamics damping=\"0.5\" friction=\"0.0\"/>\n\n\n"
