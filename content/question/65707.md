    Have you set the `frame_id` of the `header` of the pointcloud-message?

    `clusteredScene->header.frame_id = "my_frame"` should probably do the trick.

    See [pointcloud2 message definition](http://www.ros.org/doc/api/sensor_msgs/html/msg/PointCloud2.html) and [header message definition](http://www.ros.org/doc/api/std_msgs/html/msg/Header.html) for further details on the datatypes.
