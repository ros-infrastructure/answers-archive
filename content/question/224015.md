    Well this obviously is possible, because otherwise the video wouldn't be available ;)

    The hector_slam setup is following the conventions laid out in the [SettingUpForYourRobot](http://wiki.ros.org/hector_slam/Tutorials/SettingUpForYourRobot) tutorial. The octomap is created by feeding Kinect data to a octomap generator node. This part is using tf data generated from SLAM+IMU and essentially performs mapping with known poses.

    To reproduce what is shown in your video, you'll want to get SLAM working and have a proper robot model (i.e. have the Kinect mounted correctly on your model and a complete tf tree). Generating the octomap then boils down to feeding the Kinect data to a octomap generator node. The underlying ROS tools (tf, octomap) then will do their magic and create a map.

    [These slides](http://www.ist.tugraz.at/ssrr13_mediawiki/images/d/d3/Kohlbrecher_3D_Perception_RoboCup_RRL_and_DRC.pdf) provide a brief overview.
