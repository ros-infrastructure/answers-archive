    The official Gazebo version for Dashing (and Eloquent) is the LTS at the time they were released, i.e. Gazebo 9, as documented on [REP 2000](https://www.ros.org/reps/rep-2000.html#dashing-diademata-may-2019-may-2021). That's what you'll get if you install `ros-dashing-gazebo-ros-pkgs`.

    But there's no reason not to compile `gazebo_ros_pkgs`'s `dashing` branch from source against Gazebo 11. If it doesn't work out of the box, it shouldn't be too much work to open a PR adding support. We could also consider shipping `ros-dashing-gazebo11-ros-pkgs` from https://packages.osrfoundation.org if there's enough interest.

    As for Foxy, Gazebo 11 and Ignition Citadel will be the [official versions](https://github.com/ros-infrastructure/rep/pull/217/files).
