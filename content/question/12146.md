  body_markdown: "The issue with calibration failing is probably due to the algorithm\
    \ used to convert the scan of a wall to an angular measurement. On the Kinect,\
    \ the field of view is very small (about 57 degrees wide), whereas your Hokuyo\
    \ probably sees 180 degrees. Given that, your calculation for wall angle is seriously\
    \ disturbed, since it uses all the scans. I'd suggest using dynamic_reconfigure\
    \ to reduce the scan angle of the Hokuyo to about 60 degrees before calibration\
    \ (you can use the full scan later on for map making). "
