    Costmaps are generally aligned to a semi-fixed frame like odometry for efficiency of moving the costmap. If the costmap needs to move forward , the operation of moving rows is much quicker than calculating a transform and applying that to existing costmap data. The [code is not even set up to monitor rotations](https://github.com/ros-planning/navigation/blob/kinetic-devel/costmap_2d/src/costmap_2d.cpp#L260). So I think the short answer is no.
