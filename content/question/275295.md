  body_markdown: |-
    There are no convenience wrappers for this afaik (so no `rospy.loginfo_named(..)` or similar), but `rospy` does support named loggers since [ros/ros_comm#948](https://github.com/ros/ros_comm/pull/948).

    They would be used like so (taken from the unit test):

        rospy.loginfo("test child logger 1", logger_name="log1")

    Note: this is only available on Lunar <del>and Kinetic (backported in [ros/ros_comm#1205](https://github.com/ros/ros_comm/pull/1205))</del> not on Kinetic ("new feature, not backported").

Is there a `rospy` equivalent of the `ROS_*_NAMED` family of logging functions? Any release of ROS will do.
