    Best way is probably to use [URDF](http://www.ros.org/wiki/urdf/Tutorials/Create%20your%20own%20urdf%20file) and [robot_state_publisher](http://www.ros.org/wiki/robot_state_publisher) to define and publish your robot description, including sensor location, to tf.

    Then, if you publish your sensor data using one of the message types with a [built-in rviz display](http://www.ros.org/wiki/rviz/DisplayTypes) (e.g. sensor_msgs/Range) you will be able to easily visualize it on 3D.
