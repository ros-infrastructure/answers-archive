    Looks like you didn't source the usual `/usr/share/drcsim/setup.sh` on the OCU, which resulted in bad/unset Gazebo/OGRE paths.  I've added that to the [User Guide](http://gazebosim.org/wiki/CloudSim/VRCUserGuide#Step_2:_Setup_the_private_network_on_the_OCU) and opened [a ticket](https://bitbucket.org/osrf/cloudsim/issue/117/add-usr-share-drcsim-setupsh-to-router) to add that line to the generated `router/ros.bash`.
