    I'm not sure, but I don't think its usually the case that manipulators expose a `geometry_msgs/Twist` interface. So i'd go for your option 3.

    Most of the planners ROS uses out-of-the-box generate plans in *joint space*, so a [trajectory_msgs/JointTrajectory](http://docs.ros.org/api/trajectory_msgs/html/msg/JointTrajectory.html) interface would seem like a logical thing to have. You can probably look at some other implementations for inspiration.

    ---

    Edit: if you're starting from scratch: be sure to take a look at [ros_control](http://wiki.ros.org/ros_control), and for arms/manipulators, the [joint_trajectory_controller](http://wiki.ros.org/joint_trajectory_controller). Could save you a lot of work.
