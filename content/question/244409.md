    Firstly, you need a ROS driver node for URG-04LX. Both [hokuyo_node](http://wiki.ros.org/hokuyo_node) and [urg_node](http://wiki.ros.org/urg_node) can do this. However, neither of these nodes support ROS Kinetic directly. You can refer to my question [How to install hokuyo_node/urg_node on kinetic?](http://answers.ros.org/question/243232/how-to-install-hokuyo_nodeurg_node-on-kinetic/)

    And for SLAM mapping, I can't tell which method is best. But I used [gmapping](http://wiki.ros.org/gmapping) with URG-04LX-UG01, it works well.
