    \ using a serial communicating between ROS (your PC or Notebook) and the embedded\
    \ application (your parallax in the robot) . The small device is going to drive\
    \ all pheriperical devices like motor, servos, sensors etc... but sendd and receives\
    \ what to do and how to do from the PC (through a serial communication).\nWith\
    \ ROS you are able to run really complex systems to manage all you want to do.\
    \ \nI know it could be a little bit surprising for you, but this is the philosophy\
    \ of ROS. You create Nodes which Communicate...\nI hope this help you a little\
