    \ for us to track MoveIt! specific questions there.</s>\n\nMoveIt! uses KDL for\
    \ setting up the default kinematics solver. KDL does not support planar joints\
    \ so that's why you are having trouble setting up a group like this. However,\
    \ the fact that KDL does not support the planar joint only means that MoveIt!\
    \ will not do the full inverse kinematics for you directly. You can still send\
    \ individual joint values as a goal for the motion planning and MoveIt! will plan\
    \ a path for you. \n\nWe will look into adding planar joint support into KDL in\
    \ the future."
