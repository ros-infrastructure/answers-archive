    ROS provides the `/rosout` topic, logging functions in both [C++](http://wiki.ros.org/roscpp/Overview/Logging) (`ROS_INFO()`) and [Python](http://wiki.ros.org/rospy/Overview/Logging) (`rospy.loginfo()`), and [rqt_console](http://wiki.ros.org/rqt_console), to facilitate viewing log messages from remote machines. (and much, much more!)

    There's a [rospy Tutorial on Logging](http://wiki.ros.org/rospy_tutorials/Tutorials/Logging) and describes how all of these tools fit together.
