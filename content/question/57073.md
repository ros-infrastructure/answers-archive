    If you need a simple 3D viewer of the pointcloud produced by the kinect, I'd go with the PCL. They provide a lightweight viewer as well as a full-featured one:

    * simple CloudViewer: [http://www.pointclouds.org/documentation/tutorials/cloud_viewer.php](http://www.pointclouds.org/documentation/tutorials/cloud_viewer.php)
    * PCL Visualizer: [http://pointclouds.org/documentation/tutorials/pcl_visualizer.php](http://pointclouds.org/documentation/tutorials/pcl_visualizer.php)

    Just register a callback that accesses the XYZRGB-Pointcloud of the kinect and, inside the callback, provide the pointcloud to the viewer.
