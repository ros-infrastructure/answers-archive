    AFAIR, ROS2 (post-bouncy) uses `ament`  and, preferrably, [`colcon` (tutorial)](https://index.ros.org/doc/ros2/Tutorials/Colcon-Tutorial/) to build its packages. `catkin` is ROS1 only.


    Side Note:
    Quick googling led to a possibility of building ROS2 packages with `catkin_make_isolated` [(see here)](https://index.ros.org/doc/ros2/Tutorials/catment/#id5), but I don't think this is what you want...
