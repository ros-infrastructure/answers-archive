  body_markdown: "In your costmap configuration, include the obstacle marking from\
    \ both sensors using:\n\n    observation_sources: laser_scan_sensor us_sensor_data\n\
    \    \n    laser_scan_sensor: {sensor_frame: link, data_type: LaserScan, topic:\
    \ /scan, marking: true, clearing: true}\n    \n    us_sensor_data: {sensor_frame:\
    \ link, data_type: Range, topic: /us_data_topic, marking: true, clearing: true}\n\
    \nThis will result in data from both sensors to mark obstacles in your costmaps."
