    The most common way that people do this is to use either [robot_pose_ekf](http://wiki.ros.org/robot_pose_ekf) or [robot_localization](http://wiki.ros.org/robot_localization) (more common these days) to fuse together the IMU and odometry data, and publish the fused result as a new tf frame. Then you can specify the `~odom_frame_id` in amcl to be this new filtered frame (usually called `/odom_combined` with robot_pose_ekf and `/odom_filt` for robot_localization).
