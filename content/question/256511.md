    well after doing some research, I found out that my ROS_IP was not set correctly.  So, I followed the instructions [here](http://projects.csail.mit.edu/pr2/wiki/index.php?title=ROS_IP)  and set my ROS_IP to 127.0.0.1 using:
    export ROS_IP=127.0.0.1

    Hope this helps someone else.
