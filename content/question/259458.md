    After trying a few tools recommended here and various sources, the one that worked end to end is provided by the [MRPT](http://www.mrpt.org/list-of-mrpt-apps/application_camera-calib/) project.

    It uses the pinhole method to calibrate the camera. It has worked fine on an iPhone 5, although I have little practice with the generated parameters at this point.
