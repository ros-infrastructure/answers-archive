  body_markdown: "If the problem is only when rotating then, as suggested by @spmaniato,\
    \  you need to adjust your robot's wheel base (distance between wheels) parameter\
    \ on your kinematic model.  \n\nFrom what I remember when I had to calibrate my\
    \ robot's odometry, if your angular error grows clockwise you need to reduce the\
    \ wheel base parameter. If the angular error grows counterclockwise then increase\
    \ the parameter (might be the other way round :P)\n\nGood luck!"
