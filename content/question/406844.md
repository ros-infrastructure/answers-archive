    This is possible. There are various options for headsets and cameras. I'd encourage you to research this yourself. Most cameras are plug 'n play with ROS(2) using [usb_cam](https://github.com/ros-drivers/usb_cam/tree/ros2). You can either build your own robot from scratch or you can buy one of the many robots available. One example to look at is the ROS2 Turtlebot.

    Here's an [article on sensor selection for mobile robots](https://www.freedomrobotics.com/blog/how-to-pick-navigation-sensors-for-autonomous-mobile-robot) that might help (disclaimer: I wrote it).
