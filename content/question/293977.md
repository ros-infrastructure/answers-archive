My ideal state of 2 link arm is the following.
When I change the ensor of inertia as the following , it is better than the above model.

> Before

     <inertia
      ixx="${mass / 12.0 * (width*width + width*width)}" ixy="0.0" ixz="0.0"

      izz="${mass / 12.0 * (height1*height1 + width*width)}"/>
    </inertial>

> After

     <inertia
      ixx="${mass / 12.0 * (width*width + height1*height1)}" ixy="0.0" ixz="0.0"

      izz="${mass / 12.0 * (width*width + width*width)}"/>
    </inertial>
