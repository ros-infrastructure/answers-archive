  body_markdown: " Gazebo ships with a [DiffDrivePlugin](https://bitbucket.org/osrf/gazebo/src/c61ffc681bb0f06b5d5ec500d758fa1d97f27737/plugins/DiffDrivePlugin.cc?at=default&fileviewer=file-view-default)\
    \ example. Unfortunately, it isn't very well documented. But it is used in the\
    \ [pioneer2dx](https://bitbucket.org/osrf/gazebo_models/src/e024fe922c810f468e2a7ab277750354465478cd/pioneer2dx/model.sdf?at=default&fileviewer=file-view-default)\
    \ model, so you can try it out and then adapt it to your needs. You can test using\
    \ it like this:\n\n1. Open Gazebo and insert the `pioneer2dx` from the left panel's\
    \ insert tab. The model loads the plugin, which will listen to pose messages on\
    \ topic `~/pioneer2dx/vel_cmd`.\n\n2. To test sending commands, you'll need a\
    \ publisher. You can modify the stand-alone [publisher](https://bitbucket.org/osrf/gazebo/src/c61ffc681bb0/examples/stand_alone/publisher/?at=default)\
    \ example by changing the topic to `~/pioneer2dx/vel_cmd` and the pose to whatever\
    \ you want. \n\n"
