  body_markdown: "You instantiate a new publisher everytime your callback posCallback\
    \ is called. There is a time gap between instantiating a publisher till a publish\
    \ call to it actually publishes, as things are threaded behind the scenes. So\
    \ what happens everytime your posCallback is called:\n\n - Publisher pub_twist\
    \ gets instantiated\n - publish() is called on the instantiated pub_twist publisher\
    \ at the end of your callback. The publisher is not set up correctly yet at that\
    \ point in time, so nothing actually gets published.\n - pub_twist goes out of\
    \ scope and it's\n   destructor is called as it is a local\n   variable of your\
    \ posCallback.\n\nWhat you can do is make the Publisher a global variable and\
    \ use it. Minimal example:\n\n    #include <ros/ros.h>\n    [...]\n    \n    ros::Publisher\
    \ pub_twist;\n    \n    [...]\n        void posCallback(const geometry_msgs::Point&\
    \ msg)\n        {\n            [...]\n            pub_twist.publish(twist_msg);\n\
    \        }\n    \n    int main(int argc, char **argv)\n    {\n        [...] \n\
    \    \n        pub_twist= nh.advertise<geometry_msgs::Twist>(\"/cmd_vel\", 1);\n\
    \    \n        ros::Subscriber sub = nh.subscribe(\"color_position\", 100, posCallback);\n\
    \        ros::spin();\n    }\n\n"
