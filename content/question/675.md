    Sorry I am unclear what you are trying to achieve here.

    But in general, [reset\_pose.py](https://bitbucket.org/osrf/drcsim/src/8e072fa972a0e2cd0104a35de1ecf1870f5c6c01/ros/atlas_utils/scripts/reset_pose.py?at=default) only works with [atlas\_position\_controllers.launch](https://bitbucket.org/osrf/drcsim/src/8e072fa972a0/ros/atlas_utils/launch/atlas_position_controllers.launch?at=default) by setting controllers desired joint positions.  Once the published commands are received by individual position controllers, each controller will actuate the joints towards desired position.

    The `Reset Model Poses` button in the GUI simply [resets the Model pose](https://bitbucket.org/osrf/gazebo/src/d50d145f8639646720c5c1738bc74b0645614e18/gazebo/physics/Entity.cc?at=default#cl-517), and leaves model joint configuration untouched, the model is "teleported" to its starting pose, but the dynamic properties (linear and angular velocities of the links) are unchanged.
