    I have worked around this issue based on the post discussion [here](https://github.com/ros-drivers/openni_camera/issues/54)

    The issue is solved when I uninstall the SensorKinect driver I cloned from [here](https://github.com/avin2/SensorKinect). The work around it is to uninstall the SensorKinect driver by running:

        . ~/SensorKinect/Platform/Linux/Redist/Sensor-Bin-Linux-x64-v5.1.2.1/install.sh -u

    asssuming that SensorKinect is installed in the Home directory.
