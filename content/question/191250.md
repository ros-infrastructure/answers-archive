    I think you're asking about <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cognitive_architecture">cognitive architectures</a>, or the broader class of <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agent_architecture">agent architectures</a>. Following any of the links from those pages will be just a start in discovering alternatives.

    You should know Rodney Brooks and the 'subsumption' architecture, since that kicked off a lot of architecture research back in the 80s/90s (and I might as well mention <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shakey_the_robot">Shakey</a> to really provide some history). Read something about 3T, SOAR, and ACT-R. You might want to read <a href="http://www.isle.org/~langley/papers/cogarch.cogsys08.pdf"> Cognitive architectures: Research issues and challenges</a>, P. Langley, 2009.

    You might instead read the <a href="http://www.mdpi.com/2218-6581/3/2/207/pdf">FroboMind paper</a> (from their website), then follow up with any of the papers cited in the bibliography.
