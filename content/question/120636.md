    Ok, with laser scanner fixed on two servos you need following:

     - create appropriate [URDF model](http://wiki.ros.org/urdf) (including robot, those servos / joints, your laser scanner)
     - publish current angles of servos to `joint_states` topic (`sensor_msgs/JointState`)
     - run [robot_state_publisher](http://wiki.ros.org/robot_state_publisher) which will transform joint state to TF
     - read carefully tutorial on [how to setup hector_slam](http://wiki.ros.org/hector_slam/Tutorials/SettingUpForYourRobot) on your robot


    Then, there will be TF transformation between laser scanner (laser_link in the tutorial) and robot base (base_link).
