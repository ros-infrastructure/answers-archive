The README in https://github.com/ros-perception/open_karto says it's a catkinized version of SRI International's OpenKarto library. 

In https://github.com/skasperski/navigation_2d/tree/master/nav2d_karto/OpenKarto, Sebastian Kasperski has included a copy of the OpenKarto library (non-catkinized).

The question is, why does the source code look so different? Compare [open_karto/src](https://github.com/ros-perception/open_karto/tree/indigo-devel/src) and [open_karto/include/open_karto](https://github.com/ros-perception/open_karto/tree/indigo-devel/include/open_karto) with [nav2d_karto/OpenKarto/source](https://github.com/skasperski/navigation_2d/tree/master/nav2d_karto/OpenKarto/source). Was the library refactored as part of the catkinization?

Most importantly, are the two more or less equivalent?
