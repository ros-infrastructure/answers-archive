  body_markdown: "When you use slam, it's going to try to convert the laser scan from\
    \ the frame_id of the sensor to the base_frame of your robot. As such, you must\
    \ provide the transformation from your robot's base_frame to the sensor's frame.\
    \ This can be done with a `static_transform` if the transformation is static,\
    \ or a `transform_broadcaster` if it's not static. Both of these can be found\
    \ in the [tf tutorials](http://www.ros.org/wiki/tf). "
