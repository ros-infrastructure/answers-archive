    Seems you are trying to synchronize messages that do not have [std_msgs/Header](http://docs.ros.org/api/std_msgs/html/msg/Header.html) and thus time stamp as well. Without knowing the time stamp it is obviously impossible to synchronize anything.

    Usually the message types that have a time stamp are called `xxxStamped` like, for example, [geometry_msgs/PoseStamped](http://docs.ros.org/api/geometry_msgs/html/msg/PoseStamped.html).
