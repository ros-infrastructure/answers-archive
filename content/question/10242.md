  body_markdown: "There is no official or programmatic way of doing this, as the process\
    \ is strongly dependent on the platform that you are using.\n\nWhen converting\
    \ from a URDF to a xacro file, I did the following:\n\n - Create properties for\
    \ commonly-used constants \n  - Mathematics constants: M_PI, M_2PI\n  - Conversion\
    \ constants: Radians to Degrees, inches to centimeters\n  - Physics constants:\
    \ Speed of Light, gravity (if needed)\n - Create properties for variable components\
    \ of your robot (Parts that will be constant over a run, but may change between\
    \ runs)\n  - Wheel Radius\n  - Ground Clearance\n  - Camera Height/Angle\n - Create\
    \ macros for arrays of parts on your robot (a good example would be the super-structure\
    \ of the turtlebot).\n - Create macros for any components that are \"mirrored\"\
    \n   - arms\n   - wheels\n\nThis would be a good starting point for creating a\
    \ xacro file, in general.\n\nEdit: \n\nSee [this tutorial](http://www.ros.org/wiki/urdf/Tutorials/Using%20Xacro%20to%20Clean%20Up%20a%20URDF%20File)\
    \ for further details and syntax. "
