    By default, callbacks are executed sequentially by `ros::spin()` and `ros::spinOnce()`. If you want to execute multiple callbacks in parallel you can use an async spinner or a multithreaded spinner. See [roscpp Callbacks and Spinning]( http://wiki.ros.org/roscpp/Overview/Callbacks%20and%20Spinning) for details.
