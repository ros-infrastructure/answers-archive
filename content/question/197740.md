    It's the [tf](http://wiki.ros.org/tf) frame that all positions/measurements that you see are relative to. For example, let's say you have a robot that has two frames, *odom* (a world-fixed frame) and *base_link* (the body frame of the robot). The robot is at position `(10, 0)` with a heading of `pi/2` in the *odom* frame, and it has a LIDAR on board that sees an obstacle 5 meters directly in front of it. If you set your Fixed Frame to *odom*, that obstacle will appear at position `(10, 5)`. If you set your Fixed Frame to base_link, it will appear at `(5, 0)`.
