  body_markdown: "As has been mentioned, the OpenNI drivers give you access to the\
    \ factory calibration, which means you don't need to calibrate manually. \n\n\
    However, if you want to anyway (for example, to use libfreenect on systems that\
    \ don't have ROS), it makes a huge difference in the alignment of the points and\
    \ RGB values.\n\nAn easy way to demonstrate this is to fire up rviz, and visualize\
    \ the PointCloud2 that's published by the kinect_camera driver; if you haven't\
    \ calibrated, you'll notice a serious mismatch between the points and colors.\
    \ If you then switch to the openni_camera driver, you'll notice how much better\
    \ things are. Calibrating the sensor manually (kinect_calibration) will get alignment\
    \ performance on par with openni_camera. "
