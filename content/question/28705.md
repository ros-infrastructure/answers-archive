    [SDF Spec](http://sdformat.org/spec?ver=1.5&elem=collision)

    <collide_without_contact> Element
    Required: 0
    Type: bool
    Default: false
    Description: Flag to disable contact force generation, while still allowing collision checks and contact visualization to occur.

    tldr: collide_without_contact means the object will stop even though no "forces" are generated.
