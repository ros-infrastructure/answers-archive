    \ in the termina, that is not the proper way to finish/dispose a living node,\
    \ you should do instead:\n\n> $rosnode kill *yourNodeName*\n\nthe reason is that\
    \ Ctrl +C may not give the chance to the node to dispose itself properly, before\
    \ the node dies it must isself disconnect from Master, release resources, etc\
    \ etc which may not happen by doing just Ctrl +C\n\nbtw \n\n> $rosnode cleanup\
    \ \n\nwill guide you diagnose, and purge those zombie nodes"
