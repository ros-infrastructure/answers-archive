    \ your compiler/linker uses another qt version than what is loaded at runtime?\
    \ Find out what rgbdslam is compiled with (`make VERBOSE=1`) and linked against\
    \ (`ldd bin/rgbdslam|grep Q`). \n\nMaybe you need to adjust the environment variable\
    \ LD_LIBRARY_PATH by adding the path of the correct Qt or putting it in front\
    \ of the \"incorrect\" path.\nOr see if you can adapth the settings in CMakeLists.txt\
    \ (the line \"find_package(Qt4 REQUIRED\"?) to use the other Qt version."
