    @chapulina provided the right direction.

    To avoid the drop I followed these steps:

     1. Change the sensor type from `ray` to `gpu_ray`.
     2. Since I was using Gazebo and this [plugin](https://bitbucket.org/DataspeedInc/velodyne_simulator/src/master/) I needed to update Gazebo from Gazebo_7.0.0 to Gazebo_7.16.0 using the following approach provided in the section ["Alternative installation"](http://gazebosim.org/tutorials?tut=install_ubuntu&cat=install). The update is to cope with this [issue](https://bitbucket.org/DataspeedInc/velodyne_simulator/src/master/gazebo_upgrade.md) and this [issue](https://bitbucket.org/DataspeedInc/velodyne_simulator/issues/15/gpu-mode-very-roughly-resoluted-scans-in)

    Now I able to simulate three lidars (2x16 samples and 1 x64 samples with almost no time_rate variation)
