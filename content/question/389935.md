    You tagged this `foxy`, and that's likely why you can't find the checkbox (which you mention in the comments you posted on [ros2/rviz#714](https://github.com/ros2/rviz/pull/714)).

    Unfortunately the [CHANGELOG](https://github.com/ros2/rviz/blob/afebc17338b6e5d3d608d1c3b2c23e666862e70e/rviz2/CHANGELOG.rst) does not seem to make any mention of that PR, nor the feature, but as you can see in [ros2/rviz@2b551f80](https://github.com/ros2/rviz/commit/2b551f808e8e8c9bd29bc006d06a549c00db4861), this feature was merged into RViz2 version `8.7.0`:

    ![PR merge commit](https://ftp.osuosl.org/pub/ros/download.ros.org/downloads/se_migration/ros/16359421303362757.jpg)

    According to [index.ros.org/p/rviz2](https://index.ros.org/p/rviz2/#foxy) (and [status_page/ros_foxy_default.html?q=rviz2](http://repo.ros2.org/status_page/ros_foxy_default.html?q=rviz2)), RViz2 is at version `8.2.x` in Foxy.

    The only ROS 2 version which has RViz2 at `8.7.0` would be Rolling at this time. See [index.ros.org/p/rviz2/#rolling](https://index.ros.org/p/rviz2/#rolling).

    Galactic gets `8.5.0` ([index.ros.org/p/rviz2/#galactic](https://index.ros.org/p/rviz2/#galactic)).

    So that would explain why you don't have that checkbox.
