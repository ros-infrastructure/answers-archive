  body_markdown: "The [coordinate frames for `ardrone_autonomy`](http://ardrone-autonomy.readthedocs.io/en/latest/frames.html)\
    \ are given in the [documentation](http://ardrone-autonomy.readthedocs.io/en/latest/index.html)\
    \ and conform to the conventions given in [REP-103](http://www.ros.org/reps/rep-0103.html#coordinate-frame-conventions).\n\
    \nAs for how the odometry is calculated, again, from the [documentation](http://ardrone-autonomy.readthedocs.io/en/latest/reading.html#odometry-data):\n\
    \n\n> **Odometry data**\n\n> New in version 1.4.\n\n> The driver calculates and\
    \ publishes Odometry data by integrating velocity estimates reported by the drone\
    \ (which is based on optical flow). The data is published as `nav_msgs/Odometry`\
    \ messages to `ardrone/odometry` topic. The corresponding TF transform is also\
    \ published as *odom -> base* transformation.\n\nSo, basically the odometry is\
    \ calculated using [dead reckoning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dead_reckoning).\
    \ It's noisy because that's just the nature of dead reckoning. \n\n----------\n\
    \n**Update:**\n\nYou can add a `tf` frame and visualize that in RViz. Please refer\
    \ to the [`tf` tutorials](http://wiki.ros.org/tf2/Tutorials) to learn how to do\
    \ that."
