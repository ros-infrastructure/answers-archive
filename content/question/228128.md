  body_markdown: "Orientation is a high-level term. It includes roll, pitch and yaw\
    \ if you are representing orientation with [Euler angles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euler_angles).\
    \ If you are representing orientation with [Quaternion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quaternions_and_spatial_rotation),\
    \ it includes x, y, z, and w corresponding to Quaternion components. \n\nIn other\
    \ words, yaw is only one component of orientation."
