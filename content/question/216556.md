    Yes, that's possible, you just have to add it to the URDF model and adjust your setup accordingly. A modified Turtlebot with a simulated Hokuyo UTM30-LX LIDAR mounted can be found in the [hector_turtlebot](https://github.com/tu-darmstadt-ros-pkg/hector_turtlebot) repo.
    The LIDAR is added [here in L20](https://github.com/tu-darmstadt-ros-pkg/hector_turtlebot/blob/master/hector_turtlebot_description/urdf/turtlebot_sick_setup.xacro#L20) of the turtlebot_sick_setup.xacro file. The simulated robot is spawned using the [spawn_sick_robot_day_2014_setup.launch](https://github.com/tu-darmstadt-ros-pkg/hector_turtlebot/blob/master/hector_turtlebot_gazebo/launch/spawn_sick_robot_day_2014_setup.launch).
