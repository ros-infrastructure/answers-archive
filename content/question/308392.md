    According to the [wiki](http://wiki.ros.org/rosbag/Commandline#rosbag_info) this is the **median** frequency. Since it's a `median`, the topic have received/published the same amount of messages at a faster and at a slower rate than `99,2hz`. Your calculation is actually the `mean` rate of the topic. If the `mean = median` then you have a symetric distribution, here you have `mean < median` so it's a negatively skewed distribution.
