    There's an option in `colcon test` ([ref (colcon.readthedocs.io)](https://colcon.readthedocs.io/en/released/reference/verb/test.html#python-specific-arguments)):

        --python-testing {pytest,setuppy_test}

    Note that these are for "pure Python" test cases, i.e. tests that don't involve ROS nodes. For tests with ROS nodes, in ROS1 you can use [rostest](http://wiki.ros.org/rostest). In ROS2, #q292100 (started in 2018) indicates there's no equivalent.
