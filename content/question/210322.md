  body_markdown: "You should use a nodehandle with a relative namespace instead.\n\
    The node's namespace includes the full path of the node, including the root. However,\
    \ your nodehandle is probably the default, which is relative to the node. In this\
    \ case, it's `/` . Creating a new nodehandle with the node's private namespace,\
    \ `~`  should fix your error.\n\nTry this:\n\n    ros::NodeHandle private_n(\"\
    ~\");\n    ros::Subscriber sub = private_n.subscribe(\"attitude\", queue_size,\
    \ callbackAngularVelocities);\n\nEDIT for further reading: \n\nhttp://wiki.ros.org/roscpp/Overview/NodeHandles\n\
    \nhttp://wiki.ros.org/Names"
