    The solution is to run `ulimit -Sn 524288 && ulimit -Hn 524288`. Thank you to [Mike Scheutzow](https://answers.ros.org/users/75154/mike-scheutzow/) and [this post](https://answers.ros.org/question/336963/rosout-high-memory-usage/)
