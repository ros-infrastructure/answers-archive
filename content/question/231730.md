     As for Ubuntu, AFAIK the non free modules cannot just be installed directly using apt-get from the official ppa. In order to get the non free modules (including SURF) you could delete the opencv you have and install opencv by compiling it from source. Another alternative is to find some repositories that include the non free modules already. Some of them are mentioned [here](http://stackoverflow.com/questions/27481849/include-nonfree-opencv-2-4-10-on-ubuntu).
