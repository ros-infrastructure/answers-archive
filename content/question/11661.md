    There is no need to set it up. Just [create a package](http://www.ros.org/wiki/ROS/Tutorials/CreatingPackage), open its CMakeLists.txt with QtCreator and you are done.

    If you are not accustomed to using CMake with Qt you can have a look at [this page](http://developer.qt.nokia.com/quarterly/view/using_cmake_to_build_qt_projects). Getting to know CMake might require some reading, but I do not regret ditching qmake for CMake.
