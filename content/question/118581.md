    You can use hector_mapping with a robot providing odometry (see "Use in place of gmapping" in the [setup tutorial](http://wiki.ros.org/hector_slam/Tutorials/SettingUpForYourRobot)). Odometry is not used within hector_mapping though, as in the original use case it was advantageous to be able to perform mapping without reliable odometry.
