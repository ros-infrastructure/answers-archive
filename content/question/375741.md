- body_markdown: |-
    > It seems that restarting of the data is causing the EKF to converge to different things sometimes. Is that something that can happen? Maybe it has to do with my playback speed?

    Yes, the playback speed may overload message buffers, so which messages get processed by the EKF node differ run-to-run based on system timing.  The play node may publish at 100x speed, but the callbacks won't necessarily keep up.  You can test this by playing the bag more slowly to see if you get consistent results.

    > will the values of the measurement covariance matrices (as long as they aren't zero) change what the actual pose estimation converges to?

    Yes. As [the wikipedia article](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extended_Kalman_filter) explains, the updated state estimate depends on the Kalman gain, which depends on the covariance estimates.
