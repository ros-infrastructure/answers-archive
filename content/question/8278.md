Hi All!

As my Gazebo project is getting larger, I am beginning to have some trouble keeping track of all of the nodes I have created inside gazebo plugins through "gazebo::transport::Node()" methods.

So far, I have been using "gz topic -l" to keep track of the custom topics I am communicating with, and I have also been using "gz topic -i [topic name]" to get information about the publishers/subscribers for those specific topics. This approach is still not ideal, as it is quite cumbersom. Also, the publishers/subscribers are not listed by their node name, but rather as an IP address :s 

I was just wondering if there where a more convenient way to go about this, such as an rqt_graph approach? When I use "rosrun rqt_graph rqt_graph" I can only get node/topic information about /gazebo and /rosout , which is not particularly useful.

How do you guys and girls keep track of your nodes/topics? Thanks for you help,
