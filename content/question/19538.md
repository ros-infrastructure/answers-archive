    1. Can you try running `gzserver` without `gzclient`, does the problem still happen?  You can monitor the real time factor with the command `gz stats`

    1. If the problem doesn't happen, you can try setting the level of detail for heightmaps for `gzclient` by adding this to `~/gui.ini`:

            [heightmap]
            lod=3

    1. You could also try reducing the definition of your heightmap
