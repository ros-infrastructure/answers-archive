    Try changing your subscriber to this:

    `node->Subscribe("~/model/info", &WorldInterface::messageReceived1, this));`

    And your callback to this:

    `void messageReceived1(ConstModelPtr &_msg)`

    See an example [here](https://bitbucket.org/osrf/gazebo/src/bae84f76b4ad7a6a7cecaeb3e76e545b8c8d5f3e/plugins/BlinkVisualPlugin.cc?fileviewer=file-view-default#BlinkVisualPlugin.cc-137).
