    Not according to [REP 3: Target Platforms](https://www.ros.org/reps/rep-0003.html).

    Noetic only targets Focal (as it's an LTS), see [REP 3: Noetic Ninjemys (May 2020 - May 2025)](https://www.ros.org/reps/rep-0003.html#noetic-ninjemys-may-2020-may-2025).

    As that was the last (OR initiated) ROS 1 release, it doesn't appear there will ever be an official release of ROS Noetic on `20.10`.
