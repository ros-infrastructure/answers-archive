    Be careful: `rviz` is *not* a simulator. It only *displays* what the real robot or the Nao's simulator (webots) publishes.

    So what you likely want to do (if you can not work directly on the real robot) is to install "Webots for Nao" (download it [from this page](https://community.aldebaran-robotics.com/resources/) -- requires an Aldebaran account) and then follow the [installation instruction for ROS + Nao](http://wiki.ros.org/nao/Installation/remote).
