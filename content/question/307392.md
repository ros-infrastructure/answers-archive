    From [the documentation](http://wiki.ros.org/urdf/XML/joint):

    > The value of this joint can be computed as `value = multiplier * other_joint_value + offset`.

    So no, referring to two joints is not supported right now.

    You *may* be able to do something with [transmission](http://wiki.ros.org/urdf/XML/Transmission), but I'm not sure.
