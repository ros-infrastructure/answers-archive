  body_markdown: "This is a good basic reference for A* vs  Dijkstra. A* has an added\
    \ heuristic trying to expand the potential field in the direction of the goal.\
    \ Its not necessarily promised to be faster, but in most cases it is. \n\nhttp://theory.stanford.edu/~amitp/GameProgramming/AStarComparison.html\n\
    \nRRT and RRT-variants are sampling based and not search based. They will require\
    \ smoothing after the search. In practice RRTs are nice for higher dimensional\
    \ spaces like manipulators, but in lower dimensional space (like 2D mobile robots)\
    \ its not really adventitious over search based. WIth that said, there are RRT\
    \ implemented global planner plugins if you google for it for you to try out youself\
