  body_markdown: "ROS and Player are both middlewares. Gazebo, Stage, and USARSim\
    \ are each simulators. I believe you can interact with all three simulators via\
    \ ROS or Player. And there are bridges between Player and ROS to enable compatibility\
    \ with hybrid systems which cannot fully convert to one or the other middleware,\
    \ often due to legacy applications or hardware compatibility. Depending on your\
    \ applications requirements different combinations are applicable. "
