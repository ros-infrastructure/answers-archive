  body_markdown: "`base_link` is typically the center of mass or the center of turning\
    \ for your robot, while `base_footprint` is located at ground level directly below\
    \ `base_link`. `base_footprint` is a projection of `base_link` onto the ground.\
    \ \n\n**EDIT**:\n\nIf your Center of Mass is 0.5m above the ground, you can publish\
    \ a transform with parent=`base_footprint` and child=`base_link` with a transform\
    \ of 0.5z. If you want base_link to be the parent, you would publish -0.5z. "
