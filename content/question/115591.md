     This is old, but I just had to find that out too.

    Most probably, you are using a [TransformListener](http://docs.ros.org/jade/api/tf2_ros/html/c++/classtf2__ros_1_1TransformListener.html#details). The reason `tf_static` is reported as unconnected is that it automatically subscribes to both `tf` and `tf_static` (see [source](http://docs.ros.org/jade/api/tf2_ros/html/c++/classtf2__ros_1_1TransformListener.html#a95efa667c6c28633a814b2372b960302)). Now if you only use `tf` in your system (which is perfectly legal afaik), `tf_static` will not be connected.
