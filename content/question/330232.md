    I think, what coul happen is: Once you terminate, `rospy.is_shutdown()` is False. And the currently waiting `rate.sleep()` is also terminated. But your for-loop still runs into the next `rate.sleep()`.
    You could do something like:

        while not rospy.is_shutdown():
            for i, filename in enumerate(sort_images, 0):
                print("Publish Image with Name: %s" %os.path.basename(filename))
                rate.sleep()
                if rospy.is_shutdown():
                    break
