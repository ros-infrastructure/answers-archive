  body_markdown: "What solved it for me:\n\nOn the laptop, `ROS_HOSTNAME` wasn't set\
    \ (nothing in `.bashrc`). After launching rviz on the laptop, I looked at the\
    \ environmental variables again, and saw that something had set `ROS_HOSTNAME=localhost`,\
    \ which seems to have the effect of causing it to reject remote connections.\n\
    \nSolution: on the laptop, set `ROS_HOSTNAME=$ROS_IP` (I added this to `.bachrc`,\
    \ after `ROS_IP=<ip addr of laptop>`). Then remote connections are accepted. "
