    \ odom -> base_link` chain correctly setup. ( Or `map -> base_link` with `global_frame`\

    \ localization. So either one of them could be used. The difference is for AMCL\
    \ you have to provide a map through the map_server to localize against and to\
    \ use for global_costmap.  hector_mapping generates the map that you can use in\
    \ global_costmap in addition to doing the localization.\n\n\n> I have noticed\
