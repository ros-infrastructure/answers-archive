- body_markdown: "Since the odom is not accurate enough, you can use a sensor fusion\
    \ (odom + IMU) or (odom + camera) or correct the odom error with AR tags and use\
    \ it as ground truth. Also, you can run a small benchmark to see the odom output\
    \ deviation and correct it manually. \nThe environment (dynamic or static and\
    \ open or closed) has a big impact on the sensor selected to create the ground\
    \ truth.\nFor your Qs:\n\n - /Odom is just the encoder without the IMU.\n- For\
    \ scientific validation, it is correct to use the Odom path as the ground truth,\
    \ but you must Perform benchmark on Odom to check the uncertainty of this system\
    \ (deviation error) and correct it manually.\nthere are other techniques that\
    \ are more accurate than Odom to generate ground truth, but they are much more\
    \ complex and take a lot of time.\n- The sensors are not linear, so it is correct\
    \ to compare the estimated pose of SLAM with the estimated (or filtered / or the\
    \ signal of the relatively accurate Odom pose\nAre you writing a paper about SLAM?"
