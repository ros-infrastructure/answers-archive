    \ of retrieving exactly what you are looking for. The \"result\" you are referring\
    \ to is specifically because of this line\n\n    resp_seeds = struct.unpack('<%dB'\
    \ % len(resp.result_type),\n                           resp.result_type)\n\nWhere\
    \ you are only looking at what seeding methods were used in the original IK request.\
    \ What you want to be looking at is the `resp.joints` field. Here is the [complete\
    \ service definition](https://github.com/RethinkRobotics/baxter_common/blob/master/baxter_core_msgs/srv/SolvePositionIK.srv)\
    \ including comments on what all of the fields are, and here is the relevant snippet\
    \ from the previously-linked walkthrough:\n\n    if (resp.isValid[0]):\n     \
    \   print(\"SUCCESS - Valid Joint Solution Found:\")\n        # Format solution\
    \ into Limb API-compatible dictionary\n        limb_joints = dict(zip(resp.joints[0].name,\
    \ resp.joints[0].position))\n        print limb_joints\n    else:\n        print(\"\
    INVALID POSE - No Valid Joint Solution Found.\")\n \n    return 0\n"
