    Hi @eogeo,

    If you look at the SDF format in the [sensor](http://sdformat.org/spec?ver=1.7&elem=sensor) tag, inside `camera` tag, there is a section for camera `intrinsics` in which you can define, among other things:

     1. `fx`: X focal length (in pixels, overrides horizontal_fov).
     2. `fy`: Y focal length (in pixels, overrides horizontal_fov).
     4. `cx`: X principal point (in pixels).
     5. `cy`: Y principal point (in pixels).
     6. `s`: XY axis skew.

    Hope this helps you.

    Regards.
