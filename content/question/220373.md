  body_markdown: "OpenCV's  `imread` will load images as `Mat`. Unlike `IplImage*`\
    \ the `Mat` object will automatically be released, once it goes out of the scope\
    \ in which it was declared.\n\nThat being said using, \n\n`cv::Mat src = cv::imread('xyz.jpg',\
    \ CV_LOAD_IMAGE_COLOR);  \nsrc.release();`\n\nwill also release the memory."
