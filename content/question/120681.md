    I found two possible solutions:

    - or deactivating xHCI and EHCI Hand-off in the bios as suggested [here](http://answers.ros.org/question/106036/asus-xtion-not-enough-bandwidth/)
    - there is also some firmware update [from Asus](http://reconstructme.net/asus-xtion-usb-3-0-hotfix-2/) related to USB 3.0 problems
