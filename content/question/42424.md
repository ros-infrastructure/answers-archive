    If you'd like to use PCL versions greater than 1.5.1 on Fuerte, take a look at the `ros-fuerte-perception-pcl-fuerte-unstable` package; that provides 1.6.0.

    If you need an even-newer version, [take a look at the source for that package](https://bitbucket.org/macmason/perception_pcl_fuerte_unstable). There's a branch that's closer to the current trunk revision. The Makefile makes it clear how to bump that value to something even closer to trunk.
