    Yes, in gazebo4. These two SDF parameters(`spring_reference` and `spring_stiffness`) are read in [Joint.cc](https://bitbucket.org/osrf/gazebo/src/bcfe9f8268cc32ec43bc57fe6374882c46ec02c8/gazebo/physics/Joint.cc?at=gazebo_4.1#cl-146), which then calls SetStiffnessDamping on the joint. The following physics engine implement SetStiffnessDamping:

    * [ODE](https://bitbucket.org/osrf/gazebo/src/bcfe9f8268cc32ec43bc57fe6374882c46ec02c8/gazebo/physics/ode/ODEJoint.cc?at=gazebo_4.1#cl-1237)
    * [Bullet](https://bitbucket.org/osrf/gazebo/src/bcfe9f8268cc32ec43bc57fe6374882c46ec02c8/gazebo/physics/bullet/BulletJoint.cc?at=gazebo_4.1#cl-394)
    * [DART](https://bitbucket.org/osrf/gazebo/src/bcfe9f8268cc32ec43bc57fe6374882c46ec02c8/gazebo/physics/dart/DARTJoint.cc?at=gazebo_4.1#cl-258)
    * [Simbody](https://bitbucket.org/osrf/gazebo/src/bcfe9f8268cc32ec43bc57fe6374882c46ec02c8/gazebo/physics/simbody/SimbodyJoint.cc?at=gazebo_4.1#cl-423)
