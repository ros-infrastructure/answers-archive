Citing the ROS Wiki Attachments page:

"Attached Files
To refer to attachments on a page, use **attachment:filename**, as shown below in the list of files. Do **NOT** use the URL of the [get] link, since this is subject to change and can break easily."

However, I get a 500 Internal Server Error whenever I link that way (example [here](http://www.ros.org/wiki/rgbdslam?action=AttachFile&do=view&target=rgbdslam-fuerte-r3761.patch)). The [[get]](http://www.ros.org/wiki/rgbdslam?action=AttachFile&do=get&target=rgbdslam-fuerte-r3761.patch) link, on the other hand, works fine.

Who should I contact to get this fixed? The 500 Internal Server Error page only refers to `webmaster@localhost` (i.e. no contact info is provided).
