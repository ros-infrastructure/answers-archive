    \ the nxt_all package; however, you installed Fuerte, not Electric (the most recent\

    \ they don't exist under your Fuerte install. \n\nTo verify that this is the circumstance:\
    \ \n `ls /opt/ros`\n\nIf I'm correct, you will see two directories output. \n\n\
    To fix this issue, install electric. Unfortunately, the nxt doesn't cooperate\
    \ with Fuerte at the moment. \n\n-Hunter A. \n"
