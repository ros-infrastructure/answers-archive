    The place to go to find out about target platforms is the REP for the version of ROS you are trying to use.

    - For ROS 1, this is [REP 3](https://ros.org/reps/rep-0003.html)
    - For ROS 2, this is [REP 2000](https://ros.org/reps/rep-2000.html)

    The [section for Melodic in REP 3](https://ros.org/reps/rep-0003.html#melodic-morenia-may-2018-may-2023) says that it is supported on Ubuntu Bionic on arm64.

    Official MacOS support is unfortunately not available, although this doesn't mean that it's impossible to use. You will just have to compile it from source yourself, resolving dependencies manually using something like [Homebrew](https://brew.sh/) or [MacPorts](https://www.macports.org/). You may even be able to find a version of ROS 1 in one of those, or a guide on how to compile it. I don't think it will be easy, though. Using it in Ubuntu Bionic would be simpler.
