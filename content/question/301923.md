    ROS 2 doesn't have / need `dynamic_reconfigure`. The functionality has been integrated into the parameter subsystem (see the corresponding [design article](http://design.ros2.org/articles/ros_parameters.html)).

    `rqt` has not been ported to ROS 2 at this time.
