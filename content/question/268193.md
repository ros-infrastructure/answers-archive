    ROS communications require that you have viable routes between both hosts. [Network Setup Overview](http://wiki.ros.org/ROS/NetworkSetup) Your "floating IP" is more accurately termed a NAT address and is not routable from outside. The most common solution to this is to put both machines on a VPN where your static IP can host the VPN server and the machine behind the 4G dongle can connect to the VPN via the static IP.

    There's lots of advice for ROS + VPNs: https://www.google.com/search?q=ros+vpn
