    NVM, figured it out.

    Just needed to remap `/tf` and `/tf_static` per [here](https://wiki.ros.org/Remapping%20Arguments).

    E.g. `rosrun tf view_frames /tf:=/namespace/tf /tf_static:=/namespace/tf_static`
