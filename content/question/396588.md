    There is a lot going on here.

    > as far as these frames are conected in tf tree, can we say that there is transforms from base_link->imu_link or base_link->gps_link

    Sorry, it's not clear what you are asking here. Are you asking if those transforms _do_ exist, or if they _need_ to exist? All I can tell you is that your IMU data is in the `imu_link` frame and your GPS data is in the `novatel_mount_link` frame, so you need to make sure you have transforms from `base_link` to both of those frames. Your robot description appears to be taking care of that.

    > when gps is not working properly, i mean in some part of the environment it shows nan for latitude and longitude, and odometry/gps covariance starts growing too much, and it makes the robot to jump back and forth a lot in rviz, so what is the solution for such a kind of cases?? i am in an environment that gps isnot good enough.

    Your configuration is a bit messy. In your map-frame EKF, you are fusing X and Y position from the GPS data, but then you are also fusing X position, pitch, and yaw from `/rtabmap/localization_pose` (but not Y position?). I am not sure what you are trying to do there, but I doubt that `rtabmap` and the GPS data are going to agree with each other, especially if `rtabmap` is actually mapping. The EKF is just going just jump back and forth between their position estimates.

    You also have `two_d_mode` set to true, but are trying to fuse a bunch of 3D variables, like roll and pitch. Not a problem really, but it makes your intent unclear.

    Looking at this, it feels like you are just throwing a bunch of data sources together and hoping it will sort itself out. You should describe what it is you are trying to do. I'd recommend that you start small: start by just fusing your wheel encoder data and IMU data, and make sure everything looks correct. Then bring in the GPS data and `navsat_transform_node` and verify everything is behaving. Then bring in rtabmap data, though I'd recommend making either the GPS data _or_ the rtapmap pose data `differential`. And also maybe figure out why you are fusing X and yaw from rtabmap, but not Y.
