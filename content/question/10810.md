  body_markdown: "tf2 is a rewrite of tf.  Our goal at the moment is to release it\
    \ into Fuerte.  It is targeted to replace tf.  It is mostly a redesign for easier\
    \ use in code.  It's also noteably lower overhead and doesn't require anywhere\
    \ near as many dependencies.  The design page can be found at [tf2/Design](http://www.ros.org/wiki/tf2/Design).\
    \  \n\nIt is backwards compatible with tf on the ROS interface thus tf and tf2\
    \ can work side by side.  "
