  body_markdown: |-
    At the end of the [gmapping](http://ros.org/wiki/gmapping) wiki page you can see which tf transformations are required by gmapping and which ones are provided by it.

    Essentially, your robot's odometry node should publish the transform between /odom and /base_link while gmapping will publish the transform from /map to /odom. You can then get your robot's pose in map by requesting the transform from /map to /base_link. [this](http://ros.org/wiki/navigation/Tutorials/RobotSetup/TF) and [this](http://ros.org/wiki/navigation/Tutorials/RobotSetup/Odom) tutorial could be helpful if you didn't find them already.
