The only issue I am having is the following:
I have a demo program in which I am removing a bin from a shelf, placing it on the desk of my mobile platform and then I am starting my object-detection pipeline in order to remove screws from the bin (classical bin-picking-scenario).

For all of this, I am using SMACH. In order to pick the 3 screws, I am using a SMACH-Iterator, as described in the tutorial (http://wiki.ros.org/smach/Tutorials/Iterator%20container). I've been trying to use a callback from outside of the iterator and within in combination with a simple global "counter"-variable, but this always results in the THIRD cavity to be selected.
