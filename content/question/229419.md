  body_markdown: "The publishing rate given by `rostopic hz` is by default 'the average\
    \ rate over the entire time rostopic has been running.' : http://wiki.ros.org/rostopic#rostopic_hz\n\
    If you want an estimate based on the last N messages(window), you can use the\
    \ `-w` option, e.g `rostopic hz -w 5 /pose` to average over the last 5 messages. "
