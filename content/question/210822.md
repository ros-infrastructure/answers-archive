    Can you be a little more specific? If you are already using the Kinect, then you already have a 3D point cloud.

    Edit:

    It's very easy to perform calibration in ROS, using the [camera_calibration](http://wiki.ros.org/camera_calibration) package. The idea is that you match the VGA pixel to the DepthPoint of the Kinect IR (3D) camera. You can read more [here](http://www.pcl-users.org/What-is-happening-behind-the-Depth-to-RGB-registration-mode-in-OpenNI-td4029195.html).

    You can also perform extrinsic calibration, following [this](http://wiki.ros.org/openni_launch/Tutorials/ExtrinsicCalibration) tutorial. In general, look at the [OpenNI tutorials](http://wiki.ros.org/openni_launch/Tutorials) to understand how to proceed.
