  body_markdown: "The problem comes because python can not find modules in different\
    \ paths.\nIn my instalation \"cameracalibrator.py\" is on /opt/ros/indigo/lib/camera_calibration/\
    \ , while \"calibrator.py\" is on /opt/ros/indigo/lib/python2.7/dist-packages/camera_calibration/\n\
    \nOne fast way to solve the problem is to copy \"calibrator.py\" to a directory\
    \ camera_calibrator, so that the imports in python find  what they are expected\
    \ to find:\n\n\n    /opt/ros/indigo/lib/camera_calibration/cameracalibrator.py\n\
