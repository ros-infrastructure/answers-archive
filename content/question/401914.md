    I'm not sure if I understood your question, but if by communicating between other scripts you mean share the distances with other scripts, you could simply `import rospy` module, initialise a node, create a `rospy.Publisher()` to publish data to a Topic of your choice.     Take a look at the ROS tutorial here to see how to create `Publishers` and `Subscribers`: http://wiki.ros.org/ROS/Tutorials/WritingPublisherSubscriber%28python%29
