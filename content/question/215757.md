  body_markdown: "Hi guys! I solved the problem. \n\nIf anybody of you encounter this\
    \ problem on Groovy (I can't confirm for other version, although you can try),\
    \ you **don't have to catkin_make octomap_mapping package**. \n\nFollow the 1.1\
    \ Installation @\nhttp://wiki.ros.org/octomap_mapping\n\n    sudo apt-get install\
    \ ros-groovy-octomap ros-groovy-octomap-mapping\n\n Make sure your environment\
    \ setup is correct as stated in tutorial.\n\nAnd run\n\n    rosdep install octomap_mapping\n\
    \    rosmake octomap_mapping\n\nWith that, octomap and its dependencies are installed.\n\
    \nTo navigate and run, just\n\n    roscd octomap_server\nand\n\n    ls\n\nThe\
    \ launch/src folders are all there."
