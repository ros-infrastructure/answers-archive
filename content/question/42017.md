    Neither of those is an *image* in the JPEG sense. They are *depth* images.

    If you want the RGB camera image, use [one of these topics](http://www.ros.org/wiki/openni_launch#Published_Topics) (or the rectified versions):

     * camera/rgb/image_mono
     * camera/rgb/image_color

    See [REP-0118](http://www.ros.org/reps/rep-0118.html) for details.
