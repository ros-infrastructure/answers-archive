    You can publish a [joint trajectory message](http://docs.ros.org/api/trajectory_msgs/html/msg/JointTrajectory.html). You will need a Gazebo plugin that listens for the joint trajectory message. Take a look at the [`gazebo_ros_joint_trajectory` plugin](https://github.com/ros-simulation/gazebo_ros_pkgs/blob/hydro-devel/gazebo_plugins/src/gazebo_ros_joint_trajectory.cpp), part of the `gazebo_plugins` ROS package.

    Here is a [tutorial](http://gazebosim.org/wiki/Tutorials/1.9/ROS_Control_with_Gazebo) about using ROS to control a robot in Gazebo.
