    \ should be paid attention if want to make it work:\na,  make sure \"sudo ifconfig\

    \  becomes  192.168.3.100).    There is a problem here: eth0 may not become \"\
    192.168.3.100\" after \"sudo ifconfig eth0 192.168.3.100\",  I may do this several\
    \ times\uFF08For the ifconfig issue ,  do \u201Csudo ifdown eth0\u201D and then\
    \ after a few seconds do \u201Csudo ifup eth0\u201D.  After change the interface\
    \ config you may have to force it to read the new config changes by shutting it\
    \ down and bringing it back up ,  and then the problem can be solved\uFF09.\n\n\
