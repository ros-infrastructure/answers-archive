    > The following packages have unmet dependencies

    This error is FAQ in lower level on Ubuntu, not ROS. I suggest you fix that by looking up one of many results from web search result ([e.g. Google](https://www.google.com/search?client=ubuntu&channel=fs&q=The+following+packages+have+unmet+dependencies%3A&ie=utf-8&oe=utf-8)).

    Also suggested is how you not care if the dependency is met or not; see [this section in rosdep tutorial](http://wiki.ros.org/rosdep#Install_dependency_of_all_packages_in_the_workspace) to automatically install ALL packages that the ROS packages in your workspace depend upon.
