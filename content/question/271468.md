    Hi there! Can you do a rostopic echo and check that the controllers are sending effort commands?
    If they are sending them then you are on the right path, might want to check your PID gains because they could be so low that it doesn't overcome the weights of your prismatic link.

    You can check how to tune the PIDs for gazebo in this tutorial:
    http://gazebosim.org/tutorials/?tut=ros_control
