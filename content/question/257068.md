    The tf2::Transform is in `<tf2/LinearMath/Transform.h>` [here](https://github.com/ros/geometry2/blob/indigo-devel/tf2/include/tf2/LinearMath/Transform.h#L32).  My guess is that you are not adding the correct paths to your include directories in you CMakeLists.txt


    Note that in general it's recommended to use a standalone linear math library like [eigen](http://wiki.ros.org/eigen) or [bullet](http://www.continuousphysics.com/Bullet/BulletFull/index.html) or [kdl](http://www.orocos.org/kdl)

    You can used packages like [tf2_bullet](http://wiki.ros.org/tf2_bullet) or  [tf2_eigen](http://wiki.ros.org/tf2_eigen) or  [tf2_kdl](http://wiki.ros.org/tf2_kdl)

    Or you can add support for your own datatypes: http://wiki.ros.org/tf2/Tutorials/Create%20Data%20Conversion%20Package%20%28C%2B%2B%29
