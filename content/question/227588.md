  body_markdown: "Two part answer:\n\n1) You are publishing incorrectly. The command\
    \ should be something like:\n\n    rostopic pub --once /turtle1/pose turtlesim/Pose\
    \ \"{x: 0.0, y: 0.0, theta: 0.0, linear_velocity: 0.0, angular_velocity: 0.0}\"\
    \n\n2) You cannot teleport the turtle by publishing to the `pose` topic. You need\
    \ to use a service call:\n\n    rosservice call /turtle1/teleport_absolute ...\
    \ # Use tab-tab and it will auto-fill the '...'\n\n "
