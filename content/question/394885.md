    Shouldn't it be rather `print("list: ", msg.data)` in your callback? Check ROS documentation [there](http://wiki.ros.org/ROS/Tutorials/WritingPublisherSubscriber%28python%29#rospy_tutorials.2FTutorials.2FWritingPublisherSubscriber.Writing_the_Subscriber_Node) for correct form of ROS1 subscriber.
