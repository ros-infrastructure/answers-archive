    \ broad and well researched.\nExcluding hardware demands as this another broad\
    \ subject, I will just give you some guidance to start your research.\n\nFor example\
    \ ORB_SLAM2 is well established and popular ROS community. \n\n> ORB_SLAM2:  ROS\
    \ implementation of the\n> ORB-SLAM2 real-time SLAM library for\n> Monocular,\
    \ Stereo and RGB-D cameras\n> that computes the camera trajectory\n> and a sparse\
    \ 3D reconstruction (in the\n> stereo and RGB-D case with true\n> scale). It is\
    \ able to detect loops and\n> relocalize the camera in real time.\n\n**Reference**:\
    \ https://github.com/appliedAI-Initiative/orb_slam_2_ros\n\nTo compare the performance\
    \ against others, you can read ORB-SLAM: a Versatile and Accurate Monocular SLAM\
    \ System goes in depth about the particulars of benefits, benchmarks and results\n\
    http://webdiis.unizar.es/~raulmur/MurMontielTardosTRO15.pdf\n\nIf you visit **KITTI\
    \ Vision Benchmark Suite**:\nYou will find hundreds of implementations, with papers\
    \ and many algorithms that optimize for particular requirements.\nhttp://www.cvlibs.net/datasets/kitti/eval_odometry.php\n\
    \nFrom this you can use KITTI dataset to test your algorithm and compare performance\
    \ to others as well.\nAll these implementations can be integrated with ROS. \n\
    \nOther implementation to consider is Open VSLAM: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1910.01122.pdf\
    \ \n\nAnd more recent and extremely promising ORB_SLAM3: https://github.com/UZ-SLAMLab/ORB_SLAM3\n\

    I'm not usually about advertising my papers in this venue, but I think some work here could give you some good context https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.07589 "A Comparison of Modern General-Purpose Visual SLAM Approaches". The aim of the paper was comparing existing openly available VSLAM systems for practical service robot application.

    While we do not specifically focus on "Outdoor" you can see extensive discussions about the weaknesses and strengths of different methods given access to different hardware in various service environments (including outdoors as an important subsection).
