  body_markdown: "You can use rosbags to save the data published in different topics\
    \ (and play it later). You will find more informations [here](http://wiki.ros.org/rosbag)\
    \ and [here](http://wiki.ros.org/rosbag/Commandline). The main command line you\
    \ need to know is \n\n    rosbag record imu_data\nwhere imu_data is the name of\
    \ your IMU topic.\n\nif you want to access your data outside ROS you can use\n\
    \n    rostopic echo -b file.bag -p /topic > data.txt\n\nThis method works well\
    \ for simple message formats and will redirect your data to a text file."
