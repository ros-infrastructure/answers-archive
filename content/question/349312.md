    \n    visual_tools_->publishTrajectoryLine(my_plan.trajectory_, joint_model_group->getLinkModel(\"\
    tcp_link\"), joint_model_group, rvt::LIME_GREEN);\n\nWhere as \"tcp_link\" is\
    \ my end effector link name for Moveit. I was having the same problem and this\
    \ is what worked for me.\n"
