    ROS Diamondback's pluginlib (image_transport depends on pluginlib) compiles with Boost 1.43,0-1  but not with Boost 1.47.01 on Arch. Use Arch Rollback Machine to downgrade to older version of Boost to solve this issue (thanks Patrick for this link:  [http://www.ros.org/reps/rep-0003.html](http://www.ros.org/reps/rep-0003.html)).
