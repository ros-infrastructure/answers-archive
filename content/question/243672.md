    \ by (meta-)package managers (such as `rosdep`, but ultimately `apt`, `dfn` and\
    \ others) to understand how your package relates to all others (in terms of dependencies,\

    \ That is `CMakeLists.txt`'s task (via `find_package(..)`), and you deliberately\

    <catkin_ws>/src/package2/src/MyFile.cpp:2:32: fatal error: package1/Type1.h: No such file or directory
