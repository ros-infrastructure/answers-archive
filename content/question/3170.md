    The drc_vehicle model in the [drcsim](https://bitbucket.org/osrf/drcsim/src/b36170c2460b645c420a8e65c0f92139bf845990/gazebo_models/environments/drc_vehicle?at=default) repository has Ackermann steering implemented in the [DRCVehiclePlugin](https://bitbucket.org/osrf/drcsim/src/b36170c2460b645c420a8e65c0f92139bf845990/plugins/DRCVehiclePlugin.cc?at=default) with PID controllers. I would be happier if the steering model used a mechanical linkage, but it works ok. There's some tutorials on driving it [here](http://gazebosim.org/wiki/Tutorials/drcsim/2.2/VRC_Plugin_DRC_Vehicle).
