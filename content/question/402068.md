    I solved the issue by forking the ros1_bridge.
    The issue was that ros1_bridge only forward messages that are from a package finishing by "_msgs"

    If needed here is my fork, you can also easily implement any package not finishing by _msgs just by modifying the lines 481 and 482 of __init.py
