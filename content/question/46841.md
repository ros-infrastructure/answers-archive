    \ check that the `ROS_PACKAGE_PATH` is correct (see \"Environment setup\" in the\
    \ [ROS installation instructions](http://www.ros.org/wiki/fuerte/Installation/Ubuntu)).\n\
    \n    $ echo $ROS_PACKAGE_PATH \n\nshould say something like this (first some\

    \ with Iran, it turned out that the problem's cause was that an older revision\
    \ of `rosjava_core` was built with electric, then `rosjava_core` was updated to\
    \ the current tip and compiled with Fuerte. Removing the `rosjava_core` directory\
    \ and cloning it fresh solved the problem."
