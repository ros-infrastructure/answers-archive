    Hi there, are you doing motion planning for a vehicle or something that moves in 2D?
    You don't need OMPL to achieve so, the [ROS navigation stack](http://wiki.ros.org/navigation) does that for you with more time optimized algorithms.
    A great example of that is the [Turtlebot who navigates in a known environment](http://wiki.ros.org/turtlebot_navigation/Tutorials/indigo/Autonomously%20navigate%20in%20a%20known%20map) (it can also be an unknown environment so no worries.)

    You can check how it works in this youtube video: https://youtu.be/PJH2oMeSjFQ
