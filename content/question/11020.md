  body_markdown: |-
    This is typically a sensor-dependent "brightness" of return, representing some quality of measurement: energy returned, pixel value, etc.  Sometimes it can look like a greyscale brightness.  See the [images on the right here](http://www.ros.org/wiki/pr2_calibration/Tutorials/Calibrating%20the%20PR2#pr2_calibration.2BAC8-SubTutorials.2BAC8-Capture_Data.Capturing_Large_Checkerboards) to view some images produced by a laser scanner returning intensities.

    Just be aware that sometimes it isn't human color dependent.  Some materials reflect IR laser beams very well but appear black to our eyes.
