  body_markdown: "This is confusing, because `rosdep` changed significantly between\
    \ Electric and Fuerte. \n\n * On Fuerte it resolves to `libtbb-dev`.\n\n * On\
    \ Electric, it needs to be defined in some stack's rosdep.yaml. \n\nWhat does\
    \ this show?\n\n    $ rosdep where_defined tbb\n\nUPDATE: your result shows that\
    \ `tbb` is not defined by any stack in your `$ROS_PACKAGE_PATH`. \n\nIf you are\
    \ developing a stack of your own, add a `rosdep.yaml` containing something like\
    \ this stanza:\n\n    tbb:\n      arch: intel-tbb\n      ubuntu: libtbb-dev\n\
    \      fedora: tbb-devel "
