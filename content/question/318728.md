  body_markdown: "HI,\n\nyes, you are right the encoder based localization is accurate.\
    \ Make sure you are using the Ideal differential drive model(wheels axis rotation\
    \ is in the center of the robot) otherwise drift error will be accumulative. Your\
    \ localization accuracy depends upon the encoder resolution. Please take a look\
    \ at [this site](http://rossum.sourceforge.net/papers/DiffSteer/) for kinematic\
    \ of differential drive model. \nYou can convert the encoder data x, y, and theta(odometry\
    \ data), take a look at [this code](https://github.com/vanadiumlabs/arbotix_ros/blob/indigo-devel/arbotix_python/src/arbotix_python/diff_controller.py)\n\
    \nNOTE: You can also add magnetometer to improve theta. as per my experience,\
    \ angular displacement gives more errors."
