The simulation allows the fingers to pass through the steering wheel, which makes it difficult to hold the hand while trying to turn the wheel. 

[Sandia collision break through](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cg2P0eQjnwo)

In 0:35 it can be seen that there is some kind of "transparency" in the collision polygon, or it is in the hand or it is in the steering wheel, but this shows that some collision polygons are not 100% solid.
