Using gazebo 1.3.1, objects do not cast shadows on textured objects.

How to reproduce: start an empty world, add a coke can and a cube. The can casts shadows on the cube (and the ground), but the cube does not shadow the can (it shadows only the ground).

Importance: I would like to use a texturized terrain with shadows on the ground.
