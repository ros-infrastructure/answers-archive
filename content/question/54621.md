    The `btQuaternion`/`btVector3` bug was a leftover from the conversion of the plugin from ROS electric to fuerte. It was already fixed in [r679](https://code.google.com/p/tu-darmstadt-ros-pkg/source/detail?r=679) two months ago. Obviously at the time of the 0.1.1 relase of the [hector_gazebo](http://ros.org/wiki/hector_gazebo) stack, usage of the old bullet datatypes did not cause a compilation error.

    Today we released [version 0.1.2](http://tu-darmstadt-ros-pkg.googlecode.com/svn/tags/hector_gazebo/hector_gazebo-0.1.2/) of the stack (check the [ChangeList](http://ros.org/wiki/hector_gazebo/ChangeList)). You should either use this one or the latest version from [trunk](http://tu-darmstadt-ros-pkg.googlecode.com/svn/trunk/hector_gazebo).
