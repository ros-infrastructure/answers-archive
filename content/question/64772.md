    This question is directly related to a problem you are trying to solve in your previous [question](http://answers.ros.org/question/64763/change-path-of-rosrun-couldnt-find-executables/).

    Make sure you are familiar with [this tutorial](http://www.ros.org/wiki/catkin/Tutorials/using_a_workspace).

    In short, there are *install space* and *development space*. If you source `devel/setup.bash` then `roscd beginner_tutorials` should bring you to `~/catkin_ws/src/beginner_tutorials`. But if you source `install/setup.bash` then it will bring you to the directory where the package was installed.
