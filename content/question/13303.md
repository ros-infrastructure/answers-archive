  body_markdown: "Using `rqt_gui`, you're probably trying to publish to ROS topics,\
    \ rather than Gazebo topics, which is what your plugin is subscribed to. \n\n\
    You can check the list of Gazebo topics with active publishers/subscribers with\
    \ `gz topic -l`, and the list of ROS topics with `rostopic list`.\n\nYou can try\
    \ writing a Gazebo publisher like [this](https://bitbucket.org/osrf/gazebo/src/deb0665b2f18/examples/stand_alone/publisher/?at=default)."
