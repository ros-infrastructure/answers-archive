    Depending on your needs it might make sense to create a setup in (gazebo) simulation for easily generating stereo datasets. This way, you could also easily reconfigure the baseline and other parameters of both cameras. The PR2 head has two stereo cameras, so you could look into the corresponding URDF file for reference (see [here](http://ros.org/wiki/pr2_description)). A 3 pixel max disparity seems really small to me, but you know your application better than me ;)
