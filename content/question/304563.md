    It's not well documented as I believe it's not really intended to be reused outside of `roslaunch`, but you do this by essentially calling `substitution_args.py::resolve_args(..)` ([here](https://github.com/ros/ros_comm/blob/ef54759bf847ec53ac387a63dbefc556241fcf41/tools/roslaunch/src/roslaunch/substitution_args.py#L349-L393)).

    An example can be seen in `xacro` [here](https://github.com/ros/xacro/blob/0ae956da439bde5dc3fe19d9abc06c618a5ef6e9/src/xacro/__init__.py#L181-L193).
