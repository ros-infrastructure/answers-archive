      So you have a topic publishing a PointCloud2 message and you want to subscribe to it? If so, just subscribe to it as to any other topic.
      inside your code declare a publisher a node handle and then subscribe to it:
      ros::Subscriber cloudsub_=n_.subscribe("/camera/depth/points", 1, cloudcb);
      where cloudcb is your callback function.
