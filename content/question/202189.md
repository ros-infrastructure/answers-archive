    The PointCloud2 is packed into a format that is not automatically unpacked. [sensor_msgs/point_cloud2.py](http://docs.ros.org/indigo/api/sensor_msgs/html/point__cloud2_8py.html) can help you unpack the data (though it is a bit slow).

    Alternatively, if you just want depth, and not x,y,z points, you could subscribe to the depth image and use cv_bridge to get the data.
