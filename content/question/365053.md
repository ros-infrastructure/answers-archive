  body_markdown: "Hi Hassan,\n\nIt's very simple, really. You'll want to add an RViz\
    \ node tag inside your launch file. For example, in this launch file I created,\
    \ you can see the tag after what is needed to launch a turtlebot3 simulation:\n\
    \n    <launch>\n        \n        <!-- Launch Turtlebot3 World -->\n        <include\
    \ file=\"$(find turtlebot3_gazebo)/launch/turtlebot3_world.launch\"/>\n      \
    \  <!-- Launch Turtlebot3 robot state publisher and joint state publisher -->\n\
    \        <include file=\"$(find turtlebot3_bringup)/launch/turtlebot3_remote.launch\"\
    />\n    \n        <!--Run Rviz-->\n        <node name=\"rviz\" pkg=\"rviz\" type=\"\
    rviz\"/>\n    \n    </launch>\n\nThis will automatically launch RViz as soon as\
    \ you launch your simulate.launch file.\n\nIf you want to, you can go to the link\
    \ below to check out a video I created explaining this with a specific example:\n\
    \nhttps://youtu.be/SowG_XziP3s\n\nCheers!\n\nRodrigo"
