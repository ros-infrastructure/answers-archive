    Some googling brought me to [teleop_tools/issues/10](https://github.com/ros-teleop/teleop_tools/issues/10), where the author points to [reem_robot/reem_bringup/launch/joystick_teleop.launch](https://github.com/pal-robotics/reem_robot/blob/a22cfdbe7dcf2cc533ad8954f0430ff0ffbb86c8/reem_bringup/launch/joystick_teleop.launch) for an example launch file. That file refers to [reem_robot/reem_bringup/config/joy_teleop.yaml](https://github.com/pal-robotics/reem_robot/blob/df7e7973de1207c99a6cfb1a5c4c89bff863f49a/reem_bringup/config/joy_teleop.yaml), which should give you some clue as to how to configure things for your particular joystick.

    Note that the author of `joy_teleop` adds the following in [teleop_tools/issues/10](https://github.com/ros-teleop/teleop_tools/issues/10):

    > joy_teleop is subscribing to the `/joy` topic to provide high level functionalities. Unless you have a driver for your joystick it won't do any good.

    ---

    Edit: also, I think the [teleop_twist_joy](http://wiki.ros.org/teleop_twist_joy) is better suited for what you want to do. [joy_teleop](http://wiki.ros.org/joy_teleop) is a higher level package.
