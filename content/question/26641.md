    You can build a movement plugin to attach to your SDF model of the camera.
    The easier way I think is to change the position of the model in the world. Otherwise, you can set a velocity to your model and move the camera using it.
    You can start having a look at this [Plugin 101](http://gazebosim.org/tutorials?tut=plugins_hello_world&cat=write_plugin) and then have a look at [Model plugin](http://gazebosim.org/tutorials?tut=plugins_model&cat=write_plugin).
