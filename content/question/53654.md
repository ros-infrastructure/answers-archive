  body_markdown: "Assuming 8UC3 rgb image, and 16UC1 depth image, this is what I use\
    \ for saving and loading:\n\n    // save images \n    cv::imwrite(\"rgb.png\"\
    ,   rgb_img);\n    cv::imwrite(\"depth.png\", depth_img);\n\n    // load images\n\
    \    rgb_img = cv::imread(\"rgb.png\");\n    depth_img = cv::imread(\"depth.png\"\
    , -1);\n\nI believe the crucial part is the **-1** flag. From the [imread](http://docs.opencv.org/modules/highgui/doc/reading_and_writing_images_and_video.html?highlight=imread#imread)\
    \ page:\n \n> flags: <0 Return the loaded image as is (with alpha channel).\n\n"
