    Simply create a node that subscribes to you odometry topic, add noise on your data and republish them to another topic. It's up to you to choose the topics names regarding the rest of your setup.
  comments:
  - body_markdown: "You can also add it to your sensor modele e.g \n<sensor type=\"\
      ray\" name=\"sensor\">\n  <pose>0 0 -0.004645 1.5707 0 0</pose>\n  <visualize>true</visualize>\n\
      \  <ray>\n    <noise>\n      <!-- Use gaussian noise -->\n      <type>gaussian</type>\n\
      \      <mean>0.0</mean>\n      <stddev>0.1</stddev>\n    </noise>"
