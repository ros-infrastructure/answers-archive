    For the RPi you can setup a chroot image with debootstrap and qemu, assuming you are using Debian or Ubuntu, and ROS Groovy on the RPi, you can follow the instructions on the following page:
    [http://superpiadventures.com/2012/07/development-environment/](http://superpiadventures.com/2012/07/development-environment/)

    Then when you are inside the chroot, I would recommend you install *ros-groovy-ros* following the instructions available on the wiki: [http://www.ros.org/wiki/groovy/Installation/Raspbian](http://www.ros.org/wiki/groovy/Installation/Raspbian) as well as *build-essential*, you should then have all you need to build packages for your RPi.
