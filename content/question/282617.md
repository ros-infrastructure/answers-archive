  body_markdown: "The most basic ROS tutorials don't really require any specific knowledge\
    \ about C++/Python or Ubuntu Linux, but familiarity with Ubuntu Linux at least\
    \ is highly recommended. To complete the tutorials you really only need to know\
    \ how to open the terminal in Ubuntu and follow instructions:\n\nhttps://wiki.ros.org/ROS/Tutorials#Core_ROS_Tutorials\n\
    \nGoing beyond the tutorials and even understanding what they're teaching will\
    \ require more knowledge of Python and/or C++/CMake.\n\nI also recommend reading\
    \ the conceptual overview, or at least going back to it frequently to help you\
    \ understand the core concepts:\n\nhttps://wiki.ros.org/ROS/Introduction\n\nAs\
    \ for which version of ROS to use, please see:\n\nhttps://wiki.ros.org/Distributions#Which_distribution_to_use "
