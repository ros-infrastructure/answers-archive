  body_markdown: "I managed to find a workaround. I researched on other possible answers.\
    \ I realized that there are two representations of the angles -- quaternion and\
    \ euler. \n\nWhat I inputted was expressed in euler -- RPY -- but then when we\
    \ get the rotation, it returns a quaternion. \n\nOn the steps that I did, I did\
    \ not use the constructor in tf::Quaternion even though it did accept parameters\
    \ as yaw, pitch, roll. I tried that but the RPY keeps on changing.. Instead I\
    \ used the member function of tf::Quaternion in setting the yaw, pitch, roll which\
    \ totally worked. \n\nSo here are the steps:\n  \n     1. To broadcast RPY transformation\
    \ in radians\n          \n        tf::Quaternion q;\n        q.setRPY(roll,pitch,yaw);\n\
    \        transform.setRotation(q);\n\n      2. To read the RPY in radians\n  \
    \      \n        tf.waitForTransform(\"turtle1\", \"carrot1\", now, ros::Duration(3)\
    \ );\n        tf.lookupTransform(\"turtle1\", \"carrot1\", now, transform_);\n\
    \        transform_.getBasis().getRPY(roll,pitch,yaw);\n\n    #include <ros/ros.h>\n\
    \    #include <tf/transform_broadcaster.h>\n    #include <tf/transform_listener.h>\n\
