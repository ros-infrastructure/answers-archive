    found some reference benchmarks for reference:

     * [quadro 2000](http://www.videocardbenchmark.net/gpu.php?gpu=Quadro+2000)
     * [quadro 600](http://www.videocardbenchmark.net/gpu.php?gpu=Quadro+600)
     * [gtx 650](http://www.videocardbenchmark.net/gpu.php?gpu=GeForce+GTX+650)


    In addition, from specs on Nvidia site, the memory bandwidth of [Quadro 2000 (@41.5 GB/sec)](http://www.nvidia.com/object/product-quadro-2000-us.html) is about half of [GTX 650 (@80.0 GB/sec)](http://www.geforce.com/hardware/desktop-gpus/geforce-gtx-650/specifications), and [Quadro 600 (@25.6 GB/sec)](http://www.nvidia.com/object/product-quadro-600-us.html) will be about half of Quadro 2000.  Since a lot of the simulation work is getting the images off of these cards, one can probably expect performance to scale accordingly as well.
