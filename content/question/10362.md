    The [nxt_lxf2urdf tutorial](http://www.ros.org/wiki/nxt_lxf2urdf/Tutorials/Creating%20a%20simple%20robot%20model%20using%20lxf2urdf.py) explains how to convert your Lego digital designer files to URDF. That enables you to visualize your robot model in RViz. However, simulation in Gazebo is not possible at the moment (see [this question](http://answers.ros.org/question/1071/is-there-already-a-lego-mindstorms-nxt-simulation)).
