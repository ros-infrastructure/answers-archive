    There were incompatible changes to the way ROS uses PCL between Groovy and Hydro.

    Please consult the [Hydro migration guide](http://wiki.ros.org/hydro/Migration#PCL) for details.

    **EDIT**: since Groovy there is a new [pcl_conversions](http://wiki.ros.org/pcl_conversions) package that may help with your problem. The [API doc is here](http://docs.ros.org/indigo/api/pcl_conversions/html/namespacepcl__conversions.html).
