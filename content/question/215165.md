title: What is the endianness of ROS message fields?
upvote_count: 2

The documentation on ROS [messages](http://wiki.ros.org/msg) omits an essential detail for anyone implementing a new client library: what is the [endianness](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Endianness) of multi-byte fields such as `uint32` and `float64`?

Are these fields to be interpreted as little-endian (some of the bag serialization code would seem to imply that) or big-endian (which is generally the standard network byte order)?
