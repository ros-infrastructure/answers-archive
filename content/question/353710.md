    It is quite common to play `rosbag` files on a different machine than the one on which they were recorded.
    If you install the same ROS distribution on your laptop, this should work just fine. (There are also ways to migrate from older `rosbag` files using the [rosbag CLI](http://wiki.ros.org/rosbag/Commandline), if necessary)

    Since you mentioned that you need to extract images from the bag files, [this rosbag tutorial](http://wiki.ros.org/rosbag/Tutorials/Exporting%20image%20and%20video%20data) might also be of interest to you.
    I hope that helps.
