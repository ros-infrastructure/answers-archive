  body_markdown: "Yes, at the moment non-GPU sensors cannot see actor skeleton animations.\
    \ \n\nThe reason is that non-GPU sensors locate [collision](http://sdformat.org/spec?ver=1.6&elem=collision)\
    \ shapes, which are managed by the physics engine. Animated **skeletons** in actors\
    \ don't have collisions (for now). But other links animated within an actor may\
    \ have collisions which should be seen by non-GPU sensors.\n\nGPU based sensors,\
    \ on the other hand, use [visual](http://sdformat.org/spec?ver=1.6&elem=visual)\
    \ shapes, which are managed by the render engine, and actor skeletons have those."
