    \ be implemented. The service will not be able to keep the cameras turned on without\
    \ the buffer problem. (the first image was shown by the .read() method and after\

    \ keep the frames in realtime being publish (this is posible is you are working\
    \ wil low-mid resolution pictures, something like 4k will give you an exponential\
    \ latency depending on your memory, if so, pease try not to use realtime, but\
    \ clean your buffer before using using the image, this means use some .read()\
    \ as a photo rampage and select the last one to continue the flow, as shown next).\n\
