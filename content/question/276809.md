    You could take a look at [mapviz](http://wiki.ros.org/mapviz). It's not RViz, but similar enough.

    Less of a complete solution (and an actual RViz plugin): [gareth-cross/rviz_satellite](https://github.com/gareth-cross/rviz_satellite).

    Another option might be [osm_cartography](http://wiki.ros.org/osm_cartography).

    I haven't used either, but I know the ppl behind `mapviz` and that is well maintained, sees a lot of usage and is still actively developed.
