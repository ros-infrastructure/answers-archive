    But as a quick comment: ROS software is expected to adhere to [REP 103: Standard Units of Measure and Coordinate Conventions](https://www.ros.org/reps/rep-0103.html), and this includes the Gazebo<->ROS integration.

    `REP 103` states that all *length*s are in metres.
