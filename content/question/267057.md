    This is a duplicate of [this question](http://answers.ros.org/question/266831/action-server-to-move-robotic-arm/). The only way to make smooth motions is to implement a suitable trajectory. Quintic [trajectories](https://see.stanford.edu/materials/aiircs223a/handout6_Trajectory.pdf) (5-th order polynomials) will yield the least jerk. The [joint_trajectory_controller](http://wiki.ros.org/joint_trajectory_controller) package is a good place to start.
