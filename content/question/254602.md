      To shed some light on this: the ROS buildfarm and process do not guarantee ABI compatibility at the moment. This means that if you do a partial upgrade of ROS, you may end up having incompatible binaries and / or libraries. That typically results in 'mysterious' `SEGFAULT`s like you enountered.
