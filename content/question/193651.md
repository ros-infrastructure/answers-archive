  body_markdown: "The call `cv_ptr->toImageMsg();` retuns you an instance of `sensor_msgs::ImagePtr`\n\
    \nSee:\n\nhttp://docs.ros.org/indigo/api/cv_bridge/html/c++/classcv__bridge_1_1CvImage.html#a48a7cc72fa96bdf0180d0306543ceb07\n\
    \nIn your line \n\n    sensor_msgs::Image img2_ = cv_ptr->toImageMsg();\n\nyou\
    \ attempt to assign the pointer to an object.\n\nSo try either:\n\n    sensor_msgs::Image\
    \ img2_ = *(cv_ptr->toImageMsg());  // dereference pointer to get content before\
    \ assigning to object\n\nor:\n\n    sensor_msgs::ImagePtr img2_ = cv_ptr->toImageMsg();\
    \  //assign pointer to pointer"
