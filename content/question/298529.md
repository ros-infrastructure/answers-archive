    You probably have differential kinematics? Am I right? Angular velocity would than be calculated from a speed difference between left and right wheels and distance between them. An example for this you can find in [neo_driver](https://github.com/neobotix/neo_driver/blob/indigo_dev/neo_platformctrl_diff/common/src/DiffDrive2WKinematics.cpp) package. See function `execForwKin`. For theory make some Internet search on differential kinematics. There are plenty of presentations from university courses.

    EDIT:
    After clarifying the kinematics which is skid-steering one should look at [diff_drive_controller](https://github.com/ros-controls/ros_controllers/tree/a9e650737d6d04d8a768c84a48763bdbc5e05800/diff_drive_controller) which also enables skid-stearing. For start see this [file](https://github.com/ros-controls/ros_controllers/blob/a9e650737d6d04d8a768c84a48763bdbc5e05800/diff_drive_controller/test/skidsteerbot.cpp).
