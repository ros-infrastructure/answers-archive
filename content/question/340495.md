      Not an option in Nav Stack. It would just be standard usage.  The 'markers' are simply part of the environment that the laser scanner can see and so AMCL could use for localization. The markers would need to be in the map so that AMCL would recognize them as reference.

      BUT: you've said you need good accuracy. The Nav Stack will move the robot from one location to near another. You can tune the tolerance for how close it's gets to an exact location, but it won't necessarily nail it on the first approach and may dither around for a bit. And each repeated move between two points may not follow the same path.  The localization is based on Monte-Carlo analysis - randomized guessed about where the robot may be. It's not deterministic and the more you explain about the project the less I think straight nav stack is what you want. ACML and laser scanner or other methods suggested by gvdhoon for localization is good, But for path planning, Nav Stack isn't going to help much for painting things
