  body_markdown: "If you had access to the software (and had a working CAD model of\
    \ your robot), you could use [simmechanics_to_urdf](http://www.ros.org/wiki/simmechanics_to_urdf/Tutorials/Converting%20SimMechanics%20To%20URDF)\
    \ to go directly to URDF. \n\nAs that might not be the case, you could try the\
    \ [Guardian](http://www.ros.org/wiki/Robots/Guardian) stack. This is a four-wheeled\
    \ robot with a hokuyo and accelerometer model already included. If your robot\
    \ is skid-steered, the Guardian provides you with an example of how to model those\
    \ physics in Gazebo. \n\nFor the bump sensors and the IR sensors, you might have\
    \ to do some extra research. \n\n[Here](http://www.ros.org/wiki/urdf/Tutorials/UnderstandingPR2URDFGazeboExtension)\
    \ is an example of a bump sensor. \n\n[Here](http://code.google.com/p/uds-roomba-ros/source/browse/trunk/roomba_description/urdf/sensors/infrared.gazebo.xacro?spec=svn23&r=23)\
    \ is an implementation of an IR sensor. If that doesn't work, you could try [this](http://answers.ros.org/question/2239/gazebo-irsensor-parameters-configuration-xml)\
    \ question. \n\n[Here](http://www.ros.org/wiki/pr2_gazebo_plugins) is an example\
    \ of the pr2's Prosilica camera that you could use. "
