  body_markdown: "Yes, those parameters can be set through rosparam, you'll have to\
    \ replace hyphens with underscores though (see http://wiki.ros.org/fovis_ros#Parameters).\n\
    \nI havn't played much with parameter settings. My first guess to get a higher\
    \ rate would be:\n  \n  * decrease `feature_window_size` (could lead to false\
    \ matches)\n  * set `use_subpixel_refinement` to 0 (could lead to worse estimates)\n\
    \  * if the features in your environment are small, you could lower `max_pyramid_level`\
    \ (could lead to less features)\n\nIt is difficult to tune so many parameters\
    \ at once. I would suggest you try different sets of parameters and compare the\
    \ output."
