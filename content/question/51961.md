  body_markdown: "What does the generated URDF look like?\n\nDoes the <gazebo reference=\"\
    x\"> match the <link name=\"y\"> ? \n\nTry adding:\n\n    <gazebo reference=\"\
    PinkCube\">\n        <material>Gazebo/Blue</material>\n    </gazebo>\nbetween\
    \ the </link> and </robot> tags.  That should make it blue. If so, we're on the\
    \ right track. From there you can play around with the different materials available.\
    \ In Fuerte, they're listed in here: `/opt/ros/fuerte/stacks/simulator_gazebo/gazebo/gazebo/share/gazebo-1.0.2/Media/materials/scripts/Gazebo.material`\n\
    for Groovy, I think the G in \"Gazebo.material\" is lower case."
