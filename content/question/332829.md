  body_markdown: |-
    This turned out to be connected with the known issue of subprocesses from launch having their stdout streams buffered (https://github.com/ros2/launch/issues/188). There is a `emulate_tty` flag that [has recently been added](https://github.com/ros2/launch/blob/0f81d987039420036a7153c622b075c993ff1558/launch/launch/actions/execute_process.py#L178) to the `ExecuteProcess` action that defaults to `False`. Setting it to `True` in my launch file (through the `Node` action constructor) fixes the problem. Eventually it may default to `True`. (https://github.com/ros2/launch/pull/286)
