    Use `to_msg()` to convert from `rclpy.time.Time` to `builtin_interfaces.msg.Time`

          msg.header.stamp = node.get_clock().now().to_msg()

    [Link to `Time.to_msg()` in rclpy/time.py](https://github.com/ros2/rclpy/blob/196669e539dd51bc56f9f4fdf6f0222d99480d0d/rclpy/rclpy/time.py#L138-L140)
