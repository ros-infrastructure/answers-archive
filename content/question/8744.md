    There are some parameters defined in the [sdformat spec](http://sdformat.org/spec?ver=1.5&elem=sensor#sensor_camera), such as [horizontal\_fov](http://sdformat.org/spec?ver=1.5&elem=sensor#camera_horizontal_fov) and [distortion parameters](http://sdformat.org/spec?ver=1.5&elem=sensor#camera_distortion) documented on [this wikipedia page](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Distortion_(optics\)#Software_correction). I'm not sure how those map to focal length or principal point offsets.
