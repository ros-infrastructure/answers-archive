  body_markdown: "Look at the error message on the last line. Somewhere in your catkin\
    \ workspace, there is a malformed `package.xml`. The maintainer line should look\
    \ like this:\n\n    <maintainer email=\"martin.guenther@example.com\">Martin G\xFC\
    nther</maintainer>\n\n... but the `email` attribute is missing, i.e. it looks\
    \ like this, which is wrong:\n\n    <maintainer>Martin G\xFCnther</maintainer>"
