    I don't know why, but according to [debbuild/jade.html?q=controller_manager_msgs](http://www.ros.org/debbuild/jade.html?q=controller_manager_msgs), `controller_manager_msgs` (and almost [all other ros_control](http://www.ros.org/debbuild/jade.html?q=ros_control) packages) are not yet available in the *public* ROS package repositories. They should be installable from the [shadow repository](http://wiki.ros.org/ShadowRepository) though.

    That would explain why you can't `apt-get install ros-jade-ros-control`. Were you able to install any of the other `ros_control`packages on Jade?
