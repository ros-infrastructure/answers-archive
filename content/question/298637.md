    Solved the problem by adding a ssh host key for the jenkins-agent as mentioned below in the common.yaml file:

         'jenkins-agent@ip-address':
                key: AAAAB3NzaC1yc2EAAAADAQABAAAC
                type: ssh-rsa
                user: jenkins-agent
                require: User[jenkins-agent]
