    The base camera frame for a stereo system is the left optical frame.
    The viso2_ros stereo odometer reads all information including baseline from the corresponding camera info messages (i.e. `<stereo>/left/camera_info` and `<stereo>right/camera_info`). Be sure to [calibrate your stereo system using the camera_calibration package](http://www.ros.org/wiki/camera_calibration/Tutorials/StereoCalibration)!
