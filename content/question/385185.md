I'm trying to use navsat_transform_node and the localization package to filter in my GPS location with wheel odometry and IMU data.  For now I am testing in gazebo (11.5.1 on ROS noetic) with odometry provided by the `libgazebo_ros_skid_steer_drive` gazebo plugin and IMU provided by the `libgazebo_ros_imu_sensor` plugin.  I suspect that the IMU plugin is NED rather than ENU as I have to give a PI/2 offset to navsat_transform_node for the odometry/gps to move in the correct direction.  So far so good.

However, the odometry created by navsat_transform_node moves twice as much as the robot moves.  For example, if the robot moves 10m along the X axis, the GPS-based odometry moves by 20m along the X axis.  The same happens along the Y axis, and when moving along both axes.  I wanted to compare the speeds but the GPS-based odometry does not populate the twist part of the message.

What am I missing?
