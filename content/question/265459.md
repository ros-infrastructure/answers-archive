    This is really a C/C++ question rather than a ROS one. In the future: please try to find a more appropriate forum for these kind of questions.

    As to what `ROSBAG_DECL` is: it's a macro that configures visibility of the declaration / definition that follows it. See [ros_comm/tools/rosbag_storage/include/rosbag/macros.h](https://github.com/ros/ros_comm/blob/63f8fefadba4d80c38cf544cda4556aa3559cb12/tools/rosbag_storage/include/rosbag/macros.h#L35-L43) for what it does specifically, and see [gcc/wiki/visibility](https://gcc.gnu.org/wiki/Visibility) for general info about symbol visibility.
