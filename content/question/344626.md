    The prefix of the function `rosidl_typesupport_c` indicates the package where that symbol is being defined: https://github.com/ros2/rosidl_typesupport/tree/4847322f6a03e5a2ec5cf0498ec8d93d0be8f7b4/rosidl_typesupport_c
