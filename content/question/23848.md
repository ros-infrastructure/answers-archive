  body_markdown: "You can combine the joints. It will be technically 3 links connected\
    \ by 2 elementary joints, not 2 links connected by 1 complex joint. \n\n    link1\
    \ --> joint1:prismatic --> link2(dummy link) --> joint2:revolute --> link3\n\n\
    You will have to think about how to set it (joints position, links dimension and\
    \ inertial) so it made sense for your concrete application. "
