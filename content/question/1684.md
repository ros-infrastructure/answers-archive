  body_markdown: |
    Current solution for reading URDF models in Gazebo assumes the ROS package containing the model must also have a valid [Gazebo `model.config` file](http://gazebosim.org/user_guide/started__models__database.html) in addition to the [ROS manifest.xml file](http://www.ros.org/doc/independent/api/rospkg/html/manifest_xml.html).  And the model must be in both `GAZEBO_MODEL_PATH` and `ROS_PACKAGE_PATH` for both ROS and Gazebo to find the model.

    With that assumption, `package://` is simply replaced by `model://` by [parser_urdf.cc](https://bitbucket.org/osrf/gazebo/src/3d057ec159d60aaff37dd03f4c2160853776d7d9/gazebo/sdf/interface/parser_urdf.cc?at=default#cl-711).

  body_markdown: "If you do not want to have your model in two different places like\
    \ @hsu said (`GAZEBO_MODEL_PATH` and `ROS_PACKAGE_PATH`) then what you can do\
    \ is edit the launch file for your model's package, and after the <launch> tag,\
    \ but before you run any gazebo related stuff, insert an <env> tag which will\
    \ allow you to temporarily set that environment variable, without changing across\
    \ your entire system. [http://wiki.ros.org/roslaunch/XML/env](http://wiki.ros.org/roslaunch/XML/env)\n\
    \nHere would be an example:\n\n    <launch>\n    \n      <env name=\"GAZEBO_MODEL_PATH\"\
    \ value=\"$(find surus_sim)\"/>\n\n      <param name=\"robot_description\" textfile=\"\
    $(find surus_sim)/robots/surus_sim.sdf\" />\n      <node\n        name=\"spawn_model\"\
    \n        pkg=\"gazebo_ros\"\n        type=\"spawn_model\"\n        args=\"-sdf\
    \ -param robot_description -model surus_sim\"\n        output=\"screen\">\n  \
    \    </node>\n    </launch>\n\nand then in your SDF (and i believe this would\
    \ work for URDF too) you specify the path of your mesh(s) relative to the value\
    \ specified in the <env> tag. \n\n       <geometry>\n          <mesh>\n      \
    \      <scale>1 1 1</scale>\n            <uri>model://meshes/base_link.STL</uri>\n\
    \          </mesh>\n        </geometry>\n"
