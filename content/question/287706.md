    In the [tutorial](http://wiki.ros.org/rtabmap_ros/Tutorials/MappingAndNavigationOnTurtlebot), the example was to map, then do localization like the [official turtlebot navigation tutorial](http://wiki.ros.org/turtlebot_navigation/Tutorials/indigo/Build%20a%20map%20with%20SLAM) using gmapping/amcl. However, like in the original tutorial, you can still send poses to navigation system ([move_base](http://wiki.ros.org/move_base)) during the mapping phase. To automatically explore the environment, you would have to integrate an exploration package like [frontier_exploration](http://wiki.ros.org/frontier_exploration), [hector_exploration_planner](http://wiki.ros.org/hector_exploration_planner) or [explore_lite](http://wiki.ros.org/explore_lite) using the map published by rtabmap and remapping the goals sent by the exploration to move_base.
