    The easiest method is probably to convert the data in the bag file to a csv file, have a look at this topic for some suggestions how to do that:

    [how to extract data from a bag](http://answers.ros.org/question/9102/how-to-extract-data-from-bag/)

    [exporting ros bag files to matlab](http://nimbus.unl.edu/2014/11/exporting-ros-bag-files-to-matlab/)

    Good luck
