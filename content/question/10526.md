    You need to **export** the include and possibly linker flags of the header in its [manifest](http://www.ros.org/wiki/Manifest).
  comments:
  - body_markdown: |-
      Right. Here's an example. It goes in package_a's manifest file and assumes you have your includes in package_a's include directory:   <export>
          <cpp cflags="-I${prefix}/include"/>
       </export>
