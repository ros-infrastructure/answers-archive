    IIRC, those values are actually stamps in [Unix time](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unix_time), so "*time in seconds since Thursday, 1 January 1970*". You can convert those timestamps pretty easily ([this](http://www.epochconverter.com/) site can do it).

    For instance, for `map -> odom_combined`, the last transform is dated `2015-05-01T18:29:40+00:00` ([link](http://www.convert-unix-time.com/?t=1430504980.224)).

    The time 'between' two transforms (if that even makes sense) would be calculated by subtracting their respective timestamps.
