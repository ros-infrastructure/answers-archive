    Each controller is running in a single namespace (see [here](https://github.com/husky/husky_simulator/blob/indigo-devel/husky_gazebo/urdf/description.gazebo.xacro#L37)), so you will need to adjust that. You will also need to configure the namespace for the [imu](https://github.com/husky/husky_simulator/blob/indigo-devel/husky_gazebo/urdf/husky.gazebo.xacro#L34) and for the [gps](https://github.com/husky/husky_simulator/blob/indigo-devel/husky_gazebo/urdf/husky.gazebo.xacro#L50).

    You need to also modify the -model parameter in spawn_husky_model or both robots will get an error.

    here's a screenshot of the two huskies moving independently after applying the modifications

    ![](https://ftp.osuosl.org/pub/ros/download.ros.org/downloads/se_migration/ros/14544708207932291.png)
