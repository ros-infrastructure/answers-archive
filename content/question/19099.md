    I haven't seen a ready-made package for it, but you should be able to implement it by:

    1. using some ROS package to capture key presses (I assume you want key presses on the terminal?)
    1. Publish key presses with ROS
    1. Have a `gazebo::ModelPlugin` attached to your actor which is listening to the ROS messages and controlling the actor. [Here](https://github.com/osrf/servicesim/blob/master/servicesim_competition/src/TrajectoryActorPlugin.cc)'s a plugin which makes an actor walk/run to specific target points, you could adapt it to your needs.
