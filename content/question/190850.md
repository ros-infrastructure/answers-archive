    Any stamped (i.e. containing timing/transform info) message will have a [header](http://docs.ros.org/indigo/api/std_msgs/html/msg/Header.html) (e.g. [PointCloud](http://docs.ros.org/api/sensor_msgs/html/msg/PointCloud.html)) that contains the relevant timestamp and frame id.

    `cloud->header.frame_id`
