    First of all you can detect object with camera, but you cannot get coordinates of the object since camera is a 2D sensor, you need laserscan, pointcloud or stereo-camera. But if you want to do it with just camera, take a look [here](https://visp-doc.inria.fr/doxygen/visp-daily/tutorial-detection-object.html). You can add the distance you measure as a new transform and give it's coordinates to end effector to move towards. [Here](http://wiki.ros.org/urdf/Tutorials/Building%20a%20Visual%20Robot%20Model%20with%20URDF%20from%20Scratch) is a URDF tutorial that will help you to add a new link.
