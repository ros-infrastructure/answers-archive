    1. Elevate the privileges to superuser and source the setup.bash of the workspace

        `sudo su` then `source devel/setup.bash`.

    2. Install the package
    `catkin_make -DCMAKE_INSTALL_PREFIX=/opt/ros/$ROS_DISTRO install`
