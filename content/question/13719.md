Hi,

I am writing here instead of reporting a Gazebo issue because I am not sure if I am doing something wrong.

[REP117](http://www.ros.org/reps/rep-0117.html#specification) states that: "Detections that are too close to the sensor to quantify shall be represented by -Inf. Erroneous detections shall be represented by quiet (non-signaling) NaNs. Finally, out of range detections will be represented by +Inf." And [this PR](https://bitbucket.org/osrf/gazebo/pull-requests/1567/report-inf-for-ranges-outside-of-ray/diff) implemented it. I do not know which release is that, but Gazebo 5.3.0, the one I am using, has [this fix](https://bitbucket.org/osrf/gazebo/src/ffcd2174abf49622b7ac050bcb359b7f1958d7c7/gazebo/sensors/RaySensor.cc?at=default&fileviewer=file-view-default#RaySensor.cc-562:569).

However, if I simply insert a Hokuyo laser and place and object too close, we see that the measurements below minimum range are incorrect (they go within the obstacle) and ranges with max_range are reported as max_range:

![image description](https://ftp.osuosl.org/pub/ros/download.ros.org/downloads/se_migration/gazebosim/14666773352504884.png)

Furthermore, using the gazebo_ros plugin libgazebo_ros_laser.so I can see that it is reported exactly the same as in the topic visualization (wrong min_range instead of -inf and max_range instead of inf).

Is this a bug or I am doing something wrong?

**EDIT:** I have tested this in Gazebo7, and max range is converted to +inf correctly, but minimum range keeps doing the same (more or less).

Thanks a lot!
