    Works correctly.
    You have to create your own node and inherit from QNode class. For example, when you want to subscribe to some topic, you should send a signal from the callback. The slot should be implemented in the MainWindow class as well as connection between the signal and the slot.

    EDIT:
    You might want to take a look at qlistener/qtalker demo features and investigate how to integrate your ROS design with Qt.
