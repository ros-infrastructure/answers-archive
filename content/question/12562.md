  body_markdown: "Found where the problem was by looking through the rxconsole printouts:\n\
    \n    [WARN]    Could not transform imu message from IMU to base_footprint. Imu\
    \ will not be activated yet. \n\nTurns out that the frame_id when I published\
    \ the /imu/data was not set to gyro_link to match the URDF. That's why robot_pose_ekf\
    \ could not work properly.\n\nFor those of you reading this who are just starting\
    \ out ROS/navigation stack, it would be good to remember the difference between\
    \ \"topic\" and \"frame\", and the need to connect the two when publishing the\
    \ topic."
