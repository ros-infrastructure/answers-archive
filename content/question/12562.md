  body_markdown: "The acceleration is the derivative of the velocity and the velocity\
    \ is the derivative of the position/angle. \nYou can just create a small class,\
    \ that keeps in memory the previous (or many previous) angle/velocity and get\
    \ the acceleration from that. Typically, if you get the velocity of your joint\
    \ at each iteration i, then the acceleration would be something like:\n\n    accelaration[i]\
    \ = (velocity[i-1] - velocity[i]) / (time[i-1] - time[i])\nThis is not perfectly\
    \ exact since we should use:\n\n    accelaration[i] = (velocity[i-1] - velocity[i+1])\
    \ / (time[i-1] - time[i+1])\nBut since we can't predict the future, it should\
    \ give you  good aproximation.\nAn other way would be to use the force sum on\
    \ your joint and compute the formula F = m.a but this requires much more knowledge\
    \ and is too cumbersome. ` \n\n   "
