    Depending on the RMW you're using, you'd have to make sure UDP multicast traffic is also routed, as it's often used for discovery. Some RMWs can be configured to use unicast, TCP only (easier to route) or even be provided with a list of "initial peers/participants" (less / no discovery at all).

    eProsima, who maintains Fast-DDS and all related software, has a "routing service", which, ironically, sort-of re-introduces the concept of a 'master' to ROS 2 (not really, as it really just helps with discovery, it doesn't do anything else). Cyclone DDS can also be configured to not use UDP multicast for discovery, which is typically what makes discovery either unreliable, or difficult to get working.

The RPi4 is able to see ROS2 data from both the PC on the robot and remote PC's. The problem is that a remote PC cannot access the robots PC, and vice versa. How would I go about routing the traffic between the `wlan0` and `usb0` interface on the RPi?

The RPi4 is running Ubuntu 20.04 Server with ROS2 Galactic.
