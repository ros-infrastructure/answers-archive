    There are some contact parameters that can be used to improve contact force stability, especially the `min_depth` parameter (see [another question/answer](http://answers.gazebosim.org/question/5360/toggling-contact-of-wheel/)). Hi all,

In my simulation I have a simple box on the ground plane with the following parameters:

    Dimensions: 1[m]x1[m]x1[m]
    Moment of Inertia: ixx=0.166666667 iyy=0.166666667 izz=0.166666667
    Mass: 1[kg]

As seen from this [youtube video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Z7HPGmw-KoM) the contact forces are oscillating for this simple setup. Using the ros bumper plugin it was confirmed on rviz ([youtube video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=djaeiVS7hJw)) as well. When the moment of inertia was changed to deafult values (ixx=1.0 iyy=1.0 izz=1.0) there was no such oscillations as seen by:[C:\fakepath\gazebo-stable.jpg](https://ftp.osuosl.org/pub/ros/download.ros.org/downloads/se_migration/gazebosim/13996061409402466.jpg)

According to [ODE wiki](http://ode-wiki.org/wiki/index.php?title=Manual:_Concepts) ODE engine has an accuracy issue. So, does that mean I cannot use it for example, calculating collision forces, torques in simulation?
