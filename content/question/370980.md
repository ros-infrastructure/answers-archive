  body_markdown: "There are a bunch of things you'll want to change here.\n\n 1. Your\
    \ IMU specification says you are measuring all orientation variables, all linear\
    \ velocity variables, all angular velocity variables, and all linear acceleration\
    \ variables. However, if you look at your IMU message, linear velocity is not\
    \ a quantity that your IMU can provide. So the 7th, 8th, and 9th values (I'm not\
    \ indexing from 0 here) in that specification should be false.\n 2. Following\
    \ from that, you don't have anything measuring Z or Z velocity. You have something\
    \ measuring Z acceleration, but that means your only reference for Z (without\
    \ the IMU, anyway) is from acceleration, which is (a) noisy and (b) has to be\
    \ integrated twice to get position. If I were you, I would fuse your Z velocity\
    \ from your wheel encoders instead. The value will be 0, but this is accurate,\
    \ as your robot cannot fly. :) But the general rule of thumb for the EKF is that\
    \ you need to provide a reference (measurement) for every pose variable *or* its\
    \ velocity. This can be a challenge when you move to 3D.\n 3. You are fusing absolute\
    \ yaw from your wheel encoders _and_ your IMU. This won't go well. They aren't\
    \ going to agree, so the estimate will jump back and forth.\n 4. Even with that,\
    \ though, your yaw from your IMU will be subject to drift, because it comes from\
    \ a magnetometer, and you appear to be inside. Just be aware that metal, including\
    \ metal used in building construction, can have strong effects on magnetometers.\n\
    \ 5. I assume you have a *base_link*->*imu* transform specified somewhere? If\
    \ not, the IMU data will just be ignored.\n\nI recommend this for your setup:\n\
    \n        <rosparam param=\"odom0_config\">[false, false, false, \n\t        \
    \                            false, false, false, \n\t                       \
    \             true,  true,  true, \n\t                                    false,\
    \ false, true,\n\t                                    false, false, false]</rosparam>\
    \                                                                   \n\n     \
    \   <rosparam param=\"imu0_config\">[false, false, false, \n\t               \
    \                    true,  true,  true,\n\t                                 \
    \  false, false, false, \n\t                                   true,  true,  true,\n\
    \t                                   true,  true,  true]</rosparam>\n\nAlso, if\
    \ you are still struggling, try not fusing acceleration until the rest is working."
