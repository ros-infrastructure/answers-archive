- body_markdown: |-
    You cannot run the *same node* and make it register itself with two different masters. Or at least, you cannot run a single *instance* of a node and have it register itself with two different masters at the same time. That's not supported.

    You can pass the parameters you refer to to `ros::init(..)`, but you cannot pass them as *remaps*, as they are not remaps.

    You'd have to add them to the `argv` and update `argc` accordingly. Then pass those to `ros::init(..)`.

    But I'm not sure this will let you do what you describe.
