    \ value because\n> this enables the robot to back off\n> when it needs to unstuck\
    \ itself, but\n> it should prefer moving forward in\n> most cases. For minimum\
    \ rotational\n> velocity, we also want to set it to\n> negative (if the parameter\

    \ about robot\n> navigation is that the robot may get\n> stuck. Fortunately, the\
    \ navigation\n> stack has recovery behaviors built-in.\n> Even so, sometimes the\
    \ robot will\n> exhaust all available recovery\n> behaviors and stay still. Therefore,\n\
    > we may need to figure out a more\n> robust solution. \n\n> Types of recovery\n\
    > behaviors ROS navigation has two\n> recovery behaviors. They are clear\n> costmap\
    \ recovery and rotate recovery.\n> Clear costmap recovery is basically\n> reverting\
