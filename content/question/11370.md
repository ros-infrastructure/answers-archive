  body_markdown: "Do you have two packages that depend on one another anywhere in\
    \ your ROS_PACKAGE_PATH? If you have any two packages that depend on one another,\
    \ you could cause this circular dependency. In case you're curious, the output\
    \ of `rospack depends geometry_msgs` on your system should look like this: \n\n\
    \    rosbuild\n    roslang\n    cpp_common\n    roscpp_traits\n    rostime\n \
    \   roscpp_serialization\n    rospack\n    roslib\n    xmlrpcpp\n    rosconsole\n\
