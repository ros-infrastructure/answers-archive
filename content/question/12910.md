  body_markdown: "\"/world\" frame is typically used to represent absolute positions\
    \ in the world. It has no parent, so its value is always (0, 0, 0).\n\n(0, 0,\
    \ 0) in the world frame represents the intersection of the Prime Meridian and\
    \ the Equator at sea level in outdoor applications, but could represent the southwest\
    \ corner of a building on the 1st floor, or any other fixed point. \n\nThe world\
    \ frame is your permanent frame. It is the frame against which all other frames\
    \ are measured. If you're only working inside of a building, your world frame\
    \ should be a fixed point in that building. If your robot can go anywhere in the\
    \ world, maybe your world frame should be the world frame I mentioned above. "
