    > Are the basics of Jade and Indigo similar enough that reading a book on Indigo will be practical when working with Jade?

    Yes.

    In fact, Indigo and Jade are as far as I can see completely identical at that level (you can always check the [Jade migration guide](http://wiki.ros.org/jade/Migration)). AGITR doesn't use any really complex components (such as navigation or MoveIt) which are more likely to change between ROS releases.

    You'll have to replace `jade` wherever you read `indigo` (in paths, etc), but other than that you should be able to use the book without issue.
