    RVIZ doesn't have any IK internally -- I presume you are using the MoveIt configuration to drag the arm with Interactive Markers? If so, the IK is probably the [KDL plugin](https://github.com/ros-planning/moveit/tree/kinetic-devel/moveit_kinematics/kdl_kinematics_plugin) from MoveIt. Just a note though: it does not provide any guarantee that the arm won't "flip" -- you're probably just getting lucky since the Fetch has so many continuous rotation joints.
