  body_markdown: "The docker images are built during a job on gitlab. You can have\
    \ a look at the gitlab-ci file [here](https://gitlab.com/autowarefoundation/autoware.auto/AutowareAuto/-/blob/master/.gitlab-ci.yml).\
    \  \nHave a look at the lines `24` and `36` for the docker build commands.\n\n\
    The docker files used for the build are located in the [AutowareAuto/tools/ade_image\n\
    ](https://gitlab.com/autowarefoundation/autoware.auto/AutowareAuto/-/tree/master/tools/ade_image)\
    \ directory in the repo.\n\nAlso you can have a look at the [container registry](https://gitlab.com/autowarefoundation/autoware.auto/AutowareAuto/container_registry)\
    \ to see the built docker images."
