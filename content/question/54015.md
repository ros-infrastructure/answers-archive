    Which message are listening for from the navigation package? If you are listening for [nav_msgs/Path](http://ros.org/doc/api/nav_msgs/html/msg/Path.html), the coordinates should be in meters. You should be able to get the total distance to the goal by adding up the small distances between the individual points.
