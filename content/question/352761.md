  body_markdown: "This depends on what settings you use. \n[http://wiki.ros.org/hector_mapping](http://wiki.ros.org/hector_mapping)\n\
    \nThe description of the map:  \"Get the map data from this topic, which is latched,\
    \ and updated periodically \"\nAnd we having settings such as: \"laser_max_dist\"\
    \ and \"laser_min_dist\".\n\n> Does the occupancy grid update 1:1\n> with our\
    \ scan? Or does the scan go\n> past our occupancy grid (or vice\n> versa)\n\n\
    No, especially when the scan can likely be 50hz and higher, the map will be updated\
    \ periodically (this does not mean the algorithm can't process all the scans).\
    \ In fact, the publish rate of the map is based on the parameter \"map_pub_period\"\
    \ which will at default publish the map at 0.5Hz. [See here](https://github.com/tu-darmstadt-ros-pkg/hector_slam/blob/melodic-devel/hector_mapping/src/HectorMappingRos.cpp#L94).\n\
    \nIf you use laser_max_dist and laser_min_dist with a narrow interval the scan\
    \ will go far beyond the occupancy grid."
