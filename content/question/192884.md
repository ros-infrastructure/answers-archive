    For most robots that include a mobile base, a robot (or mobile base) specific *driver* package is created, which includes functionality like you describe. The husky packages are examples of this.

    For a more generic, plugin, type approach you might take a look at the recently announced [diff_drive_controller](http://wiki.ros.org/diff_drive_controller) for [ros_control](http://wiki.ros.org/ros_controllers).
