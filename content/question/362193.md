    You're including two top-level `.xacro` files. That is not what you need/want to do.

    You need to include the `_macro.xacro` (similar to how it's done in the `robotiq_2f_140_model.xacro` and then instantiate (ie: call) the macro (again, as it's done in the `robotiq_2f_140_model.xacro`).

    Same for your robot model actually.

    Top-level `.xacro`s are just that: top-level. They cannot be used to create a composite.
