 Hello, I have a simulated Turtlebot3 Waffle Pi in Gazebo that uses the Rasperry Pi V2 camera. According to this documents: https://www.raspberrypi.com/documentation/accessories/camera.html it should be possible to have video resolution of 1080p. Wondering where you can change settings, looking at the turtlebot3_waffle_pi.gazebo.xacro there's these lines of code:

    <gazebo reference="camera_rgb_frame">
        <sensor type="camera" name="Pi Camera">
          <always_on>true</always_on>
          <visualize>$(arg camera_visual)</visualize>
          <camera>
              <horizontal_fov>1.085595</horizontal_fov>
              <image>
                  <width>640</width>
                  <height>480</height>
                  <format>R8G8B8</format>
              </image>
              <clip>
                  <near>0.03</near>
                  <far>100</far>
              </clip>
          </camera>

Would it be possible to change the camera FOV and resolution by editing this?
