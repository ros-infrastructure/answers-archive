    There is a [multi_robot_test.launch](https://code.ros.org/svn/wg-ros-pkg/stacks/wg_robots_gazebo/trunk/pr2_gazebo_wg/launch/multi_robot_test.launch) that works with trunk of [simulator_gazebo](http://ros.org/wiki/simulator_gazebo).  It starts up 3 robots under separate ros namespaces.

    I basically started with standard [pr2_empty_world.launch](https://code.ros.org/svn/wg-ros-pkg/stacks/pr2_simulator/trunk/pr2_gazebo/pr2_empty_world.launch) and namespaced nodes / added tf_prefixes.

    robots spawn and pieces of it are controllable if you configure your teleop to work under a namespace, but otherwise much of this is untested.
