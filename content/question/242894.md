    Within ROS you could define the TF frames for your cameras as being inverted.

    Note that image processing expects an frame ID with the ``_optical`` suffix, which [uses a different orientation convention](http://www.ros.org/reps/rep-0103.html#suffix-frames) than normal ROS frames of reference.
