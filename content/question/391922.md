I have a problem with robot_localization. 
In my project I have two sensors: a camera (Realsense T265) and an IMU. I want to merge the two sensor data in robot_localization.

When I start my launcher the program works without any problems.
However, when my robot is in a horizontal Position, the covariances are getting huge and the topic /odomerty_filtered stops publishing.

I have no idea, why this could happen.
