  body_markdown: "Your `racondition` is a callback that is called every time it receives\
    \ a vehicle status message.  This then calls publish.\nIf you only want to pub\
    \ once (e.g. on first message), you could add a some flag that is initialized\
    \ true in constructor, and then false after first publishing.\n\nYou should also\
    \ define the publisher `driver_wish_pub` once in the constructor, not every time\
    \ your `racondition` is called.\nThen just call publish `driver_wish_pub.publish\
    \ ` when you want to publish.  "
