  body_markdown: |-
    The `moveit_ros/planning_interface/move_group_interface` module is usually used to generate `MotionPlanRequests` for simple pose goals.

    If you look at its implementation [here](https://github.com/ros-planning/moveit/blob/kinetic-devel/moveit_ros/planning_interface/move_group_interface/src/move_group_interface.cpp#L1092), you will see this simply creates Position and Orientation goal constraints out of the requested pose. It basically creates a small target volume (.1mm^3) out of the position to create a position constraint and adds axis tolerances of .001rad and the provided orientation as an orientation constraint.
