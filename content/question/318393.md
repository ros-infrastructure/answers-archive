    The way you attempted to produce the desired CSV isn't really a ROS question at all. Rather, that's a Bash/sed question that would be better asked on something like [Super User](https://superuser.com/).  That said, there are several ROS ways that you could likely get what you want.

    + The [rosbag api](http://wiki.ros.org/rosbag/Code%20API) would allow you to easily open the bag file and then save the data you want in whatever your desired format. A Python script to do what you're looking for would only be a few lines
    + With a combination of the `-p` and `-b` args to `rostopic echo` you could easily produce a CSV file that included all fields in the topic. Then it would be trivial to extract the columns that you are looking for. See [this question on SO](https://stackoverflow.com/questions/7857090/extract-specific-columns-from-delimited-file-using-awk) for some good examples. I've used `cut` for this purpose many times.
