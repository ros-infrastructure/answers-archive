    You could also try the [gazebo_ros_force_based_move](https://github.com/tu-darmstadt-ros-pkg/hector_gazebo/blob/indigo-devel/hector_gazebo_plugins/src/gazebo_ros_force_based_move.cpp#L174) plugin. It is similar to the planar move plugin, but applies forces and torques to the body of the robot, not bypassing the physics engine. I previously observed issues with the planar move plugin and a manipulator arm mounted on a robot (arm wobbling  around the z-axis when robot rotates). This doesn't happen with the force-based plugin. [Here's a example video](https://youtu.be/dHWFviA2S3E) of using it for a simulated tracked robot.
