    The problem could be in this line: `h = rospy.Header()`

    [`Header` belongs to std_msgs](http://docs.ros.org/en/noetic/api/std_msgs/html/msg/Header.html), and it's already being imported in `from std_msgs.msg import Header, Float32`. Try this instead:

        h = Header()
        h.stamp = rospy.Time.now()

    (check out the accepted answer to [What is the proper way to create a Header with python?](https://answers.ros.org/question/60209/what-is-the-proper-way-to-create-a-header-with-python/))
