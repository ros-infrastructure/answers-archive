    Hi @Shapy,

    For the first compilation issue you missed a strong dependency: `sudo apt-get install ros-melodic-qt-build`.
    For the second: Maybe a naive question but, Do you have ROS installed in your system? Because the fact that the CMake is unable to find roscpp is a clear indication that you do not have ROS installed or at least you have it misconfigured. Maybe your `ROS_PACKAGE_PATH` environment variable is not set accordingly. Take a look at [this](https://wiki.ros.org/ROS/Tutorials/InstallingandConfiguringROSEnvironment) wiki post, maybe you find it useful.
