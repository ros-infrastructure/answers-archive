    You can use [offline data](http://wiki.ros.org/slam_gmapping/Tutorials/MappingFromLoggedData#Downloading_a_test_bag) with  [rosbag](http://wiki.ros.org/rosbag)  to create the map with `gmapping` or `hector mapping` . Later you can load the map and can navigate using [amcl](http://wiki.ros.org/amcl).
