    Not sure if your walls are just boxes or not, but you should be able to apply material to visual of your walls

    e.g.

            <visual name="visual">
              <geometry>
                <box>
                  <size>5 1 0.2</size>
                </box>
              </geometry>
              <material>
                <ambient>0.2 0.2 0.2 1.0</ambient>
                <diffuse>.421 0.225 0.0 1.0</diffuse>
              </material>
            </visual>
