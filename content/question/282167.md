    Just about all of the `message_filter` filters only work with messages with a [Header](http://docs.ros.org/api/std_msgs/html/msg/Header.html) field, as the filters need timestamps to work with.

    `std_msgs/String` doesn't have a header, so that's most likely why you get the compilation error.

    Search for `header` on the [wiki/message_filters - Time Synchronizer](https://wiki.ros.org/message_filters#Time_Synchronizer) page you linked.
