  body_markdown: "It is mapping in between real size and pixelated size.\n\nResolution\
    \ parameter used to define your mapped area virtually. \nsuppose you has room\
    \ of size 10m x 10m and you have mapped that room with your robot. so now you\
    \ have occupancy grid of that room and want to use it in simulation. \n\nSuppose\
    \ your Occupancy grid image size is 1000 pixel by 1000 pixel.\n\nnow to adjust\
    \ your pixelated occupancy grid to original size, your resolution should be 10/1000\
    \ = 0.01\n\nIn conclusion, **resolution = Real size(in meters) / pixelated size**"
