I have a Raspberry Pi running ROS Groovy on a Lego NXT with a Webcam and Wifi.

Youtube: [Pictures](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VD3GDK7JBK4 "only Odometry") and [complete Visualization](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fAgHLZvX774 "with Ultrasonic Grid Cells").

The [nxt_ros](https://stack-nxt.foote-ros-pkg.googlecode.com/hg) package is using the old nxt-python 1.2.0 and the performance of the Motors is good enough for teleop, but not for controlling. Therefore, I rewrote my controller: [linefollower](https://github.com/ipa-fmw-ms/nxt_apps/blob/master/nxt_assisted_teleop/scripts/linefollower.py) in [NXC](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Not_eXactly_C), and I am starting the .rxe Machinecode with ROS directly on the NXT.

Then I found a [MotorControl for NXT](http://www.mindstorms.rwth-aachen.de/trac/wiki/MotorControl), and that it is already included in the new [nxt-python](http://code.google.com/p/nxt-python/) 2.2.2 version.

Before rewriting, the complete nxt_ros Package from nxt-python 1.2.0 to 2.2.2, does anyone knows, if this already exists somewhere?

Or maybe someone has experience with the new nxt-pythons 2.2.2 **Motor-sync**, and ideas for a new nxt base controller?
