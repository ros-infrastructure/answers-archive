    This doesn't appear to be a problem with keys or with unauthenticated packages, as the package just really isn't there.

    The Pointgrey camera driver builds are broken:

     - [ros-drivers/pointgrey_camera_driver#188](https://github.com/ros-drivers/pointgrey_camera_driver/issues/188)
     - [ros-drivers/pointgrey_camera_driver#113](https://github.com/ros-drivers/pointgrey_camera_driver/issues/113)

As it probably hasn't changed much over the years, that could be a short-term workaround.

    It would perhaps be nice to help @mikepurvis fix the underlying problem.
