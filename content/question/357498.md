    You may want to check [this](https://answers.ros.org/question/31779/how-to-use-a-message-that-i-made/) and [this](https://www.theconstructsim.com/subscribe-cpp-to-custom-msg-array-published-python/). The first link explain you how to implement a custom msg and the second how to use it in a subscriber. You forgot the namespace: `your_package_msg::leg_control_data_lcmt` and the proper definition of the argument in the callback:

        void legCallback (const your_package_msg::leg_control_data_lcmt::ConstPtr &msg) { ROS_INFO("test"); }

    Also you need to take into accout the proper cmake instructions to generate your messages bindings and package dependencies. Finally please remember to `include` your message bindings header in your code, like:

        #include <your_package_msg/leg_control_data_lcmt.h>

    Hope that solve your problem.
