  body_markdown: "My guess is, that you use openni_launch instead of openni_camera\
    \ and need to adapt the topics in the launch file:\n\n    <param name=\"config/topic_image_mono\"\
    \              value=\"/camera/rgb/image_color\"/>\n    <param name=\"config/topic_image_depth\"\
    \             value=\"/camera/depth_registered/image\"/>\n    <param name=\"config/topic_points\"\
    \                  value=\"/camera/depth_registered/points\"/> \n\nAlso you need\
    \ to enable registration of depth and color, e.g. via dynamic_reconfigure. [See\
    \ this answer](http://answers.ros.org/question/2905/activating-openni-depth-registration-by-default)\
    \ for how to enable registration by default."
