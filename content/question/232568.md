    You likely can setup a few state estimation packages out there for purely integrating IMU data. [robot_localization](http://wiki.ros.org/robot_localization) has the most complete documentation on how to perform a corresponding setup I believe.

    Note that unless you have a really expensive IMU (let's say upwards of 10000 US$), this kind purely IMU-based approach will quickly diverge from the true pose of the sensor and will be of limited usefulness.
