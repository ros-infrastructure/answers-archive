    Put line breaks `"\n"` (or `std::endl` https://stackoverflow.com/questions/213907/c-stdendl-vs-n) at the end of your std::cout calls.

    You probably also want to dereference msg otherwise you'll only get a point location in hexadecimal:

        std::cout << *msg << "\n";

    [ROS_INFO_STREAM](http://wiki.ros.org/roscpp/Overview/Logging) also would work and ends up in a log file among other features, and doesn't need a linebreak.

    Additionally, comparing a char* c_str() to a char* isn't what you want, just omit c_str() and do a string to string (automatically created from the char* "yes") comparison:
    https://stackoverflow.com/questions/23707392/value-equality-of-c-str-string-vs-c-str-c-str
