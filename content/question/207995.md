    In your `CMakeLists.txt` file, find the `include_directories` macro. Make sure it has `${Eigen_INCLUDE_DIRS}` in it, e.g.,

        include_directories(include ${catkin_INCLUDE_DIRS} ${Eigen_INCLUDE_DIRS})
