    \ a message or not.\n\n3_ In your main function, you should at most do the initial\
    \ setup, configure the subscription callback, and call ros::spin(). Every time\
    \ your message is received, the callback function will be processed, and that's\
    \ where you can publish odometry message and transform. \n\nTry refactoring the\
