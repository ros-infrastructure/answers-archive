    The list of variants for Electric are described in [REP 113](http://ros.org/reps/rep-0113.html), and those for Diamondback in [REP 108](http://ros.org/reps/rep-0108.html). I couldn't find a corresponding list for Fuerte.

    The robot-specific variants are listed only in REP 108; the variant you are looking for is called `pr2_desktop` (also in Electric).
