  body_markdown: "Start with the moveit [tutorials](http://docs.ros.org/kinetic/api/moveit_tutorials/html/doc/getting_started/getting_started.html).\n\
    Then get a bit familiar with moveit, especially follow the Moveit Setup Assistant,\
    \ Moveit Perception Pipeline Tutorial.\n\nFind a good and simple .urdf for the\
    \ kinect and then set it up according to this [video here](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kjgEdRJ72Tw).\
    \ Then you can follow the [planning scene tutorial](http://docs.ros.org/kinetic/api/moveit_tutorials/html/doc/planning_scene/planning_scene_tutorial.html)\
    \ to setup a collision checking node.\n\nIt will be a bit hard at first, but keep\
    \ trying. You will get it. \nThere probably is another way to do this, I am not\
    \ totally sure, but watch [this too](https://youtu.be/q3Dn5U3cSWk), it might help\
    \ you.\n\nGood luck!"
