    Are you asking how to do this for specific robot brands, or in general?

    I believe it would be important to make this clear (both in the question text as well as the title), as right now it looks like a generic question, but then you refer to a *very* specific Q&A about a single robot series from one specific OEM.

    Also:

    > Is there a roadmap for ROS to support collaborative features in the future?

    this is not really a question you can ask. "roadmap for ROS" does not exist, or at least not in the way I believe you are using that phrase here.

    There *is* a "roadmap for ROS" ([here](https://docs.ros.org/en/galactic/Roadmap.html)), but that would be ROS 2, and it deals with the development of "core ROS 2", not with specific drivers for specific brands of HW.

    There is a [safety working group](https://github.com/ros-safety/safety_working_group) though, for which this may be a topic. You could reach out to them (via ROS Discourse probably) and see whether they've discussed this.
