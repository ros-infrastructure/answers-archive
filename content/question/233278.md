    \ topic. Of course you can always replace both nodes with any other navigation\
    \ solution (e.g. move_base). \n\nHowever, if your robot platform is able to turn\
    \ in place, the Operator should make use of this motion. If your robot gets stuck\
    \ in corners, this is possible due to wrong parameter settings of robot_radius\
    \ in Operator and Navigator. The Navigator should have a slightly larger value\
    \ than the Operator."
