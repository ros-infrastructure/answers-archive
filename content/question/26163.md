    You can configure your camera sensor [to generate 16bit images](https://github.com/osrf/gazebo/blob/gazebo11/gazebo/rendering/Camera.cc#L924). Then you can just fork the [camera plugin](https://github.com/ros-simulation/gazebo_ros_pkgs/blob/kinetic-devel/gazebo_plugins/src/gazebo_ros_camera.cpp), and add a step for shifting the values [right before the image is copied](https://github.com/ros-simulation/gazebo_ros_pkgs/blob/kinetic-devel/gazebo_plugins/src/gazebo_ros_camera_utils.cpp#L627) from the internal buffer to the ROS msg.
