    Simbody is not yet fully supported in gazebo. I looked at the error message for SimbodyPhysics.cc:1154, and apparently meshes are not yet supported as collision shapes. I've created [gazebo issue #883](https://bitbucket.org/osrf/gazebo/issue/883/simbodycollision-doesnt-support-meshes-or) to track this. There's a list of [other open issues for simbody](https://bitbucket.org/osrf/gazebo/issues?component=physics%3A%3ASimbodyPhysics).

    As a workaround, can you use a combination of boxes, spheres, and cylinders to model the contact surfaces of your humanoid? You can probably start with the feet and hands.
