    You can add [rtabmap_ros/Info](http://wiki.ros.org/rtabmap_ros#Info_Display) display to RVIZ and look at `Loop closures` and `Proximity detections` values. They should increment when a localization occurs.

    Another way from terminal, check values of (not null if localization occurs):

        $ rostopic echo /rtabmap/info/loopClosureId
        $ rostopic echo /rtabmap/info/proximityDetectionId

    cheers,<br/>
    Mathieu
