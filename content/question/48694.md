    \ navigation (gmapping) require ROS running on a computer with some linux distro\
    \ that usually is onboard the robot with sensors attached like kinect or laser\
    \ scanners to create 2d/3d maps. \n\nGive Turtlebot a good look over to get some\
    \ ideas and capabilities for you project. There is also the Lego NXT if you do\
    \ not want to invest too much in hardware, it may be more similar to what you\
    \ have in mind.  \n\n http://www.ros.org/wiki/nxt"
