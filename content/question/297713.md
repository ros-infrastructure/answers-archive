    I'll answer your question to best of my ability.

    - From this [answer on superuser](https://superuser.com/a/949522), you should stick with `localhost`:

    > - `127.0.0.1` is the loopback address (also known as localhost).
    > - `0.0.0.0` is a non-routable meta-address used to designate an invalid, unknown or non applicable target (a no particular address placeholder).

    - That [tutorial that you linked to](http://emanual.robotis.com/docs/en/platform/turtlebot3/pc_setup/#network-configuration) is incorrect. `ROS_HOSTNAME` is for hostnames such as `localhost`, not IP addresses as that's what ROS_IP is for.

    - You can [use either `ROS_IP` or `ROS_HOSTNAME`](https://wiki.ros.org/ROS/EnvironmentVariables#ROS_IP.2BAC8-ROS_HOSTNAME). If you have both set, `ROS_HOSTNAME` will take precedence.

    As for the bottom line, it's a matter of preference as to which to use. I prefer `ROS_HOSTNAME` because I can use it in launch files in the `<machine>` tag and it's easier to remember hostnames vs. IP addresses.
