    https://bitbucket.org/theconstructcor...

    <sensor name="camera" type="depth">
        <update_rate>20</update_rate>
        <camera>
          <horizontal_fov>1.047198</horizontal_fov>
          <image>
            <width>640</width>
            <height>480</height>
            <format>R8G8B8</format>
          </image>
          <clip>
            <near>0.05</near>
            <far>3</far>
          </clip>
        </camera>
        <plugin name="camera_plugin" filename="libgazebo_ros_openni_kinect.so">
          <baseline>0.2</baseline>
          <alwaysOn>true</alwaysOn>
          <!-- Keep this zero, update_rate in the parent <sensor> tag
            will control the frame rate. -->
          <updateRate>0.0</updateRate>
          <cameraName>camera_ir</cameraName>
          <imageTopicName>/camera/color/image_raw</imageTopicName>
          <cameraInfoTopicName>/camera/color/camera_info</cameraInfoTopicName>
          <depthImageTopicName>/camera/depth/image_raw</depthImageTopicName>
          <depthImageCameraInfoTopicName>/camera/depth/camera_info</depthImageCameraInfoTopicName>

          <pointCloudCutoffMax>3.0</pointCloudCutoffMax>
          <distortionK1>0</distortionK1>
          <distortionK2>0</distortionK2>

          <CxPrime>0</CxPrime>
          <Cx>0</Cx>
          <Cy>0</Cy>
          <focalLength>0</focalLength>
          <hackBaseline>0</hackBaseline>
        </plugin>
      </sensor>

I put these under the down_camera plugins, however, when I rostopic list, all the topics I specified in the camera_plugin did not show up.

Any tips on how to solve this issue? Thank you.

update: I noticed that, when I removed this line from start_gaserver:

export GAZEBO_PLUGIN_PATH=$pack_path/plugins:$GAZEBO_PLUGIN_PATH

Then the Kinect camera can publish topics, but the drone topics are missing. On contrast, when I include this line, I got the drone topics but not the Kinect topics.
