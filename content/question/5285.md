  body_markdown: "In general have a look at the sdf specification (for example: [sdf\
    \ 1.4](http://gazebosim.org/sdf/1.4.html) )\nIn general you have to rewrite all\
    \ `<ode>` blocks by adding a corresponding `<bullet>` block.\nI don't think that\
    \ having both (ode and bullet blocks will make problem but i have never tried\
    \ )\n\nFor Example your first ode tag\n\n                <ode>\n             \
    \    <mu> 0.1 </mu>\n                </ode>\n\nwill change to \n\n           \
    \     <bullet>\n                 <friction> 0.1 </friction>\n                </bullet>\n\
    \nBut some of your settings might not be available with bullet. For Example there\
    \ seems to be no `joint/physics/bullet` tag in SDF...\n\nWhen starting gazebo\
    \ don't forget to specify bullet as pysic engine\n\n   `gazebo -e bullet` \n\n\
    \n"
