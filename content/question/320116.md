    > Is there any way to convert the `sensor_msgs/Image` data into `sensor_msgs/CompressedImage` data?

    yes: the `republish` node of the [image_transport](http://wiki.ros.org/image_transport#Nodes) package can convert between uncompressed and compressed images (between any registered transports actually).

    Note: this will incur overhead, as it subscribes, compressed and then publishes messages.
