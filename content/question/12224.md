    After a brief play with urdf file i fixed it. I inverted the sign of base_plate origin's Y co-ordinate.

    Error:

        <inertial>
              <origin
                xyz="-0.3317 -0.71959 -0.39019"
                rpy="0 0 0" />

    Solution:

         <inertial>
              <origin
                xyz="-0.3317 0.71959 -0.39019"
                rpy="0 0 0" />
