    <s>Looks like from the error message you are looking at installation instruction for Ubuntu, not [the one for Mac OSX](http://wiki.ros.org/hydro/Installation/OSX/Homebrew/Source).</s>

    UPDATE: That seems Ubuntu problem that doesn't necessarilly pertain to ROS. Referring to e.g. [this QA](http://askubuntu.com/questions/41605/trouble-downloading-packages-list-due-to-a-hash-sum-mismatch-error) and looking specifically at your errors, you may want to do:

        rm /var/lib/apt/lists/extras.ubuntu.com_ubuntu_dists_precise_main_source_Sources
        rm /var/lib/apt/lists/extras.ubuntu.com_ubuntu_dists_precise_main_binary-i386_Packages
        rm /var/lib/apt/lists/packages.ros.org_ros_ubuntu_dists_precise_main_binary-i386_Packages
        apt-get update

    And try installation again.
