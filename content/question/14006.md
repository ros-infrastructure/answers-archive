  body_markdown: "You can't yet include a model inside another one, but you can copy\
    \ its links and joints directly under the `<actor>` tag. \n\nTake the box example\
    \ world in the tutorial for example and add a camera sensor to the box link:\n\
    \n        <link name=\"box_link\">\n          ...\n          <sensor name=\"camera\"\
    \ type=\"camera\">\n            <camera>\n            </camera>\n            <visualize>true</visualize>\n\
    \          </sensor>\n        </link>\n\nAnd you get a camera that moves with\
    \ the box, see a video [here](https://youtu.be/1jTWfeZ1lLc).\n\n"
