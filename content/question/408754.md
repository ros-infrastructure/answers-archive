    I had the same problem. It was related with custom configuratoin of the cyclonedds file.
    I needed to add a larger number of participants in the cyclonedds configuration file. Have a look to the following and the MaxAutoParticipantIndex parameter:

        <CycloneDDS>
          <Domain>
            <General>
              <AllowMulticast>false</AllowMulticast>
            </General>
            <Discovery>
              <ParticipantIndex>auto</ParticipantIndex>
              <MaxAutoParticipantIndex>1000</MaxAutoParticipantIndex>
            </Discovery>
         </Domain>
        </CycloneDDS>
