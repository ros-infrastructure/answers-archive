  body_markdown: "I had the same problem, one of my friends told me I was missing\
    \ some linker dependencies to ros::serialization::throwStreamOverrun().\n\nAnd\
    \ also that I needed to add the package that contains ros::serialization to the\
    \ build_dependencies and the run_dependencies in the package.xml and also to the\
    \ CMakeLists.txt. \n\nIn the CMake-file the build_dependencies are in the line\
    \ that starts with\nfind_package(catkin REQUIRED COMPONENTS\nand the run_dependencies\
    \ usually are in the \ncatkin_package( ...\nblock.\nAlso, in the line\ntarget_link_libraries(your_executable\
    \ ${catkin_LIBRARIES})\nthat belongs to your executable you might need to add\
    \ the ${catkin_LIBRARIES} if it is not already there.\n\nFor me that worked and\
    \ hope for you also."
