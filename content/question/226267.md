  body_markdown: " you can try to set the parameter `inf_is_valid: false` . in this\
    \ way the local costmap (or global) will not treat scans with \"inf\" values.\
    \ \n\nthis is a quick fix, and you may have problems with not clearing the costmaps\
    \ in front of the robot if your sensor outputs \"inf\" for scans farther than\
    \ `max_range`, for example. in that case you might want to use a filter to correct\
    \ that kind of measurements from your data as in [here](http://answers.ros.org/question/30014/costmap-clearing-of-obstacles/?answer=30045#post-id-30045)."
