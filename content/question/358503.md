    \  ---\n    StringArray[] topics\n\n  List item\n\n * Or just implement a custom\
    \ msg Multiarray like [Float32Multiarray](http://docs.ros.org/api/std_msgs/html/msg/Float32MultiArray.html)\
    \ but `StringMultiarray` that let you declare a multi dimensional variable. It\
