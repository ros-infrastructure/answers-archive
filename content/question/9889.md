    Hi,
    Please take a look at the service call [gazebo/set_model_configuration](http://ros.org/wiki/gazebo) in gazebo wiki to set pose of the robot.  The strategy I've tested here is:

     - start simulation paused.
     - call gazebo/set_model_configuration with list of joint angles
     - [if holding position, trigger controller reset to set new goals]
     - unpause simulation

    Hope this helps.

    John
