    .msg files are not importable by python. Python modules will be generated when you build your package (with catkin or rosbuild), and placed in your package's `src/PKG_NAME/msg` directory.     Turns out the problem was with calling `setup.py` myself. The [documentation](http://docs.ros.org/hydro/api/catkin/html/user_guide/setup_dot_py.html) explains issues with calling `setup.py` manually screws things up.
