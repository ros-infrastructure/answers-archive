  body_markdown: "As i mentioned above, one solution could be to use lower and upper\
    \ limits inside your urdf file.\n\n\t<joint name=\"articulated\" type=\"revolute\"\
    >\n\t\t<parent link=\"link1_rot\"/>\n\t\t<child link=\"link2_lin\"/>\n\t\t<axis\
    \ xyz=\"0 1 0\"/>\n\t\t<origin xyz =\"0 0 0.05\" rpy=\"0 0 0\"/>\n\t\t<limit effort=\"\
    1000.0\" lower=\"-1.5\" upper=\"1.5\" velocity=\"0.5\"/>\n\t</joint>\n\nThe joint\
    \ \"articulated\", which connects link1_rot and link2_lin can now spin around\
    \ the y-axis from -90 to +90 degrees."
