    I believe I have found my answer. In the [sensor_msgs/LaserScan Message](http://docs.ros.org/api/sensor_msgs/html/msg/LaserScan.html), there are parameters range_min and range_max. Some of my laser scan ranges were coming in under the range_min value (both 0.0 and others) and those are causing the outlier points. I don't know why this is happening, but it can be fixed either by changing the data to conform to the specified max and mins, or by changing the max and mins to conform to the data.
