  body_markdown: "It depends on what you pass in for the time to lookup. A good reference\
    \ is [here](http://wiki.ros.org/tf2/Tutorials/tf2%20and%20time%20%28C%2B%2B%29)\n\
    \nThus, it depends on the value supply to the time parameter. \n\nhttps://github.com/ros/geometry2/blob/c92cd9a9073c255388599495d2f86799ecafa1bb/tf2/include/tf2/buffer_core.h#L119\n\
    \nIf you want the latest available, supply 0. This will be slightly in the past\
    \ from your current ROS wall time. \n\nIf you want the transform at now time,\
    \ supply the current wall time, but you need to supply a timeout, since that transform\
    \ is in the future of where the current buffer is. If you don't then you will\
    \ get an ExtrapolationException. \n\nFinally, if you want a timestamp at some\
    \ arbitrary time in the past, just supply that to the call. "
