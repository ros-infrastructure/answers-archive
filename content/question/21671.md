    You can send a Gazebo message to the "/gazebo/default/model/modify" topic. See the message definition [here](https://bitbucket.org/osrf/gazebo/src/582827025ea59c832cc50e33d64a07ae13cad76a/gazebo/msgs/model.proto?at=default&fileviewer=file-view-default).
