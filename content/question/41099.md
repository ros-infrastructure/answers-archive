    To answer your question directly, yes, the different kinect drivers have different ROS APIs (meaning the topics they publish are different). You can take a look at the wiki pages for the different drivers ([kinect_camera](http://www.ros.org/wiki/kinect_camera) which uses libfreenect, and [openni_camera](http://www.ros.org/wiki/openni_camera), which uses the openni drivers) to compare.
