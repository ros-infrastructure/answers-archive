    There could be a way achieve this kind of control over which faces the texture is displayed on from an Ogre script, but I haven't looked into it.

    * One common approach I see is to create a COLLADA model of a box with proper UV mapping. See [this tutorial](https://all3dp.com/2/blender-how-to-add-a-texture/) for example.

    * Another approach is to create a box with 6 other thin boxes on its sides and give those boxes colors / textures. That's how I did the Rubik's cube stickers [here](https://twitter.com/chapulinaBR/status/762418303520256000).
