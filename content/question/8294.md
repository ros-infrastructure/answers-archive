    It [seems](https://wps3dprinter.wordpress.com/designing/stl-units/) that there isn't a way to specify units in STL files. So whatever unit you use will be treated as meters by Gazebo. That's why your `400 mm` cube came out as a `400 m` cube.

    Your solution seems to be the easiest one, to specify a scaling factor for the mesh on [SDF](http://sdformat.org/spec?ver=1.5&elem=geometry#mesh_scale), from `mm` to `m` for example:

        <mesh>
          <uri>model://model_name/meshes/your_mesh.stl</uri>
          <scale>0.001 0.001 0.001</scale>
        </mesh>

    An alternative would be to rescale the mesh with an external editor.
