  body_markdown: "This behaviour is perfectly normal according to the axis convention\
    \ specified in [REP 103](https://www.ros.org/reps/rep-0103.html).<br/>\nThe correction\
    \ for camera optical transforms is `-90\xB0, 0\xB0, -90\xB0` in the roll, pitch\
    \ and yaw rotations specified in the [Suffix Frames](https://www.ros.org/reps/rep-0103.html#suffix-frames)\
    \ section.\n\n * First, publish a transform from `/world` to your Kinect ( say\
    \ `/camera_link`):\n\n        <!-- publish tf from world to the kinect_camera\
    \ (camera_link) -->\n        <node name=\"kinect_static_transform_publisher\"\
    \ pkg=\"tf\" type=\"static_transform_publisher\"\n          args=\"1.274070 -0.011558\
    \ 1.961223 -3.121294 0.611102 0.021866 world camera_link 100\" />\n\n * Finally,\
    \ publish the correct optical transform from `/camera_link` to `camera_rgb_optical_frame`:\n\
    \n        <!-- publish tf from camera_link to camera_rgb_optical_frame -->\n \
    \       <node name=\"kinect_optical_transform\" pkg=\"tf\" type=\"static_transform_publisher\"\
    \n          args=\"0 0 0 -1.5707 0 -1.5707 camera_link camera_rgb_optical_frame\
    \ 100\" />\n          \n"
