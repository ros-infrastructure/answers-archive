    The full definition of the [CameraInfo](http://ros.org/doc/api/sensor_msgs/html/msg/CameraInfo.html) message has a pretty good description of what each term means.

    For a monocular camera, P and K will be the same. For a stereo pair:

    > [Tx Ty 0]' is related to the position
    > of the optical center of the second
    > camera in the first camera's frame.
