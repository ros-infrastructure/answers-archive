Migrated from [ROS' community's website](http://goo.gl/j7srJ).

In [the QA thread "SDF vs URDF what should one use?"](http://goo.gl/GMujA) (on answers.gazebosim.org), `URDF` is not recommended on `gazebo` in favor of `SDF`. But from [the answer in this thread](http://answers.ros.org/question/41294/gazebo-with-controller/?answer=41770#post-id-41770), sounds like URDF will still be supported on `gazebo`. Can we keep the tutorial using `URDF`?
