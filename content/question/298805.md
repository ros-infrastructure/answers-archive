    \nI understand this is a common phrase when talking about suitability of software\

    \ I consider \"toy projects\" could be your final project in your MSc, a very\
    \ complex project for me could be a \"toy project\" for the next guy, and so on.\n\
    \nThe suitability of a particular piece of software will always have to be determined\
    \ relative to a set of requirements and constraints, and as such, any of the client\
    \ libraries listed on that page could be more than sufficient in certain situations.\n\
    \nIf all I need is to get a bare message from a Windows UI across to a ROS application\
    \ hosted on an Ubuntu machine and I happen to work in R, chances are that `rosR`\
    \ provides sufficient functionality for that. The fact that it may not have support\
    \ for actions or dynamic reconfigure or other more 'advanced' functionality is\
    \ completely unimportant as long as it covers my primary requirement(s)."
