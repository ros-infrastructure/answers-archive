    Just make sure that the IMU produces data in ENU frame. Most IMUs produce NED-frame data by default, which will not work with `r_l`. I may be incorrect, but looking at the VN-100 driver [source](https://github.com/KumarRobotics/imu_vn_100/blob/master/src/imu_vn_100.cpp#L328), it appears to be copying data straight from the IMU, which indicates to me that it may be in the wrong frame. Please correct me if you find that's not accurate.
