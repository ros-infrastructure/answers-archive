  body_markdown: "The naming appears to be a bit confusing indeed. There are different\
    \ representations of a robot\xB4s state. The `state_publisher` in the tutorial\
    \ publishes the [JointState](http://docs.ros.org/api/sensor_msgs/html/msg/JointState.html)\
    \ of the robot, while the `robot_state_publisher` publishes full 6DOF [tf](http://wiki.ros.org/tf)\
    \ data for all links of the robot, essentially fusing the static information about\
    \ the robot from the URDF model and the dynamically changing JointState information."
