    Hi,

    you should [create xacro](https://wiki.ros.org/urdf/Tutorials/Using%20Xacro%20to%20Clean%20Up%20a%20URDF%20File) script for your robot or setup. Than you can easily get your TF frames. Other possibility is to use [MarkerArray](https://wiki.ros.org/rviz/DisplayTypes/Marker) where you can set position of each sensor in reference to one coordinate system.
