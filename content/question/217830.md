    General comment: the [ROS-Industrial](http://ros-i.org) 'branch' of ROS is where we focus on using ROS with industrial robots. You might want to get in touch with us on the ROS-Industrial [mailing list](https://groups.google.com/forum/#!forum/swri-ros-pkg-dev).

    > Initially I was looking for an ethernet connection but I couldn't find one on the panel [..]

    The [fanuc_driver](http://wiki.ros.org/fanuc_driver?distro=hydro) page lists the requirements on both the software and the hardware of your controller: you must have ethernet support, as well as options R632 (KAREL) and R648 (User Socket Messaging). Afaik, all R-30iB controllers come with ethernet networking installed, I'm not sure where the port(s) is (are) located inside the controller itself though.

    As for the other options: please make sure you have them (they are not installed by default), otherwise the `fanuc_driver` package will not be compatible.

    ---

    Edit: according to the *R-30iB Controller Maintenance Manual* (B-83195EN/03), Section 4.10.2.1 (Connection to Ethernet), the port is labelled CD38{A,B,C}. Pages 223 and 224 should show you where it is located.
