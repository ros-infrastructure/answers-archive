  body_markdown: "The max_cache_time parameterizes how long of a history to store\
    \ for each transform. In general it will determine how much memory is used storing\
    \ the older data. A longer cache will have low overhead as long as you don't query\
    \ far into the past very often. \n\nThe length of the cache determines how old\
    \ a timestamp you can query, once the cache has been filled. \n\nIt will not have\
    \ an effect on waitForTransform as waitForTransform only goes forward not backwards.\
    \ (It assumes new data will be newer.)"
