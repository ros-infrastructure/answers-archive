  body_markdown: "You could use [eval](http://wiki.ros.org/roslaunch/XML) (as of Kinetic)\
    \ to create a recursive launchfile:\n\n    <launch> \n    \n       <!-- start\
    \ node -->\n       <arg name=\"nr\" default=\"1\"/> \n       <group ns=\"$(eval\
    \ 'turtle' + str(arg('nr')))\">\n          <node name=\"turtle\" pkg=\"turtlesim\"\
    \ type=\"turtlesim_node\"/>\n       </group>\n    \n       <!-- recursively start\
    \ new node -->\n       <include file=\"$(find your_package)/launch/this_launch_file.launch\"\
    \ if=\"$(eval arg('nr') - 1 > 0)\">\n           <arg name=\"nr\" value=\"$(eval\
    \ arg('nr') - 1)\"/>\n       </include>\n    \n    </launch>\n\nAs of Lunar you\
    \ could also use $(dirname) instead of $(find your_package)/launch."
