    You can use [rosserial_mbed](http://wiki.ros.org/rosserial_mbed). It is a client library similar to rosserial_arduino that will help you to easily interface your hardware with mbed compatible micro-controllers. You can find a tutorial [here](http://wiki.ros.org/rosserial_mbed/Tutorials).

    If you are interested in development boards like Arduino, you can use the [ST Nucleo](http://www.st.com/en/evaluation-tools/stm32-mcu-nucleo.html?querycriteria=productId=LN1847) development boards that are powerful, cheap compared to Arduino and with a consequent community.

    It seems that most of the solutions are based on ARM. If you  want to use a PIC or other architectures, you need to implement the [rosserial](http://wiki.ros.org/rosserial) protocol on the micro-controller.
