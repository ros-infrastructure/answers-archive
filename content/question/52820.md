    The [wiki page of robot_pose_ekf](http://www.ros.org/wiki/robot_pose_ekf) says that all poses are *robot poses*. So every source of information should publish pose information of the robot (and not the sensor). [REP 105](http://www.ros.org/reps/rep-0105.html) could help you to understand odometry frames. If your odometry source publishes sensor movements you will have to transform this to robot movements.

    Have a look at [viso2_ros::OdometerBase::integrateAndPublish()](http://ros.org/doc/fuerte/api/viso2_ros/html/odometer__base_8h_source.html#l00099) for an example of how to transform sensor pose information to robot pose information using tf.
