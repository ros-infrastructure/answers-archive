    One of publisher/subscribers is looking at a `sensor_msgs/Image`, the other at a `sensor_msgs/CompressedImage`. Make sure the type of message you are subscribing to matches what you declare in your code. There is also a `sensor_msgs/Image` topic in `raspicam_node` under the `/raspicam_node/image` topic... (you need to set `enable_raw` to true) See the [readmed](https://github.com/UbiquityRobotics/raspicam_node)
