I'm a bit confused about this. The [Eigen wiki page](http://www.ros.org/wiki/eigen) indicates that, for Fuerte, a package's manifest.xml needs to have

      <depend package="common_rosdeps" />
      <rosdep name="eigen" />

.. but the [common_rosdeps wiki page](http://www.ros.org/wiki/common_rosdeps) says that this "stack/package" (which is it?) is "deprecated" and "no longer necessary".

I've tried commenting out both lines above and my code still seems to build happily so maybe this only matters in a rosinstall situation (which I tend not to do). But at any rate the information on the Eigen wiki page is still a bit confusing since it recommends depending on a deprecated package.
