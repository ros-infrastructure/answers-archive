  body_markdown: "It looks like your imu is adding some noise into the system. In\
    \ my experience, unless conditions are perfectly correct, the imu can potentially\
    \ degrade the quality of your estimate.  That being said:\n\n1) If you are only\
    \ traveling in 2D, set `two_d_mode` to true. \n\n2) Your covariances are so small\
    \ right now (from what I see) that you are essentially forcing the filter to integrate\
    \ every measurement directly. If you can increase the size of the measurement\
    \ covariances I think you will get a more smooth behavior \n\n3) It looks like\
    \ your IMU does not even generate linear velocity, but you specify in `<rosparam\
    \ param=\"imu0_config\">` that you do. You should switch this to accept the angular\
    \ velocity, which it looks like your IMU does generate. \n\n4) I assume your odom\
    \ message usually has a linear velocity and it just is empty here because the\
    \ robot was stationary when you took that screenshot? \n\n 5) Make sure you IMU\
    \ is configured correctly and has the right base_link tf. It seems that it introduces\
    \ a very consistent bias, which is not the behavior I would expect for a properly\
    \ configured IMU adding random noise.\n\n 6) Make you sensor timeout larger, maybe\
    \ 0.5 seconds, I don't think your filter can/should be running that fast\n\nI\
    \ hope someone else can answer your question directly, but these are my thoughts\
    \ with respect to my personal experience which I think may be of some help."
