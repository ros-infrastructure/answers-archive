  body_markdown: "The new, with little spread should be better.\n\nWhat is going on\
    \ with the old image you shown is that the algorithms is not getting a good match\
    \ of the laser sensed data correlated with the predicted state of your robot.\
    \ \n\nIn simple words, what the filter does is to \"spread\" particles around\
    \ in an attempt to get a better match from any of these random poses.\nWhen you\
    \ are getting a good correlation between the sensed data and and estimated position,\
    \ the \"spread\" of particles is smaller, because the algorithm has a high confidence\
    \ that the estimated pose is the correct one. In the other case, it needs to \"\
    spread\" estimations farther. \n\nCheck this explanation of the [monte carlo localization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monte_Carlo_localization).\n\
    \nEDIT1:\n\nI think you can say they are better, but you have to also take into\
    \ account its stability and test it in different scenarios. If you have access\
    \ of the ground truth position of your robot you can also measure the error in\
    \ your localization. In the end, having a smaller \"spread\" of particles is an\
    \ indication of good convergence, but you may have fallen into a local minima,\
    \ so you cannot used that criteria alone to assess the quality of your filter\
    \ results."
