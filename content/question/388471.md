    \ **three special conditions will be well-defined**. These readings shall be:\n\
    > \n>  1. detection too close to determine true distance but not cause an erroneous\
    \ measurement\n>  1. an erroneous measurement that has no useful physical quantification;\
    \ and\n>  1. no information within the useful range of the sensor but likely no\
    \ object within range.\n> \n> **Detections that are too close to the sensor to\
