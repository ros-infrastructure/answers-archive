    Hello, there are a few resources that can be useful for you:

    - `gazebo_ros_pkg` contains a set of ROS packages to communicate Gazebo and your ROS code. I recommend to read the [wiki](http://wiki.ros.org/gazebo_ros_pkgs) to get a high level overview.
    - More specifically, take a look at the "[Using a URDF in Gazebo](http://gazebosim.org/tutorials?tut=ros_urdf&cat=connect_ros)" and [the follow-up tutorial](http://gazebosim.org/tutorials?tut=ros_gzplugins), where you can learn how to load a plugin (`libgazebo_ros_camera.so`) that will publish images via a ROS topic and how to visualize them in `rviz`.
    - Additionally, you can take a look at the [Gazebo intermediate guided tutorials](http://gazebosim.org/tutorials?cat=guided_i&tut=guided_i1). It's composed by six tutorials where the process of customizing a model in Gazebo is explained. The [tutorial number #6](http://gazebosim.org/tutorials?cat=guided_i&tut=guided_i6) contains instructions for receiving ROS messages and modify your simulation (in this case, spinning a Velodyne sensor).
