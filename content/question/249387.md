  body_markdown: "I believe the message you see indicates either (as it states) the\
    \ robot is not powered on meaning you should try pushing the large \"Clean\" button,\
    \ or the baud rate is not correct.  \n\nSee [Create 2 OI Spec](https://cdn-shop.adafruit.com/datasheets/create_2_Open_Interface_Spec.pdf)\
    \ for information regarding baud. It could be 115200 (I believe the driver's default)\
    \ or 19200. You can try a different rate by adding a parameter to the launch file\
    \ `ca_driver/launch/create_2.launch`:\n\n`<param name=\"baud\" value=\"19200\"\
    \ />`\n\nGood luck."
