    Chances are that the gazebo executable is not in your search path.

    If you installed Gazebo from source, then `gazebo` should be in `/usr/bin` or `/usr/local/bin`. If `gazebo` is not in either of those two places, then check your gazebo build directory and make sure you have run `sudo make install`.
