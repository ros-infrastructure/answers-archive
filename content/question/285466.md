    \ since you are a beginner I would like to give you a couple of pieces of advice\
    \ so that you avoid mistakes like this in the future.\n\n - As stated in the [tutorial](http://wiki.ros.org/ROS/Tutorials/InstallingandConfiguringROSEnvironment#Managing_Your_Environment)\
    \ you should source your `setup.bash` because it gives you access to ROS commands\
    \ (as stated in the tutorial). Also, **you should do that in every new terminal\
    \ you open**, unless you put the command in `.bashrc`. You can find more about\
    \ it [here](https://answers.ros.org/question/211008/where-is-my-bashrc-file-in-ros/)\
    \ and more about source command [here](https://answers.ros.org/question/188309/what-does-source-command-actually-do/).\n\
    \ - Furthermore, I think it is a good practice to have a ROS folder in your home\
    \ folder and, inside it, catkin workspaces you are working on. That way, you would\
    \ have all your catkin workspaces in ROS folder and in every workspace you would\
    \ have some ROS packages related to that workspace.\n\nIf you have any further\
    \ question, feel free to ask."
