  body_markdown: "Maybe you can get your anwser by reading through the  [paper](https://april.eecs.umich.edu/media/pdfs/olson2011tags.pdf),\
    \ where the implementation is explained. Maybe one explanation is, that during\
    \ the quad detection process only close enough lines are considered.\n\n> Robustness\
    \ to occlusions and\n> segmentation errors is handled by\n> adjusting the \u201C\
    close enough\u201D\n> threshold: by making the threshold\n> large, significant\
    \ gaps around the\n> edges can be handled. Our threshold\n> for \u201Cclose enough\u201D\
    \ is twice the length\n> of the line plus five additional\n> pixels. This is a\
    \ large threshold\n> which leads to a low false negative\n> rate, but also results\
    \ in a high false\n> positive rate.\n\nThat would explain why reducing the resolution\
    \ helps detecting the closer tag."
