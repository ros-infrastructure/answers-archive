    I guess you set camera_link as reference for camera plugin. The problem is that camera is attached to the origin of camera_link, which is in the body of the box.
    Solution A: add another joint and virtual link (without view tag) to move camera to the surface of the box.
    Solution B: make camera_link virtual/transparent

      <joint name="camera_joint" type="fixed">

      <!-- Camera -->
      <link name="camera_link">

        <inertial>
