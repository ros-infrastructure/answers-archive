    I seem to remember that heightmap support was broken in electric but has been re-added in recent gazebo (1.0+) versions. There also is a [tutorial](http://gazebosim.org/wiki/Tutorials/beginner/heightmap) on using height maps on the new gazebo wiki, so that might be worth a try.

    An alternative approach I used in our [hector_gazebo_worlds](http://www.ros.org/wiki/hector_gazebo_worlds) package is the use of a COLLADA terrain mesh generated using blender. This works both in electric and fuerte.
