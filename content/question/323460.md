    \ as input not the subscriber.\n\nYour callback should be something like: (where\
    \ `data` is your image message)\n\n      def callback(self,data):\n          cv_image\
    \ = self.bridge.imgmsg_to_cv2(data, \"bgr8\")\n\n\nYou may add `try and except`\
    \ to raise any related exceptions. Review the [cvbridge tutorial](http://wiki.ros.org/cv_bridge/Tutorials/ConvertingBetweenROSImagesAndOpenCVImagesPython)\
