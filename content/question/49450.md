  body_markdown: |2-
     - AMCL is a localization method that
       localizes your robot in a previously
       known map.
     - Gmapping is a SLAM (simultaneous
       localization and mapping) method that
       simultaneously generates a map and
       localizes your robot in it.

    In a nutshell (for details on the difficulties of the SLAM problem see for example [this overview paper](http://www-personal.acfr.usyd.edu.au/tbailey/papers/slamtute1.pdf)), SLAM can be more brittle than pure localization as the map used for localization is not static.
