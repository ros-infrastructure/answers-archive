    In 2020 IINM, [NVIDIA officially made ROS bridge for Isaac SDK](https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/building-collaborative-robotics-using-ros-and-isaac-sdk/).

    I have yet used it so I can't tell anything. I couldn't find their release policy though, so far their release cycle doesn't seem rapid (release once or twice a year?). As of 2021/03/02 the latest version is still [2020.02 and runs on Ubuntu 18.04](https://docs.nvidia.com/isaac/isaac/packages/ros_bridge/doc/ros_bridge.html) (I'm not necessarily saying no frequent release is bad. It's just something to keep in mind).
