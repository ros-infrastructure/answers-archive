  body_markdown: "The TurtleBot publishes two IMU data streams, imu/data and imu/raw.\
    \  Where the raw one is unfiltered and the imu/data is bias compensated.  This\
    \ is allows for better performance with the cheap IMU used on the TurtleBot. \
    \ \n\nThe calibration uses the imu/raw and estimates the bias.  You will need\
    \ to remap the input to the calibration to match the output of your 3dmgx.  "
