    You don't need to calculate anything. If you specify the wrong focal length and spawn your model, you'll get a [warning](https://github.com/ros-simulation/gazebo_ros_pkgs/blob/kinetic-devel/gazebo_plugins/src/gazebo_ros_camera_utils.cpp#L517) with the expected focal length for the given camera. Then you can just copy that value in your urdf.
