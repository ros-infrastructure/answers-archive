  body_markdown: "How are you polling? Are you spanning across multiple transforms\
    \ which are published at a different rate? There's no limit on how fast you can\
    \ poll tf until you run out of processor time.\n\nIf the other transform has not\
    \ arrived and you're asking for the latest information it will jump ahead with\
    \ the slowest transform published in the chain connecting the requested transforms.\
    \ You can get much more accurate time readings by querying for a time history\
    \ from the buffer into the past instead of recording the latest value as it comes\
    \ in. "
