    > Is this to be expected?

    Yes, and it is also not a limit to array/list/sequence sizes in the message format (as your question seems to imply), but a usability feature.

    The ROS 2 `rostopic` CLI tool by default will not print full length arrays for lists/arrays/sequences longer than [`128` elements](https://github.com/ros2/ros2cli/blob/2f9e81d86b06e1b5f397bdfdbd33493cbc5f27c1/ros2topic/ros2topic/verb/echo.py#L30) to prevent "runaway" terminals (where you are waiting 5 minutes for `rostopic` to quit after having `rostopic echo`d a topic carrying `PointCloud` msgs fi).

    You can disable/change this behaviour in two ways:

     1. provide an alternative value with the `--truncate-length` option ([here](https://github.com/ros2/ros2cli/blob/2f9e81d86b06e1b5f397bdfdbd33493cbc5f27c1/ros2topic/ros2topic/verb/echo.py#L64-L67))
     1. add `--full-length` to your `echo` invocation ([here](https://github.com/ros2/ros2cli/blob/2f9e81d86b06e1b5f397bdfdbd33493cbc5f27c1/ros2topic/ros2topic/verb/echo.py#L59-L63))

    Note that this is similar to `rostopic echo`'s `--noarr` in ROS 1 (although that didn't allow you to specify a limit to the nr of elements it did print).
