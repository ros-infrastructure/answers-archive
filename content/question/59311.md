  body_markdown: "AMCL does not try to model dynamic obstacles. However, probabilistic\
    \ methods like AMCL are generally fairly robust to things like dynamic obstacles,\
    \ as long as they don't comprise most of your sensor data. For instance, a person\
    \ walking by the robot while it navigates down a hallway is unlikely to cause\
    \ to lose localization -- whereas several people circling around the robot and\
    \ blocking out the laser's view of the walls around it may very well cause it\
    \ to lose localization. "
