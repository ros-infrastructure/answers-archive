    > want to know if MoveIt sets the values
    > for the joint from 0-360 deg or from
    > -180 to +180. (or +/-radians)

    The range is `[-pi, pi]`. See:

     - [#q268375](https://answers.ros.org/question/268375/moveit-joint-with-more-than-2pi-rotation/)'s accepted answer
     - [constructor](http://docs.ros.org/en/indigo/api/moveit_core/html/revolute__joint__model_8cpp_source.html#l00044) of `RevoluteJointModel()`:

    >     variable_bounds_[0].min_position_ = -boost::math::constants::pi<double>();
    >     variable_bounds_[0].max_position_ = boost::math::constants::pi<double>();
