    I believe with the new [grinch kernel](https://devtalk.nvidia.com/default/topic/795734/) for L4T 21.2.1 you shouldnt have a problem with usb3.0. Here is a [link](http://www.e-consystems.com/USB-3-Camera-on-JetsonTK1-kit.asp) where See3Cam (USB3.0) had some successes with the Jetson over USB 3.0, however in the link they are instructing to use L4T 19.3 which I read for most people did not work well. Also take a look on the elinux page for jetson for [known usb 3.0 cameras](http://elinux.org/Jetson/Cameras#USB_3.0_webcams_known_to_be_working) to work. Whether you know or not, make sure you turn off the USB autosuspend feature (you dont have to worry about this if you use the grinch kernel for r21.2.1 because its disabled by default).
