  body_markdown: "This means you probably haven't defined the `gps` link in your URDF.\
    \ The `gps` link should be a link defined on your robot at the location where\
    \ your GPS is mounted. Make the joint from the `gps` link to its parent link a\
    \ fixed joint, and the transform should then be available to `navsat_transform_node`. "

          <origin xyz="0 0 0" rpy="0 0 0"/>
          </joint>
