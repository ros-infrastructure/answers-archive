  body_markdown: "Just in general, Global Planner nor NavFn are actually A* implementations.\
    \ They're Global Dynamic Window Approach implementations that can use A* or Dijkstra's\
    \ for expanding the potential field, but that's not the search itself to generate\
    \ the path. Its using a gradient descent algorithm on the potential field to generate\
    \ a path. \n\n"
