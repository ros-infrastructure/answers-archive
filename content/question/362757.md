    \nPower up your AR.Drone <br>\nConnect your computer to the AR.Drone\u2019s network<br>\n\
    Open a telnet session to 192.168.1.1<br>\ntype the following: echo \"1.1.1\" >\
    \ /firmware/version.txt<br>\npress enter<br>\ntype the following: echo \"2.4.1\"\
    \ > /update/version.txt<br>\nConnect an FTP client to 192.168.1.1 Port: 5551 (i\
    \ use nautilius ctrl + l then ftp://192.168.1.1:551)<br>\nUpload the downloaded\
    \ plf file to the FTP server (AR.Drone)<br>\nDisconnect your computer from the\
    \ AR.Drone<br>\nDisconnect the battery from the AR.Drone<br>\nReconnect the battery<br>\n\
    Wait about 5 minutes while the upgrade completes, leave your drone switched on<br>\n\
