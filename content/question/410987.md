    ROS Noetic is the last ROS 1 distribution, and it is only supported in Ubuntu 20.04. You won't be able to use any ROS 1 packages in Ubuntu 22.04; you'll have to migrate your packages to ROS 2 Humble first. This is definitely not a trivial process, but here is a good place to start: [Migration Guide](https://docs.ros.org/en/humble/The-ROS2-Project/Contributing/Migration-Guide.html)
