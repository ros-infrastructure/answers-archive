    \ it fetch the dependency's by itself. The related tutorial is [here](http://www.ros.org/doc/api/rosinstall/html/rosws_tutorial.html).\n\
    \n    i. cd ~/ros_workspace (I put all checked out packages in here)\n    \n \
    \   ii. roslocate info package_name > package_name.rosinstall\n    \n    iii.\
    \ rosinstall . /etc/ros/diamondback|electric|fuerte package_name.rosinstall\n\
    \    \n    iv. rosmake package_name (rosdep --install package_name to install\
    \ system dependencies)\n    \n    v. deal with ROS dependencies by iterating 1\
    \ or 2"
