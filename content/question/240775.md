  body_markdown: "**Problem:**\nStupidity.\n\n**Solution:**\nRTFM. \n\n**Details**\n\
    By pressing q/w/e you are changing default speeds. To actually make it move at\
    \ those speeds, you need to press the keys in the 'Moving around' matrix that\
    \ you included in the picture of what you see in the teleop terminal. E.g., 'u,\
    \ i, and o'. \n\nSo, in the printout on the screen when it said that w/x increased\
    \ the speed, I took that to mean press w/x to make it move. I didn't notice the\
    \ large matrix of movement instructions:\n\n    Moving around:\n       u    i\
    \    o\n       j    k    l\n       m    ,    .\n\nWhen I started to press *these*\
    \ keys, the turtle began to move around in the map. Basically the instructions\
    \ are a topographic map:\n>u : rotate ccw and move forward (forward left)    \n\
    i : move forward    \no : rotate cw and move forward (forward right)    \nj :\
    \ rotate ccw    \nk : stop    \nl : rotate cw    \nm : rotate cw and move back\
    \ (backward left)    \n, : move back    \n. : rotate ccw and move back (backward\
    \ right)    \n\nSo...oops."
