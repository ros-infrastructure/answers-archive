EDIT:

solved with:

 

    double siny_cosp = 2 * (orientation_qw * orientation_qz + orientation_qx * orientation_qy);
        double cosy_cosp = 1 - 2 * (orientation_qy * orientation_qy + orientation_qz * orientation_qz);
        double yaw = std::atan2(siny_cosp, cosy_cosp);
