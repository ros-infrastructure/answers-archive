    What I found out that if you do not provide Inertial, you get the error. Try adding following within `<link></link>` , then the error will be gone.

          <inertial>
              <mass value="${mass}" />
              <inertia ixx="1.0" ixy="0.0" ixz="0.0" iyy="1.0" iyz="0.0" izz="1.0" />
            </inertial>
