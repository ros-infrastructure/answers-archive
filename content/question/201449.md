    Briefly, yes, you're missing something. :)

    More expansively, a node is a process; separate nodes execute independently. In your case, you have at least two nodes, one with two subscriber members (call it <i>subNode</i>) and (at least) one other node with publisher member(s) (call it <i>pubNode</I>). <i>SubNode</i> executes by endlessly looping -- during each loop, the 'spinOnce' checks the callback queue for messages. Unless and until <i>pubNode</i> publishes a message that <i>subNode</i> receives, nothing will happen (beyond the endless looping).

    In general, there's no guarantee which message <i>subNode</i> will receive first, but it will handle its callbacks in the order it receives the messages.
