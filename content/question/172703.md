  body_markdown: "Hello Ken,\n\nYou can probably use both types of planners. It really\
    \ depends on how you would like to control the system. For the turtlebot I think\
    \ geometric planners may be more appropriate, and they will run faster. You could\
    \ parametrize the velocity as a post-processing step on the path. If you have\
    \ an ODE describing your model of motion and would like to plan for that, then\
    \ control planners would be more suitable.\n\nPRM constructs a roadmap and we\
    \ do not assume the ability to solve the 2 point boundary value problem, so there\
    \ is no implementation in control/. \n\nMore documentation about OMPL is at http://ompl.kavrakilab.org\
    \ and the OMPL mailing list."
