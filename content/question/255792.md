    You can use the MoveGroupCommander.set_planner_id() function for this. Have a look at the [API documentation](http://docs.ros.org/jade/api/moveit_commander/html/classmoveit__commander_1_1move__group_1_1MoveGroupCommander.html#a51bc4850dbeecbe086a0d1e90c2981b6)

    An example:

        group = moveit_commander.MoveGroupCommander("manipulator")
        group.set_planner_id("PRMkConfigDefault")

    Good luck!
