    Your issue is simply a C++ issue, the method `to_string` from the library `<string>` is available with C++11. With kinetic, the standard is C++03 (Cf [REP 3](http://www.ros.org/reps/rep-0003.html#c)), so you have to specify in the `CMakeLists.txt` to use the standard C++11 with this line (just after `project(YOUR_PROJECT)`) :

        add_compile_options(-std=c++11)
