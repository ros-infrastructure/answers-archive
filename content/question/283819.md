    Yes, this is possible. They only have to share one ROS master. You have to source the respective Workspace in the terminal where you launch the nodes, though.

    However, IMO, this is not what you should do. This could easily lead to strange behaviour as it could take, e.g., some stuff from workspace1 in one terminal, and from /opt/ros in a second.
    This is why you typically do [workspace overlaying](http://wiki.ros.org/catkin/Tutorials/workspace_overlaying). Why don't you want to do that?
