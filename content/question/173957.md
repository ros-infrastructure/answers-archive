    Would [pcl::PointIndices class](http://docs.pointclouds.org/1.7.0/structpcl_1_1_point_indices.html) be what you are looking for? Throughout a lot of PCL code, they use these indices to select which point in a cloud to perform a particular operation on. Most of the core filters have a "SetInputCloud" and "SetIndices" functions and then a "filter" or "segment" or "extract" or whatnot function that acts on that subset of the cloud (if no indices are set, it is assumed to use the entire cloud).
