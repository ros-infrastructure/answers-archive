    Hi Acecryz,

    That's a pretty broad question and depending on your experience and/or knowledge of ROS, the answer widely varies.

    If you're new to ROS. It would probably be a good idea to start with learning a little bit about ROS, for instance by looking at the [ROS Tutorials](http://wiki.ros.org/ROS/Tutorials). The first tutorial deals with setting up your environment and a workspace, which you will need to compile and run the nodes.

    For a more thorough introduction a book might be easier, several are listed at the ROS wiki over [here](http://wiki.ros.org/Books). This includes both free and paid books.

    As a last piece of advice, you have a better chance of getting a satisfactorily answer if your question is a little more specific, and also include what you already know and have tried yourself.
