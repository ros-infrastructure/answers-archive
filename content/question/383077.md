 Hi all,

It seems like there is a gap between the command linear velocity published in /cmd_vel and the real turtlebot linear velocity. When I publish 0.2 m/s to /cmd_vel for 10 seconds, the robot only travels 1.7 meters (on  blanket or wood floor). In the navigation tasks, when I send a trajectory to turtlebot with planned linear velocity 0.3 m/s, the velocity calculated from the odom or SLAM pose feedback controller is actually about 3.4 m/s which published in /cmd_vel topic to keep the robot on the desired trajectory. So my conclusion is that the real robot cannot reach the command velocity sent to it. Here are some guesses for the reason:

1. Turtlebot uses torque controller as base controller. When you give it velocity command, it will convert to a torque for the driver of the wheels. Due to the friction, such torque cannot reach the desired torque that could drive the robot to the desired velocity.
2. Turtlebot will receive the velocity command and move its wheels intermittently which causes accelerate-decelerate cycles and fails to remain a constant velocity.
3. Turtlebot does not have any velocity inner loop feedback to regulate the velocity toward the command velocity in the velocity control mode.

Does anyone find the same problem or know the reason? It would be better if someone could tell me the control logic or process after I send a speed to turtlebot. I am using Ubuntu 16.04+ROS Kinetic on ThinkPad X1 and this problem happens on both turtlebot2 and LoCoBot. Thanks!
