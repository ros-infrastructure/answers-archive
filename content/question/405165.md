I have a UR3e, coupled with a VGC10 vacuum gripper at its end-effector.
I managed to control the UR3e to move etc.
However, I am failing to control the gripper via ROS.
I found the ROS drivers for the gripper [here](https://github.com/Osaka-University-Harada-Laboratory/onrobot), but they are only for the gripper as standalone (with a compute box) and not coupled with a UR. I migrated them to ROS2 [here](https://github.com/alexandrosnic/vgc10_ros2_drivers).
I also tried the following methods but none of them worked:

 - to use the “set_io” service by calling:

    ros2 service call /io_and_status_controller/set_io ur_msgs/srv/SetIO “{fun: 4, pin: 0, state: 12.0}”

 - or by sending: 

    set_digital_out(1, True)

as described [here](https://www.zacobria.com/universal-robots-knowledge-base-tech-support-forum-hints-tips-cb2-cb3/index.php/ur-script-send-commands-from-host-pc-to-robot-via-socket-connection/) 

 - Alternatively, I extracted the grip.script and sent it via URScript, similarly to [here](https://github-com.translate.goog/AIA-uniandes/BrazoRobotico/blob/main/Gripper/socketGrip.py?_x_tr_sl=auto&_x_tr_tl=en&_x_tr_hl=el) and [here](http://axisnj.com/controlling-a-universal-robots-cobot-using-python/)

I saw that ROS1 UR drivers had some compatibility for controlling the tool through the use_tool_communication parameter and I am wondering whether this would be the way to control the end-effector.

Since it is a vacuum gripper, it only needs to receive on/off data from the end-effector flange of the UR so I would assume that there would be a way to do this through the UR ROS2 drivers, but did not find the way yet.

I work on ROS2 Foxy.

Any information is much appreciated. Thank you
