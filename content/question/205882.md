  body_markdown: "**vx, vy, and vz are in meters/sec.  vth is in radians/second.**\n\
    \n**Take a look at this [site](http://www.geology.smu.edu/~dpa-www/robots/doc/odometry.txt).**\n\
    \n**One thing I would add about finding distance travelled and theta:**\n\nTo\
    \ get distance, and therefore (x,y) position, you will have to know wheel circumference.\
    \  Encoder counts gives you how much the motor has turned, but if that motor is\
    \ attached to a larger wheel, then the distance will obviously be greater.\n\n\
    **Example:** \n\nIf your robot was just riding on the motor shafts and your motor\
    \ shafts were 0.04 m in circumference.  If you rotated your motor one full rotation,\
    \ you will have traveled 0.04 m.  \n\nHowever, if your motor shaft was fixed to\
    \ a wheel with a circumference of 0.25 m and you rotated your motor one full revolution,\
    \ then you will have traveled 0.25m (ignoring friction and drag).\n\n"
