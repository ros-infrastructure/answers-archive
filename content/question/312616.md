  body_markdown: "You have to add appropriate CMake macros at the end of your `CMakeLists.txt`\
    \ file:\n\nAt the very bottom add these two lines for each executable:\n\n 1.\
    \ `add_executable(node_name src/file_name.cpp)`\n 2. `target_link_libraries(node_name\
    \ ${catkin_LIBRARIES})`\n\nThis will tell catkin to build and install the executable\
    \ in your `devel/lib/package_name` directory.\n\nIf none of the above works I\
    \ am presuming there's something wrong with your ROS install. Providing your `CMakeLists.txt`\
    \ file would be helpful.\n \nEDIT: Update your `CMakeLists.txt` to:\n\n    add_executable(hello\
