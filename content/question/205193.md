    Yes it is possible, take a look at the [3.7 Stereo B section](http://wiki.ros.org/rtabmap_ros/Tutorials/SetupOnYourRobot#Stereo_B_.28recommended.29) of your link above. You need to combine your odometry sources before connecting it to rtabmap (the blue link with [nav_mgs/Odometry](http://docs.ros.org/api/nav_msgs/html/msg/Odometry.html)). The blue links over [rtabmap](http://wiki.ros.org/rtabmap_ros#rtabmap) node are used for loop closure detection and to generate the 3D point cloud.
