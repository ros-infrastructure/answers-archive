    \ filename=\"libgazebo_ros_diff_drive.so\">\nin a <gazebo> tag, in an URDF file,\
    \ and\n\nThis is a Gazebo plugin, and hence will work only in Gazebo ([doc](http://gazebosim.org/tutorials?tut=ros_gzplugins#DifferentialDrive)).\n\
    \n- a controller spawned by a controller_manager spawner node of type \"diff_drive_controller/DiffDriveController\"\
    \n\nThis is a `ros_control` plugin, and will work with any supported robot backend,\
    \ and includes not only Gazebo, but `ros_control`-enabled hardware, and potentially\
    \ other simulators as well ([doc](http://wiki.ros.org/diff_drive_controller)).\n\
    \nFeature-wise they seem roughly similar: take twist commands, publish odometry.\
    \ I would suggest you to look at the documentation of each alternative and figure\
    \ out which works best for your setup."
