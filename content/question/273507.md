    \nIn short: yes, TF frames should preferably have only one backing publisher.\n\
    \nIf you have a urdf, `robot_state_publisher` (RSP) can do that for you, no need\
    \ for `static_transform_publisher` (STP).\n\nYou're not required to use RSP though,\
    \ you can use URDF and still use STP as well.\n\nOne advantage of the RSP over\
    \ STP is that the former will listen to `JointState` msgs (on the `joint_states`\
    \ topic) and update the frames it knows about (from your urdf) using FK. That\
    \ is something STP cannot do.\n"
