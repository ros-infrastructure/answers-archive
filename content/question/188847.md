  body_markdown: |-
    To get rid of that message you should set the parameter *meter_scoring* to either true or false, depending on your preferences. At the end of your *base_local_planner.yaml* add:

        meter_scoring: true

    or

        meter_scoring: false

    It is recommended to use meter scoring (*meter\_scoring: true*) when you expect that the resolution of your map might change.
