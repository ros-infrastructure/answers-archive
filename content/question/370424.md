    \ now by just adding these includes: \n\n    #include <asio/asio.hpp> \n    #include\
    \ <asio/ts/buffer.hpp>\n    #include <asio/ts/internet.hpp>\n\nI don't get any\
    \ compiler error now. As you can see in my solution, I did not change anything\
    \ in my CMakeList.txt or package.xml files. This is how I solved my problem. I\
