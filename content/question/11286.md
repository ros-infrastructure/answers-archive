I created two simple SDF models to represent a wall section and a door prop (that is, just a board with the picture of a door on it). When I place a "door" in front of a wall and get a camera (via the ROS camera plugin) to record the scene,

![image description](https://ftp.osuosl.org/pub/ros/download.ros.org/downloads/se_migration/gazebosim/14455682141615094.png)

The door appears behind the wall in the camera image, even though it is clearly in front of it in the environment view.

![image description](https://ftp.osuosl.org/pub/ros/download.ros.org/downloads/se_migration/gazebosim/1445568237405134.png)

However if I use the "Brick Box 3x1x3" from the model library instead of my own wall model, images are generated correctly:

![image description](https://ftp.osuosl.org/pub/ros/download.ros.org/downloads/se_migration/gazebosim/14455792055121301.png)

![image description](https://ftp.osuosl.org/pub/ros/download.ros.org/downloads/se_migration/gazebosim/14455792251227193.png)

What could be the problem here?
