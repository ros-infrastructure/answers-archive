    \n    In [1]: import socket\n    \n    In [2]: socket.AF_INE\n    socket.AF_INET\
    \   socket.AF_INET6  \n    \n    In [2]: socket.AF_INET6\n    Out[2]: 10\n\nimplies\
    \ that address family 10 is IPv6.\n\nThis [Google Group Posting](https://groups.google.com/forum/?fromgroups=#!topic/gevent/2uaMnDgVsEo)\
    \ seems to imply that you may get that error if your Python isn't compiled with\
    \ IPv6 support, which could be the case given you have a custom embedded Linux\
    \ install.\n"
