    \ Thus, it will explore trajectories from [[-max_vel_theta, max_vel_theta]](https://github.com/ros-planning/navigation/blob/melodic-devel/base_local_planner/src/simple_trajectory_generator.cpp#L71).\
    \ For you, 1.0 falls in the range [-11, 11]. \n\nOkay, so what does min_vel_theta\
    \ do? It makes sure your robot is moving [in some direction.](https://github.com/ros-planning/navigation/blob/melodic-devel/base_local_planner/src/simple_trajectory_generator.cpp#L193)\n\
    \nHowever, the above command *should* fail the check given, so I'm unclear what\
