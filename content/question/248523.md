    \ URDF and XACRO. After developing model, you need to access joint states (which\
    \ can be accessed by ROS-Control Package). Once, you have joint states, you can\
    \ write your ROS Node in C++ or Python by first subscribing to that states and\
    \ then generating ros torque. You can study [this](https://github.com/JoshMarino/gazebo_and_ros_control#Torque%20Control)\
