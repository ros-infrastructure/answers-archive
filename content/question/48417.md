    You can use the script listed at [this answer](http://answers.ros.org/question/11545/plotprint-rpy-from-quaternion/#17106) to subscribe to quaternions that are published from your bag file, convert them to Euler angles, and then republish as RPY in NWU coordinates. You can then plot your Euler angles with rxplot. Use the same node multiple times while remapping topics and you can visualize Euler angles from multiple sources simultaneously.
