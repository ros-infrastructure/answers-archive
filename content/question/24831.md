    Make sure the path to the dae files in the sdf can be found.
    Example `model.sdf`:

        <?xml version="1.0"?>
        <sdf version="1.5">
        <actor name="actor">
          <skin>
            <filename>../../src/actor_pkg/animations/my_animation.dae</filename>
          </skin>
          <animation name="animation">
            <filename>../../src/actor_pkg/animations/my_animation.dae</filename>
          </animation>
        </actor>
        </sdf>


    Reminder: the root of `<filename>` is in `catkin_ws/devel/lib`.
