  body_markdown: "Found an answer: \n\nBecause I used `group.execute(wait=False)`,\
    \ moveIt does not wait for the trajectory to complete the first path, as it does\
    \ not track the status of trajectory execution. And as soon as moveit received\
    \ second `execute` command, it sends this above error. `set_max_velocity_factor()`\
    \ was not an issue. So, used this `wait` parameter cautiously."
