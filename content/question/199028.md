  body_markdown: |-
    Specify the `zeros` dictionary at the **root** namespace, (I don't know why `joint_state_publisher` does not use the node's private one, but that's how it is). In yaml syntax, this should be (untested):

        zeros:
          foo_joint: 1.0
          bar_joint: -1.0

    If it doesn't work, ask the [source](https://github.com/ros/robot_model/blob/indigo-devel/joint_state_publisher/joint_state_publisher/joint_state_publisher#L83).
