  body_markdown: "Any module with a UI is valid to consider improving the UI/UX. I\
    \ would recommend that you pick one that is interesting to you and explore it\
    \ and research it and then come back with a proposal as to how it might be improved.\
    \ This is probably best doen through an enhancement proposal ticket on the repositiory.\
    \ If you proposal is positively recieved by the maintainers then you can go ahead\
    \ and work it up and submit it as a pull request. \n\nThere are many different\
    \ aspects of the system and each individual engages best with different parts\
    \ of the system. So pick the part that is most interesting to you. Starting small\
    \ will help you get your bearings and learn about the software engineering processes\
    \ we use in the project. After that you can grow the scale of your contributions.\
    \ If you try to do very big changes early it's likely to end up with frustration\
    \ due to following different processes, even if the technical goals and results\
    \ are likely similar."
