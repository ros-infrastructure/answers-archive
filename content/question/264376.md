We are developing our road map for R&D. One of the questions we have is whether or not we should invest in R&D in ROS. Therefore, I was searching for comparisons between robotics frameworks. The most recent article I found was: [Robotics Middleware: A Comprehensive Literature Survey and Attribute-Based Bibliography](http://dx.doi.org/10.1155/2012/959013) from 2012. The article gives an overview. However, a lot of the frameworks mentioned in the article are not actively maintained anymore.

Is there a more recent (from 2015 or later) comparison between robotics frameworks? If not, are there competing frameworks that we should consider?
