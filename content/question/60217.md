  body_markdown: "Your \n\n    IplImage copy = currentImage;\n\ncannot work because\
    \ at that point the image cannot be filled. Your callback is called in ros::spinOnce(),\
    \ so before your first call of spinOnce() your currentImage is 100% empty.Afterwards\
    \ it might be empty.\n\nCheck in your while loop and process only if filled:\n\
    \n\n    ros::Rate loop_rate(5);  \n    \n    while (ros::ok())\n     {\n     \
    \       ros::spinOnce();\n            \n           if ( !currentImage.empty()\
    \ )\n           {\n               IplImage copy = currentImage;\n    \n      \
    \        //other computations.\n            }\n    \n            loop_rate.sleep();\n\
    \     }\n\n"
