    As far as I know, the only three formats supported by URDF/Gazebo/RViz are:

     - .dae  (Collada)
     - .mesh (Ogre)
     - .stl

    (also see [this answer](http://answers.ros.org/question/1144/query-on-urdfxacro-file#1904)). Perhaps ArchiCAD can export to a format that can be imported by another program which can export into one of those formats. Perhaps [this list](https://secure.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/de/wiki/Liste_von_CAD-Programmen) of CAD programs and formats is of help (sorry, German link; the English Wikipedia page doesn't have ArchiCAD).
