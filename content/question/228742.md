    There are some possibilities to do this (like [ipsec tunnels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IPsec) fi, or SSH tunneling), but success of any approach will depend on what kinds of traffic the firewalls on both university networks will allow. Using a third party 'in the middle' might make things easier, as all connections will be considered *outgoing* by both university networks then (no need to 'open up ports' for a server at one of the ends).

    After setting up the actual connection, it might make sense to look into a multi-master setup in combination with throttling of some of the more 'verbose' topics (such as `tf`). Most of the times this is taken care of by the multi-master system though.
