     If you want a simple example of the ALVAR tracking bridge in ROS you can [check this answer](http://answers.ros.org/question/203207/how-to-use-ar_track_alvar-for-detecting-markers-using-kinect/#203234). Or directly the examples on [this repo](https://github.com/sniekum/ar_track_alvar/blob/hydro-devel/launch/pr2_indiv.launch).

    If you want to use the original ALVAR library. From what you say all you're missing is highgui in you package. So adding a <code>find_package(OpenCV REQUIRED components core highgui)</code> to you cmakelist should do the job.
