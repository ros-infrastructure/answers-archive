    OpenCV is a system package in Hydro; not a ROS package.

    According to the [vision_opencv](http://wiki.ros.org/vision_opencv?distro=hydro) docs, you should have a **build_depend** and a **run_depend** on opencv2 in your package.xml, and you should add the following to your cmakelists:

        find_package(OpenCV)
        include_directories(${OpenCV_INCLUDE_DIRS})
        target_link_libraries(my_awesome_library ${OpenCV_LIBRARIES})
