    I got the solution for this problem.
    Actually what I found out is like, whenever we use any action in our code, and with that 7 messages are generated, as below:

    1. XXXGoal.msg
    2. XXXFeedback.msg
    3. XXXResult.msg
    4. XXXAction.msg
    5. XXXActionGoal.msg
    6. XXXActionFeedback.msg
    7. XXXActionResult.msg

    So the problem here is that, we have to use XXXGoal.msg in the callback.
    But I was using XXXActionGoal.msg in the callback. And that why I was getting the compilation error.

    Now I changed to `void execute_cb(const robot_calibration_msgs::GripperLedCommandConstPtr &action)` instead of `void execute_cb(const robot_calibration_msgs::GripperLedCommandActionConstPtr &action)`. And it is compiling and I am able to use it.
