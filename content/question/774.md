    \ syncronized time. \n\nI published gazebo's simulation time into ROS as '/clock'.\
    \ \n\nAnd I set ROS parameter 'use_sim_time' into true. \n\nThere is no more error\
    \ and finally PR2 works with ROS and Gazebo-1.3. \n\nIt is worth to read: [http://www.ros.org/wiki/Clock](http://www.ros.org/wiki/Clock)\n\
