    I belief you would need to create a [static transform publisher](http://wiki.ros.org/tf) to put your /camera_optical_depth_frame in the right perspective.  Take a look at [these tutoritals](http://wiki.ros.org/tf/Tutorials) and it should help you.

    I had much the same issue until I 'put' my kinect on my robot with the static transform publisher (I added the kinect to the p2os/p2os_urdf/defs/pioneer3dx_body.xacro).  Then I changed RViz Fixed Frame to base_link or odom and it displayed the kinect's pointcloud in the correct perspective relative to the grid and robot.

    ![image description](https://ftp.osuosl.org/pub/ros/download.ros.org/downloads/se_migration/ros/14285107397529779.png)
