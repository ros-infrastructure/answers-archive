    This really depends on the type of spinner you use to process ROS messages.

    The [page here](http://wiki.ros.org/roscpp/Overview/Callbacks%20and%20Spinning) has a good summary, the simple `ros::spin()` is single threaded so they will block the rest of your code, but there are several multi-threaded versions to. You'll have to make sure your callbacks are thread safe for the use of these to be reliable though.
