  body_markdown: "Typically, flexible objects/cables are modeled in Gazebo using a\
    \ series of small links connected with joints.\n\nCheck out [this related question](http://answers.gazebosim.org/question/8058/how-can-improve-cable-simulation-stability/)\
    \ as well as [this fire hose model](https://bitbucket.org/osrf/gazebo_models/src/de9abaab0588d76bc0e940dbdf0078b4bfe7b78a/fire_hose_long/?at=default).\n\
    \n----------\nNote: \nAdding lots of links and joints to your simulation will\
    \ inevitably decrease simulation performance and may cause instability. \n\nTherefore,\
    \ you *may* be better off determining some heuristic function that takes in the\
    \ distance vector between the quadcopter and the tether point and outputs a force\
    \ vector estimating the applied force of the tether on the quadcopter. You could\
    \ then integrate the function into Gazebo via a Gazebo Plugin (perhaps even roll\
    \ it into whatever quadcopter Gazebo Plugin you are using)"
