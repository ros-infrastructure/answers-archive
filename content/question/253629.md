    The problem was solved by adding the following line in the beginning

        add_definitions(-DBOOST_LOG_DYN_LINK=1)

    and also changing `${Boost_INCLUDE_DIR}` to `${Boost_INCLUDE_DIRS}` (Note: "DIRS" not "DIR") as per the wiki page on [CMakeLists](http://wiki.ros.org/catkin/CMakeLists.txt).

    catkin_package(

      CATKIN_DEPENDS cv_bridge image_transport roscpp

    include_directories(
