    There's a [new tutorial](http://gazebosim.org/wiki/Tutorials/drcsim/2.6/controller_synchronization) for the forthcoming drcsim 2.6 that's in C++.  That should mostly work for you on 2.5, with the exception of the `desired_controller_period_ms` variable in the command message; that's not present in 2.5.

    You can also look at [the code](https://bitbucket.org/osrf/drcsim/src/e5a3e7fe5ddacea2dcdf74fb55d336961bdf6ad1/ros/atlas_msgs/pub_atlas_command.cpp?at=drcsim_2.5) for the `pub_atlas_command` tool, which we use for testing.
