    I just asked the maintainer on your behalf, who said:

    You can't do orientation constraints with the moveit plugin, only when you're using [trac_ik_lib](https://bitbucket.org/traclabs/trac_ik/src/HEAD/trac_ik_lib/) directly. As outlined at the bottom of the README, you can specify an orientation (and position) *tolerance* in the `KDL::Twist` message provided to `ik_solver.CartToJnt(...)`, which are `+/-` symmetric.

    Hope that helps.
