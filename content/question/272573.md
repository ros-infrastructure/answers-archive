    Not directly.

    MoveIt always works with 3D information.

    The most straight-forward solution would be to generate an octomap from your 2d-map by projecting all occupied pixels to cuboids of some fixed height and use this octomap to plan.
    You could implement a specialized [Octomap updater](https://github.com/ros-planning/moveit/blob/kinetic-devel/moveit_ros/perception/pointcloud_octomap_updater_plugin_description.xml) to do that.
