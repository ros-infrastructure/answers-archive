    [ROS Melodic](http://wiki.ros.org/melodic) will support Ubuntu 17.10 once it's released.

    [REP-0003](http://www.ros.org/reps/rep-0003.html#melodic-morenia-may-2018-may-2023) is the place where supported platforms and libraries are specified.

    Note that the support for non-LTS Ubuntu distros like 17.10 will be terminated when the corresponding support for Ubuntu reaches EOL (end-of-life) (e.g. [announcement of ROS Lunar ending supporting Ubuntu 17.04](https://discourse.ros.org/t/suspension-of-debian-packaging-for-eol-ubuntu-zesty-17-04/3742)).
