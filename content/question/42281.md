  body_markdown: "At first it's necessary to connect the joint with actuator:  \n\t\
    \n\n    <transmission name=\"joint_back_right_wheel_trans\" type=\"SimpleTransmission\"\
    >\n    \t <actuator name=\"back_right_wheel_motor\"/>\n    \t <joint name=\"wheel_to_back_right_wheelholder\"\
    />\n             <mechanicalReduction>1</mechanicalReduction>\n             <motorTorqueConstant>1</motorTorqueConstant>\n\
    \            </transmission>\n\nThen add these lines to your robot description\
    \ file:  \n  \n\n    <gazebo>\n       <controller:gazebo_ros_controller_manager\
    \ name=\"gazebo_ros_controller_manager\" plugin=\"libgazebo_ros_controller_manager.so\"\
    >\n          <alwaysOn>true</alwaysOn>\n          <updateRate>1000.0</updateRate>\n\
    \          <interface:audio name=\"gazebo_ros_controller_manager_dummy_iface\"\
    \ />\n        </controller:gazebo_ros_controller_manager>\n      </gazebo>\n\n\
    It should work then."
