    I'll give one possible answer myself. Creating my own 'rosgrep' script, this exactly matches my requirements.

         #!/bin/bash
         pkg=$(rospack find $1)
         shift
         grep --exclude-dir=\.svn --exclude-dir=bin --exclude-dir=build --exclude-dir=lib $* -r $pkg --color=always
