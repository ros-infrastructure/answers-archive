  body_markdown: "I would recommend you the pointcloud library. It's similar to OpenCV,\
    \ just for point clouds. A generic filter approach is to use a downsampling (see\
    \ [voxelgrid](http://pointclouds.org/documentation/tutorials/voxel_grid.php))\
    \ first. Maybe you can skip this for pico flexx witk 38k points...\nHowever, the\
    \ next step may be the statistical outlier removal filter (see [here](http://pointclouds.org/documentation/tutorials/statistical_outlier.php#statistical-outlier-removal)).\
    \ The SOR-filter is a bit expensive. You have to check whether it's worth or not...The\
    \ contional outlier radius filter ([link](http://pointclouds.org/documentation/tutorials/remove_outliers.php#remove-outliers))\
    \ was a bit faster in my case, but the result wasn't that satisfing as with the\
    \ SOR.\n\nSo, that's already a bunch of filters. \n\nCheers\nMatthias\n\nP.S.\n\
    \nJust in case you haven't so far: Make sure you won't put any forces to the usb\
    \ connector on the pico flexx. We made a huge effort to avoid any pulling on the\
    \ cable but unfortunatly two cameras broke down."
