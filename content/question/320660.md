    Yes, hector_mapping uses the standard ROS coordinate frame convention (see [REP 103](http://www.ros.org/reps/rep-0103.html)). It starts mapping at the zero pose (translations zero and identity quaternion) with x pointing forwards, y left, z up and estimates the robot pose relative to that.
