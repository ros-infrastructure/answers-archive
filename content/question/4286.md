    I've been helping to get experimental support for OSX as of gazebo 1.9.1; the biggest sticking point that required a major change to the gazebo code base involved supporting [Case Insensitive Filesystems](https://bitbucket.org/osrf/gazebo/issue/775/installation-fails-on-case-insensitive), which is now solved and should benefit a Windows port as well.

    Dependency resolution is always tricky; we were fortunate to use the [homebrew formulae developed by the ROS team](https://github.com/ros/homebrew-hydro) for that, although I've heard that OGRE support on Windows is good. Take a peek at the [gazebo formula](https://github.com/osrf/homebrew-simulation/blob/master/gazebo.rb) for a brief list of some dependencies, though there are more that are unlisted because they are included with osx.

    I haven't used Cygwin in quite a while, but I wish you luck. Please feel free to share your experience here or on the [gazebo issue tracker](https://bitbucket.org/osrf/gazebo/issues?status=new&status=open).
