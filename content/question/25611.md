    It works fine for me when loaded in the default empty world. Your problem is probably the physics configuration in your world file.

    Sidenote 1: Inertias are not involved in collision checking. What you care about are the collision geometries.
    Sidenote 2: The inertia in the model doesn't seem to correspond to your part (visualize it in the gzclient). Make sure to use the correct reference frame when calculating it.
