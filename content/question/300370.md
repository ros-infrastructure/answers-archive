  body_markdown: |-
    Make sure that you are allowing [multicast traffic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multicast) in the firewall. By default, the `ufw` firewall included in 16.04 blocks multicast traffic, even for loopback (i.e. on the same system). [Here's an example of allowing multicast in `ufw`](https://einar.slaskete.net/2014/05/03/allow-multicast-and-igmp-with-ufw-for-iptv-to-work/).
