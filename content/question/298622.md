    An array of `int8` 28 values long will give you 224 bits. This is the usual approach to storing a binary blob such as your `RadarDetection` type. It will be presented as a `boost::array` of `int8_t`, which you can get the raw pointer to and read into your data type. See the [msg data types page](http://wiki.ros.org/msg) for more information.
