  body_markdown: "This is solved by mentioning the library location and the path separately\
    \ in the CMakeLists.txt\n\n    set(OpenCV_INCLUDE_DIRS /usr/local/)\n\n    set(OpenCV_LIBS\
    \ /usr/local/lib/libopencv_core.so)\n\nThis fixed the problem on most cases. \n\
    \nIf catkin fails to compile with the local openCV after setting the CMakeLists.txt\
    \ as above, open the  CMakeCache.txt after it compiles and change the opencv path\
    \  in that and recompile. Works!"
