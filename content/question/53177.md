    \ wants it as a package: [https://code.ros.org/svn/ros-pkg/stacks/simulator_gazebo/tags/latest/gazebo/manifest.xml](https://code.ros.org/svn/ros-pkg/stacks/simulator_gazebo/tags/latest/gazebo/manifest.xml)\n\
    \n*So you are getting the wrong version of gazebo.*\n\nI suggest getting the version\
    \ for fuerte since that is what you are using. See the list of tags in the SVN\
    \ repository here: \n**[https://code.ros.org/svn/ros-pkg/stacks/simulator_gazebo/tags/](https://code.ros.org/svn/ros-pkg/stacks/simulator_gazebo/tags/)**\n\
