    \ have catkin instructions in `CMakeLists.txt`) you must use  `catkin_make_isolated`\
    \ in order to compile your workspace\n\nSee [this answer](https://answers.ros.org/question/320613/catkin_make-vs-catkin_make_isolated-which-is-preferred/)\

    \ even if I don't have plain CMake modules"
