    \ the way to fix this problem is to add the following options to catkin_make_isolated:\n\
    \n    -DCMAKE_MACOSX_RPATH=ON \n    -DCMAKE_INSTALL_RPATH=\"[path_to_your_workspace]/install_isolated/lib\""
