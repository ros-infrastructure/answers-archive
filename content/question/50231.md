  body_markdown: |-
    [gmapping](http://www.ros.org/wiki/gmapping) receives the odometry information in the form of a transform from the `odom` frame to the `base_link` frame. You need another package (for example [robot_pose_ekf](http://www.ros.org/wiki/robot_pose_ekf)) publishing that transform in order for gmapping to work.

      Odometry is a stream of messages on the /odom topic. But in ROS, an odometry node is also supposed to continuously publish the transform odom -> base_link and that's what, for instance, the robot_pose_ekf node does after fusing /odom and IMU data.
