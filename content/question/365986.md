    A *very* quick comment: look up the *bundles* packaging concept by Clearpath. There are several ROSCon presentations about it. Colcon also has bundle support.

    But note: since you mention Python, you may have to take care not to package the `.py` files. I'm not sure how well that part has been automated.

    Note 2: decompilation of `.pyc` is very much possible (to varying degrees of success), so if your goal is to "protect" your Python programs (or those of others), this might pose an additional challenge.

    ---

    As an alternative to bundles: distributing "complex programs" is sort-of what [Singularity](https://sylabs.io/docs/) was created for. It's like Docker, but with a focus on distribution of complete applications and environments (in contrast to the (micro) service approach Docker takes). Using UIs and other hw (like GPUs) etc is trivial with Singularity.
