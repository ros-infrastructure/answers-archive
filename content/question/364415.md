  body_markdown: "There are ROS Enhancement Proposals (REPs) that can be thought of\
    \ as standards.Check out [REP 105](https://www.ros.org/reps/rep-0105.html)  for\
    \ a description of platform coordinate frames and [REP 103](https://www.ros.org/reps/rep-0103.html)\
    \ for axis standards. Your `base_link` should be fixed to your robot, whereas\
    \ `odom` is 'fixed' to a point in the real world. As your robot moves, the transformation\
    \ between the two should change: this is how you determine your position. \n\n\
    I've also seen `base_footprint` used, but it's a matter of application and preference."
