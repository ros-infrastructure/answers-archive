    I'm not sure how up-to-date it is (especially with the recent releases of newer versions in the `7.x` and `8.x` series of Gazebo), but [gazebosim.org/Tutorial: Ros Plugins](http://gazebosim.org/tutorials?tut=ros_plugins) would seem to be the "hello world" of writing Gazebo plugins that integrate with ROS (with [gazebosim.org/Tutorial: Using Gazebo plugins with ROS](http://gazebosim.org/tutorials?tut=ros_gzplugins) showing how to configure your urdf/sdf to include the plugins in a model/sim).

    I would also recommend to take a look at [gazebo_ros_pkgs](http://wiki.ros.org/gazebo_ros_pkgs) (source: [ros-simulation/gazebo_ros_pkgs](https://github.com/ros-simulation/gazebo_ros_pkgs)) as that package provides most of the standard Gazebo-ROS plugins. Personally I find reading other peoples sources very informative.
