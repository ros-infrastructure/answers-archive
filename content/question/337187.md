    I doubt boost::bind is the problem. I'd guess the issue is more related to the `message_filters`. Never tried this myself, but a quick google showed a few people having trouble with time sync and  PCL message types. It seems the issue is that the [header in the pcl::PCLPointCloud2](http://docs.pointclouds.org/1.7.0/structpcl_1_1_p_c_l_header.html) uses a `uint64` that can't automatically be converted to a `ros::Time` instance. The sensor_msgs/PointCloud2 option is an okay workaround, but it does likely incur an extra expense of a PCL conversion or two.
