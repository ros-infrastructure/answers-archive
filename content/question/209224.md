    Just to add to the options mentioned by others: you could perhaps also look at [moveit_visual_tools](https://github.com/davetcoleman/moveit_visual_tools) by Dave Coleman. From [README.md](https://github.com/davetcoleman/moveit_visual_tools/blob/indigo-devel/README.md):

    > Animate Trajectories

    > Higher level robot and trajectory functions

    > - **publishTrajectoryPath**
    > - publishTrajectoryPoint
    > - publishRobotState
    > - publishAnimatedGrasps
    > - publishIKSolutions

    `publishTrajectoryPath(..)` would seem to fit with what you describe.

    Edit: whether this would work obviously depends on what particular type of *robot trajectory* you are working with.
