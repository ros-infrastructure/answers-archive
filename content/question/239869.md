  body_markdown: "Hi\n\nYou could write a node that publishes your images one after\
    \ the other and then launch that node from a launch file.\n\nTo do that you can\
    \ probably follow [this tutorial](http://wiki.ros.org/image_transport/Tutorials/PublishingImages)\
    \ and modify it to loop through your images.\n\nI haven't tested any of this but\
    \ I guess you would have to change `  cv::Mat image = cv::imread(argv[1], CV_LOAD_IMAGE_COLOR);`\
    \ \nto something like :\n\n `  cv::Mat image1 = cv::imread(path/to/image1, CV_LOAD_IMAGE_COLOR);`\n\
    \n ` cv::Mat image2 = cv::imread(path/to/image2, CV_LOAD_IMAGE_COLOR);`\n\n `\
    \ cv::Mat image3 = cv::imread(path/to/image3, CV_LOAD_IMAGE_COLOR);`\n\nand then\
    \ change `msg` at a regular time interval in the loop\n\n"
