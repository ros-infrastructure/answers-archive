  body_markdown: "Generally ros returns odometry but not for ardrone. For quadcoptors\
    \ odometry readings are not easily estimated unlike in the case of mobile robots.\
    \ To estimate the position either you can integrate velocity values that u get\
    \ in navdata but as you mentioned it is very erroneous . One way to estimate position\
    \ of Drone is using Ar tags. You can either put a artag on drone and let some\
    \ external camera track its location or you can put artags at predefined location\
    \ and let ardrone camera see it and then estimate its position. For artags look\
    \ ar_pose package in ROS.\n\nAnother way is to implement VSLAM using monocular\
    \ camera like PTAM. "
