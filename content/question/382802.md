    > the plan is to continuously provide the robot with its current location,

    You should then pick up the position from simulation, and publish it as a transform similar to how it is done in [amcl](http://wiki.ros.org/amcl#Transforms). I.e. publish the transform from the global frame to the odometry frame (in case you do have an odometry frame). This is exactly what the `fake_localization` package does (according to its [docs](http://wiki.ros.org/fake_localization?distro=noetic))...
