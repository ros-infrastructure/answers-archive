    You can improve the precision by decreasing the `resolution` parameter in your costmap config, and reducing the `xy_goal_tolerance` parameter in your local planner.

      xy_goal_tolerance means how close to the goal you want to be. If you use 1m, for example, you will stop navigating once you are inside this threshold.
      resolution decrease means you will have smaller cells in your occupancy grid. this will also means you can get a more realistic position of obstacles and, therefore, navigate more precisely around then (narrow passages, get closer to obstacles, etc.)
