I thought I ask this question before submitting ticket [on github](http://goo.gl/MaSW6) since I'm not yet sure 100%. 

Why does msg type `diagnostic_msg/DiagnosticStatus` not have level of operations `STALE`?

`DiagnosticStatus` [defines 3 levels of operations (from 0 to 2)](http://www.ros.org/doc/api/diagnostic_msgs/html/msg/DiagnosticStatus.html). But `GenericAnalyzer` in `diagnostic_aggregator` [publishes level 3, or stale](http://ros.org/doc/fuerte/api/diagnostic_aggregator/html/generic__analyzer_8cpp_source.html) (I recognized this while running `turtlebot_gazebo/launch/turtlebot_empty_world.launch`). 

Assuming it's recommended to extend `GenericAnalyzer` when implementing your own robot, the cleanest solution would to include `stale` in the msg format imho but how does this sound?
