  body_markdown: "What do you need the transformation for?\n\nIf you use a URDF and\
    \ make sure your IMU link is upside down in there too, the packages that use the\
    \ IMU data will use your URDF to get the TF from `base_link` to `imu_link` automatically\
    \ (I've done the same with lidars and never had issues).\n\nYou could also use\
    \ a tf listener to request the transform between `base_link` to `imu_link` yourself\
    \ ([tutorial](http://wiki.ros.org/tf/Tutorials), I'm not sure if you want cpp\
    \ or python).\n\nIf you want the \"right side up\" IMU data yourself, is point\
    \ [4 or 5](http://wiki.ros.org/tf2/Tutorials/Quaternions) relevant to you? It's\
    \ about applying rotation or inverting your TF. "
