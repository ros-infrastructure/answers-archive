  body_markdown: |-
    ubuntu tries to access the kinect when you plug it in in newer versions using a mod. Go to terminal and type,

        sudo rmmod gspca_kinect

    and you should be good (it does this every time you connect the device). There are other troubleshooting methods on the [OpenNi Grabbar Framework page](http://pointclouds.org/documentation/tutorials/openni_grabber.php)

    EDIT:
    If this is your problem, blacklist the gspca_kinect module on your system,

        sudo echo "blacklist gspca_kinect" >> /etc/modprobe.d/blacklist.conf
