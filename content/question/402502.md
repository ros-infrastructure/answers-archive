I am using RSI and KRC2 by following steps from [kuka_experimental](https://github.com/ros-industrial/kuka_experimental) which I have done until the `$ rosrun rqt_joint_trajectory_controller rqt_joint_trajectory_controller` step.

I previously found [this interesting thread](https://answers.ros.org/question/345728/connect-real-kuka-to-moveit/?fbclid=IwAR02bZXKVQAB5By8NTz-6CCV12fOaESKFLydizUTs9QpmmjyLzQ2TZyKhaw) and tried to follow it, but I still have concerns about robot_interface_streaming.launch from [here](https://github.com/gavanderhoorn/kuka_experimental/blob/kr6r900sixx_moveit_rsi_convenience/kuka_rsi_hw_interface/launch/robot_interface_streaming.launch), it's because as far as I know it was written based on KRC4.
