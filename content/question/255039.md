  body_markdown: "No. Compression is the way to reduce the size of the data footprint\
    \ while recording data. This is the principle that governs video data as well,\
    \ known as [codec](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Video_codec). The actual data\
    \ of a recorded movie is lot larger than the one you stream or watch on your computer.\
    \  \n\nI believe that ros does a pretty good job of compressing incoming image\
    \ data and hence you see a bloat in the image size when you decompress it frame\
    \ by frame. You can read the source code of compressions [here](https://github.com/ros-perception/image_transport_plugins/blob/indigo-devel/compressed_image_transport/src/compressed_publisher.cpp)\
    \ \n\nIf you want to compress it further I would recommend use ros to generate\
    \ all the images and then compress everything using a video codec using [ffmpeg](https://ffmpeg.org/).\
    \ There is information about that on [this stack overflow question](http://stackoverflow.com/questions/24961127/ffmpeg-create-video-from-images)."
