  body_markdown: "The information you want is on the [openni_camera](http://ros.org/wiki/openni_camera)\
    \ wiki page.\n\nWhat to save depends on what you want to see. Those topics can\
    \ use up a lot of bandwidth. \n\nIf you want the RBG camera data, save `rgb/camera_info`\
    \ and `rgb/image_raw/compressed`. \n\nIf you want the depth camera, save `depth/camera_info`\
    \ and `depth/image_raw`. Image compression does not work well on depth images.\n\
    \nDepending on what frame of reference you want to use, it probably makes sense\
    \ to save the `/tf` topic, too."
