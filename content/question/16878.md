Hi,

I am running gazebo7 with ROS Indigo on Ubuntu 14.04. In my simulation, a robot manipulates an object and observes the results to learn the effects of its action. After each iteration, I reset the simulation. However, I would like to vary the friction parameters mu and mu2 of the ground plane and the manipulated object automatically after each iteration. 

Can I achieve this using a service call from ROS? This would be my preferred approach.

I have looked at the [gazebo_msgs documentation](http://docs.ros.org/indigo/api/ric_mc/html/namespacegazebo__msgs.html) but have not found a message that allows me to do that. [SetLinkPropertiesRequest](http://docs.ros.org/indigo/api/ric_mc/html/classgazebo__msgs_1_1SetLinkPropertiesRequest.html) only allows me to change intertial parameters, mass, gravity mode, and the centre of mass.

[This](http://answers.gazebosim.org/question/16442/how-to-programatically-set-joint-friction-torque/) question may be related and concerns setting friction torque parameters for wheels programatically. The suggested answer is writing a model plugin. If possible, I would like to avoid that.

I hypothesise that another method would be to delete the ground plane and/or object each time and spawn a new version with modified friction parameters using [SpawnModelRequest](http://docs.ros.org/indigo/api/ric_mc/html/classgazebo__msgs_1_1SpawnModelRequest.html). That would also be less convenient and (presumably) efficient than just changing parameters.

I would greatly appreciate any guidance or suggestions.

Cheers.
