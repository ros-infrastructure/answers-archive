  body_markdown: "From the [sensor_msgs/JointState comments](http://docs.ros.org/api/sensor_msgs/html/msg/JointState.html):\n\
    \n>     The state of each joint (revolute or prismatic) is defined by:\n     \
    \  * the position of the joint (rad or m),\n       * the velocity of the joint\
    \ (rad/s or m/s) and \n       * the effort that is applied in the joint (Nm or\
    \ N).\n\nSo `rad/s` or `m/s`, depending on whether the joint is a revolute or\
    \ linear joint.\n\nNote that this is what the message semantics are specced to\
    \ be. If a node abuses the message and puts `ticks/foot` in there, that will work,\
    \ but will obviously violate semantics.\n"
