    You can simply delete those lines in the `configure.bat` - that call is downloading and unzipping the files in that directory. If you have already found, downloaded, and unzipped the files (and properly installed protobuf), then you're good to go.

    If when you try to compile any other packages, they don't find the dependencies, you can add their path locations in the `configure.bat` files by adding them to the `cmake` line here: `-DCMAKE_PREFIX_PATH="C:/gz-ws/protobuf-3.4.1-vs19"`, along with anything else it can't find.
