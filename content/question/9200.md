    A sensor will turn off when the [<always_on> SDF element](http://sdformat.org/spec?ver=1.5&elem=sensor#sensor_always_on) is false, and there are no subscribers to the sensor's output stream.

    If you want to control a sensor, then you'll have to write a sensor plugin.
