    In android_tutorial_pubsub, the NodeConfiguration is private. Nodes on your PC will not be able to communicate with those on your Android device.

    Try changing the [NodeConfiguration](http://docs.rosjava.googlecode.com/hg/rosjava_core/html/javadoc/org/ros/node/NodeConfiguration.html) to a public one. See the NodeConfiguration.newPublic documentation.
