    If you are talking about the ["clock model" in diamondback](https://code.ros.org/svn/ros-pkg/stacks/simulator_gazebo/branches/simulator_gazebo-1.0/gazebo_worlds/worlds/empty.world),
    as of electric, the "clock model" has been removed from the [world file](https://code.ros.org/svn/ros-pkg/stacks/simulator_gazebo/branches/simulator_gazebo-1.2/gazebo_worlds/worlds/empty.world).  The [gazebo binary built from gazeboros.cpp](https://code.ros.org/svn/ros-pkg/stacks/simulator_gazebo/branches/simulator_gazebo-1.4/gazebo/src/gazeboros.cpp) contains a publisher for `/clock` and various services such as the model factory for spawning models.  In fuerte, the model is slightly shifted as all ros related topics/services are now available there through [system plugins](https://code.ros.org/svn/ros-pkg/stacks/simulator_gazebo/trunk/gazebo/src/gazebo_ros_api_plugin.cpp), invoked by loading them with the `-p` option when [starting up gazebo](https://code.ros.org/svn/ros-pkg/stacks/simulator_gazebo/trunk/gazebo/scripts/gazebo).

    In terms of "controlling the speed of simulation", can you explain in more detail what you mean by it?  Thanks.
