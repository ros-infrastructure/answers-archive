    <s>Can someone clarify the install process after make completes the build in /tmp? </s>

    I think I understand why the default is to install to /usr/local but
    I'm not sure that it is obvious that /usr/local/setup.sh is part of ROS

    The method I used is roughly

    $ make

    Edit CMakeCache.txt
    CMAKE_INSTALL_PREFIX:PATH=/opt/ros

    $ sudo make install

    Testing continues...

    Also, gcc/g++ 4.6 seems to have issues, but gcc/g++ 4.5 seems to work.
