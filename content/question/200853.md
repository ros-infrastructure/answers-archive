From the [HDL-32E User's Manual & Programming Guide](http://velodynelidar.com/lidar/products/manual/63-9113%20HDL-32E%20manual_Rev%20E_NOV2012.pdf), pg 9:

***Packet Format and Status Byte for GPS Time Stamping**<br/>
The 6 extra bytes at the end of the HDL-32E data packet are used to report GPS timing. For every packet, the last 6 bytes are formatted as follows:<br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;4 bytes: 32 bit unsigned integer time stamp. This value represents microseconds from the top of the hour to the first laser firing in the packet.<br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;2 bytes: blank*

Unless I am failing to follow the code, [rawdata.cc](https://github.com/ros-drivers/velodyne/blob/master/velodyne_pointcloud/src/lib/rawdata.cc) never touches the time stamp bytes in the data packet.

**Question:** Are we to assume that the time stamp reported by `pcl_ros/bag_to_pcd` is the time the UDP packet was received in software as opposed to the hardware-provided time stamp?
