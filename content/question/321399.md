    \ see this in your code:\n\n>     subscriber = rospy.Subscriber('ipadres',String,callback)\n\
    \nThe `String` there tells your `Subscriber` that it should present the message\
    \ that will be given to `callback` as a `std_msgs/String` type.\n\nAnd as `String`\
    \ has no field `ip`, you get the error message you quote."
