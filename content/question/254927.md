    Your Gazebo setup seems to be incomplete.

    Addition: Be aware that if trajectory start-state validation does not make sense for your use-case,
    you can also just disable it by putting this into your moveit_config's `launch/trajectory_execution.launch.xml`:

    `<param name="trajectory_execution/allowed_start_tolerance" value="0.0"/>`

    There are several possible reasons for this issue:

     1. As v4hn pointed out, there might be no publisher populating the /joint_states topic.
     2. As reported [here](https://answers.ros.org/question/252114/failed-to-validate-trajectory-couldnt-receive-full-current-joint-state-within-1s-error/?answer=276159#post-id-276159), MoveIt might be too busy (e.g. processing perception data) to actually process the joint_states topic.
     3. You are using MoveIt < Kinetic and some joints of your robot are never published on joint_states. MoveIt maintainers are preparing a fix for this.
