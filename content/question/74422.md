    The `frame_id` field represents the "frame this data is associated with" (source: [ std_msgs/Header](http://ros.org/doc/api/std_msgs/html/msg/Header.html)). Messages carrying joint-space information (like in your case) have no frame associated to their data.

    Note that the message definition states that the special value *zero* represents *no frame*, but in practice it's much more common to leave the field unspecified than to set it to zero.
