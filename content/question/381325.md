    There is an [open issue](https://github.com/microsoft/WSL/issues/3023) related to this on WSL GitHub. [This StackOverflow answer](https://stackoverflow.com/a/61739001/7589046) mentions the steps that should solve the problem. Run them with `sudo` inside your Ubuntu environment.

    In [another answer](https://stackoverflow.com/a/61739001/7589046) as well, ```sudo strip --remove-section=.note.ABI-tag /usr/lib/x86_64-linux-gnu/libQt5Core.so.5``` seems to have solved the issue.
