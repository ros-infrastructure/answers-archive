    I assume you are trying to publish a `CollisionObject` to the `PlanningScene`, like in [this C++ tutorial](http://docs.ros.org/api/moveit_tutorials/html/doc/planning_scene_ros_api/planning_scene_ros_api_tutorial.html).

    There is currently no convenience function in the Python [planning_scene_interface](http://docs.ros.org/melodic/api/moveit_commander/html/classmoveit__commander_1_1planning__scene__interface_1_1PlanningSceneInterface.html), but there is an [add_box](http://docs.ros.org/melodic/api/moveit_commander/html/classmoveit__commander_1_1planning__scene__interface_1_1PlanningSceneInterface.html#a1db6e918e333f5c1079c4744ea40045e) method which may already solve your problem.

    If you want to publish other objects to the PlanningScene from Python, have a look at the file's [source code](http://docs.ros.org/melodic/api/moveit_commander/html/planning__scene__interface_8py_source.html), and consider filing a PR.
