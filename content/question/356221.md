  body_markdown: "Published version of ROS book stated to fire up the control node\
    \ using:\n\n```\n./red_light_green_light.py cmd_vel:=cmd_vel_mux/input/teleop\n\
    ```\n\nWhere `cmd_vel_mux/input/teleop` was to be the topic subscribed to the\
    \ `cmd_vel` node. Later Gazebo versions after publication use a new `cmd_vel`\
    \ topic needing to subscribe to the publisher also named `cmd_vel`.\n\nTherefore:\n\
    \n```\nroslaunch turtlebot3_gazebo turtlebot3_house.launch \n```\n\nAnd in a new\
    \ terminal:\n\n```\n./red_light_green_light.py cmd_vel:=cmd_vel\n```"
