  body_markdown: "I am not familiar with the turtlebot simulator, but what matters\
    \ is the type of your topic. \nVelocity messages, as the `/cmd_vel`  produced\
    \ by move_base have the type [Twist](http://www.ros.org/doc/api/geometry_msgs/html/msg/Twist.html)\
    \ .\n\nSo, as long as you have the same topic type, you can always rename the\
    \ topic to match the one expected by your subscriber, using the [remap](http://wiki.ros.org/roslaunch/XML/remap)\
    \ command."
