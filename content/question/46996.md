  body_markdown: "The mapping problem has troubled robots for years now... The corridor\
    \ is not a good place to map. The more landmarks present in an environment, the\
    \ more accurate the map can be. You may have noticed that the results are never\
    \ consistent in mapping. There are always small differences. \n\nFrom my experiences\
    \ with the turtlebot, the best way to improve mapping is:\n\n1. increase the number\
    \ of particles used\n2. increase the update speed\n\nThat's about all one can\
    \ do for the robot... \n\nBest of luck, \n\n-Hunter A. \n"
