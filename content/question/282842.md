    >  Is there a way for me to write the events to a file similar to the /dev/input/js0 file so that ROS Joy can utilize it?

    yes: keep the binary format that you read from `/dev/input/js0` and use something like `uinput` to inject it into the kernel of your control station. Alernatively use `socat` (or a similar tool) to pipe `/dev/input/js0` (on your control station) to a socket on your on-board PC (similar to [this](https://unix.stackexchange.com/questions/201757)). Then have `joy` open that socket instead of an actual device.

    Other than that I would say you have two options:

     1. publish `Joy` msgs on your control station and subscribe those on your on-board pc
     1. use a custom node running on your on-board pc, that, through a custom protocol, gets joystick event data and then publishes it as `Joy` msgs there
