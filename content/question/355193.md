- body_markdown: |-
    @Delb wrote:
    > Yes. You can't launch this node twice, even if it's another group and with a different name.

    I don't want to detract from the main topic here, but you can certainly start `robot_state_publisher` twice -- or more times. There's nothing special about the RSP, it just transforms `JointState`s into `TFMessage`s via FK + the URDF. And multiple publishers to `/tf` is perfectly fine, as long as you take care of clashes (ie: don't publish conflicting information about frames from two different RSPs).

    @Dale:
    > well I thought group tag and tf_prefix can make launch node individual,

    it's not about the `group` tag, but the namespace and/or name of the node. And `tf_prefix` can indeed be used to avoid clashes (although it's deprecated).
