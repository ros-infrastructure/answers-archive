I am using the [Universal Robot Driver for ROS](https://github.com/fmauch/Universal_Robots_ROS_Driver) to control independently two UR10 robots (with their own teach pendant), and it works fine. 

However, I have tried to control them both at the same time with a launch file derived from [this thread](https://answers.ros.org/question/339076/controlling-2-real-ur5-robots/), and the launch aborts when the terminal complains a port is already used. 

I traced it back to PORT and RT_PORT being hard-coded in driver.py of fmauch's ur_driver package. 

How are we supposed to control several arms at once if some ports cannot be changed?
