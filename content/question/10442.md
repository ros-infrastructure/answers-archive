  body_markdown: "The magic trick (*particularly* when dealing with Kinect point clouds)\
    \ is to have serious hardware-accelerated 3D graphics. If you're doing 3D rendering\
    \ in software, it will be agonizingly slow, no matter what. \n\nPut a different\
    \ way: a single Kinect pointcloud message is on the order of 10MB. Multiply that\
    \ by 30fps, and you're putting serious, serious load on your system, even before\
    \ you have to visualize it.\n\nThe [PCL OpenNI wrappers and visualization tools](http://pointclouds.org/documentation/tutorials/openni_grabber.php#openni-grabber)\
    \ might get you better results (although that's outside of ROS proper), but the\
    \ sheer datarate problem isn't going away.\n\nIf you're willing to handle the\
    \ clouds at a lower framerate (not that this will solve your visualization problem),\
    \ take a look at [Topic Tools](http://www.ros.org/wiki/topic_tools), particularly\
    \ the `throttle` node."
