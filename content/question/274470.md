  body_markdown: |-
    Add "`--Rtabmap/CreateIntermediateNodes true`" to [rtabmap_args](https://gist.github.com/matlabbe/5731ce3b2bc25ec5e0295a80c9f5f671#file-stereo_20hz-launch-L39). This will add empty nodes in the graph with their pose between nodes used for mapping. You may not have one pose per image if the odometry cannot process as fast as the images are published.
