    RViz != MoveIt. There is no *direct* connection between RViz and MoveIt's internal models. A visualisation marker is just that, a marker for RViz.

    To get objects registered in the Planning Scene, see fi [ROS moveit programmatically adding collision object](http://answers.ros.org/question/56466/ros-moveit-programmatically-adding-collision-object/), the MoveIt Environment API [examples](http://moveit.ros.org/wiki/Environment_Representation/ROS_API) or [any of the messages](https://groups.google.com/forum/#!searchin/moveit-users/planning$20scene) on the `moveit-users` list.
