    The joint types supported by URDF are (from [wiki/urdf/XML/joint - Attributes](http://wiki.ros.org/urdf/XML/joint#Attributes)):

     - revolute
     - continuous
     - prismatic
     - fixed
     - floating
     - planar

    It would appear that SDF (Gazebo's modelling format) does support them (and they *are* used in ROS (see [this](http://answers.ros.org/question/35525/need-guidance-to-create-a-simple-simulation-in-gazebo/?answer=39724#post-id-39724) answer by John Hsu for example), but this has not been mirrored in / ported to URDF.
