    I assume your problem is controlling the Pioneer remotely and using rgbdslam at the same time. You could try using rgbdslam in [headless mode](http://www.ros.org/wiki/rgbdslam#Usage_via_ROS_service_calls_.28possibly_headless.29) and use the `frame` service call. As for controlling the Pioneer with a joystick remotely, you can try a [multiple machine](http://www.ros.org/wiki/ROS/Tutorials/MultipleMachines) setup with a desktop PC running the joystick node.
