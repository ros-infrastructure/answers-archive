  body_markdown: |-
    Xacro assumes the `<joint>` tag refers to the `xacro:joint` macro implicitly.
    And it complains because your `xacro:joint` doesn't declare any argument named `type`

    Renaming your macro `xacro:joint` to something not colliding with other tags like `xacro:customjoint` should fix the issue
