The function tf2::doTransform does not seem to transform my pointcloud. I made a minimal example to showcase my problem or maybe I am misunderstanding something.

In rviz i subscribe to both topics: "/realsense_zr300/points2" and "/world/source", but the two pointclouds lie on each other, why? Shouldn't they be different because the source is in the realsense frame and the transformed cloud is in the world frame? I also checked the data field of the input and output and they are the same, how can this be if I am applying a transform? The transform sets the frame_id of the output correctly to world, but does not change any value in the data.

      geometry_msgs::TransformStamped C_to_W_transform;
