  body_markdown: " You can see the bindings and change them as you wish in `turtlebot_teleop/scripts/turtlebot_teleop_key`\
    \ (see https://github.com/turtlebot/turtlebot/blob/indigo/turtlebot_teleop/scripts/turtlebot_teleop_key#L53)\n\
    \nFor example, in binding `'m':(-1,-1)`, first element is x velocity, second one\
    \ is angular velocity.\n\nFor your question, \n\n    'o':(1,-1),\n    'u':(1,1),\n\
    \n**u** makes TurleBot move forward and rotate counter clockwise, while **o**\
    \ makes it forward and clockwise. Same thing applies for **m** and **.** and others."
