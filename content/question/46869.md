    \ (on Ubuntu) I've found that adding the following lines to my ~/.bashrc file\
    \ keeps everything for me in working condition:\n\n    source /opt/ros/fuerte/setup.bash\n\
    \    export ROS_PACKAGE_PATH=~/ros_workspace:$ROS_PACKAGE_PATH\n    export ROS_WORKSPACE=~/ros_workspace\n\
    \nand in your case I think you would want it to be: \n\n\n    source ~/fuerte_workspace/setup.bash\n\
    \    export ROS_PACKAGE_PATH=~/fuerte_workspace:$ROS_PACKAGE_PATH\n    export\
    \ ROS_WORKSPACE=~/fuerte_workspace\n\nThat should let you use roscd and other\
    \ ros command line tools with your setup."
