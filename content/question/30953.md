    As the laser scanmatcher only matches the current scan with the preceding one, drift over time can't be avoided when using noisy sensor data (which always is the case in the real world). Depending on your needs you could use a SLAM system like [gmapping](http://www.ros.org/wiki/gmapping) and generate the needed odometry using laser scan matcher. /edit: See [this question/answers](http://answers.ros.org/question/10909/combining-laser_scan_matcher-with-gmapping) for details.

    Alternatively (shameless plug ahead) you can have a look at [hector_mapping](http://www.ros.org/wiki/hector_mapping). Here, there is no need for odometry information and scans are matched against the existing map. For this reason, there is not drift when standing still.
