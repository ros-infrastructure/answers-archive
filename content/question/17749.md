    Considering your robot is your model, you can [get each link](http://gazebosim.org/api/dev/classgazebo_1_1physics_1_1Model.html#aa0a638718d7a3e8b60727ebef48a44e0) by its name and then get the link's pose:


        // robot is a ModelPtr
        auto link1 = robot->GetLink("linkname");
        auto linkPoseWrtParent = link1->RelativePose();
        auto linkPoseWrtWorld = link1->WorldPose();
