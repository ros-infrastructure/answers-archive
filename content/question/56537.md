    Hello seanarm,

    I used the URDF tutorials found [here](http://www.ros.org/wiki/urdf/Tutorials). As for accessing the info, what kind of robot are you trying to model? Different programs access the URDF, for example with my manipulator robot (2arms) I used the arm navigation tutorials found [here](http://www.ros.org/wiki/arm_navigation/Tutorials/tools/Planning%20Description%20Configuration%20Wizard). I've had pretty good success with my robot working in the Warehouse Planner and using real robot data feedback. I've also heard of people using Gazebo as a simulation platform, here are the [tutorials](http://www.ros.org/wiki/simulator_gazebo/Tutorials) for that.

    Best of luck with your research!
