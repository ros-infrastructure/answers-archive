    \ tags](http://sdformat.org/spec?ver=1.7&elem=collision#ode_min_depth) over those\
    \ found in the tutorial. Something like this has worked for me:\n  \n\n      <gazebo\
    \ reference=\"wheel_rear_right_link\">\n        <collision>\n          <surface>\n\
    \            <contact>\n              <ode>\n                <min_depth>0.01</min_depth>\n\
    \              </ode>\n            </contact>\n          </surface>\n        </collision>\n\
    \      </gazebo>\n\nThis took a little digging through gazebo and sdf, but this\
    \ is the [gazebo code](https://github.com/osrf/gazebo/blob/gazebo11/gazebo/physics/ode/ODESurfaceParams.cc#L132)\
    \ that loads the `min_depth` parameter as a surface parameter in gazebo. This\
    \ [sdf code](https://github.com/osrf/sdformat/blob/master/src/parser_urdf.cc#L1368)\
    \ is where the tag `<minDepth>` is parsed. I have not gotten the `<minDepth>`\
    \ tag to work either, but I do know some of those tags listed in the tutorial\
