I am using a [picoflexx sensors](http://pmdtec.com/picoflexx/) and a ros package that wraps up the driver and offers a ros interface. There are two packages, [pico_flexx_driver](https://github.com/code-iai/pico_flexx_driver) and [ros_picoflexx](https://github.com/fmina/ros_picoflexx). I tried both of them (modifying ros_picoflexx to publish camera_info as well) and I noticed that when displaying the pointcloud in rviz, it doesn't match exactly with the reconstructed pointcloud from image depth (DepthCloud). Here is an example:
![image description](https://ftp.osuosl.org/pub/ros/download.ros.org/downloads/se_migration/ros/14797279735406246.png)

In red the pointcloud obtained directly from the sensor as a list of 3d points, in white the depthcloud reconstructed by Rviz using image_depth and camera_info.

I didn't test with other camera models, but could this be caused by wrong intrinsic calibration parameters?
