    Well according to the [documentation](http://wiki.ros.org/velodyne_driver?distro=kinetic) velodyne_node captures Velodyne 3D LIDAR data and publishes it in raw form. This uses the same device driver class as velodyne_driver/DriverNodelet. <br>
    I guess you have been looking at this [answer](https://answers.ros.org/question/213080/convert-raw-velodyne-vlp16-pcap-to-bagfile/). Looks like the next thing yo need to do is convert the  velodyne_msgs/VelodyneScan into sensor_msgs/PointCloud2 by using the `CloudNodelet` from [velodyne_pointcloud](http://wiki.ros.org/velodyne_pointcloud) or the `cloud_node`.
