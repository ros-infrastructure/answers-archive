  body_markdown: "The odom frame is used for providing compatibility with [REP-105](http://www.ros.org/reps/rep-0105.html)\
    \ (and thus, gmapping). Currently, odometry information is otherwise ignored internally\
    \ by hector_mapping, as in the original application it\xB4s use actually lead\
    \ to degradation of performance (due to severe slippage of robot platforms)."
