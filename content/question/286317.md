  body_markdown: "I think you're really asking two questions here: 1) How do you convert\
    \ a geographic coordinate into a coordinate in your boat's map frame, and 2) How\
    \ do you navigate to a point in your boat's map frame. The details of the answers\
    \ will depend a lot on your setup, but I'll give some high level ideas for each.\n\
    \n**For 1:**\n\nIf you are okay with doing some manual work every time you want\
    \ to move your boat, you can convert the lat-long coordinates of the point you\
    \ want to move to into UTM coordinates. Assuming you are localizing your boat\
    \ with GPS, you should be able to convert the UTM coordinates into coordinates\
    \ in your boat's map frame. \n\nIf you want to be able to click a point on a map\
    \ and automatically have your boat navigate to that point, a good starting point\
    \ would be to look at the ROS packages [RVizSatellite](https://github.com/gareth-cross/rviz_satellite)\
    \ and [mapviz](https://github.com/swri-robotics/mapviz), which can both be used\
    \ to display maps (maps are generated using [map tiles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tiled_web_map)).\
    \ Something to note though is that Google map tiles are only officially available\
    \ through their [API](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/tile),\
    \ which you'll need a key to use. There are free map tile providers though (like\
    \ [mapbox](https://www.mapbox.com/)), which you can use with RVizSatellie & mapviz.\
    \ \n\n**For 2:** \n\nOnce you have the coordinates of your desired position in\
    \ your boat's map frame, you should be able to use the existing ros navigation\
    \ stack for sending commands (see tutorials [here](http://wiki.ros.org/navigation#Basic_ROS_Navigation_Tutorials))."

    \ But Point Click Publisher publish point using [geometry_msgs::PointStamped](http://docs.ros.org/api/geometry_msgs/html/msg/PointStamped.html)\
    \ and gps_goal listen for [geometry_msgs/PoseStamped](http://docs.ros.org/api/geometry_msgs/html/msg/PoseStamped.html)\
    \ or [sensor_msgs/NavSatFix](http://docs.ros.org/api/sensor_msgs/html/msg/NavSatFix.html).\
    \ You need modify gps_goal or convert message to required type for your purpose.\n\
    \nTo display Google Maps in Mapviz you can use this [solution](https://github.com/danielsnider/MapViz-Tile-Map-Google-Maps-Satellite).\
