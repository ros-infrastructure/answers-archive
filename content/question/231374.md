  body_markdown: "The only thing to think about, especially that you said you can\
    \ only move in a straight line, that your turtle bot is detecting the 2 torso\
    \ present on it as obstacles.  \n\nThe solution for this is that you have to set\
    \ the position of the kinect in the URDF in the exact place, so that the turtle\
    \ bot will understand that its own torso is not an obstacle.\n\nIf you are a beginner\
    \ and you dont want to mess with the URDF, simply change the range of the angle\
    \ in such away it wont detect the torso (for example start angle 60 degree and\
    \ end angle -60 degrees) \n\nTry one the above solutions, but for sure your turtle\
    \ bot is detecting the torso as obstacles. Good luck  "
