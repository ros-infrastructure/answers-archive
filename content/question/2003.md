  body_markdown: |-
    tf is generated outside gazebo using the robot\_state\_publisher(s) (One for the robot, one for the multisense head). Gazebo publishes joint states of the robot. The robot\_state\_publisher nodes receive those, generate the tf transforms based on them and publish them to tf.
    By running

        rxgraph

    You can look at the computation graph and see the topic connections between gazebo, robot_state_publisher and all other nodes.

    See also the [robot\_state\_publisher](http://www.ros.org/wiki/robot_state_publisher) wiki page.
