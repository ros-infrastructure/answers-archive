    You could look at making a Python [rqt plugin](http://wiki.ros.org/rqt/Tutorials/Writing%20a%20Python%20Plugin).

    If you want a standalone GUI with none of rqt's extra features, here's a really good video series to get you started in 10 minutes:
    [Designing a GUI](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CNgfM2UAReA)

    Unfortunately it doesn't cover ROS integration.

    Either way, I don't think ROSbridge is required unless you want a web interface.
