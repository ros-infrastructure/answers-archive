    \ to. You will see a lot of image related topics. Particularly, you can\n\n> rosrun\
    \ image_view image_view image:=/camera/rgb/image_color compressed\n\nAnd that\
    \ should work. Notice that the *compressed* is optional. `/camera/rgb/image_mono`\
