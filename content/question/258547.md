  body_markdown: "The usual method for saving ROS messages for later processing is\
    \ via the [rosbag record](http://wiki.ros.org/rosbag/Commandline#record) command.\n\
    \nFor velodyne data, it's better to record the [/velodyne_packets topic](http://wiki.ros.org/velodyne_driver?distro=indigo#Published_Topics)\
    \ published by the driver. Those messages contain all the data that are subsequently\
    \ published as a point cloud, but in much more compact form.\n\nFor your camera\
    \ images, you may want to consider saving their compressed format:\n\n * http://wiki.ros.org/image_transport#Published_topics "
