Hello,

I want to tweak or customize the costmap my turtlebot generates such that it assigns higher costs to the cells ahead of a moving person, based on his direction of motion. That is, I want it to predict where the person will be in the next few seconds in order to more intelligently perform its path-planning. 

I will be using the [PCL People Detection Code](http://docs.ros.org/hydro/api/pcl/html/people_2apps_2main__ground__based__people__detection_8cpp_source.html) to first of all detect and recognize that an 'object' is a person. I will be using the [costmap_2d](http://wiki.ros.org/costmap_2d) and [gmapping](http://wiki.ros.org/gmapping) packages for the costmap generation. So once I combine these two, I assume it's just a matter of detecting the direction of motion and then knowing which cells' costs to elevate. 

Any thoughts on how to do this? How do I detect direction of motion? How do I track motion of ONLY 'people objects'? How do I customize and choose which cells to elevate costs?

Thanks.
