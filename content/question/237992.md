    In general, adding '*from [the] internet*' to your question doesn't really add much to [the already asked question](http://answers.ros.org/question/237348/do-i-need-to-use-rosbridge-for-controlling-my-robot-from-my-pc/?answer=237356#post-id-237356).

    If, by '*from [the] internet*', you mean '*on a computer connected to the internet*', networking is already baked into ROS, so rosbridge is not necessary. You need to learn about the ROS_MASTER_URI and possibly the ROS_IP environment variables.

    If, on the other hand, you mean '*from within a web browser*', then yes, [rosbridge ](http://wiki.ros.org/rosbridge_suite) can serve as a tool to help you do that. You can look at the list of [experimental ROS client libraries](http://wiki.ros.org/Client%20Libraries#Experimental_client_libraries); specifically, you'd likely be interested in [rosnodejs](http://wiki.ros.org/rosnodejs). Even more specifically, you should look into [robot web tools](http://robotwebtools.org/), and even more specifically than that, at the *Widgets* section on the [Tools](http://robotwebtools.org/tools.html) page.
