  body_markdown: "I have some general techniques that seem to work with haar classifiers.\
    \ The same may work for you. \n\nIf I'm getting too many false positives, a great\
    \ tool to use is the opencv_createsamples executable. It allows you to generate\
    \ many additional positive samples by stretching, rotating and zooming in/out\
    \ positives. It also throws these samples in other negative images, so your classifier\
    \ learns to ignore backgrounds.  [Here's](http://memememememememe.me/post/training-haar-cascades/)\
    \ a good tutorial for it. \n\nIf I'm not getting enough true positives, I simply\
    \ need more positives examples. These could be just brute-forcing more examples,\
    \ or trying more feature-rich positive examples."
