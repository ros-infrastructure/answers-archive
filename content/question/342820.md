    \nThe issue was that you were creating a `Publisher` in a callback. That will\
    \ not work.\n\nYou'll need to make sure to initialise your publishers and subscribers\
    \ in `main(..)` and then in your callback you use the publisher you created earlier\
    \ to publish the result.\n\n>  i thought it was impossible to declare two node\
    \ in the same main. \n\nyou mean `NodeHandle`s? You don't need two `NodeHandle`s.\
    \ One is sufficient. But you can create as many `NodeHandle` instances as you'd\
    \ want/need.\n\nThe reason you don't see any output is that you're not publishing.\n\
    \nYou'll need to fix that."
