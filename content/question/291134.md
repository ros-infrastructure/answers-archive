  body_markdown: "ROS uses SI units for all its messages so pointclouds should be\
    \ described in meters at all times. \n\nTo answer your question about converting\
    \ from image space (pixels) to world space (m) I think you'll want to know about\
    \ [Camera calibration](http://wiki.ros.org/camera_calibration). This is the process\
    \ of measuring a camera lens so that you know exactly what angle a pixel represents.\n\
    \nConverting calibrated images to 3D information is a very large and complex area.\
    \ I recommend you read up on Stereopsis and structure from motion to get a general\
    \ understanding. "
