  body_markdown: "To get rid of the bad gyro calibration error you need to see the\
    \ has_gyro param to False since you do not have a gyro. If you would like to add\
    \ a gyro you can follow the directions here: http://answers.ros.org/question/717/is-an-imu-required-to-run-turtlebot-stack.\n\
    \nThe kinect_breaker_enable is a boot strapping thing we did to turn on the breaker\
    \ to power the kinect. you can remove it from the base.launch file. But it shouldn't\
    \ effect anything you're doing all it does is come up and request the breaker\
    \ to turn on and then exits.\n\nOn a side note, you should be aware that if you\
    \ do not get good depth information from the bumblebee2 you may not be able to\
    \ navigate well. Also not having a gyro will greatly diminish your ability to\
    \ navigate. \n\n"
