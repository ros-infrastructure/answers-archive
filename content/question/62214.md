    One way to integrate your `Qt`-based GUI and `ROS` is to use [qt_ros](http://www.ros.org/wiki/qt_ros) package. Work on its [tutorial](http://www.ros.org/wiki/qt_create/Tutorials/Qt%20App%20Templates) to get an idea how it works. In a nutshell, that pkg provides an interface component `qnode` where you can pass `ROS` values to `Qt` and vice versa.
