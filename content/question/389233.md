    \ https://github.com/RoboticsYY/moveit_tutorials and found the way to activate\
    \ the OccupancyMapMonitorUpdater for Pointclouds. Just these Settings in the Launch\
    \ file:\n\n    octomap_config = {'octomap_frame': 'camera_rgb_optical_frame',\
    \ \n                          'octomap_resolution': 0.05,\n                  \
    \        'max_range': 5.0}\n    \n    octomap_updater_config = load_yaml('avoid_obstacle_scene',\
    \ 'config/pointcloud_octomap.yaml')\n\nAnd then add the new two parameters to\
    \ your move_group_node."
