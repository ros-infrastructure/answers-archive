  body_markdown: "Check your rqt_graph and ensure that both your scan topic from the\
    \ husky laser scanner, its odometry, the tf between the two, and map topic are\
    \ published and that the amcl node is subscribing correctly. Additionally a properly\
    \ working amcl should update the tf to include a relationship between odometry\
    \ and map. \n\nAlso setup rviz so you can see the map, the transforms between\
    \ the odometry and scanner, and the scan output are displayed correctly. RViz\
    \ will help identify any problems with your configuration with handy colour coding\
    \ of issues. \n\nIf your map topic isn't being published, a simple fix is to save\
    \ a couple of /map messages into a bag topic when using gmapping and getting \
    \ a pretty good map, and simply play this when starting your amcl session. You\
    \ only need one occupancy map message to be published as amcl can latch that or\
    \ use the last map recieved."
