  body_markdown: " I have found a solution.\nThe problem was the wrong odometry data.\
    \ My robot gives the correct odometry position in the odom frame but calculates\
    \ the velocitys for linear.x and linear.y with two timed poses (   new.x - old.x\
    \ / dt    and    new.y - old.y / dt).\n\nBecause i don't know how to get the Ticks\
    \ of the encoders, i just hardcoded the y-velocity to 0 and tested the planner\
    \ once more. \nThe planner works now fine! No /cmd/linear/y values and the movement\
    \ of the robot has improved!\n\nI don't know why, but if the DWA Planner receives\
    \ velocity values in y direction, it will also send /cmd_vel commands in y direction.\n\
    \nNow i only have to correct the calculation of the odometry velocity and i think\
    \ the movement will improve once more."
