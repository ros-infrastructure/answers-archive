    The total cost of a cell in a costmap is calculated from a [set of layers](http://wiki.ros.org/costmap_2d?distro=lunar#Overview) that, together, define the whole costmap. The three most common layers and the associated documentation of all parameters are:

    1. [Static Map Layer](http://wiki.ros.org/costmap_2d/hydro/staticmap)
    2. [Obstacle Map Layer](http://wiki.ros.org/costmap_2d/hydro/obstacles)
    3. [Inflation Layer](http://wiki.ros.org/costmap_2d/hydro/inflation)

    Nearly all the examples you have likely found on GitHub are using defining the parameters in their YAML files according to the documentation on the 3 previous links. Note that there is also an old, pre-Hydro way of defining costmap params that you may still find lurking on the internet, and there are a few additional layers that people have developed. This is all described at the end of the [costmap_2d page](http://wiki.ros.org/costmap_2d?distro=lunar#Component_API).
