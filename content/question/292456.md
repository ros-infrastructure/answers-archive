    If all your nodes are launched by a single launch file, you could make your checking node [required](http://wiki.ros.org/roslaunch/XML/node) and let it stop itself to take down the whole launch file. If you have several launch files, have a required node in each launch and connect the killer nodes with [bonds](http://wiki.ros.org/bond).
