    > Can I do a simple chmod? Or is there a way to run ROS as root? When I run it with sudo it get 'command not found':

    No, you don't need to and you should never (well, never ..) do that. See [Rosrun as super user](http://answers.ros.org/question/189457/rosrun-as-super-user/) (the accepted answer also explains how to solve your problem).

    ---

    Edit:

    > [..] I believe the problem is that the USB to UART driver is installed with root permissions [..]

    Under Linux, drivers are always installed *as root* (directly or via `sudo`), as that is the only user allowed to do that. The permissions of the [*device file*](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Device_file#Unix_and_Unix-like_systems) (in your case `/dev/ttyUSB0`) can be configured separately.
