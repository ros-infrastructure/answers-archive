    This sounds like your newly created package is not in your ROS_PACKAGE_PATH. See these Q/As for more info on that: [1](http://answers.ros.org/question/46501/ros-newbie-and-questions-about-adding-to-ros_package_path/), [2](http://answers.ros.org/question/70435/confusion-with-ros_package_path/), [3](http://answers.ros.org/question/62520/ros_package_path-environment/). You either have to put your new package into a folder that is already in your ROS_PACKAGE_PATH, or add your package folder to it.
