  body_markdown: "Option 1:  \nSee this link for how to multiplex the cmd_vel topic\
    \ instead of having two nodes sending conflicting commands to the robot.\n\nhttp://wiki.ros.org/cmd_vel_mux\n\
    \nIf you see an obstacle, have your node take over control of the multiplexer\
    \ and stop the robot. Of course then you need to figure out how to give control\
    \ back to telop.\n\n\nOption 2:  \nRemap the output of telop to go through your\
    \ node so only your node publishes on the cmd_vel topic. If you see an obstacle,\
    \ modify the telop output running through your node to stop the robot."
