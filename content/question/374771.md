    Found the answer!

    use `--pause`

        <launch>
           <node pkg="rosbag" type="play" name="rosbag_play" output="screen" required="true"
                 args="--pause /opt/robomaker/datasources/mybaggroup_1/myS3prefix/log_0.bag"/>
        </launch>
