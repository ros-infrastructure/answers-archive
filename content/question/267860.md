    \ create a subscriber to the marker\"\n\nWhen you do `rosrun using_markers basic_shapes`,\
    \ this node publishes to topic `/visualization_markers`. You can verify that markers\
    \ are being published by running `rostopic echo /visualisation_markers` in another\
    \ terminal.\n\nTo visualise the marker in rviz, you have to do few additional\
    \ things in rviz:\n\n1. Add display of `Marker` (this subscribes to the marker\
    \ topic)\n2. Change the Fixed frame to `my_frame` (this is specified in line 47\
    \ of basic_shapes node)\n\n[gif illustrating above points](https://drive.google.com/file/d/0B1jURzlre6cAV2lvdmpJRFZWUmM/view?usp=sharing)\n\
