    Probably a network problem. I guess you can ping your robot from the computer running rviz but not the other way around. In that case, rviz can connect to topics published on the robot, but the robot cannot connect to topics on your PC. Have a look at the [network setup page](http://www.ros.org/wiki/ROS/NetworkSetup).
