    You could subscribe to the `/clock` topic. Otherwise, you can get it during an update and store at a local variable.

    `SimTime` was kept out of `ignition::gazebo::World` on purpose, so users would get it from the update callbacks. More info on [this tutorial](https://ignitionrobotics.org/api/gazebo/4.1/migrationworldapi.html).
