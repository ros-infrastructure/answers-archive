    Based on the comments above from @eitan and @xylo.  The problem was that the computer was being overloaded by the navigation stack trying to run too fast.  The solution was to turn down the update rates for the controller and update frequency.  More details of parameters can be found on the [navagation tuning guide](http://www.ros.org/wiki/navigation/Tutorials/Navigation%20Tuning%20Guide)
