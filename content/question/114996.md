    For those who may concern, I solved this problem by:

    1: upgrade to Android Studio 0.4 and Gradle 0.7

    2: using

        compile('org.ros.android_core:android_honeycomb_mr2:0.1.+'){
                exclude group: 'junit'
                exclude group: 'xml-apis'
            }

    instead of

        compile 'org.ros.android_core:android_honeycomb_mr2:0.1.+'
