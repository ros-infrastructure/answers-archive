    Depending on what you need it for, you might be looking for [get_node_base_interface()](http://docs.ros2.org/bouncy/api/rclcpp/classrclcpp_1_1_node.html#a37e5b136ef0a29853e01d5cecb8e4690) and/or one of the other interfaces an rclcpp Node has: there's more context around them [in this PR](https://github.com/ros2/rclcpp/pull/277#issuecomment-264062088).

    We don't use that function in many of our ROS 2 demos but [this is one example](https://github.com/ros2/demos/blob/bfd63e19dbcecd77febd8f1dea573a6d3e047d94/lifecycle/src/lifecycle_talker.cpp#L265) (others are in rclcpp's testing code).
