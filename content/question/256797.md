  body_markdown: "I'm new to this myself, but I resently got DiffDriveController to\
    \ work on my robot. If I understand your problem correctly, I think you need to\
    \ fix the mass / inertia. From what I have read, the identity matrix is a bad\
    \ default for the moment of inertia matrix. \n\nI use these macros in my model:\n\
    \n      <xacro:macro name=\"cylinder_inertia\" params=\"m r h\">\n           \
    \     <inertia  ixx=\"${m*(3*r*r+h*h)/12}\" ixy = \"0\" ixz = \"0\"\n        \
    \                  iyy=\"${m*(3*r*r+h*h)/12}\" iyz = \"0\"\n                 \
    \         izz=\"${m*r*r/2}\" /> \n      </xacro:macro>\n    \n      <xacro:macro\
    \ name=\"box_inertia\" params=\"m w h d\">\n              <inertia\n         \
    \       ixx=\"${m / 12.0 * (d*d + h*h)}\" ixy=\"0.0\" ixz=\"0.0\"\n          \
    \      iyy=\"${m / 12.0 * (w*w + h*h)}\" iyz=\"0.0\"\n                izz=\"${m\
    \ / 12.0 * (w*w + d*d)}\"/>\n      </xacro:macro>\n\n\nMake sure you configure\
    \ the friction of your wheels so they don't slip. Here's a sample from my model.\
    \ You will probably have to play with the values, some of them may be overkill.\n\
    \n      <gazebo reference=\"wheel_FL\">\n        <mu1 value=\"100000.0\"/>\n \
    \       <mu2 value=\"100000.0\"/>\n        <fdir1>0 1 0</fdir1>\n        <minDepth>0.01</minDepth>\n\
    \        <slip1>0</slip1>\n        <slip2>0</slip2>\n      </gazebo>"
