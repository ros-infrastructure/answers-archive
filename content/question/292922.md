    Hi,
    The issue is that you forgot to use the odometry from the hector_mapping.
    In your launch file, add this line or edit the param if exist

     `<param name="pub_odometry" value="true" />`

    From the tf I can see that you don't have odom linked below map.
    Follow [REP 105](http://www.ros.org/reps/rep-0105.html) to make your tf intact.
