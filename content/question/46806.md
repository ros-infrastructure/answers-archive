  body_markdown: |-
    [This](http://wiki.ros.org/rosconsole) page's section about **Console Output Formatting** is what you are looking for.

    Essentially

        export ROSCONSOLE_FORMAT='[${severity}] [${time}]@${line}: ${message}'

    * ${line} for \__LINE__
    * ${function} for \__func__
    * ${file} for \__FILE__
    * ${node} for the node name, no pre-processor equivalent.
