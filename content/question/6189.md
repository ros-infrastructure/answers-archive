Basically I have collisions only with part of the model that are made by primitive shapes and not with meshes. You can see the behavior in the following picture:
![image description](https://ftp.osuosl.org/pub/ros/download.ros.org/downloads/se_migration/gazebosim/13998976912197458.png)
