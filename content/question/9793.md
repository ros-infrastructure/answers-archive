What is the meaning of "scale 0.25 meters"?

![image description](https://ftp.osuosl.org/pub/ros/download.ros.org/downloads/se_migration/ros/13033887979876025.png)

The picture has been generated with package fiducial in object_recognition stack. See function `PoseDrawer` in [fiducial.cpp](http://https://code.ros.org/trac/wg-ros-pkg/browser/branches/trunk_diamondback/stacks/object_recognition/fiducial/src/fiducial.cpp). What came to my mind is that the vectors representing the three axes are of length 0.25m. But alas that's just a guess.
