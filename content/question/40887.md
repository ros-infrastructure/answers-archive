Update:  For some reason I can't add an answer for this post so here is the "solution" to my problem.

Update RVIZ to the latest version, as of a week after I originally asked the question.

I originally had in my post that I suspected the source of my problem was a broken code commit.  This hypothesis was removed by a moderator.  Given the solution to the problem, it is almost certain that my hypothesis was correct.  In the interests of providing others with information to help their problems, I would ask that this solution and explanation is not removed.
