  body_markdown: |-
    You need this in your `CMakeLists.txt`:

        rosbuild_add_boost_directories()
        rosbuild_add_executable(fibonacci_server src/fibonacci_server.cpp)
        rosbuild_link_boost(fibonacci_server thread)

    This [changed in Fuerte](http://ros.org/wiki/fuerte/Migration#Errors_linking_boost_.28e.g._with_Boost_signals_or_thread.29). The tutorial explains it correctly, but you need to click on the `fuerte` button at the top of the page to see the new `CMakeLists.txt` update.
