    I just found out that recently (Sep 2020), a `to_yaml(msg, ostream)` function was added to ROS2 in response to this [issue](https://github.com/ros2/rosidl/issues/259). That means we will just have to wait a bit until that's available in a release. The core function is defined [here](https://github.com/ros2/rosidl/pull/527/files#diff-cdc4e8b43fae0477c12bb259861be7b9908846f0bbf423d80464a42b0fb41548R66) and has this interface

        void to_yaml(const @(message_typename) & msg,  std::ostream & out, size_t indentation = 0)
