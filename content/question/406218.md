  body_markdown: "Make sure to do export all ROS network variables (ROS_MASTER_URI,\
    \ ROS_IP, ROS_HOSTNAME)\n\nYou can put something similar in the .bashrc of your\
    \ ROS MASTER (exchange `wlp2s0` with your device name) \n\n     ip_addr=\"$(ip\
    \ addr show wlp2s0 | grep \"inet \" | awk '{print $2}')\"\n     ip_addr=\"${ip_addr::-3}\"\
    \n     export ROS_MASTER_URI=\"http://$ip_addr:11311\"\n     export ROS_IP=\"\
    $ip_addr\"\n     export ROS_HOSTNAME=\"$ip_addr\"\n\nOn the other computer do\
    \ the same but set the ROS_MASTER_URI by hand\n\nIf this does not work you can\
    \ also add the IP of the master in your `/etc/hosts` file.\n\nSee here for more:\
    \ http://wiki.ros.org/ROS/NetworkSetup"
