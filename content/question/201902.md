    You don't have to compile it by hand. Catkin can compile libraries too.

    The command you need is `add_library()`. Have a look at the [CMakeLists.txt documentation section 7.5](http://wiki.ros.org/catkin/CMakeLists.txt).

    The library then gets put in den lib folder of your devel space.
