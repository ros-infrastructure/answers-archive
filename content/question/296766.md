  body_markdown: "Hey \n\nBasically, Kinematics explain the motion representation\
    \ of the joint frame of the manipulator without considering the force and the\
    \ torque that cause the joint\u2019s motion. Thus, it depends on the robot that\
    \ you want to find the kinematics, {how many degree-of-freedom / and what are\
    \ the joints types (revolute/prismatic)}\n\nYou will not find a straight forward\
    \ answer, you should first understand the idea and build your own kinematics.\
    \ \n\nI recommend you to check some kinematics videos in [HERE](https://www.youtube.com/user/asodemann3/videos)\
    \ you will understand the concept behind it, then you will be able to construct\
    \ the kinematics solutions based on your robot. \n\nI also recommend you to check\
    \ this book,\n\nSpong, Mark W., Seth Hutchinson, and Mathukumalli Vidyasagar.\
    \ Robot modeling and control. Vol. 3. New York: Wiley, 2006.\n\nHope this helps,"
