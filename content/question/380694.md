  body_markdown: "The PointCloud2Ptr is just a pointer to your PointCloud2 datatype.\
    \ The actual sensor data is held in the PointCloud2 message structure and a pointer\
    \ is passed to the callback to prevent duplication of data (saving time and memory)\
    \ and other benefits that CS majors can fill you in. \n\nYou can still access\
    \ your data in your callback using the `->` operator. For eg: `cloud_msg->size()`"
