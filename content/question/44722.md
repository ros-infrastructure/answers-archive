    My guess is that the problem does not have anything to do with your custom message. Instead, I guess it is a network problem.

    I assume that you have a publisher with your custom message running on a PC (host name arturo-desktop), but you didn't set the variable ROS_IP on the PC. The publisher will then advertise using the PC's hostname and the android device doesn't know about that hostname. Have a look at the [network setup](http://www.ros.org/wiki/ROS/NetworkSetup) page.
