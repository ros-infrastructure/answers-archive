    The key issue here is that different versions of boost can't be installed side-by-side, and the ROS binaries depend on the older version of Boost. See [this question](http://answers.ros.org/question/170406/ros-hydro-and-boost-148/) for a more detailed explanation.

    You can use ROS, but you'll have to compile it from source.
