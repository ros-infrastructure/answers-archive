    Use the updated [demo_stereo_outdoor.launch](https://github.com/introlab/rtabmap_ros/blob/master/launch/demo/demo_stereo_outdoor.launch) from 0.10.12, not the one installed by 0.10.10. Just download the launch file, you would not need to rebuild rtabmap from source.
