  I have implemented the "waypoint_nav" package from "https://github.com/nickcharron/waypoint_nav" on the actual Husky A200 robot with Novatel GPS sensor, UM7 IMU sensor, and Lm5xx LIDAR sensor. However, when sending the destination point, the robot does not move correctly. Specifically, the "local cost map" and "global cost map" are skewed and the destination point is shifted accordingly, leading to the robot not precisely following the target destination.
