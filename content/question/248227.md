    $ sudo su - \n\n$ echo 0 > /proc/sys/kernel/yama/ptrace_scope\n\nfound here: http://blog.mellenthin.de/archives/2010/10/18/gdb-attach-fails-with-ptrace-operation-not-permitted/#comment-141535\n\
