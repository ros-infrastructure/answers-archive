     First, every time you receive a message (that is, when the <code>imageCallback</code> method is executed), you are re-creating your publishers, etc. Clearly, that's not what you want to do: all that should happen in the callback (as you have it set up) is image message processing.

    Start there...straightening that out may also take care of your <code>ros.spinOnce</code> in the callback and <code>ros.spin</code> in <code>main</code>.
