    They are both fully supported with drivers readily available. For the Orbbec Astra drivers, see [this package](http://wiki.ros.org/astra_camera). For the Kinect 360 see [this package](http://wiki.ros.org/openni_camera). The better camera is simply the one with the better performance characteristics that suit your application.
