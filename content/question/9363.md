    If you run only the hokuyo node on your 'limited' CPU and have it connected with setting [your environment variables](http://www.ros.org/wiki/ROS/EnvironmentVariables) correctly (look at ROS_IP, ROS_MASTER_URI and ROS_HOSTNAME). Then you can run a roscore on your laptop and visualise all data with rviz. The communication will be handles for you over the published [ROS topics](http://www.ros.org/wiki/ROS/Concepts) as a [sensor_msgs laserscan msg](http://www.ros.org/doc/api/sensor_msgs/html/msg/LaserScan.html).
    You want to export the ROS_IP of your limited CPU on that CPU and also export the ROS_MASTER_URI to http://<your_laptop_ip_or_hostname>:11311
