    Release schedules (or more correctly: dates) are typically listed on the [wiki/Distributions](http://wiki.ros.org/Distributions) page. Specifically in the [Upcoming Releases](http://wiki.ros.org/Distributions#Upcoming_releases) section.

    For Noetic, there is this:

    > The next release of ROS 1 will be called ROS *Noetic Ninjemys*.

    > See [ros-infrastructure/rep#202](https://github.com/ros-infrastructure/rep/pull/202) for proposed updates to [Target Platforms (REP 3)](http://www.ros.org/reps/rep-0003.html) with details on targetted versions of OS, C++, Python and other libraries and tools.

    Both `wiki/Distributions` and `ros-infrastructure/rep#202` show the current planned released date for Noetic to be: May, 2020.

    And it's not really a roadmap per se (more of a TODO list), but [ros/rosdistro#21513](https://github.com/ros/rosdistro/issues/21513) (*Noetic Kick-off and Release Checklist*) has details on the status of the (many) tasks that have to be completed before May.
