    \nFollowing their advice and ahendrix answer I did the following on my Mac and\
    \ it worked \n1) Downloaded https://github.com/ros/class_loader/blob/indigo-devel/cmake/PocoConfig.cmake\
    \ to somewhere on your disk\n2) Added the following lines to CMakeLists.txt  of\
    \ your project\n\n`set(Poco_DIR  _path_)` # replace _path_ with a path to PocoConfig.cmake\
    \ that you downloaded before\n\n`find_package(Poco REQUIRED COMPONENTS Foundation\
    \ Net Netd XML XMLd)` # add other components here\n\n`catkin_package(DEPENDS Poco)`\n\
    \n`include_directories(${Poco_INCLUDE_DIRS})`\n\n`target_link_libraries(node_name\
    \ ${Poco_LIBRARIES})` #replace node_name with the name of your project/node"
