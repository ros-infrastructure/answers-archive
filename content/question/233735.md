    \ USB-to-CAN device.\nYou are right ros_canopen only supports SocketCAN interface.\
    \ If you want to use another kernel driver i.e. ECI you will have to implement\
    \ a custom driver plugin. You could have a look at how socketcan_interface is\
    \ implemented in ros_canopen [**here**](https://github.com/ros-industrial/ros_canopen/tree/indigo-devel/socketcan_interface).\n\
    \nThat being said it seems that your USB-to-CAN device also supports SocketCAN.\
    \ The driver is located [**here**](http://www.ixxat.com/support/file-and-documents-download/drivers/socketcan-driver).\
    \ So you could have a look at it. \n\nAnother question is does your robot use\
    \ OpenCAN protocol? If it does not then ros_canopen package might not be suitable\
    \ for you at all. And even if your robot does support OpenCAN you might have to\
    \ implement additional profiles because ros_canopen for now only supports basic\
    \ (301) and motion control (402) profiles. But I can not comment on that more\
    \ without knowing what robot you are trying to control.\n\nHopefully that helps\
    \ to clarify things a bit."
