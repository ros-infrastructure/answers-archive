    \ that nothing needs to be built.  \n\n2. ROS_NOBUILD is usually used in read\

    \ the rosmake that someone is managing the tree and to ignore it.  You can use\
    \ rosmake -i and rosmake -u to mark and unmark.  If you update the tree, you should\
    \ remove the ROS_NOBUILD flags and then recompile and mark ROS_NOBUILD again.\n\
