  body_markdown: |-
    In my experience there are two reasons for questions not being answered.

    1. The question is not done very well, e.g. missing basic information, formatting, structure, answer is in docs/tutorials/google, etc.
    The [guidelines](http://wiki.ros.org/Support#Guidelines_for_asking_a_question_.28Please_read_before_posting.29) help and in this case an update is a good idea. Also when new information was discovered.

    2. No one that has seen the question knows an answer. To trigger relevant people choose a valid title and use correct tags. Ideally reuse tags that are already in use as some people might selectively only look at tags they care about.
