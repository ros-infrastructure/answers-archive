  body_markdown: "Yes the `createQuaternionFromRPY` constructor is deprecated. But\
    \ you can do it using two lines with the `setRPY` method:\n\n    float roll =\
    \ 0;\n    float pitch = 0;\n    float yaw = 0;\n    \n    tf2::Quaternion myQuaternion;\n\
    \    myQuaternion.setRPY( roll, pitch, yaw );\n\nHope this helps."

What is the recommended way to construct a quaternion from roll/pitch/yaw with tf2? 

The [tf2 wiki page](http://wiki.ros.org/tf2/Tutorials/Quaternions) gives this code:

    tf::Quaternion q = tf::createQuaternionFromRPY(0, 0, 0);

Unfortunately tf itself is deprecated and I'm looking for a pure tf2 solution. I can't find the `createQuaternionFromRPY` function in tf2, although the [migration guide](http://wiki.ros.org/tf2/Migration) says it is a superset of tf. Am I missing something?
