    Assuming the time-stamping in the drivers is correct, both are running on sufficient frequency (whatever that means for your application, i.e. any delays between the two nodes don't lead to significant errors; this is usually the case for high speed cases like drones) and you have your URDF/SDF set up properly so you can actually directly associated a detected object from the camera with the physical location w.r.t. the lidar, you can use the [message_filters](http://wiki.ros.org/message_filters) package to get this done.
    I suggest to use the `ApproximateTime Policy`.
