  body_markdown: "I have this problem, too. In my case, I consider it as a laser problem.(because\
    \ the process always die approximately after loading the laser.)\n\nYou may try\
    \ this:\n\nFirst, replace the `<sensor>` tag of hokuyo in rrbot.gazebo\n\n   \
    \ <sensor type=\"gpu_ray\" name=\"head_hokuyo_sensor\">\nwith\n\n    <sensor type=\"\
    ray\" name=\"head_hokuyo_sensor\">\nSecond, replace\n\n    <plugin name=\"gazebo_ros_head_hokuyo_controller\"\
    \ filename=\"libgazebo_ros_gpu_laser.so\">\nwith\n\n    <plugin name=\"hokuyo_node\"\
    \ filename=\"libgazebo_ros_laser.so\">\n\nSo, after this process, the whole `<gazebo>`\
    \ tag of hokuyo is:\n\n    <!-- hokuyo -->\n    <gazebo reference=\"hokuyo_link\"\
    >\n      <sensor type=\"ray\" name=\"head_hokuyo_sensor\">\n        <pose>0 0\
    \ 0 0 0 0</pose>\n        <visualize>false</visualize>\n        <update_rate>40</update_rate>\n\
    \        <ray>\n          <scan>\n            <horizontal>\n              <samples>720</samples>\n\
    \              <resolution>1</resolution>\n              <min_angle>-1.570796</min_angle>\n\
    \              <max_angle>1.570796</max_angle>\n            </horizontal>\n  \
    \        </scan>\n          <range>\n            <min>0.10</min>\n           \
    \ <max>30.0</max>\n            <resolution>0.01</resolution>\n          </range>\n\
    \          <noise>\n            <type>gaussian</type>\n            <!-- Noise\
    \ parameters based on published spec for Hokuyo laser\n                 achieving\
    \ \"+-30mm\" accuracy at range < 10m.  A mean of 0.0m and\n                 stddev\
    \ of 0.01m will put 99.7% of samples within 0.03m of the true\n              \
    \   reading. -->\n            <mean>0.0</mean>\n            <stddev>0.01</stddev>\n\
    \          </noise>\n        </ray>\n        <plugin name=\"hokuyo_node\" filename=\"\
    libgazebo_ros_laser.so\">\n          <topicName>/rrbot/laser/scan</topicName>\n\
    \          <frameName>hokuyo_link</frameName>\n        </plugin>\n      </sensor>\n\
    \    </gazebo>\n\nI use ros hydro, and this works for me. \n\nPlease feel free\
    \ to suggest any better methods, thanks!"
