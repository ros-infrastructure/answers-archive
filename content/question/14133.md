    A good starting point is [this](http://gazebosim.org/tutorials?tut=plugins_model&cat=write_plugin) tutorial, where you learn how to use [Model::SetLinearVel](http://osrf-distributions.s3.amazonaws.com/gazebo/api/7.1.0/classgazebo_1_1physics_1_1Model.html#a8def5a3fc866e7a180ea81c095353809) to push a model.

    To control joints, you could use:

    * [Model::SetJointPositions](http://osrf-distributions.s3.amazonaws.com/gazebo/api/7.1.0/classgazebo_1_1physics_1_1Model.html#ad17e064c2455e1170b3da05ee2ed42d9) to set the position for each joint in the model.

    * Get the joint controller with [Model::GetJointController](http://osrf-distributions.s3.amazonaws.com/gazebo/api/7.1.0/classgazebo_1_1physics_1_1Model.html#a109b5ce92ab756db3459a17166fa53f9) and hand-pick the PID parameters with [JointController::SetPositionPID](http://osrf-distributions.s3.amazonaws.com/gazebo/api/7.1.0/classgazebo_1_1physics_1_1JointController.html#a28d093602e282cb03caa328396e79164).

    * You can also use the [JointController](http://osrf-distributions.s3.amazonaws.com/gazebo/api/7.1.0/classgazebo_1_1physics_1_1JointController.html) directly instead of calling `Model::SetJointPositions`

    It might be interesting to also take a look at these plugins:

    * [JointTrajectoryPlugin](https://bitbucket.org/osrf/gazebo/src/8f65af9d519d70e03a8f7ea99f669d140001d7d3/plugins/JointTrajectoryPlugin.cc?fileviewer=file-view-default)

    * [DiffDrivePlugin](https://bitbucket.org/osrf/gazebo/src/8f65af9d519d70e03a8f7ea99f669d140001d7d3/plugins/DiffDrivePlugin.cc?fileviewer=file-view-default)

    * [ElevatorPlugin](https://bitbucket.org/osrf/gazebo/src/8f65af9d519d70e03a8f7ea99f669d140001d7d3/plugins/ElevatorPlugin.cc?fileviewer=file-view-default)
