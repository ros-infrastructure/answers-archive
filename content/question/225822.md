    The LocalizerNode is just a wrapper around the particle filter used in [amcl](http://wiki.ros.org/amcl). You can check the documentation there, and there is also a lot of literature on localization methods, likely the best being [Probabilistic Robotics](http://www.probabilistic-robotics.org/).

    To understand how the rest of nav2d works, please refer to the [Tutorials](http://wiki.ros.org/nav2d/Tutorials) and the [API](http://docs.ros.org/hydro/api/nav2d_navigator/html/classRobotNavigator.html)-Documentation.
