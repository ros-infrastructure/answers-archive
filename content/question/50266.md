    There are several bilateral filters in PCL:

     1. [BilateralFilter](https://github.com/PointCloudLibrary/pcl/blob/master/filters/include/pcl/filters/bilateral.h), no defaults.
     2. [FastBilateralFilter](https://github.com/PointCloudLibrary/pcl/blob/master/filters/include/pcl/filters/fast_bilateral.h), default is 15.0f spatial width and 0.05f range sigma.
     3. [Bilateral filter in KinFu](https://github.com/PointCloudLibrary/pcl/blob/master/gpu/kinfu/src/cuda/bilateral_pyrdown.cu), uses 4.5f spatial width and 0.03f range sigma.
