     Probably the easiest way is to manually set the coefficient of friction for the wheels in your model's SDF file.  Here is a tutorial on how to add friction:  http://gazebosim.org/tutorials?tut=friction

    In your case, you might want to set the coefficient to a high number (like 100).  The higher you set this number, the less likely your wheels will slip/slide against the ground.
