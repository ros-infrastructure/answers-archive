  body_markdown: "May be a problem with the path's that gazebo is using. For example\
    \ when you use `model://` to include a file\n\n    <uri>model://yosemite/materials/textures/yosemite.png</uri>\n\
    \nit will look in `$GAZEBO_MODEL_PATH`/yosemite/materials/textures/yosemite.png\n\
    \ to load the image.\nEverything you include with `file://` \n\n    <diffuse>file://media/materials/textures/dirt_diffusespecular.png</diffuse>\n\
    gazebo will search in `$GAZEBO_RESOURCE_PATH`/media/materials/textures/dirt_diffusespecular.png\n\
    \nDid you made sure that these path's and directories are set correct and that\
    \ the png files are in the right locations? You can check the path's with\n\n\
    \    echo $GAZEBO_MODEL_PATH\n    echo $GAZEBO_RESOURCE_PATH\n\n"
