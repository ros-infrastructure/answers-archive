  body_markdown: "I think the key take away here, which paulbovbel might have been\
    \ hinting at, is that the _TYPE field in your Java message interface needs to\
    \ match the type listed in your published topic. \n\nIn your example the Interface's\
    \ _TYPE field listed: \"custom_msgs/vehicle_model_dynamics\";\n\nbut the topic\
    \ actually published messages of type: ros_messages/vehicle_model_dynamics\n\n\
    It is not necessarily the name of the JAR."
