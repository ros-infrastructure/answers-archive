    \ folder and all the libs are installed in the correct place. And in CMakeLists.txt\
    \ for your program after add_executable line, add \nfind_package(OpenCV)\ninclude_directories(${OpenCV_INCLUDE_DIRS})\n\
    and in target_link_libraries, add ${OpenCV_LIBS} too along with catkin libraries.\
    \ And this worked for me and this is specific to ros melodic. I had to install\
    \ specific opencv version and all. But the above changes worked for me!\n\n"
