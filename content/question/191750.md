    To integrate a **custom local planner** with the navigation stack is **fairly easy:** You need to **write a plugin** for [move_base](http://wiki.ros.org/move_base) that adheres to the [nav_core::BaseLocalPlanner](http://docs.ros.org/api/nav_core/html/classnav__core_1_1BaseLocalPlanner.html) interface.

    You can find documentation [here](http://wiki.ros.org/nav_core#BaseLocalPlanner) and as an example you can take a look at the code of the [dwa_local_planner](https://github.com/ros-planning/navigation/tree/hydro-devel/dwa_local_planner) (pay special attention to [dwa_planner_ros.h](https://github.com/ros-planning/navigation/blob/hydro-devel/dwa_local_planner/include/dwa_local_planner/dwa_planner_ros.h) and [dwa_planner_ros.cpp](https://github.com/ros-planning/navigation/blob/hydro-devel/dwa_local_planner/src/dwa_planner_ros.cpp))
