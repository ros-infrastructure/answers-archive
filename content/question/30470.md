    The `<dampingFactor>0.01</dampingFactor>` is a [pseudo-damping term applied by ODE](http://ode-wiki.org/wiki/index.php?title=Manual:_World#Damping), (different from the viscous damping in `<dynamics damping="0.0" friction="0.0" />` which is applied by the `gazebo_ros_controller_manager.cpp` plugin).  Can you try setting both of them to zero and see if that helps?  Also, make sure the door is not touching the floor.
