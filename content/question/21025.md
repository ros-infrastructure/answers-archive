  body_markdown: "Another thing to consider is your joint damping. Some of the values\
    \ in your example (test2) have a joint damping of 0.5. Unfortunately, with the\
    \ default settings, ODE becomes unstable for large damping values like that. The\
    \ easiest thing to do is to add the following snippet in each of your joints:\n\
    \n    <physics>\n        <ode>\n            <implicit_spring_damper>1</implicit_spring_damper>\n\
    \        </ode>\n    </physics> \n\nHere's an answer that also shows how to do\
    \ it in URDF: http://answers.gazebosim.org/question/8317/setting-high-damping-breaks-model/\n\
    \nAlternatively, you can look into using DART as the physics engine. In my testing,\
    \ it was able to run your example without any modifications."

Hopefully someone can help, as I am really stuck here.
