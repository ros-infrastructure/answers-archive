  body_markdown: "No, it isn't. According to wikipedia, *A robot is holonomic if the\
    \ controllable degrees of freedom are equal to the total degrees of freedom.*\
    \ As a ground robot, the degrees of freedom are 3, the x axis, y axis, and rotation\
    \ about the origin. But the controllable degrees of freedom are only 2, the linear\
    \ and angular velocities. "
