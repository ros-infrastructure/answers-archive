    pure\n> virtual\n> \n> get internal force and torque values\n> at a joint.\n>\
    \ \n> The force and torque values are\n> returned in a JointWrench data\n> structure.\
    \ Where\n> JointWrench.body1Force contains the\n> force applied by the parent\
    \ Link on\n> the Joint specified in the parent Link\n> frame, and JointWrench.body2Force\n\
    > contains the force applied by the\n> child Link on the Joint specified in\n\
    > the child Link frame. Note that this\n> sign convention is opposite of the\n\
    > reaction forces of the Joint on the\n> Links.\n> \n> FIXME TODO: change name\

    \ right now\n> \n> Returns\n>     The force and torque at the joint, see above\

As far as I understand this function [from this tutorial](http://gazebosim.org/tutorials?tut=set_velocity#SetJointVelocityUsingJointMotors), it sets a force/torque to the joint such that the resulting acceleration results in achieving the specified velocity within one time step, where the force is saturated to "fmax".

Is there a way to return the force being applied to the joint?
