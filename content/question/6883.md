  body_markdown: "If you have an if statement and you can call a macro within itself,\
    \ you can build recursion with a counter which breaks the recursion as it reaches\
    \ zero.\n\n    <xacro:macro name=\"loop\" params=\"links_qty\">\n\t\t<!-- call\
    \ some macros or do other things -->\n\t\t<xacro:if value=\"${links_qty}\">\n\t\
    \t\t<xacro:loop links_qty=\"${links_qty-1}\" />\n\t\t</xacro:if>\n\t</xacro:macro>\n\
    \n    <xacro:loop links_qty=\"15\" />\n\nI just tested if this works and it does."
