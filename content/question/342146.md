    As far as I know, you can't really publish them simultaneously.  One option may be to make a [custom message](http://wiki.ros.org/ROS/Tutorials/CreatingMsgAndSrv#Creating_a_msg) that contains data for two different point clouds.  Another option may be to assign the same timestamp to the headers of two messages you already have and still publish them separately.  I suppose it depends on what you're trying to achieve and why they need to be simultaneous.
