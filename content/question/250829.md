Kind of continuing from a discussion [Drop support for Wily](https://discourse.ros.org/t/drop-support-for-wily/727/8). 

[MoveIt! release for Indigo is stuck at Ubuntu Saucy](https://github.com/ros-planning/moveit/pull/389), which reached the [end of life in July 2014](http://fridge.ubuntu.com/2014/07/17/ubuntu-13-10-saucy-salamander-end-of-life-reached-on-july-17-2014/). 

Is there a way to make a release ignoring a particular EOL Ubuntu distros that are still supported by ROS (e.g. Saucy for Indigo, Wily for Kinetic)? 

A potential workaround is [suggested](https://discourse.ros.org/t/drop-support-for-wily/727/6) but I'm not quite sure what steps exactly need to happen to do that.

I can't agree more that mending for EOL distros is nothing but a waste of time for all involved maintainers as discussed in the same thread...
