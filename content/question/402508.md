  body_markdown: "ROS packages generally accepts senor_msgs/imu messages. To fill\
    \ the message, you will need:\n\n 1. Quaternion orientation: This is usually done\
    \ by fusing accelerometer, gyroscope and magnetometer. For MPU6050, due to the\
    \ lack of magnetometer, it is not possible to derive \u201Cabsolute\u201D orientation.\
    \ However, it is still possible to derive a \u201Crelative\u201D orientation.\
    \ But without magnetometer, the problem become under constrained, which makes\
    \ the math very tricky.\n 2. acceleration in m/s^2. When the imu sits flat on\
    \ the table, it should measure +9.81 on the z axis.\n 3. Angular acceleration:\
    \ basically the reading from gyroscope in rad/s.\n"
