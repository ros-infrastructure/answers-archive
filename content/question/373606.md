    As to your problem: as stated in the [sensor_msgs/Range](http://docs.ros.org/en/api/sensor_msgs/html/msg/Range.html) documentation, `min_range`, `max_range` and `range` use units of *metres*.

    Does your sensor really have a max range of 350 metres?

    It's likely you're populating the fields with data in centimetres.
