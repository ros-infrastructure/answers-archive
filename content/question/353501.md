**What Could it be? and what I have done so far to solve the problem**

After doing research also about the terminal error  `TF_OLD_DATA` I went to the [official documentation](http://wiki.ros.org/tf/Errors%20explained) and it seems that "The fix for this is to send an Empty message the topic /reset_time. There is a button in rviz to do this."

![reset](https://i.imgur.com/7Jm4Kvz.png)
