    Based on my prior experience, [ar_track_alvar](http://wiki.ros.org/ar_track_alvar) can robustly detect and track QR like tags on AR-Drone 2.0 video feed when the drone is hovering or slowly moving. Parrot Bebop Drone's (the successor to AR-Drone 2.0) video stream is quite stable since it performs on-board video stabilization. You may be able to use `visp_auto_tracker` with Bebop.
