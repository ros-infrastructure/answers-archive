    Quick comment:

     1. no. Only thing needed is for the messages to have their `frame_id` set to something which can be reached from `base_link` and includes your calibrated transform
     1. this is the inverse of your first question. Consequently: yes, RViz transforms clouds such that they are "in the right position".
     1. what you are doing is a good first step. You could add a robot model display to your RViz configuration and let the camera look at the robot. Everything should line up if you've modelled and calibrated things correctly.
