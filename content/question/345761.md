  body_markdown: "There are many ways to build a robot, or make your robot walk.\n\
    \nI have build a very simple quadruped before. Mine had just 4 servos, one for\
    \ each stiff little leg. While this project was fun, using ROS was not the way\
    \ to go. (I used a Atmega microcontroller to control the servos and some LEDS)\
    \ \n\nAfter experimenting with different methods of gait, asked my professor for\
    \ tips. He pointed me to Sine wave Locomotion. This concept worked well on my\
    \ first little robot.  It may be worth looking into for you too. \n\nFor links,\
    \ have a look at a general article on the concept.\n[Wiki explaining sine wave\
    \ ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sine_wave) The second link is a \n[scientific\
    \ article ](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.5772/50979?icid=int.sj-full-text.similar-articles.1&)\
    \ explaining how this concept applies to robotics. "
