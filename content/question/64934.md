  body_markdown: "The Best approach for creating a URDF from a Solid Works assembly\
    \ is to define all the joints and link and reference axes manually. [sw_urdf_exporter](http://wiki.ros.org/sw_urdf_exporter)\
    \ does a decent job while calculating the inertia and mass parameters. However,\
    \ it doesn't work very well with auto-detecting axes and joints.\n\nI'd suggest\
    \ you to\n\n1. Follow a naming convention for each axes, joint and corresponding\
    \ links. \n    Like manually define axis as '_axis_joint1' and joint as '_joint_link1_link2'\
    \ and similarly for link as '_link_name'. \n   \n   The naming convention is up\
    \ to you as long as it's consistent.\n\n2. While selecting the joint links and\
    \ axes in the sw_urdf_exporter menu in solid works, manually add the joints, axes\
    \ and \n    links. This naming convention saves you a lot of time later and helps\
    \ you in Identifying them correctly.\n\n3. Select export after you accumulate\
    \ the tree with the required info and you'll get a good looking URDF.\n"
