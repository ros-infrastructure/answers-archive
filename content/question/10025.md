    Two thoughts:

     1. You'll have the usual powering-the-Kinect problem. Just a USB port isn't enough; see the [Create <-> Kinect Tutorial](http://www.ros.org/wiki/kinect/Tutorials/Adding%20a%20Kinect%20to%20an%20iRobot%20Create) for details on how to build a power supply in a particular case.
     2. The Kinect (both the OpenNI and libfreenect versions) are in C[++], not Java. I don't know if user processes on Android have to be in Java, but just having ROSJava doesn't get you Kinect support out of the box.
