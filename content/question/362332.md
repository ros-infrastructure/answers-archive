  body_markdown: "To get approximate velocities out of a tf system you can do discrete\
    \ differentiation. \n\nYou can compute the pose of the object in question at t0\
    \ and at t1, giving you p0 and p1. Use tf to get those poses in the coordinate\
    \ frame of interest. Once they're in the same coordinate frame then the velocity\
    \ is simply (p1 - p0)/(t1-t0) ala the change in pose divided by the change in\
    \ time.\n\nYou can do the same with the transforms as well, they have slightly\
    \ more information, but I find imagining the poses above to be easier. "
