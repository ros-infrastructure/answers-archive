    Sadly, you can't plot strings in rqt_plot, and the diagnostics messages don't support floating-point diagnostic values.

    You may want to look at the [diagnostic_analysis](http://wiki.ros.org/diagnostic_analysis) package for alternate ways to analyze diagnostic data, or consider publishing your numeric data on a dedicated topic.
