  body_markdown: "If you're writing a library which will be used from another package\
    \ in your manifest.xml you should export required flags, e.g.\n &lt;export&gt;\n\
    \    &lt;cpp lflags=\"-lGL -lglut\" /&gt;\n &lt;/export&gt;\n\nFor the a binary\
    \ in your CMakeLists.txt you could add \ntarget_link_libraries(yourprogram GL\
    \ glut)\n\nor \n\nlink_libraries(GL glut)\n\n"
