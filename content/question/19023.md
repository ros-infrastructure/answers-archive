  body_markdown: "I assume you mean the submarine in the [Hydrodynamics Tutorial](http://gazebosim.org/tutorials?tut=hydrodynamics&cat=plugins).\n\
    \nThe submarine model body spins in the opposite direction of the propeller thanks\
    \ to Newton's 3rd Law.\n\nTwo things you can do:\n\n 1. **Increase the mass of\
    \ the submarine model's body to be >> than the mass of the propeller.** This will\
    \ reduce the effects on the submarine model's body.\n\n 2. **Write your own Plugin\
    \ to apply an equal and opposing torque on the submarine model body** (simulating\
    \ the effects of fins and/or rudder compensation).  \n\nIf you choose to write\
    \ your own Plugin, I recommend completing the [Velodyne Tutorial](http://gazebosim.org/tutorials?tut=guided_i1)\
    \ as well as checking out the source code for the [LiftDrag Plugin](https://bitbucket.org/osrf/gazebo/src/25df4fb10d33a9baaccdf4d48f99ca7fd4f26584/plugins/LiftDragPlugin.cc?at=default&fileviewer=file-view-default)\
    \ and the [Buoyancy Plugin](https://bitbucket.org/osrf/gazebo/src/25df4fb10d33a9baaccdf4d48f99ca7fd4f26584/plugins/BuoyancyPlugin.cc?at=default&fileviewer=file-view-default).\n\
    \n----------\nAlso, (at least for planes), there are a lot of other effects that\
    \ come into play, see: \n\n - https://www.quora.com/What-is-the-gyroscopic-effect-when-an-aeroplane-takes-a-right-turn-and-a-left-turn\
    \  \n - https://aviation.stackexchange.com/questions/32439/why-doesnt-a-single-engine-airplane-rotate-along-the-longitudinal-axis\
    \  \n - https://aviation.stackexchange.com/questions/20909/how-do-propeller-driven-fixed-wing-uavs-counter-negative-torque-from-engine-rot\
    \  \n - https://www.quora.com/Why-dont-single-propeller-planes-need-to-counteract-the-torque-like-a-helicopter-does-with-the-tail-rotor"
