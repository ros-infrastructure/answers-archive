    There is no link called `base_footprint` in your URDF, so you need to select either `laser` or `laser_mount_link` as the fixed frame in RViz. Usually, you would create an URDF for your robot (which would include a `base_footprint` link) and include the laser scanner URDF from there. Have a look at the [Calvin robot URDF](https://github.com/uos/calvin_robot/blob/878f3100082702dd7a82bb50c2674c1742ac4558/calvin_description/robots/calvin.urdf.xacro) and the [URDF tutorials](http://wiki.ros.org/urdf/Tutorials).
