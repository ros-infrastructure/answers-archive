  body_markdown: "So, the -27 for error_code, can be decoded by looking at the message\
    \ for arm_navigation: http://ros.org/doc/api/arm_navigation_msgs/html/msg/ArmNavigationErrorCodes.html\n\
    \n-27 apparently == FRAME_TRANSFORM_FAILURE\n\nWhich I presume means you have\
    \ some sort of TF problem.\n\nIs your turtlebot arm on a turtlebot? After electric,\
    \ with the release of turtlebot 2, much (if not all) of turtlebot core stacks\
    \ got completely ripped up and changed -- and so something in the URDF might not\
    \ be properly connected, or some joint state may not be published. \n\nIn addition,\
    \ with the move to MoveIt, arm_navigation is really no longer maintained or developed\
    \ (and MoveIt was not really ready for users until Groovy). Unfortunately, this\
    \ combo leaves the Fuerte turtlebot_arm a bit of a mess.\n\nI've recently started\
    \ to move the turtlebot_arm to Groovy/MoveIt, with a custom ikfast plugin to replace\
    \ my hacky old simple_arm_server. Unfortunately it is just a side project, and\
    \ so progress has been intermittent."
