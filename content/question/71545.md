  body_markdown: "You should first configure your environment variables on your both\
    \ computer, Turtlebot and workstation. \nPut Turtlebot computer as master on both\
    \ station with:\nexport ROS_MASTER_URI=http://turtlebot_ip like:\n\n    export\
    \ ROS_MASTER_URI=http://192.168.1.101:11311\n\nIn the workstation do also:\n\n\
    \    export ROS_HOSTNAME=workstation_ip\n\nIn your Turtlebot laptop:\n\n    export\
    \ ROS_HOSTNAME=turtlebot_ip\n    export ROS_IP=turtleot_ip\n\neven local-host\
    \ should be defined explicitly.\n\nCheck those links for more documentation:\n\
    http://www.ros.org/wiki/ROS/EnvironmentVariables\nhttp://www.ros.org/wiki/ROS/Tutorials/MultipleMachines"
