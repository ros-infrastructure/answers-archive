  - body_markdown: |-
      `/rtabmap/cloud_map` doesn't have normals published. When doing File->Export Clouds, normals are adjusted depending on each camera point of view. See [adjustNormalsToViewPoints()](https://github.com/introlab/rtabmap/blob/cad184e82bef25b90967ab88ebab5e99a7ce26bf/corelib/src/util3d_surface.cpp#L3481-L3525). For each point, we search the camera looking at it, then flip the normal is needed.
