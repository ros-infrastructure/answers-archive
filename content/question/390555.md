  body_markdown: "I believe you have two options: \n\n - the `min_height` and `max_height`\
    \ [parameters](https://github.com/ros-perception/pointcloud_to_laserscan/blob/lunar-devel/src/pointcloud_to_laserscan_nodelet.cpp#L62-L63)\
    \ to change the range of points from the cloud that are converted into the scan,\
    \ or\n - the `target_frame` [parameter](https://github.com/ros-perception/pointcloud_to_laserscan/blob/lunar-devel/src/pointcloud_to_laserscan_nodelet.cpp#L60)\
    \ to change from whatever the default frame (e.g. `lidar_frame`) to the frame\
    \ you're interested in (e.g. `base_link`)\n\nThe latter is probably the more appropriate\
    \ solution, but its worth being aware of the min and max height parameters as\
    \ well"
