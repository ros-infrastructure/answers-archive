    To answer your question in the comment, you can use `ament_target_dependencies` to link against other ament packages ([for example](https://github.com/ros2/rclcpp/blob/01d03c5d4e5d9b141240ca8c9077fdbc381d8c28/rclcpp/CMakeLists.txt#L390-L395)). But if you are linking against a target in the same project, there's no ament command that I am aware of. `target_link_libraries` is the way to do it (as you have done in your posted cmake code).
