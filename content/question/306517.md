    In typical setups the joint states of a robot are published on a `joint_states` topic with messages of type [sensor_msgs/JointState](http://docs.ros.org/api/sensor_msgs/html/msg/JointState.html).

    Those would allow you to be notified of the current pose (in joint space) of a robot, simulated or not.
