  body_markdown: "Hi,\n\nyou could check for contacts between the cups and the object,\
    \ when contact is detected you can dynamically create a fixed joint between the\
    \ two links to simulate the vacuum, thus the object will follow the movement of\
    \ the vacuum cup. \n\nIf the idea suits you, check out the `gazebo/physics/Gripper.cc`\
    \ example where fix joints are created. For detecting the contact use a contact\
    \ sensor, [here](http://gazebosim.org/tutorials/?tut=contact_sensor) is a tutorial.\n\
    \nCheers,\nAndrei\n\n"
