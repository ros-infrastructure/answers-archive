    packages.ros.org has been migrated to a new host and in the process we have had to update services.  http://prerelease.ros.org/rosinstall is an attempt to revive the rosinstall generators. It appears to be broken at the moment and we'll try to revive it when time is available.  Fuerte and earlier distributions have passed the end of their support cycle and are not supported anymore so this will be a low priority.  In addition the web service overall has been deprecated in favor of a simple command line tool [rosinstall_generator](http://ros.org/wiki/rosinstall_generator)
