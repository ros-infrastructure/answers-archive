    There are a lot of ways to teleop your robot. You'll have to choose the one that fits your needs best.

    As for converting existing ROS nodes into Java, it's often possible but rosjava is still in alpha (i.e. the API is subject to change and does frequently) and is missing some features found in roscpp/rospy. See the [rosjava wiki](http://www.ros.org/wiki/rosjava) page and [javadoc](http://rosjava.googlecode.com/hg/rosjava/doc/index.html).
