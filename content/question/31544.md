    Its labelling everything as a person for me as well (see the image).

    But I have a solution, don't use ppl_detection, use [openni_tracker](http://ros.org/wiki/openni_tracker) with the latest openni and nite libraries instead. It can automatically find people, is orders of magnitude more accurate than [ppl_detection](http://ros.org/wiki/ppl_detection) and tracks skeleton data too. [This](http://igorbarbosa.com/articles/how-to-install-kin-in-linux-mint-12-ubuntu/) is a good tutorial on how to set it up.
