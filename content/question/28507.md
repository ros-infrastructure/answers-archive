    This BadDrawable error is generally related to issues with either your GPU being too old or buggy drivers. See the answers to [rviz problem with ubuntu 10.04](http://answers.ros.org/question/12711/rviz-problem-with-ubuntu-1004) and [rviz crashing at startup with baddrawable](http://answers.ros.org/question/11809/rviz-crashing-at-startup-with-baddrawable). Also see the [RViz troubleshooting page](http://www.ros.org/wiki/rviz/Troubleshooting#Segfault_during_startup).

    Since you are only having problems after closing it, I would guess something is leaving your graphics driver in a bad state. Try changing up the RTT mode as referenced in the above answers to see if that fixes things.
