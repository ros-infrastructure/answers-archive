  body_markdown: "I've been having trouble with that, too.\n\nTurns out you can escape\
    \ '(' with \"%28\" and ')' with \"%29\", the ASCII hex values. The close parenthesis\
    \ is all that's really required, but the other helps to make things look less\
    \ \"unbalanced\".\n\nEDIT: \n\nEven with that escape, it can be tricky. Sometimes\
    \ the link works while editing, yet fails after the edit is committed.\n\nWe should\
    \ probably open a ticket on this behavior."
