    The orientation you have defined for your camera's optical frame does not match the ros convention. The optical frame has its origin at the image sensor, with +z forward, +x to the right and +y downward. This is documented in [REP103](https://ros.org/reps/rep-0103.html#coordinate-frame-conventions).

    See also #q395930.
