  body_markdown: "If you don't provide the full output, you may miss important things\
    \ that happen during the build. If the information is not logged it is gone because\
    \ you don't have access to the build environment for introspection afterwards.\
    \ \n\nWhen viewing a failed build I usually start by searching for \"error\" caps\
    \ insensitively and find what the error is and work back from there. Using this\
    \ technique, especially searching from the bottom it usually takes only a couple\
    \ of seconds to find the failure in a log. \n\n"
