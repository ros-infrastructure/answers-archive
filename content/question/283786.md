    Update from comments by @malharjajoo:

    [Camera info](http://wiki.ros.org/image_pipeline/CameraInfo) parameters are described and illustrated at that link.

    You can see some illustrations at [stereo_image_proc](http://wiki.ros.org/stereo_image_proc) that show left and right are with respect to the cameras, not to any external reference frame such as an observer.

    From [OpenCV](https://docs.opencv.org/2.4/modules/calib3d/doc/camera_calibration_and_3d_reconstruction.html) the projection matrices are P1 and P2 as described in [cv::stereoRectify()](https://docs.opencv.org/2.4/modules/calib3d/doc/camera_calibration_and_3d_reconstruction.html#stereorectify),
     `P = [K | [Tx 0 0]'`.
