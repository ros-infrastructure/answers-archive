    The underlying problem is`time_increment: 0.0`. `time_increment` needs to be initialized to the correct value or else the unskewing of the cartographer won't work correctly. A good approximation could be `time_increment = scan_time / ranges.size()`.

    For more information addressing this issue, you can check the [docs](http://docs.ros.org/en/api/sensor_msgs/html/msg/LaserScan.html).
