    Hi @horseatinweeds

    After doing some tests with your package I found a several problems that, once fixed, let me display and control the base on Gazebo.

    First of all, you need to take care of the tag `<selfCollide>` in the gazebo reference, because is causing self colliding problems in your model.

    Next, you will need to change the VLP-16 description to adjust to your environment: you are loading a `samples:=187`5 Lidar, that is huge depending on the machine and resources allocate by Gazebo, Try to lower that number and launch the simulation. With those fixes you will be able to launch it and Gazebo will have enough time to load the controllers.
