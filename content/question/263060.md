  body_markdown: "As @yzheng0310 said, you must use the class that defines your service.\n\
    \nLet's suppose that the service used by `/camera/start_capture` is a `StartCatpure`\
    \ that is defined in your package, and let's suppose that your package is named\
    \ `my_package`.\n\nIn this case you would do the following:\n\n    import rospy\
    \ \n    from my_package.srv import StartCatpure\n    start_capture = rospy.ServiceProxy('start_capture_node',\
    \ StartCapture)\n\nNow, let's call the service. In this case, supposing that the\
    \ service doesn't accept parameters:\n\n`start_capture()`\n\n\nYou can check the\
    \ ROS Tutorial [Writing a SImple Publisher and Subscriber in Python](http://wiki.ros.org/ROS/Tutorials/WritingPublisherSubscriber%28python%29)\
    \ that explains step by step."
