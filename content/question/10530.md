    Whenever you want to use code from packages other than the one you are developing in, you need to add dependency on that package to your manifest.xml (see [manifest documentation](http://www.ros.org/wiki/Manifest) for full description). And to answer your question adding ``<depend package="opencv2"/>`` to the manifest should solve the problem. Also your slashes are wrong, change all ``\`` to ``/``:

        #include <opencv2/opencv.hpp>
        #include <opencv2/highgui/highgui.hpp>
        #include <opencv2/imgproc/imgproc.hpp>
