  body_markdown: "You didn't mention which version you're using in your question so\
    \ I'm assuming that you're using the 2.0. While your drone is on a flat surface,\
    \ run\n\n    rosservice call /ardrone/flattrim\n\nFrom the [docs](https://ardrone-autonomy.readthedocs.io/en/latest/services.html#flat-trim)\n\
    \n> ## Flat Trim\nCalling `ardrone/flattrim` service without any parameter will\
    \ send a \u201CFlat Trim\u201D request to AR-Drone to re-calibrate its rotation\
    \ estimates assuming that it is on a flat surface. Do not call this service while\
    \ Drone is flying or while the drone is not actually on a flat surface.\n\nIf\
    \ this still doesn't work, then try the calibration command from the phone app\
    \ (again, while on a flat surface).\n\n\n----------\n**Edit:**\n\nIf the lights\
    \ are red then you probably have an error due a high acceleration (even carrying\
    \ the drone can cause this) or rotating the drone too much. Publish an `std_msgs/Empty`\
    \ message to the `ardrone/reset` topic to reset it and then flat trim."
