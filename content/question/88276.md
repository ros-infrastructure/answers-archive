    I know you want a solution without rqt_logger_level. Although I still refer to it, this answer doesn't depend on it.

    Get a list of node names by `rosnode list`. Then, as in `rqt_logger_level.LoggerLevelServiceCaller.send_logger_change_message`, you can iteratively [call rosservice.get_service_class_by_name](https://github.com/ros-visualization/rqt_common_plugins/blob/groovy-devel/rqt_logger_level/src/rqt_logger_level/logger_level_service_caller.py#L114) to get a corresponding service to the given `node` name.

    HTH.
