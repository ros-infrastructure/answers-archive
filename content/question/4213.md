    1. A PID position or velocity controller is built into Gazebo for every joint. These controllers will do nothing until you send them a command.

    1. See above.

    1. The gain limit you refer to is just a GUI limit. You can send a message to Gazebo with any gain.

    1. Look [here](https://bitbucket.org/osrf/gazebo/src/b2d2cf062c2b09177badd4a63a1aa5a4e6791485/gazebo/physics/JointController.cc?at=default#cl-88) for position control, and [here](https://bitbucket.org/osrf/gazebo/src/b2d2cf062c2b09177badd4a63a1aa5a4e6791485/gazebo/physics/JointController.cc?at=default#cl-103) for velocity control.

    1. Only one force is applied to a joint in the following order of precedence: Force, Position PID, Velocity PID.

    1. Search the [wiki](http://gazebosim.org/wiki/Tutorials/intermediate/pid_control_joint), and look in the [API](http://gazebosim.org/api/1.9.0/classgazebo_1_1physics_1_1JointController.html)

    1. If you add another controller (say from ROS), then they two will conflict. You should really only use only controller at a time.
