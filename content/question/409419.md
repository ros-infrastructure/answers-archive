Hi,

I've been able to reproduce this problem on at least 5 different turtlebot3s, using Foxy.

I run:

`ros2 launch turtlebot3_bringup robot.launch.py`

`ros2 run turtlebot3_teleop teleop_keyboard`

`ros2 bag record -a`

and nothing else.

I run the robot around for a bit, logging its activity into the rosbag, then view what comes up. Occasionally, the `joint_states`wheel positions will jump to crazy values, as if the bot had teleported kilometers away, and sometimes beyond the edge of the known universe (out to `7e+224`).

Here's a plotjuggler graph showing how the `wheel_left_joint` position is jumping, and the effect it has on `odom`, which in turn affects the `odom->base_link` transform.

![image description](https://ftp.osuosl.org/pub/ros/download.ros.org/downloads/se_migration/ros/166880425440819.png)

The scale of change of `wheel_left_joint` is obscuring the full detail. `wheel_left_joint` jumps back to its original position, but `odom` does not.

![image description](https://ftp.osuosl.org/pub/ros/download.ros.org/downloads/se_migration/ros/1668804362883135.png)

This in turn causes the transform to jump.

![image description](https://ftp.osuosl.org/pub/ros/download.ros.org/downloads/se_migration/ros/16688043852917875.png)

Has anyone seen this? Know what is going on?

Also, is there anything that can be done about it? Any localization I do going through the `base_link -> odom` step in the transform gets ruined by this. Is there a way to take the `odom` out of the transform tree for a turtlebot? I'm using cartographer, so not sure if that is able to step up to fill in the missing functionality.

The velocity is also jumping, but only sometimes at the same time as the wheel position, not consistently.
![image description](https://ftp.osuosl.org/pub/ros/download.ros.org/downloads/se_migration/ros/16690571559147654.png)
