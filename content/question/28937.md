Dear all,

I was looking into the conveyor demo and specifically [why it didn't work](https://github.com/gazebosim/gz-sim/issues/1662#issuecomment-1485840870). As it turns out it is due to a patch not having been merged until `dart6.13`.

Anyway, before I realized that I had been looking extensively at the source code, and there's a certain mechanism I'd like to understand better:

Given a dart version < 6.13, `DART_HAS_CONTACT_SURFACE` is not set, so [this featurelist](https://github.com/gazebosim/gz-physics/blob/ee36344af9e5049ee72e525d22e02b891cd863fa/dartsim/src/SimulationFeatures.hh#L54-L60) does not contain `SetContactPropertiesCallbackFeature`, and consequentially neither the [DartsimFeatures](https://github.com/gazebosim/gz-physics/blob/ee36344af9e5049ee72e525d22e02b891cd863fa/dartsim/src/plugin.cc#L37-L49).

This results in [this EntityCast](https://github.com/gazebosim/gz-sim/blob/3592c201094b94b36a3edbd1f03f89d959f4e578/src/systems/physics/Physics.cc#L3635-L3639) returning `nullptr`.

Can anyone clarify this a bit?

It seems that:

- EntityCast [calls RequestFeatures::From](https://github.com/gazebosim/gz-sim/blob/3592c201094b94b36a3edbd1f03f89d959f4e578/src/systems/physics/EntityFeatureMap.hh#L129-L130),
- Which is implemented [here](https://github.com/gazebosim/gz-physics/blob/cdeb0a782524b95f9fde3179579d964e50dcc529/include/gz/physics/detail/RequestFeatures.hh#L34-L70).

I didn't dig into that further, as it is rather hard to read (for me at least :-) and it answers the `how is the cast done` question, but not `where is the relation between the world entity and the DartsimFeatures defined`? 

 I think [this might be it](https://github.com/gazebosim/gz-sim/blob/d2475ff905c31d27247d2d9e85f0d6a8f813e0e2/src/systems/physics/Physics.cc#L982-L983), but I don't see how the `worldPtrPhys` is linked to the DartsimFeatures.

Can anyone clarify this?
