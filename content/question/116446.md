    \ You can also try both of these commands:\n\n    rosrun tf tf_echo base_link\

    \ show a transfom, your static publisher does not work. if the second one fails,\
    \ your TransformBroadcaster is the culprit.\n\n/edit: As you can see from the\
    \ posted frames.pdf, the transform between odom and base_link published to tf\
    \ is 44 years old, while the transform published to by the static publishers is\
    \ timestamped ~0.5 seconds into the future (as it should be). \nThis suggests\
    \ you don\xB4t timestamp the data when publishing via the TransformBroadcaster\
    \ correctly.\n"
