  body_markdown: "Hi Shuo,\n\nThis problem is caused by a change in ROS between electric\
    \ and fuerte.  The rostime module was moved from roslib to rospy.  Thus, when\
    \ importing roslib.rostime, we now get an ImportError\n\nWe have implemented a\
    \ fix for this and it has been checked into the experimental version of rosbridge.\
    \  As you mentioned in your post, you can check this out using \n\n    svn co\
    \ http://brown-ros-pkg.googlecode.com/svn/trunk/experimental/rosbridge rosbridge\n\
    \nWith regard to your svn error, I think you might be trying to SVN checkout into\
    \ a folder that is already under SVN version control.  Please try checking out\
    \ experimental rosbridge into a fresh folder.  \n\nFinally, since you will probably\
    \ have multiple rosbridge packages on your ROS package path, do a quick check\
    \ to make sure that the experimental rosbridge is the first rosbridge that ROS\
    \ finds.  (Just roscd rosbridge and make sure that takes you to the folder that\
    \ you just created)\n\nThis should fix your problem.\n\nCheers,\nJon"
