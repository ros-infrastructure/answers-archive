  body_markdown: |-
    As @moritz said, use roslaunch. The [launch file](http://www.ros.org/wiki/roslaunch/XML/node) you provide will look something like this:

        <launch>
          <node pkg="re_kinect_object_detector" type="re_kinect" name="re_kinect" />
          <node pkg="re_object_detector_gui" type="detect" name="detect" />
        </launch>

    UPDATE: there is more to creating a source overlay than just adding to your $ROS_PACKAGE_PATH. The [recommended approach](http://www.ros.org/wiki/fuerte/Installation/Overlays) is to use **rosws**.
