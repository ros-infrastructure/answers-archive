    If your Distribution does not have a OpenNi2-package, you need to build [OpenNi2](https://github.com/OpenNI/OpenNI2) from source and add a pkgconfig-file, e.g. /usr/local/lib/pkgconfig/openni2.pc:

        prefix=/usr/local/OpenNI2
        includedir=${prefix}/Include
        libdir=${prefix}/Bin/Arm-Release

        Name: openni2
        Description: OpenNI2
        Version: 2.2.0.33
        Cflags: -I${includedir}
        Libs: -L${libdir} -lOpenNI2
