  body_markdown: "For exploration, you don't even need to create a map in advance.\
    \ \n\nYou can launch hector_slam, the [hector_exploration_planner](https://github.com/tu-darmstadt-ros-pkg/hector_navigation/blob/catkin/hector_exploration_node/launch/exploration_planner.launch),\
    \ and the [hector_exploration_controller](https://github.com/tu-darmstadt-ros-pkg/hector_navigation/tree/catkin/hector_exploration_controller/src).\
    \ The result will be simultaneous exploration and mapping.\n\nFor navigation,\
    \ you can use your map with the standard ROS navigation stack. See http://wiki.ros.org/navigation/Tutorials"
