    Well, to accomplish these tasks you need some lines of code...

    I think that ROS/GazeboSim is the best alternative to accomplish these tasks. You will need to learn how to use path planning (deliberative navigation) and reactive navigation. There are some stacks in ROS that will facilitate your job. Take a look in motion planning (OMPL stack) and navigation stack [Motion planners](http://www.ros.org/wiki/motion_planners) and [Stack navigation](http://www.ros.org/wiki/navigation)

    If your environment is static, probably you know the "map" of environment and you can use a motion planning algorithm to compute the path between the two points. Once the path was planned the next step is execute the actions that will take the robot until the target. So you can collect data sensors using ros-enabled model plugins . Take a look: [Gazebo tutorials](http://gazebosim.org/wiki/Tutorials) and [ROS-Enabled Plugins](http://gazebosim.org/wiki/Tutorials/1.4/ros_enabled_model_plugin)
