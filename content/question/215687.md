    No idea if this is related to your issue, but I was seeing something similar (also 14.04 and Mega2650). As it turns out, I had debugging turned on with a 3rd-party (i.e., non-ROS, non-arduino-supplied) object that was attempting to send data over the USB (as you know, on a Mega2650 board, no ethernet or bluetooth).

    As was obvious to me once I realized what was happening, when another object fills the serial line with data, ROS won't be able to sync. Since your board *does* connect (at least sometimes), it can't be quite that easy; but, since your question was &quot;*Any ideas?*&quot;, maybe the data line is being utilized by a non-ROS object?
