    The hokuyo model uses a [ray sensor](http://sdformat.org/spec?ver=1.6&elem=sensor#sensor_ray). The two parameters you're looking for are `<min_angle>` and `<max_angle>`. The reason you're only getting 180 degrees of readings is because the author of the tutorial set the min and max angle  to -90 and 90 degrees [(see the values here -1.57 and 1.57 radians)](https://github.com/richardw05/mybot_ws/blob/master/src/mybot_description/urdf/mybot.gazebo#L96). To get 360 degrees of range change the minimum angle to -pi radians, and the maximum angle to +pi radians.
