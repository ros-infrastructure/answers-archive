  body_markdown: "hello, from my experience, I had to increase the joint damping to\
    \ stabilize the simulation, and also set the parameter [implicitSpringDamper](http://gazebosim.org/tutorials?tut=ros_urdf&cat=connect_ros#Joints)\
    \ to true. When set to false (default), too high damping coefficient also lead\
    \ to unstable simulation. I also noticed that increasing the mass of lightweighted\
    \ pieces helped. \n\nBy the way i'm using ode. Dart was a little more stable,\
    \ but it is not as integrated in gazebo as ode (it does not take into account\
    \ some parameters in sdf/urdf)."
