    I added a bit on the wiki showing what to do specifically: [rviz/Troubleshooting#Segfault_during_startup](http://www.ros.org/wiki/rviz/Troubleshooting#Segfault_during_startup), but if you're still trying to figure it out, OGRE_RTT_MODE is just an environment variable, which you can set from the command-line like so:

        export OGRE_RTT_MODE=Copy

    or:

        export OGRE_RTT_MODE=FBO
