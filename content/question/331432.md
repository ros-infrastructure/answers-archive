    \ adjustments :\n\n - Your `cameras.urdf.xacro` should actually use some macros\
    \ to receive the parameters :\n\n          <?xml version=\"1.0\"?>\n         \
    \ <robot name=\"camera\" xmlns:xacro=\"http://ros.org/wiki/xacro\">\n        \
    \  <xacro:macro name=\"camera\" params=\"tf_prefix_camera1 tf_prefix_camera2\"\
    >\n              <link name=\"${tf_prefix_camera1}_pose_frame\"/>\n          \
    \    <link name=\"${tf_prefix_camera2}_link\"/>\n    \n              <joint name=\"\
    ${tf_prefix_camera1)_to_${tf_prefix_camera2)\" type=\"fixed\">\n             \
    \   <parent link=\"${tf_prefix_camera1}_pose_frame\"/>\n                <child\
    \ link=\"${tf_prefix_camera2}_link\"/>\n                <origin xyz=\"0.009 0.021\
    \ 0.027\" rpy=\"0.000 -0.018 0.005\"/>\n              </joint>\n          </xacro:macro>\n\
    \          </robot>\n\nThe important parts here are `xmlns:xacro=\"http://ros.org/wiki/xacro\"\
    ` to properly use the macro and to define the macro `camera` like a function,\
    \ i.e. specifying its arguments (here its params).\nNote also that it's not like\
    \ in the launch files, `$(arg ARG)` you just need brackets around the name of\
    \ your param.\n\n - In your other file you just need :\n\n        <?xml version=\"\
    1.0\"?>\n        <robot name=\"camera\" xmlns:xacro=\"http://ros.org/wiki/xacro\"\
    >\n        <xacro:include filename=\"$(find YOUR_PKG)/urdf/cameras.urdf.xacro\"\
    />\n        <camera tf_prefix_camera1=\"t265\" tf_prefix_camera2=\"d435\"/>\n\
    \        </robot>\nIn your example you didn't include `cameras.urdf.xacro` but\
    \ another urdf file is this a copy/paste mistake ?\nTo call the macro you have\
    \ previously defined, you just need a tag with the name of your macro and simply\
    \ assign a value to your parameters. That will simply copy and paste everything\
    \ from the `camera.urdf.xacro` file between the `<xacro>` tag (so everything except\
    \ the 2 first lines and the last one) and replace the paramaters with their value.\n\
