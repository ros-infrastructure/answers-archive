    gazebo 7.x uses gazebo topics instead of ign-transport topics. There is plan to replace the gazebo transport layer with ign-transport sometime in the future. If you're using gazebo through ROS, you can just publish to the [ROS topics](http://wiki.ros.org/gazebo#gazebo.2BAC8-fuerte.ROS_API_Plugin) provided by gazebo_ros_pkgs.
