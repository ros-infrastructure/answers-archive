  body_markdown: "I finally succeeded after 2 weeks of slipping. The problem was that\
    \ although i was using an effort controller for my grippers, because i wasn't\
    \ for the rest of the arm, gazebo was unable to calculate any lifting force (i\
    \ don't know the specifics, this is just an observation).\n\nI was using a gazebo\
    \ service to map the movements of a ur5 from another simulation to gazebo, since\
    \ there was no need for 2 controllers per joint. Now i am using trajectory control\
    \ to map those same join states, which did require a bit of pid tuning.\n\nAlso\
    \ i have frictional coefficients of 10 for mu1 mu2 and torsional coefficient.\n\
    \nHere's a urdf snippet:\n\n      <!-- Add friction to right gripper -->\n   \
    \   <gazebo reference=\"right_inner_finger_pad_link\">\n        <collision name=\"\
    right_outer_knuckle_link_fixed_joint_lump__right_inner_finger_pad_link_collision_3\"\
    >\n          <surface>\n            <friction>\n              <torsional>\n  \
    \              <coefficient>10.0</coefficient>\n                <use_patch_radius>true</use_patch_radius>\n\
    \                <patch_radius>0.05</patch_radius>\n              </torsional>\n\
    \            </friction>\n            <contact>\n              <poissons_ratio>0.5</poissons_ratio>\n\
    \              <elastic_modulus>1e8</elastic_modulus>\n            </contact>\n\

  have to offer?
upvote_count: 1

There are a few Gazebo simulations that I've seen with a robot grasping an object, and manipulating objects was part of the (DARPA) Virtual Robotics Challenge, so I know it is feasible and therefore can't give up on getting my simulation working.  (In case it matters, I'm using Kinetic and Gazebo 9.)  If you've performed grasping with a robot in ROS and Gazebo, will you let me know what settings, tweaks, modifications, or caveats one should be aware of in this realm?

**edit:**
To provide more focus, let me say that this question is mainly concerned with the contact physics, but I'm looking for any and all tips since I've seen that [even the joint controller can break the physics](https://github.com/ros-simulation/gazebo_ros_pkgs/issues/612).  From what I've read online, it appears that modeling contact for grasping in Gazebo is problematic.  (For example, [1](https://answers.ros.org/question/201625/turtlebot-arm-on-gazebo-lets-fall-all-grasped-objects/?comment=202774#comment-202774) and [2](http://answers.gazebosim.org/question/17100/does-the-gripper-tag-still-work-in-gazebo-7/).)  To ameliorate this problem, I have tried changing kp, kd, max_vel, min_dist, mu1, mu2, fdir1, max_step_size, iters, and even the physics engine.
