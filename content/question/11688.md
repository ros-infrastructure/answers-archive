  body_markdown: "Normally, I would say you should create a library. Just use the\
    \ CMake macro \n\n    rosbuild_add_library(foo src/foo.cpp src/bar.cpp)\n\nIn\
    \ this example, a library named libfoo.so is created from files foo.cpp and bar.cpp.\
    \ \n\nTo use your library from other packages, you also need to update your manifest\
    \ file. In our example, you need to add this:\n\n    <export>\n      <cpp cflags=\"\
    -I${prefix}/include\" lflags=\"-Wl,-rpath,${prefix}/lib -L${prefix}/lib -lfoo\"\
    />\n    </export>\n\nTo use your library in another package you can now just depend\
    \ on your package."
