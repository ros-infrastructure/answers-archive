    \ on sending commands here: http://gazebosim.org/tutorials/?tut=ros_control\n\n\

    \ if the URDF specifies nonzero safety limits for effort and velocity for the\

- body_markdown: |-
    Hi @ridwan,

    In order to decrease this type of behaviour you can do several things:

     1. Parametrize properly your contact coefficient for your wheels and your floor: In particular I am refering to the coefficients `mu1` and `mu2` as well as `kp` and `kd`. You can check what those values mean [here](http://gazebosim.org/tutorials?tut=friction) and [here](http://gazebosim.org/tutorials?tut=physics_params&cat=physics). As well as in the SDF [documentation](http://sdformat.org/spec?ver=1.7&elem=collision).
     2. You can also try to tune the physics parameters from ODE engine used by Gazebo. Kp and Kd params have a direct repercusion over ODE inner params called `ERP` and `CFM` used to balance the inestability of the simulation. You can learn more about his params [here](http://ode.org/wiki/index.php?title=Manual#Joint_error_and_the_Error_Reduction_Parameter_.28ERP.29).
