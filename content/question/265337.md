    You can follow the usual tutorials on navigation. As mentioned in another [answer](http://answers.ros.org/question/251072/is-it-possible-to-run-navigation-stack-in-tracked-robot/), tracked vehicles have a poor odometry, but that can be overcome with alternative odometry packages such as [hector_slam](http://wiki.ros.org/hector_slam) or [laser_scan_matcher](http://wiki.ros.org/laser_scan_matcher).

    As you are outdoors, you can also use [robot_localization](http://wiki.ros.org/robot_localization) to integrate your GPS with other localization sources.
