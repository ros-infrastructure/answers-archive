  body_markdown: "This is not a problem of `robot_upstart`.\n\nEither make sure that\
    \ you have the access rights as your user. Typically, the group `dialout` has\
    \ read-write access to those  ports (if I'm correct). So add your user to this\
    \ group: `sudo usermod -a -G dialout $USER` or `sudo adduser $USER dialout`. Then\
    \ log out or reboot and you should be in this group and have access.\n\nAn alternative\
    \ would be to define a \x7F`udev` rule to permanantly change the access rights.\
    \ But I would not do this if it is not required."
