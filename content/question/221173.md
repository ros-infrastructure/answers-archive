  body_markdown: "I had the same problem. Two things to watch ou are 1. the namespace\
    \ (note the `ns=...`), and 2. remapping the topic (note the `<remap ....`). \n\
    \n    <!-- load the controllers -->\n            <node \n                name=\"\
    ros_controller_manager\" \n                pkg=\"controller_manager\" \n     \
    \           type=\"spawner\" \n                ns=\"$(arg robot_name)$(arg model)\"\
    \n                respawn=\"false\"\n                output=\"screen\" \n    \
    \            args=\"$(arg controllers_to_start)\"\n            >\n           \
    \     <remap \n                   from='robot_description'\n                 \
    \  to=\"/$(arg robot_name)$(arg model)/description\"\n                />\n   \
    \         </node>"
