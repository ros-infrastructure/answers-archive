  body_markdown: "gazebo supports a screw joint natively, if you take a look at pr2_description,\
    \ there's a small example in torso.gazebo.xacro:\n\n      <joint:screw name=\"\
    ${name}_screw_torso_lift_joint\">\n          <body1>${name}_motor_screw_link</body1>\n\
    \          <body2>${name}_link</body2>\n          <anchor>${name}_motor_screw_link</anchor>\n\
    \          <anchorOffset>0 0 0</anchorOffset>\n          <axis>0 0 1</axis>\n\
    \          <threadPitch>3141.6</threadPitch>\n      </joint:screw>\n\n[here is\
    \ a video of a simple screw joint](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FUz-vOqKxr0)\n\
    \nif this is what you were looking for, I can provide a more succinct example?\n\
