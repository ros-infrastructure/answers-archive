    You need to determine a 3D position from your 2D image to send a meaningful command. This is an [active field of research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/3D_pose_estimation). The most common simplification is to attach a [2D marker](https://docs.opencv.org/master/d5/dae/tutorial_aruco_detection.html) of some sort and reconstruct its 3D pose with a package like [aruco_ros](https://github.com/pal-robotics/aruco_ros). There is [a tutorial](http://wiki.ros.org/Robots/TIAGo/Tutorials/ArucoBoard).

    You can also google with keywords like "visual servoing" and send relative movement commands to your robot, but the libraries probably won't be as plug-and-play.
