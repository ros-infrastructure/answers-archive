    It looks like it's trying to pull from quantal sources and thus can't find precise dependencies. Did you leave off the 'http:' from your commands? It should be

        sudo sh -c 'echo "deb http://packages.ros.org/ros/ubuntu precise main" > /etc/apt/sources.list.d/ros-latest.list

    You should copy and paste from the [tutorial](http://www.ros.org/wiki/fuerte/Installation/Ubuntu) rather than typing it in.

    If that doesn't work, maybe try the answer to [this question](http://answers.ros.org/question/33585/unable-to-install-ros-on-ubuntu-1204/#33604). Good luck!
