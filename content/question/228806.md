    \ Thus, you need to configure the `CMakeLists.txt` accordingly.\n\nAlso, you should\
    \ consider switching to `catkin` ;-) \n\nFor `catkin` and with `CMake >= 2.8.12`\
    \ and a suitable g++ version (from Ubuntu 14.04 onward? I'm not quite sure) best\
    \ use:\n\n    target_compile_options(<TARGET> PRIVATE -std=c++11)"
