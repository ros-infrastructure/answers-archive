- body_markdown: |-
    ROS Kinetic packages are linked against Boost 1.55 (see [REP-3](http://www.ros.org/reps/rep-0003.html#kinetic-kame-may-2016-may-2021)). This cannot be mixed with other Boost versions unfortunately.

    Typical approach here would be to either recompile library or compile ROS from source with new Boost.
