    If you want to record the linear velocity of the end effector of your UR10 then you're going to have to program a simple node to do this yourself.

    The [python transform listener tutorial](http://wiki.ros.org/tf/Tutorials/Writing%20a%20tf%20listener%20%28Python%29) should be a good place to start. You'll want to look up the transform between the base frame and EE frame, then by comparing two different samples you can work out the change in position and change in time and therefore working out the velocity.

    Hope this makes sense.
