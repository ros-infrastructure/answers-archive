  body_markdown: "I think your calculation is correct. Unfortunately, you might have\

    \ of `some_frame` to `1.57, 0.2, 0.3` shows the issue more clearly.  \n\nBTW,\
    \ I think we are moving away from using the `-` and `+` operators in favor of\
    \ the `*` operator for `ignition::math::Pose3d`."
