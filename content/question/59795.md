    In our lab we successfully calibrated two different kinds of Bumblebee2 stereo cameras (2.5 and 3.8 mm focal length) with the [ROS stereo calibration tool](http://www.ros.org/wiki/camera_calibration/Tutorials/StereoCalibration). We used the `-k 6` option to allow for more parameters in the distortion model.
    We run the [camera1394stereo](http://www.ros.org/wiki/camera1394stereo) driver for the camera and [stereo_image_proc](http://www.ros.org/wiki/stereo_image_proc) to generate rectified images and point clouds.
    Regarding the parameters for stereo_image_proc we followed [this tutorial](http://www.ros.org/wiki/stereo_image_proc/Tutorials/ChoosingGoodStereoParameters). We had to increase the `disparity_range` to 128. The point clouds look quite nice (see the video on the [viso2_ros wiki page](http://www.ros.org/wiki/viso2_ros)).

    Hope that helps!
