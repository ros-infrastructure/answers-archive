    I believe the issue is not "with or without a network connection", it is that some DDS implementations ignore `lo` (ie: `localhost`) network interfaces. They should be configurable to enable the `lo` interface as well and then everything should work as normal.

    See [ros2/rmw_fastrtps#228](https://github.com/ros2/rmw_fastrtps/issues/228), [eProsima/Fast-RTPS#167](https://github.com/eProsima/Fast-RTPS/issues/167) and [eProsima/Fast-RTPS#267](https://github.com/eProsima/Fast-RTPS/pull/267) for what I believe are related issues.
