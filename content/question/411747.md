Hi ROS 2 community,

over the last weeks I tried to get nav2 running with humble (real hardware and in simulation), but it looks like the local costmap is just not working while it was fine in foxy. 

I created docker-container for quick testing for both, for [foxy](https://github.com/brean/ros2-turtlebot3-gazebo-docker/tree/foxy-gazebo-classic) and [humble](https://github.com/brean/ros2-turtlebot3-gazebo-docker/tree/humble-gazebo-classic)

**Does anybody have an idea how to get a local costmap for autonomous navigation in ROS 2 humble?**

There are no errors or tracebacks in the logs, humble seems a bit more verbose with its infos but it looks fine.

According to [this issue](https://github.com/ros-planning/navigation2/issues/3014) its a problem with FastDDS, but switching to cyclondds does not solve the issue (see my docker setup [here](https://github.com/brean/ros2-turtlebot3-gazebo-docker/tree/humble-gazebo-classic-cyclonedds) and [here](https://github.com/brean/ros2-turtlebot3-remote-pc-docker/tree/humble-cyclonedds) ), same for installing the current versions of ROS humble as suggested [here](https://github.com/ros-planning/navigation2/issues/3298) (see docker setup [here](https://github.com/brean/ros2-turtlebot3-gazebo-docker/tree/humble-gazebo-classic-upgrade-2023-01) and [here](https://github.com/brean/ros2-turtlebot3-remote-pc-docker/tree/humble-upgrade-2023-01)).
I also tried to compile the humble-branch of navigation2 from source.

Interestingly even in the gif from the [official documentation](https://navigation.ros.org/getting_started/index.html) the local costmap is not shown:

![image description](https://navigation.ros.org/_images/navigation_with_recovery_behaviours.gif)

while it is shown in foxy:

![image description](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/brean/ros2-turtlebot3-gazebo-docker/foxy-gazebo-classic/doc/gazebo_classic_and_rviz2.gif)
