I am currently trying to remote access camera topics of realsense2_camera node (rs_camera.launch), but some of the topics are empty. They appear in rostopic list, but rostopic hz shows that no messages are published.

**Setup:**

-Intel RealSense D435i camera connected to Raspberry Pi 4 (Raspbian Buster, ROS Kinetic) with USB 3

-Laptop (Ubuntu 16.04, ROS Kinetic) currently connected to Pi via Ethernet

The Pi ist the ROS master. I have set ROS_IP on both devices and exported ROS_MASTER_URI on the Laptop. Some of the topics like /camera/color/image_raw or /camera/color/camera_info are published and accessible. CompressedImage topics as well as /tf are not. CPU usage on the Pi is only at ~40% according to top, MEM is even lower.

I am starting to think that there must be some other limitation of the Pi, as the node works fine when I let it run on my laptop. Has anyone ever managed to use a RealSense camera on a Pi? Has anyone encountered similar problems with other nodes? Could it be some kind of threading problem? All hints are aprechiated as I am new to using ROS on micro controllers.
