    Swap the turtlesim velocity with [geometry_msgs/Twist](http://docs.ros.org/api/geometry_msgs/html/msg/Twist.html).

    `vel_pub_ = nh_.advertise<turtlesim::Velocity>("turtle1/turtle1/cmd_vel", 1);` needs to be changed to
    `vel_pub_ = nh_.advertise<geometry_msgs::Twist>("turtle1/turtle1/cmd_vel", 1);`
    ` turtlesim::Velocity vel;` to `geometry_msgs::Twist vel;` Remove `command_velocity` at the end.
