  body_markdown: "In ROS, services are 'only' means of communication. Not to be confused\
    \ with a cloud-service or something. When node A, calls a service of node B, whatever\
    \ runs in the service is executed by node B and the result is passed back to A.\
    \ https://wiki.ros.org/rospy/Overview/Services#Providing_services  "
