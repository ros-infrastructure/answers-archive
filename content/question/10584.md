    If you take a look at the definition of the [geometry_msgs/Twist](http://www.ros.org/doc/api/geometry_msgs/html/msg/Twist.html) message, it's fairly straightforward. Generally, the coordinates are aligned such that linear/x is the forward/backward direction for your robot,. Since your robot is likely non-holonomic, you can probably ignore linear/y and linear/z. Angular/z is in-plane rotation, and you can ignore x and y.

    Linear velocities are generally in meters/s, and angular velocities in radians/s.
