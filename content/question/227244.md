  body_markdown: "to rotate the robot by a certain angle, you have to choose a rotational\
    \ velocity and compute for how long you should send it. for example, to rotate\
    \ 45 degrees you can publish an angular velocity of 4.5 degrees/s during 10 seconds.\
    \ \n\nbear in mind that in this way, you are commanding the robot in an open loop,\
    \ so due to accelerations and other errors you will not get exactly the orientation\
    \ or the position that you expected."
