    No, you have to use a [transform listener](http://www.ros.org/wiki/tf/Tutorials/Writing%20a%20tf%20broadcaster%20%28Python%29) to get the pose as a `tf::Stamped<tf::pose>` message type. If your function parameter needs `nav_msgs::Odometry` you use a standard ROS subscriber to get the pose as a `<nav_msgs::odometry>` message type.
