  body_markdown: "Running the `rosinstall` again should work. \n\nIt's easiest to\
    \ use the [update option](http://ros.org/wiki/rosinstall#Updating_a_rosinstall_tree)\
    \ of rosinstall, like this:\n\n    $ rosinstall ~/ros\n\n(Assuming you had installed\
    \ it to `~/ros` initially.)\n\nYou probably do not need to rebuild everything,\
    \ just the parts you actually depend on. To do that, run `rosmake` on your packages\
    \ or stacks in the normal way."
