  body_markdown: "\"Implementing ROS\" with an arm sounds a bit misleading I think.\
    \ ROS is already implemented (but of course evolving over time). You can certainly\
    \ use a multitude of ROS tools with hobbyist hardware though. If you create a\
    \ URDF model, you could easily visualize a robot model in [rviz](http://wiki.ros.org/rviz)\
    \ or even plan motions using [MoveIt!](http://moveit.ros.org/). Some caveats apply\
    \ of course, for example if your arm doesn\xB4t have joint angle sensing, you\
    \ could provide fake joint angle measurements by just taking the desired values\
    \ you sent to the system (which might or might not work very well).\nYou\xB4d\
    \ likely also have to do some low level interfacing with a Microcontroller, but\
    \ [rosserial_arduino](http://wiki.ros.org/rosserial_arduino), [ros_arduino_bridge](http://wiki.ros.org/ros_arduino_bridge),\
    \ [rosc](http://wiki.ros.org/rosc) and others could help with that."
