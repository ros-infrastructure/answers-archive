    That kind of behavior is hard to achieve with the default TrajectoryPlannerROS. You should look into local planners that support car-like movement. For example you can check: <br>
    [teb_local_planner](http://wiki.ros.org/teb_local_planner)  and [tutorials](http://wiki.ros.org/teb_local_planner/Tutorials/Planning%20for%20car-like%20robots)<br>
    [sbpl_lattice_planner](http://wiki.ros.org/sbpl_lattice_planner) <br>
    This [question](https://answers.ros.org/question/217091/planner-for-car-like-robot/) also tackles the problem. More on Ackermann steering can be found here [here](http://wiki.ros.org/Ackermann%20Group).
