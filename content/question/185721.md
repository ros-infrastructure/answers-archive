    MORSE supports many [different middlewares](http://www.openrobots.org/morse/doc/latest/user/integration.html), including ROS.

    As briefly [explained here](http://www.openrobots.org/morse/doc/latest/user/middlewares/ros.html), when using the ROS middleware, each sensor and actuator writes/reads from a ros topic and, if the sensors/actuators has asynchronous services, they will also exposes ROS action servers.

    I recommend you to check the [ROS + MORSE tutorial](http://www.openrobots.org/morse/doc/latest/user/beginner_tutorials/tutorial_builder_ros.html).

    If you do not need ROS and simply want to communicate with MORSE from various language, you may also want to check the simple [socket interface ](http://www.openrobots.org/morse/doc/latest/user/middlewares/socket.html)(virtually every language should be able to write/read from a socket).
