    I assume you already have the images for the pictures.

    You can make a box model which is thin like a picture and use the image as its texture. [Here](https://bitbucket.org/osrf/gazebo_models/src/605a402bd4c23232002b4a2ba7113ae885051bbe/textured_shapes/?at=default)'s an example of a model with textures.

    If you don't want the pictures to move, you can pin it to the wall by setting the model's `<pose>` and making the model `<static>`.

    I recommend you take a look at this tutorial before anything:

    http://gazebosim.org/tutorials?tut=build_robot&cat=build_robot
