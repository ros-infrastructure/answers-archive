  body_markdown: "The problem is you never service the callbacks.\nThis only happens\
    \ when you do a `spin` call.\n\nHowever, you are looping without calling spin.\n\
    \nThus, instead of:\n\n    while (ros::ok())\n    {\n        ROS_INFO(\"This is\
    \ x: %.2f\", moverobot.x);\n    }\n    loop_rate.sleep();\n    ros::spin();\n\n\
    You need to have\n\n    while (ros::ok())\n    {\n        ROS_INFO(\"This is x:\
    \ %.2f\", moverobot.x);\n        loop_rate.sleep();\n        ros::spinOnce();\n\
    \    }\n\nNote: `spin()` is \xB4looping itself, thus the call to `spinOnce` here.\n\
    \nSee also: http://wiki.ros.org/roscpp/Overview/Callbacks%20and%20Spinning"
