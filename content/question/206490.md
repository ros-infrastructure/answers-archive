  body_markdown: "Assuming you are using ROS alongside gazebo, you will want to add\
    \ a drivetrain plugin to your URDF or SDF model. If you look at [this link](http://gazebosim.org/tutorials?tut=ros_gzplugins)\
    \ scroll down and you will see either Differential Drive or Skid Steering Drive.\
    \ You can just copy and paste the code into your URDF (if its SDF then dont include\
    \ the <gazebo> tags) and specify the respective joints of your robot. \n\nThis\
    \ ROS-Gazebo plugin will then basically act as a node that subscribes to a \"\
    /cmd_vel\" topic, and you can install the package \"ros-indigo-key-teleop\" (assuming\
    \ your on indigo) to be able to use your keyboard to send a command velocity."
