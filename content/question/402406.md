I have a differential drive robot car with odometry and laser scanning that localizes fine (amcl) and navigates to requested
goals (move_base) fine but not getting obstacle avoidance for novel items not in the global map. Such obstacles are seen in the live laser scan but there does not appear to be any marking/clearing going on or attempts to navigate around these objects. Car just crashes into them if in the way. I post the relevant configs below. Note static map and inflation appear to be working as expected. Just no novel obstacle avoidance. Any suggestions? 

Using noetic on ubuntu 20.04. Closely following setup http://wiki.ros.org/navigation/Tutorials/RobotSetup.

obstacle_layer:

  inflation_radius: 0.55

  global_frame: map
