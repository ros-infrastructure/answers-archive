    You can get a list of gazebo links and their states from `/gazebo/link_states` topic.

    Here is a [python script](https://github.com/bgromov/thymio_gazebo/blob/master/thymio_description/scripts/gazebo_link_pose) that republishes poses in `geometry_msgs/Pose` format. You can find the names of links either in Gazebo GUI or on `/gazebo/link_states` topic itself.
