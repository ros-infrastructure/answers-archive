  body_markdown: "The error tells you what the problem is and where to find it.\n\n\
    \    Invalid roslaunch XML syntax: not well-formed (invalid token): \n\ntells\
    \ you that there is something wrong with the XML syntax of your file.\n\n    line\
    \ 35, column 6 The traceback for the exception was written to the log file\n\n\
    tells you that the problem is at *approximately* line 35. Many times errors come\
    \ before where you're told the error is. In this case it's at line 32 where you\
    \ have a malformed `node` tag.\n\nChange\n\n    <node name=\"pose\" pkg=\"crazyflie_demo\"\
    \ type=\"publish_pose.py\" output=\"scree$\n\nto\n\n    <node name=\"pose\" pkg=\"\
    crazyflie_demo\" type=\"publish_pose.py\" output=\"screen\">\n\nNotice how I added\
    \ the missing quotation mark and closing `>` and changed `$` to `n`."
