    I'm going to close this as a duplicate of #q307097.

    In short: Ubuntu 18.10 is not supported by ROS Melodic. Only Ubuntu 18.04 and 17.10 are.

    ---

    Edit: but you can of course try building from source on 18.10 and/or use the Docker images available.

    It's just that no binary packages are provided for 18.10 by the ROS buildfarm.
