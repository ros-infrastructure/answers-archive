    The `/tf` data isn't really designed to work like a normal topic. You don't typically just subscribe to it. More about how `/tf` data works can be read on the [tf wiki page.](http://wiki.ros.org/tf)  Here are 2 simple solutions for you to consider:

     1. You could write a node that creates a `TransformListener`, and then in some callback (maybe from a [Timer](http://wiki.ros.org/roscpp/Overview/Timers)) you lookup the transform from some frame attached to the depth sensor (maybe `/<camera>_depth_optical_frame`) to the frame at the joint you are interested in.  This transform could then be converted into some other message type (maybe a [PoseStamped](http://wiki.ros.org/geometry_msgs)) and published on some other topic.  Or it could be directly worked with to do whatever you want.
     2. You could edit the `openni_tracker` source code to publish a message that contains the data you want.  I have a  package that I worked on a few years ago that does this for all of the skeleton tracking data.  [Here is the package](https://github.com/jakeware/skeletontracker_nu) and here are the [associated message definitions](https://github.com/jakeware/skeletonmsgs_nu).  You might also want to look at the [pi_tracker](http://wiki.ros.org/pi_tracker) package as I think they do something very similar.
