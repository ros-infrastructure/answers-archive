    With Ubuntu 18.04 you should use [ROS Melodic](http://wiki.ros.org/melodic) as it is primarily targeted at the Ubuntu 18.04 (Bionic) release. At the moment ROS Kinetic is the most used one and has a number of packages that are probably still not ported to the newer distributions.
