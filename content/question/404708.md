    I have not used the [gmapping](http://wiki.ros.org/gmapping) package. However, looking at the wiki page, it seems that it requires `sensor_msgs/LaserScan` type of data. The Intel RealSense D435 does not produce such type of data. While looking at the [depthimage_to_laserscan](http://wiki.ros.org/depthimage_to_laserscan) package, which takes a depth image and generates a 2D laser scan, we should be able to run `gmapping`. You need to subscribe to the following topics:

     - image: /camera/depth/image_raw
     - camera_info: /camera/depth/camera_info

    The `DepthImageToLaserScanNodelet` nodelet will publish on scan topic which should be the input for `gmapping` package.

    I do not have D435 at hand right now, therefore the image and camera_info topics may be incorrect. Please check them from terminal.
