  body_markdown: "You have to explicitly give the path to the file, for example :\n\
    \n    <node pkg=\"rosbag\" type=\"play\" name=\"player\" output=\"screen\" args=\"\
    --clock /home/me/ros_files/my_file.bag\"/> \n\nYou can also use arguments, if\
    \ you have multiple files to include\n\n    <arg name=\"path\" default=\"/home/me/ros_files/\"\
    \ />\n    <arg name=\"file_1\" default=\"my_file_1\" />\n    <arg name=\"file_2\"\
    \ default=\"my_file_2\" />\n    <node pkg=\"rosbag\" type=\"play\" name=\"player\"\
    \ output=\"screen\" args=\"--clock $(arg path)$(arg file_1).bag $(arg path)$(arg\
    \ file_2).bag\"/> \n\nI hope it helped you. Go to [this page](http://www.ros.org/wiki/rosbag/Commandline)\
