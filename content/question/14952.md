    The [beginner tutorial series](http://gazebosim.org/tutorials?cat=guided_b&tut=guided_b1) would probably be a good starting point. They teach you how to make a simple vehicle and control it with a pre-existing plugin.

    Once you've mastered the main concepts in that tutorial, you could either write a [Gazebo plugin](http://gazebosim.org/tutorials?tut=plugins_model&cat=write_plugin) to control your robot, or use [ROS](http://gazebosim.org/tutorials?tut=ros_control&cat=connect_ros) to do it.
