    \ type the '$'):\n$ roscore\n\nIn another Terminal:\n$ rosrun urg_node urg_node\n\
    \nIn another Terminal:\n$ rosrun rviz rviz\n\nIn rviz, you can use the 'add' button\
    \ on the lower left of the screen to add a display\nI think you want to use pointcloud\
    \ or pointcloud2 but you should be able click around and find what you need.\n\
    Then you just need to set the topic for the display to /laserscan and rviz should\
    \ show you the laserscan data on the screen. \n\nThis is all off the top of my\
    \ head however, and I don't have that specific Hokuyo. Good luck!"
