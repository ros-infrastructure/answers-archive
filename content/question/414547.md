  body_markdown: "the error is caused by `LDS_MODEL = os.environ['LDS_MODEL']` a missing\
    \ environment variable.\n\nTry\n`export LDS_MODEL=\"none\"`  \n\nbefore running\n\
    \n`ros2 launch turtlebot3_bringup robot.launch.py`\n\nthat solved it for me. (mine\
    \ turtlebot3 does not have a lidar, that's why I went with \"none\", you might\
    \ need to figure it out). Note that on Foxy there is no such variable."
