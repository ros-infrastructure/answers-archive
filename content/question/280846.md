Is there an action planning framework in ROS that supports large scale general action planning?

I'm trying to flesh out some simple action sequences for a small robot using [actionlib](http://wiki.ros.org/actionlib) and its SimpleActionServer and client, but I'm finding it's anything but simple or usable. Even for trivial actions, they require a complex server/client architecture along with large scripts that essentially are just glorified service calls. Even worse, the "action" message is requires doesn't let you chain actions together. It specifies a goal and results, but these aren't structured in a way that a STRIPS or PDDL planner could use to form more sophisticated plans, and there's nothing in the docs to suggest this was even a goal of actionlib.

Some of the motion-specific planners seem more geared for this, but they're almost exclusive for moving arms, and aren't general purpose planners.
