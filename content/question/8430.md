    A model plugin should work for you. Just use [Joint::SetVelocity](http://osrf-distributions.s3.amazonaws.com/gazebo/api/dev/classgazebo_1_1physics_1_1Joint.html#a30b3b4fd789504c9dc265cddb276f3bf) or [Joint::SetForce](http://osrf-distributions.s3.amazonaws.com/gazebo/api/dev/classgazebo_1_1physics_1_1Joint.html#a1eb00145de3a61aaa0ee7506a6ab44c2).

    See the [DiffDrivePlugin](https://bitbucket.org/osrf/gazebo/src/938eaf5747cee1dce194614a2cbaf91d1a53d145/plugins/DiffDrivePlugin.cc?at=default) for an example.
