    There isn't really a way to look for PIDs in the ROS 2 API.  The main reason for this is that in ROS 2, a PID isn't a good indication of a single unit of work.  That is, there can be multiple nodes in a single process by using [composition](https://index.ros.org/doc/ros2/Tutorials/Composition/).

    What are you trying to accomplish?  Maybe there is another way to do what you want.
