    There's no way to load a saved world from the menu, see [this issue](https://bitbucket.org/osrf/gazebo/issues/158/add-load-world-option-to-menu).

    You should load the world at startup by passing the file path to Gazebo, i.e.

    `gazebo /path/to/world.sdf`
