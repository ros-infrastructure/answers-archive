    There are a couple of ways of getting a PointCloud2 msg:

     1. You can use the [laser_geometry](https://wiki.ros.org/laser_geometry) as laid out [in this answer](https://answers.ros.org/question/11232/how-to-turn-laser-scan-to-point-cloud-map/?answer=16667#post-id-16667). This is the easier of the two approaches.
     2. You will have to manually convert the data into a PointCloud2 msg. You basically know the data format and layout of both the [LaserScan](http://docs.ros.org/en/noetic/api/sensor_msgs/html/msg/LaserScan.html) msg and [PointCloud2](http://docs.ros.org/en/noetic/api/sensor_msgs/html/msg/PointCloud2.html) msg from their definitions. You will have to manually build up the pointcloud2 msg using data used in the laserscan msg. This is not necessarily harder, just a bit more involved.
