    This problem was not related to robot_localization:

    The driver of the realsense camera publishes IMU messages with wrong timestamps (future dated).
    robot_localization was using the right IMU messages according to timestamps.
    So the rotation was just delayed in IMU data.

    When correcting the timestamps the result is not optimal - there is still a small latency.
    Maybe this is now related to my covariance matrix?
