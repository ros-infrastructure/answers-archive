  body_markdown: "If you are not using any exteroceptive sensors such as camera /\
    \ laser/ sonar etc, then the conventional mapping solutions won't work for you.\n\
    \nBased on your comments, what I understood is that you need to understand where\
    \ in the environment you can navigate without collisions. This information constitutes\
    \ a map for you. To create that map you will navigate the robot in a pattern in\
    \ the open space.\n\nAssuming I have understood your problem correctly, I can\
    \ see a couple of problems in this approach.\n\n 1. You are entirely dependent\
    \ on odometry, which will definitely drift while the robot moves\n 2. You need\
    \ to fix your odometry coordinate system's origin somewhere and keep track of\
    \ it\n\nIf your odometry drift is tolerable and you can always initialize the\
    \ robot accurately in the same place, I propose the following mapping approach.\n\
    \n    Initialize map MxN 2D matrix to occupied everywhere\n    \n    while navigating\n\
    \        get position x,y from odometry\n        convert x,y to corresponding\
    \ i,j indices of the map (based on map resolution and map origin)\n        set\
    \ map(i,j) free \n\n "
