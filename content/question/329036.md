  body_markdown: "This is for a couple reasons:\n\n 1. Your odom frame doesn't have\
    \ feedback going into it. All odom does is integrate your velocities to compute\
    \ a pose relative to where you started from. I'm not super familiar with cartographer,\
    \ but I suspect that it uses the map and laser scan data to feedback into your\
    \ odom pose. Ie, odom provides a rough guess as to where the robot is, and cartographer\
    \ refines that guess. \n 2. Sensors are complex (citation needed).They have biases,\
    \ random walks, scale factors, alignment issues, not to mention that all those\
    \ change depending on temperature. When you integrate a linear velocity for example,\
    \ you're also going to be integrating the error of your sensor. \n\nBottom line\
    \ is that your odom frame drifting is quite expected. If your robot position is\
    \ accurate enough for you, then you shouldn't have any issues with odom. If you're\
    \ concerned about it drifting, then you'll need to do some more advanced filtering\
    \ of your sensor data or get better sensors."
