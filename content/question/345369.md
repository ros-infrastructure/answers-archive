    I would suggest to go through some tutorials with a functioning navigation stack. I personally liked the [Turtlebot 3](http://emanual.robotis.com/docs/en/platform/turtlebot3/overview/) tutorials and the [Clearpath](http://www.clearpathrobotics.com/assets/guides/husky/SimulatingHusky.html) instructions and demos. Both describe how to simulate the robot, so you don't need the hardware to learn from them. If you are more into reading, I would suggest the [ROS Robot Programming](https://community.robotsource.org/t/download-the-ros-robot-programming-book-for-free/51) book. Chapter 10 addresses mobile robots and chapter 11 SLAM and Navigation.
