  body_markdown: "rosbag will not modify timestamp data inside the bag file. However\
    \ it will publish as accurately as possible related to the receive time during\
    \ the playback. Two parallel message topics are not synchronized or guarenteed\
    \ in order. Going through rosbag adds another layer of complexity. \n\nThe recommended\
    \ approach to synchronize parallel topics is a message_filter based synchronizer:\
    \ http://wiki.ros.org/message_filters#Policy-Based_Synchronizer_.5BROS_1.1.2B-.5D\n\
    \nThere are standard filters for ExactTime Policy as well as ApproximateTIme documented\
    \ there."
