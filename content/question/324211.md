- body_markdown: "Depends on your application and how fast everything is running.\
    \ I typically recommend as a blanket 100hz, but really you're limited by the \"\
    fastest\" thing happening. If there's something trying to get the pose of your\
    \ robot at >10hz, you're going to get stale or interpolated information. \n\n\
    100hz for a typical ground robot running the typical ros navigation-like things\
    \ is usually fine. That may be going overboard depending on your application.\
    \ 30hz is a good hobby-number if your encoders have enough ticks to make that\
    \ meaningful."
