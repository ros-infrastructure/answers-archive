  body_markdown: "Are you trying to integrate rosjava code or roscpp code?   \n\n\
    The instructions for rosjava (in progress) are here:\n\nhttp://www.ros.org/wiki/rosjava/Build/Eclipse\n\
    \nFor roscpp, your project needs to follow the standard package template, where\
    \ the ``Makefile`` looks like:\n\n\n     include $(shell rospack find mk)/cmake.mk\n\
    \n\nAnd nothing more (i.e. the rest of your bulid is configured via CMake).  The\
    \ above cmake.mk file declares the eclipse-project target.  \n\nIf you don't wish\
    \ to use the cmake.mk file, then you can grab the necessary Makefile rule from\
    \ mk/cmake.mk and adapt it to your needs."
