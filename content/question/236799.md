     You forgot to enable X11 Forwarding. Try to use

        ssh -X thinker.local -l think
        rosrun rviz rviz -d `rospack find rbx1_nav`/sim.rviz

    Note: -X (upper case) enables X11 Forwarding whereas -x (lower case) disables it.
