    \ EE with a revolute joint\n\n    <joint name=\"gripper_joint\" type=\"revolute\"\
    >\n      <axis xyz=\"1 0 0\" />\n      <parent link=\"ee_link\" />\n      <child\
    \ link=\"vacuum_gripper\" />\n      <origin rpy=\"0 1.5708 0\" xyz=\"0.01 0.0125\

    \      <dynamics damping=\"0.0\" friction=\"10\"/>\n    </joint>\n\nThe vacuum\
