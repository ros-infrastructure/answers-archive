     It seems at least the packages [in this page](http://repositories.ros.org/status_page/ros_kinetic_default.html?q=mavros) are already build and their binaries are available already on Kinetic. Have you run `apt-get update`?

    Also, Jade and Kinetic are not meant to work on the same Ubuntu distro. See REP-0003 for the supported hosts:

    - [Jade](http://www.ros.org/reps/rep-0003.html#jade-turtle-may-2015-may-2017)
    - [Kinetic](http://www.ros.org/reps/rep-0003.html#kinetic-kame-may-2016-may-2021)
