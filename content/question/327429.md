    May or may not be an answer but this doesn't fit into comment section so posting as an answer.

    Building `opencv` from source for ROS is documented [on this section on its wiki page](http://wiki.ros.org/opencv3#Building_the_Package), which seems to cover not just building from source but mixed with how to make binary packages out of built artifact IINM. I haven't tried the whole steps by myself, but I'd say go through the steps in that section up to the following line, then start building:

        44 echo "install(FILES package.xml DESTINATION share/opencv3)" >> ./CMakeLists.txt
