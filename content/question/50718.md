  body_markdown: |-
    The map for footstep_planner needs to be a regular obstacle map, as it is defined in [nav_msgs/OccupancyGrid](http://ros.org/doc/api/nav_msgs/html/msg/OccupancyGrid.html). For static maps, that can be a simple black & white bitmap that you send out with [map_server](http://ros.org/wiki/map_server).

    If you get obstacle positions from artificial markers, you will have to create this obstacle map yourself.
