  body_markdown: "hector_mapping does not use a particle filter, but pose estimation\
    \ using fast scanmatching that is accurate enough to not require multiple hypotheses\
    \ (such as in a rao-blackwellized particle filter approach like gmapping) in many\
    \ real world applications. Thus, there is no particle count that you could increase.\
    \ You could take a shot at adding a particle filter implementation on top of hector_mapping\
    \ of course, but that\xB4s a non-trivial change."
