  - body_markdown: |-
      "Batch clouds" is a convenience feature that is meant to be used *after* mapping. When you trigger it

       - all clouds that have been computed and stored within rgbdslam_v2 are sent out with send_clouds_rate
       - simultaneously, the current estimates of the camera poses that belong to the clouds are sent out on /tf
       - both, clouds and poses are timestamped with the original image timestamps
           - That means: data from the past (during mapping) is sent out again on /tf.
           - This might interfere with other nodes.

      The "position sanity check", as you say, is just the optimizer making use of new information. When the current image "A" is received, it is matched against past images. Afterwards, the newer images are matched against A and others (before and after A). The resulting network of estimations leads to corrections to the pose of A.
      If you use the batch clouds feature periodically, the information is sent again and again, the original pose estimate, and later the improved estimate
