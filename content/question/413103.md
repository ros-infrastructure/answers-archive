  body_markdown: "The error appears to come from the GeniCam implementation used,\
    \ which tries to update a particular node (ie: setting) in your camera, called\
    \ `ExposureAuto`. The camera reports it doesn't support setting it, which causes\
    \ GeniCam to report to the `arena_camera_node` updating that value failed. The\
    \ node then prints the error message you show.\n\nThis is not really a \"ROS problem\"\
    , but a problem in `arena_camera_node`, your camera and/or the way Lucid is using\
    \ it.\n\nBut also note:\n\n> Machine setup:\n> \n>     ROS: Noetic\n\nthe page\
    \ you link states `ROS Kinetic for Ubuntu 16.04 or ROS Melodic for Ubuntu 18.04`\
    \ under *System Requirements*, while you are using Noetic.\n\nI'm not claiming\
    \ that's the cause of your problems, but I'd verify with your vendor Noetic is\
    \ supported."
