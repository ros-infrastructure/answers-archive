    There isn't a way to do this out of the box in Gazebo. In the past, we've used solutions like spawning / deleting models on demand.

    On Ignition (Gazebo's latest rewrite), there's the concept of [levels](https://ignitionrobotics.org/api/gazebo/2.0/levels.html) which are loaded and unloaded incrementally as robots move around the world.
