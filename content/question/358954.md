  body_markdown: "1. I will suggest you to checkout [Dynamixel WorkBench](https://emanual.robotis.com/docs/en/software/dynamixel/dynamixel_workbench/)\
    \ to familiarize yourself. \n After that checkout [Dynamixel SDK](https://emanual.robotis.com/docs/en/software/dynamixel/dynamixel_sdk/overview/)\
    \ that will show you how to command your motors.  There are very good examples\
    \ given already. I think they are more than enough atleast to move a robot and\
    \ an example([Python](https://github.com/tonnesfn/DynamixelSDK/tree/master/python)\
    \ and [CPP](https://github.com/tonnesfn/DynamixelSDK/tree/master/c%2B%2B/example/))\
    \ how to use dynamixel motors and SDK.\n\n2. Calculate robots velocity commands.\
    \ A nice tutorial can be found [here](https://yainnoware.blogspot.com/2019/03/three-wheeled-holonomic-robot-theory.html)\
    \ and its corrresponding [ros example](https://github.com/YugAjmera/teleop_keyboard_omni3/blob/master/teleop_keyboard_omni3.py).\
    \ \n\n3. Now just connect step 1 and 2 and your robot should move."
