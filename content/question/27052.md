    This might be related to [https://github.com/ignition-forks/dart/pull/6](https://github.com/ignition-forks/dart/pull/6). Using the [fork](https://github.com/ignition-forks/dart) fixed such issue for me, but it is only really a problem in worlds with way too many objects.

    [Not related to this issue] For anyone getting the same error, it can also occur if you let objects fall forever into the abyss. If that is the case, consider adding an invisible collision plane under your world.
