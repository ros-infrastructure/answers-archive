  body_markdown: "Hi, \n\nUnless you're logged in a root then you will not have permission\
    \ to write to the /dev/bus/usb/001/002 device.\n\nI've just worked around this\
    \ and it's a bit messy and you have to do it every time you connect the camera.\
    \ Change your path to /dev/bus/usb/001/ and type \"sudo chown <your username>\
    \ 002\"\n\nThis will give your user the permissions to access this device."
