    You can use the `gazebo_ros_range` plugin to simulate an ultrasonic (sonar) sensor. Look at lines 82 to 115 of [this test world file](https://github.com/ros-simulation/gazebo_ros_pkgs/blob/kinetic-devel/gazebo_plugins/test/test_worlds/gazebo_ros_range.world#L82) to see how to add one to your own simulated robot.
