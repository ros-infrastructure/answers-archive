  body_markdown: "You have `tf_prefix` set. Simply removing it from your launch file\
    \ might not help, unless you start a new ROS master afterwards, or manually delete\
    \ the `tf_prefix` parameter. I would stop your ROS master, remove `tf_prefix`,\
    \ then run everything *without* AMCL, and check the frames. The only things that\
    \ affect the EKF's use of frames are the parameters you give it and `tf_prefix`,\
    \ so if it's publishing transforms in the wrong namespace, it's one of those two\
    \ things. "
