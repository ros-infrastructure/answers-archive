    Is there somewhere specific that tells you to use a RegionOfInterest message? You can access a specific point from an organized pointcloud (which the openni driver produces) by using [cloud.at(u,v)](http://docs.pointclouds.org/1.4.0/classpcl_1_1_point_cloud.html#a0dcdaca83e02807e154d82d42bdf631f).

    Alternatively, if you just want the depth, you can subscribe to the depth image, and just index into the image, which will provide you with the depth.
