  body_markdown: "I found out what was wrong. \n1. The model parts were placed in\
    \ transparency mode and the view joints was selected. \n2. The wheel was rotating\
    \ with the point of origin being z=0 around the point of z=0.\nalso,\n3. I had\
    \ a camera part sitting at z=0.\n\nTo fix this this:\n\n1. The wheel radius was\
    \ measured and then placed the joint at that height.\n2. The wheel origin was\
    \ placed at the the negative value of the wheel radius. \n3. The camera was moved\
    \ to where the camera visual setting location.\n\nI hope this helps someone else!"

The robot model I am working on looks fine in Gazebo; as soon as I send a command for the wheels to move the robot forward:

1. the wheels start to rotate, 
2. the rear of the robot lifts, 
and then 
3. the wheels leave the robot. 

Here is a link to the github of the files: https://github.com/matrhint/wheels_problem 

I did have this working, but I edited the stl files and changed where the joints are because the robot would not travel in a straight line (I assumed it was the wheels not being exactly 90 degrees to the ground). 

Note: The wheel appears to rotate as though the joint is either at z=0 or the wheel is rotating in a circular, planetary fashion around where I believe the joint to be.

Thanks in advance for any assistance.
