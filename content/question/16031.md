    There have been efforts to integrate fluid simulation into Gazebo in the past, you can see a tutorial [here](http://gazebosim.org/tutorials?tut=fluids&cat=), but that's a bit out of date.

    If you don't care about the physical behavior of the fluid, but only about it's appearance, you could try animating visuals that move like a fluid. [Here](http://gazebosim.org/tutorials?tut=animated_box&cat=build_robot)'s an example of how to animate a simple box, you could change the box appearance to look like a fluid, and have many small boxes animated in sync to look like a fluid.

    Hope that helps!
