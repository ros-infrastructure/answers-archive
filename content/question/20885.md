  body_markdown: "Just a guess (cause i don't know the sdf files of your models),\
    \ but it seems that you are asking for the model_states in a specific frame\n\n\
    \       `resp_coordinates = model_coordinates(blockName,block._relative_entity_name)`\n\
    each model position will be relative to `block._relative_entity_name`. I assume\
    \ that the service will just take the 'link' frame of the models. and there might\
    \ be the static offset. \n\nYou should instead call the service like this:\n\n\
    \       `resp_coordinates = model_coordinates(blockName,\"\")`\nthe empty relative\
    \ frame will cause that the position is in global \"world\" or \"map\" frame.\n\
    \n\n"
