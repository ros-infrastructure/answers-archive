    Your goal might be achievable with [Trajectory Constraints](http://docs.ros.org/api/moveit_msgs/html/msg/TrajectoryConstraints.html), especially [Joint Constraints](http://docs.ros.org/api/moveit_msgs/html/msg/JointConstraint.html) which you can [set](http://docs.ros.org/kinetic/api/moveit_ros_planning_interface/html/classmoveit_1_1planning__interface_1_1MoveGroupInterface.html#a977cf44d1977f69e5dd95bb3433df79a). [Get your current joint values](http://docs.ros.org/kinetic/api/moveit_ros_planning_interface/html/classmoveit_1_1planning__interface_1_1MoveGroupInterface.html#a83676c1aca54429ff0de9919ffb79b7a) and construct the `TrajectoryConstraint` for your disabled joint to stay fixed to that value.
