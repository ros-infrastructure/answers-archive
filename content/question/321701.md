    If you want to try to project a RGB video onto the point cloud, I used to do that with asus xtion which was providing a point cloud and rgb image stream. I was able to visualize video on the point cloud by adding Camera and choosing "Image Rendering" as overlay while also visualizing point cloud. If you dont have a video stream and you want to project a constant image, you can convert your image to sensor_msgs/Image as in [here](http://wiki.ros.org/cv_bridge/Tutorials/UsingCvBridgeToConvertBetweenROSImagesAndOpenCVImages), then do the same thing. Hope this helps.
