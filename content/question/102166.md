    I suggest you to take a look to the [Navigation Stack](http://wiki.ros.org/navigation). You have to load the map with a map_server node and then you have to run a localization node (like [AMCL](http://wiki.ros.org/amcl)), then you can use the estimate pose 2D button on rviz to estimate your initial pose.
    To move your robot you will need a planner (like [move_base](http://wiki.ros.org/move_base)) and send goals to this node.
