  body_markdown: "Short answer:  You need a gyro to detect turns.    Encoders are\
    \ not perfect because of the he amount of slippage in turns\n\nThen look at the\
    \ encoder on each track, use the encoder value from the side that is highest to\
    \ get distance traveled by that side.  Assume the track in the inside is getting\
    \ the most slippage.  \n\nOr use the gyro to get heading and the average of the\
    \ two tracks for distance\n\nAlso remember that tracked vehicles can go up and\
    \ down step slopes.  f tracking position in X,Y remember to project the slope\
    \ onto the plan using cos(slope)"
