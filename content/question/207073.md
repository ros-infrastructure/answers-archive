  body_markdown: "ROS is designed so that communication between nodes, regardless\
    \ of where they are running, happens through the ROS master.\n\nI'm not familiar\
    \ with UWSIM, but if it's a sim that just has ROS plugins the following architecture\
    \ is pretty standard: \nYou can write your nodes so that each robot has a [publisher\
    \ and subscriber](http://wiki.ros.org/ROS/Tutorials/WritingPublisherSubscriber%28c%2B%2B%29).\n\
    \nWhen you initialize a publisher or subscriber with a topic: \n\n    ros::Publisher\
    \ chatter_pub = n.advertise<std_msgs::String>(\"chatter\", 1000);\n\nThat topic\
    \ is created and the ROS master handles all communication to it. In this case\
    \ the topic is called `chatter`. Aside from writing the publisher/subscriber nodes\
    \ there are no additional steps to take when you want a topic created.\n\nSo,\
    \ for each simulated robot you would have a node that handles outbound communication\
    \ through a publisher and inbound through a subscriber. If your robots are called\
    \ rob_one and rob_two, then their publishers can publish on topics: `rob_one_timestamp`\
    \ and `rob_two_timestamp`, respectively. Then rob_one's subscriber can subscribe\
    \ to `rob_two_timestamp` and vice versa.\n\nIt's a bit tough without seeing some\
    \ of your code, and your CMakeLists.txt to understand why it isn't compiling. "
