    Yes there is : [camera calibration](http://www.ros.org/wiki/camera_calibration) package.

    [EDIT] By the way, it permits to calibrate RGB camera, IR camera. And one camera reguarding the other one. But not the depth.

    So if you use multiple kinect, the depth may be a bit shifted from one to the other one.
