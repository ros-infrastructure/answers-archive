  body_markdown: "ODOM messages contain 2 different sets of data. \n\nThe first is\
    \ the robot POSE in the ODOM frame. This can contain non-zero Y as part of the\
    \ pose(location of the robot).\n\nThe second part is the TWIST message that shows\
    \ movement. It should not have a non-zero Y for differential drive.  For a holonomic\
    \ robot having non-zero Y would be expected. "
