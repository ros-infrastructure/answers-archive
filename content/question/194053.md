  body_markdown: "The typical Nav setup has at least three tf frames: One for the\
    \ map, one for the odometry root, and one for the robot. As described on [this\
    \ page](http://wiki.ros.org/amcl?distro=indigo#Transforms), the link between odom\
    \ and robot usually comes from the robot's odometry, and the link between the\
    \ map and the odom comes from AMCL. In order to use an alternate localization\
    \ method, you just need something else that provides that transform. "
