  body_markdown: "I assume the question to be: \"What values do we add to a robot\
    \ by building a world in gazebo simulator, like the one [here](http://www.ros.org/wiki/simulator_gazebo/Tutorials/SpawningObjectInSimulation)\
    \ ?\" \n\nAnswer: If you build a world like that one, a cup on top of a table,\
    \ in gazebo, you could write and verify a code for PR2 to grab the cup in simulation\
    \ and later implement the code in the real PR2 without much trouble. For example\
    \ you could find the proper orientation of the cup at which the robot picks it\
    \ up easily ([here](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3tt3sp1zq3Q)), or the kind\
    \ of objects (cup, bottle or box) which the robot identifies easily, or the orientation\
    \ for handing over of objects (see the images [here](http://www.cc.gatech.edu/~maya/papers/cakmak11_iros_handover.pdf).\
    \ Or build a world with doors and try to get the robot open the doors in simulation\
    \ before you do it in real. Not just for PR2, it might give you ideas for other\
    \ robots too. Etc..Etc! (links are just to give you ideas, not the exact ones)\n\
    \nOr if you were looking for advantages of gazebo simulator in general look [here](http://books.google.co.in/books?id=fbgVQABxDEQC&pg=PA75&lpg=PA75&dq=advantages+of+gazebo+simulator&source=bl&ots=XHGUh1ptV0&sig=DiSva48lV-dS8Ew93Jtnpu2fv2Y&hl=en&sa=X&ei=GEskUfH5AsuOrgf-iYDYAw&ved=0CEgQ6AEwAw#v=onepage&q=advantages%20of%20gazebo%20simulator&f=false).
