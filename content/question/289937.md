    \ raw out:=/xxxx/image_raw/raw\n\n\nNow in your node, you can subscribe to the\
    \ raw topic, convert to cv2 image and save to disk.([Ref](https://answers.ros.org/question/210294/ros-python-save-snapshot-from-camera/)).\
    \ \n\nEDIT: You can directly convert the compressed ROS image to opencv matrix\
    \ by using compressed_imgmsg_to_cv2(cmprs_img_msg). ([Ref](http://docs.ros.org/lunar/api/cv_bridge/html/python/))."
