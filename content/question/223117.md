    The command is: <pre><code>rosrun &lt;PACKAGE&gt; &lt;EXECUTABLE&gt; [ARGS]</code></pre>
    where angle and square brackets indicate <i>required</i> and <i>optional</i> items, respectively. According to what you've written, you've only done: <pre><code>rosrun &lt;PACKAGE&gt;</code></pre>
    and are missing the executable. You should be able to &quot;tab-complete&quot; (hit the TAB key twice in rapid succession) to get a list of valid executables.
