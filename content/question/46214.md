    You should use [actionlib](http://ros.org/wiki/actionlib) to send goals to your robot as shown in [this tutorial](http://ros.org/wiki/navigation/Tutorials/SendingSimpleGoals). The action client provides the method [cancelGoal](http://ros.org/doc/electric/api/actionlib/html/classactionlib_1_1SimpleActionClient.html#ab267cd5f2e3bb0e180a91b2bf1666ef5). When you call it, the robot should stop moving.
