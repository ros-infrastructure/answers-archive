    As @DimitriProsser said, simulating non-rigid bodies in Gazebo is tricky and not supported natively. An example of how such bodies can still be emulated by using particles can be seen in [this video](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q0iRf4O1MOQ), but that is a special case that probably required quite some effort to realize.

    Some rigid foot items like a milk carton and others are available in the [cob_gazebo_worlds](http://www.ros.org/wiki/cob_gazebo_worlds) package, in case you don't want to model them yourself. Spawned into the IPA kitchen environment, they look like in this image:
