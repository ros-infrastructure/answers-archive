  body_markdown: "robot_pose_ekf produces a [PoseWithCovarianceStamped](http://docs.ros.org/api/geometry_msgs/html/msg/PoseWithCovarianceStamped.html)\
    \ message. This is due to the fact that robot_pose_ekf does not include linear\
    \ or angular velocities in its state estimate; it only estimates the pose (x,\
    \ y, z, roll, pitch, yaw) of the robot. You should change your callbacks to accept\
    \ PoseWithCovarianceStamped messages. \n\nIf you need an Odometry message, though,\
    \ I would encourage you to try the [robot_localization](http://wiki.ros.org/robot_localization)\
    \ package."
