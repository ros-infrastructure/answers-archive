  body_markdown: "Answering your comments, a RGB-D images is simply an rgb image associated\
    \ with a depth image.\n\nAccording to the rtabmap documentation, all the infos\
    \ coming from the kinect are given as entry in the rtabmap node as 3 msgs : \n\
    \n 1. A sensor_msgs/Image which gives the\n    rgb image\n 2. A sensor_msgs/Image\
    \ which gives the\n    depth image\n 3. A sensors_msgs/Camerainfo which\n    gives\
    \ the info of the rgb camera.\n\nSo what you can do ss give the left rgb image\
    \ and left camera info directly as if it was the kinect. \nThen for the depth\
    \ image, you add a node which contains a stereo algorithm for exemple [this one](http://wiki.ros.org/stereo_image_proc)\
    \ then you define its output image as the depth image in entry of the rtabmap.\n\
    If you don't know about it, you should look into what's called \"remapping\" to\
    \ do those steps.\n"
