    Some of your options are:

    * Create an STL or DAE and import it, as you mentioned. Here's a [tutorial](http://gazebosim.org/tutorials?tut=import_mesh&cat=build_robot).

    * Use the [building editor](http://gazebosim.org/tutorials?tut=building_editor&cat=build_world) and make the curved walls out of many small straight walls.

    * Instead of making the walls by hand with the editor, make them programmatically with embedded ruby, [here](https://bitbucket.org/osrf/gazebo_models/src/92888709c301429ba1b5236063702857026c38cc/cart_front_steer/?at=default)'s an example.

    Since you mentioned you're new to Gazebo, I also recommend the [beginner tutorial series](http://gazebosim.org/tutorials?cat=guided_b&tut=guided_b1) to get you started.
