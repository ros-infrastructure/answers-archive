  body_markdown: "To Quote [the wiki](http://wiki.ros.org/dwa_local_planner):\n\n\
    > 2.1.1 Published Topics\n> \n> ~<name>/global_plan (nav_msgs/Path)\n> The portion\
    \ of the global plan that\n> the local planner is currently\n> attempting to follow.\
    \ Used primarily\n> for visualization purposes. \n> \n> ~<name>/local_plan (nav_msgs/Path)\
    \ The\n> local plan or trajectory that scored\n> the highest on the last cycle.\
    \ Used\n> primarily for visualization purposes.\n\nBasically, `global_plan` is\
    \ what is currently viewed as \"follow this path\", i.e. the **Input** to the\
    \ planner, which is not the full path to the goal. Otherwise, it might be that\
    \ tremendous shortcuts could appear. How long this is depends on several Parameters,\
    \ maily those in 2.2.3 of the wiki.\n\nThe local plan is the adjusted/sampled\
    \ trajectory which is executed in the following, i.e. the **Output**. The execution,\
    \ however, happens in terms of velocities that are published through move_base.\n"
