To break it down as simple as possible:

When working with a Realsense D435 with the respective package, you can enable the 'colorizer' filter which delivers an RGB depth image topic, meaning that blue colors are close, red colors are far, and the regular color spectrum inbetween. I have to work with rosbags filled with colorized depth data.

I want to convert this rgb8 encoded depth image into 32FC1 while still having all of the depth information. [Making use of OpenCV's cv2.COLOR_RGB2GRAY](https://github.com/ros-perception/depthimage_to_laserscan/issues/36#issuecomment-658526347) to convert it leads to nothing, as a blue color won't end up black, while a red color won't turn white.

I hope I stated my problem appropriate. I did my research, but as I am new to OpenCV and image processing in general, I had no success so far to find an appropriate conversion. As I am struggling to read cpp code, I am not able to [reverse engineer](https://github.com/IntelRealSense/realsense-ros/search?q=colorizer) the process where the grayscale depth image gets converted into that rgb8 encoded format.
