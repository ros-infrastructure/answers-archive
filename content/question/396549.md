    Since you have installed ROS on Windows successfully, you should be able to complete the tutorials as most catkin commands are available with the installation.

    For example you could use:

        catkin_create_pkg helloworld roscpp rospy std_msgs

    If you want to integrate to Visual Studio, I suggest take a look at this [tutorial](https://github.com/Brabalawuka/RosOnWindows), it's not the most updated tutorial but it will give you a good idea.
