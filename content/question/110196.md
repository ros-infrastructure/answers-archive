    I think probably your ROS_IP/ROS_HOSTNAME/ROS_MASTER_URI are not correctly set on the turtlebot for multi-device-pc communication. Some information on the [roswiki](http://wiki.ros.org/ROS/EnvironmentVariables#ROS_IP.2BAC8-ROS_HOSTNAME) and the [turtlebot wiki](http://wiki.ros.org/Robots/TurtleBot/Network%20Setup#TurtleBot_Setup).

    The 'dumb' error message unfortunately is a late hydro regression I have yet to fix - it used to actually provide a hint to make sure you have your ROS_IP/ROS_HOSTNAME set.
