    That question depends on what kind of budget you have but it's likely a higher level project. The cheapest quadrotor on the market at the moment is the [AR Drone](http://www.amazon.com/Parrot-AR-Drone-Quadricopter-Controlled-Android/dp/B003ZVSHB0/ref=sr_1_1?ie=UTF8&qid=1335154142&sr=8-1). While a lot of fun to fly they are limited and may or may not be applicable to your project. You can build something considerably more capable for about $600. I've done this by combining a [Guai 330X](http://www.rcgroups.com/forums/showthread.php?t=1229089) chassis (which is no longer for sale) and an [ArduPilot Mega](http://store.diydrones.com/ArduPilot_Mega_kit_p/kt-apm-01.htm). Beware that this route requires a sizable time investment to achieve programmatic control over such a device. If you are not tied to the idea of using real robots you could consider the [hector_quadrotor statck](http://www.ros.org/news/2012/03/announcement-of-hector-quadrotor-stack.html). IIRC it is a quadrotor simulator built on Gazebo and should allow you to work with a swarm.
