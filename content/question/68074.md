    I had the same problem. Here's the solution:

     1. Open Preferences
     2. Go to C/C++ -> Indexer
     3. Uncheck "Allow heuristic resolution of includes" and click OK
     4. Right-click on your project
     5. Click Index -> Rebuild

    Cheers!
