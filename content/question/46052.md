    I'm not sure about the availability of what you request for ROS/rviz (hopefully someone else will shed light on this), but OpenRAVE offers functionality for this. It is pretty easy to convert a URDF based robot arm to the OpenRAVE format, so you could give that a shot. I covered the conversion process as part of a answer [here](http://answers.ros.org/question/12846/could-ik-solver-called-by-arm_navigation-auto-generated-file-work-for-5-dof-manipulator/).

    See also the [kinematicreachability](http://openrave.org/docs/latest_stable/openravepy/databases.kinematicreachability/) OpenRAVE docs and the other [database generators](http://openrave.org/docs/latest_stable/databases/#openrave-database-generators).
