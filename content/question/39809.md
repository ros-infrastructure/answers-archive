    You need to supply the port parameter in minimal.launch. See [http://ros.org/wiki/turtlebot_node#Parameters](http://ros.org/wiki/turtlebot_node#Parameters)

    You'll have to change minimal.launch as follows:

    FROM:

        <!-- Turtlebot Driver -->
        <node pkg="turtlebot_node" type="turtlebot_node.py" name="turtlebot_node" output="screen" respawn="true" args="--respawnable">
          <param name="bonus" value="false" />
          <param name="update_rate" value="30.0" />
        </node>

    TO:

        <!-- Turtlebot Driver -->
        <node pkg="turtlebot_node" type="turtlebot_node.py" name="turtlebot_node" output="screen" respawn="true" args="--respawnable">
          <param name="bonus" value="false" />
          <param name="update_rate" value="30.0" />
          <param name="port" value="/dev/irobot_create" />
        </node>

    The ROS wiki contains more documentation [here](http://ros.org/wiki/roslaunch/XML) if you want to read up on supplying parameters through launch files.
