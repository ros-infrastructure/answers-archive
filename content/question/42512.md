    \ the following:\n\n    rosed gazebo_worlds empty.world\n\nand change \n\n   \
    \ <dt>0.001</dt>\n\nto something like\n\n    <dt>0.0001</dt>\n\nAnd then, whenever\
    \ you launch the simulator, you just wait until everything has loaded and just\
    \ set back the step size to whichever you prefer."
