    They are published over a gazebo transport topic. [This tutorial](http://gazebosim.org/tutorials?tut=topics_subscribed&cat=transport) shows how to subscribe to a topic. The message is an [ImageStamped](https://bitbucket.org/osrf/gazebo/src/cddf2613ce88d814cbe599aded96b356a5ffefd0/gazebo/msgs/image_stamped.proto?at=gazebo7&fileviewer=file-view-default) message which includes an [Image](https://bitbucket.org/osrf/gazebo/src/cddf2613ce88d814cbe599aded96b356a5ffefd0/gazebo/msgs/image.proto?at=gazebo7&fileviewer=file-view-default) message. The data formats are listed in [gazebo/common/Image.hh](https://bitbucket.org/osrf/gazebo/src/cddf2613ce88d814cbe599aded96b356a5ffefd0/gazebo/common/Image.hh?at=gazebo7&fileviewer=file-view-default#Image.hh-49)
