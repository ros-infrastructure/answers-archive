    Besides a light's pose, they also have a [direction](http://sdformat.org/spec?ver=1.6&elem=light#light_direction) property. [Here](https://bitbucket.org/osrf/gazebo_models/src/4b4490dc9e938b42d2e9c8c7cce1bad177ad65f5/sun/model.sdf?fileviewer=file-view-default#model.sdf-15)'s where it is defined for the sun.

    Unfortunately, the direction is not reflected in the 3D green lines, [nor it is displayed in the world tree](https://bitbucket.org/osrf/gazebo/issues/1405/display-light-direction-on-world-tree). Basically, as a new user, you had no way of knowing it ;)
