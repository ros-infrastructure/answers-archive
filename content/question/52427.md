  body_markdown: "The error is to be fixed in your CMakeLists.txt file and manifest.xml.\n\
    \nAs the Page here says: http://ros.org/wiki/cereal_port\n\n\"make sure you have\
    \ the serial_communication stack installed and don't forget to add cereal_port\
    \ as a dependency on your package\"\n\nSo install ros-fuerte-serial-communication\
    \ unless you already have, then add \n\n    <depend package=\"cereal_port\" />\n\
    \nto your manifest.xml. That should do the trick."
