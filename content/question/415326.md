    > will ros2 be available soon on ubuntu 23

    Both Rolling and Iron Irwini [only support Ubuntu Jammy](https://www.ros.org/reps/rep-2000.html#iron-irwini-may-2023-november-2024).

    I'm not aware of any ROS 2 release which has plans to support Ubuntu `23.x`.

    > is it worth to install still 22?

    Ubuntu Jammy is an LTS release. According to [The Ubuntu lifecycle and release cadence](https://ubuntu.com/about/release-cycle), it will be supported until (at least) 2032. That's about 9 years from now.

    We can't know your requirements, but at least from the perspective of "would Ubuntu 22 be supported long enough?", I'd say, *yes*.

    ROS 2 Humble (first ROS 2 release to support Jammy) is also LTS, and [will be supported until 2027](https://www.ros.org/reps/rep-2000.html#humble-hawksbill-may-2022-may-2027).
