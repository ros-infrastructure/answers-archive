    Global planner plans whole path towards your goal and local planner is responsible for execution (publishing velocities), to follow global plan as closely as possible and according to it's configuration. You can find list of common global and local planners [here.](http://wiki.ros.org/nav_core#BaseGlobalPlanner) And DWA is a local planner, you can find it in the list and read about what it does and configuration params.

      If a planner is not specified in launch file, then default one is used, which in case of move_base is GlobalPlanner. Within base_node you can pass both local and global planner as following, and also load their configurations using rosparam.

          <param name="base_local_planner" value="dwa_local_planner/DWAPlannerROS"/>
          <param name="base_global_planner" value="global_planner/GlobalPlanner" />
