    If it were me, I'd feed the IMU and magnetometer data to [imu_filter_madwick](http://wiki.ros.org/imu_filter_madgwick). Then I'd fuse the output of that node with the wheel odometry data in an `r_l` EKF instance. That's a pretty straightforward setup, and the EKF [config example](https://github.com/cra-ros-pkg/robot_localization/blob/799ad4481b8b16b12a50c34725dbbe4d82b4a84a/params/ekf_template.yaml) would be a good place to start.
