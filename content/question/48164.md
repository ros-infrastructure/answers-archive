    \ So, I'll respond to why your arm keeps moving.\n\nActually, your example already\
    \ hints the answer. \n\n    Action failed: ACTIVE\n    Timed out achieving goal\
    \ A\n    Action failed: PENDING\n\nYour action goal only failed, because it got\
    \ timed out (go to A in X seconds).\nHowever, move_arm uses another action server\
    \ for executing arm motions (FollowJointTrajectoryAction). Unfortunately, move_arm\
    \ does not cancel the execution action, once its own goal has been aborted/preempted.\
    \ In your case that is not too bad, but in some cases you really want to stop\
    \ the execution.\n\nHave a look at [this Q&A](http://answers.ros.org/question/39257/interrupting-a-joint-trajectory/#44634)\
    \ to find out how to cancel the execution, if you cancel a move_arm action."
