  body_markdown: "Using a monocular camera in the general case this is not possible.\
    \ The problem is that a 2D point on a camera image does not define a point in\
    \ 3D space relative to the camera.\n\nYou can solve this problem in two ways;\
    \ restrict the problem or get a 3D camera system. \n\nYou could restrict the problem\
    \ to points on a horizontal surface, then if the pose of the camera relative to\
    \ this surface was known then point in 3D space could be determined. \n\nYou could\
    \ use either a stereo camera or RGB-D camera instead which would provide you with\
    \ depth information. This additional information means that a point on an image\
    \ then does define a point in 3D space. \n\nI hope this helps to get you started. "
