I'm using ROS Melodic on a Raspberry Pi 2 and trying to subscribe to a publisher node, but they won't properly connect. Running `roscore` then starting a node publishing to `/heading` followed by `roswtf` yields the following error message:

    ERROR The following nodes should be connected but aren't:
    * /compass_5809_1619560617004->/rostopic_5824_1619560644400 (/self_heading)

Additionally, `rostopic echo /heading` on the RPi doesn't produce anything. `rosnode ping <node>` works, outputting something like:

    pi@raspberrypi:~ $ rosnode ping /compass_5809_1619560617004 
    rosnode: node is [/compass_5809_1619560617004]
    pinging /compass_5809_1619560617004 with a timeout of 3.0s
    xmlrpc reply from http://192.168.2.2:40401/     time=7.442951ms
    xmlrpc reply from http://192.168.2.2:40401/     time=5.650043ms
    xmlrpc reply from http://192.168.2.2:40401/     time=7.437944ms
    xmlrpc reply from http://192.168.2.2:40401/     time=7.274866ms
    ping average: 6.951451ms

`echo $ROS_MASTER_URI` and `echo $ROS_IP` yield `http://192.168.2.2:11311` and `192.168.2.2`, respectively.

I had this originally configured to work with an external PC, which actually works correctly when I do `rostopic echo /heading`.

I've tried unsetting both `ROS_MASTER_URI` and `ROS_IP` and setting them to various IPs in `/etc/hosts` but it still doesn't work. What am I doing wrong? Why can I connect to the nodes from another machine but not on the RPi itself?
