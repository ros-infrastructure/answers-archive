  body_markdown: " General Answer, also valid for 3D:\n\n 1. Translate your point,\
    \ so that your rotation axis goes through the origin of your coordinate system.\n\
    \ 2. Rotate the transformed point around your axis.\n 3. Translate the origin\
    \ of the coordinate system back to the old position.\n\nIn your case (P is  the\
    \ point to rotate, F is the rotation point, R is the rotation matrix): \n\nP'\
    \ = F+(R*(P-F))\n\n\n\n"
