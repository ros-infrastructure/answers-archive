    It is absolutely not recommended to use `rosbuild` with `indigo`. According to the [migration guide](http://wiki.ros.org/indigo/Migration#rosbuild_support) builds from source are still supported. And there is [a tutorial](http://wiki.ros.org/catkin/Tutorials/using_rosbuild_with_catkin) on how to use rosbuild with catkin.

    However, as you cannot depend from a `catkin` package to a `rosbuild` package, it might limit the portability and maintainability of your packages.
