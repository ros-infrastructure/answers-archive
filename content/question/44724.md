    Sure. You can have all ros distros installed in parallel. You can switch between them by sourcing the different setup.bash files. Just make sure you create separate [overlays](http://www.ros.org/wiki/fuerte/Installation/Overlays) for different ros distros.
  comments:
  - body_markdown: |-
      Also in this case, you should not source setup.sh in your .bashrc, but decide every time you open a new shell which distro you want to use, and source the corresponding setup.sh. Swithcing between distros in the same shell can cause errors.
