    There's an open [issue](https://github.com/gazebosim/gz-sim/issues/628) about adding such a tutorial, but direct interaction with the ECM is not well documented partly because we were hoping to provide convenience classes (similar to Gazebo-classic) that interact with the ECM for you. So, for working with joints, you can take a look at the [Joint](https://gazebosim.org/api/sim/7/classgz_1_1sim_1_1Joint.html) convenience class. If the class doesn't have all the APIs you need, you can take a look at the [Joint implementation](https://github.com/gazebosim/gz-sim/blob/bb85d05a373eb8cb21d0c8881918374234b2227f/src/Joint.cc) as an example.
