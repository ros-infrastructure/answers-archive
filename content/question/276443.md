  body_markdown: |-
    Logger allows programmer to control the verbosity of the print statements, with change of verbosity level in configuration file, obviously without recompiling the code. As you have read in the [Overview page](http://wiki.ros.org/roscpp/Overview/Logging), ROS logger support different versions. By changing the [verbosity level](http://wiki.ros.org/Verbosity%20Levels) in the [configuration file](http://wiki.ros.org/rosconsole#Configuration), one can control what will get printed on the system console. Please read section 2 of [rosconsole](http://wiki.ros.org/rosconsole) page for further details. Also rqt_console, allow much better control over print statements and display of them.
