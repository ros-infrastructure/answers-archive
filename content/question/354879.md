    I think your problem is that `<xacro:macro name="grinding_tool" params="ee_link_name">` down to its corresponding `</xacro:macro>` defines a macro that can be instantiated potentially multiple times elsewhere, but does not create the contents on its own. Some more info on macros can be found here: [http://wiki.ros.org/xacro#Macros](http://wiki.ros.org/xacro#Macros)

    If this is the entire issue, then simply removing the `<xacro:macro name="grinding_tool" params="ee_link_name">` and `</xacro:macro>` should fix it. Based on the info you posted, this is all that jumps out to me that could be the problem.
