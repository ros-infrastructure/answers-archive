  body_markdown: "Your question is rather vague and doesn't have enough details. What\
    \ do you mean by precision? What kind of tracking? What's your desired framerate?\
    \ Is color important? What resolution? Field of view? Etc. \n\nFirst, you should\
    \ take a look at the [list of existing camera drivers](http://wiki.ros.org/camera_drivers)\
    \ for ROS. There may be other drivers out there. We've had good luck over here\
    \ with Allied Vision cameras, and they have quite a few to choose from.\n\nSecond,\
    \ I think you will and should be basing your decision on your application requirements.\
    \ Yes, you will save time if the camera is already supported, but you can always\
    \ write a ROS wrapper for a camera. You can't add new features to a camera.\n\n\
    Short version: see what Allied Systems has to offer, and then choose your lens\
    \ appropriately."
