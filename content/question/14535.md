  body_markdown: "You can activate the gravity of each link in your robot in the SDF\
    \ file and the self collide too, \nAt the end of the inertial tags in each link\
    \ you can put this code:\n\n    <gravity>1</gravity>\n    <self_collide>1</self_collide>\n\
    \nBut if your model was made only with gazebo (no with ROS URDF) this may be not\
    \ your real problem; you may have issues with the inertia of your robot, because,\
    \ gazebo uses to activate gravity by itself in all the models, or you can have\
    \ misplaced the center of mass in your links.\n"
