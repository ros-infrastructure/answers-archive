    AMCL is meant for localization only (based on a known map) and will not work with a non-static map. SLAM (Simultaneous Localization and Mapping) methods are meant for the task you're looking at. Options for that are [gmapping](http://www.ros.org/wiki/gmapping) and (shameless plug :) ) [hector_mapping](http://www.ros.org/wiki/hector_mapping).

    I suppose you could look into hacking AMCL to work with a map generated online by some other approach, but for that other approach to be able to generate a consistent map you need localization. And that's the chicken-egg problem of SLAM ;)
