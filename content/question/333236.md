I believe I found a possible bug in the WritingServiceClient(C++) tutorial. I was unable to get [this tutorial](http://wiki.ros.org/ROS/Tutorials/WritingServiceClient%28c%2B%2B%29), to successfully build in `catkin_make`, until I went into the AddTwoInts.srv file created [here](http://wiki.ros.org/ROS/Tutorials/CreatingMsgAndSrv), and edited the contents to be

    int64 a
    int64 b
    ---
    int64 sum

instead of the default file

    int64 A
    int64 B
    ---
    int64 Sum

Took me forever to figure out what was wrong. I am sure this is frustrating many people who are going through the tutorials.
