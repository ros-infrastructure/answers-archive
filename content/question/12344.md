  body_markdown: "The \"msg.covariance\" field represents the uncertainty in the deterministic\
    \ field \"msg.pose\". \n\nThis uncertainty is described by a [normal distribution](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Normal_distribution)\
    \ centered on the pose field so the probability of the state x follows:  p(x)~\
    \ N(msg.pose,msg.covariance)"
  comments:
