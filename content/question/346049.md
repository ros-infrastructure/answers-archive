    \nTechnically if you talk in terms of simulation with Gazebo, joint states are\

    \ state controller. You can just verify it by launching the ROS node for the same\

Is `joint_state_controller` required when working with `diff_drive_controller`?  Or is `joint_state_controller` only required to publish `JointState` messages for other nodes and not relevant for `diff_drive_controller` to function properly?

For example [eborghi10/my_ROS_mobile_robot](https://github.com/eborghi10/my_ROS_mobile_robot/tree/e04acfd3e7eb4584ba0aab8a969a74d6c30eed34/my_robot_control/config) uses two yaml config files, [one](https://github.com/eborghi10/my_ROS_mobile_robot/blob/master/my_robot_control/config/my_robot_common.yaml) containing `diff_drive_controller/DiffDriveController` as expected and [the other one](https://github.com/eborghi10/my_ROS_mobile_robot/blob/master/my_robot_control/config/my_robot_control.yaml) contains `joint_state_controller/JointStateController` and even `effort_controllers/JointEffortController`. Are these controllers really required?
