  body_markdown: "If you're navigating without a map, then your robot will only be\
    \ using distances and bearings to get to places. Therefore the robot will need\
    \ to measure distance and bearings. To get to different waypoints it would be\
    \ simply geometry to get to one point or another with the accuracy depending on\
    \ how well your robot tracked its path (dead reckoning). \n\nIf there is not SLAM\
    \ or map then the idea of a planner is redundant. The robot just needs a start\
    \ point and a list of navigation points that can be determined in relation to\
    \ the start point.   "
