    There's no out-of-the-box NED support, but users can convert the values received from simulation.

    The default of Ignition Gazebo is ENU, and some plugins may support other XXU variations by handling different `<spherical_coordinate><heading>`s (NWU, WSU and SEU). But since it's difficult to make sure every plugin is respecting the heading, I think it's safer to go with ENU (zero heading) and convert to NED at the controller interface.
