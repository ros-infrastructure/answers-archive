  body_markdown: "go to your home folder, on the top menu hit view, then select show\
    \ hidden files. Click on the .bashrc file and scroll to the bottom and change\
    \ the line: \n\nexport ROS_PACKAGE_PATH=WHATEVER YOU WANT\n\nwhere WHATEVER YOU\
    \ WANT is a valid location on your file system, multiple locations can be seperated\
    \ by a ':' for example mine is\n\nexport ROS_PACKAGE_PATH=~/ros_workspace:/opt/ros/electric/stacks"
