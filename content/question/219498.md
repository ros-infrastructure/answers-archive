    ---

    Edit: looks like `gazebo2rviz` is declaring `rospy` and `pysdf` both as `build_depend` and `run_depend` in [package.xml](https://github.com/andreasBihlmaier/gazebo2rviz/blob/b4ad2bea92a7f8a97d9d347437b26d87839c65e2/package.xml#L12-L13) and [CMakeLists.txt](https://github.com/andreasBihlmaier/gazebo2rviz/blob/27e0e38bfe7d1fb16ec5a885da755ad1270f3e63/CMakeLists.txt#L5-L6). That is not necessary for Python only packages (see the [catkin documentation](http://docs.ros.org/jade/api/catkin/html/howto/format1/python_module_dependencies.html)).

    Quick fix: remove those lines (but leave the `run_depend` elements).

    Nicer: report upstream and provide a Pull Request against [andreasBihlmaier/gazebo2rviz](https://github.com/andreasBihlmaier/gazebo2rviz).
