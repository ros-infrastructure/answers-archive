  body_markdown: |-
    The camera follow distance is hard-coded as variable `origDistance` to 8.0 meters in [Camera::Update](https://bitbucket.org/osrf/gazebo/src/e510a6adbdc4f92f2a615a3a9fd5068abc4fd80f/gazebo/rendering/Camera.cc?at=default#cl-357). You will have to build from source to change this value.

    As an alternative, you can attach a camera sensor to your robot at a specified offset and use the Topic Visualizer to watch the camera images.
