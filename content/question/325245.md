  body_markdown: "The repos file is an artifact of our development process from before\
    \ ROS2 distros existed. You can get what you want using the rosinstall_generator\
    \ just like you can with ROS 1 distros. e.g.\n\nRelease tags:\n\n    rosinstall_generator\
    \ --deps --rosdistro dashing ros_base\n\nUpstream tags:\n\n    rosinstall_generator\
    \ --deps --upstream --rosdistro dashing ros_base\n\nUpstream devel branches\n\n\
    \    rosinstall_generator --deps --upstream-devel --rosdistro dashing ros_base\n\
    \nFor faster checkouts you don't plan to edit you can use the tarballs option.\
    \ \n\n"
