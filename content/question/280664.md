    You can do this with ROS times and durations: [roscpp](http://wiki.ros.org/roscpp/Overview/Time#Time_and_Duration_Arithmetic), [rospy](http://wiki.ros.org/rospy/Overview/Time#Converting_Time_and_Duration_instances). You will have to save the previous message, then when you get a new one you can do `double elapsed_time = (msg.header.stamp - prev_msg.header.stamp).toSec()` (rospy has similar logic).
