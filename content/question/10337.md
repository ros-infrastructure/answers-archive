    That should be possible. You'll want to run everything described in the [tutorial](http://www.ros.org/wiki/p2os/Tutorials/Getting%20Started%20with%20p2os) on the computer on the robot, and you'll have to attach your joystick to computer on the robot.

    You shouldn't have to set ROS_MASTER_URI since all of your ros nodes are running on the same computer.
