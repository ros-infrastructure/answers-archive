  body_markdown: "stage's published topics (including `tf`) aren't in a namespace,\
    \ so you can't remap them in them one go.  But you can remap them individually\
    \ using the [usual remapping arguments](http://www.ros.org/wiki/Remapping%20Arguments).\n\
    \nE.g., at the command-line (assuming one robot):\n\n    roscd stage     \n  \
    \  ./bin/stageros odom:=stage/odom base_scan:=stage/base_scan tf:=stage/tf world/willow-erratic.world\n\
    \nOut of curiosity, why do you want to remap stage's outputs?"
