  body_markdown: "There isn\xB4t really a specific platform that I heard about to\
    \ work best. Ideally, your platform should\n\n - have a Hokuyo UTM-30 (or similar)\n\
    \   LIDAR\n - provide enough computing power (old\n   Atom Z530 was sufficient,\
    \ most more\n   modern options should be sufficient)\n - provide sensible IMU\
    \ data (especially\n   roll/pitch). That\xB4s basically a given\n   for a quadrotor,\
    \ as it wouldn\xB4t be\n   able to fly otherwise ;)\n\nA few examples of (mostly\
    \ Mikrokopter based) quads running hector_mapping can be found in [this playlist\
    \ on YouTube](http://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PL0E462904E5D35E29). [Here\
    \ in Fig. 4](http://www.gkmm.tu-darmstadt.de/publications/files/kohlbrecher2013opensource.pdf)\
    \ you can see an example of running a bag-file of the CCNY dataset through hector_mapping\
    \ (originally recorded with a Asctec quad).\n\n"
