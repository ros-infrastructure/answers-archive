  body_markdown: "If I am correct the BeagleBone Rev C has a `AM335x 1GHz ARM\xAE\
    \ Cortex-A8` CPU. This is a 32 bits CPU.\nIf you take a look at what Debian packages\
    \ are compiled on the top of the tutorial, you'll see that there is only `amd64`\
    \ and `arm64`\n\nThe BeagleBone CPU falls into the `armhf` (ARM Hard-Float) category,\
    \ there is no binary package available for your platform on Debian.\n\nYou have\
    \ two choices:\n\n - Compile from source (there is a link\n   for that at the\
    \ top of the tutorial\n   you mention) \n - Use Ubuntu on your\n   BeagleBone,\
    \ binaries are provided for\n   `armhf` CPUs platform on Ubuntu"
