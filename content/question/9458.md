    Note that you can publish/subscribe to `pcl::PointCloud` types directly using [pcl_ros](http://www.ros.org/wiki/pcl_ros); no need to write your own conversion to/from `PointCloud2`. `PointCloud2` and `pcl::PointCloud` were designed in tandem, so in general `PointCloud2` can hold any data expressible as a `pcl::PointCloud` and vice versa.
