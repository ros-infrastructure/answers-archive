    \ makes sense, even if you don't want to use your robot/driver with MoveIt, as\
    \ it provides a convenient way for clients to command your robot (in joint space).\n\
    \nFor a simple setup I would recommend to take a look at the [SimpleActionServer](http://wiki.ros.org/actionlib#Implementing_an_ActionServer),\
