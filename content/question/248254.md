    >  Is it through using [image_transport::SubscriberFilter](http://docs.ros.org/api/image_transport/html/classimage__transport_1_1SubscriberFilter.html) ?

    Yes. From the documentation you linked:

    > This class wraps [Subscriber](http://docs.ros.org/api/image_transport/html/classimage__transport_1_1Subscriber.html) as a "filter" compatible with the [message_filters](http://docs.ros.org/api/message_filters/html/c++/namespacemessage__filters.html) package. It acts as a highest-level filter, simply passing messages from an image transport subscription through to the filters which have connected to it.

    You should then be able to use the [ExactTime](http://wiki.ros.org/message_filters#ExactTime_Policy) or [Approximate Time](http://wiki.ros.org/message_filters#ApproximateTime_Policy) policies from `message_filters` to synchronise your topics.
