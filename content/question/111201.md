In ROS we can find two different packages to use publishers and subscribers in real-time:

 - realtime_tools (in ros_control) http://wiki.ros.org/realtime_tools?distro=hydro
 - rosrt (in ros_realtime) http://wiki.ros.org/rosrt?distro=groovy

The capacities of both packages look overlapped. Is any of them obsolete? Are they being developed by different research groups?
realtime_tools looks more updated, however it does not provide a realtime subscriber.
What are the main differences? What are the future guidelines of each project?
