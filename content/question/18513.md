    I assume you mean a ROS launch file. Check out [this tutorial](http://gazebosim.org/tutorials?tut=ros_roslaunch&cat=connect_ros), basically:


        <launch>
          <include file="$(find gazebo_ros)/launch/empty_world.launch">
            <arg name="world_name" value="<your_world>.world"/>
          </include>
        </launch>
