    Although it is possible to make it using *rviz* plugin I don't believe its what you want to achieve.
    *Rviz* is a tool for visualisation of what is seen by *the eyes of the robot*, via its sensors or cameras.

    I think that the better place for putting animated objects is the simulator *gazebo*, where you can build your own objects to be the part of the simulated world.
