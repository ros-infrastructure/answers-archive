  body_markdown: "Here is the more fancy udev solution that we use (needs the hokuyo_node\
    \ package) and /etc/ros/run.sh:\n\n    SUBSYSTEMS==\"usb\", KERNEL==\"ttyACM[0-9]*\"\
    , ATTRS{manufacturer}==\"Hokuyo Data Flex for USB\", ATTRS{product}==\"URG-Series\
    \ USB Driver\", MODE=\"0666\", GROUP=\"dialout\", PROGRAM==\"/etc/ros/run.sh hokuyo_node\
    \ getID %N q\", SYMLINK+=\"sensors/hokuyo_%c\"\n\nBesides access rights this uses\
    \ the getID util in hokuyo_node to provide device symlinks as /dev/sensors/hokuyo_SERIAL\
    \ to enable identifying specific sensors neverminds which ttyACM they land at.\n\
    \n/etc/ros/run.sh enables ROS\n\n    #!/bin/sh\n    . /etc/ros/setup.sh \n   \
    \ rosrun $@\n\n/etc/ros/setup.sh is a symlink to the setup.sh in /opt/ros/DISTRO/setup.sh"
