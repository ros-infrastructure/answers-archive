  body_markdown: "Hello Kansai,\n\n> I know that if I have a node that publishes messages\
    \ (in particular Image messages) I can record these messages with rosbag record\
    \ into a rosbag.\n\nYes, you can record the image message through ros bag file.\
    \ You can do so by the following the command. Even the message is single I think\
    \ it can record it.\n\n    rosbag record -o subset <Topic-Name-Image>\n\nAnd if\
    \ you want to record multiple topic then you can \n\nrosbag record -o subset <Topic-Name-Image\
    \ 1> <Topic-Name-Image 2>\n\nThe -O argument tells rosbag record to log to a file\
    \ named subset.bag.\n\nPlay back recording with:\n\n    rosbag play <bagfile-name>\n\
    \nYou can check your recording in Rviz just by subscribing to your recording node.\n\
    \nFor exporting single image here is a good document http://wiki.ros.org/rosbag/Tutorials/Exporting%20image%20and%20video%20data"
