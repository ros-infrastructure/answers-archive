  body_markdown: "Three  comments after seeing your param settings: \n\n1) As suggested\
    \ in [amcl wiki](http://wiki.ros.org/amcl) , set the odom_model_type to:\n\n \
    \   <param name=\"odom_model_type\" value=\"diff-corrected\"/>\n\n we changed\
    \ from \"diff\" to \"diff-corrected\" and experienced a significant improvement\n\
    \n2) You may also use more beams of the scan, it could improve the localization,\
    \ obviously paying computational cost.\n\n3) likelihood_field laser model only\
    \ uses laser_z_hit and laser_z_rand. Setting both to 0.5, means you are weighting\
    \ somehow equally randomness and map... I think you could trust a little more\
    \ on your map.\n\nbest\n\nandreu"

      "hit" refers to the situation when a laser beam hits an obstacle. "rand" means the uniform distribution which is used to model the situation in which there might exist some unexplained measurements.
