    you can use `tf2::transformToEigen` to convert `geometry_msgs::Transform` or `geometry_msgs::TransformStamped` to `Eigen::Isometry3d` as shown in [tf2_eigen.h](http://docs.ros.org/en/noetic/api/tf2_eigen/html/tf2__eigen_8h.html).

    The `Eigen::Isometry3d` is actually a 4x4 matrix.
