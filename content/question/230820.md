  body_markdown: "I assume you have a self made robot or something. The gmapping and\
    \ amcl (for localization only) need 3 thinks to work:\n - Odometry\n - Laser scans\
    \ (can also be fake laser scans)\n - Transformations from the laser scan to the\
    \ global position as measured by the motor encoders (which later will be corrected\
    \ by the localization)\n\nYou have to figure out by your own how to code it for\
    \ your robot in particular. A good starting point are the tutorials at [Navigation\
    \ tutorials](http://wiki.ros.org/navigation). \n\nWhat kind of sonar do you use?\
    \ A single sonar sensor won't make it, you need to fake a laser scanner with at\
    \ least 20-30 directions. Maybe a Kinect or a similar sensor would be a better\
    \ choice."
