    \    roslaunch velodyne_pointcloud 32e_points.launch calibration:=/home/user/32db.yaml\n\
    \n**Note:** set the `calibration` parameter to whatever your calibration file\
    \ is.\n\n\n----------\n\n\n**Update:**\n\nIt looks like it may not be [supported](http://wiki.ros.org/velodyne_driver#Supported_Devices):\n\
