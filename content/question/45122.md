  body_markdown: "Hello , think of your system(dc motors) as a lineer system which\
    \ is not\n xdot = A(t)x + B(t)u \n  y = C(t)x + D(t)u\n\nx is your state vectors\
    \ and u is your inputs. When you apply inputs( such as a step function) to your\
    \ system, you can't get same output because of system dynamics, etc..\n\nSo you\
    \ have to implement a PID controller( because it is the easiest one to implement)\
    \ to follow your inputs. If you have 2 wheels actuated by dc motor, you have to\
    \ apply pid controller for both of them. If third wheel is a castor wheel, you\
    \ can't apply pid control for it because it doesn't have any actuator.\n\nJumping\
    \ stuff occurs maybe because of your pid gains or damping ratio of the joints\
    \ in gazebo, have you added SimpleTransmission to your joints in your xacro file\
    \ such as shown below?\n\n    <transmission type=\"pr2_mechanism_model/SimpleTransmission\"\
    \ name=\"righwheeljoint\">\n                <actuator name=\"rightwheelmotor\"\
    \ />\n                <joint name=\"rightWheelJoint\" />\n                <mechanicalReduction>1.0</mechanicalReduction>\n\
    \                <motorTorqueConstant>1.32</motorTorqueConstant>\n    </transmission>\n\
    \n  \n\nHere is a great tutorial about dc motor modeling and controlling for continuous\
    \ time \n\n[modeling](http://www.engin.umich.edu/class/ctms/matlab42/examples/motor/motor.htm)\n\
    [controlling](http://www.engin.umich.edu/class/ctms/matlab42/examples/motor/pid2.htm)\n\
    \ndon't forget you apply your control algorithm in discrete time.\n\nHope it helps,\
    \ good luck"

Hi guys!

I got a robot simulated in Gazebo which i'd like to control easily. I made 3 controllers (thanks to the [tutorials](http://www.ros.org/wiki/pr2_mechanism/Tutorials/Writing%20a%20realtime%20joint%20controller)) : one to control the head and a controller for each wheel. So, I tried to [add a PID](http://ros.org/wiki/pr2_mechanism/Tutorials/Adding%20a%20PID%20to%20a%20realtime%20joint%20controller?highlight=%28%28pr2_mechanism%7CTutorials%29%29) to these controllers.       </gazebo>
