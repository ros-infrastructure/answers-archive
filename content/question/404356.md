    The canonical answer to this would likely be: [rqt_reconfigure](http://wiki.ros.org/rqt_reconfigure) (which also [exists for ROS 2](https://index.ros.org/p/rqt_reconfigure/)):

    ![rqt_reconfigure example](http://wiki.ros.org/rqt_reconfigure?action=AttachFile&do=get&target=reconfigure_gui2.png)

    Whether that would fit your requirements only you can decide of course.

    Your post confuses me somewhat though:

    > I've some years of experience in running ROS1 applications and tuning a limited number of scalar and vector parameters by means of `rosparam`.

    did you have to restart the node(s) for which you changed those parameters? Nodes re-reading their ROS parameters at runtime is a known anti-pattern, but that would be the only way to "tune" parameters while things are running.

    [dynamic_reconfigure](http://wiki.ros.org/dynamic_reconfigure) would be the infrastructure to use for runtime updating of parameters, but if you were already using that, I'd expect `rqt_reconfigure` to be a known tool.
