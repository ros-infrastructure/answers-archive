    Here are two options that come to mind:

     - Leaving Px4 alone and running fusion
       just on the ROS side.
       [robot_localization](http://wiki.ros.org/robot_localization)
       is probably the easiest and best
       documented sensor fusion approach for ROS currently.
       This way, there are two "loosely
       coupled" state estimates and you'd send commands to the PX4 based on your "ROS-side" state estimate without updating the internal PX4 estimator.
     - There are also other approaches like
       the
       [ethzasl_sensor_fusion](http://wiki.ros.org/ethzasl_sensor_fusion/Tutorials/Introductory%20Tutorial%20for%20Multi-Sensor%20Fusion%20Framework)
       approach that were developed with
       MAVs in mind. See [this page on the
       pixhawk wiki for extensive
       information](https://pixhawk.org/dev/ros/visual_estimation). If you manage to send a pose estimate on the topics described there to the PX4, things should work. I'm not sure in how far there is support for partial pose estimates however (e.g. a 2D subset of pose information generated from the two approaches you use). As the internal state estimate of the PX4 is updated here, there probably is a somewhat higher risk of messing up.
