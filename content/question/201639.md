    I'm not sure what the real solution is (such that gazebo will directly publish odometry and tf for multiple robots), but I put together a hacky solution which was usable for me. Check out the two launch files in [rosh_turtlebot_demo](https://github.com/dlaz/rosh_turtlebot_demo) for how to startup gazebo and spawn two robots, and [publish_odom.py](https://github.com/dlaz/rosh_turtlebot_demo/blob/master/scripts/publish_odom.py) for publishing odometry and tf.

    The key is subscribing to [gazebo/model_states](http://docs.ros.org/indigo/api/gazebo_msgs/html/msg/ModelState.html) and republishing the odom/tf data.
