    Hi @Scarley,

    The problem may be in dealing with vectors. First thing is why you are using a STL method (merge). If you are going to work with PointClouds why not using the [PCL](http://www.pointclouds.org/documentation/) library that already have a operator to merge pointclouds (+=).

    Second is that you will need the proper namespace for the method: `std::merge`
