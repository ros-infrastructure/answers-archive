    In the section titled [Adding Released Packages](http://wiki.ros.org/ROSberryPi/Installing%20ROS%20Indigo%20on%20Raspberry%20Pi#Adding_Released_Packages), include ``uuid_msgs`` in the ``rosinstall_generator`` command, along with any other released packages you use:

        $ rosinstall_generator uuid_msgs ros_comm ros_control joystick_drivers --rosdistro indigo --deps --wet-only --exclude roslisp --tar > indigo-custom_ros.rosinstall
