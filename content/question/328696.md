  body_markdown: "If you want to know the influence of different QoS settings I would\
    \ highly recommend checking out the shapes demo provided by most DDS vendors.\n\
    \nhttps://www.eprosima.com/index.php/products-all/eprosima-shapes-demo\n\nhttp://opendds.org/quickstart/GettingStartedShapesDemo.html\n\
    \nThis demo can give you a good idea of what certain QoS settings do by playing\
    \ around with different settings in the GUI.\n\nFor an explanation of the different\
    \ QoS options you could have a look here:\n\nhttp://download.prismtech.com/docs/Vortex/html/ospl/DDSTutorial/qos.html\
    \ \n\nDepending on your use case different QoS settings will apply.\nFor example:\n\
    \nLate-joiners need not receive old data -> use Volatile instead of Transient(-Local)\n\
    \nCommunication does not need to be reliable -> use Best Effort instead of Reliable\n\
    \nOnly the latest data is relevant -> use history depth of 1 instead of 5 \n\n\
    As you mentioned the choice of QoS settings can influence your systems performance\
    \ by a lot, so it is recommended to familiarize yourself with the meaning of these\
    \ settings. In most cases the default profiles will give decent results (the choice\
    \ of QoS settings is quite sensible), but best results are obtained by fitting\
    \ the QoS settings to your use-case. Given the limited information you provided\
    \ about your use-case I'm afraid I can not give you a more extensive answer on\
    \ what QoS settings to choose. \n"
