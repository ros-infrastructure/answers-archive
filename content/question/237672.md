    There is a bit more to publish/subscribe and client/*server* (or remote-procedure-call) than just the direction in which messages flow.

    A good paper that discusses the main benefits of the publish/subscribe communication *style* and compares it to traditional remote procedure call is [The Many Faces of Publish/Subscribe](http://www.cs.ru.nl/~pieter/oss/manyfaces.pdf) by Patrick Eugster et al.
