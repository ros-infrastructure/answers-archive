I use ur_modern_driver move UR3 . If the distance short, move success.
If the distance far , UR3 move a short distance(Not reached the goal)，stop and PolyScope show the error info:

    Runtime error: The program used too much time without instructing the robot what
    to do. Consider using a Wait or sync() to split long program sequences that do not
    move the robot.
