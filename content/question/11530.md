    \ the pointclouds afterwards. It cuts down on the diskspace, and makes recording\
    \ much more consistent. \n\nI had a [thread](http://answers.ros.org/question/1543/bag-a-kinect-for-more-than-30-seconds#2601)\

    \ The launch files were written back when the driver was called `openni_camera_unstable`,\
    \ so if you're running electric, you will have to modify them to point to `openni_launch`\
    \ instead. \n\nHope this helps."
