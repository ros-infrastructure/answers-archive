  body_markdown: "SOLVED: The problem was not with the cob trajectory controller but\
    \ with the `/arm_controller/joint_trajectory_controller/follow_joint_trajectory/goal`\
    \ topic that is published by MoveIt!. The planning group I created in the setup\
    \ assistant did not include all the arms joints and therefore the vector of positions\
    \ that MoveIt! published was too small causing COB trajectory controller to continuously\
    \ run out of range. "
