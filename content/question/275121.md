    It doesn't have to be a *standard* message, but `rosbag` works with messages, so anything in there will have to be some message type.

    ---

    Edit:

    > I am using a ROS driver.  Here is the link: https://github.com/GAVLab/novatel.

    That driver seems to only publish `NavSatFix` and `Odometry` messages.

    You could perhaps take a look at [novatel_span_driver](http://wiki.ros.org/novatel_span_driver). Provided it is compatible with your hardware, that would seem to publish a lot of low-level data: see the [novatel_msgs](http://docs.ros.org/jade/api/novatel_msgs/html/index-msg.html) docs.
