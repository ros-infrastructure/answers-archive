    You're using Ubuntu 17.10, but it's not supported on that distribution. From [ROS REP-3](http://www.ros.org/reps/rep-0003.html#lunar-loggerhead-may-2017-may-2019)

    > ## Lunar Loggerhead (May 2017 - May 2019)
    > Required Support for:
    >
    > - Ubuntu Xenial (16.04)
    > - Ubuntu Yakkety (16.10)
    > - Ubuntu Zesty (17.04)

    [Melodic is supported on 17.10](http://www.ros.org/reps/rep-0003.html#lunar-loggerhead-may-2017-may-2019), however

    > ## Melodic Morenia (May 2018 - May 2023)
    > Required Support for:
    >
    > - Ubuntu Artful (17.10)
    > - Ubuntu Bionic (18.04)
