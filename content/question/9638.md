    Just start your publisher on one machine and the subscriber on another, as described in [this tutorial](http://www.ros.org/wiki/ROS/Tutorials/MultipleMachines). This is not specific to wireless networks.

    In case you have to limit the bandwidth, have a look at the [topic_tools stack](http://www.ros.org/wiki/topic_tools/), especially the [throttle node](http://www.ros.org/wiki/topic_tools/throttle).
