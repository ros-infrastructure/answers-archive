    \ things with it. I get bored if I read or watch videos without doing anything.\n\
    \nIn that case (I too struggle to learn things in a vacuum), I recommend that\
    \ you identify a project/goal that interests you, *e.g. face-tracking turret that\
    \ tracks, identifies, and shoots foam darts at (specific) coworkers.* \n\nOnce\
    \ you have an interesting goal, try to achieve it using ROS. You will have a strong\
    \ incentive to learn about the parts of the ROS ecosystem that are relevant to\
    \ your goal.\n\n"
