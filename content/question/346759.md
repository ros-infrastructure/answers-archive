 IR camera
Published by the driver only when no subscriptions to the rgb image exist.

ir/camera_info (sensor_msgs/CameraInfo)

    Camera calibration and metadata. 

ir/image_raw (sensor_msgs/Image)

    Raw uint16 IR image.
