  body_markdown: "We debated this at length during the Thirdparty Library/Target Platforms\
    \ SIG:\n\nhttp://ros.org/wiki/fuerte/Planning/Thirdparty\n\nWe supported Maverick\
    \ for Electric and it was a *big* waste of time because:\n\n * Most people using\
    \ non-LTS releases upgrade to the next non-LTS release\n * It wasted a lot of\
    \ developer time providing compatibility fixes\n * non-LTS releases by Ubuntu\
    \ generally have significant bugs that hurt integration\n * it requires many more\
    \ servers for us to build another distribution\n\nApplying the same logic to Fuerte,\
    \ plus with Precise (new LTS) around the corner, it would be a huge developer\
    \ effort with little benefit. \n\nYou can still build Fuerte from source on Natty;\
    \ you just don't get binary builds nor Natty-specific patches."
