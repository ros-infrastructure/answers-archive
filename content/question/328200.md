  body_markdown: "Once you have received a LaserScan message you can access it's individual\
    \ measurement. Because you know the resolution of your sensor you can calculate\
    \ the angle for each measurement. With that angle and the distance that the measurement\
    \ represents you can filter out anything that is part of the platform. You will\
    \ then have a set of distances that correspond to the ball. \n\nA detailed description\
    \ of how to transform lidar measurements to coordinates can be found [here](https://hackernoon.com/lidar-basics-the-coordinate-system-a26529615df9)\
    \ (in your case the z coordinate would be 0)"
