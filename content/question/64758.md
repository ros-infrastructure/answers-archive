    `rxbag` is, [as it's noted on its web site, deprecated](http://www.ros.org/wiki/rxbag#Deprecated) since ROS Groovy and taken over by [rqt_bag](http://ros.org/wiki/rqt_bag). Thus I highly recommend you switch over to `rqt_bag`. Some advantages of `rqt`, the new GUI framework of ROS, are [noted here](http://ros.org/wiki/rqt#Advantage_of_rqt_framework) including the availability of sample codes for ROS' GUI applications, which might help you a lot upon your GUI programming.
