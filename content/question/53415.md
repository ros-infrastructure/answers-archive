    One simple method is recording some image messages using [rosbag](http://ros.org/wiki/rosbag/Commandline#record) and viewing them with [rxbag](http://ros.org/wiki/rxbag). The rxbag GUI will let you zoom in on the message timestamps, making it easy to see how close they are for the two cameras.
