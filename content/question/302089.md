    Sure, you can copy the URDF of a similar robot, insert it into your package and modify it as much as you like. [This xacro wiki page](http://wiki.ros.org/xacro) is what you will probably refer to most while rewriting the URDF.

    Note: If there already is an official URDF for your robot and you want to add things (e.g. a gripper or a camera), you should not modify the original file, but add the extra attachments in a separate file of your own.

    [Related](https://answers.ros.org/question/301164/adding-a-3d-camera-to-a-robot-armur5-in-urdf-for-rviz-and-gazebo/#301657).
