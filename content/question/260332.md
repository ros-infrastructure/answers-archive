    AFAIK ROS does not natively support any of the listed proprietary industrial protocols.
    But you can find some previous work on bridging between ROS and industrial protocols, like [EtherCAT](http://wiki.ros.org/Industrial/Tutorials/Simple_EtherCAT_Network_IO). Here is a nice collection of [ROS bridges](http://rosindustrial.org/news/2016/2/23/ros-bridges-for-common-field-busses) to several industrial field busses.
