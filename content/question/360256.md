    I think you could pull just `moveit/moveit_ros/visualization/` out of the moveit repo and build that in your catkin workspace, source it, then rviz will load those built plugins instead of the ones already installed on your system (probably via `apt`).  If you wanted to isolate the octomap one further you would have to edit the CMakeLists.txt and several of the xml files to tell it not to build anything except the octomap plugin.
