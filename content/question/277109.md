    In addition to what @mig said, checkout [@joq's answer](https://answers.ros.org/question/243192/catkin_make-vs-catkin-build/?answer=243360#post-id-243360) in which he states *not* to mix `catkin_make` and `catkin build`. Here's the body of his answer.
