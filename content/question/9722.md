    This may not be relevant to you, but RVIZ does support the saving and loading of configurations ([http://www.ros.org/wiki/rviz/UserGuide#Configurations](http://www.ros.org/wiki/rviz/UserGuide#Configurations)).  This is most relevant in launch files so that you could launch some code and have everything already set up.

    `<launch>
        <node pkg="rviz" type="rviz" name="rviz" args="-d $(find package_name)/config/rviz_config.vcg" />
    </launch>`
