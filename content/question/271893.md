    The `i_actual` field from the 'real-time interface' datastream is currently being used to populate the `effort` field of the `JointState` msg published on the `/joint_state` topic (see [RosWrapper::publishRTMsg() - line 676](https://github.com/ThomasTimm/ur_modern_driver/blob/fe9762e9d52b20bf6d1640bd8d046db27b0774d1/src/ur_ros_wrapper.cpp#L676)).

    See document `16496` on the UR support site for more information about what `i_actual` actually represents.
