  body_markdown: "There are currently no controllers supporting explicitly parallel\
    \ (delta) robots.\n\nBut what you can always do is to use [`ForwardCommandControllers`](https://control.ros.org/ros2_controllers/forward_command_controller/doc/userdoc.html)\
    \ ([example](https://github.com/ros-controls/ros2_control_demos/blob/c9ab5c18e130742180d28009acbefa2f78f1a64e/ros2_control_demo_bringup/config/rrbot_controllers.yaml#L15)),\
    \ or even [`JointTrajectoryController`](https://control.ros.org/ros2_controllers/joint_trajectory_controller/doc/userdoc.html)\
    \ to control the joints of the robot. If you want to do Cartesian control, then\
    \ you will have to calculate forward kinematics on your own.\n\nYou can simply\
    \ create an URDF without \u201Cclosing\u201D the kinematics and if you add ros2_control\
    \ part there, everything should work fine."
