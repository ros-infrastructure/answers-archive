    You can write a simple suscriber ( you can follow [this tutorial](http://wiki.ros.org/ROS/Tutorials/WritingPublisherSubscriber%28c%2B%2B%29) ) that will suscribe to your battery state topic.

    In the callback function you can access and display the voltage (for example) using `msg->voltage` .
    All the parameters you can access to are available [here](http://docs.ros.org/api/sensor_msgs/html/msg/BatteryState.html) .
