    Perhaps in `move_group_options` try changing `/robot_description` to `robot_description`. It appears that you use some namespace parameters during the launch and using a `/` in front of `robot_description` sets the MoveGroupInterface to search for it in the global namespace, which might mean - outside of the namespace you use. For more info on namespaces you can check out [Names](http://wiki.ros.org/Names). To see where everything is launched, you can use [rqt_graph](http://wiki.ros.org/rqt_graph)! Let me know if this has been useful!
