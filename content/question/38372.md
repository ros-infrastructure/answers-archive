    According to [wiki/hector_quadrotor_gazebo_plugins](http://www.ros.org/wiki/hector_quadrotor_gazebo_plugins), the Gazebo plugin subscribes to [geomety_msgs/Twist](http://www.ros.org/doc/api/geometry_msgs/html/msg/Twist.html) messages, so publishing these should allow you to control your UAV.

    See the rest of that page for more info on the Gazebo plugins.
