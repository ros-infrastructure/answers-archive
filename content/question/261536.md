but the robot is not moving in RVIZ.
I think that `move_base` should be used on a map, is it correct?

but I see the points of the map in RVIZ jumping and shifting without any logical reason.
If I do not launch the gmapping node, then the robot path showed in RVIZ is correct.

What is my mistake?
