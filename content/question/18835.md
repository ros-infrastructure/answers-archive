    First get the world:

    `auto world = this->GetWorld()`

    Then from the world get the other model:

    `auto beaconModel = world->GetModel("name_of_beacon_model")`

    Then get the link:

    `auto beaconLink = beaconModel->GetLink("name_of_beacon_link")`
