  body_markdown: |-
    You are filling the box incorrectly, if you look at [boundingBox](http://docs.ros.org/hydro/api/jsk_recognition_msgs/html/msg/BoundingBox.html) , you need to give pose (position) that will be the centre point of box, and dimensions that will be the length, width and height of box.

    It should look like following:

        box.pose.position.x = (max_x + min_x)  / 2;
        box.pose.position.y = (max_y + min_y)  / 2;
        box.pose.position.z = (max_z + min_z)  / 2;
        box.dimensions.x = (max_x - min_x);
        box.dimensions.y = (max_y - min_y);
        box.dimensions.z = (max_z - min_z);
    This should solve it.
